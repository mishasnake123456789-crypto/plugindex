What Pro Lens Add-On does

Pro Lens Add-On gives Blender artists customizable bokeh that imitates real camera optics. Effects imitate real glass, including cut-off bokeh edges, fringing and chromatic aberration, which add photographic character to a render.

Two base cameras are included, an anamorphic rig and a spherical one, each carrying three lenses. It is designed for Cycles, version 2.92 or higher, and documentation is included. William Landgren made it for artists who want their depth of field to look like it came through a real lens rather than a perfect one.

Key features

  • Customizable lens bokeh
  • Imitates cut-off bokeh, fringing and chromatic aberration
  • Anamorphic and spherical base cameras
  • Three lenses per camera
  • Requires Cycles 2.92 or newer
