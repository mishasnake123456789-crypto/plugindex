What Procedural Birds Flocks does

3D CONFIGURATION's Procedural Birds Flocks populates Blender scenes with flying birds without hand animation, handling birds, flocks and crowds procedurally.

A video tutorial and documentation explain usage. It supports Blender 3.4 through 5.0 under the GPL. Environment artists and animators who need ambient wildlife can fill skies and landscapes with motion for wide establishing shots with little setup. Because the result is procedural, flock size and behaviour can be revisited instead of re-animated, which suits revisions.
