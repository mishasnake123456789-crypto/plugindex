What Scatter does

Scatter by SiNi Software is a free tool for spreading 3D geometry over large surfaces in 3ds Max. Objects, proxies and cache files are added to the list and placed with a distribution map capable of up to one million points.

A paintbrush adds and erases geometry by hand, and objects can be transformed, added, replaced or erased by type without regenerating the entire distribution. Colour and texture variation, spline and camera clipping, and export to Edit Mesh are included. It works with V-Ray or Corona users who need forests, grass fields and crowds of props.

Key features

  • Distributes geometry, ProxSi and third-party proxies
  • Distribution map up to one million points
  • Paintbrush to add and erase
  • Edit by type without regenerating
  • Spline and camera clipping
