Media asset management panel for searching and importing archive footage, proxies and captions in Premiere Pro and After Effects.

What Mimir does

Mimir by Mjoll connects Premiere Pro and After Effects to a media, production and digital asset management system. Editors can search a library and bring in material at proxy or full resolution, reaching everything from deep archive to content that is still being captured live.

Transcription services generate captions that come into Premiere Pro, and AI-driven metadata tagging helps with content discovery. When a sequence has been cut with proxies, the high-resolution files can be rendered centrally rather than on the editor's machine. It suits teams who keep large libraries and want one searchable source for them.

Key features

  • Search and import in proxy or full resolution
  • Reaches deep archive through live content
  • Automatic captions imported into Premiere Pro
  • AI-driven metadata tagging
  • Central high-res render of proxy edits
