What Rotor does

Rotor is a small mirror tool for Blender that makes mirroring objects faster. Press Alt X or choose it as the active tool, then indicate the side you want to mirror along.

Right-clicking drops the active tool, a behavior added in version 2.2.0. The add-on needs no special permissions and works with Blender 5.0 and newer, making it a lightweight shortcut for anyone who mirrors geometry often.

Key features

  • Mirror objects from a single tool
  • Alt X shortcut
  • Choose the mirror side interactively
  • Right-click exits the tool
  • Blender 5.0 and newer
