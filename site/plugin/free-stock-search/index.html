What Free Stock Search does

Free Stock Search is a compact extension, usable in After Effects and Premiere Pro, that finds free stock photography from three sources: Unsplash, Pixabay and Pexels. It spares you the trip to a browser, the download folder and the manual import.

Type a keyword and results come from all available files across the agencies; English search terms tend to work best. Choosing an image downloads it and places it straight into your project with one click. The photos can be used in commercial and non-commercial work. The extension is built for Creative Cloud 2018 and newer.

Key features

  • Searches Unsplash, Pixabay and Pexels together
  • One-click download and import
  • Photos usable commercially and non-commercially
  • Works in After Effects and Premiere Pro
  • Supports Creative Cloud 2018 and newer
