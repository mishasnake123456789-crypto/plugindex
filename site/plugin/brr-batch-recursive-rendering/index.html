What Brr - Batch Recursive Rendering does

BRR automates rendering many variations of a Blender scene. It cycles through combinations of objects, cameras, lights, worlds and materials, so product variants or multi-scene projects do not need to be set up one render at a time.

A task system with Group IDs manages per-task materials, locked and solo objects, linked collections and dedicated cameras. You can render a chosen index range or a random percentage, and interrupted jobs can resume without losing progress. Files are organised by object, camera or world, and finished renders can be delivered by email, webhook, S3 or FTP/SFTP. Headless and background rendering are supported.

Key features

  • Renders combinations of objects, cameras, lights, worlds and materials
  • Task system with Group IDs and per-task settings
  • Partial rendering by index range or random percentage
  • Resume interrupted renders
  • Delivery via SMTP, webhooks, S3 and FTP/SFTP
