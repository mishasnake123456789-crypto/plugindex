Blender add-on that puts a topbar button for toggling Emulate 3 Button Mouse, aimed at graphics tablet users.

What Toggle Emulate 3 Button Mouse does

Toggle Emulate 3 Button Mouse adds a mouse-icon button beside the topbar menus in Blender. A click flips the Emulate 3 Button Mouse setting, which tablet users often need because a pen has no center button.

The add-on needs no special permissions, works with Blender 4.2 LTS and newer, is distributed under GPL v3.0 or later, and can be installed by dragging it into Blender or from a file.
