What AEJuice Night Sky Aurora does

Night Sky Aurora is a footage pack for adding sky atmosphere to movies, CGI, music videos and TV. It contains seven animated auroras, five twinkling star fields and five meteors or shooting stars, all in 4K.

Clips are delivered as videos with a transparent or black background, which makes them usable in After Effects, Cinema 4D, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, Final Cut, Nuke, OBS, Pinnacle Studio, Premiere Rush, Sony Vegas, XSplit and other editors. It runs on Windows 10 or later and macOS 10.14 or later, M1 and M2 included.

Key features

  • 7 animated auroras in 4K
  • 5 twinkling star clips
  • 5 meteors and shooting stars
  • Transparent or black backgrounds
  • Works in most video editors
