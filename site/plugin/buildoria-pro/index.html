What Buildoria Pro does

Buildoria Pro generates classical structures in Blender, from castles and palaces to temples, cathedrals and mansions, including interiors. Pillars, arches, walls, windows, stairs and roofs are fully procedural.

Arches and windows snap to walls and cut their own openings, with semicircle, gothic and round window styles. Staircases can be straight, path, round or spiral with handrails, and domes, spires and roofs follow curve profiles. Interactive handles use consistent hotkeys. The bundle has 200+ profile curves, 25+ capitals and 38 patterns, plus custom library support and instant arrays along curves.

Key features

  • Procedural pillars, arches, walls, stairs and roofs
  • Arches and windows that cut openings
  • 200+ profile curves and 38 patterns
  • Custom libraries and curve-followed arrays
