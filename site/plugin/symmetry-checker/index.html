Lightweight Blender add-on that finds asymmetric vertices along an axis and corrects them with a native symmetrize.

What Symmetry Checker does

Azerilo's Symmetry Checker spots where a model fails to be symmetrical in Blender. Vertices that break symmetry on the X, Y or Z axis are found and highlighted as a selection.

A customizable threshold sets tolerance, which suits high-resolution models. A click applies Blender's own symmetrize, with a choice of mirror direction like -X to +X or +Y to -Y. Character and hard-surface modelers can verify and repair symmetry before rigging or export.

Key features

  • Detects asymmetric vertices on X, Y or Z
  • Highlights the selection automatically
  • Adjustable tolerance threshold
  • One-click symmetrize
  • Choose mirror direction
