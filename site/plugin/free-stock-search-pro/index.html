What Free Stock Search PRO does

A tool from Free Stock Search PRO's developer that finds stock media from inside Adobe applications. It searches Unsplash, Pixabay and Pexels, and can also query Adobe Stock and Shutterstock without leaving the window.

One click downloads an asset and imports or places it directly into the project. Hosts include After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop, InDesign and Illustrator, from CC 2018 onward. Assets from the free sources can be used commercially and non-commercially at no cost, and English keywords give the best results. Editors needing a quick placeholder or final image avoid a trip to the browser.

  • Adobe Stock and Shutterstock search too
