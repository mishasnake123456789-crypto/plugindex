What Ergb Streaks Panel does

The Ergb Streaks Panel builds glowing animated trails in Blender without any shader work. Press Generate Streaks and editable emission streaks appear.

Speed, colors, thickness and strength can be adjusted, and edits happen in real time. Streaks can be rebuilt, revised or remixed at any time. Motion designers can create light trails and abstract energy lines quickly for backgrounds and title sequences. Among its tools are single Generate Streaks button and no node setup or shader chains.

Key features

  • Single Generate Streaks button
  • No node setup or shader chains
  • Speed, color, thickness and strength controls
  • Real-time editing and remixing
