What Motioneer Lite does

Motioneer Lite is a motion engine for reusing animation. A Copy tool captures selected keyframes and masks, or the transform properties and masks of selected layers, and an Add tool attaches them to other layers.

It retains comp and layer ratios during transfer, so animation designed for one size fits another. The engine handles expressions, parents and track mattes, and it works on After Effects from CS6 through 2026. It is helpful for animators reusing a move across many layers or adapting animation to a different composition size.

Key features

  • Copy keyframes, masks and transforms
  • Add tool applies them to other layers
  • Keeps comp and layer ratios
  • Supports expressions, parents and track mattes
  • Works with CS6 through 2026
