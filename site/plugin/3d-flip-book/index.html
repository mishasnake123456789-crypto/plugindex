What 3D Flip Book does

3D Flip Book from Froggy Team is a script for After Effects that turns a folder of page images into a fully animated 3D book. Select the folder, click once, and the book is generated.

Two opening modes are available, Symmetrical and Right to Left (or Left to Right), and the pages can be curved by using the Ray-traced 3D Renderer from Cinema 4D. Pseudo Effect controls adjust the result, and page duration can be Still, Loop or Pingpong. It suits magazines, albums and storybook presentations.

Key features

  • Animated 3D flip book from a folder of images
  • Symmetrical and Right to Left opening modes
  • Curved pages with the Ray-traced 3D Renderer
  • Pseudo Effect controls
  • Still, Loop and Pingpong page durations
