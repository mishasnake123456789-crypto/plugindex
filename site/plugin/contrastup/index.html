What ContrastUP does

ContrastUP, by vimager, boosts the contrast of footage and lets you fine-tune the darkest, middle and brightest tonal ranges separately. It works in After Effects and Premiere Pro.

A histogram adjustment slider provides further shaping. Multi-Frame Rendering and Apple Silicon are supported, and if graphics cards aren't detected correctly, processing falls back to the CPU.

Key features

  • Image contrast enhancement
  • Separate shadows, midtones and highlights
  • Histogram adjustment slider
  • Multi-Frame Rendering and Apple Silicon
  • CPU fallback without GPU detection
