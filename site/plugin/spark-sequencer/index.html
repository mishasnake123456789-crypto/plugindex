What SPArk Sequencer does

SPArk Sequencer is a Blender add-on by TinyNick that smooths out sequence-based storyboarding and layout work. It lets you handle separate shots using Blender's scenes, cameras and the Video Sequence Editor.

VSE and scene timelines stay synchronized, and a VSE-based shot manager adds, renames and deletes shots. Shots can be batch rendered from the VSE, editorial data moves in and out through OTIO, and scenes share folders by collection linking. Timeline sync across scenes in one window may not work fully without the Blender SPA build.

Key features

  • Synchronized VSE and scene timelines
  • Shot management in the VSE
  • Batch rendering of shots
  • OTIO editorial import and export
  • Shared folders via collection linking
