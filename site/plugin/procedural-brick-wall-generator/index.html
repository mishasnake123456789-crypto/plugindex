What Procedural Brick Wall Generator does

Procedural Brick Wall Generator converts Blender's default cube into a brick wall using a geometry node. Length, height, weathering and brick displacement are adjustable, and you can add several walls, each with its own settings.

Bricks can stack vertically, with the shortest side on the ground, or hide their joints. Light and dark texture menu items give different looks, and two included wall textures come with generated UV maps so you can use custom textures. Bricks and mortar get separate materials. Architects and environment artists building ruined or weathered walls will find it flexible.

Key features

  • Brick wall generated from a cube
  • Length, height, weathering and displacement controls
  • Vertical stacking or hidden joints
  • Two textures with generated UVs
  • Separate brick and mortar materials
