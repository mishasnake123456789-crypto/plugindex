What Escalerillas_Pro does

Escalerillas_Pro creates staircases for architectural models. You draw two guide curves and the add-on builds the stairs between them with millimeter precision.

More than 25 templates cover straight, L-shaped, U-shaped, spiral and imperial styles. Tread, riser and thickness are fully controllable, and structural components such as customizable railings are included, along with finishing options. Architects and interior modelers avoid building every step manually. Editing the guide curves later reshapes the whole staircase, which makes design changes painless during early planning.

Key features

  • Stairs between two guide curves
  • 25+ templates: straight, L, U, spiral, imperial
  • Control of tread, riser and thickness
  • Customizable railings and structural parts
