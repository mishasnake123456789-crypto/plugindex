What Show/Hide Maps does

Show/Hide Maps from mirza is a free 3ds Max utility for switching texture display on and off in the viewport. Heavy scenes with many textures can feel sluggish, and turning off map display helps while modeling or arranging objects.

It can work on selected objects only or on the entire scene, making it easy to compare the shaded and textured looks. The tool is designed for quick, simple use. It is at version 1.1, updated in February 2023, and a demo video is available to preview how it works.

Key features

  • Toggles viewport display of texture maps
  • Works on selected objects or the whole scene
  • Free and simple to use
  • Version 1.1 updated February 2023
  • Demo video available
