What R2Loki does

R2Loki is aimed at architectural and structural modeling in Blender. Rooms can be L-shaped, U-shaped or box-type, and structural elements like footings, columns and beams are added in a few clicks.

Staircases can be designed instantly, and a pie menu on Ctrl+R gives quick access to the tools. The interface is simple enough for beginners yet suitable for experienced users, and it adapts to residential and other building types. Useful for quick building massing and layout.

Key features

  • L, U and box-type rooms
  • Footings, columns and beams
  • Instant staircases
  • Pie menu on Ctrl + R
