What Shape Shifter: Awesome Shape Shifting With Blender Addon does

Tri-vima Studio's Shape Shifter pulls scattered Shape Key settings into a single panel so you stop hunting through the properties editor.

Its other trick is copying shape keys from an object to another in a few clicks, which saves effort when characters or variants share facial or corrective shapes. A demo video is on YouTube, documentation is included, and the royalty-free license covers Blender from the 2.8 series through 4.2.
