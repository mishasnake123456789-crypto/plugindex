GPU-accelerated After Effects plugin that fakes extrusion thickness by duplicating layers, controlled from one null.

What Depthion does

Depthion by grape design gives flat layers a pseudo-thickness in After Effects by stacking duplicates of them. Because it fakes depth rather than rendering true geometry, the look is quick to create and quick to preview on the GPU.

Shape controls include Tilt, Spin, Back Scale and bevel, and all adjustments are managed from a single control null. It works with After Effects 2023 through 2026. Designers wanting chunky, dimensional text or graphics without a 3D renderer will find it convenient.

Key features

  • Pseudo-thickness from duplicated layers
  • Tilt, Spin, Back Scale and bevel controls
  • Everything managed from one control null
  • GPU-supported rendering
  • Works with After Effects 2023-2026
