What PAI video search does

PAI video search, by Post AI, analyzes video frames, identifies the objects present and labels them automatically. Results appear in a timeline visualization, so finding a particular shot or subject becomes quicker.

It integrates with Adobe Premiere Pro 22.0 and later. Editors with large amounts of footage can use it to avoid scrubbing through clips. It targets projects with hours of rushes, where finding a specific object by eye would take a long time.

Key features

  • AI analysis of video frames
  • Automated object tagging
  • Timeline visualization for navigation
