What Loop Selector does

Loop Selector gives modelers precise control over loop-based selections. It adds options to the sidebar for picking edge loops, face loops and rings, rather than relying on shortcuts alone.

Edge loops can be chosen around a mesh with ring options for complex operations, and face loops expand a selection across larger regions. Alternate-edge selection and next or previous stepping are included, with real-time sliders to manage the selection and extrusion. Useful for topology work and detailed panel modeling.

Key features

  • Edge loop and ring selection
  • Face loop selection to grow regions
  • Alternate-edge selection
  • Real-time sliders for selection and extrusion
