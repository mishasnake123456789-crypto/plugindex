What Markers Pro does

Markers Pro by Leyero adds editing power to Premiere's markers. Change colors, cut, copy, paste and delete markers, offset them by frames or seconds, and snap them to the playhead.

Actions can be limited to markers inside in and out points, before or after the playhead, with control over special markers whose edges fall outside. Up to five presets are saved, markers can be added to a sequence or to clips, and undo and redo are custom. Keyboard shortcuts can be assigned.

Key features

  • Recolor, cut, copy, paste and delete markers
  • Offset by frames or seconds
  • Align markers to the playhead
  • Up to 5 marker presets
  • Assignable keyboard shortcuts
