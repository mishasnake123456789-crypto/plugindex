Floating Premiere Pro panel that shows live clip metadata and copies specs for handoffs.

What Spool does

Spool floats over Premiere Pro as a compact panel, reading out what you are looking at on the timeline. As the playhead moves, it refreshes with file name, path, codec, resolution, frame rate, camera model, reel, timecodes, used frame range, drop-frame state and audio format.

Clicking a row copies that single value, while Copy Specs produces a formatted summary of every visible field, handy for emails, bid documents, conform notes or VFX handoffs. A picker chooses which track or selected clips to read, and a gear menu hides fields you do not need, with choices remembered between sessions. An end-timecode toggle helps match Premiere and Resolve.

Key features

  • Live metadata that follows the playhead
  • Click any row to copy its value
  • Copy Specs formatted summary
  • Track picker and multi-clip selection
  • Configurable fields remembered across sessions
