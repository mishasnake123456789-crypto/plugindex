What Power Paste does

Power Paste is a Blender plugin that turns an image on your clipboard into usable content. Paste it over the editor under the mouse, and the matching material, texture node or geometry is created for you.

Two shortcuts are provided: Ctrl+Alt+V for images with alpha, and Ctrl+Shift+Alt+V for height and displacement compatibility. The second mode pre-processes maps to avoid stepping artifacts typical of 8-bit images. It speeds up reference, decal and texture workflows.

Key features

  • Paste into the viewport or node editors
  • Ctrl+Alt+V for images with alpha
  • Height and displacement pre-processing
  • Avoids 8-bit stepping artifacts
