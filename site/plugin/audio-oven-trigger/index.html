What Audio Oven Trigger does

Audio Oven Trigger, by Baraka-1, links audio playback to animation in Blender, so sounds can be triggered by what happens on screen. It is tagged under Sequencer and Geometry Nodes.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.
