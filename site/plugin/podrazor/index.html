Builds a first-pass multi-camera edit for podcasts and interviews in Premiere Pro based on who is speaking.

What PodRazor does

PodRazor makes initial cuts for dialogue-heavy video. Map each speaker to an audio track and one or more camera angles, and the Auto Cut workflow switches to whoever is talking, alternating between angles when several are available.

Sensitivity controls for speaker switching and for separating a real speaker from audio bleed work globally or per speaker. A wide-shot workflow sets when and how often group shots appear, Audio Enhancement targets voice clarity, and presets speed up repeat jobs.

Key features

  • Auto Cut by speaker, audio track and camera
  • Multiple angles per speaker
  • Adjustable switching and bleed sensitivity
  • Wide-shot frequency and duration control
  • Voice clarity tools and saved presets
