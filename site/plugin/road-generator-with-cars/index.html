What Road Generator With Cars does

studioromanrec's Road Generator With Cars makes road scenes with traffic included, which is especially useful for backdrops where placing vehicles manually isn't worth the effort.

A documentation set and demo video accompany it. Supported versions run from Blender 3.4 to 4.3, and it uses the GPL license. City shots, driving sequences and animated environments can gain a lived-in feel quickly, leaving more time for hero assets. Creators of animatics and previs can fill a street shot in moments.
