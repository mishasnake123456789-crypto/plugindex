What MarkCam does

MarkCam is a lightweight Blender add-on from SomRender for camera-bound timeline markers. Clicking the camera icon in the Timeline header attaches the active camera to a marker on the current frame, and a management panel sits in the Timeline's N-panel.

Shift-click creates a camera from the current 3D view, and Ctrl-click does the same and binds it to a marker. The X button clears markers on the current frame but leaves cameras alone, and Ctrl-click removes all markers after confirmation. A Camera Ranges panel lists only cameras with a bound marker. Useful for multi-camera shot switching.

Key features

  • Header icon ties the active camera to a marker
  • Shift-click creates a camera from the 3D view
  • Remove markers without deleting cameras
  • Camera Ranges panel for bound cameras
  • Management panel in the Timeline N-panel
