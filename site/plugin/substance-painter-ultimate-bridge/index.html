Bridge for 3ds Max and Substance Painter that sends models out and builds shaders from exported textures for several renderers.

What Substance Painter Ultimate Bridge does

Substance Painter Ultimate Bridge links 3ds Max with Substance Painter. Models travel from Max to Painter, and when texturing is finished the exported textures come back and shaders are created automatically for the render engine you choose.

On export it takes care of shader naming and ID masking. On import, multi-shader setups and all textures are brought into the renderer automatically. V-Ray is supported with both PBR and reflection and glossiness workflows, along with Corona, Arnold, Redshift and Scanline, and UDIMs work in every supported engine. Texture artists can skip the repetitive manual hookup of maps.

Key features

  • Sends 3ds Max models out to Substance Painter
  • Auto-creates shaders from imported textures
  • Shader naming and ID masks handled on export
  • V-Ray, Corona, Arnold, Redshift and Scanline support
  • UDIM support in all supported engines
