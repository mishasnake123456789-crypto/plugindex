What INSYDIUM Fused Perpetual License does

INSYDIUM Fused Perpetual License packages the company's Cinema 4D products into a single purchase that you keep. It covers GPU-accelerated particle effects, plant animation, realistic terrains, modeling and final rendering, and adds a set of Library Assets.

X-Particles supplies the core particle and VFX system, driven by a Questions and Actions rule set for cloth, smoke, fire, fluids, grains and dynamics. ExplosiaFX creates smoke, fire and explosions that export as VDB volumes, and xpFluidFLIP offers APIC and FLIP solvers with a collision system for static and fast-moving objects. It targets motion designers who want the entire toolset permanently.
