Paper-fold style animation generator for text, alpha mattes and keyed footage in After Effects and Premiere Pro.

What FoldCraft does

FoldCraft transforms titles, logos and cut-out footage into layered paper-fold animations. After importing a source image, you choose the mode that matches it: Standard for general footage, Alpha Channel for mattes, Green Screen for keyed material or Text with Expand Bounds for type.

Texture, borders, blending, shake and timing all have controls. Assemble styles include two Torn Paper variants, and a Paper Style option supports halftone. It works in After Effects and Premiere Pro and suits titles, logo reveals, social content and general motion graphics.

Key features

  • Standard, Alpha, Green Screen and Text modes
  • Text Mode with Expand Bounds
  • Texture, border, blend, shake and timing controls
  • Torn Paper assemble styles
  • Paper Style with halftone
