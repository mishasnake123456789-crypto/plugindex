What lipsyncr 2 does

lipsyncr 2, by Joachim Holler, builds mouth animation automatically in After Effects. You import a spoken track together with its transcription, and it analyzes the English speech and animates a mouth composition in seconds.

Three viseme mappings are offered: four illustrations at lower quality, seven at medium and ten at high. Template viseme images with illustration guides are supplied, and you can create a template composition at your chosen count and replace the templates with your own artwork. Any number of audio files and mouth compositions can be combined.

Key features

  • Full-featured English speech analysis
  • 4, 7 or 10 viseme mappings
  • Template viseme images with guides
  • Replace templates with your own artwork
  • Combine multiple audio files and mouth comps
