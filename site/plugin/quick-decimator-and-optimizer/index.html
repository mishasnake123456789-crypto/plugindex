What Quick Decimator & Optimizer does

Quick Decimator & Optimizer gathers the steps of preparing lightweight models into a single sidebar panel. Typical uses are lightweight assets for games, AR, VR and the web, where polygon budgets are tight.

Collapse decimation comes with ratio presets for AR/WebGL, game assets and previews, while a planar mode dissolves flat subdivided areas by angle. A one-click cleanup applies transforms, merges by distance, removes loose geometry and recalculates normals. A live face counter shows the effect, and models can be exported in batches afterward.

Key features

  • Collapse decimation with ratio presets
  • Planar angle-dissolve mode
  • One-click cleanup of transforms, doubles and normals
  • Live face counter
  • Batch export
