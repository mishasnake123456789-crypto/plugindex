What Fum - Fix Your Mesh does

FUM, Fix Your Mesh, is a lightweight validator and export preflight tool for Blender. It detects non-manifold edges, flipped normals, N-gons, duplicate vertices and isolated vertices.

Results are reported as PASS, WARNING or FAIL, problem areas are highlighted in the viewport, and a one-click Export Preflight workflow runs the checks before delivery. Good for 3D printing and game pipelines. Key capabilities include detects non-manifold, flipped and isolated geometry and PASS, WARNING and FAIL results.

Key features

  • Detects non-manifold, flipped and isolated geometry
  • PASS, WARNING and FAIL results
  • Viewport highlighting
  • One-click Export Preflight
