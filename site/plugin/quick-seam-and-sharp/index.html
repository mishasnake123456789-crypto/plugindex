What Quick Seam & Sharp does

Quick Seam & Sharp automates edge marking in Blender. Press Alt+C to run a macro on all selected objects that finds sharp edges and seams using a customizable angle threshold, with 45 degrees as the default.

It also detects rings and tubes in cylinders, pipes and spherical shapes. Bevel weights are applied too. Hard-surface modelers and UV artists can skip the manual selection and marking process. Under the hood you get alt+C one-click macro and angle threshold, default 45 degrees.

Key features

  • Alt+C one-click macro
  • Angle threshold, default 45 degrees
  • Ring and tube detection
  • Sharp, seam and bevel weight marking
