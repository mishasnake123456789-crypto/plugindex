What Productstudio - Fast Product Render Tools does

ProductStudio creates the basics of a product shot without manual lighting work. A single click sets up the camera, a three-light key, fill and rim arrangement, a shadow-catching plane and tidy collections.

Render presets apply quality settings tuned to balance look and render time, and the background can be white or transparent. It supports both Cycles and Eevee. Product visualizers, e-commerce artists and hobbyists who want clean results quickly can start from a sound setup and adjust from there.

Key features

  • One-click studio with camera, key, fill and rim lights
  • Shadow plane and organized collections
  • Render presets balancing quality and time
  • White or transparent background
  • Supports Cycles and Eevee
