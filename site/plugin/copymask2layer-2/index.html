What CopyMask2Layer 2 does

CopyMask2Layer 2, from mamoworld tools, transfers masks from one layer to another without shifting their on-screen position. Copying a mask normally breaks alignment if the layers differ in size, position or movement, and this tool compensates.

Alignment is maintained when the source or target layers are moving, and masks with keyframed paths are handled. It works with After Effects from CS3 through 2026. Version 2.2.003 fixes online activation connection issues and uses an updated license framework, and a demo video is available.

Key features

  • Transfers masks between layers
  • Preserves exact position
  • Works with moving layers
  • Handles keyframed mask paths
  • Compatible with CS3 through 2026
