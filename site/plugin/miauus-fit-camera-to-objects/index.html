What miauu's Fit Camera to Objects does

Fit Camera to Objects, by miauu, moves the active camera in 3ds Max so that the selected objects fit completely inside its view. Camera parameters stay as they are, which keeps FOV, focal length and zoom unchanged, so only the position is adjusted.

Tested cameras include VRayCam, FStormCamera, CoronaCamera, Target, Free and Physical ones; objects with deformable points, meaning geometry and splines, can be framed. A viewport showing an active camera is needed, since perspective, orthographic and other views are not handled. This is a commercial script for 3ds Max and 3ds Max Design 2020 to 2026. Good for quickly framing product shots.

Key features

  • Repositions the camera to fit selected objects in view
  • FOV, focal length and zoom remain unchanged
  • Tested with Physical, Free, Target, Corona, FStorm and VRay cameras
  • Works with geometry and splines
  • Works with 3ds Max 2020 through 2026
