What Spline Check does

Spline Check from SIMPOLIUM is a diagnostic helper for 3ds Max. It identifies open splines and displays them right in the viewport so you can see which curves are not properly closed.

It also detects self-intersections and reports the total segment count of a spline. The plugin supports 3ds Max 2017 through 2023 and is installed with a custom installer rather than the standard App Store one. Modelers who extrude, sweep or lathe splines, where open ends cause problems, can catch mistakes early.

Key features

  • Shows open splines in the viewport
  • Detects self-intersections
  • Reports total segment count
  • 3ds Max 2017 through 2023
  • Custom installer
