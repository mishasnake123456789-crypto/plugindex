What Lucero - Light And Render Manager Add-On does

Lucero is a lighting and render manager for artists making portfolio renders in Blender. It brings lights, projections, environments, cameras and compositing into the N-Panel.

Lights get camera and surface alignment, color and temperature control, falloff curves and IES support through a custom Light Editor. The GOBO system takes images or videos, with animation, tiling and a caustic option, and the HDR system offers blurred or solid backgrounds. Cameras and depth of field are managed, and a master compositor node provides bloom, glare, grain and more. Presets can be saved.

Key features

  • Light Editor with IES and falloff curves
  • GOBO and HDR systems with free content
  • Multi-camera and depth of field management
  • Master compositor node for finishing
