What Vr Cinerig does

VR CineRig lets you perform camera moves and object animation with VR hardware. It tracks headsets and controllers and records their movements into the scene, so you can shoot like a real camera operator.

Live feedback shows your movement inside Blender. It works with Blender's built-in VR Scene Inspection add-on, and its interface is designed to be approachable for newcomers. It brings a natural handheld feel to virtual cinematography. You get VR headset and controller tracking, recording to camera or object motion and real-time feedback, along with the rest of the feature set.

Key features

  • VR headset and controller tracking
  • Recording to camera or object motion
  • Real-time feedback
  • Works with VR Scene Inspection
