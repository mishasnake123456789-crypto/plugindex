What miauu's Objects Replacer does

Objects Replacer by miauu swaps objects or groups in a 3ds Max scene for other objects or groups, creating the replacements as copies, instances or references. You choose what carries over to the replacement, such as transform, material, name, wire color, layer and hierarchy.

Position, rotation, scale and visibility animation can be carried across, and the child objects' transform controllers are copied or instanced. Groups may be treated as one object, replaced member by member, or partially replaced while the hierarchy is kept. Children can optionally be cloned, and all scene instances of a replaced object can be swapped at once, useful when upgrading placeholder geometry.

Key features

  • Replace objects or groups with copies, instances or references
  • Inherit transform, material, name, layer, parent and wire color
  • Transfers position, rotation, scale and visibility animation
  • Flexible group handling with hierarchy preserved
  • Option to replace all scene instances
