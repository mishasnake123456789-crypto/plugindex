What BOOM Library Signature Series - Stereo does

BOOM Library Signature Series Stereo gathers every one of the 100 packs in the original Signature Series into one purchase, with over 210,000 sounds. It serves After Effects and Premiere Pro users who want a wide royalty-free effects collection.

Themes run from cinematic effects to vehicles, medieval to science fiction. Packs are split into Construction Kits and Designed variants where appropriate, delivered as 24-bit WAV for Windows and Mac. UCS labelling and metadata can be read by Soundly, Basehead and Soundminer. Basics, Stereo and Surround variants exist, so choose by format.
