What Traffic Convex Mirror Generator does

Traffic Convex Mirror Generator makes road-safety mirror assets from a sidebar tab. It supports single or dual setups and round or rectangular shapes selected with the Mirror_Type option.

Colors and safety strips are adjustable, and textures can be baked. Because it is built on Geometry Nodes, the mirror stays parametric. Street scene and city environment artists can add variations without remodeling. Variants can be tuned to match local conventions, so one tool can furnish many different street scenes.

Key features

  • Single or dual mirrors
  • Round and rectangular shapes
  • Adjustable colors and safety strips
  • Texture baking
  • Geometry Nodes based
