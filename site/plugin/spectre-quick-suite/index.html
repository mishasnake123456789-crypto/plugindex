What Spectre Quick Suite does

Spectre Quick Suite combines three Blender add-ons that all run on Cycles and need nothing external. They cover instant lighting setups, a shelf of procedural materials and a handful of object-based effect simulations.

One click configures lights, camera, backdrop, color management and denoising using Studio, Cinematic, Product Shot, Neon, Outdoor or Dramatic looks, with volumetric fog and a choice of OIDN or OptiX denoiser. Over fifty procedural PBR materials in nine categories apply instantly without image textures. Nine effects, such as fire, lightning, tornado, ice and a magic orb, build rigs ready to bake or play. A PDF guide is included.
