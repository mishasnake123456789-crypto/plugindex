What Terrain Mixer Pro (Alpha) does

Terrain Mixer Pro (Alpha) is a Blender system for building landscapes from real geometry, working in both Cycles and Eevee. It targets people who need either believable or abstract terrain without sculpting every hill by hand.

Its modular approach lets you stack and combine multiple landscape sources into a single environment, and the blending stays non-destructive so earlier choices can be revisited. That makes it quick to explore variations of a scene. Artists, game developers, matte painters and illustrators are the intended users, and the developer provides support and updates.

Key features

  • Geometry-based terrain generation
  • Works in Cycles and Eevee
  • Modular, non-destructive terrain blending
  • Combines multiple landscape sources
  • Fast iteration on terrain variations
