What Thumbs Up does

The Machine Must Work's Thumbs Up exports still images from your current project in Premiere Pro, After Effects or Photoshop. It pulls from a Sequence, Composition or Document, depending on the host.

Output resolution, width and height can be set, and files are named after the sequence, document or composition. Date information can be added, either as a full date or as separate values for day, month, year, hours, minutes and seconds. It supports releases from 2020 to 2024. Editors and designers producing YouTube thumbnails or approval stills can automate repetitive exports.

Key features

  • Thumbnails and frame grabs from Premiere, After Effects and Photoshop
  • Naming from sequence, document or composition
  • Resolution, width and height settings
  • Date and time stamps in names
  • Supports 2020 to 2024 releases
