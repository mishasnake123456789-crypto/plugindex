What Swift 4D - The Intuitive Mograph Workflow does

Swift 4D brings a motion graphics style of working to Blender, inspired by industry-standard software. Everything stays parametric and non-destructive, so earlier decisions can be revisited.

A stackable extrude and inset system lets you edit the distance or thickness of an earlier step while later operations adapt. Bevels applied by group, sweeps, wrapping objects onto splines and deformers that rig themselves round out the toolkit. Motion designers moving to Blender can build logos and abstract forms in a familiar way.

Key features

  • Parametric Extrude and Inset stack
  • Smart group-based bevels
  • Sweep generation and spline wrapping
  • Auto-rigged deformers
  • Non-destructive throughout
