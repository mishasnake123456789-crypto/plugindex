What AR-media does

AR-media, from Inglobe Technologies, sends models from 3ds Max directly into AR-media Studio, a platform for creating augmented and virtual reality experiences. Once there, you add events and interactions and attach more digital assets and media to enrich the project.

A free registration on the Studio platform is needed to export and deploy models, and the finished experiences can be viewed in the free AR-media Player app for iOS and Android. Support for the 2024 release was added. Architects and product designers who want an interactive AR walkthrough of a model can use it.

Key features

  • Direct export of 3ds Max models to AR-media Studio
  • Build AR and VR experiences from models
  • Add events, interactions and media
  • View in the free AR-media Player for iOS and Android
  • 3ds Max 2024 support
