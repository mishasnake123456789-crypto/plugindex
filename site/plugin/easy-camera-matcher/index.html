What Easy Camera Matcher does

Easy Camera Matcher aligns a Blender camera with real footage so that 3D elements sit correctly in a photograph or clip. Open a photo, draw your perspective lines, define the world origin and scale, and Blender's camera is solved for you.

The camera updates in real time while you draw, scenes can be reconstructed by projecting the image onto geometry, and lens distortion handling is included. VFX artists adding CG objects to plates can get matching perspective and scale without tracking markers.

Key features

  • Camera matching from drawn perspective lines
  • Real-time camera updates
  • Origin and scale setting
  • Image projection onto reconstructed geometry
  • Lens distortion support
