What Sonoton Music Search does

Sonoton Music Search lets editors hunt for production music within Premiere Pro. The catalogue exceeds 610,000 tracks and comes from the largest independent music library in the world.

It includes vocals, instrumentals, alternate versions and stems, plus a similar-tracks search. Results can be filtered by genre, style, length, mood and rhythm, with 60, 30 and 15 second cutdowns. Several tracks can be downloaded together as WAV or MP3 and tried in the production.

Key features

  • 610,000+ tracks
  • Vocals, instrumentals, alt versions and stems
  • Similar tracks search
  • 60, 30 and 15 second versions
  • WAV and MP3 downloads
