What Dosch 3D: Jewelry and Watches does

Dosch 3D: Jewelry and Watches contains 50 detailed models of rings, earrings, cufflinks, bracelets, necklaces and twelve wristwatches, ready for Cinema 4D. Formats include C4D from version 8.5, an R15 to R24 download, FBX, 3DS, OBJ, Maya, Lightwave, Softimage XSI, VRML and KeyShot.

Scaling, materials, textures and colors are adjustable, and you can put on your own textures and labels. Parts can be switched on or off, set to partial transparency, and animated. Product visualizers and advertising artists can use the models for catalog imagery and luxury-style renders.
