What Bonsai does

Bonsai, from the IfcOpenShell project, is a large add-on for Blender that spans modeling, import and export, rendering, animation, materials and lighting. It is available on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Builds are provided for Windows, Linux and macOS on Intel and Apple Silicon, and it installs by drag and drop or from disk. It is aimed at Blender 4.2 LTS, and newer releases from 5.1 are not supported. The license is GPL v3 or later. Architects and building-information specialists working in Blender are the main audience.

Key features

  • Cross-platform builds for Windows, macOS, Linux
  • Covers modeling, import-export and rendering
  • Installs by drag-and-drop or from disk
  • Compatible with Blender 4.2 LTS
