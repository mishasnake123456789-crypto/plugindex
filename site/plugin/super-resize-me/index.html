What Super Resize Me does

Super Resize Me, from Mograph Mindset, changes the resolution of an entire After Effects project in one go. Rather than rebuilding comps by hand, it scales complex projects up or down and converts between HD and 4K in either direction.

It also resizes an individual comp while you work, supports bulk versioning, and deals correctly with 2D layers, 3D layers, camera layers and the default 3D camera. Parented and locked layers are handled too, so structures stay intact after scaling.

Key features

  • Upscale or downscale whole projects
  • HD to 4K and back
  • Resize single comps
  • Handles 2D, 3D, camera and parented layers
  • Bulk versioning
