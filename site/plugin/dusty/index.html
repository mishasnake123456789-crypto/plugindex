Dusty

Developer: WillUrquhart3D

FreeBlender
Download Free

Applies a dust shader and hair particles to any Blender object with a single click.

What Dusty does

Dusty makes surfaces look neglected. Select an object, click once, and a realistic dust shader with hair particles is put in place.

Editor controls refine the result, and two customizable node groups offer deeper adjustment. Version 1.1.0 adds cobwebs and floating dust. It works in both Cycles and Eevee and includes documentation. Environment artists dressing abandoned rooms, attics or old props can age scenes in seconds. Because it is applied to the object itself, you can add it to individual props instead of re-lighting or reshading the whole scene.

Key features

  • One-click dust shader and hair particles
  • Editor controls and two node groups
  • Cobwebs and floating dust
  • Cycles and Eevee support
