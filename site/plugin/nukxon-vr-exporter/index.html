What Nukxon VR Exporter does

Nukxon VR Exporter is a Blender plugin that converts a scene into a walkable virtual reality tour. You place VR cameras in the scene, render panoramas from each, export the geometry and pack it all into one shareable .nukxon file.

Cameras are placed by clicking, with snap guides for rows, columns and intersections. Each renders a six-face cubemap in Cycles at 1024 or 2048 pixels, and the mesh goes out as Y-up glTF with Draco compression. A spacing graph shows coverage, and teleport points and project links connect multiple scenes.

Key features

  • Click-to-place cameras with snap guides
  • Six-face cubemap renders in Cycles
  • Draco-compressed glTF mesh export
  • Coverage graph for camera spacing
  • Teleport points and project links
