What Pivot & Align Tools does

Pivot & Align Tools, or PAT, is a collection of macros in a quad menu for aligning objects, aligning pivots and snapping objects. Most functions work on a selection of several objects at once, including open or closed groups.

Pivot Align To Polygon places the pivot at the center of a chosen polygon and orients it to the surface normal. Clone To Surface makes instances on a picked surface with each click, optionally with dragging, and Drag On Surface uses each object's negative local Z axis to find an intersection and snap to its normal. The quad setup fits the menu system in 3ds Max 2025 and later while older versions stay supported.

Key features

  • Quad menu of alignment and snapping macros
  • Multi-object and grouped selections
  • Pivot Align To Polygon with surface normal
  • Clone To Surface with optional dragging
  • Drag On Surface snapping
