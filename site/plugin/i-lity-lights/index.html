What I-Lity Lights does

i-Lity Lights simplifies studio lighting in Blender for beginners and novices. Everything lives in one panel, so there is little to hunt for.

Quick studio setup adds pre-configured lights with a few clicks from a choice of presets. HDRI Dome World Projection maps an HDRI onto a dome-shaped half sphere as the background, and an HDRI browser has a built-in preview. Camera management adds and aligns cameras to your view, and object utilities round out the toolset.

Key features

  • Pre-configured studio light presets
  • HDRI Dome World Projection
  • HDRI browser with preview
  • Camera add and align tools
  • Object utilities in one panel
