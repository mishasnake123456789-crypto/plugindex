Beat-aware editing for After Effects and DaVinci Resolve that cuts footage to the rhythm of music.

What BeatEdit does

BeatEdit analyses a music track and lets you build edits around its beat in After Effects and DaVinci Resolve. It helps editors who want cuts to land on the rhythm without placing every marker by hand.

It can create edits that lengthen, shorten or rearrange a track while keeping it musical. Beat information can be turned into timeline markers or clip markers, and audio beat clicks work like a metronome to confirm the detection. Subtitles are supported as well. It is aimed at music-driven projects such as promos, montages and lyric videos.

Key features

  • Beat-based cutting to a music track
  • Make a track longer, shorter or reshuffled
  • Timeline and clip markers from beats
  • Audio beat clicks as a metronome
  • Subtitle support
