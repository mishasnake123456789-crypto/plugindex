What Switch & Click (Camera Switching Addon) does

Switch & Click adds a panel showing the current camera and listing all cameras in the scene. Click a name to make it active, or use Previous, Next and Random buttons.

Cameras can be renamed on the fly, and you can switch and render between cameras in the scene. It is handy for multi-angle product and architectural renders. Notable points: camera list with click to activate, previous, Next and Random buttons and rename cameras on the fly.

Key features

  • Camera list with click to activate
  • Previous, Next and Random buttons
  • Rename cameras on the fly
  • Render between cameras
