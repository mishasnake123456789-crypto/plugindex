What DOSCH 3D: Car Details V3 - Combustion does

Version 3 of the Car Details series includes this brand-neutral sedan with an internal combustion engine, fully textured with all its important parts and systems. It comes for Cinema 4D and many other formats.

Scale can be adapted, materials, textures and colors freely changed, and your own labels applied. Elements may be switched on or off, set to a chosen transparency and animated independently or all at once. Because the car carries no brand identity, it is easy to use in advertising, training and technical illustration without licensing concerns.
