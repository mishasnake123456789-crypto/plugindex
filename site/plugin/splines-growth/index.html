What Splines Growth does

Splines Growth is a Blender add-on for creating organic spline structures that spread over any mesh surface. It suits visual effects, abstract motion graphics and nature-inspired systems such as vines, roots or sci-fi energy paths.

Growth follows the surface of a selected mesh with randomized paths so no two splines are alike. Start and end frames, growth speed and reverse growth control the animation, and a curvature offset adds random distortion. Motion designers can produce growing line effects without keyframing each curve.

Key features

  • Splines grow across a selected mesh
  • Randomized paths avoid duplicates
  • Start and end frames, speed and reverse growth
  • Curvature offset for distortion
