What Digital Anarchy Samurai Sharpen for Video does

Samurai Sharpen for Video is a sharpening filter for After Effects and Premiere Pro that works only on the details you want enhanced. Its algorithm recognizes significant edges, so areas like eyes gain crispness while flat regions like skin stay smooth.

Built-in masking shields shadows and highlights so noise doesn't get boosted, and blend controls adjust the light and dark halo along edges separately. GPU rendering speeds things up, reaching real time on some cards. It also runs in Final Cut Pro X, Avid Media Composer and DaVinci Resolve.

Key features

  • Edge-aware sharpening
  • Isolate specific areas of footage
  • Shadow and highlight protection
  • Separate light and dark halo blending
  • GPU rendering, real time on some cards
