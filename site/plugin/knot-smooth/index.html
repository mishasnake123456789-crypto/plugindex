What Knot smooth does

Knot smooth is a small 3ds Max script that evens out the handles of Bezier knots on splines. Irregular handles produce lumps and kinks in curves, and straightening them out by hand is slow on long paths.

Chuggnut, whose official page is chuggnut.com, makes the script, now at version 2.3.5. It is catalogued in the ScriptSpot library and is free. It will appeal to modelers working with splines for lofts, sweeps, paths and logos who want cleaner, smoother curves.
