A Blender add-on that generates walk cycles and free walks for Rigify rigs, with real-time preview.

What Free Walk For Rigify does

Free Walk For Rigify creates walking animation for characters built with Rigify. You can output either a repeating walk cycle or a free walk, and preview both in real time while tuning the controls.

Four walking types are available: drunk, normal, rotated and zombie, and the character can walk forward, sideways or backward from the panel. Free sequence mode moves the body without the root, with per-step direction control and a freeze option so you can edit steps for uneven ground and obstacles. Action generators produce the result, and personality controls shape the walk. Animators needing quick locomotion will save time.

Key features

  • Walk cycle and free walk outputs with live preview
  • Drunk, normal, rotated and zombie styles
  • Forward, sideways and backward walking
  • Per-step control for uneven ground
  • Action generators for both modes
