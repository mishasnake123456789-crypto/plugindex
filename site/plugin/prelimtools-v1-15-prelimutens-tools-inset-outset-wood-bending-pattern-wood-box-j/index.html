What Prelimtools V1.15 - Prelimutens Tools ( Inset/Outset,Wood Bending Pattern,Wood Box Joint ) does

Gabor Horvath's Prelimtools targets makers who prepare flat designs in Blender. Its Fill with inset function draws a sequence of concentric outlines inside a shape, or outside it for outsets.

An Inset Width value, positive or negative, sets the spacing between outlines, and Maximum Outline Number limits their count. The add-on can also produce bending patterns for wood laser printers and includes a wood box joint tool. Laser-cutting and CNC hobbyists get patterns ready for fabrication.

Key features

  • Fill with inset for concentric outlines
  • Inward or outward direction
  • Adjustable inset width and outline count
  • Bending patterns for laser-cut wood
  • Wood box joint tool
