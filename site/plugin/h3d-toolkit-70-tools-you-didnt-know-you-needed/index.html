What H3D Toolkit - 70+ Tools You Didn't Know You Needed does

hodgson3d's H3D Toolkit packs more than seventy tools aimed at automating everyday Blender work, in areas such as navigation, lattices, shape keys, UVs, curves, materials, modifiers, selection, sculpting, vertex groups and baking.

Box Unwrap and Cylinder Unwrap mark seams and flatten boxy or tube-shaped topology, with the option to hide seams from the side facing the camera. Select UV Errors detects zero-length edges, degenerate or overlapping UVs, stretching, and UDIM crossing or border problems. General modelers get breadth.
