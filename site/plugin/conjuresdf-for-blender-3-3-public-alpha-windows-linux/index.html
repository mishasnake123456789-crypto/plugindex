What Conjuresdf For Blender 3.3 - Public Alpha (Windows/Linux) does

ConjureSDF is a non-destructive modeling add-on for Blender that assembles shapes out of signed distance field primitives, booleans and blend modes. The public alpha is available for Windows and Linux and ships with a raymarched rendering engine of its own.

There are 12 primitives, including box, sphere and cylinder; four booleans (union, subtract, difference, intersect); and four blend modes: none, smooth, chamfered, inverted round. Primitives can be nested to assemble complex forms from simple ones. Conjure Vision displays the SDF result, and an SDF to Mesh conversion turns it into regular geometry.

Key features

  • 12 non-destructive SDF primitives
  • Union, subtract, difference and intersect booleans
  • Smooth, chamfered and inverted round blending
  • Nested primitives for complex shapes
  • Conjure Vision raymarched preview engine
