What Aura does

Aura, from Rowbyte, creates elegant geometric shapes in 3D space within After Effects. Instead of scattering particles, it builds vector-like waves that evolve over time, reminiscent of the classic Radiowaves plug-in.

It can render hundreds of thousands of high-frequency waves and supports Multi-Frame Rendering. Sequences can be exported as SVG, and there are a Dots Rendering Mode, color maps for gradients and a gradient position mode. Camera near and far visibility settings control what appears, and Apple Silicon and M1 are supported. It is suited to abstract visuals and music-driven graphics.

Key features

  • Procedural 3D wave generation
  • Hundreds of thousands of high-frequency waves
  • SVG export for sequences
  • Dots mode and gradient color maps
  • Camera near and far visibility
