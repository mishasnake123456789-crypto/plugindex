Lego Generator

Developer: Milad_Kambari

FreeBlender
Download Free

Free Geometry Nodes add-on that rebuilds a UV-textured Blender model as a Lego brick mosaic colored from its texture.

What Lego Generator does

Lego Generator turns a textured model into a mosaic of bricks with a single button. Each brick samples the colour of the model's texture at its position, and it works with single or multi-material models.

Bricks can be hollowed with an adjustable shell thickness to cut the count, and you may use the built-in brick, any object in the scene or one appended from another .blend file. A viewport percentage previews only some bricks while renders show all. Bricks snap into a clean grid, and the whole thing is non-destructive: one click builds it, another removes it, and the original model is untouched.

Key features

  • Samples texture color per brick
  • Hollow shell with adjustable thickness
  • Custom brick from scene object or external .blend
  • Viewport percentage preview, full bricks in render
  • Non-destructive build and remove buttons
