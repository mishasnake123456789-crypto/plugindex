Physics-based bevel and light sweep plugin for After Effects that works on a single layer without precomposing.

What LightSweepPro does

LightSweepPro from InovativeWorks adds a physically inspired bevel and a moving light sweep to a layer in After Effects. It works directly on one layer, so no precompose is necessary.

Seven presets give a starting point, and controls cover chromatic aberration, surface source and texture, and an adjustable bevel profile. Sweeps can be linear or radial, with center-out, center-in, edge-to-center and custom-origin modes. A luminance mode and edge, noise, angle and color controls refine the look, while auto spin and jitter animate it automatically.

Key features

  • 7 starting presets
  • Adjustable bevel profile
  • Linear and radial sweep modes
  • Chromatic aberration and surface texture controls
  • Auto spin and jitter animation
