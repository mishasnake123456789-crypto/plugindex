What Sculpt Subtools does

Sculpt Subtools, by Daniel-Soler, brings a ZBrush-style subtool palette to Blender's sculpt workflow, so separate parts of a sculpt can be managed from one list. The tagline is written in Spanish. It is listed under Sculpt and Mesh.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.0.0 or newer.
