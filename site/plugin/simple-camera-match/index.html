What Simple Camera Match does

Simple Camera Match is a lightweight Blender add-on by MingWitty for reconstructing a camera and matching perspective. As X, Y and Z vanishing lines are drawn over a background image, the camera's focal length, location and rotation are solved live.

Drawing happens entirely in the camera viewport's 2D space with live visual feedback. A horizon constraint with draggable handles fine-tunes pitch, and drawing has its own independent undo and redo stack. It helps VFX artists place 3D objects into photographs.

Key features

  • Live solve driven by X, Y, Z vanishing lines
  • Draw in the camera viewport with live feedback
  • Horizon constraint with draggable handles
  • Independent undo and redo for drawing
  • Solves focal length, location and rotation
