What XperienceED does

XperienceED is an online learning platform now covering Premiere Pro as well as Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign. It is designed to help students understand key concepts across Adobe applications.

It includes pre and post course assessments that follow Adobe certification objectives, self-paced lessons with progress tracking, flash cards, short videos, eBooks and project challenges. Instructors can manage groups and customize curriculum, and the system is LTI compliant for integration with learning platforms.

Key features

  • Pre and post course assessments
  • Self-paced courseware with progress tracking
  • Flash cards, videos and eBooks
  • Group management and custom curriculum
  • LTI compliant
