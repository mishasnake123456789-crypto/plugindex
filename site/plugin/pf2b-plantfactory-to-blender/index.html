What Pf2B - Plantfactory To Blender does

Pf2B brings PlantFactory vegetation into Blender and sets it up automatically.

Access covers more than 200 species and over 10,000 presets, each procedurally unique, in both low-poly and high-poly versions, working with Cycles and Eevee. Environment artists and archviz users can add plants without fixing materials by hand. Automating the import and shading steps removes a tedious conversion process, so vegetation from that library can be placed in scenes with far less manual cleanup.

Key features

  • Automated PlantFactory import
  • 200+ species, 10,000+ presets
  • Low-poly and high-poly support
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
