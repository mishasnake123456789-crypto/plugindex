Connects Blender to Stable Diffusion WebUI for txt2img, img2img and ControlNet generation.

What Sdwebuitools does

Sdwebuitools links Blender to a Stable Diffusion WebUI installation so AI images can be generated without leaving the application. Both text-to-image and image-to-image are supported, with inpainting available in img2img.

Up to three ControlNet units can use depth and canny information from the scene, and extras tools modify or enhance the results. Interrogation through CLIP and DeepBooru, Lora management with adjustable weights and ADetailer support are also provided. Concept artists and texture creators gain a direct AI loop.

Key features

  • txt2img and img2img with inpainting
  • Up to three ControlNet units
  • CLIP and DeepBooru interrogation
  • Lora add/remove with weights
  • ADetailer support
