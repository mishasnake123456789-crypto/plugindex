What wizzymotion does

wizzymotion is a free panel for After Effects and Premiere Pro that combines a template library with AI-based media creation. Editors can start from a ready-made video template or generate new material without opening a separate website.

Templates are customized through the Essential Graphics Panel. The generation side covers video, images, music and voice-over, drawing on models and services such as Veo 3.1, Nano Banana Pro, Sora 2, Suno, ElevenLabs, Runway, Hailuo and Seedance. Extra free tools for After Effects are included, along with a community of video creators and games that raise your daily usage limits.
