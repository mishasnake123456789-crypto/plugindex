What Area Switcher does

Area Switcher speeds up changing editors. Rather than opening a menu and choosing an area type, one click or Ctrl+Shift+D switches among areas you configure.

Source and destination areas are set in the add-on properties, several areas can be cycled in sequence, and a switch button lives in the area header. Documentation is included. Highlights include single-click area switching, configurable source and destination and sequential switching. Also on board: ctrl+Shift+D shortcut.

Key features

  • Single-click area switching
  • Configurable source and destination
  • Sequential switching
  • Ctrl+Shift+D shortcut
