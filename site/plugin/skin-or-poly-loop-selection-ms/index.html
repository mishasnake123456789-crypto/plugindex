What Skin_Or_Poly_Loop_Selection.ms does

Paul Hormis made this MaxScript for 3ds Max to select loops while working in Skin or Poly. It is published on ScriptSpot as version 1.

Compatibility is listed for 3ds Max versions 8, 7 and 6, and a demo video is available on YouTube. Loop selection picks an edge or vertex loop around a mesh, which is a core step in both polygon editing and skin weighting. The details provided are sparse, so it mainly serves users of those older releases who want a script-based loop selection.
