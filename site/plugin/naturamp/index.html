Procedural natural gradient generator for After Effects with Atmosphere, Organic and Mineral modes.

What NatuRamp does

NatuRamp is a grape design plugin that creates gradients with an uneven, non-linear spread of color, something the standard After Effects gradient tools do not produce. The result looks closer to sky, foliage or stone than to a smooth two-color blend.

Three modes shape the character: Atmosphere for light-scattering transitions inspired by air, Organic for growth-like flow similar to petals and living tissue, and Mineral for weathered transitions suggesting erosion and oxidation. Color infiltration is adjustable, and the Scatter control adds spatial noise. The controls are kept simple and intuitive.

Key features

  • Non-linear natural gradient generation
  • Atmosphere, Organic and Mineral modes
  • Adjustable color infiltration
  • Scatter control for spatial noise
