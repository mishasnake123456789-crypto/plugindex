What AEJuice Flash FX does

AEJuice Flash FX is a large library of hand-animated, frame-by-frame effects elements. Fire, water, lightning, explosions, smoke and particle bursts are all included, giving editors a ready supply of stylised action and fantasy overlays.

The 890-plus elements work inside After Effects (CC 2015 onward) and Premiere Pro (CC 2021 onward), and as transparent or black-background videos they also fit CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Filmora, Final Cut, Nuke and OBS. Older After Effects versions can use project files provided as is. It runs on Windows 10 or newer and macOS 10.14 or newer, M1 and M2 included. Motion designers can layer effects quickly without simulating them.
