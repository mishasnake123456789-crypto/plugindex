What Laser System does

CGPoly's Laser System generates laser effects in Blender scenes. A click adds a laser, and its properties are tuned afterwards.

Beam color, intensity, width, length and further options are adjusted procedurally through Blender's Geometry Nodes framework, and results look consistent in Eevee and Cycles alike. Sci-fi scenes, laser shows, scanners and security grids are examples where it can save setup time. Since the beams are built procedurally, changing a color or length later does not mean rebuilding the effect.

Key features

  • One-click laser generation
  • Adjustable color, intensity, width and length
  • Built on Geometry Nodes
  • Works in Eevee and Cycles
  • Procedural adjustments
