What Stock Search does

Stock Search from Vakago Tools lets creatives look up free stock material without leaving the program they are working in. It covers images, vector graphics, music and video, and reaches millions of files.

It integrates with After Effects and Premiere Pro as well as Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign, so a chosen file drops into a document, edit or animation quickly. Downloads arrive in high quality. A free version is available, and a PRO version supplies 4K or higher quality.
