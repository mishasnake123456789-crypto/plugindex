What Two Super Addons does

studioromanrec's Two Super Addons bundles a pair of tools for lifting flat artwork into three dimensions in Blender. Sketch something or load a picture, and a 3D object appears together with its texture almost immediately.

JPG and PNG files work. The set supports Blender 3.3 up to 4.0, includes documentation and is GPL licensed. It offers a fast route for logos, doodles and simple illustrations to become scene geometry without modelling them yourself.

Key features

  • Converts 2D images into 3D objects
  • Carries the texture along
  • Accepts JPG and PNG files
  • Two add-ons in one bundle
  • Blender 3.3 to 4.0
