What Frameforge - Camera Sets & Batch Render Workflow does

Frameforge is a production-focused Blender add-on for architectural visualization. It groups cameras into sets, manages client revisions and batch renders still images into structured output folders.

A scene camera browser and picker selects cameras, sets can be enabled or disabled individually, and each camera can override resolution. Clear revision naming and navigation keep versions orderly. Archviz studios delivering many views and revision rounds will find it keeps delivery consistent. Among its tools are camera browser and picker and camera sets with enable and disable.

Key features

  • Camera browser and picker
  • Camera sets with enable and disable
  • Per-camera resolution overrides
  • Revision naming and navigation
  • Batch stills to structured folders
