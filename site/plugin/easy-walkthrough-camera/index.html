What Easy Walkthrough Camera does

Easy Walkthrough Camera simplifies flythrough animation in Blender. Architects and visualization artists can build a walkthrough without calculating keyframes manually.

Place a camera at a screen position, create the walkthrough with one click and let the add-on compute keys for constant movement. A quick scale speeds up or slows an existing animation. Multiple cameras can be added, removed and reordered in a list ready to render, and shortcuts handle camera view, orbiting between cameras, moving objects and inserting keys.

Key features

  • Camera placed at a screen position
  • One-click walkthrough with constant speed
  • Quick scale for animation timing
  • Multi-camera list for rendering
