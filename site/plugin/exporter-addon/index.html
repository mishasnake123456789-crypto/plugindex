What Exporter Addon does

Exporter Addon is a straightforward Blender tool from A.T Design Studio for getting models out of the application without fuss. It supports FBX export and aims to keep the process quick and tidy.

Documentation walks through usage. It is built for Blender 4.1 and distributed under a Creative Commons license. Anyone who regularly sends models and textures to game engines or other software and wants fewer export dialog settings to remember may find it convenient.
