What Skin_Or_Poly_Ring_Selection.ms does

Paul Hormis wrote this MaxScript to handle ring selection on skin or polygon components in 3ds Max. It is published on ScriptSpot and designed for 3ds Max versions 6, 7 and 8.

The tool is a single .ms file, marked as version 1, with a demo video available. A ring selection picks a band of parallel edges around a mesh, which is useful when adding loops or weighting rows of vertices. It is relevant for users of older 3ds Max releases who want a scripted approach to this selection task.
