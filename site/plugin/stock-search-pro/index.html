What Stock Search Pro does

LetsMotion's Stock Search Pro lets you find free stock material without leaving your editing software. It is available in After Effects, Premiere Pro and other Creative Cloud apps.

Search filters narrow results, a download manager handles files you have pulled, and higher-quality, larger photos are downloaded. A sound effects library is built in. Editors and motion designers looking for quick placeholders or final assets can search and import in the same panel.
