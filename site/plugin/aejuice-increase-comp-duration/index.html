A script that sets or adjusts the duration of compositions, nested comps and layers in After Effects, singly or in batches.

What AEJuice Increase Comp Duration does

AEJuice Increase Comp Duration changes how long compositions and their nested contents run. Type a fixed value such as 5 seconds, or enter +5 or -5 to lengthen or shorten the existing duration by that amount.

It can work on compositions selected in the project panel, the open composition, or chosen layers, and it leaves alone layers that are already shorter than the comp and do not need extending. It integrates with kBar. Motion designers extending a finished animation will avoid changing durations one comp at a time.

Key features

  • Set a fixed new duration
  • Add or subtract time with +/- values
  • Batch change for selected comps, open comp or layers
  • Leaves shorter layers untouched
  • kBar integration
