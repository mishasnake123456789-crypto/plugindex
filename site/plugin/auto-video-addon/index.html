What Auto Video Addon does

Auto Video Addon turns rendered image sequences into finished video or GIF files without a separate converter. A manual mode allows careful tuning of the output, while the automatic mode converts hands-free.

Output can be MP4, MOV, AVI or GIF, with adjustable frame rate. For GIFs, quality and the number of skipped frames can be customised. Support for Blender 4.1 and newer arrived in version 0.0.7. It is convenient for sharing quick previews and animated loops.

Key features

  • Image sequence to video or GIF
  • Manual and automatic modes
  • MP4, MOV, AVI and GIF output
  • Frame rate and GIF quality control
