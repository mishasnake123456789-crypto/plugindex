What Fusion does

Fusion is a Blender plugin from Egret Studios LLC for joining meshes cleanly. It uses a three-step process that preserves topology, so merged results remain tidy.

It works with Blender 3.0 through 5.2 in Cycles and Eevee, includes documentation, and includes a year of support and updates. Good for hard surface and character assembly. Keeping the topology tidy is valuable when the merged result will be subdivided, sculpted or animated later on.

Key features

  • Three-step mesh merge
  • Preserves topology
  • Blender 3.0 through 5.2
  • Twelve months of updates and support
