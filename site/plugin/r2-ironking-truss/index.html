What R2-Ironking Truss does

R2-Ironking Truss generates structural steel frameworks in Blender, aimed at temporary sheds, site shelters, workshops and pre-engineered buildings.

Styles include King Post, Mono Truss, Bowstring, Mansard and Dome Structure. Span, height, roof slope angles, number of divisions and more are customizable, and metal sheet covers can be added. Architects and visualizers can produce roof structures without modeling each member by hand. Key capabilities include king Post, Mono, Bowstring, Mansard and Dome styles and customizable span, height and slope.

Key features

  • King Post, Mono, Bowstring, Mansard and Dome styles
  • Customizable span, height and slope
  • Metal sheet roof covers
  • Automated framework creation
