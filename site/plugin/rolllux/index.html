What RollLux does

RollLux studies a reference image and recreates its lighting in Blender. It reads off key, fill and ambient tints, contrast, mood and color temperature, then builds a rig of key, fill, rim, accent and world lights to match.

A portrait-tuned mode estimates light direction, such as left, right, top or backlight, with a confidence value, and the rig can be tuned live. It works with Cycles and Eevee, giving cinematic, portrait, product or stylized looks a fast starting point.

Key features

  • Reference photo lighting analysis
  • Key, fill, rim, accent and world light rig
  • Portrait light direction estimate with confidence
  • Live tuning
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
