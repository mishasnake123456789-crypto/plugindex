What AEJuice Liquid Elements does

AEJuice Liquid Elements holds more than 1,200 frame-by-frame animations drawn at 12 fps, made entirely from After Effects layers and sorted into over 50 categories. Splashes, smoke, fire and lightning are typical of its contents.

A style engine can apply drop shadow, glow, pixelate, roughen edges, scatter, texture, doodle, stroke or isometric looks to every element in a click, and colours change globally too. Premiere Pro users can export .mogrt files and web or mobile developers can export Lottie JSON via Bodymovin. The Pack Manager offers preview, drag and drop, favourites and shortcuts, with over 10,000 style variations available.

Key features

  • 1,200+ hand-drawn animations in 50+ categories
  • Style engine applies looks to all elements at once
  • Global editable colours
