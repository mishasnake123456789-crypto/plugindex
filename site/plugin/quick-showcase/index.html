FreeBlender

Builds a 360-degree camera and light ring around the active Blender object, batch-renders every angle and creates a sprite sheet.

What Quick Showcase does

Quick Showcase creates a ready-to-render turntable-style scene around whatever object is active. A customizable ring of cameras and lights is parented to the object.

The number of cameras and lights and the background colors are set in a UI panel. Render Showcase then renders every camera in the set, and the frames are stitched into a sprite sheet. It suits game developers, shop owners and portfolio builders who need multi-angle views of an asset.

Key features

  • Instant 360-degree camera and light ring
  • Adjustable camera count, light count and background
  • Batch render of every camera
  • Frames combined into a sprite sheet
  • Rig parented to the active object
