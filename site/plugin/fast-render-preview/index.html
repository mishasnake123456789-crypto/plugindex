Blender add-on that fires off a quick low-cost test render on Alt+F12, then puts your render settings back.

What Fast Render Preview does

Checking progress with a full-quality render wastes time, while lowering samples and resolution by hand invites mistakes. Fast Render Preview by GilaDDD makes one keypress, Alt+F12, render a small and simple version of your scene.

Once the preview completes, your original settings are restored automatically. It cooperates with both Cycles and Eevee and has documentation. Handy for artists iterating on lighting, materials or layout who want frequent sanity checks. Because nothing is left changed afterwards, you can repeat the check as often as you wish.

Key features

  • Alt+F12 preview render
  • Applies fast, small render settings
  • Restores original settings afterward
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
  • Documentation included
