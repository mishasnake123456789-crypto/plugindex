What Cube Wave Generator does

Cube Wave Generator is a small Blender add-on that produces rippling waves made of cubes, with settings to change the look of the effect. Its origin is a YouTube demo showing how to put together an add-on with ChatGPT.

It was built for Blender 3.4, comes with documentation and a demo video, and uses a royalty-free license. Motion designers wanting a quick wave pattern, and anyone curious how an AI-assisted add-on is put together, may find it a handy example.
