What Font Previewer Lite does

Font Previewer Lite shows how fonts will look without leaving After Effects. Instead of trying typefaces one by one on a text layer, you can preview them directly and apply your choice from within the application.

It is a native plug-in with Apple Silicon support on Mac and works with After Effects versions from CS6 through 2025. Designers and motion artists choosing type for titles will find it speeds up font selection.

Key features

  • Preview fonts inside After Effects
  • Apply the chosen font directly
  • Apple Silicon support
  • Supports CS6 through 2025
