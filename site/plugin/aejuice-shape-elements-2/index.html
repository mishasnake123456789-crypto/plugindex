What AEJuice Shape Elements 2 does

AEJuice Shape Elements 2 supplies more than 1,000 animated shapes for building title sequences, lower thirds and other motion graphics templates. Colors are editable throughout, and most categories are made from After Effects layers.

It works with After Effects, Premiere Pro and other video editors. The Pack Manager provides fast preview, drag-and-drop import and favorites, so browsing and applying elements is quick. Editors can assemble animated graphics from ready parts rather than animating each from zero.
