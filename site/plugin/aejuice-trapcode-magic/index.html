What AEJuice Trapcode Magic does

AEJuice Trapcode Magic supplies spell-casting and fantasy effects for After Effects, driven natively by Red Giant's Trapcode Particular version 6, which you must already own. There are 15 elements, each fully adjustable.

For people who do not use After Effects or lack the particle plugin, pre-rendered versions come as videos with transparent or black backgrounds and work in DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and other editors. After Effects CC 2022 or later is required for the native effects, and a demo video is available. Artists wanting magical visuals without designing emitters from nothing get a head start.

Key features

  • 15 customisable magical VFX elements
  • Native effects powered by Trapcode Particular
  • Pre-rendered versions for other editors
  • Transparent or black background videos
  • After Effects CC 2022+ with Particular v6
