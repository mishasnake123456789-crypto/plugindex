What Templater Pro Subscription Recurring does

Templater Pro Subscription Recurring extends Templater Rig for After Effects with desktop automation. It renders or replicates batches of versioned compositions from a data source, and this listing is the yearly recurring subscription.

Recent features include per-target output configurations in multi-target jobs, footage validation before a batch starts, and a preference to halt on missing aep files. Manual batches can track status in Google Sheets, and multi-target support covers the $D.target() API. Studios producing many unique versions, such as promos or broadcast interstitial slates, are the target audience.

Key features

  • Desktop batch rendering from a data source
  • Per-target outputs in multi-target jobs
