What Dosch 3D: Airplane Details does

Here is a richly detailed airplane from Dosch Design, supplied in Cinema 4D format from version 8.5 upward as well as many other formats. It carries all the important details of an aircraft and is fully textured.

Materials, textures and every color can be modified, and you can add your own labels. Individual elements may be turned off or made partly transparent, scaling is adjustable, and parts can be animated separately or as a whole. The model works for technical illustrations, flythrough animations and advertising visuals where the aircraft needs to be customized.

Key features

  • Detailed, fully textured airplane model
  • C4D 8.5+ plus many other formats
  • Editable materials, colors and labels
  • Elements switchable with adjustable transparency
  • Individual or whole-model animation
