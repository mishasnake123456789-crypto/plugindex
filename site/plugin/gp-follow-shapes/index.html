Blender add-on that keeps Grease Pencil drawings attached to meshes as rigs or shape keys deform them.

What Gp Follow Shapes does

Malisan Luca's Gp Follow Shapes lets hand-drawn strokes ride on deforming 3D geometry. Strokes remain aligned with meshes animated by rigs, shape keys or elaborate character setups, and your original drawings are never altered.

Automatic binding does the following, and shape key support covers facial animation, including ARKit-style mocap pipelines. Controls for precision and influence distance fine-tune the effect. 2D-in-3D animators adding line work to 3D characters get a non-destructive route.

Key features

  • Dynamic binding to mesh deformation
  • Shape key and ARKit facial support
  • Non-destructive to drawings
  • Precision and influence distance controls
  • Works with rigs and complex characters
