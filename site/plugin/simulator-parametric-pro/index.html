What Simulator Parametric Pro does

Simulator Parametric Pro brings environmental analysis into Blender for architects, engineers and designers. The wind simulation exposes speed, direction and turbulence, finds pressure zones on surfaces by itself, and can animate loads for studies over time.

Solar tracking follows the sun based on geographic location, and smart hook-based deformation lets you control shapes with real-time parameters. It helps test how a building or structure responds to conditions early in design.

Key features

  • Wind simulation with speed, direction, turbulence
  • Automatic pressure zone detection
  • Animated wind loads
  • Geographically based solar tracking
  • Hook-based deformation with real-time control
