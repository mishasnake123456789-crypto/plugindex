What Volumetric Clouds Generator does

Volumetric Clouds Generator places clouds in a Blender scene without requiring shader work. It runs in Cycles and Eevee and requires Blender 2.83 or newer.

Adjustable settings span density, color, scale, shape, wind, coverage, roundness and detail. Cloud types range from simple cumulus to larger cloudscapes. Environment artists and animators can create sky scenes and animated weather with a few controls. Everything is driven from simple sliders, so even those unfamiliar with volumetric shaders can reach a believable sky quickly.

Key features

  • Clouds added without material editing
  • Cycles and Eevee support
  • Density, size, wind, coverage controls
  • Cumulus and cloudscape types
