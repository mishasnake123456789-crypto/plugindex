Blender add-on for real-camera controls: sensor size, focal length, ISO, aperture, shutter speed and white balance synced to the scene.

What Kamera does

Kamera lets Blender users build a camera with real-world photographic settings that update in sync with the scene.

Sensor choices include Cinema 8K, Full Frame and APS-C, with focal lengths from an 8mm fisheye to a 135mm telephoto. ISO (50 to 6400), aperture (f/1.4 to f/22) and shutter speed (1/8000s to 1s) are calculated with the real camera EV formula. White balance offers Kelvin presets, and color management modes such as AgX and Filmic are available.

Key features

  • Sensor sizes from Cinema 8K to APS-C
  • Focal lengths 8mm to 135mm
  • ISO, aperture and shutter via EV formula
  • Kelvin white balance presets
  • Color management modes including AgX and Filmic
