Sets Blender render resolution from one-click presets for HD, 4K, 8K and social formats, with landscape and portrait switching.

What Resolution Pro does

Resolution Pro adds a preset row to the Format panel in Blender's Output Properties, directly under the normal resolution fields. It spares you from typing pixel dimensions every time the delivery format changes.

Presets cover HD, 4K and 8K as well as social dimensions for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts. A dropdown flips between landscape and portrait, and X and Y values swap automatically. Because it lives in native UI, motion artists and social video creators can use it without learning a new panel.

Key features

  • One-click presets for HD, 4K and 8K
  • Social media sizes for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts
  • Landscape and portrait dropdown
  • Automatic X/Y swap on orientation change
  • Integrated in Output Properties Format panel
