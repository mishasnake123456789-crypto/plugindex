An animated typeface for After Effects whose letters are built from two overlapping shapes.

What Binary - Animated Typeface does

Binary is an Animography typeface where every character is composed of two geometric shapes that overlap. Each shape, and the area where they intersect, can be given its own color, enabling bold graphic lettering.

Letters can switch between filled and outline styles, and timing is driven by a 0 to 100 percent slider. Custom easing or bounce expressions can be applied for extra personality. It is a good fit for motion designers making expressive titles in After Effects 2020 through 2024.

Key features

  • Characters from two overlapping shapes
  • Individual coloring of each shape and overlap
  • Solid and outline styles
  • 0 to 100% timing slider
  • Custom easing or bounce expressions
