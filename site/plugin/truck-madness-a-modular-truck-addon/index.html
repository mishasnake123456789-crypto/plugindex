What Truck Madness - A Modular Truck Addon does

Truck Madness builds truck models in Blender from interchangeable parts. 3D artists and designers choose pieces like chassis, wheels and headlights to assemble a customized vehicle.

The models are non-branded to avoid copyrighted logos, shapes and design elements. Lighting setups are customizable, collections are organized and workflows are optimized. Documentation is included and the add-on uses the GPL.
