What Freesound Audio Search does

Freesound Audio Search brings the Freesound database into After Effects, Premiere Pro and Audition. Editors can look up background audio, sound effects and music without opening a browser and sorting through downloads by hand.

The library contains more than half a million files organized into categories, and many are cleared for both commercial and non-commercial use. Most carry Creative Commons licences or are in the public domain, so it is worth checking each file's terms. The extension is free and made by Vakago Tools.

Key features

  • Searches 500,000+ free audio files
  • Categories: background audio, effects and music
  • Many files cleared for commercial use
  • Mostly Creative Commons or public domain
  • Works in After Effects, Premiere Pro and Audition
