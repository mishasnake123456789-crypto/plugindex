What Odometer does

Odometer from Plugin Everything animates numbers, text and symbols in After Effects. It has three modes, Numbers, Words and Glyphs, and the numeric engine can count as high as 9 duotrigintillion, a figure with 99 zeroes.

Perspective and feathering are built in. Numbers mode inherits your easing, while Text and Glyphs modes include Penner easing presets. Five order modifiers (reverse, random, increment, inside out and outside in) change how characters roll. It renders at 32bpc with GPU-accelerated motion blur and runs natively on Intel and Apple Silicon with MFR enabled.

Key features

  • Numbers, Words and Glyphs modes
  • Counts up to 9 duotrigintillion
  • Penner easing presets for text and glyphs
  • Five order modifiers including random and inside out
  • 32bpc with GPU motion blur and MFR
