Blender add-on for spawning rigid bodies with control over velocity, offsets, rotation and order.

What Quick Spawn Rigid Bodies does

Quick Spawn Rigid Bodies adds many physics objects to a scene without configuring each one, including particle-style setups.

You can set a starting velocity for spawned instances, apply location and animation offsets, rotate instances, and choose the spawn order. It is distributed as a Blender extension under the GPL and includes documentation.

Key features

  • Fast rigid body spawning
  • Starting velocity control
  • Location and animation offsets
  • Rotation and spawn order settings
