A free Premiere Pro extension that synchronizes clips by their timecode.

What Parallel does

Parallel lines up clips in Premiere Pro using timecode, which is handy for multi-camera shoots where devices recorded matching timecode. It runs as an extension panel and is free.

The current version is 1.1.1, updated in August 2024. It supports Premiere Pro 2022 through 2026 and has a demo video. It is simple, quick to apply and costs nothing, which suits editors working with several recorded sources. It is handy for lining up footage from several cameras or recorders that share timecode.
