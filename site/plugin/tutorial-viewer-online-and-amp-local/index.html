What Tutorial Viewer (Online &amp; Local) does

Tutorial Viewer lets you watch video lessons without leaving Blender. It can play videos from the internet as well as files saved on your computer, and sound is included.

That saves switching windows or monitors while following along with a tutorial, and keeps the lesson beside your work. Learners who prefer to pause and practice step by step will find it convenient.

Key features

  • Plays online videos
  • Plays local video files
  • Audio playback included
  • Stays inside the Blender window
