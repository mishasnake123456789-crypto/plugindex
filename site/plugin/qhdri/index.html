What Qhdri does

qHDRI is a small Blender add-on for anyone who keeps a library of HDRI maps. You point it at your HDRI folder once, and from then on you can scroll through previews and pick the one that lights your scene.

It works with both Cycles and Eevee, supports Blender 3.0 through 4.5, and is released under the GPL license with documentation included. The point is to replace the repeated routine of opening the world node tree and browsing for image files with a quick visual picker.

Key features

  • Set the HDRI folder once and reuse it
  • Scroll through HDRI previews to pick one
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
  • Compatible with Blender 3.0 to 4.5
  • Documentation included
