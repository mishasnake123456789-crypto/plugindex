What Pro IO does

Pro IO from Hyper Brew automates the repetitive part of moving media into and out of After Effects and Premiere Pro. It is meant for pipelines where files arrive and leave in predictable patterns.

Width, height and FPS wildcards help name outputs, ARI footage can come in as an image sequence, and DPX sequences are supported on import. Export is compatible with After Codecs through Media Encoder, and dot files are ignored on import. It works with the 2026, 2025 and 2024 releases of both applications. Editors and artists handling large batches of footage will save steps.

Key features

  • Automated import and export
  • Width, Height and FPS wildcards
  • ARI files as image sequences and DPX import
  • After Codecs AME export compatibility
  • Ignores dot files on import
