What Stroke Material Display does

Stroke Material Display, by JohnNyquist, reveals which materials are assigned to the strokes you select in Grease Pencil, a quick way to check what you are working with. It is listed under Grease Pencil.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.
