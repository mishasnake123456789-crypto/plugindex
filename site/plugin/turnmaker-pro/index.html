Blender add-on that creates looping turntable rotations for objects or orbiting cameras with per-axis speed sliders.

What Turnmaker Pro does

Turnmaker Pro sets up turntables in Blender in one click, in two modes. Object mode places an Empty at the center of your selection, parents the objects to it and adds rotational drivers. Camera mode puts the Empty at the 3D Cursor, parents the active camera and adds a Track To constraint so it orbits and keeps the center in view.

Speeds for X, Y and Z are number fields on the Empty rather than keyframes, so the setup stays non-destructive, and a speed of 1.0 equals one turn per 100 frames. A TURNMAKER panel in the N-Panel includes a reset button. Product renders are an obvious use.

Key features

  • Object and camera turntable modes
  • Independent X, Y and Z speed controls
  • Driver-based, no keyframes or f-curves
  • Speed 1.0 equals one rotation per 100 frames
  • Reset button in the N-Panel
