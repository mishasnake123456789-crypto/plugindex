What MaterialHelper does

MaterialHelper is a Blender add-on by ACGGIT_LJ that keeps materials in a local asset library that works like a painter's palette. Hotkeys give fast access: a quick bar lets you pick from the library and assign with a click, while dragging with modifier keys moves a material from one object to another.

An intelligent material board collects materials and assigns them to selected objects automatically, and asset management tools sit inside the Asset Browser. It speeds up look development when you are constantly trying different materials on parts of a scene.

Key features

  • Quick bar for one-click material assignment
  • Drag-and-drop transfer between objects
  • Material board that collects and auto-assigns
  • Local material library as a palette
  • Management tools inside the Asset Browser
