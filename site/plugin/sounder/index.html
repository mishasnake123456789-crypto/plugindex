Blender Video Sequence Editor toolkit that synthesizes sound effects and music on the timeline without outside software.

What Sounder does

CONTOR's Sounder brings sound design into Blender's Video Sequence Editor. Effects and music are made right on the timeline, with no external program needed.

For effects there are 12 one-click presets, 6 synthesis modes and 7 waveforms, which include white and pink noise. Twenty-two instruments span keyboards, synths, orchestral and woodwind sounds, bells, chimes and bass, played as single notes or chords like Major, Minor, 7th and Diminished. A multi-note editor builds sequences. Video editors can score cuts quickly.

Key features

  • 12 SFX presets, 6 synthesis modes, 7 waveforms
  • 22 built-in instruments
  • Single notes or chords
  • Multi-note sequence editor
  • Works directly in the VSE
