What Gamemat Baker - Non-Blocking Pbr Baking For Game Engines does

GameMat Baker bakes texture maps from a Principled BSDF material for use in game engines. Its queue works through one map at a time on a timer, so Blender's interface keeps updating, and pressing Esc cancels while restoring the scene.

It bakes albedo, normal, roughness, metallic, opacity, AO and emission, and offers naming templates and channel packing for Unity URP, Unity HDRP, and Unreal or Godot ORM. Meshes without UVs are unwrapped automatically, tiling textures can be blended seamlessly, and resolution can follow texel density. Batch bake handles a whole selection and reports failures as warnings instead of stopping.

Key features

  • Non-blocking modal bake queue with Esc to cancel
  • Bakes albedo, normal, roughness, metallic, opacity, AO and emission
  • Channel packing presets for Unity URP, HDRP and Unreal/Godot ORM
  • Auto UV unwrap and seamless tiling option
  • Batch bake that continues past failed objects
