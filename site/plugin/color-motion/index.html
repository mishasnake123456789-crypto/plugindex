Blender add-on to extract colors from images, apply them to Color Ramps and animate color shifts.

What Color Motion does

Color Motion deals with the lifecycle of color in Blender: pulling it from an image, organizing it, applying it and animating it. It is split into four sections.

The Color Extractor lists all unique colors from an image, filtering out near duplicates by a threshold, and a Smart Extractor controls how many are taken. Colors can be added, inserted or replaced in Color Ramp nodes. Five automatic transition effects and six customizable modes shift colors over time, and Adobe-style color inputs are offered.

Key features

  • Extracts unique colors from images
  • Threshold filtering of similar colors
  • Add, Insert or Replace on Color Ramps
  • Five transition effects for animating color
  • Adobe-style color inputs
