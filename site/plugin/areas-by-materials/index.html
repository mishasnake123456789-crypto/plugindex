What Areas by Materials does

Areas by Materials, from Justinas-Dudenas, displays information about how much area each material occupies in the current selection, handy for estimating coverage. It is listed under Mesh, Object and User Interface.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Shows material areas for a selection
