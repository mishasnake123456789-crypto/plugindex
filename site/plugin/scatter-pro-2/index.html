What Scatter Pro 2 does

Scatter Pro 2 distributes instances across a target mesh, using its vertices, edges or faces, either across the whole object or a subset of vertices. It can help build landscapes or explosions.

Rotation and scale can be randomized on any axis per instance, with the option to keep the normal angle. Density is controlled with a minimum distance between objects, and coordinate filtering limits scattering on the X, Y and Z axes. It gives quick control for populating terrain with props, vegetation or debris.

Key features

  • Scatter on vertices, edges or faces
  • Random rotation and scale per axis
  • Minimum distance density control
  • Coordinate filtering on X, Y, Z
