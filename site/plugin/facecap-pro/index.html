Blender add-on that creates facial animation from ordinary video using AI and 52 ARKit blendshapes.

What Facecap Pro does

Facecap Pro turns video footage into facial animation without special hardware. MP4, MOV or AVI clips are enough, with no iPhone, depth sensor or mocap suit required.

AI extracts all 52 ARKit-compatible blendshapes, with eye tracking, head rotation and audio-to-viseme sync. Auto-Rig Pro integration transfers the animation to ARP characters in one click, driving face controllers, eye targets and head bones. Custom rigs can be used by mapping data to your shape keys, and built-in smoothing refines the motion.

Key features

  • Facial animation from MP4, MOV or AVI
  • 52 ARKit-compatible blendshapes
  • Eye tracking, head rotation and viseme sync
  • Auto-Rig Pro transfer and smoothing
