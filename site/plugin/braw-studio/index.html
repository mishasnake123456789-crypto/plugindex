What BRAW Studio does

BRAW Studio from Autokroma lets Adobe applications open .braw footage natively. It works in After Effects, Premiere Pro and Media Encoder, and uses the official Blackmagic colour science and SDK so the RAW parameters match those in DaVinci Resolve.

A desktop player opens, plays and grades files from Finder or Explorer, and sidecar settings carry across Resolve, Premiere Pro, After Effects and Media Encoder. There is a white balance picker, A/B comparison, 20 global presets, custom LUTs via Add LUT To Sidecar, and quick access to metadata. A trim tool cuts unused frames yet keeps the BRAW codec at identical quality. Editors and colourists get a smoother RAW workflow.

Key features

  • Native .braw import for After Effects, Premiere Pro and Media Encoder
  • Same RAW parameters as DaVinci Resolve
  • Desktop player for playback and grading
  • Sidecar settings, 20 global presets and LUT support
  • Trim tool that keeps BRAW quality
