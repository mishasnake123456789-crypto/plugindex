What Styler Durden DCTL does

Styler Durden DCTL offers a huge range of adjustable looks for DaVinci Resolve, all produced by combining just two tools: Simple Mix DCTL and Stone DCTL. Rather than shipping a pile of fixed LUTs, it lets colorists build and tweak a grade themselves.

It functions in many working color spaces, including DaVinci Wide Gamut, ACES, Arri LogC4, RED Log3G10 and Sony S-Log3. Highlights and shadows can be paired in complementary hues such as magenta with green or blue with yellow, and Lift, Gamma, Gain and Offset mixes each come with twelve presets. It runs on Resolve 16 through 21, and a demo video is offered.

Key features

  • Looks built from Simple Mix and Stone DCTLs
  • Works in DaVinci Wide Gamut, ACES, LogC4, Log3G10, S-Log3
  • Complementary highlight and shadow color pairings
  • 12 presets each for Lift, Gamma, Gain and Offset mixes
  • Compatible with Resolve 16 through 21
