Blender add-on that eases keyframes by adjusting F-curve handles with four sliders, in Lite and Pro editions.

What Graphpilot does

Graph Pilot simplifies animation easing in Blender. Instead of dragging F-curve handles by hand, four sliders shape how keyframes ease in and out, producing smooth motion quickly.

It comes in Lite and Pro editions as a GPL-licensed extension compatible with Blender 2.8 through 5.1, and documentation is available.

Key features

  • Ease keyframes with four sliders
  • Adjusts F-curve handles
  • Lite and Pro editions
  • Blender 2.8 through 5.1
  • Documentation available
