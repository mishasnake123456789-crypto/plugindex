What Icars does

iCars is aimed at artists who need vehicles and traffic in Blender. It sets up realistic traffic simulations quickly, offers modular car rigs, and includes crash physics.

Rendering works with Cycles and Eevee, documentation is included, and the tools are found in iCity's Free, Traffic, Animation and Vehicles packs.

Key features

  • Traffic simulation
  • Modular car rigs
  • Crash physics
  • Fast setup
  • Cycles and Eevee rendering
