Marks linked collections in Blender's Outliner so you do not edit them by accident.

What Mark Linked Collections does

Mark Linked Collections adds a visual cue in the Outliner for collections that come from elsewhere. Anything linked from another file or scene is flagged at a glance.

That prevents mistaken edits to shared assets, which is a common headache in library-based pipelines. Documentation is included and the add-on is released under the GPL license as a Dev Fund contributor product. Teams working with linked environments or props benefit most.

Key features

  • Visual marks in the Outliner
  • Identifies collections linked from other files or scenes
  • Prevents accidental edits
  • GPL license
