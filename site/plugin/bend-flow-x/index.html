What Bend Flow X does

Bend Flow X is an interactive deformation toolkit for Blender. It lets you curve and twist a mesh directly in the viewport using gizmos, and it needs no Empty objects and only a single hotkey to start.

Three modes are offered: Single Bend for simple curves, Multi-Segment Bend for complex forms, and Twist for rotation around an axis. Multi-Segment Bend splits the mesh into separate sections, every one with its own angle, direction and radius, and Shift+Scroll adds or removes segments. It suits hard surface modelers and anyone shaping pipes, cables or props.

Key features

  • Single Bend, Multi-Segment Bend and Twist modes
  • Per-segment angle, direction and radius
  • Shift+Scroll to add or remove segments
  • No Empties needed, one hotkey
