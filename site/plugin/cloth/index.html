What Cloth does

Cloth by Holapro supplies ready-made cloth animations for Blender, saving the time needed to set up and bake simulations. The fabrics move as if already simulated, so flags, curtains or draped material can be added to a scene right away.

It is delivered as a Blender extension, compatible with Blender 4.0 through 4.3, and released under the GPL. Documentation and a demo video are included. Artists on tight schedules or with modest hardware who want believable fabric motion without running cloth physics will find it convenient.

Key features

  • Pre-simulated cloth and fabric animations
  • Delivered as a Blender extension
  • Compatible with Blender 4.0 to 4.3
