What True-Vdb does

True-VDB is a library of ready-made volume simulations for Blender. Animated VDB assets for effects like fire, explosions, weather and magic can be browsed and appended to a scene with a click.

More than fifty sequences are available overall, with ten in the base version, and some loop seamlessly. To use one, browse to a VDB and press ADD. VFX artists and motion designers can add believable volumetric effects without running heavy simulations themselves.

Key features

  • 50+ VDB sequences, 10 in the base version
  • Fire, explosions, weather and magic
  • Some assets loop seamlessly
  • Browse and click ADD
