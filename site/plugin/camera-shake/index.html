Adds camera shake to Blender with realtime preview, four shake types, presets, curve falloff and keyframe baking.

What Camera Shake does

Camera Shake offers a controllable way to add motion to a camera. A realtime preview shows the effect in the viewport without baking.

Four types are available, Noise, Perlin, Sine and Decay, along with presets for Handheld, Explosion and Earthquake, or custom shakes of your own. Curve falloff shapes intensity over time, a follow-object option is supported, and the final result can be baked to keyframes. Useful for action shots and realism.

Key features

  • Realtime viewport preview
  • Noise, Perlin, Sine and Decay types
  • Handheld, Explosion and Earthquake presets
  • Curve falloff and follow-object support
  • Bake to keyframes
