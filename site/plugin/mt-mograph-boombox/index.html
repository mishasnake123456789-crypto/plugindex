What Mt. Mograph Boombox does

Boombox is a library of royalty-free sound effects joined to a visual editing environment, aimed at animators working in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve. It treats sound design as something you can see and rearrange rather than a linear list of clips.

There are 15,007 effects in 44 consistently mastered packs, with over 2,000 tags to search. The Constellation visualizer lets you preview, edit and balance a mix, while Audio Skin and Audio Ghosts link effects together. Users can build and exchange their own packs. Files are 48 kHz stereo WAV with tags and cue points, and the library syncs new content automatically.

Key features

  • 15,007 royalty-free effects in 44 packs
  • Constellation visualizer for previewing and mixing
  • Create, import and export your own packs
  • 48 kHz stereo WAV with tags and cue points
  • Auto-sync of new packs; works with Audition too
