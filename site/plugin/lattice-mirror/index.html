What Lattice Mirror does

Lattice Mirror makes symmetrical lattice edits easy in Blender. Toggle X, Y and Z, and as you move a point its counterparts move across the selected axes at the same time.

Mirroring can be started or stopped on the fly, middle points stay selectable and can slide along the mirror plane, and it works with Proportional Edit. Bulk operations cut overhead on high-resolution lattices. Controls are in the sidebar Item tab.

Key features

  • Mirror across X, Y and Z at once
  • Start or stop mirroring while editing
  • Bulk operations for dense lattices
  • Proportional Edit compatible
  • Panel under the Item tab
