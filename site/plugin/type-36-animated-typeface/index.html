What Type-36 - Animated Typeface does

Type-36 is a geometric typeface from Animography built on clean, rounded capsule shapes. Its animations are applied to the ExtraBold weight, where a glitch-style reveal brings text on screen.

The reveal can be customized: you can control color, distortion and secondary elements to make it subtle or aggressive. It supports After Effects 2020 through 2024 and fits tech, music and gaming titles. Because the animation lives on the heaviest weight, the reveal stays bold and readable even on busy footage.

Key features

  • Clean capsule-like geometric design
  • Animated ExtraBold weight
  • Customizable glitch-style reveal
  • Control over color, distortion and extras
  • Supports After Effects 2020 to 2024
