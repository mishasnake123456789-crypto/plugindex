What Flowcam (Blender Addon By S4C) does

Flowcam, from Shift4cube, collects camera animation tools into a studio-style panel split into lenses, motion control and framing. It is meant for artists setting up product turntables, orbits and path-driven shots.

One-click rigs attach a camera to a curve or build a 360-degree orbit. A Lens Kit switches between 24, 35, 50 and 85mm, a Smart Hero Light follows the subject, and a Dolly Zoom Generator produces the Vertigo effect. Other tools include live offsets, linear speed, follow tilt, auto-focus, quick aspect ratios and composition guides.

Key features

  • Camera rigs for curves and 360 orbits
  • Dolly Zoom generator
  • Lens presets and auto-follow hero light
  • Turntable, resolutions and composition guides
