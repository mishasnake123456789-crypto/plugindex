What Auto Eye Animator does

Auto Eye Animator produces eye animation based on real oculomotor behavior, such as fixational motion, microsaccades and blinks.

The workflow is simple: set the control bones and click Generate Animation. It works with Rigify, Auto-Rig Pro, Faceit, Human Generator or any custom rig with an eye master control bone, and automatic blinking adds further life. Characters feel alive without hand keyframing every flick. The tool suits character animators who want a believable baseline for eye movement before refining it.

Key features

  • Based on real eye movement behavior
  • One-click Generate Animation
  • Works with Rigify, Auto-Rig Pro and Faceit
  • Automatic blinking
  • No manual keyframes
