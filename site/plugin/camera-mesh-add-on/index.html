What Camera-Mesh-Add-On does

Camera-Mesh-Add-On creates cameras from the geometry of a mesh. Placement can be at vertices, edge centers, face centers or face corners, which suits architectural and film previs setups.

An angle filter by normal orientation and a selection-only mode limit where cameras spawn. Focal length and other settings can be customized, and controllers, animation and management tools are provided. Architects, filmmakers and 3D artists planning multiple viewpoints save manual placement. Once created, the cameras can be managed and animated from the same tool.

Key features

  • Cameras at vertices, edges, faces or corners
  • Angle filter by normal orientation
  • Selection-only mode
  • Customization, controllers and animation
