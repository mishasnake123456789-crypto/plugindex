What Procedural Stone Wall Generator does

Procedural Stone Wall Generator turns any rectangular mesh in Blender into a stone construction, whether a wall, road, pavement or garden. Each stone becomes a separate object, giving you full control while keeping polygon count in check.

Stone size, geometry, displacement, texture scale and colors are customizable, and randomness can be raised or lowered for regular or varied structures. Suitable materials are added to every stone automatically. Environment artists and architectural visualizers who need irregular stonework without placing blocks individually will find it a fast route.

Key features

  • Converts rectangular meshes to stone versions
  • Adjustable stone size, displacement and colors
  • Controllable randomness
  • Materials added to each stone
  • Stones as separate objects, polygon count controlled
