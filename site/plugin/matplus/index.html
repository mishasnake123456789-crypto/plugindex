What Matplus does

Matplus, made by BeFX Studios, puts layered texture painting inside the Blender scene and draws inspiration from Substance Painter and ArmorPaint.

Layers carry live effects, painting can mirror in 2D, and the baking step has been tuned. It works with Cycles and Eevee, includes documentation, and removes the need to leave Blender for texturing. Material and prop artists keep the whole workflow in one program. Keeping painting and baking in the scene avoids round trips to separate texturing software when a material needs a tweak.

Key features

  • Layered painting with live effects
  • 2D symmetry painting
  • Optimized texture baking
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
