Developer: Cuzmic

FreeBlender

Blender add-on bringing ZBrush-style canvas navigation to sculpting and painting, ideal for pen tablets.

What Sculpt Navigation does

Sculpt Navigation gives Blender the viewport behavior familiar from ZBrush and 3D-Coat. Clicking empty canvas orbits, Shift+click pans, and clicking the mesh sculpts or paints, so a pen tablet works without a middle mouse button.

Right-click gestures dolly with a vertical drag, roll with a horizontal drag and zoom with Shift. A dedicated roll keybinding defaults to Shift+Alt+LMB, brush size and strength show ring feedback, and double-clicking empty space frames selected objects. A safety border always triggers navigation, and keybindings and gestures are configurable.

Key features

  • Orbit and pan from empty canvas space
  • Right-click dolly, roll and zoom gestures
  • Brush size and strength ring feedback
  • Double-click to frame selection
  • Configurable keybindings and safety border
