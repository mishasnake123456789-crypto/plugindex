What RE:Vision Effects ReelSmart Motion Blur Regular does

ReelSmart Motion Blur Regular adds realistic motion blur in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve by following each pixel's travel across frames. It suits footage shot with a fast shutter as well as animation and 3D renders that arrive without blur.

When your 3D package outputs motion vectors, those can drive the blur directly. A Fine Tune control changes how much blur is applied, and existing blur can be removed. A 360 mode understands the connected sides, top and bottom of panoramic footage. It is GPU accelerated and falls back to CPU if a compatible GPU is missing.

Key features

  • Motion blur from automatic pixel tracking
  • Blur from 3D-supplied motion vectors
  • Fine Tune control for blur amount
  • Removal of existing blur
  • 360 mode; GPU acceleration with CPU fallback
