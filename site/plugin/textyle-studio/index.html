What Textyle Studio does

Textyle Studio is a title and text animation tool for After Effects from MotionDesk. It is built around presets, so animated titles can be applied from a panel instead of being constructed with animators and keyframes.

The library has more than 550 title animations in 8 categories and over 30 styles in 4 categories, with free updates for both. Full settings are available for animations and for styles, and in and out animations come with duration control. It suits editors and designers who produce lots of lower thirds, captions and title cards and want fast, consistent results.

Key features

  • 550+ title animations in 8 categories
  • 30+ title styles with free updates
  • Full settings for animations and styles
  • In and out animations with duration control
