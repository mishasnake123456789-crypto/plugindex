What Web3D Asset Compiler does

Web3D Asset Compiler, by Abdul-Rafay-Khalid, prepares scenes for the web by baking lightmaps, optimizing meshes and exporting for Three.js. It is tagged under Import-Export, 3D View and Material.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 5.1.0 or newer.
