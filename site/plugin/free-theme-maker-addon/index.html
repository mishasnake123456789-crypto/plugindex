What Free Theme Maker Addon does

Free Theme Maker lets you restyle Blender's interface through a simple panel. Colors, contrast and individual elements such as headers, backgrounds, buttons and text can all be tuned.

Changes preview in real time as you work, so there is little guessing about the final look. Finished themes can be saved for later use and shared with others. Anyone who wants a more comfortable or personal workspace, such as a darker palette or higher contrast, can build one without editing preference files.

Key features

  • Color scheme adjustments
  • Control of headers, backgrounds, buttons and text
  • Real-time previews
  • Save and share themes
