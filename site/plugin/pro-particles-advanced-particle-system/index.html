What Pro Particles - Advanced Particle System does

CG_Flow's Pro Particles is a GPU-based particle toolkit for Blender. One workflow covers big particle effects, granular matter, fluids, collisions, logic-driven behavior, procedural color, instancing, trails, plexus and mesh output.

Version 5 moves the whole simulation core onto GPU compute. The reworked granular solver gives stable piles, wet clumps, brittle breakup, sleeping, warm starting and persistent contacts, plus presets for soil, mud, clay, gravel, snow and sand. Scene objects also get collision response. It targets motion graphics and VFX artists.

Key features

  • Version 5 simulates entirely on the GPU
  • Rebuilt granular solver with material presets
  • Fluids, collisions and logic-driven behavior
  • Instancing, trails, plexus and mesh output
  • Procedural color
