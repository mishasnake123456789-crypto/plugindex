What 1-Click Product Render Pro does

1-Click Product Render Pro creates a complete product shot setup in Blender from a single button, with every parameter updating live in the viewport.

The camera auto-frames with an 85mm lens and depth of field, named PRS_Camera with a Track To constraint. Four lighting presets, Studio, Soft Box, Dramatic and Outdoor, offer energy and color control and a Light Mixer for the three lights. Background, render settings, animation, watermark and export are included.

Key features

  • Auto-framing 85mm camera with depth of field
  • Studio, Soft Box, Dramatic and Outdoor lighting
  • Light Mixer
  • Background, animation and watermark options
  • Render and export setup
