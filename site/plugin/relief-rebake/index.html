What Relief Rebake does

Relief Rebake by Agregarts takes a relief mesh with poor topology and regenerates it as a clean height field made of quads. It is useful when a sculpted or scanned relief needs tidier geometry. It is tagged under Mesh and Modeling.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
