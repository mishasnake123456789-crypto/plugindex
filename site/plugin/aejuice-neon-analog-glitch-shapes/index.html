What AEJuice Neon Analog Glitch Shapes does

This collection of glowing, glitching geometric elements with an analog feel is called AEJuice Neon Analog Glitch Shapes. There are 52 animations at 3840x2160 and 30 fps, suited to music videos, concerts and shows.
