What Quick Tricks does

Quick Tricks, by Bright Future 3D LLC, is a grab bag of time savers for Blender. It aims to cut the effort of tasks you repeat constantly while modeling and animating.

It lets you create and modify primitive mesh attributes, manage groups, rotate-snap meshes and animate directly in the viewport. Documentation and a demo video are provided. General Blender users who want fewer manual steps with primitives and simple animation are its audience.

Key features

  • Create and modify primitive mesh attributes
  • Group management
  • Rotate-snap meshes
  • Instant animation in the viewport
  • Documentation and demo video
