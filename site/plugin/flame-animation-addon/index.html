What Flame Animation Addon does

Flame Animation Addon puts fire effects in a Blender scene with very little setup: add a flame, press play, and the animation runs. It avoids the cost and complexity of a full fire simulation for stylized or background flames.

It renders under both EEVEE and Cycles, supports Blender 3.5 through 4.3, is distributed under the GPL and comes with documentation and a demo video. Artists creating torches, candles, campfires or magical effects can add flames quickly and move on with their scenes.

Key features

  • Add a flame and press play
  • Renders in EEVEE and Cycles
  • Blender 3.5 to 4.3
  • Documentation and demo video
