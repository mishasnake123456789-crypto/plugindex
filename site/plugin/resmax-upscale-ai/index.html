Blender add-on that runs Real-ESRGAN AI upscaling on images and sequences inside Blender, including anime models.

What Resmax - Upscale Ai does

Resmax brings Real-ESRGAN super-resolution into Blender, so you can upscale single images and full sequences without leaving the program. It can also lift texture quality.

Models include realesr-animevideov3 in x2, x3 and x4 variants for anime-style visuals, realesrgan-x4plus for general-purpose detail, and realesrgan-x4plus-anime for stylized content. No external tools are required, and it runs on Blender 3.0 and above. Good for rendering at lower resolution and enlarging afterward. Both stills and frame sequences can go through the same process.

Key features

  • Upscales images and sequences in Blender
  • Anime video models in x2, x3 and x4
  • General-purpose x4plus model
  • Anime-tailored x4plus model
  • Improves texture quality
