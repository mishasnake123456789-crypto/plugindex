What Text Scatter does

Text Scatter, made by Pablotrk, is a script for After Effects that breaks a text layer into individual words and shuffles them around. It is useful for collage-style titles, quote layouts and typographic backgrounds.

The resulting layers are organized with a single click. A red limit box shows the composition area in real time so you can see and control where words are allowed to land. It takes the manual work out of splitting, positioning and tidying large amounts of text.

Key features

  • Splits text into individual words
  • Randomizes word placement
  • One-click layer organization
  • Red limit box shows the composition area live
