What Aplus Outliner Supporter does

Aplus Outliner Supporter is a Blender add-on for handling big scenes and many objects. It arranges collection, object, light group and type-specific panels together, with direct renaming and red highlighting for the current selection.

A Scene and ViewLayer panel shows the active scene and view layer for quick switching, deleting or renaming, with the active one marked in red. The collection panel offers current-level and level-N display, sub-collection syncing and color handling. Artists with crowded outliners get a faster way to find, rename and organize things.

Key features

  • Scene and ViewLayer panel with quick switching
  • Collection panel with level display
  • Direct in-place renaming
  • Red highlighting of selected items
  • Light group and type-specific panels
