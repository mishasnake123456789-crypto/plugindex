What Easy Lip-Sync does

Easy Lip-Sync automates the tedious job of matching a character's mouth to recorded speech. It reads an audio file and creates the keyframes, working either with Grease Pencil layers or regular objects.

It accepts 16-bit WAV files in mono or stereo, and frame rate, thresholds and keyframe intervals can be tuned to suit the shot. The interface is aimed at animators of every level, and documentation and a demo video are provided. Useful for films, games and other 3D projects with dialogue.

Key features

  • Syncs mouth shapes to 16-bit WAV audio
  • Works with Grease Pencil layers or objects
  • Adjustable frame rate, thresholds and intervals
  • Documentation and demo video available
