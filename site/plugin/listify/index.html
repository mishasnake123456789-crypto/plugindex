What Listify does

Listify turns the contents of a Premiere Pro sequence into the paperwork that post-production needs. It can produce VFX sheets, ADR lists, music cue sheets, stock footage lists and other custom lists, so nobody has to type them out by hand.

Filters decide which clips make it into a list, and the result can be exported as CSV, PDF or Excel. For sheets that include pictures, you choose whether the thumbnail comes from the start, middle or end of each clip. It needs Premiere Pro 15.0 or later and is aimed at editors and assistants handling deliverables.

Key features

  • VFX sheets, ADR lists and music cue sheets
  • Stock video lists and custom lists
  • Export to CSV, PDF and Excel
  • Thumbnail from clip start, middle or end
  • Filter options for what gets listed
