What Mesh Pyramid Generator does

5 Minutes Blender's Mesh Pyramid Generator makes pyramid meshes from a few numbers: how many sides, how tall and how long.

Any polygon, regular or irregular, can form the base, with an Irregular Factor adjusting the lean of the apex. A Steps field turns the shape into a stepped pyramid or podium. All settings are in the operator box, and documentation covers installation. A quick answer for temples, roofs, monuments and abstract scenes.

Key features

  • Pyramid from sides, height and length
  • Any polygon base
  • Irregular Factor for skewness
  • Stepped pyramids and podiums
  • Settings in the operator box
