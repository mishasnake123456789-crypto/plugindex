What Scene MVR (.mvr) and GDTF (.gdtf) does

Scene MVR and GDTF, by NRGSille, supports My Virtual Rig (.mvr) and General Device Type Format (.gdtf) files in Blender, formats used for stage and lighting rigs. It is listed under Import-Export, Scene, Lighting, Tracking and Rigging.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
