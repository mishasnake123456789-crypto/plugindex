FreeBlender

Free add-on that adds a bloom effect in Eevee and Cycles with size, quality, threshold and color controls.

What Quick Bloom does

Quick Bloom brings a glow effect to both Eevee and Cycles. Bloom Size sets the spread, where 9 covers the entire screen, 8 half of it, 7 a quarter and so on.

Quality can be Low or Medium for testing and High for the final render, a light threshold sets the minimum brightness that blooms, and color is adjustable. An optional blur is controlled with include and exclude lists. It is a simple way to add glow without building compositor nodes.

Key features

  • Bloom in Eevee and Cycles
  • Size and quality settings
  • Light threshold and color
  • Optional blur with include/exclude lists
