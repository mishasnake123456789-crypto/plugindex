What X-Ray Select : Limited Sale does

X-Ray Select isolates X-ray display to chosen objects in Blender instead of making the entire scene see-through.

Shortcut keys toggle the effect on and off and can be reassigned. The behavior will feel familiar to artists moving from 3ds Max or Maya, where isolated X-ray on a model is common. It is helpful for modelers working on complex objects with internal parts. Documentation is included. That makes it practical to check the inside of one asset while the remainder of the scene stays solid and readable.
