Native After Effects effect that fits four perspective pins to a mask area in one click for corner-pin work.

What MaskPin does

MaskPin is a native After Effects effect for perspective pinning based on masks. Instead of fitting pins to the whole layer or composition, it snaps four pins to the area enclosed by your mask with a single click.

You can still adjust corners by hand using custom handles in the viewer, fit to the comp instead, or use a four-point mask mode. It suits screen replacement, billboard and device mockups, and sign or poster swaps within larger comps. It supports SmartFX, Multi-Frame Rendering, 8, 16 and 32 bpc, on Windows and macOS.

Key features

  • One-click Fit Pins To Mask
  • Fit Pins To Comp and 4-Point Mask modes
  • Manual corner handles in the viewer
  • SmartFX and Multi-Frame Rendering ready
  • 8, 16 and 32 bpc support
