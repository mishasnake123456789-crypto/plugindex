What Lightpainter does

LightPainter from ShaderError lets you paint lighting directly in Blender's viewport. Brush-based controls shape gobos, highlights and shadows, which the developer presents as a new way to design light.

It targets Cycles, covers Blender 3.6 through 5.1, installs as an extension, and ships with documentation under the GPL. Lighting artists and cinematographers who think visually can sketch illumination onto subjects rather than placing and tuning lamps one at a time.

Key features

  • Paint light with brushes
  • Gobos, highlights and shadows
  • Built for Cycles
  • Blender 3.6 through 5.1
