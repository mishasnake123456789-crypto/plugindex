Creates and manages Blender lights and emissive meshes from one panel, grouped into collections.

What Custom Lights does

Custom Lights combines quick light creation with a central management interface. You can add Point, Sun, Spot and Area lights (rectangle or ellipse) at the cursor, and they are grouped into collections automatically.

A Manage Lights panel lists lights and emissive meshes by collection with direct controls for each. It keeps lighting setups organized in scenes with many sources, which helps lighting and visualization artists adjust everything without hunting through the outliner.

Key features

  • Point, Sun, Spot and Area lights at the cursor
  • Automatic collection grouping
  • Manage Lights panel for lights and emissive meshes
  • Direct controls from one panel
