What Rigify Exporter does

Rigify Exporter, from Stellart, handles both rig preparation and export in a single workflow. It is for Blender users who need to move Rigify characters into game engines.

Targets are Unreal Engine, Unity and Godot, and exports typically take 10 to 30 seconds. It runs on Linux and Windows with Blender 4.5 through 5.1, shows an error if no Rigify rig is selected, and receives free updates from the developer.

Key features

  • Exports to Unreal, Unity and Godot
  • Rig preparation and export in one step
  • Typically 10 to 30 seconds
  • Blender 4.5 to 5.1
