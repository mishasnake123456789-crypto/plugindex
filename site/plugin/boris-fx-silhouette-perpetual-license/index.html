What Boris FX Silhouette Perpetual License does

Silhouette from Boris FX combines rotoscoping, paint and node-based compositing and works as a plugin in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro or as a standalone application. This listing is the perpetual licence.

Its node graph offers more than 400 VFX nodes, including Sapphire, Mocha, Particle Illusion and Continuum tools, alongside green screen keying, tracking, warping and morphing. Roto and paint tools include auto paint. Machine-learning helpers cover matting through Face, Depth Map, Mask, Matte Assist and Matte Refine models, and tracking through Head Track, Object Tracker and Point Track.
