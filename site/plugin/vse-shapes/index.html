What VSE Shapes does

VSE Shapes by Volko adds simple graphic elements to Blender's video sequencer, including rectangles, ellipses, arrows and drawing, handy for annotations and callouts in edits. It is listed under Sequencer.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Rectangles and ellipses
