What Drive Them All - Colors & Controls does

Drive Them All works with Blender's driver system to make linking properties less tedious. Properties, colors and controls are grouped into named Teams so related items stay together.

Right-click a property and choose "Catch this Wild Control", then apply the driver. Colors link in two clicks instead of wiring RGB and Alpha separately, and a Control Vector drives Vector3 values together. Riggers and shader artists who manage many custom controls can build dashboards of drivers more quickly.

Key features

  • Teams to group properties, colors and controls
  • Capture a property by right-click
  • Two-click color linking
  • Control Vector drives X, Y and Z together
  • Built on the driver system
