What AnyTex does

AnyTex writes out mathematical formulae as if drawn by hand, directly within After Effects and Premiere Pro. Formulae of any complexity are handled.

It currently runs on Windows only and supports After Effects and Premiere Pro from CC 2018 to the 2025 releases. A demo video is available. Educators, explainer creators and science channels animating equations can skip drawing each stroke manually.

Key features

  • Handwriting-style LaTeX formula animation
  • Handles formulae of any complexity
  • Works in After Effects and Premiere Pro
  • Windows only
  • CC 2018 through 2025
