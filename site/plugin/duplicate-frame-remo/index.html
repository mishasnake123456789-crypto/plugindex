What Duplicate Frame Remo does

Duplicate Frame Remo, made by Lloyd Alvarez, is a tool for After Effects aimed at cleaning up footage that contains repeated frames, a common problem with frame-rate conversions and stuttering clips. It finds the duplicates and takes them out.

A keyframable Region of Interest directs where detection happens. Afterwards the footage can be retimed back to its original length with After Effects Pixel Motion or Frame Mix, or you can choose to only set time-remapping keys at the good frames. Processing is about 50 percent faster than before, and it works with After Effects from CC 2015 through 2026.

Key features

  • Removes duplicate frames in footage
  • Keyframable Region of Interest
  • Optional retime with Pixel Motion or Frame Mix
  • Set time-remapping keys without retiming
  • Works with CC 2015 through 2026
