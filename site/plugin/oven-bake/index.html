What Oven Bake does

Oven Bake converts procedural textures into image textures in Blender, no matter how complex the material.

It supports source-to-target baking of textures, normals and ambient occlusion from a detailed model, plus node baking, UDIMs, channel packing and vertex color output. Game artists and exporters turn procedural looks into portable textures. Turning node-based looks into image files makes materials portable to other applications and engines, and it can also lighten scenes that carry heavy procedural networks.

Key features

  • Procedural to image texture baking
  • Source-to-target baking
  • UDIM support
  • Channel packing and vertex color output
