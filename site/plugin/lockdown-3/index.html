What Lockdown 3 does

Lockdown 3 tracks warping and planar surfaces inside After Effects. This free edition covers one- and two-point tracking and gives the tracked mesh real 3D depth, so it can be relit and have objects attached in three dimensions.

Tracking data can leave After Effects for other 3D software, as a C4D file for Cinema 4D or through Alembic for Blender and similar apps. For cleanup, you can paint in a stabilized composition, and Paint Link sends frames to Photoshop's Generative Fill for moving footage. Composite Brush is also included.

Key features

  • Warp and planar surface tracking
  • 1 and 2 point tracking in the free version
  • 3D mesh depth with relighting
  • Export to Cinema 4D (C4D) or Alembic for Blender
  • Paint Link and Composite Brush for cleanup
