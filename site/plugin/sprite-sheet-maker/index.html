What Sprite Sheet Maker does

Sprite Sheet Maker is a Blender addon by manas-R.-Makde that renders 3D animations out as 2D sprite sheets, delivered as one sheet, strips or separate images, and single sprites can also be made.

An inbuilt auto camera supports perspective and orthographic views, or you can supply your own camera. A toggleable pixelation tool gives a retro look, and per-row labeling can include frame counts. It helps 2D game developers who want to model and animate in 3D but ship flat sprites.

Key features

  • Combined sheet, strips or individual images
  • Auto camera, perspective or orthographic
  • Toggleable pixelation
  • Per-row labeling
  • Custom camera option
