What Ignite - Fire Dynamics does

Ignite adds a new kind of physics simulation to Blender: combustion. Start a small fire on an object and watch it spread, consume the material and leave charred surfaces behind as heat diffuses through the mesh.

Parameters control burning behavior, from fast, fuse-like flames to wood fires that slow down as insulating char builds up. Dynamic materials change through the stages of burning, so the object visibly transforms. It is a good fit for artists creating scorched props, burning fuses or environmental destruction.

Key features

  • Combustion with heat diffusion and char formation
  • Adjustable burning characteristics
  • Dynamic materials that change as they burn
  • Fire that spreads across an object
