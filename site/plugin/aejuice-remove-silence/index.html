What AEJuice Remove Silence does

AEJuice Remove Silence is a script for After Effects that trims out quiet stretches in audio or video, which makes it useful for tightening interviews and podcasts. You set the volume level that counts as silence.

Separate left and right offsets control where the layer is cut, for example leaving a five-frame buffer on each side so speech is not clipped. It integrates with kBar and supports After Effects CC 2015 and CC 2017 through CC 2022 and newer.

Key features

  • Removes silent sections below a threshold
  • Adjustable silence level
  • Separate left and right trim offsets
  • kBar integration
  • Compatible with CC 2015 and CC 2017+
