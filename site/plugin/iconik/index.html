What iconik does

The iconik panel lets editors work with their organization's media library from inside After Effects and Premiere Pro. Rather than switching to a web interface, you can look up assets, browse collections and view clips right in the Adobe workspace.

The connection runs both ways. Work in progress can be posted back to iconik so colleagues see the latest state, previews can be shared for review and approval, and finished deliverables can be sent there for long-term storage. It is meant for teams that already manage their footage and files through an iconik account.

Key features

  • Search and browse iconik collections in the panel
  • View assets without leaving Creative Cloud
  • Post work in progress back to iconik
  • Share previews for review and approval
  • Send final deliverables to storage
