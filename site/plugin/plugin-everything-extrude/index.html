What Plugin Everything Extrude does

Plugin Everything Extrude turns flat text, shapes and masks in After Effects into extruded geometry with one click. Three modes are offered: directional, vanishing, and a full 3D camera mode that works with After Effects cameras.

Shading can be solid or gradient, using a gradient color wheel and a radial editor with up to 32 colors. Inner extrusion, pivot anchor control and separate front and back depth give fine control. It handles overlapping paths, merge paths and mask modes, works with text animators, and is GPU accelerated, 32bpc, MFR capable and native on Apple silicon.

Key features

  • Directional, Vanishing and 3D Camera extrusion
  • Solid or gradient shading with up to 32 colors
  • Inner extrusion with pivot and front/back depth
  • Works with text animators and merge paths
  • GPU accelerated, 32bpc and MFR capable
