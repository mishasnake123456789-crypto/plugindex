What Timelapse Recorder does

Timelapse Recorder documents your working process. It takes screenshots of your session at set intervals and builds a timelapse video without leaving Blender, all from a panel in the 3D View sidebar.

You start and stop recording with a click, set the interval between 0.5 and 5 seconds, and adjust screenshot resolution as a percentage. Recording pauses automatically when you are inactive and resumes when you return, with a custom timeout. It is ideal for tutorial makers and social posts.

Key features

  • Single-click start and stop
  • 0.5 to 5 second interval
  • Auto pause on inactivity
  • Resolution percentage control
