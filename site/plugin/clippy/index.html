A Blender add-on offering adjustable clipping presets for the viewport and the camera.

What Clippy does

Clippy tackles near and far clipping in Blender with a set of presets. It supplies tighter clipping values that can be customized, which helps avoid depth artifacts and clipped geometry when working on very small or very large objects.

The presets can be applied in the 3D view and in the camera properties, and are configurable from the user preferences. It works with Blender 3.6 through 4.4, is licensed under the GPL and installs through Edit, Preferences, Add-ons, or by dragging in the zip since Blender 4.2. Modelers and lookdev artists will switch clip ranges faster.

Key features

  • Clipping presets for viewport and camera
  • Adjustable, customizable values
  • Accessible in the 3D view and camera properties
  • Blender 3.6 to 4.4
