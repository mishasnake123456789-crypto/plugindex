What BOOM Library World War II Firearms does

BOOM Library World War II Firearms covers 25 well-known weapons from all sides of the conflict, among them the MG42 and M1 Garand. It ships as WAV files for After Effects, Premiere Pro and other tools.

Each gun has ten shots, in single, burst or full-auto depending on the weapon, plus reloads, magazine changes, pickups, cocking, dryfire and bipod sounds. A designed pack in Mechanical, Modern and Punch styles supports drag-and-drop use. Recording took place outdoors using AB, XY, ORTF, ambisonic and ORTF 3D setups. The bundle holds 9,178 sounds in 24-bit, 96 kHz.

  • 25 WWII firearms including MG42 and M1 Garand
  • Ten shots per gun, single to full-auto
  • Reloads, magazine changes, cocking and dryfire
