Repositions anchor points and layers relative to precomps, masks, mattes and shapes in After Effects.

What Precomp Anchor Repo does

Precomp Anchor Repo aligns anchors and positions to a layer, composition or parent area. It works out that area by examining precomps, masks, track mattes, expressions and more, which a built-in alignment cannot do.

Corner and Vertex modes decide how bounds are calculated. Track mattes are measured from vertices and edges rather than alpha, and parametric shapes plus shape contents like strokes, trim paths and skew are supported, with a few limits on roundness and inner radius. 3D layers are skipped unless forced in Settings.

Key features

  • Corner and Vertex calculation modes
  • Detects precomp, mask, matte and expression areas
  • Track mattes measured from vertices and edges
  • Supports parametric shapes and shape contents
  • Shortcuts for Use Parent, Slide and Ignore Precomp
