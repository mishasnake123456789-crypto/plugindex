What Smart Align (Blender Add-On) does

Smart Align brings precision alignment to Edit Mode. A guided source-to-target workflow lines up faces, edges, vertices or whole geometry islands against one another.

With Fast Align you pick two elements and they align immediately, dragging the connected geometry along if needed, while the step-by-step Align accepts partial selections. Fine-tune controls allow post-alignment adjustment. Hard-surface and architectural modelers assembling parts will save manual snapping. The fine-tune stage means small offsets can be corrected without undoing the whole alignment.

Key features

  • Source-to-target alignment
  • Fast Align with two elements
  • Step-by-step Align for partial selections
  • Post-alignment fine-tuning
