What Nom Nom Vox - Stereo Audio-Driven Lipsync & Sound Reactive Animation does

Nom Nom Vox generates lipsync and sound-reactive animation from audio. It works from a mixed stereo WAV with left and right panning, together with separated vocal and background-music files.

A single stereo track can drive automatic, sound-reactive lipsync and motion, and shader-driven animation can respond to the audio too. Animators and music-video creators can get mouth movement and reactive visuals without hand-keying every syllable. Highlights cover lipsync from a stereo audio track and sound-reactive motion and shaders.

Key features

  • Lipsync from a stereo audio track
  • Sound-reactive motion and shaders
  • Uses vocal and music tracks plus mixed WAV
  • Left and right panning support
