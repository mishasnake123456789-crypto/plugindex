What KEYPH does

KEYPH is a Blender add-on that turns the A to Z keys into a number-pad style input. It is meant for laptops and compact keyboards that lack a dedicated numeric keypad, where Blender's numpad shortcuts are otherwise hard to reach.

The add-on is listed under the Add Curve category, though the description is limited. Users of small keyboards who rely on numpad view shortcuts and similar commands are the clear audience.

Key features

  • Letter keys act as a number pad
  • Helps on keyboards without a numpad
  • Unicode-based key mapping
