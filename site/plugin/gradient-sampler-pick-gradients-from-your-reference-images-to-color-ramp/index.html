What Gradient Sampler - Pick Gradients From Your Reference Images To Color Ramp does

Gradient Sampler lets you take colors straight from a reference picture and place them in a Blender color ramp. You simply draw across the image where the colors you want appear.

The sampled colors become the ramp's stops, which is a quick way to build palettes that match a reference. It works with Blender 2.8 through 4.4, is connected to the Cycles render engine, and is distributed under the GPL license with documentation included. Artists who texture or shade from concept art will save on eyedropping each color.

Key features

  • Sample colors by drawing over a reference
  • Assigns picked colors to a color ramp
  • Builds palettes from references
  • Works with Blender 2.8 to 4.4
