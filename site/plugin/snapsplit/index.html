Blender add-on for splitting models and generating snap-fit connectors for 3D printing.

What Snapsplit does

SnapSplit automates cutting, splitting and slicing models for 3D printing, and adds glue-free connectors at the seams. It uses robust booleans and adaptive tolerances.

Split along a global axis into a set number of parts with preview, then choose cylindrical pin, rectangular tenon, snap-pin or snap-tenon connectors. Profiles for PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, TPU and SLA derive tolerances, connectors can be arranged in a seam line, grid or individually, and friction and insertion depth can be adjusted.

Key features

  • Planar split into multiple parts
  • Four connector types
  • Printer material tolerance profiles
  • Grid, seam line and individual connector layouts
