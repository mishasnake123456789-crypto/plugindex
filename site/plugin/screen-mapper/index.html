What Screen Mapper does

Screen Mapper is James Ronan's tool for After Effects aimed at arranging content across several screens. It helps motion designers prepare multi-screen display templates and bento-style grids quickly instead of laying out panels by hand.

Once a layout is built, it can be animated and exported. The tool runs on After Effects from CC 2017 through the 2025 release. It is a good fit for product showcases, app promos and presentation pieces where several device or card frames appear together.

Key features

  • Quick multi-screen template setup
  • Bento grid layout creation
  • Animate and export layouts
  • Works with After Effects CC 2017 to 2025
