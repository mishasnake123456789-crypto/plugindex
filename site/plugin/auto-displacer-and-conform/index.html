What Auto Displacer And Conform does

Nucleonsoft's Auto Displacer And Conform changes detail on garments, accessories, weapons and similar models while leaving the original mesh intact, on both curved and flat surfaces.

It takes two steps: generate an ornament, then conform it to the target. It runs across Blender-Internal, Cycles and Eevee and is Blender Extension compatible. Costume and prop artists can add engraving and ornament without hand-modeling it. Because the base model is untouched, you can remove or swap the added detail at any time.

Key features

  • Two-step ornament generation and conforming
  • Original mesh preserved
  • Works on curved and flat surfaces
  • Blender-Internal, Cycles and Eevee
  • Blender Extension compatible
