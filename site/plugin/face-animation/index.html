Pre-configured face animation add-on for Blender character heads that works with Faceit.

What Face Animation does

Face Animation gives character heads ready-to-use facial animation. It arrives configured after installation, with an editable head you can adjust.

Animations can be layered on one another with customized naming, a head is added to your character for setup, and it works with the Faceit add-on for easy retargeting. Documentation is included. Support begins at Blender 2.93. Among its capabilities are pre-configured and ready to use, editable head and combinable face animations.

Key features

  • Pre-configured and ready to use
  • Editable head
  • Combinable face animations
  • Faceit retargeting compatibility
