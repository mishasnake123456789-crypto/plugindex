What Particles-X does

Particles-X streamlines particle creation in Blender, mainly for sci-fi environments. Frequently used settings, effect presets and prebuilt particle objects sit in a few panels.

Four panels cover creation, settings and presets. Tabs named Custom and Preset support tweaking your own particles or choosing effects such as plexus and trail, and particle objects like Mirror and Glow can be assigned. Motion designers can set up effects quickly. Collecting the most-used settings into a few panels shortens the usual hunt through particle properties, and presets give a starting point that can be refined by eye.

Key features

  • Four panels for creation, settings and presets
  • Custom and Preset tabs
  • Plexus and trail effects
  • Assignable particle objects
