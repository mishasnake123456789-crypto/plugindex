What Hair Lab (Now 30% Off) does

Hair Lab, from NENGHUO Studio, creates hair in Blender by drawing curves. Its Hair Painter generates strands from two Bezier curves, with frizz and gradients, which suits eyebrows, lashes, beards, braids and hairlines.

Over 25 hair nodes adjust length, curl, waves, clumping, child hair and collisions, and density, root thickness and growth angle can be edited non-destructively in real time. Special nodes cover a fur generator, mesh-to-hair conversion, a physics proxy for wind and gravity, one-click hair cards and surface deform. It is built on geometry nodes and works with Cycles and Eevee.

Key features

  • Hair Painter builds strands from two Bezier curves
  • 25+ nodes for style, clumping and collisions
  • Fur generator and mesh-to-hair conversion
  • Physics proxy and hair card maker
  • Image or texture projection onto hair
