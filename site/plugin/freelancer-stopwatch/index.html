What Freelancer Stopwatch does

Freelancer Stopwatch measures how long you work on 3D projects without leaving Blender. It records sessions, computes earnings, and produces data you can hand to a client.

A history keeps detailed session records with custom titles, notes and time-lapse review. Total time and earnings are calculated from your hourly rate and can be shown in the header. Reports export as CSV for Google Sheets, Excel and similar programs.

Key features

  • In-Blender time tracking per project
  • Session history with notes
  • Earnings calculated from hourly rate
  • CSV export for invoicing
