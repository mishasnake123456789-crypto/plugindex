What Coresee does

Coresee from Like Minded Labs turns Premiere Pro and After Effects projects into live virtual review sessions. Instead of exporting a file and waiting for notes, editors stream their work to collaborators as it plays.

It provides live video and audio streaming, frame-accurate playback and secure sessions for team collaboration. It works with Premiere Pro and After Effects 24.0 and up, including the 2026 releases. Teams that review cuts remotely can discuss changes in real time with everyone seeing the same frame.
