What Parallel Animated Typeface does

Parallel Animated Typeface, from WE ARE THERE, packages an animated font together with its own interface. Instead of animating letters by hand, you build and customize the type through an integrated dockable panel inside After Effects.

Compatibility is very broad, from CS3 through 2026, including CS6 and the CC releases, so it works on older workstations as well as current ones. A demo video is available. Designers making kinetic titles and typographic sequences are the intended audience.
