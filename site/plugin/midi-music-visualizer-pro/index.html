Turns MIDI and MP3 files into animated 3D scenes in Blender, offering several layouts and movement styles.

What Midi Music Visualizer Pro does

MIDI Music Visualizer Pro builds a complete animated environment from a music file. Geometry, materials and keyframes are generated in one click.

Layouts are Side-Scroller, Piano Roll, Waterfall and a Piano Mode that gives the main channel separate left and right hands. Six movement styles, such as Bouncing Arcs, Roll & Glide, Meteor Drop, Piston Strike and Laser Beam, shape the motion, and Geometry Nodes effects are added on top. Music video makers and streamers get visuals fast.

Key features

  • MIDI and MP3 conversion
  • Four layouts including Piano Roll and Waterfall
  • Six movement dynamics
  • Geometry Node effects
