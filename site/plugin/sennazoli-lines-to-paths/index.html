What SenNazoli Lines to Paths does

SenNazoli Lines to Paths is a tool from Terrible Junk Show for turning hand-drawn lines into editable paths in After Effects. Rather than tracing a sketch point by point, you let the tool create the path from the drawn stroke.

It works with After Effects 2025 and 2026. The first release, version 1.0, came out on December 1, 2025, and a demo video is available. It will appeal to animators who sketch loosely and need clean path data for further animation.
