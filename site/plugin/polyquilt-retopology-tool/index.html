What PolyQuilt Retopology Tool does

PolyQuilt Retopology Tool, published by Dan-Gry, is a retopology add-on for Blender, listed under Modeling. Retopology is the process of rebuilding a clean, animation-friendly mesh over a denser or scanned model.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.
