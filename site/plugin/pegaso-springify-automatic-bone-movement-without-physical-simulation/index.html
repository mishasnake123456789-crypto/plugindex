What Pegaso Springify (Automatic Bone Movement Without Physical Simulation) does

Pegaso Springify gives wings, tails, hair, cloth and accessories automatic secondary motion using spring bones. It responds immediately in the viewport, with no baking and no complex physics simulation.

Damping, stiffness and influence are tuned per bone, from soft and floppy to rigid and snappy, with rotation-only or position-plus-rotation options. Colliders are supported. It saves animators from hand-animating follow-through. Day to day, that covers real-time spring bones, per-bone damping, stiffness, influence and rotation or position plus rotation.

Key features

  • Real-time spring bones
  • Per-bone damping, stiffness, influence
  • Rotation or position plus rotation
  • Collider support
