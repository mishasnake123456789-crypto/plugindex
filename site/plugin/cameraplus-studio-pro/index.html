What Cameraplus_Studio_Pro does

CameraPlus Studio PRO collects camera and lighting setups for Blender into one add-on. It offers cinematic presets, studio light arrangements, HDRI control and target tracking, plus PRO animation tools.

Version 1.1.4 refines how presets, the PRO layout, lighting and animation behave. A Camera Distance slider adjusts the active rig live, rescaling existing animation keyframes, and applying a new preset replaces the previous CameraPlus rig rather than leaving old cameras around. A Use Selected workflow assigns a chosen object as the target.

Key features

  • Cinematic camera presets
  • Studio lighting and HDRI controls
  • Target tracking
  • Camera Distance slider that rescales keyframes
  • New presets replace the previous rig
