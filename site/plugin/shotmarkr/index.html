Converts shot marks embedded by Sony cameras such as the FX3 into Premiere Pro clip markers with one click.

What ShotMarkr does

ShotMarkr reads the shot mark metadata that Sony cameras record in-camera and turns it into markers on the matching clips in Premiere Pro. Operators flag good takes on set, and this plugin brings those flags into the edit.

Select your imported footage, click the button, and every shot mark becomes a marker. It works with the Sony FX3 and other Sony models, and requires Premiere Pro 26.0 or later. Editors who cut Sony footage can skip hunting through takes for the moments the camera crew marked as important.

Key features

  • Reads in-camera Sony shot mark metadata
  • Works with the FX3 and other Sony models
  • Creates a marker per shot mark on the clip
  • One button on selected footage
  • Needs Premiere Pro 26.0 or later
