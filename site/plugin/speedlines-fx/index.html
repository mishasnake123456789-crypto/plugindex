What Speedlines Fx does

Speedlines FX adds action lines directly to your 3D scene with no compositing or green screens. It is built on Geometry Nodes, so every parameter is customizable and animates cleanly on the timeline.

Two modes are available: radial lines radiating from a focal point, and linear horizontal strips sweeping across the camera frame. Motion designers and stylized animators can use it for impact and speed effects. In practice that means radial and linear speedline modes and geometry Nodes driven.

Key features

  • Radial and linear speedline modes
  • Geometry Nodes driven
  • Parameters animate on the timeline
  • No compositing needed
