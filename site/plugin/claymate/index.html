Blender add-on that gives plain clay renders extra surface detail from bump, normal or displacement maps you supply.

What Claymate does

Clay renders show off form, but a perfectly smooth surface can look flat. Claymate from Extra 3D adds fine detail back by applying bump, normal and displacement maps that you bring yourself.

It functions under both Cycles and Eevee, making it practical for sculpt presentations, model showcases and portfolio turntables where you want that matte look without full texturing. The listing mentions support for Blender 4.0 through 4.4, GPL distribution and a demo video you can watch first.

Key features

  • Uses your own bump, normal and displacement maps
  • Works in Cycles and Eevee
  • Keeps the clay render look
  • Compatible with Blender 4.0 to 4.4
  • GPL licensed
