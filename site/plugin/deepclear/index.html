What DeepClear does

DeepClear, from vimager, is an AI enhancement plugin that cleans up soft, noisy or flat-looking video. It combines deblurring, noise reduction and contrast enhancement in a single tool for After Effects and Premiere Pro.

Deblurring tackles out-of-focus shots and motion blur on general images, portraits, text and cartoons while avoiding halos. AI denoising removes low-light and high-ISO grain without softening detail, and contrast is improved through histogram equalization that keeps a natural appearance. It uses GPU acceleration on Mac and Windows.

Key features

  • Deblurs out-of-focus and motion-blurred footage
  • AI denoising that keeps detail
  • Removes low-light and high-ISO grain
  • Histogram-based contrast enhancement
  • GPU accelerated on Mac and Windows
