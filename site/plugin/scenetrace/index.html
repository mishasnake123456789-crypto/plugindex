Blender add-on for running performance regression tests on scenes.

What SceneTrace does

SceneTrace, by Dustin-Hanke, brings regression testing to Blender scenes so you can notice when a change makes a scene slower. It is categorised under Workflow.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
