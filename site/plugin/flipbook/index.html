What Flipbook does

Flipbook overhauls how Blender handles viewport rendering. One click renders the viewport with Eevee so animation plays back in realtime.

It rebuilds the viewport-render system, installs as an add-on compatible with the extension system, and includes documentation. Animators who need to check timing without a full render can review motion faster. It is aimed at checking motion and timing smoothly, without waiting for a complete final render. That makes quick checks of timing far less tedious.

Key features

  • One-click viewport render
  • Realtime animation playback
  • Uses Eevee
  • Rebuilt viewport-render system
