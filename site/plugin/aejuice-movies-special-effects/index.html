What AEJuice Movies Special Effects does

AEJuice Movies Special Effects offers ready-made animated overlays in a movie-effects style. It includes 431 drag-and-drop clips across five categories, all at 3840x2160 and available horizontal, vertical and square.

Each comes as MOV ProRes 4444 with RGB and alpha, plus a black-background version for blending, at 23.98 or 25 fps. A standalone Pack Manager runs apart from any video editor, with an interface in nine languages, among them English, Japanese, Spanish, Korean and Traditional Chinese. After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve users can drop in effects instead of building them.

Key features

  • 431 animations in five categories
  • 4K in horizontal, vertical and square
  • ProRes 4444 with alpha and black-background versions
  • 23.98 and 25 fps
  • Standalone Pack Manager in nine languages
