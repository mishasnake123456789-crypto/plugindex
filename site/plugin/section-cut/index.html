What Section Cut does

Section Cut, by Agregarts, lets you place a cutting plane in the scene and produces the resulting section as actual geometry. It is listed under Mesh and Modeling.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Place a plane to cut a section
  • Section output as real geometry
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later
