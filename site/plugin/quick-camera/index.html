A Blender add-on for creating and managing cameras with thumbnail previews and photo, animation and panorama modes.

What Quick Camera does

Quick Camera makes handling many cameras in Blender easier. Cameras are created, selected and managed through a panel with thumbnail previews, so you can pick the correct view visually.

Lens parameters adjust quickly, with the camera viewport available through Shift+`. Animation mode builds animated cameras from an existing one, Panorama mode renders panoramas quickly through Cycles, and render management lets you start renders and open the output folder with a click. Photographers turned 3D artists and archviz specialists will appreciate the speed.

Key features

  • Camera thumbnails for previewing
  • Quick lens parameter adjustment
  • Animation mode from an existing camera
  • Fast panorama rendering in Cycles
  • Render management and output folder shortcut
