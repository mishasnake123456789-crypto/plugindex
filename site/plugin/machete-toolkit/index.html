What Machete Toolkit does

Machete Toolkit collects smart geometry tools for Blender modelers into one package. Tools can be reached through a pie menu, sidebar panels, a workspace tool or operator search, whichever suits your habits.

Edge Flow Correction adjusts edges to follow surrounding geometry, Circularize turns vertices into circles while keeping their 3D orientation, and an Edge to Curve converter offers live bevel control. Straightening edge loops and selection management are also included. Useful for cleaning topology and hard-surface work.

Key features

  • Edge Flow Correction and Circularize
  • Edge to Curve with live bevel control
  • Straighten edge loops
  • Pie menu, panels and searchable operators
