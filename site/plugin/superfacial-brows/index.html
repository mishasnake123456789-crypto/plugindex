Blender add-on that grows customizable procedural eyebrows on any face mesh and bakes them to game-ready hair cards.

What Superfacial Brows does

himenoyui's Superfacial Brows creates organic eyebrows on any face in Blender with a click. A tidy side panel offers factory and user presets, with save and load.

Shape is controlled by free vertex editing in Edit Mode or by Mood and Style choices, including Arc and Tail. Options for flow, hair, baby hair and makeup-level detail refine the look, and brows can be baked into optimized haircard assets for real-time engines. Character artists skip placing strips manually.

Key features

  • One-click eyebrow generation
  • Factory and user presets
  • Arc and Tail shape control
  • Natural flow and baby hair detail
  • Bake to game-ready haircards
