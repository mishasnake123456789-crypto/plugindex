What Looper Pro does

Looper Pro, by Leyero, repeats keyframed motion so short animations can run for as long as needed. Three looping types are offered: cycle, pingpong and offset, covering common expression-style behaviors.

It works in Premiere Pro 2021 through 2026, a free trial is available, and there is a demo video. Editors who animate in the timeline will appreciate not having to copy keyframes repeatedly. It is a good fit for repeating spinners, pulses and other short motion without copying keyframes by hand.

Key features

  • Cycle, pingpong and offset looping
  • Loops animations inside Premiere Pro
  • Premiere Pro 2021 to 2026
  • Free trial and demo video
