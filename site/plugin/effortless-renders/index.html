What Effortless Renders does

Effortless Renders makes presenting a model quick. A tab in the 3D Viewport produces a turntable, render, wireframe view or thumbnail with one click.

Preset scenes are included and you can add your own, and an external asset library can be customized within Blender. Render modes range from high-quality finishes to fast previews. Asset creators and portfolio builders who regularly show models to clients or marketplaces will save the repetitive scene setup.

Key features

  • One-click turntables, renders, wireframes, thumbnails
  • Preset scenes with custom additions
  • Customizable external asset library
  • Quality and preview render modes
