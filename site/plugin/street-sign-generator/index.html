What Street Sign Generator does

Street Sign Generator creates street signs inside Blender with very little effort. It is intended for artists who need signage for city scenes and want it rigged from the start.

In a simple panel you enter a street name or ask for a random one, then generate the sign in a few clicks. The result is a fully rigged sign. Documentation and a demo video are included.
