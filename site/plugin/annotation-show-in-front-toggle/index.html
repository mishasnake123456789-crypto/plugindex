What Annotation Show In Front Toggle does

Annotation Show In Front Toggle gives annotation layers in Blender a quick way to turn off the in-front, X-ray display. By default, notes drawn on top can obscure the model, and this add-on adds an on/off button to each layer in the upper annotation panel.

It works with Blender 2.8 through 2.93, is distributed under a royalty-free license and includes documentation and a demo video. Artists who annotate models for review, planning or teaching will find it makes their notes easier to show or hide without changing anything else.

Key features

  • X-ray on/off button per annotation layer
  • Controls in the upper annotation panel
  • Blender 2.8 to 2.93
  • Documentation and demo video
