Blender add-on that puts a Lock Camera to View toggle in the 3D View header with a status icon.

What Easy Camera Lock does

Easy Camera Lock moves the Lock Camera to View option to the 3D View header so one click toggles it.

An icon shows the current state, highlighted when locked. It works with Blender 2.80 and later, with support listed from 2.93 to 4.0, and is GPL licensed. Artists, animators, architects, educators and students who frequently adjust the camera while in camera view will appreciate the quick access. It saves a trip through the sidebar each time you want to adjust the view.

Key features

  • One-click Lock Camera to View toggle
  • Header icon shows status
  • Highlighted when locked
  • Blender 2.80 and later
  • GPL license
