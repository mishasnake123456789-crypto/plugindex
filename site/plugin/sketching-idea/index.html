What Sketching Idea does

Sketching Idea supports the early stage of sculpting by letting you draw rough concepts before deciding which to build. It opens with the Shift + Ctrl + A shortcut and was built for Blender 2.8.

The sketch strokes disappear from view depending on your camera angle, so several ideas can coexist around a model without cluttering the viewport. Once one direction looks best, you sculpt it. The add-on is released under the GPL license and includes documentation. It suits sculptors who like to explore a few variations first.

Key features

  • Quick idea sketching while sculpting
  • Strokes fade by camera angle
  • Shift + Ctrl + A shortcut
  • Documentation included
