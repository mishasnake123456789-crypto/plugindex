What Cfr V1 Camera Fps Render Addon does

CFR v1 is a lightweight Blender add-on that keeps camera and render controls in the viewport. It helps animators, game developers and architectural visualizers who change cameras and render often.

A camera switcher moves between cameras with a click, FPS mode lets you walk through a scene in first person, and quick render tools start renders without menu hunting. Documentation is included and the license is royalty-free.
