What Math To Mesh (Mtm) does

EBTRR's Math To Mesh (MTM) creates geometry from mathematical expressions in Blender. Functions take one or two inputs, x and y, written in Python-style notation with trig functions.

The Custom Functions Generator produces a triangular mesh from your formula, with settings for name, collection, axis ranges and per-axis vertex counts. Expressions may use arithmetic, trig and hyperbolic functions, the constants e and pi, and square roots, and a Spherical Functions Generator works in degrees. Math teachers and generative artists can visualize surfaces.

Key features

  • Mesh from typed math functions
  • Configurable axis ranges and resolution
  • Trigonometric and hyperbolic functions
  • Constants like e and pi
  • Spherical functions generator
