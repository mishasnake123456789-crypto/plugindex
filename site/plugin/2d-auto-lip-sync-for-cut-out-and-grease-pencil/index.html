Blender add-on that auto-generates mouth shapes from an audio strip for Grease Pencil and image-based cut-out characters.

What 2D Auto Lip Sync (For Cut-Out And Grease Pencil) does

2D Auto Lip Sync automates mouth animation for 2D characters in Blender. It analyses an audio strip with FFT frequency tracking to pick out vowels and consonants, then switches mouth shapes accordingly, whether your character is built from Grease Pencil layers or cut-out image collections.

A jaw bone can also be driven with adjustable openness for each viseme, and a manual grid lets you punch in particular shapes by hand. Smoothing prevents the mouth from flickering at any frame rate. Audio is synced through the Video Sequencer with a start-frame detector, and the Rhubarb Lip Sync engine is supported as an alternative.

Key features

  • FFT frequency tracking to detect vowels and consonants
  • Works with Grease Pencil layers or image viseme collections
  • Jaw bone animation with per-viseme openness
  • Manual viseme grid for hand-placed shapes
  • Compatible with the Rhubarb Lip Sync engine
