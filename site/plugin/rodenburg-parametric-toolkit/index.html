Bounding-box handles and snapping for selecting, scaling, rotating and positioning parametric objects in Cinema 4D.

What Rodenburg Parametric Toolkit does

Rodenburg Parametric Toolkit gives Cinema 4D direct controls for parametric shapes and for objects already converted to polygons. Grab a corner or edge of an object's bounding box and drag to change its size or orientation.

Snapping options place objects onto other bounding boxes or surfaces, and an object can be sized to reach another or rotated to point at a target. Options are icon-based so they fit into your layout. A second included plugin handles parametric selection of caps, roundings, and static or animated random selections. Modelers and designers who build with primitives will find it speeds layout.

Key features

  • Bounding-box drag to scale and rotate
  • Works on parametric and converted objects
  • Snap to other bounding boxes or surfaces
  • Size to reach or rotate toward a target
  • Extra plugin for parametric selections
