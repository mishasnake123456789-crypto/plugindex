What LinkFlow does

LinkFlow, by Gihan-Maddumage, is a production toolkit for handling linked library data in Blender. It is aimed at studios and teams that work with linked assets and want to keep those links organised. It is categorised under Pipeline.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.
