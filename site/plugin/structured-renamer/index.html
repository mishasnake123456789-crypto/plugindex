What Structured Renamer does

Structured Renamer constructs names from reusable components rather than typing each one. Names are assembled from text, position and counter parts, joined with underscores, dashes, dots, spaces or camelCase.

A factory preset library covers patterns for Character Rigging, UE5 Standard, Unity Humanoid, VRM Avatar, PMX, Architecture and Hard Surface. It applies to objects and bones. Riggers and pipeline artists who must satisfy engine naming conventions get consistent results without manual retyping.

Key features

  • Names built from text, position and counter
  • Choice of separators including camelCase
  • Presets for UE5, Unity Humanoid, VRM and more
  • Works on objects and bones
