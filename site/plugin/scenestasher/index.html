What SceneStasher does

Built by Elad Baron, SceneStasher is a panel running inside After Effects aimed at bookmarking parts of an animation. You save layer and keyframe selections as buttons, and a click brings back those key moments later.

The buttons teleport you across compositions, which helps when retiming and refining work spread over many comps. Everything lives in a panel inside After Effects, and it is compatible with versions 2023 through 2026. Animators working on complex timelines gain a quick way to return to the exact spots they keep editing.

Key features

  • Saves layer and keyframe selections as buttons
  • Recalls key moments instantly
  • Jumps across compositions
  • Compatible with After Effects 2023 to 2026
