Blender add-on for saving, listing and switching viewport camera positions with ortho and perspective memory.

What Lutaya Viewport Camera does

Lutaya Viewport Camera helps you return to important views in Blender. One click stores the current viewport location and rotation, and a list shows every saved position with an indicator for orthographic or perspective.

View Face Orthographic aligns to a selected face, and the view can rotate around a face normal by custom steps. Each position remembers its own projection mode, and you can step through positions forward or back. The panel is in the View tab of the sidebar. It targets architectural visualization and product presentation.

Key features

  • One-click save of viewport positions
  • Visual list with ortho and perspective markers
  • View Face Orthographic alignment
  • Rotation around face normals
  • Per-position projection memory
