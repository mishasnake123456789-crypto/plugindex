What OMI Physics Body glTF Extension does

OMI Physics Body glTF Extension is one script file for Blender add-on that writes the OMI_physics_body and OMI_physics_shape extensions into glTF and GLB exports. Its purpose is carrying simple collision data from Blender into game engines without a manual rebuild.

In Godot 4 and newer, each collider arrives as a CollisionObject3D with a CollisionShape3D of the correct primitive type, so no extra setup is needed after import. It is helpful for level designers who place collision volumes while modeling and want them to survive the trip into the engine.

Key features

  • Writes OMI_physics_body and OMI_physics_shape
  • Primitive collision shapes in glTF or GLB
  • Colliders import into Godot 4+ ready to use
  • Single-file add-on
