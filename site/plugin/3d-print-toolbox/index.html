Blender add-on with analysis, repair, hollowing, alignment and export utilities for 3D printing.

What 3D Print Toolbox does

The 3D Print Toolbox helps prepare Blender models for a printer. It measures a mesh's volume and surface area and looks for geometry problems that can ruin a print, with a Make Manifold option to repair them.

A hollow tool creates uniform wall thickness on complex shapes, rotation can be set by aligning selected faces with the print bed, and scaling tools fit a model to given dimensions or volume. Selections export quickly to STL, PLY and OBJ. Hobbyists, miniature designers and product makers can catch errors early rather than discovering them in the slicer.

Key features

  • Volume and surface area analysis
  • Bad geometry checks with Make Manifold
  • Hollowing with consistent thickness
  • Align faces to the print bed
  • Quick STL, PLY and OBJ export
