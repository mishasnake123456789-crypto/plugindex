Blender add-on that grows animated ivy by drawing on meshes or generating it procedurally in three modes.

What Baga Ivy Simulation & Generator does

Baga Ivy Simulation & Generator brings climbing ivy to Blender scenes. In Simulation Mode the plant grows gradually as you draw across a mesh.

Growth can be baked all at once or refined with Auto-Bake. Three procedural modes suit different jobs: Fast for broad coverage, Accurate for branch networks that hug the surface, and Precision for drawing one branch at a time. A library of assets is also provided. Environment artists dress ruins, walls and props with believable vegetation.

Key features

  • Draw-directed animated ivy growth
  • Fast, Accurate and Precision modes
  • Auto-Bake refinement
  • Asset library
