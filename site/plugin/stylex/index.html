What StyleX does

StyleX by vimager brings AI stylisation to After Effects and Premiere Pro. Its Cartoon mode converts footage into drawn and painted looks, while Style Transfer mode imitates the style of any image or video you supply, keeping good consistency from frame to frame.

The method was trained on the structures, textures and colours of tens of thousands of cartoon images. A scale slider moves between fine and coarse style, chroma blend retains original colour in drawing and painting styles, and a luma blend slider enables colour transfer. It is GPU accelerated for Mac and Windows, and more than 60 sample style images ship in the install package.

Key features

  • Cartoon and Style Transfer modes
  • Style from any reference image or video
  • Style scale from fine to coarse
  • Chroma and luma blend controls
  • GPU accelerated with 60+ sample styles
