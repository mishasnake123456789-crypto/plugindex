What Brevidy does

Brevidy, from Plugin Play, streamlines short-form video work in Premiere Pro. It produces animated captions for any sequence, with SRT download, and browses stock B-roll and emoji media, with AI suggestions.

It can autocut content into social clips, and AutoCrop Beta converts landscape footage to vertical with crop movement and tracking presets. Trending and custom presets are available, and Captioner and Producer subscription options include priority support and yearly credits.

Key features

  • Animated captions with SRT download
  • B-roll and emoji browser with AI suggestions
  • Autocut into social clips
  • AutoCrop Beta for vertical video
  • Trending and custom presets
