A panel for After Effects and Illustrator that organises, browses and runs JavaScript scripts in one place.

What Script Panel does

Script Panel by OVJECT keeps your JavaScript scripts within reach inside Adobe applications. Instead of digging through the File menu or folders, you open a panel and launch what you need with one click.

Scripts can be arranged into folders, favourites keep chosen ones close, and everything can be browsed and managed from within the host. It needs After Effects 17.0 or later, or Illustrator 19.0 or later. Creatives who collect many utility scripts will find it tidies a messy toolbox.

Key features

  • Script folder organisation
  • Favourites management
  • One-click script execution
  • Browse and manage scripts inside Adobe apps
  • After Effects 17.0+ and Illustrator 19.0+
