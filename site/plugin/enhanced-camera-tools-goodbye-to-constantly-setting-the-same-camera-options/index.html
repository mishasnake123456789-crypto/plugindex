What Enhanced Camera Tools - Goodbye To Constantly Setting The Same Camera Options does

Enhanced Camera Tools, from McCamish Studios, stops you from re-entering the same camera options in every project. Store a camera as a named preset and reuse it whenever you need.

A preset records the lens setup, from type, focal length and units to shift and clipping, along with depth of field, safe area, viewport display and composition guides. A preset can be imported as a new camera, applied to the camera you selected, or batch applied to several cameras at once. Reset and focal length helpers round out the set, which suits studios wanting consistent camera setups.

Key features

  • Save camera settings as named presets
  • Import a preset as a new camera
  • Apply a preset to a selected camera
  • Batch apply to multiple cameras
  • Covers lens, DOF, clipping, safe area and guides
