What NFT does

NFT by Exposure is a novelty plugin, framed around the endless client change requests many animators know. Its joke is that text can be committed to the blockchain so it can never be changed again.

It works with After Effects from CS3 through 2021, and there is a demo video. Treat it as a gag rather than a production tool. It will mainly appeal to anyone who enjoys a bit of industry humor about revisions and approvals.

Key features

  • Satirical take on client revisions
  • Joke blockchain text commitment
  • Supports CS3 through 2021
  • Demo video available
