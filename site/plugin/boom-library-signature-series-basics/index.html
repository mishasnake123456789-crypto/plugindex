What BOOM Library Signature Series - Basics does

BOOM Library Signature Series Basics condenses the publisher's catalog into one royalty-free collection. It draws roughly ten percent of the stereo content from each of the 100 Signature Series packs, giving over 15,000 sounds in more than 4,700 files.

Themes include cinematic effects, ambiences, sci-fi and fantasy, weapons, transportation, Foley, and animal and creature sounds. Files are 24-bit, 96 kHz WAV, upwards of 100 GB in all, labelled with UCS and embedded metadata readable in Basehead and Soundminer. Stereo and Surround variants of the series are available separately. Useful for trailers, films, games and post-production.
