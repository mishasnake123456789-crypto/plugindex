What Rbdlab does

RBDLab is a VFX and simulation add-on for Blender created by BeFX Studios. Its focus is rigid body dynamics and destruction, the sort of collapsing, shattering and crumbling effects used in film and games.

It comes with documentation and free updates for future releases, is released under the GPL, and supports Blender 4.0 through 5.1. Buyers also gain access to a Discord channel with more than 600 members. Effects artists and animators needing to break and simulate objects can build setups with less manual preparation.

Key features

  • Rigid body and destruction workflows
  • Free updates and documentation
  • Supports Blender 4.0 through 5.1
  • Discord community access
