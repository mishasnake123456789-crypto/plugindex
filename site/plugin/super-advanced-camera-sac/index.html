What Super Advanced Camera (Sac) does

Super Advanced Camera combines color grading, camera controls and compositor effects, with changes visible in the viewport in real time. Color settings are extensive and layered.

A render panel handles resolution, frame range, FPS and time stretching with presets. Lens options include tilt shift, focal length, field of view and visible distance range, and bokeh uses 84 real-camera images. It suits artists wanting photographic control in one place. Highlights include layered color grading, render settings panel with presets and tilt shift and lens settings.

Key features

  • Layered color grading
  • Render settings panel with presets
  • Tilt shift and lens settings
  • Bokeh from 84 real-camera images
