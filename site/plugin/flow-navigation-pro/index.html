Blender add-on giving six-degree-of-freedom viewport navigation from a single key, with no extra hardware.

What Flow Navigation Pro does

Flow Navigation Pro offers free-form viewport movement in Blender without a SpaceMouse. Orbit, zoom, pan and roll are combined on one key of your choice, behaving the same on a mouse, pen or laptop.

Any key or mouse button can be bound with click or hold actions to orbit, pan, roll, focus, align, snap or a custom operator. Extras include a spacebar Free Flight modal, a radial View Switcher, a visual UI, physics and auto-focus. Documentation is available from CG Flow.

Key features

  • Six degrees of freedom without hardware
  • Orbit, zoom, pan and roll on one key
  • Bind any key or button with click or hold
  • Free Flight modal and radial View Switcher
  • Consistent on mouse, pen and laptop
