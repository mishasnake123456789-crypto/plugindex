Crops an After Effects composition to the size of its content, at one frame or across the full animation.

What Auto Crop does

Auto Crop, a native plugin from BatchFrame Software, trims a composition down to the area its layers actually occupy. This avoids empty margins and oversized renders.

It can measure the current frame or analyse the whole duration so no animated detail is clipped, and crops are pixel-accurate down to a single faintly visible pixel in any layer's alpha. Instances of a comp reused in other comps are adjusted so visuals stay the same, and Auto Crop 3 can crop the active comp without it being a pre-comp. It can expand and crop at once, and offers a layer menu with shortcuts, an optional panel and preferences.

Key features

  • Crops to content at current frame or across full duration
  • Pixel-perfect alpha-based detection
  • Compensates other comps that use the cropped one
  • Expand and crop in one step
  • Layer menu, script panel and preferences
