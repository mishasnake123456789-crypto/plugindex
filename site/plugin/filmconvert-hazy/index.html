What FilmConvert Hazy does

FilmConvert Hazy recreates the soft, glowing look of diffusion filters shot in front of a lens, but applies it in post. It works as a standalone plugin in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro, whatever workflow surrounds it.

Presets follow classic mist filter styles and offer strength options for spread and intensity. Filter size decides whether bloom hugs bright lights tightly or spreads a gentle haze across the frame, and response controls with an adaptive graph shape the rolloff. It also softens uneven skin and redness, boosts halation, and diffuses shiny highlights to lower contrast.

Key features

  • Presets modeled on mist filter styles
  • Filter size from tight bloom to wide glow
  • Response graph for effect rolloff
  • Softens skin tones and blemishes
  • Halation boost and highlight diffusion
