What Light Scenario does

Light Scenario stores complete lighting states in Blender and lets you swap between them with one click.

A scenario captures light properties, on or off states, colors and power. Animation helpers include a Global Power slider, keyframeable blending from one scenario to another with live preview, and a Scenario Mixer with per-scenario sliders. An HDRI Manager shows a visual grid preview. It is good for comparing lighting options for clients.

Key features

  • Unlimited lighting scenarios
  • Captures state, color and power
  • Keyframeable A to B blending
  • Scenario Mixer sliders
  • HDRI Manager with grid preview
