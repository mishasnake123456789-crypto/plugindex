What Boris FX Mocha Pro - Annual Subscription does

Boris FX's Mocha Pro is built around planar tracking, following flat surfaces through footage so roto shapes, patches and inserts stay locked in place. It works as a plugin inside After Effects, Premiere Pro and other hosts, and also as a standalone application.

The toolset covers rotoscoping, object removal, image and camera stabilization, and 3D camera solves powered by SynthEyes. PowerMesh tracks deforming surfaces with a dynamic mesh, and it now includes Point Track ML for surfaces that turn or become partly hidden. Workflows for 360 and VR footage are also supported.

Key features

  • Planar tracking at its core
  • Rotoscoping and advanced object removal
  • PowerMesh tracking with Point Track ML
  • SynthEyes-powered 3D camera solves
  • Plugin or standalone, with stabilization and 360/VR support
