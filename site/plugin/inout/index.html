What Inout does

Inout is an After Effects tool that creates in and out animations without placing keyframes manually. Select a property, and the transition is built for the start and end of the layer.

It works on one layer at a time and handles up to two properties per selection, keeping the workflow simple. Motion designers who produce lots of quick title or element animations can use it to avoid repetitive keyframing. It supports After Effects 2022 through 2025.

Key features

  • Keyframe-free in and out transitions
  • Up to two properties per selection
  • One layer processed at a time
  • Supports After Effects 2022 to 2025
