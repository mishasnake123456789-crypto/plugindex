What Piemaster does

PieMaster is a Blender plugin for designing your own menus. Pie menus, dropdown menus and floating panels can all be built, and make them run any operator, add-on or tool.

It works with Blender 4.2 through 5.2, is released under the GPL license and includes documentation. Support is provided by Pitiwazou. Great for tailoring the interface to personal habits. Building menus around your own workflow shortens mouse travel and puts the commands you use most under your fingertips.

Key features

  • Pie menus, dropdowns and floating panels
  • Trigger any operator, add-on or tool
  • Blender 4.2 through 5.2
  • GPL licensed with documentation
