What Light-X Pro does

Light-X Pro unifies lighting management in Blender in a single interactive list.

Lights and collections appear together with collection drill-down and filtering. You can change energy and color for single lights or whole collections, and Solo Mode isolates one while hiding the rest. Light groups are named sequentially and share group-wide controls, and light linking is supported. It reduces the need to hunt through the Outliner when a scene has dozens of lights.

Key features

  • Unified list of lights and collections
  • Energy and color per light or collection
  • Solo Mode
  • Light groups with automatic naming
  • Light linking
