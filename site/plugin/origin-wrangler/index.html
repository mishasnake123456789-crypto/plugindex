Extended origin placement for Blender: selected geometry, specific axes, bounding box points and post-modifier meshes.

What Origin Wrangler does

Origin Wrangler widens Blender's Set Origin function. You can place origins on selected faces, edges or vertices, restrict them to chosen axes in local or global space, or put them at bounding box points.

It can also take final meshes into account, including shape keys and modifiers like Mirror, Array and Displace, and smart-align the origin to the middle of the geometry on any of an object's six sides. It helps when preparing props, pivots and game assets.

Key features

  • Origin at selected faces, edges or vertices
  • Axis-limited placement, local or global
  • Accounts for modifiers and shape keys
  • Smart-align on six object sides
  • Bounding box points
