What Ray Dynamic Color 2 does

Ray Dynamic Color 2 by Sander van Dijk manages color from a central palette in After Effects. Click a swatch to color a scene, or extract the colors already used into a palette, then change them later with or without expression links.

Colors can be linked to and unlinked from the palette, shape strokes and fills colored separately, and two properties can swap colors. Palette colors can be randomized or cycled, and differences are resolved when merging scenes. KBar and Overlord integration come in a compact, responsive interface, helpful for motion designers revising projects.

Key features

  • One-click coloring with swatches
  • Extract scene colors into a palette
  • Link and unlink colors to the palette
  • Swap colors, randomize and cycle
  • KBar and Overlord integration
