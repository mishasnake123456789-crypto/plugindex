What Midi Piano Motion Tools Pro does

Midi Piano Motion Tools Pro converts MIDI data into a played piano inside Blender. It is aimed at music video makers, piano teachers and anyone producing visualizers or demo reels.

Key motion is frame-accurate and velocity-driven, with hammer and pedal movement including damper, sostenuto and una corda. Pitch bend, modulation, transposition and channel filtering are supported. Keys can emit light with per-hand or per-note colors and gradients, and physics-based falling balls add bounce and glow. An editing panel in the 3D View sidebar offers live tests and a reset.

Key features

  • Velocity-driven, frame-accurate key animation
  • Hammer and pedal movement
  • Per-note and per-hand light colors, gradients
  • Physics-based falling balls with impact glow
