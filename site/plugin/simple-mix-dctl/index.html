What Simple Mix DCTL does

Simple Mix DCTL is a grading tool for DaVinci Resolve Studio that works along hue axes. Two sliders, Squeeze and Axis, push colors toward any hue line using a normalized RGB Mixer, which suits palette building and split-toning.

Tonal Offsets slide colors along the axis by brightness range (shadows, mids, highlights, global), and an Orthogonal offset moves them at right angles to it. Skin-Tone Recovery blends original skin back in, with a mask view and a wider hue range than ColorSlice. Seven non-destructive saturation logics and a Saturation Boost keep shadows intact. A Monochrome section offers four grayscale types, and tooltips and a color space control are included.

Key features

  • Squeeze and Axis controls on a normalized RGB Mixer
  • Tonal offsets for shadows, mids, highlights and global
  • Skin-Tone Recovery with mask preview
  • Seven non-destructive saturation logics
  • Monochrome section with four grayscale types
