What SilhouetteToole does

SilhouetteToole, by Quinzy, lets you judge a form by its outline in Blender. A single click turns the 3D Viewport into a black silhouette on white, using Solid and Flat shading, and another click brings back your earlier view settings.

It affects the viewport only and is non-destructive. That makes it useful for checking shape, proportions and readability of characters, concepts and game assets. It needs no special permissions and works with Blender 4.5 LTS and newer.

Key features

  • One-click silhouette mode
  • Black on white using Solid and Flat shading
  • Viewport-only and non-destructive
  • Restores previous view settings
  • Blender 4.5 LTS and newer
