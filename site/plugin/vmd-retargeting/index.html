What Vmd Retargeting does

Vmd Retargeting is a Blender addon by xianran that brings MMD motion from .vmd files onto Daz or Character Creator models. It works with or without an MMD model present, and treats camera, body, eyeball, facial and viseme motion as separate imports.

Adjustments help the result look right on your character: feet rotation can be ignored for characters in high heels, arm rotation rate prevents hands from poking into the chest, and interpolation and easing are configurable. Camera height offset and rotation rate can also be set for imported camera motion.

Key features

  • Imports .vmd motion without needing an MMD model
  • Separate body, eyeball, facial, viseme and camera import
  • Ignore feet rotation for high heels
  • Arm rotation rate prevents hand-chest collisions
  • Interpolation, easing and camera offsets
