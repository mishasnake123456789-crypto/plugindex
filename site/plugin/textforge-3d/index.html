Textforge 3D

Developer: Shahzaib A.

FreeBlender

Creates and edits 3D text from Blender's N-Panel with live updates, typography and geometry controls, then converts to mesh.

What Textforge 3D does

TextForge 3D generates 3D text instantly and shows every change live in the viewport. Typography controls cover font size, shear for italics, and horizontal and vertical alignment.

Geometry controls include extrude depth and bevel settings, and the result can be converted into an editable mesh. Good for titles, logos and signage that need quick iteration. Because the text stays live until converted, changes to wording or styling are easy to make late in a project.

Key features

  • Instant text generation from the N-Panel
  • Font size, shear and alignment
  • Extrude and bevel controls
  • Convert to editable mesh
  • Live viewport updates
