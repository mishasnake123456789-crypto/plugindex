Blender add-on for mapping an existing trim sheet onto model faces with fit, fill, mirror and rotate options.

What Trimmer does

Trimmer helps apply trim sheet textures in Blender. Divide a plane into faces that mark trim positions, add them to the Trimmer panel, then apply trims to selected faces on your model.

Fit keeps the selection inside the trim without stretching, while Fill stretches it to cover. Mirror reflects the result across the Y axis, and Rotate and Confirm adjust and finalize placement, with a degree slider for Fit and an edgewise button for Fill. Trims are listed as rows in the sidebar, grouped by trimsheet and reorderable.

Key features

  • Fit without stretching or Fill to stretch
  • Mirror across the Y axis
  • Rotate and Confirm placement
  • Trims listed as rows in the sidebar
  • Grouping and reordering by trimsheet
