What Arkitautoriger does

ArKitAutoRiger creates a face rig around the 52 standard ARKit blendshapes, provided your meshes share the same topology. It removes the manual work of setting up controls for facial animation.

Facial capture from UE5.6 video is supported, and the resulting animation can be brought into Blender to edit and render. It is compatible with Blender 4.0 through 4.5. Character animators and virtual production artists who record performances with a phone or video can refine the results inside Blender.

Key features

  • Builds a 52-blendshape ARKit face rig
  • Requires shared topology across blendshapes
  • Supports UE5.6 video facial capture
  • Imports capture animation for editing and rendering
  • Compatible with Blender 4.0 through 4.5
