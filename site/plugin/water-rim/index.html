A Blender geometry node group that draws tension lines and optional foam where water meets an object.

What Water Rim does

Water Rim adds the small details that sell a water surface in Blender: the thin tension lines and foam that form where water touches an object. It is a geometry node group, so the effect is built procedurally and adjusts along with the objects it surrounds.

It works in Blender 4.1 and later, specifically 4.1 through 4.3, and comes with documentation and a tutorial video. The royalty-free license covers personal, portfolio and commercial work. Environment artists and animators making ponds, lakes, pools or shorelines can add convincing edge detail without sculpting or painting it.

Key features

  • Tension lines where water meets an object
  • Optional foam generation
  • Geometry node group
  • Documentation and tutorial video
  • Royalty-free for commercial use
