Spawns baked lightning and electricity in Blender with a fluid-style simulation domain for storms and sci-fi arcs.

What Ezlightning does

EzLightning creates lightning and electric arcs dynamically in a scene, suited to storm effects and sci-fi. A simulation domain behaves like Blender's fluid sim and can be moved and scaled before and after baking.

Baking turns the optimized simulation into keyframes and shaders, with traditional lightning spreading behavior. VFX artists can produce branching bolts quickly for cinematic skies and energy effects. Among its tools are fluid-style movable simulation domain and bakes to keyframes and shaders.

Key features

  • Fluid-style movable simulation domain
  • Bakes to keyframes and shaders
  • Traditional lightning spread
  • Storm and sci-fi arc effects
