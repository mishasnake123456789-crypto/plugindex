What Keycap Generator Pro Print-Ready Mechanical Keycaps For Fdm does

Keycap Generator Pro turns a blank scene into a keycap that can be printed on an FDM printer at real-world size. Five profiles are included (Cherry, OEM, SA, DSA, XDA), using row heights and tilts taken from published GMK and Cherry data.

Sizes range from 1U to 7U spacebars on a 19.05 mm pitch, with stabilizer support, and files are written as STL. Keyboard hobbyists can design and prototype custom caps without separate CAD. Matching real dimensions and profile data makes the printed caps fit common switch layouts without extra adjustment.

Key features

  • Cherry, OEM, SA, DSA and XDA profiles
  • Per-row heights and tilts
  • Sizes from 1U to 7U spacebars
  • Strict 1:1 real-world scale
  • FDM-ready STL export
