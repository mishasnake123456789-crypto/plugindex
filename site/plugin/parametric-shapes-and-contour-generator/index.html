What Parametric Shapes & Contour Generator does

Parametric Shapes & Contour Generator creates geometry from mathematics and images inside Blender. It pairs an equation engine with contour splines, a vertex caching system and export to OBJ or STL.

The engine evaluates Cartesian and polar multi-variable grids. Contours are built with recursive, sliced or concentric splines. A custom .npc NumPy cache keeps the math topology across frames without axis remapping errors, and image displacement turns grayscale textures into topographic heights. It suits data visualization and generative design.

Key features

  • Equation engine for Cartesian and polar grids
  • Recursive, sliced and concentric contour splines
  • Custom .npc NumPy vertex caching
  • Image displacement to topographic heights
  • OBJ and STL export
