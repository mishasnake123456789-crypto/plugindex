What AEJuice Split Screens does

AEJuice Split Screens is a library of ready-made split screen layouts for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. Editors can place them over video to show several clips at once, in versions suited to widescreen or vertical social formats.

The layouts are organized by frame style: animated frames, animated frames with grain, still frames, clean still frames and still frames with grain. They are applied by dragging, and the Pack Manager adds fast previews, drag-and-drop import and favorites, and downloads are quick.

Key features

  • Drag and drop over video
  • Animated and still frame styles
  • Grain and clean variants
  • Horizontal and vertical layouts
  • Pack Manager with preview and favorites
