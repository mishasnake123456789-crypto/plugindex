What Batch Texture Converter does

Batch Texture Converter, by maylog, processes textures in bulk inside Blender. It can set the color space during conversion and split alpha channels into separate images. It is listed under Import-Export.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.0.0 or newer.
