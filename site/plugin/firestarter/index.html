What FireStarter does

FireStarter sets up a new project in a click. Choose a folder, press import, and the tool builds color-coded bins and files footage, audio and graphics where they belong, without bringing in duplicates.

It handles BRAW, R3D and ARRIRAW footage and remembers your settings for next time. It works with Premiere Pro 2023 through 2026, a quick start for editors who ingest large shoots. It speeds up the first minutes of a new edit, particularly on projects with many camera files.

Key features

  • One-click folder import
  • Automatic color-coded bins
  • Sorts footage, audio and graphics
  • Supports BRAW, R3D and ARRIRAW
  • Skips duplicate files
