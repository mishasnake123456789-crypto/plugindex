What AEJuice Shape Elements does

AEJuice Shape Elements gathers 777 animated pieces, from simple geometry to decorative accents, spread over 32 categories. Colours can be edited on every element so they match your project.

Most categories were created from After Effects layers, and the collection is browsed in the AEJuice Pack Manager, which offers quick previews, drag-in import and favourites. It targets After Effects and DaVinci Resolve users assembling title sequences, lower thirds and similar motion templates, giving them a ready supply of building blocks.

Key features

  • 777 animated elements
  • 32 categories
  • Editable colours on every element
  • Mostly built from After Effects layers
  • Pack Manager with preview and favourites
