What BOOM Library Cinematic Motion does

Cinematic Motion from BOOM Library focuses on sounds that accompany movement: whooshes, impacts, transitions, textures, zaps and friction. Delivered as WAV files, it fits into Premiere Pro projects like any other sound folder.

Content is sorted into six motion categories and mixes synthetic material with organic recordings such as wind, passing cars, clothing movement and paper umbrellas. The Construction Kit contains 522 files and 3,022 sounds (6.5 GB) of raw material for building your own effects, while the Designed edition has 100 files and 352 sounds (1.2 GB) ready to drag in. A bundle combines both.
