What Flight Stunt Show Generator does

Flight Stunt Show Generator automates the setup of synchronized aerial displays in Blender. Aircraft can follow curves you draw or paths the add-on generates, and a maneuver sequencer lists straight passes, rolls, loops, Immelmann turns, split-S moves and stall turns.

A banking solver derives roll angles from speed and turn radius. Chase, ground, wing and cockpit camera rigs shake according to G-forces, while proximity warnings flag crossing trajectories in 3D. Engines get glowing, flickering exhaust plumes. Models can be procedural planes, items from a built-in library or objects you append yourself.

Key features

  • Procedural or curve-based flight paths
  • Maneuver sequencer with rolls, loops and stall turns
  • Banking solver from speed and turn radius
  • Chase, ground, wing and cockpit camera rigs
  • Proximity warnings for crossing trajectories
