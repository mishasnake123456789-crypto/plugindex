Blender add-on for repairing, checking, cutting and exporting models for 3D printing.

What Meshsmith does

MeshSmith keeps the whole 3D-printing preparation process inside Blender. It repairs problem geometry, tests whether a model will print, splits oversized parts, trims polygon counts and exports STL files in batches.

One click switches Blender to real-world printing units. Repair handles non-manifold edges, holes, stray vertices, inverted normals, n-gons and overlapping faces. The analysis checks watertightness, wall thickness, unapplied transforms and whether the model fits the print bed, and thin areas are highlighted. Cutting divides large models into sections. Decimation, voxel remeshing and auto orientation help, and batch export applies modifiers and writes one STL per object. It suits miniature, cosplay and figurine makers.

Key features

  • Mesh repair for common print defects
  • Printability and wall thickness analysis
  • Print cutting for oversized models
  • Decimation, remesh and auto orientation
  • Batch STL export with scale references
