What Easy Cursor Snapper does

Easy Cursor Snapper is a light Blender add-on for moving the 3D Cursor and objects precisely. Press one button to bring the cursor to selected objects, or another to move the selection onto the cursor.

A simple panel appears in the Tool Shelf. It helps in architectural modeling, character rigging and scene organization, where exact placement saves time. The add-on is free and open source under the GNU General Public License v3.0, supports Blender 3.0 and above, and includes documentation.

Key features

  • Cursor to selection in one click
  • Selection to cursor in one click
  • Simple Tool Shelf panel
  • Open source under GPL v3
  • Works with Blender 3.0 and above
