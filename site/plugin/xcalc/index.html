What xCalc does

xCalc is a basic calculator written entirely in MAXScript by markulie for Autodesk 3ds Max. It lets you do quick sums without leaving the application or switching to another program.

A history feature keeps earlier calculations on hand so they can be checked or reused. To install, drag the script into a 3ds Max viewport. It is a free, small tool for artists who regularly work out dimensions, ratios or unit conversions while modeling and setting up scenes.

Key features

  • Simple calculator inside 3ds Max
  • History feature
  • Drag-and-drop installation
  • Written entirely in MAXScript
