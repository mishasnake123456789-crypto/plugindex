What Collablend does

CollaBlend is a Blender add-on for keeping notes where the work actually is. Rather than tracking feedback in separate documents or chat threads, you write annotations directly into the .blend file and tie them to specific objects, scenes or frames.

Because the notes are saved with the file, anyone you hand the project to sees the same context, decisions and review comments. You can add, edit and remove notes at any time, and the system stays non-destructive alongside your normal setup. It is a good fit for small teams passing scenes back and forth, or solo artists who want a lasting record of what was decided.

Key features

  • Notes stored inside the .blend file
  • Annotations attached to objects, scenes or frames
  • Add, edit and remove notes at any time
  • Notes travel with the file to teammates
  • Non-destructive alongside existing workflow
