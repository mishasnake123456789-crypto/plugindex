Creates cameras in After Effects that mirror the active camera of another composition.

What Camera Instancer does

Camera Instancer, from Blurrypixel Software, makes a camera that is an instance of the active camera in another composition. After pasting it into any comp, edits to the source camera carry over to the linked one, so multi-comp scenes keep consistent camera moves.

Instancing persists even when you change which camera is active in the source composition. The instanced camera can be cut and pasted into other compositions, and the tool works with the Advanced 3D and C4D renderers. It is compatible with After Effects from CS6 through 2025, and suits projects that nest 3D scenes across several comps.

Key features

  • Camera linked to another comp's active camera
  • Cut and paste into other compositions
  • Source edits propagate automatically
  • Works with Advanced 3D and C4D renderers
  • Compatible with CS6 through 2025
