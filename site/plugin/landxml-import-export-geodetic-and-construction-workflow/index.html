What Landxml Import Export - Geodetic & Construction Workflow does

LandXML Import Export by Jan Holinka brings geodetic data into Blender and back out again. It works with real-world coordinates and lets you build TIN-based terrain models with breaklines from Blender geometry.

Imports include TIN surfaces, breaklines, contours, boundaries, CgPoints and PlanFeatures. On export, mesh faces are triangulated automatically into TIN surfaces and edge-only mesh objects serve as breaklines. There is control over the Blender origin. Surveyors, civil engineers and site visualization artists can exchange terrain with CAD tools.

Key features

  • Imports TIN surfaces, breaklines, contours and CgPoints
  • Exports TIN surfaces and breaklines
  • Automatic triangulation of mesh faces on export
  • Breaklines from edge-only mesh objects
  • Works with real-world coordinates
