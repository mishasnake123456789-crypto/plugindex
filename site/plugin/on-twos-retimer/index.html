What On-Twos Retimer does

On-Twos Retimer, by zlz188, converts bone poses into keyframes and then holds them every N frames, giving animation a stepped, on-twos feel. It is tagged under Animation, Physics and Rigging.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features
