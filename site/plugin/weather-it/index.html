Blender add-on for weather effects from storms and heavy rain to mist and snow, with rain bounce and snow buildup.

What Weather It does

Weather It, from Blender Bits, builds customizable weather in Blender, ranging from violent storms and downpours to light mist and gentle snowfall. Rain bounces off surfaces and snow gradually builds up on the ground.

Fall speed is adjustable in the geo-nodes tab. It renders in both Cycles and Eevee, works as an add-on on Blender 4.0 through 5.0, and includes documentation. Suitable for adding rain, snow, mist, fog, lightning or storms to scenes.

Key features

  • Rain, snow, mist and storm effects
  • Rain bounce on surfaces
  • Snow buildup on the ground
  • Adjustable fall speed in geo-nodes
  • Cycles and Eevee support
