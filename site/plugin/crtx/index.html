Reactive CRT monitor simulation for After Effects and Premiere Pro that responds to footage motion and light.

What CRTX does

CRTX recreates the look of a cathode-ray tube by redrawing each frame the way a scanning beam would. What appears on screen depends on the footage, with scan structure, mask patterns, bloom and glow changing with motion, brightness and edges.

Instability can be dialed in as colour smear, sync tears and analog signal chaos. Controls are grouped into Electron Beam, Surface and Phosphor sections, with Tube Physics, Power State, Style, Color Grading, Geometry and Camera sections. Glitch & Instability, Circuit Bending and Audio sections add more character, and a CRTX Library supports import and export, plus quality, performance and GPU options.

Key features

  • Frames redrawn like a scanning beam
  • Reactive to motion, luminance and edges
  • Colour smear, sync tears and analog chaos
  • Beam, surface, phosphor and tube physics sections
  • Preset library and GPU options
