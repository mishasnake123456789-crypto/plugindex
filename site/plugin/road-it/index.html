What Road It does

Road It, from Blender Bits, concentrates on roads, streets, highways and traffic. It supports Blender 4.0 to 5.0 and renders in both Cycles and Eevee.

It works with the Blender Extensions system as an add-on and is distributed under the GPL license, with documentation and a demo video. Useful for city and transport scenes. It helps artists lay out streets and highways quickly, which is useful for architectural context and city scenes.

Key features

  • Roads, streets, highways and traffic
  • Blender 4.0 through 5.0
  • Cycles and Eevee support
