What Translational Walk Aid does

Translational Walk Aid, or TWA, from Levonotion Studios helps animators move a biped walk cycle through space without foot sliding. It advances a foot while the center of mass repositions according to stride length, with keyframes added automatically at your chosen interval.

You specify the number of steps, forward or backward, and can use it on an existing walk cycle or build one from scratch, for walking or running. It is compatible with Rigify, BlenRig and Auto-Rig Pro. Version 3.0 added options for climbing up and down stairs, extending its usefulness for character animators.

Key features

  • Advances foot and center of mass using stride length
  • Choose steps forward or backward
  • Adjustable keyframe interval
  • Works with Rigify, BlenRig and Auto-Rig Pro
  • Stairs support from version 3.0
