What choreograph video plugin does

The choreograph video plugin is meant for creative teams who prepare dynamic video templates in After Effects. It pairs with choreograph create and supports dynamic content optimization, where video elements change per audience or placement.

Version 2.4.1 is compatible with CEP 12. A redesigned review step helps by stopping a faulty template from being uploaded. Information beyond this is limited, so it's best suited to teams already using the Choreograph platform.

Key features

  • Builds dynamic video templates in After Effects
