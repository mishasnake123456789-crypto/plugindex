What Andy Disassembly Assistant does

Andy Disassembly Assistant helps take models apart. It separates objects either by loose blocks or by materials, then builds exploded-view diagrams from the pieces.

Explosion points and animation frames are controllable, and keyframe animation is generated for multi-level explosion effects. Product visualizers and technical illustrators can show how an assembly fits together without positioning each part manually. Under the hood you get separate by loose blocks or by materials and one-click exploded-view diagrams.

Key features

  • Separate by loose blocks or by materials
  • One-click exploded-view diagrams
  • Explosion points and frame control
  • Multi-level explosion keyframes
