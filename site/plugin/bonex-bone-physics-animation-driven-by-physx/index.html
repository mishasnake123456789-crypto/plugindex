What Bonex - Bone Physics Animation Driven By Physx does

BoneX, an addon by oimoyu, makes bone physics animation with the PhysX engine. It produces rigid body simulations for character physics, and rigid body settings, connection stiffness and damping can all be tuned.

A collision group mask controls which bodies interact, and custom collision body meshes are supported. Playback can be interacted with in real time, partial simulation allows detailed adjustments, and sub-step simulation handles fast movement. The interface is available in English, which helps riggers adding secondary motion to characters.

Key features

  • PhysX-driven rigid body simulation
  • Adjustable stiffness and damping
  • Collision group masks and custom collision meshes
  • Real-time interaction and partial simulation
  • Sub-step simulation for fast motion
