Blender add-on that erodes models for a worn look, with realistic HP and stylized LP modes for objects or whole brick walls.

What Wear N' Tear does

Wear N' Tear gives Blender models a used, weathered appearance by eroding their geometry. Two modes are available: HP for realistic results and LP for a stylized look.

You can erode a single object or several at once with multi-select, and process whole walls without separating individual bricks or stones. Environment artists creating ruins, old masonry and damaged props can add convincing wear without sculpting every edge. The result suits ruined walls, old statues and damaged props, and it keeps the process quick enough to try several looks on the same asset.

Key features

  • Realistic HP mode and stylized LP mode
  • Multi-object erosion
  • Process whole walls without splitting bricks
  • Quick results on single objects
