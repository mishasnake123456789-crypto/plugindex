What AI Video Background Remover Trial does

This is a trial release of Vakago Tools' AI Video Background Remover for After Effects and Premiere Pro. It generates masks that cut the subject out of a video, so the background can be removed or replaced. The trial runs for 15 days and is for non-commercial use only.

Magic and Mask tools produce higher-quality mattes, and edge smoothing with temporal blending steadies the result in both hosts. GPU and spare CPU cores work together for speed. In After Effects, a Keyframe Distance option renders every n-th frame and interpolates mask points between them, and a mask creation threshold avoids lots of tiny masks for distant background figures.

Key features

  • Generates masks to remove video backgrounds
  • Edge smoothing and temporal blending
  • Uses GPU and spare CPU cores together
  • Keyframe Distance option (After Effects)
  • 15-day, non-commercial trial
