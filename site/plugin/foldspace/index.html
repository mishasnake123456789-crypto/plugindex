GPU-accelerated After Effects plugin for creating and animating 3D curved surfaces with textures and render passes.

What Foldspace does

Foldspace by Denis Mozgovoy lets artists build and animate bent, curved 3D surfaces right in After Effects, going further than the native tools. It is GPU-accelerated for responsive editing.

Control points are animatable and can be attached to other layers to form dynamic rigs, while UI controls shape each surface's curvature. A per-surface material system lets any layer act as a texture. Render passes include Final Render, Depth, Normals in world and view space, and UV, and any camera layer can be chosen when Use Composition Camera is on.

Key features

  • GPU-accelerated 3D curved surfaces
  • Animatable control points attachable to layers
  • Per-surface materials with any layer as texture
  • Depth, Normals and UV render passes
  • Camera layer selection
