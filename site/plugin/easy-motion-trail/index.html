A Blender add-on for editing and visualizing motion paths of objects and bones live in the viewport.

What Easy Motion Trail does

Easy Motion Trail shows motion paths in Blender and lets you edit them directly. It is built with complex rigs in mind, displaying the evaluated path of bones driven by constraints, parenting and IK, which the stock motion path tool can struggle with.

Edits apply to the existing animation without creating a new action or slot. Points can be moved with G, rotated with R including trackball, and scaled with S, updating live while you drag. Animators polishing arcs on characters and cameras can adjust timing and spacing visually rather than hunting through curves.

Key features

  • Real-time motion trails for objects and bones
  • Handles constrained, parented and IK bones
  • Edits existing animation, no new action
  • Move, rotate and scale points with G, R and S
