What Particles Box - Fog, Rain , Particles, Snow, Leaves, Dust, Fluff, Butterfly, Fly does

Particles Box is a Blender add-on that supplies ready-made atmospheric and particle effects. The pack includes flies, butterflies, fluff, dust, falling leaves, snow, rain and fog, so common weather and ambient details can be added without building each system from scratch.

It is compatible with Blender 3.6, 4.0, 4.1 and 4.2, is registered as a Blender extension, and comes with documentation. Artists working on outdoor scenes, moods or small environmental animation can use it to bring life and depth into a shot.
