What Cityscape Pro does

Cityscape Pro by SIMPOLIUM builds urban scenes from surface geometry you choose with a Pick Surface button; that surface is the base for every calculation. Curbed areas can be generated on top of it, turning the exposed portion of the surface into a road.

A material library supplies more than 70 V-Ray and Corona presets that apply to Cityscape elements or any other object in the scene. The interactive Generate button turns blue and flashes when changes are detected and red when there are input problems, so you always know when a rebuild is needed. Useful for archviz artists making city blocks quickly.

Key features

  • City generation from a base surface
  • Curbed areas that form roads
  • 70+ V-Ray and Corona material presets
  • Interactive Generate button with status colors
  • Pick Surface control
