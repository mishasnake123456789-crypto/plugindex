What Quick Matte 2 does

Quick Matte 2 by Baskl.ai uses AI to cut subjects from their backgrounds, aiming to work on any scene rather than only green screen. Version 2.0 added new models and 2.1.0 introduced a resolution parameter.

It runs in After Effects and Premiere Pro from 2022 through 2026, with beta OFX support for Nuke and DaVinci Resolve. A new render engine brings performance improvements and broader GPU compatibility. Artists removing backgrounds from interview shots, product clips or rough footage can skip painstaking rotoscoping.
