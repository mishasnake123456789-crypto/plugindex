What Separate by Collision does

Separate by Collision, by Hattiffnat, sorts a combined mesh into logical pieces. It first separates the mesh by loose parts, then joins together any parts that collide with each other.

The mesh surface radius is adjustable, and two modes are offered: Surface, the standard option that calculates real surface collisions, and Bounding box for a faster approximation. It helps when cleaning up imported models made of overlapping components.

Key features

  • Separates by loose parts
  • Joins colliding parts
  • Adjustable surface radius
  • Surface and Bounding box modes
