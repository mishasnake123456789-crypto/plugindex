What DOSCH 3D: Car Details - Autonomous Car does

This Dosch Design model represents a self-driving vehicle and its critical parts and systems, with an emphasis on interior and safety functions. It is textured and prepared for Cinema 4D.

Scaling is adaptable, materials and textures can be altered, and every color may be changed or replaced with your own labels. Individual elements can be switched on or off, faded through transparency and animated separately or together, which helps when explaining how the car senses its surroundings. Suited to technical animation, illustration and presentation work.
