What BOOM Library SCI-FI - MOMENTUM does

BOOM Library SCI-FI - MOMENTUM supplies sound for science fiction projects in After Effects, Premiere Pro and other editors. Its content spans spaceships, mechanicals, pneumatics, servos, airlocks, doors, and metal and energy impacts.

The material was built from organic source recordings. Choose the Designed edition for drag-and-drop use, the Construction Kit for building your own sounds, or the Bundle for both. The Construction Kit gives six variations of each sound, apart from continuous ones. Files are 24-bit, 96 kHz WAV for Windows and Mac.

Key features

  • Spaceships, servos, pneumatics and airlocks
  • Metal and energy impacts
  • Designed, Construction Kit and Bundle editions
  • Six variations per sound in the Construction Kit
  • 24-bit, 96kHz WAV
