What Sightline³ does

SightLine³ is a precision camera-solving add-on from Momentum Creative Technology Inc. The TwinRay system describes pairs of linked rays arranged along the X, Y and Z axes.

Every axis is bound by explicit ray pairs whose convergence can be measured, so the camera's position and rotation are calculated rather than guessed. It suits matching a virtual camera to photographs or plates with known geometry. It is geared to users who need exact, repeatable camera matches rather than approximate eyeballing.

Key features

  • TwinRay paired ray relationships
  • Explicit ray pairs constrain each axis
  • Measurable convergence
  • Camera position and rotation derived mathematically
