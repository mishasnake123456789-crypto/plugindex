What 2D Special Effects does

2D Special Effects from Mister Horse is a pack of traditionally animated effects drawn frame by frame, such as the bursts and swooshes used in cartoon-style video. The listing says it is also compatible with Premiere Pro.

Colors are customizable, and the source artwork is vector .swf drawn in Adobe Animate, so it scales to any size. Master Properties provide individual controls per effect, and you can layer your own effects on top.

Key features

  • Hand-drawn frame-by-frame effects
  • Customizable colors
  • Resizable vector artwork
  • Per-effect controls in Master Properties
  • Add your own effects on top
