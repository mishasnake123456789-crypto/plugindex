What GenBridge does

GenBridge is a plugin that runs inside Premiere Pro and After Effects so AI generation can happen without exporting files or switching programs. Select a clip, generate new video, and refine it repeatedly right in your timeline or composition.

It reaches a range of models, namely Veo, Sora, Luma, Nano Banana, GPT Image 1.5 and 2 for imagery, and Seedance 2.0 with video-to-video plus an Omni variant that accepts character elements. Premiere Pro also gains Auto Podcast, a Silence Remover and a Hook Swapper, captions built on ElevenLabs Scribe 2, and updated Topaz upscaling. Editors exploring generative workflows can stay in one place.

Key features

  • Native in the Premiere Pro timeline and After Effects comp
  • Generate and iterate on AI video in real time
  • Models including Veo, Sora, Luma, Nano Banana, Seedance 2.0
  • GPT Image models for text-to-image and image-to-image
  • Auto Podcast, Silence Remover, Hook Swapper and captions
