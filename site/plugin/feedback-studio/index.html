What Feedback Studio does

Feedback Studio from Melted Electronics generates the looping, self-referencing imagery of video feedback inside After Effects. Results include fractals, trails and tunnels, and GPU acceleration keeps previews fast.

Integrated luma and chroma keying isolate what feeds back. Translation on X and Y, zoom and rotation shape the recursion, and three mirror modes create kaleidoscopic patterns. Hue, saturation and brightness controls tint the feedback, a negative filter with recursion inverts it, and blur and sharpen soften or crisp the trails. It suits music visuals and psychedelic backdrops.

Key features

  • Integrated luma and chroma key
  • Translation, zoom and rotation of feedback
  • Three mirror modes for kaleidoscopes
  • Hue, saturation and brightness controls
  • GPU-accelerated for real-time performance
