What Max-Style Bevel does

Max-Style Bevel offers a bevel workflow inspired by other modeling packages. It combines extrusion and outlining into a single controlled operation, and automatically cleans internal faces and edges.

Three bevel modes are available: Group, Local Normal and Polygon. Extrusion and scaling are normal-aware. Hard-surface and precision modelers can build panel details with fewer separate steps. In practice that means extrude and outline in one operation and group, Local Normal and Polygon modes.

Key features

  • Extrude and outline in one operation
  • Group, Local Normal and Polygon modes
  • Automatic internal cleanup
  • Normal-aware extrusion and scaling
