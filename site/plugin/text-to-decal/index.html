Turns typed text into placeable decals in Blender with live preview, color and grunge effects.

What Text To Decal does

Text To Decal lets you type a word and apply it to a surface as a decal. A live preview shows where it will land before you commit.

Decals can be single-line or multiline, with adjustable color, grunge and wear effects, reorderable layering and a font choice. Artists adding labels, serial numbers, warning signs or markings to hard-surface models can do so without making image textures separately. Layers can be reordered, so overlapping stencils and stickers are easy to arrange.

Key features

  • Typed words become decals
  • Live placement preview
  • Single and multiline text
  • Color, grunge and wear effects
  • Reorderable decal layers
