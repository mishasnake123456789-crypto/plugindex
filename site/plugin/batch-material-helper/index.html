What Batch Material Helper does

Batch Material Helper, from maylog, speeds up material changes in Blender by letting you edit several materials in one go instead of opening each in turn. It is listed under the Material category.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
