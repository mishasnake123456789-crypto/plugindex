What Scene Duration Display does

Scene Duration Display adds a readout of how long your timeline runs, keeping it in sync as you change it. It also lets you set the preview range using the I and O keys, in the style of video editors.

Animators and editors who work with specific lengths in time can see the duration at a glance. The shortcuts make trimming a working range quick.

Key features

  • Timeline duration readout
  • Synced duration display
  • Preview range via I and O
