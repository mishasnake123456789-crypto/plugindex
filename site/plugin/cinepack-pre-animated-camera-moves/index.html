What Cinepack: Pre-Animated Camera Moves does

Cinepack gives Blender users a collection of pre-animated camera moves that can be applied instantly through a custom interface. There are over 123 animations, including dollies, cranes, zooms, pushes, pulls and speed ramps.

A search bar finds moves by keyword, HD previews show each move before import, and speed, timing, scale and field of view can be customized. Aspect ratio presets are one click away. New moves are added through the year with lifetime updates. Motion designers and filmmakers can add cinematic camera work without keyframing.

Key features

  • 123+ camera animations
  • Search by keyword
  • MP4 previews before import
  • Speed, timing, scale and FOV controls
  • Aspect ratio presets
