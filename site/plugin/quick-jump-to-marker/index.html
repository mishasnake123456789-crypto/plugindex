What Quick Jump To Marker does

Quick Jump To Marker makes navigating a long animation easier in Blender. Instead of scrubbing or stepping frame by frame, you click a marker and the playhead goes straight to it.

It operates in the Timeline and Graph Editor, includes sorting to find markers easily, and recenters the view on the playhead after each jump. It is compatible with Blender Extensions and includes documentation. Animators and editors who mark shots, beats or events will move around their scenes far faster.

Key features

  • Single-click jump to any marker
  • Smart marker sorting
  • Automatic playhead centering
  • Works in Timeline and Graph Editor
