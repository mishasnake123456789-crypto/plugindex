What Creature Kitbash - Insidious Insects does

Creature Kitbash - Insidious Insects provides ready-made insect anatomy for Blender artists building insectoid, alien or exoskeletal creatures. You mix parts instead of sculpting from scratch.

The pack has 102 assets, including heads, thoraxes, abdomens, legs, wings, antennae and mandibles, plus 52 bonus humanoid parts from the Beastly Bits library. Tools help with mirroring, placement and merging, it works with the Asset Browser, and it combines with other Creature Kitbash packs for 400+ assets.
