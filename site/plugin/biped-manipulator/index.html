What Biped Manipulator does

Biped Manipulator is a MAXScript that speeds up selecting objects and bones in 3ds Max, including whole groups like arms, legs and the spine. Since it picks by object name rather than by system type, it works with Character Studio, CAT or custom rigs alike.

The interface is compact and takes little screen space, and several manipulators can run together when a scene has more than one biped. A built-in Hand Manipulator appears when you Shift+click the F/f control. Its parts are configurable, Biped Knuckles bones are handled out of the box, and an Unhide Selection macro script comes along. Animators who pose characters often will find selection quicker.

Key features

  • Quick selection of bones, objects and groups like arms and legs
  • Name-based selection works with Character Studio, CAT and custom rigs
  • Compact interface
  • Run multiple manipulators for multiple bipeds
  • Merged Hand Manipulator and Biped Knuckles support
