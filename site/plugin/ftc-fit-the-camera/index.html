What FTC – Fit The Camera does

FTC, short for Fit The Camera, by TonyS87, calibrates a Blender camera to a photograph by using vanishing points or shapes of known proportions, so 3D elements line up with the picture. It is listed under Camera and Render.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Match a camera to a photo
  • Vanishing point or known-shape methods
