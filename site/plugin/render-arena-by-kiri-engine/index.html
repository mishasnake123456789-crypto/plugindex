What Render Arena By Kiri Engine does

Render Arena, from KIRI Engine, prepares a presentation scene for Cycles quickly. It adds a background arena, lighting, cameras, animation and compositing with presets instead of manual setup.

The Basic version has 3 camera and 3 light presets, while Pro adds a full camera system, 10 camera presets and 20 light presets. Background arena assets include materials and object plinths. Pro also offers 6 object animation presets and a compositing system. Documentation is included.

Key features

  • Scene setup in fewer than 10 clicks
  • Camera and light presets
  • Arena backgrounds and plinth assets
  • Pro adds animation presets and compositing
