What Lazy 2 does

Lazy 2, from Plugin Play, spreads layers and keyframes across the timeline following a curve you shape. It adds two extra dimensions of easing to staggered animation, instead of the plain linear offset typical of sequencing tools.

A bezier curve controls spacing, and keyframe pinching offers a second way to ease. Preset curves are provided, and your own can be saved and edited. The Distribute button applies the spacing, and you can distribute at the playhead or from the first layer or keyframe. The interface is responsive and adapts to smaller scaling.

Key features

  • Distribute layers and keyframes on a bezier curve
  • Keyframe pinching as extra easing
  • Preset curve library with saving
  • Distribute at playhead or first item
  • Responsive UI
