A macOS panel that pauses and resumes your music streaming in step with the After Effects timeline.

What Doppler does

Doppler links a music streaming service to the After Effects timeline on macOS. When you press play on the timeline, your music pauses so it doesn't clash with your comp's audio, and when you stop, the music resumes.

The panel also has media control buttons, plus a button to hide or show them, and its minimum width has been reduced to take up less space. It is for editors and animators who listen while they work and are tired of managing two players.

Key features

  • Syncs streaming music with timeline playback
  • Pauses music on play, resumes on pause
  • Media control buttons in the panel
  • Show or hide controls
  • macOS only
