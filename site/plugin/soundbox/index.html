What SoundBox does

From Eje in Motion comes SoundBox, a script for After Effects aimed at adding sound design without leaving the app. Instead of searching folders and dragging files, you audition effects in a panel and send them to the timeline with a click.

It ships with 90 exclusive effects such as whooshes, hits, clicks and transitions, sorted by category, and you can add your own folders to turn it into a central hub. Markers drive smart fades, stereo pans and timing tweaks that remain adjustable, and playhead-based preview lets you test categories where the animation happens.

Key features

  • 90 categorized sound effects
  • Custom SFX library folders
  • Audition and send to timeline in one click
  • Marker-based fades and pans
  • Playhead-based preview
