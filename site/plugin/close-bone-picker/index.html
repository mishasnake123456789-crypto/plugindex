Fast bone selection for Blender character animation using shortcuts, custom hierarchies and pie menus.

What Close Bone Picker does

Close Bone Picker speeds up picking bones while animating a character. Instead of clicking through a viewport full of bones, you select with keyboard shortcuts, a custom hierarchy that you design, or pie menus holding your frequently used bones.

It enters Pose Mode correctly even when other objects are selected, and lets you toggle and filter bone collections right in the 3D View. The add-on works with any Blender armature, including Rigify, Auto-Rig Pro and custom rigs, which makes it flexible for different pipelines.

Key features

  • Keyboard shortcut bone selection
  • User-designed bone hierarchy
  • Pie menus for registered bones
  • Bone collection toggles in the viewport
  • Works with Rigify, Auto-Rig Pro and custom rigs
