What Camera Pnpoint - Perspective Matching And Lens Calibration does

Camera PnPoint adds a Perspective-n-Point solver to Blender's camera tracking workbench. Given 3D points and where they appear in a reference image, it estimates the pose of a calibrated camera.

The pose solver builds a camera carrying the solved extrinsics and intrinsics plus the background image, working from a single still picture without needing vanishing points or parallel lines. A lens calibration solver optimizes radial distortion, focal length and principal point, and shows the reprojection error. It relies on OpenCV, installable from the add-on preferences.

Key features

  • PnP camera pose solver
  • Lens calibration for focal length and distortion
  • Reprojection error display
  • Camera created with background image
  • OpenCV installed from preferences
