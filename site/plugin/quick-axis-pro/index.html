Adds a persistent Move, Rotate or Scale selector to Blender's viewport header for single-key axis transforms.

What Quick Axis Pro does

Quick Axis Pro puts a transform mode selector in the 3D Viewport header. Four buttons, On/Off, Move, Rotate and Scale, let you pre-select a mode, with only one active at a time.

Pressing X, Y or Z applies the active transform constrained to that global axis, while Shift plus X, Y or Z constrains to the other two axes. The G, R and S keys activate the corresponding modes. It cuts keystrokes for modelers who constantly move, rotate and scale along axes.

Key features

  • Header buttons for On/Off, Move, Rotate, Scale
  • X, Y or Z applies the transform to that axis
  • Shift+axis constrains to the other two
  • G, R and S activate modes
