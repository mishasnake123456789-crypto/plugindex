What Scene Director does

Scene Director gathers several scene-setup tasks into one Blender add-on. It covers lighting, the world environment, compositor layers and planning how a scene will be rendered.

Having these controls together helps when preparing shots with consistent looks, since you are not hunting across multiple editors. Artists building scenes for stills or animation can iterate on the overall look and organize the render pipeline from one place.

Key features

  • Lighting setup
  • World and environment control
  • Compositor layers
  • Render planning
