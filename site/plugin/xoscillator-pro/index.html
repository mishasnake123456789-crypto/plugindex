What XOscillator Pro does

XOscillator Pro is a procedural animation suite for After Effects that creates oscillation without keyframes. More than 30 algorithms are grouped by type: physics like elastic, bounce and spring, noise, digital waveforms, and envelopes such as ADSR.

A live canvas draws the wave as you drag sliders. Patterns including sequential, ripple, cascade, random and pinwheel spread motion over several layers, and progression can raise or lower amplitude and frequency along the way. Create Controller builds a Master Null that drives every selected layer. It can animate any property, such as blur or glow, and cycle colors.

Key features

  • 30+ oscillation algorithms across physics, noise, digital and envelopes
  • Live wave visualizer while adjusting
  • Sequential, Ripple, Cascade, Random and Pinwheel patterns
  • Master Null controller for many layers
  • Drives arbitrary properties and color cycling
