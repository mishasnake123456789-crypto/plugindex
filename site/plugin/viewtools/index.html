Blender add-on of viewport utilities for toggling normals, silhouette and wireframe, finding n-gons and managing subdivision.

What ViewTools does

ViewTools collects inspection helpers for checking geometry in the Blender viewport. Toggles exist for face normals, silhouette mode and wireframe, so topology and shape can be assessed with a click.

A background switcher offers presets, n-gon and triangle detection highlights problem faces, and subdivision modifiers can be managed from the same place. Modelers reviewing meshes before delivery will find quick diagnostic access useful. Everything sits in one panel, so checking a model's shape and topology takes a few clicks instead of digging through overlay menus.

Key features

  • Face normals toggle
  • Silhouette and wireframe toggles
  • Background preset switcher
  • N-gon and triangle detection
  • Subdivision modifier management
