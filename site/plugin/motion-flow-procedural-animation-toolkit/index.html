What Motion Flow - Procedural Animation Toolkit does

Motion Flow builds motion graphics entirely in Geometry Nodes, so no keyframes or coding are required. It ships with over 20 custom nodes, more than 40 motion text presets and over 55 examples.

An Array node offers count, spacing, offset, sphere packing, instance picking and voxel effects, and animation and value-animation nodes drive the motion. It suits designers who want procedural titles and abstract animation in Blender. Notable points: 20+ custom motion graphics nodes, 40+ motion text presets and 55+ examples.
