What SuperZoom does

SuperZoom from MotionCodes creates the popular endless-zoom look from real photography, including pictures shot on a phone. No special gear is needed; you simply capture the subject at progressively closer distances and import the shots.

The tool sorts and stacks the images, warps and aligns them, matches color, and builds a parented comp that automatically focuses on the subject. Images are layered and parented so scaling the main layer zooms through all frames and lands on the target. Mobile creators, motion designers and editors can use it to make zoom transitions.

Key features

  • Cinematic zooms from real photographs
  • Auto sorting, stacking, warping and color matching
  • Parented comp that focuses on the subject
  • One-layer scaling to zoom through all frames
