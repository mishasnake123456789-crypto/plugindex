What Flower Growth Addon Panel（6 Types Of Flowers）Discount Package does

The Flower Growth Addon Panel is a Blender bundle for building blooming flowers, combining a parameterized control panel with a ready asset library. It targets artists who want believable petal behavior without sculpting every bloom.

The panel lets you freely adjust the flower you want, and the petals collide naturally as they open. Results can be baked. The six included flower types, delphinium among them, come as drag-and-drop assets that are convenient for scattering. The package also contains the panel, the library and all project documents.

Key features

  • Parameterized panel for custom flowers

  • Drag-and-drop assets for scattering, bakeable
