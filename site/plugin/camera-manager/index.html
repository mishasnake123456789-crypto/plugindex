What CAMERA MANAGER does

Camera Manager, a Blender addon from 3not, is built for scenes with many cameras. A single panel lists them, sorts them by name or index and switches the view with one click. It works with Blender 4.5 and above.

Effects such as Dolly Zoom and Orbital Tracking, plus a shake generator and a walking-motion simulator, are produced without hand keyframing. All cameras can be rendered at once, each with its own resolution and exposure and with automatic file naming, while timeline-based switching covers complete animations. Eight framing guides, camera nudging, an interactive focus tool with depth of field and F-stop control, and JSON export and import of setups round it out.

Key features

  • Unified panel with sorting and one-click view switching
  • Dolly Zoom, Orbital Tracking, Camera Shake and Walking Motion
  • Render all cameras with per-camera resolution and exposure
  • Eight framing guides and interactive focus tool
  • JSON export and import of camera setups
