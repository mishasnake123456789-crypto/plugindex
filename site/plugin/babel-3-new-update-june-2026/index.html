What Babel 3 (New Update June 2026*) does

Making pristine models look used normally takes careful sculpting. Babel 3, from Egret Studios, inspects the geometry of a mesh and deforms it procedurally to create wear and damage on its own.

Outcomes include nicked edges, worn surfaces and gradual decay from erosion, giving props and architectural pieces a convincing aged character. Use it with Cycles or Eevee; documentation comes with the add-on. Game and film artists who must age many assets in a hurry can treat the result as a solid first pass.

Key features

  • Procedural wear and damage
  • Chipped edges and weathered surfaces
  • Erosion-style deterioration
  • Geometry analysis drives placement
  • Cycles and Eevee support
