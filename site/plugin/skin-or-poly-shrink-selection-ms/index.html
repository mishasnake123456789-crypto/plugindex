What Skin_Or_Poly_Shrink_Selection.ms does

Paul Hormis wrote this MAXScript to contract the current selection while you work with Skin or Poly in 3ds Max. It is the counterpart to a grow selection command, helping refine a selection step by step.

It is published as version 1, delivered as an .ms file, and lists compatibility with 3ds Max 6, 7 and 8. A demo video is on YouTube and the script is free. It will mainly appeal to people on those older releases who edit polygons or weights and want a scripted way to contract a selection.
