What Retriever does

Retriever is a utility for recovering lost file links. Click relink, point it at a folder, and it searches every subfolder to reconnect missing footage and files, so you don't have to relocate each item manually.

It covers After Effects, Illustrator and InDesign, with improved detection of missing link names in Illustrator. Anyone who moves projects between drives, receives collected folders from clients or restores archives will appreciate the time saved.

Key features

  • Searches all subfolders of a chosen folder
  • Recovers missing files, links and footage
  • Works in After Effects, Illustrator and InDesign
  • Improved missing-link name detection in Illustrator
