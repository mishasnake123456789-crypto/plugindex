Select-Up

Developer: Nikita Akimov

FreeBlender
Download Free

Blender add-on that widens a selection one collection level at a time, even through nested collections.

What Select-Up does

Nikita Akimov's Select-Up eases the selection of complex objects wrapped in their own collections. Click any element and press Select Level Up to grow the selection to the next level according to its collection.

Pressing again continues up through nested collections, so large assemblies get selected in a few clicks. It is meant for scenes where each object sits in its own collection. Scene organizers can avoid searching the outliner.

Key features

  • Select Level Up button
  • Repeated presses climb nested collections
  • Suited to objects wrapped in collections
  • Fewer outliner clicks
  • Quick selection of assemblies
