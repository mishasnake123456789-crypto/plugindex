What Takeitposy does

Takeitposy, from POWERBLOW, handles posing by rotating a pair of parent and child bones. It needs nothing beyond your rig and, unlike IK, does not lock bones in place.

After a rotation it aligns the position and orientation of the parent and child exactly. Rotation Limits options let you either follow constraints or ignore axis limits. It suits arms, legs and other chains where you want precise control. Because the pair is aligned after each rotation, poses stay clean without the stiffness that comes from locking bones.

Key features

  • Rotate two parent-child bones precisely
  • No setup beyond the rig
  • Exact alignment after rotation
  • Rotation Limits: follow constraints or ignore
  • Does not lock bones like IK
