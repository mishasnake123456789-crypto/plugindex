What Rowbyte Fast Bokeh Pro for OpenFX does

Rowbyte's Fast Bokeh Pro uses a depth map to add realistic lens blur to shots in DaVinci Resolve. Its render speed depends on image size rather than blur amount, so heavy defocus costs no more than a light touch.

Iris shapes cover Gaussian-like, spherical and polygonal forms such as hexagon, pentagon and octagon, with a Polygonal HQ option for top quality. You may also load a custom image as the bokeh shape. Object boundaries are expanded to reduce hard edges and halos, and Zone Control splits a scene into regions that blur independently. Anamorphic-style bokeh is possible too. Compositors and colorists can use it for fast focus fixes.

Key features

  • Blur speed independent of radius
  • Gaussian, spherical and polygonal irises
  • Custom image bokeh shapes
  • Zone Control for separate blur regions
  • Edge handling to limit halos
