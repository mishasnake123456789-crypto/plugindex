What 🔖Geodroplets Plus: Ultimate Droplet Toolkit does

GeoDroplets Plus is a Lazy3D droplet toolkit for Blender that adds believable droplets to surfaces three ways: simulated, placed statically, or drawn directly. Long droplets, procedurally generated droplet materials and foam generators round out the set.

Simulated droplets move with physically accurate water behavior and a dripping effect, while static ones keep their form for stills and product renders. Long droplets can be simulated or drawn to represent drips and flowing water. It helps with condensation, rain-covered glass and beverage shots.

Key features

  • Droplet simulation with dripping
  • Static droplets for stills and product shots
  • Draw droplets directly on objects
  • Long droplets for drips and flowing water
  • Procedural droplet materials and foam generators
