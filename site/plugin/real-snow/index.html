Blender add-on that generates a snow mesh and places it on top of selected objects.

What Real Snow does

Real Snow lays a layer of snow over objects in Blender, building the mesh on upward-facing surfaces and exposing settings in the View 3D properties panel.

It targets Blender 4.2 LTS and is listed as unsupported on 4.3 and later. The add-on is roughly 4 KB, needs no special permissions and is released under the GPL v2 or later. Environment artists making winter scenes get an easy way to add snow to terrain, roofs and props.

Key features

  • Generates snow mesh over objects
  • Controls in View 3D properties
  • For Blender 4.2 LTS only
  • Compact download
