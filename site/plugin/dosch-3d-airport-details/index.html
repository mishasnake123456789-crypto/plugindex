What Dosch 3D: Airport Details does

Dosch 3D: Airport Details provides 70 textured models of items found in and around airports, covering both interior and exterior details for visualization work. A wide range of formats is included: C4D from 8.5, 3DS, 3ds Max, Lightwave, OBJ, Maya, VRML, Softimage XSI, Collada DAE and FBX.

Scaling, materials, textures and colors are fully adjustable, with your own textures and labels supported, and elements can be switched on or off, faded to transparent or animated. The collection serves architectural and transport visualizers who need believable terminal and apron props without modeling them.

Key features

  • 70 detailed airport models
  • Interior and exterior details
  • C4D, 3DS, Max, Lightwave, OBJ, Maya, FBX and more
  • Adjustable scale, materials and colors
  • Elements animatable and transparency-adjustable
