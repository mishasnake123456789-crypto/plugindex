What Ai Depth Modeller does

Ai Depth Modeller, from 3d illusions, builds 3D models in Blender from a picture or while painting in the 2D image editor. Depth maps come from AI that runs on your own machine, and sliders and paint tools then shape the result.

The workflow is short: pick where the image comes from (clipboard, file or a Blender image), hit generate, and the model shows up. Depth, color and 3D bump map textures are created automatically, materials are assigned, and UVs and vertex colors are generated. No tokens are used and no external servers are contacted.

Key features

  • Local AI depth generation inside Blender
  • Image from clipboard, file or Blender image
  • Auto depth, color and bump textures
  • Generates materials, UVs and vertex colors
  • Works as you paint in the image editor
