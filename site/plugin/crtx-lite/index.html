What CRTX Lite does

CRTX Lite is the streamlined edition of the CRTX tube-display simulator, built for artists who want the look without a lot of setup. It runs in After Effects and Premiere Pro and avoids static overlays.

Controls cover scan structure and mask texture, bloom and glow, analog softness that suggests a camera-filmed tube, signal noise with subtle instability and colour smear. The result gives footage an old monitor appearance through a short set of parameters.

Key features

  • Scan structure and CRT mask texture
  • Bloom and glow
  • Analog softness for a tube look
  • Signal noise and subtle instability
  • Colour smear; no static overlays
