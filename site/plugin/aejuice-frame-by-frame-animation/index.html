What AEJuice Frame by Frame Animation does

AEJuice Frame by Frame Animation is an introductory course taught by traditional animator Johannes Fast. It runs about two hours and teaches the classic hand-drawn workflow using Adobe Animate and After Effects.

The lessons go through the 12 principles of animation, then apply them to a bouncing ball, fire, smoke, sparkle, a logo and a walk cycle. Workflow tips are included, along with source files from each lesson and access to the designs used. It suits newcomers with little or no animation experience.

Key features

  • Covers the 12 principles of animation
  • Bouncing ball, fire, smoke and sparkle lessons
  • Logo and walk cycle animation
  • Workflow tips for professionals
  • Lesson source files and designs included
