What INSYDIUM Fused Annual Subscription does

INSYDIUM Fused Annual Subscription gives Cinema 4D artists the whole INSYDIUM collection under a single yearly license. It spans X-Particles, NeXus, Taiao, Cycles 4D, MeshTools and TerraformFX, covering GPU-accelerated effects, plant animation, terrains, modeling and final rendering.

X-Particles brings a rule-based system for cloth, smoke, fire, fluids, grains and dynamics, while ExplosiaFX creates smoke and fire that can be exported as VDB volumes. xpFluidFLIP adds a fluid solver. Motion designers and VFX artists who rely on several INSYDIUM tools can license them all together instead of buying individually.
