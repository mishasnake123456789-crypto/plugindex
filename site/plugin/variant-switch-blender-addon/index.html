Blender add-on for switching between collections and material variants with hotkeys.

What Variant Switch Blender Addon does

Variant Switch helps users compare design options and present them. It handles both collections and materials, with grouping, looping and assignable hotkeys.

The Collection Switcher toggles viewport and render visibility and can show one collection at a time. The Material Switcher puts objects into groups, applies materials and loops through them. Hotkeys are validated for conflicts, missing items trigger warnings, hotkey lists export to .txt, and group rows can collapse to save space.

Key features

  • Collection switcher with one-at-a-time mode
  • Material switcher with groups and looping
  • Hotkey validation and export
  • Collapsible groups
