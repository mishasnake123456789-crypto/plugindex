What Fast Rigid Body does

Fast Rigid Body simplifies physics setup. Two clicks apply Rigid Body physics, with separate Active and Passive panels for the objects involved.

It comes in two packs: Lite holds the add-on, while Full adds a Marble Machine demo scene. Documentation is included and the listed support covers Blender 2.81 to 2.9. It suits quick dynamics for falling, colliding and stacking objects. Notable points: rigid Body in two clicks, active and Passive panels and lite and Full packs.
