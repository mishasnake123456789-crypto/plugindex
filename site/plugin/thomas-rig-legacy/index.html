What Thomas Rig Legacy does

Thomas Rig Legacy, by BlueEvilGFX, is an add-on made to go with a specific character rig, adding the interface and tools it expects. It is tagged under Animation and Rigging.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
