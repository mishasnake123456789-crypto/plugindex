What Mesh Tweaker does

Mesh Tweaker pairs two small mesh editing utilities in Blender: aligning vertices along an axis and triangulating faces with a choice of center.

Vertices align along X, Y or Z based on the average position or a value you set. The triangulation tool handles selected faces including n-gons, with Centroid, Face Center and Incenter center types. The Incenter option computes a weighted center position for more balanced triangles. Both tools stay out of the way until needed, which suits quick cleanup passes on hard-surface or low-poly models.

Key features

  • Align vertices along X, Y or Z
  • Average or custom alignment value
  • Triangulate faces including n-gons
  • Centroid, Face Center and Incenter options
  • Lightweight add-on
