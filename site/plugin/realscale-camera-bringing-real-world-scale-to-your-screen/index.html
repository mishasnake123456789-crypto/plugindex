What Realscale Camera: Bringing Real World Scale To Your Screen does

RealScale Camera helps Blender artists who model miniatures and figures for 3D printing see their work at true physical size on screen. It does this by using a standard credit card as a known reference object.

You create a RealScale camera, toggle it active, show or hide the card reference, and nudge the camera position until the on-screen card matches the real one. Calibration can be stored and restored later. Operators are also available from a Ctrl+Shift+R pie menu, and documentation is included.

Key features

  • Creates and removes a RealScale camera setup
  • Credit card reference object toggle
  • Fine position adjustment for calibration
  • Store and restore calibration, pie menu access
