What Reprojector Add-On does

Reprojector transfers mesh detail onto another surface through reprojection, aiming to eliminate the spiking and tearing that often occur with shrinkwrap. It is a Geometry Nodes utility operated from a sidebar panel.

The projection logic is tuned for tricky spots such as ears and fingers. Dynamic Mesh Control changes resolution live, vertex masking limits the projection zones, and vertex colors and textures can be carried over. Clean attributes are output for baking and rendering. It suits retopology and sculpt-transfer workflows.

Key features

  • Reprojection tuned for ears and fingers
  • Live resolution adjustment
  • Vertex masking of projection zones
  • Transfers vertex colors and textures
  • Clean attribute output for baking
