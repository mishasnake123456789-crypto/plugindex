What Mesh XR does

Mesh XR, by DedZedOffishal, brings VR into Blender modeling, with mesh editing, materials, textures and UV mapping available while wearing a headset. It is listed under 3D View, Mesh and User Interface.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
