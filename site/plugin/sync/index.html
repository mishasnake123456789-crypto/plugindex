Generates AI lip-sync for audio inside Premiere Pro, for animated characters, avatars and dialogue content.

What sync does

The sync. plugin adds AI lipsync to Premiere Pro. Select audio, choose settings and generate lip movements without leaving the timeline, which suits animated characters, virtual avatars and dialogue-heavy videos.

Image-to-video lets you drop a still into the visual slot and lipsync it to audio, with a preview before submitting. The panel reads clip duration, frame rate, resolution and a first-frame thumbnail, handles ProRes metadata, has an offline mode, and supports dubbing.

Key features

  • AI lipsync from selected audio
  • Image-to-video lipsync with preview
  • Reads clip duration, frame rate and resolution
  • Offline mode with disabled submit
  • Dubbing support
