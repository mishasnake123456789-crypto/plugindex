What AEJuice NFT Constructor does

AEJuice NFT Constructor supplies ready-made scenes for presenting NFT artwork in After Effects and Premiere Pro. You drop in your own media files and swap text and images, then export finished videos.

It is made entirely in After Effects, resizes to any resolution, and is managed through the AEJuice Pack Manager with quick previews, drop-in import and favourites. Output works for Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and Snapchat, and downloads are fast. Creators with limited After Effects experience can still produce a polished collection showcase.

  • Auto-resize to any resolution
