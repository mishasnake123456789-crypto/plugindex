What Product Visualizer Pro does

Product Visualizer Pro walks through the product-shot workflow. Smart analysis detects material properties such as metallic and transparent, geometry complexity, size, orientation and surface brightness, then suggests a setup.

Studio lighting is offered as presets or manual control, and camera placement, backgrounds and quality-based render settings are covered too. Designers and sellers can create clean product images without lighting expertise. Its recommendations are meant to save trial and error with exposure, lighting and material behavior on shiny or transparent products.

Key features

  • Smart analysis of materials and geometry
  • Preset or custom studio lighting
  • Camera positioning and backgrounds
  • Render quality presets
