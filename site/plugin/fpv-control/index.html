What Fpv Control does

FPV Control brings real-time drone camera animation to Blender. Fly with a game controller and the motion is recorded straight to editable keyframes.

It runs a flight simulation with collision physics and supports DJI, TBS, Radiomaster, Xbox, PlayStation and USB gamepads. Stick tuning covers expo, sensitivity and smoothing, and post-flight smoothing polishes the path for cinematic camera movement. It is a fast route to dynamic fly-through shots. Day to day, that covers wide controller support, flight simulation with collision physics and direct recording to keyframes.

Key features

  • Wide controller support
  • Flight simulation with collision physics
  • Direct recording to keyframes
  • Expo, sensitivity and smoothing tuning
  • Post-flight smoothing
