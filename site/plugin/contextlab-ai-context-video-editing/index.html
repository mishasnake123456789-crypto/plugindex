An AI plugin for Premiere Pro that reads a video's speech and structure to ease feedback, subtitles and trimming.

What ContextLab: AI Context Video Editing does

ContextLab analyzes narrative, speech and structure in a video and uses it to offer contextual suggestions. Feedback Flow imports DOCX, TXT and CSV files, as well as image feedback via OCR, groups comments by meaning and places timeline markers, creating a new version for each round.

Narrative Assist builds contextual subtitles with configurable density and SRT or text output. Story-driven clip detection trims filler and pauses, and output can be set to 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

Key features

  • Feedback import from DOCX, TXT, CSV and screenshots
  • Comments clustered into timeline markers
  • Versioned iterative editing
  • Contextual subtitle generation
  • Filler trimming and platform-ready aspect ratios
