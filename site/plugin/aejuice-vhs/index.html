A VHS look pack that scales to any resolution up to 4K, with After Effects project, plugin and Premiere Pro MOGRT files.

What AEJuice VHS does

AEJuice VHS recreates the wobble, noise and colour bleed of tape playback. It was built entirely in After Effects and resizes automatically for any frame up to 4K.

The After Effects version includes project and plugin files (378 MB) at 25 fps, while the Premiere Pro version provides plugin files and MOGRTs (2.1 GB), and DaVinci Resolve is supported too. The Pack Manager offers quick previews, drop-in import and favourites. A floating licence can be used on two computers by logging out and in again. Editors after a camcorder or home-video look can apply it quickly.

Key features
