What CutCue.io CSV Marker Importer does

CutCue.io CSV Marker Importer connects the cutcue.io analysis service to Premiere Pro. The service scans raw footage for interesting moments, and this UXP plugin brings those results onto your sequence as markers before you start cutting.

Markers keep their color coding, so different kinds of moments stay easy to tell apart on the timeline. Flagged points can include Twitch chat spikes, viral hooks, high-engagement stretches and segments that might risk monetization. Streamers' editors and content teams who already use cutcue.io can skip manual logging and begin editing with the cut points visible.

Key features

  • Imports cutcue.io markers into the timeline
  • Adds them as sequence markers
  • Keeps marker colors
  • Built as a UXP plugin
