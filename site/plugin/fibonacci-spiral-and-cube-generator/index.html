What Fibonacci Spiral & Cube Generator does

The Fibonacci Spiral & Cube Generator builds geometry based on the Fibonacci sequence in Blender: spirals, cubes and arcs.

Size, scale and rotation can all be changed, and the results connect to Blender's physics tools for spiral-driven simulations, fluid flows and dynamic interactions. The proportions suit animations and architectural designs. The add-on is MIT licensed and has documentation. Artists seeking distinctive mathematical forms will find it a quick starting point.
