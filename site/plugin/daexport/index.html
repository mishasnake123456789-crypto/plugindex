What Daexport does

DaeXport restores simple Collada export in current Blender versions, including Blender 5 and later. It converts a multi-object scene into one clean .dae file in a single click.

Textures are baked automatically with Cycles at sizes up to 8192, and clean UVs are generated and packed during export. Geometry cleanup can be applied before writing. Temporary copies keep the workflow non-destructive, leaving your originals untouched. It suits pipelines that still rely on the DAE format.

Key features

  • One-click single-file DAE export
  • Cycles texture baking up to 8192
  • UV generation and packing
  • Optional geometry cleanup
  • Non-destructive temporary copies
