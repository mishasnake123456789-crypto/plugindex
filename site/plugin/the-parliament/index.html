What The Parliament does

The Parliament is a tool by Hasan Atallah for creating parliament-style diagrams inside After Effects. Election coverage, news graphics and infographic work often need these seat charts, and building one manually is tedious.

With a single click you get a full layout, then adjust colours, sizes, animation and styling to match your brand or presentation. The interface is aimed at both beginners and experienced users, and tutorials are included. It works with recent versions of Adobe After Effects.

Key features

  • One-click parliament representation
  • Adjustable colours and sizes
  • Animation and style options
