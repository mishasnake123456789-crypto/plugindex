What Video Copilot Color Vibrance (Free) does

Color Vibrance is a free Video Copilot plug-in for adding vivid, hot colorization to footage in After Effects. It also has matte tools that help make alpha channels from stock elements.

Unmultiply creates alpha, it works on clips that are already pre-matted, and it avoids the washed-out edges that can appear when masks are feathered. It supports After Effects CS5 through 2022 including multi-frame rendering, on PC and Mac (OS X 10.7 and above).

Key features

  • Vivid colorization with simple controls
  • Alpha channel creation via unmultiply
  • Works on pre-matted clips
  • Avoids faded colors with feathered masks
  • Supports CS5 to 2022 including MFR
