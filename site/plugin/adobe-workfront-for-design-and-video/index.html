What Adobe Workfront for design and video does

This Adobe extension ties creative work in After Effects and Premiere Pro to Workfront, the company's work management platform. Designers and editors can follow a job from request to approval without leaving Creative Cloud.

Inside the app you can see and manage assignments, request and receive feedback, deliver finished assets, upload files and log time. Stakeholders, meanwhile, comment on and approve work in Workfront itself. It can be installed from the Adobe Marketplace or rolled out as a package by an administrator through the Admin Console, and it needs After Effects or Premiere Pro 22.6 or newer.

Key features

  • Manage assignments inside Creative Cloud
  • Request feedback and deliver assets
  • Upload files and log time
  • Reviewers approve from Workfront
  • Install via Marketplace or Admin Console
