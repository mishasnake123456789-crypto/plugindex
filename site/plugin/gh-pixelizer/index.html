Creates retro pixelated textures and animations in Blender with real-time dithering and PBR baking.

What Gh Pixelizer does

GH Pixelizer gives textures and footage a retro pixel look. Dithering masks and adjustable parameters update in real time in the viewport, and the result can be baked into PBR textures.

It can also stylize video, photos and image sequences, with alpha support, through a node you add to the compositor. Animated dithering is included. It suits pixel-art games and nostalgic animation styles. Highlights include real-time dithering masks, PBR texture baking and stylizes video, photos and sequences.

Key features

  • Real-time dithering masks
  • PBR texture baking
  • Stylizes video, photos and sequences
  • Compositor node
