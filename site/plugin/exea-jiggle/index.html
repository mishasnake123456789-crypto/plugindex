What EXea Jiggle does

EXea Jiggle by Axnise gives bones a springy, secondary motion in real time, handy for hair, tails, cloth bits and soft body parts on a character rig. The emphasis is on keeping the setup effortless. It is tagged under Animation and Rigging.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
