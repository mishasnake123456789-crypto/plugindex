What Digital Anarchy Data Storyteller does

Digital Anarchy Data Storyteller brings data visualization into After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and it also supports Final Cut Pro. Two plugins, Charts and Maps, convert Excel or CSV files into animated graphics.

Bar, line, scatter or bubble charts, plus US and world maps, are available. You decide which data appears, how color or size responds, and how elements animate on and off, with transitions and positioning options. Output is vector-based, so it stays sharp from HD up to 12K. News, documentary, corporate and social teams can start from scratch or use a template.

Key features

  • Imports Excel and CSV data
  • Bar, line, scatter/bubble, US map and world map
  • Controls for color, size and animation per data point
  • Vector output scales from HD to 12K
  • Charts and Maps plugins with templates
