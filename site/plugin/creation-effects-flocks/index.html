What Creation Effects: Flocks does

Creation Effects: Flocks creates birds in flight in After Effects. Twelve high-resolution 3D species are included, among them bat, crow, bald eagle, Canadian goose, owl, macaw, pelican, pigeon, seagull, sparrow, starling and vulture.

Each has built-in flight and flocking behaviour, so you only direct where they go using two or more position keyframes, with random behaviour for variety. Appearance, wing flapping, synchronisation and individual randomness are adjustable. Duplicating scales the flock to hundreds or starling murmurations of tens of thousands. Because it is built with 3D layers, it works with lights and cameras and casts shadows. Your own species can be added using transparent images.

Key features

  • 12 high-resolution 3D bird species
  • Built-in flight and flocking behaviour
  • Path control with position keyframes
  • Adjustable flapping, sync and randomness
  • Works with lights, cameras and shadows
