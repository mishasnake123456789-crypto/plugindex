What Sheffield Softworks Vintage does

Sheffield Softworks Vintage reproduces historic colour film using maths that recreates the conditions of each original process. It runs in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro through Noise Industries FxFactory on Apple Silicon and Intel.

Technicolor 3 Strip records red, green and blue for the full spectrum, Technicolor 2 Strip records only red and green images, and Multicolor records red and blue. Each process has normal and quick modes, with quick being faster but without adjustable settings. Editors and colourists seeking authentic period palettes for 1920s to 1950s styles have a direct route.

Key features

  • Technicolor 3 Strip emulation
  • Technicolor 2 Strip emulation
  • Multicolor emulation
  • Normal and quick modes
  • Runs via FxFactory on Apple Silicon and Intel
