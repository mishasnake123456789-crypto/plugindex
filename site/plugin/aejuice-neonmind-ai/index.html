What AEJuice NeonMind AI does

AEJuice NeonMind AI connects After Effects with Stable Diffusion, guiding the process from installation to turning text descriptions into artwork. You do not need deep knowledge of Stable Diffusion to use it.

System needs are notable: Linux, Windows 7 through 11 or later, or Apple Silicon Macs. It requires an NVIDIA GPU (GTX 7xx or newer with at least 2 GB VRAM), 16 GB of RAM and 10 GB of disk space, with models included. AMD graphics are not supported. It suits artists wanting generated imagery inside their compositing environment.

Key features

  • Stable Diffusion inside After Effects
  • Text descriptions to artwork
  • Guided installation
  • Models included
  • NVIDIA GPU required, 16GB RAM
