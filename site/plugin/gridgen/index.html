A procedural grid generator for After Effects with themes, presets and vector export.

What GridGen does

GridGen creates generative grid designs inside After Effects, from tidy layouts to abstract patterns. More than sixteen built-in generative tools provide the starting points, while over two hundred color themes keep palettes consistent.

Parameters such as balance, contrast and symmetry can be adjusted, and master controllers cover layout, color, animation and 3D extrusion. There are sixty-plus presets for effects, animation and Element 3D, batch generation, and custom presets. Output can be exported at 4K or as SVG or AI files, handy for motion designers and graphic artists.

Key features

  • 16+ generative tools
  • 200+ curated color themes
  • 60+ presets including Element 3D
  • Batch generation and custom presets
  • Export to 4K, SVG or AI
