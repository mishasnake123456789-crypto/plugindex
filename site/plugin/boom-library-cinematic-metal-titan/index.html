What BOOM Library Cinematic Metal - Titan does

Cinematic Metal - Titan is a BOOM Library collection of heavy metallic sound effects for trailers, action scenes, game cinematics and effects work. It can be used in Premiere Pro as ordinary WAV files.

The Construction Kit offers 700 files and 4,200 sounds in 10.9 GB, supplying individual components for layering. The Designed edition has 100 files and 400 sounds in 1.5 GB, already processed and ready to use. A bundle combines both for 800 files, 4,600 sounds and 12.4 GB. Material includes hits, braams, booms, impacts and stingers recorded at 24-bit, 96 kHz.

Key features

  • Hits, braams, booms, impacts and stingers
  • Construction Kit: 700 files, 4,200 sounds
  • Designed edition: 100 files, 400 sounds
  • Bundle of both editions at 12.4 GB
