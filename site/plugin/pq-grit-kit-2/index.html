What PQ Grit Kit 2 does

PQ Grit Kit 2 from Peter Quinn is a texture library for After Effects and Premiere Pro, also usable in Nuke. It contains more than 160 animated textures photographed up close in real life, delivered in high resolution and royalty free.

They are intended for blending: lay one over footage to add genuine noise or grunge, or use it as a luma matte to texture type, shape layers and other elements. The textures can also drive displacement in Cinema 4D. Designers who find digital noise too clean can get organic, irregular detail quickly, without shooting their own material.

Key features

  • 160+ animated macro-photographed textures
  • Royalty-free, high-resolution
  • Blend over footage for noise and grunge
  • Use as luma mattes for type and shapes
  • Drive displacement in Cinema 4D
