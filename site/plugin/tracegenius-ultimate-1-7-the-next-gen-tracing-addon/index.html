What Tracegenius Ultimate 1.7 - The Next-Gen Tracing Addon does

TraceGenius Ultimate, from Russel Studios, is a tracing add-on for Blender that is powered by its own tracing engine and does not use Blender's Grease Pencil tracer. It converts images to vector-style geometry with more control over what gets traced.

A color picker selects the exact colors to trace, a preview shows which regions will be traced under the current settings, and a transparency map option sets whether see-through PNG pixels count as black or white. Contrast adjustment helps prepare difficult images. It suits logos, line art and artwork you want to bring in as curves.

Key features

  • Custom tracing engine independent of Grease Pencil
  • Color picker for precise color tracing
  • Pre-trace image preview
  • Transparency map support for PNGs
  • Contrast adjustment
