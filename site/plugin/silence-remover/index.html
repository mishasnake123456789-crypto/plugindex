A Premiere Pro plugin from Motion FX that finds silent gaps in audio and can remove them.

What Silence Remover does

Silence Remover automates one of the most tedious parts of dialogue editing. It isolates silent gaps in footage and can strip them out directly.

You drag in or import audio, which stays loaded until replaced, pick the track and selection behavior, and choose which channel to analyze. The wave view zooms, with in and out offsets separated, and clearer audio makes detection more accurate. Useful for interviews, vlogs and podcasts.

Key features

  • Isolates silence gaps
  • Option to remove silence directly
  • Track and selection options
  • MultiChannel selection
  • Zoomable wave view
