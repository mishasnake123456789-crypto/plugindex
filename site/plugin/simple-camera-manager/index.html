What Simple Camera Manager does

Simple Camera Manager collects all cameras into a single panel in the 3D View. The list can be sorted by active first, collection, focal length, render slot, resolution or background image, and filtered to visible-only or render-selected cameras.

It supports batch rendering of multiple static cameras with different settings, showing progress in the render view, along with presets and per-camera resolutions. It suits scenes with many viewpoints, such as architecture or product visualization.

Key features

  • Sortable and filterable camera list
  • Batch rendering of several cameras
  • Camera presets
  • Per-camera resolution
