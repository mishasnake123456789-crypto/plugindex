What PQ PostPerfect does

PQ PostPerfect, by Peter Quinn, is a utility for editors preparing videos for social platforms. Its purpose is to preview the precise perimeters of a platform's frame while you work, so important content isn't placed where it will be cut off or covered.

It is listed for After Effects and Premiere Pro, with stated compatibility for Premiere Pro 2025 and 2024; 2024 support came in version 1.1. A demo video is available on YouTube.
