What C4DToMax does

C4DToMax by 3DtoAll sends a Cinema 4D scene into 3ds Max in two clicks, taking care of the usual import and export problems automatically. Models, animation and materials all come along.

Scenes containing V-Ray materials or lights are supported, with automatic conversion from Cinema 4D's V-Ray to V-Ray for 3ds Max using similar materials and settings. Studios that model or animate in one package and light and render in the other can avoid rebuilding assets by hand when a project crosses between them.

Key features

  • Two-click Cinema 4D to 3ds Max transfer
  • Handles common import/export issues
  • Transfers V-Ray materials and lights
  • Converts Cinema 4D V-Ray to 3ds Max V-Ray
  • Carries models, animation and materials
