What BOOM Library Seasons of Earth Autumn - Stereo does

BOOM Library Seasons of Earth Autumn (Stereo) presents 103 royalty-free recordings of autumn atmospheres, totaling more than 18 GB. They were captured at 96 kHz, 24-bit with the Schoeps ORTF3D Surround Set and are delivered here in regular stereo.

Soundscapes include rustling leaves, wind, forest ambience and wildlife, and the recordings avoid human noise and air traffic. PDF and XLS documentation comes with the files. The library works in After Effects, Premiere Pro and other audio and video tools, adding a natural seasonal bed to film, game or nature documentary projects.
