Blender camera animation system that records live movement, extracts editable paths and builds adjustable camera rigs.

What AutoCam does

AutoCam captures camera moves in real time in Blender, then turns them into clean tools. You fly the camera with WASD and mouse controls, record the point of view, and extract the motion as editable Bezier, Poly or NURBS curves in a single click.

A one-click rig builder adds tracking, damping and target controls, and Rig Mode switches per shot between native Blender constraints and AutoCam's real-time evaluator. Speed is keyframable with positive, zero or negative values to play, pause, ramp or reverse, motion can be baked to plain keyframes, and cleanup is one click. It suits archviz walkthroughs and product animations.

Key features

  • Real-time POV recording with WASD and mouse
  • One-click path extraction to Bezier, Poly or NURBS
  • Rig builder with tracking, damping and targets
  • Simple and Dynamic rig modes per shot
  • One-click bake to keyframes and cleanup
