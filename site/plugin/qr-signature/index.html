Blender add-on that stamps a QR code signature on finished renders.

What Qr Signature does

QR Signature adds a scannable code to your renders so viewers can reach your details. The QR can hold technical information, client details, contact info or a link such as a portfolio page.

Once enabled, the code is added to the render automatically. Location, indent and color can be adjusted for readability, and controls are in the N-panel under the QR Signature tab. It is GPL licensed with documentation.

Key features

  • QR code added to finished renders
  • Holds links, contact or technical data
  • Adjustable location, indent and color
  • N-panel QR Signature tab
