What Easycam does

EasyCam brings Blender's camera settings together in one quick-access panel. It is useful for photographers, animators and visualization artists who work with several cameras and tweak them often.

Sections cover focal length, lens shift and clipping, aperture with F-stop, blades and ratio, and depth of field with a real-time focus plane that adapts to FOV and aspect ratio. Cycles users also get an Enhance Bokeh option. Motion blur, composition guides, color management with white balance on Blender 4.3+, output settings and FFMPEG options are included, along with per-camera position saves and a camera switcher.

Key features

  • Camera Quick Box popup with grouped sections
  • Aperture, DOF and a live focus plane
  • Cycles-only Enhance Bokeh
  • Per-camera position saves and switcher
