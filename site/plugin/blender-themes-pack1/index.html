What Blender Themes Pack1 does

Blender Themes Pack1 changes the look of Blender's interface with five ready-made presets. The set includes Zion, Limon, NeonBlue, Zion Clean and BlenderClear, offering alternatives to the default colors for those who work long hours in the application.

The themes are listed as compatible with Blender 2.8 through 5.1, are distributed under the GPL, and come with documentation. They suit anyone who wants a fresher or easier-on-the-eyes workspace without designing a theme by hand.
