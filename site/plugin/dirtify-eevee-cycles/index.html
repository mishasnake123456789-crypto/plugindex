Blender add-on that adds ambient-occlusion-based dirt, edge wear and weathering to models in Eevee or Cycles.

What Dirtify (Eevee/Cycles) does

Dirtify gives models a lived-in look in Blender. It uses ambient occlusion to find crevices and contact areas where grime would gather, then adds dirt and edge wear on top.

Intensity is adjustable from subtle to heavy, and additional effects include rust, grunge and paint chipping. The dirt is added as a separate layer, so original materials are preserved. It works in Eevee and Cycles; version 2.0 supports Blender 4.0 and newer, with earlier releases covering 3.0 through 3.6. Documentation is included.

Key features

  • Ambient occlusion driven dirt
  • Adjustable intensity
  • Rust, grunge and paint chipping effects
  • Non-destructive layer over original materials
  • Works in Eevee and Cycles
