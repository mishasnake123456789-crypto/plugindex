What Texture Wrangler does

Texture Wrangler, by Cowwolfe3D, tidies up texture sets by renaming maps and organizing them into one folder per material. It is tagged under Material, Import-Export and Pipeline.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
