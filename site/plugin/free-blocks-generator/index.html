What Free Blocks Generator does

Free Blocks Generator rebuilds objects in Blender as blocky voxel shapes. Choose the block size and resolution and the original geometry turns into a grid of cubes, giving a Minecraft-like look to nearly anything in your scene.

Block colors are sampled from the object's materials or textures, and the whole process runs on modifiers so it stays non-destructive and can be adjusted later. Characters, props, terrain and more can be converted, which suits stylized renders, voxel art and Minecraft-style mashups where a consistent blocky style is the goal.

Key features

  • Converts objects into block geometry
  • Custom block size and resolution
  • Color sampling from materials or textures
  • Non-destructive modifier workflow
  • Works on characters, props and terrain
