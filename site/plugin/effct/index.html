Analyzes video in Premiere Pro and places a generated sound design of ambiences, effects and transitions on new tracks.

What Effct does

Effct builds a sound design to match your edit. It studies the video and then lays AI-matched ambiences, spot effects, impacts, risers, sub drops and transitions onto the timeline in sync.

Set in and out points to process only part of a sequence, regenerate any clip with one click, or describe a new sound and have Effct find and place it. Mood presets and layer controls are available on Creator plans and above. Everything goes on new tracks, so existing audio remains untouched.

Key features

  • Generates ambiences, SFX, impacts, risers and transitions
  • Process only the in/out range
  • One-click regeneration of any clip
  • Add sounds by describing them
  • Original audio left intact on separate tracks
