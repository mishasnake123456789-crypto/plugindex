What ChromaDrift does

ChromaDrift is an Nx Color DCTL that simulates the color fringing and warping of imperfect lenses in DaVinci Resolve. Splitting color channels apart and bending the image gives clean digital footage a more organic or glitchy character.

It is useful for music videos, title sequences and stylized scenes where a touch of lens imperfection or a heavy distortion effect adds energy. The DCTL runs on macOS, Windows and Linux and works with DaVinci Resolve 20 and 19, so it can be shared between machines in a mixed studio without any changes to the project.
