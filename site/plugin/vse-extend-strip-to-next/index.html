What Vse Extend Strip To Next does

VSE Extend Strip to Next closes gaps in Blender's Video Sequence Editor. It extends the active strip up to where the next strip in its channel begins.

It finds the closest following strip automatically and works with video, audio and other strip types. A quick editing shortcut. This avoids dragging strip edges manually, which is particularly useful in longer timelines. It is helpful when re-timing cuts, filling holes in a timeline or closing space after removing a clip.

Key features

  • Ctrl+Shift+X shortcut
  • Detects the next strip automatically
  • Works with all strip types
