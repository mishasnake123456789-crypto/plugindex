What Video Copilot Heat Distortion does

Heat Distortion from Video Copilot simulates the wavering air you see above hot asphalt, engines and explosions. It is useful for adding atmosphere to a scene or selling a hot environment in a composite.

Under the hood it produces shimmering noise, a hazy wobble and warping of the picture, and detailed noise controls shape how the air moves and flickers over time. Version 1.0.32 is natively compatible with Apple M1 machines and is ready for After Effects 2022.

Key features

  • Realistic heat noise
  • Heat haze and image distortion
  • Advanced noise controls
  • Native Apple M1 compatibility in 1.0.32
  • Ready for After Effects 2022
