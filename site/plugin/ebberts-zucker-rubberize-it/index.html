What Ebberts + Zucker Rubberize It does

Rubberize It! by Ebberts + Zucker gives layers squishy, reactive movement. It works on type, stills, video, shapes, vectors and any layer that accepts puppet pins, responding to motion without any keyframing.

Eight material presets ship with it: Rubber, Plastic, Goop, Vinyl, Sponge, Blubber, Putty and Jelly. Sliders create custom materials and materials can be combined, Chaos adds randomness, and formulas offer two reaction types. A Clean button deletes expressions from selected pins, Smart Rubberize helps setup, and Bake speeds preview and rendering. Animators wanting squash-and-stretch feel can apply it quickly.

Key features

  • Eight material presets from Rubber to Jelly
  • Custom materials and combinations
  • Chaos randomisation
  • Smart Rubberize and Clean buttons
  • Bake function for faster preview
