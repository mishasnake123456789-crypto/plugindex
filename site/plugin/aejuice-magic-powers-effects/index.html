What AEJuice Magic Powers Effects does

AEJuice Magic Powers Effects is a set of fantasy energy animations, from glowing bursts to spell-like auras. The 80 clips are split over five categories and are delivered at 3840x2160 in square and horizontal orientations.

Files are MOV ProRes 4444 with RGB and alpha channels, and a black background is available for easy blending. A standalone Pack Manager runs independently of any editor, with an interface in nine languages. Editors in After Effects, Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve can drag an effect straight onto a clip to add superhero or wizard moments to their video.

Key features

  • 80 animations in 5 categories
  • 4K, square and horizontal
  • ProRes 4444 with alpha and black background
