What Pattern Engine does

Pattern Engine produces and edits parametric 3D geometry in 3ds Max, from decorative patterns and 3D tiles to partitions, shelves, wall art and upholstery. Patterns fit any flat closed shape and can be laid out in Grid, Radial or Random array modes.

More than 40 primitive shapes serve as pattern items, with over 200 presets in several categories. Weight, Random and Expression distribution modes control each item, and per-item parameters cover depth, angle, scale, slope and visibility. Gradient, Random, Expression and Shape effects can be blended. Concave, Outline and Bumpy Convex modes add variety, the last giving a pillow effect. Version 1.4 added custom shapes, text characters and pattern picking.

Key features

  • Grid, Radial and Random array modes
  • 40+ primitive shapes and 200+ presets
  • Weight, Random and Expression distribution
  • Gradient, Random, Expression and Shape effects with blending
  • Bumpy Convex mode for pillow-style upholstery
