What Moss It does

Moss It adds moss growth to objects in Blender, giving rocks, walls and trees an aged, natural look. Both Cycles and Eevee render it correctly, and it installs as a Blender extension.

Blender 4.0 up to 5.0 is supported, includes documentation and is distributed under the GPL license. Artists building forest, ruin or garden environments can add organic detail faster than modeling or painting moss manually.

Key features
