What Voxelizer Pro - Mesh Voxelization does

Voxelizer Pro turns any mesh into voxels for low-poly game assets, stylized renders and Minecraft-style content.

Only outer voxels are produced, and adjacent blocks are merged with multi-axis greedy meshing. Recursive detail fill adds half, quarter and eighth-size voxels up to three levels, resolution runs from 5 to 200 units along the longest axis, and presets cover Low, Medium and High. A sidebar panel provides sliders and warnings, with BVH-accelerated queries.

Key features

  • Surface-only voxelization
  • Greedy meshing to merge blocks
  • Recursive detail fill up to three levels
  • Resolution presets and real-time feedback
