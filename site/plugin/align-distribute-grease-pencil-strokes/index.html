Blender add-on that aligns or evenly distributes selected Grease Pencil strokes along X or Z.

What Align-Distribute Grease Pencil Strokes does

Align-Distribute Grease Pencil Strokes brings layout tools familiar from vector editors to Blender's 2D drawing. Selected strokes can be aligned to their minimum, center or maximum along the X or Z axis.

Distribution spreads strokes at equal intervals measured between centers or between bounding boxes. Controls sit in the Align-Distribute tab of the 3D View sidebar, which also shows the number of selected strokes and checks compatibility with the active object. Version 2.1.0 touches only strokes on visible and unlocked layers and is faster.

Key features

  • Align to min, center or max
  • Distribute by centers or bounding boxes
  • X and Z axis options
  • Live selected stroke count
  • Only affects visible, unlocked layers
