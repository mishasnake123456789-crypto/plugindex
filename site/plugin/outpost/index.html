What Outpost does

Outpost, from Studio Network Solutions, connects Premiere Pro to a review process. Media is sent straight from the editor to the people who need to approve it, with no manual export and upload step.

When reviewers leave feedback, their comments appear on the editor's own timeline, where they can be answered and acted on without switching windows. The requested edits are made in the same session, keeping review and approval inside the editing environment. It suits editors and producers who want a tighter loop between cutting and client or team sign-off.

Key features

  • Send media directly to the review team
  • Reviewer comments shown on the timeline
  • Reply to comments inside Premiere Pro
  • Make requested changes without leaving the app
