What Tube2Curve - Convert Tube/Pipe Geo Back To Curves With 1 Click does

Tube2Curve reverses a common modeling step in Blender: turning tube-shaped geometry back into editable curves. Select the tubes and it draws a curve or edge down the center of each, which helps when you only have the finished pipe mesh.

It works on open tubes and on tubes capped with ngons or triangles, handles cylindrical geometry in general, and can even turn a tree mesh into curves. A bonus tool separates curves by loose parts, which vanilla Blender does not offer. Tools sit under 3D View, Object, TubeTools, and in the N-panel. Cable, pipe and tree modelers benefit.

Key features

  • One-click conversion of tubes to center curves
  • Open and Ngon or triangle capped tubes
  • Can convert a tree mesh to curves
  • Separate curves by loose parts
