What Mimir for APAC region does

Mimir for APAC region ties After Effects and Premiere Pro to assets held in a Mimir system, a service from Mjoll. Editors can look through the library and bring in what they need without leaving the host application.

Files can be imported as proxies or at full resolution, and captions that Mimir generates through its transcription service can be loaded into Premiere Pro. Sequences cut with proxies can be rendered centrally into high-resolution files. The platform handles MAM, PAM, DAM and GAM asset management, so media teams with large collections get a single connection.

Key features

  • Search and import Mimir-managed media
  • Proxy or full resolution import
  • Imports Mimir transcription captions into Premiere Pro
  • Central render from proxy-edited sequences
  • MAM, PAM, DAM and GAM coverage
