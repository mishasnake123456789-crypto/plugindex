What AEPathfinder does

AEPathfinder from PENCIL Park brings Illustrator-style Pathfinder operations to shape layers in After Effects. One click performs Outline, Divide, Intersect, Trim, Subtract or Exclude on one or several layers.

The workflow is non-destructive: it generates new shape layers and uses no expressions. A single selection reaches the built-in merge options, while multiple selections create a new layer and keep the sources. The Split shapes feature separates results into individual elements for animation, and holding Shift, Alt or Cmd/Ctrl removes the originals.

Key features

  • Outline, Divide, Intersect, Trim, Subtract and Exclude
  • Non-destructive, no expressions
  • Works on single or multiple shape layers
  • Split results into individual elements
  • Modifier keys remove original layers
