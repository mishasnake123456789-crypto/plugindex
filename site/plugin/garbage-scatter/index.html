What Garbage Scatter does

Garbage Scatter is a Blender plugin from EdenWeby 3D for dirtying up environments. Instead of placing litter by hand, you use the Paint Trash tool, found in weight paint view, to brush trash onto any surface.

Four categories and nine assets are included, covering bottles, cans, paper and debris, with more promised in updates. Multiple layers of trash are supported, and configuration is simple. The low-poly assets keep scenes light, which suits games and large urban or post-apocalyptic scenes.

Key features

  • Paint trash with a weight paint style tool
  • 4 categories and 9 included assets
  • Multiple trash layers
  • Low-poly assets
