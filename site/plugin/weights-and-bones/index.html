Blender weight painting and rigging add-on featuring fast weight mirroring that tolerates asymmetrical meshes.

What Weights And Bones does

Weights and Bones, by Makatopia, centers on mirroring weight paint quickly in Blender, even when the mesh is not perfectly symmetrical. This removes a common obstacle when characters have slight differences from side to side.

A redesigned Mirror Weights panel works with any naming convention, including suffixes and prefixes, and the add-on also provides bone parenting and brush helpers. Riggers can skin one side and copy the result over without rebuilding the weights.

Key features

  • Asymmetric weight-paint mirroring
  • Mirror Weights panel for any naming convention
  • Bone parenting tools
  • Brush tools for weight painting
