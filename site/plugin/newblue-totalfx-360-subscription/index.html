What NewBlue TotalFX 360 Subscription does

NewBlue TotalFX 360 is a subscription toolbox for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. It gathers 177 plugins and more than 1,500 presets so editors can find most finishing tools in a single purchase.

Titler Pro makes 3D animated titles, lower thirds and overlays, and Stabilizer analyses movement with region, strength and amount settings. The transition collection has 69 transitions and 861 presets, while Chroma Key Pro isolates green screens with sensitivity, range and spill controls. There are also 27 colour-enhancing filters such as Bleach Bypass and Light Leak, plus ColorFast 2 for primary and secondary correction.
