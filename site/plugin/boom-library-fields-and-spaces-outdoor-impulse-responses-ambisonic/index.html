What Boom Library Fields & Spaces: Outdoor Impulse Responses - Ambisonic does

Fields & Spaces: Outdoor Impulse Responses (Ambisonic) from BOOM Library offers more than 120 impulse responses of outdoor places that are hard to capture, such as canyons, snow-covered hills, grassy fields and narrow city streets. They load into third-party convolution reverb plugins alongside After Effects and Premiere Pro.

The ambisonic format targets VR, AR and 360-degree film, and surround and stereo packs come with it. Environments run from forests and mountains to bridges, squares, ports, dams, castles and underpasses, mostly at several distances. The modeled approach yields a near-infinite signal-to-noise ratio, in 24-bit WAV at 48 to 96 kHz.

Key features

  • 120+ impulse responses of hard-to-record locations
  • Ambisonic format for VR, AR and 360-degree film
  • Surround and stereo packs included
  • Natural and urban environments at multiple distances
