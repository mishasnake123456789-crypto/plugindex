What Social Exporter Premiere Pro does

Social Exporter builds multi-platform versions of an edit without remaking it by hand. Four scale modes reframe footage: Fill, Fit Width, Fit Height or None, and backgrounds can be black, a solid color or blur.

Up to five watermark layers of image, video or text can be controlled independently and applied to every platform sequence, with drag-and-drop placement, loop and interval modes. Nine platform presets come built in, plus unlimited custom ones up to 8K, and batch export works with Media Encoder.

Key features

  • Four smart scale modes
  • Black, color or blur backgrounds
  • Up to five independent watermark layers
  • Nine presets plus unlimited custom up to 8K
  • Batch export via Media Encoder
