What Advanced Wireframe Add-On does

Advanced Wireframe Add-On produces wireframe geometry from your meshes that renders properly in Eevee and Cycles. It can apply to whole collections, making it practical for scenes rather than single objects.

Camera Culling removes geometry the camera cannot see, with triangulation recommended beforehand. Adaptive Thickness adjusts wire radius based on face area, subdivision display is supported, and materials can be controlled per element. Motion designers and technical illustrators creating stylized wireframe looks can use it.

Key features

  • Wireframes applied to entire collections
  • Rendering in Eevee and Cycles
  • Camera Culling removes unseen geometry
  • Adaptive thickness based on face area
  • Per-element material control
