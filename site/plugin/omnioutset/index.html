Blender add-on for equidistant edge outsets and smart face extrusion with an interactive drag-to-preview workflow.

What OmniOutset does

OmniOutset is aimed at hard-surface modeling in Blender. It produces a true equidistant outset along edges, keeping uniform thickness even around complex boundaries, and offers a smart face extrude that offsets while pushing along the normal.

Dragging previews the result live; Ctrl swaps the secondary parameter between Outset and Z-Offset, and Shift slows movement for precise tweaks. It detects topology, either deleting bottom faces for a clean cavity or flipping normals on isolated faces to make a closed shell. One shortcut, Shift+Alt+E by default, covers face and edge modes, and the interface is localized in ten languages.

Key features

  • Equidistant edge outset with uniform thickness
  • Smart face extrude that caps open sheets
  • Drag to preview, Ctrl and Shift modifiers
  • Topology detection for cavities or closed shells
  • Rebindable shortcut and ten languages
