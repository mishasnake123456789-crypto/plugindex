What Webrotate 360 Turntable Animator does

WebRotate 360 Turntable Animator turns existing 3D models into 360-degree and multi-row turntables. Output can be image sequences or interactive web and offline presentations with clickable hotspots.

It sits in Properties > Scene > WebRotate 360, uses the 3D Viewport as a viewfinder, and animates the selected object rather than the camera. Product marketers can build spinnable product views. Key capabilities include 360-degree and multi-row turntables and image sequences or interactive presentations.
