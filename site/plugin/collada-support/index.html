What Collada Support does

Collada Support, by Dank_Heehaw, brings COLLADA .dae import and export to Blender 5, for pipelines or assets that still rely on that format. It is listed under Import-Export and Pipeline.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.0.0 or newer.

Key features

  • COLLADA (.dae) import
  • COLLADA (.dae) export

  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later
