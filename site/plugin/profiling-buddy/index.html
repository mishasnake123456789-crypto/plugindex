What Profiling Buddy does

Profiling Buddy is a Blender add-on by SimonThommes that adds a modifier panel for profiling. It shows how long things take to execute, which helps you find performance bottlenecks in your scene.

It requires no special permissions, installs by drag and drop or from disk, and works with Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. It is released under the GNU GPL v3 or later. Anyone building heavy modifier stacks or procedural setups can use it to see which parts are costly.

Key features

  • Modifier panel that profiles execution times
  • Reveals performance bottlenecks
  • Requires no special permissions
  • Drag-and-drop or disk installation
  • Supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
