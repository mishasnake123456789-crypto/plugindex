Adds import, editing and export of extra video and audio containers and codecs to Adobe's video apps without re-encoding.

What Autokroma Influx does

Autokroma Influx extends After Effects and Premiere Pro with support for containers and codecs Adobe does not handle natively. Files can be brought in, edited and exported without remuxing or re-encoding, and no separate transcoding program is involved.

Footage edited in Premiere Pro can be processed in After Effects, projects can be rendered through Media Encoder, and audio opens in Audition in the new formats, including FLAC, OPUS, Ogg Vorbis, DTS, AC-3, AIFF, MKA, WMA and Speex. Version 1.6.1 adds Blu-ray M2TS/MTS, MV-HEVC 3D, VVC and raw H.264/H.265 streams, using ffmpeg 7.1. It runs on macOS and Windows 10 and 11.

Key features

  • Extra containers and codecs with no re-encode or remux step
  • Works in Premiere Pro, After Effects, Media Encoder and Audition
  • Audio support including FLAC, OPUS, DTS and AC-3
  • Decoding of M2TS/MTS, MV-HEVC and VVC in 1.6.1
  • macOS (Apple Silicon, Intel) and Windows
