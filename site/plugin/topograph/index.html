What Topograph does

Topograph by Pixel Sorter Studio is an After Effects effect that brings elevation maps and topographic looks to your footage. Source material becomes contour-style designs and animations.

You can animate it over time or design on a single frame with interactive feedback. It is compatible with After Effects CC 2015 through 2025 and matching Media Encoder versions, with Mac M1 support added in version 1.0.4. A demo video is available for newcomers.

Key features

  • Elevation map and topography effect
  • Designs and animations from footage
  • Animate or design on a single frame
  • CC 2015 through 2025 with Media Encoder
  • Mac M1 support since v1.0.4
