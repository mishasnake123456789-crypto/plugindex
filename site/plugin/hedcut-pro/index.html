Plugin for DaVinci Resolve that renders video and stills in a newspaper hedcut or intaglio engraving style.

What Hedcut Pro does

Hedcut Pro recreates the dotted portrait illustration style familiar from financial newspapers, as well as intaglio-type engraving, within DaVinci Resolve. Both video clips and still images can be converted.

Creators can use it to give interviews, portraits or documentary material an editorial, hand-engraved appearance. The plugin runs on macOS, Windows and Linux and works with DaVinci Resolve 21, 20 and 19, so it fits both current and slightly older installs. It is published by Nx Color and aimed at editors who like distinctive graphic treatments.

  • Intaglio engraving effect
