What Shelf Filler 4.0 Pro does

Shelf Filler 4.0 Pro automates the building of retail shelves for product visualization, merchandising scenes, retail design and FMCG rendering. It handles product placement, facing, alignment, locking, regeneration and replacement.

One click generates a shelf, a fill percentage controls how full it is, and facing modes align products left, center, right or justified. Non-destructive locking keeps chosen items in place when the shelf is regenerated. Highlights cover one-click shelf generation and adjustable fill percentage.

Key features

  • One-click shelf generation
  • Adjustable fill percentage
  • Left, center, right and justify facing
  • Non-destructive product locking
