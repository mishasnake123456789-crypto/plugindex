What Barray - Procedural Arrays does

Kaan S.'s BArray lets you paint arrays onto any surface in Blender rather than placing copies by hand. The method is procedural, so patterns stay adjustable.

Use it for sci-fi greebles, architectural ornament and organic patterns. It works with Cycles and Eevee, supports Blender 4.0 to 4.5 and includes documentation. Hard-surface and environment artists who need to cover surfaces with repeating elements can paint them on quickly. Because the approach is procedural, density and variation can be revisited without repainting everything.

Key features

  • Paint arrays directly onto surfaces
  • Procedural approach
  • Greebles, architecture and organic patterns
  • Cycles and Eevee
  • Blender 4.0 to 4.5
