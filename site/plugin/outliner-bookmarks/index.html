A Blender add-on for bookmarking collection visibility states and isolating them in one click.

What Outliner Bookmarks does

Outliner Bookmarks makes complex scenes easier to navigate by saving collection visibility states as bookmarks. A single click restores any state of any collection, and a bookmark can be isolated to disable everything around it.

With Ctrl+Shift click you can rename a bookmark, change its icon, or reposition it with WASD and arrow keys. Bookmarks can be organized into groups with custom styles and icons, and a collector view shows an overview of the outliner's contents. Artists working in large scenes with many collections will save time hiding and showing parts of their project.

Key features

  • Bookmark collection visibility states
  • One-click isolate
  • Ctrl+Shift edit of name, icon and position
  • Groups with custom icons
  • Collector overview
