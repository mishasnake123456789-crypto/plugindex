Offline Blender add-on that builds studio setups for product renders with presets, batch queue and exports.

What Productstage does

ProductStage converts an empty Blender scene into a ready-to-shoot product studio. It works fully offline and uses no AI services or outside dependencies, aimed at product visualization and e-commerce imagery.

Studio looks such as Seamless Soft, Concrete Modern and Beauty Glass set the mood, while camera angle presets, rule-of-thirds offsets, depth of field and safe-area overlays handle framing. Lighting gains and color temperature can be tuned safely. Render profiles target things like Amazon-ready shots or car hero views. Exports support several formats, transparency and watermarks, and a persistent queue handles batch jobs with pause, resume and retry.

Key features

  • Studio look presets
  • Camera angle and framing aids
  • Lighting gain, softness and temperature controls
  • Render profiles for common product scenarios
  • Restart-safe batch render queue
