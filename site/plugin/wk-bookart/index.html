What Wk Bookart does

WK Bookart creates patterns for folded-book art. Its input is either a two-tone image or a silhouette taken from a 3D object, which you may rotate, edit or slice before capturing.

The motif previews as simplified black and white cubes so proportions and spacing can be checked in Blender. Instructions are exported as CSV and templates as SVG for printing. Crafters and designers can plan folded-page sculptures from digital models. The folding templates can then be printed and followed page by page when making the finished piece.

Key features

  • Source from an image or a 3D silhouette
  • Rotate, edit or slice before capture
  • Preview as black and white cubes
  • CSV instruction export
  • SVG template export
