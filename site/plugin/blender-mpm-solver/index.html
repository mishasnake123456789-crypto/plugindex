What Blender Mpm Solver does

Blender MPM Solver lets you run material point method simulations from within Blender, even though the solver itself is external. It is launched and managed from the interface and imports its cache live.

Behaviors include dry sand, wet snow, mud and jelly-like materials. Output can be granular particles for sand and debris, or reconstructed surfaces for smoother looks, and results can be baked to Alembic. It suits VFX artists after convincing granular and soft-body effects.

Key features

  • External MPM solver managed from Blender
  • Sand, snow, mud and jelly-like materials
  • Live cache import
  • Granular rendering or surface reconstruction
  • Alembic baking
