What Animated Water - Ocean Fx does

Animated Water - Ocean Fx, from Mamba CG, builds water surfaces in Blender for scenes ranging from open sea to a small cup. Rather than entering numeric wave values, you choose presets by appearance: Calm Ocean, Light Wind, Medium Ocean, Rough Ocean, Storm, Pool Water, Bath Water and Cup Small Water.

Presets are tuned for both animation and stills, with stable motion and no flicker, and version 1.0.1 added an infinite ocean animation that continues for any number of frames. It works in Cycles and Eevee for architectural, environment, cinematic and game projects, which makes quick, believable water simple.

Key features

  • Presets from Calm Ocean to Storm
  • Pool, bath and cup water presets
  • Stable, flicker-free motion
  • Infinite ocean animation
  • Cycles and Eevee support
