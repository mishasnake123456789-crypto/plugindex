What Fake Liquid does

Fake Liquid, from Animation Store, creates the illusion of liquid being drunk in Blender without running a fluid simulation. It stays light enough for weaker computers.

It avoids complex geometry nodes, keeping setup simple. A separate pro version is available on the developer's page, and earlier buyers get it at no additional cost. Documentation is included.
