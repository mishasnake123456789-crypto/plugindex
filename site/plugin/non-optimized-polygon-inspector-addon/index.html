What Non-Optimized Polygon Inspector Addon does

The Non-Optimized Polygon Inspector helps spot areas that are heavier than they should be. It visualizes the polygons in a scene, marking high-polygon areas in red and low-polygon areas in green.

The distinction is based on the scene's polygon count. It is compatible as a Blender extension, includes documentation and is released under the GPL license. Game artists and optimizers can get a quick visual map of where to decimate or retopologize.

Key features

  • Red marks high-polygon areas
  • Green marks low-polygon areas
  • Based on scene polygon count
  • Blender extension, GPL license
