What Chaos V-Ray Premium does

Chaos V-Ray Premium brings the V-Ray rendering engine into Cinema 4D and packages it with a wider set of extras than the entry tier. These include up to 18,500 3D assets and materials, an enhanced realistic material library and five times as many Chaos credits as Solo.

It also adds Veras AI, cloud collaboration, and a budget of AI-enhanced scenes, Nano Banana 2 images and AI videos, plus an architectural visualization deep-dive series. It is meant for artists who render architecture, interiors or products and want assets and cloud features alongside the renderer.
