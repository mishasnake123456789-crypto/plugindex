What True RoboAnimator does

True RoboAnimator is a Blender add-on by danyal-sarfraz that converts keyframed chassis movement to physically correct wheel rotation, with CSV data ready for engineering use. It checks non-holonomic motion, corrects paths that are not feasible, and either drives or bakes the wheel rotations.

Validation detects sideways slip, and two autocorrect modes are available: a smooth S-ease curve limited by the tightest possible turn radius, and a linear rotate-move-rotate sequence. Wheel kinematics compute per-frame left and right wheel angles and RPM from the chassis motion. Robotics students and animators benefit from realistic mobile robot motion.

Key features

  • Non-holonomic motion validation
  • Smooth S-ease and linear autocorrect modes
  • Per-frame wheel angles and RPM
  • Drive or bake wheel rotations
  • Engineering-ready CSV export
