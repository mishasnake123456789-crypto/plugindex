What Rapid Slideshow & Presentation Maker does

Built by Kupu Creative Media, Rapid Slideshow & Presentation Maker is a tool running inside After Effects that turns folders of images into animated slideshows without hand keyframing. Everything is handled from one interface opened by a dockable launcher.

Images import with Fit to Comp and can be arranged as Grid or Bento layouts. Pan, Zoom or Push movements apply in, out or both, with optional fade and wiggle, and styling covers borders, blurry backgrounds and thumbnails. Audio can be distributed with fades and crossfades, 3D camera shots offer shuffle and refocus, and Swing/Flash and keyframe overshoot add finishing touches for social edits, weddings and event reels.

Key features

  • Grid and Bento image layouts
  • Pan, Zoom and Push movements
  • Borders, blurry backgrounds and thumbnails
  • Audio fades and crossfades
  • 3D camera shots with Shuffle and Refocus
