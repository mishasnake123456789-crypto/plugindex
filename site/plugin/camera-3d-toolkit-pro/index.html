What Camera 3D Toolkit Pro does

Camera 3D Toolkit Pro by Edgard St Felix automates cinematic camera work in After Effects. You select layers, press Target, and the animation is created for you, including parallax depth effects across a flat composition.

It also handles autofocus and blur automation through a single depth-of-field slider, camera shake from one button, and cinematic speed ramps with ease in and ease out sliders. Version 3.5 added a graph editor, dolly zoom, a preset panel and a spacing section for distributing layers, making it helpful for quick, polished motion.

Key features

  • One-click 3D camera animation with parallax
  • Single DOF slider for focus and blur
  • Camera shake button
  • Speed ramps with ease sliders and overlap keyframes
  • Dolly zoom, graph editor and preset panel
