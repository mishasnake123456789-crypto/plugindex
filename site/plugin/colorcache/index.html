What ColorCache does

ColorCache is built for editors who work on content for many brands. It keeps each brand's colour palette, logos and reference notes in one panel, so you don't search through emails or style guides in the middle of an edit.

Saved logos and other assets can be dragged straight into a project, and notes such as font names are attached for quick reference. Everything stays reachable without leaving the timeline. It requires Premiere Pro 23.0 or After Effects 23.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Saves brand colour palettes
  • Stores logos and assets for drag-in
  • Attached notes such as fonts
  • Always available next to the timeline
  • Requires Premiere Pro or After Effects 23.0+
