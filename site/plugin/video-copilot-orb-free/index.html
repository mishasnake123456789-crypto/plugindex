What Video Copilot ORB (Free) does

ORB creates three-dimensional spheres in After Effects without needing a separate 3D application. Environment reflection maps give them shiny surfaces, and advanced UV settings control how textures wrap.

Illumination is built in, with extra options, and the sphere responds to After Effects lights and motion blur. It installs across several versions of the host app. It is a free Video Copilot tool for planets, orbs and glossy balls. Because it reacts to scene lights, the spheres sit naturally in compositions that already use After Effects lighting.

Key features

  • Environment reflection maps
  • Advanced UV settings
  • Built-in and advanced illumination
  • Works with After Effects lights
  • Motion blur support
