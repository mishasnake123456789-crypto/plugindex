What Vera Product Light Addon does

Vera Product Light Addon is a Blender lighting tool for showcasing products. Typical subjects are cars, gadgets and human figures, and builds the setup from presets.

It adds ground objects, so there's no need to prepare the scene manually. More than 16 presets are available, and each light's color, strength and radius can be customized. Neon lights are among the options. Great for quick studio-style renders. The presets give a dependable starting point that can be refined, which is helpful for commercial visualization where speed and consistency matter.

Key features

  • 16+ lighting presets
  • Automatic ground objects
  • Per-light color, strength and radius
  • Neon lights included
