What Uv-Outfit - Tile Displace does

UV-Outfit Tile Displace shifts UV islands to random spots along X, along Y, or along randomly chosen axes. It was designed to support the strip texturing technique, where islands are laid across a strip of repeating texture.

Example textures are supplied, and the add-on covers Blender 2.8 through 2.91. It ships with documentation, a demo video and a royalty free license. Stylized and game artists who texture many pieces from a small shared sheet can vary their look quickly.

Key features

  • Random island displacement on X, Y or both
  • Built for strip texturing
  • Example textures provided
  • Blender 2.8 through 2.91
