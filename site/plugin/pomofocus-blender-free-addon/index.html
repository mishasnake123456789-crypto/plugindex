What Pomofocus - Blender Free Addon does

PomoFocus applies the Pomodoro Technique inside Blender so you can alternate focused work and breaks without another app. Time lengths are fully customizable instead of fixed.

Sounds play while the timer runs and when a task ends, with an option for your own sound. A reset button, a report of completed tasks and an estimate of the time needed to finish a task are provided. A built-in updater checks for new versions, and the special edition and original edition are combined in one add-on.

Key features

  • Custom work and break durations
  • Sound cues with a custom sound option
  • Reset button
  • Report of completed tasks
  • Built-in update checker
