What Achievements does

Achievements by Illya-Ilev adds a playful layer to Blender with achievement pop-ups in the style of Steam, Xbox and PlayStation. It is listed under System.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.4.0 or newer.
