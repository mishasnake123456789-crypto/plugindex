What Audio Design Desk does

Audio Design Desk pairs a large royalty-free library of more than 70,000 sound and music cues with an extension for Premiere Pro. Material can be used in the standalone app or in editors including Final Cut and Avid as well.

Sounds for foley, sound design, scoring, transitions and effects can be played live from a computer keyboard, and your own files can be brought in and tagged with automated tools. A keystroke swaps one sound for an alternate without losing sync, and a whole arrangement can be remixed using your sounds or library content. Video editors who want faster sound design will find it most useful.

Key features

  • 70,000+ royalty-free sound and music cues
  • Perform sounds live from a QWERTY keyboard
  • Automated import and tagging of your own sounds
  • Swap sounds by keystroke while keeping sync
  • Remix entire arrangements quickly
