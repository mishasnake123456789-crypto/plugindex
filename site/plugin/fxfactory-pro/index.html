What FxFactory Pro does

FxFactory Pro from Noise Industries is a broad effects library that works in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro, plus Final Cut Pro and Motion. It supplies hundreds of presets, generators and effects for stylizing footage and building graphics.

It is built on Metal-accelerated graphics and uses perceptual colour space compositing for better quality. It includes built-in subject isolation, plus face detection and object tracking, and wide gamut HDR support for PQ and HLG timelines has improved. Version 7 added depth-based masking, an Edge Work effect and Nebula, Wormhole and Light Streams generators, and in/out animations are simple to create.

Key features

  • Hundreds of presets, generators and effects
  • Metal-accelerated, perceptual colour compositing
  • Subject isolation, face detection and tracking
  • Wide gamut HDR support for PQ and HLG
  • Version 7: depth masking, Edge Work and new generators
