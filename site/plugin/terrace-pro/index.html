What Terrace Pro does

AZZURRO-INTERIORS' Terrace Pro provides furniture and decor for outdoor spaces in Blender, spanning sofas, dining sets, lamps, fireplaces, grills and accessories.

The full edition includes 135 optional objects and the Lite edition 45. Models are built to real-world scale, carry normal maps and textures, and are optimized for Eevee and Cycles. Furniture sets can be combined and customised easily. Archviz artists staging terraces, patios and gardens can furnish a scene quickly.

Key features

  • 135 objects in the full version, 45 in Lite
  • Real-world scale models
  • Normal-mapped and textured
  • Optimized for Eevee and Cycles
  • Combinable furniture sets
