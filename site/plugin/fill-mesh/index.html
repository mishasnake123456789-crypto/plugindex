Blender add-on that detects and fills holes in meshes using grid, hole and contextual fill methods.

What Fill Mesh does

Fill Mesh finds gaps in Blender meshes automatically, so you do not have to select edges by hand. It can close a specific area or seal the whole mesh, which prepares scanned, remeshed and sculpted models for watertight use.

Methods include Grid Fill, Hole Fill, Contextual Create and Simple Bridge Fill. Fill Shape rebuilds curved, organic areas while Fill Mesh handles flat ones. Snap and merge tools keep topology clean, Repair Notches and Simple Quad Fill extend boundaries, and advanced options cover curvature adaptation, vertex distribution and smoothing.

Key features

  • Automatic hole detection
  • Grid, Hole, Contextual and Bridge fill methods
  • Fill Shape for curved surfaces
  • Snap Vertex and Merge Vertex Group
  • Adjustable density and smoothing
