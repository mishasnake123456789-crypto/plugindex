Blender add-on that generates lip-sync animation from audio using the Rhubarb engine and maps it to shapes or bones.

What Rhubarb Lipsync Ng does

Rhubarb Lipsync NG integrates the Rhubarb Lip Sync engine into Blender so spoken audio becomes mouth animation. The result is applied to character models through a streamlined interface.

Sounds can be mapped onto shape keys, bones of an armature, or action sheets, and basic animation needs only five to eight shapes plus an idle pose. Characters with separate parts such as teeth and tongue can be mapped and baked together. It supports 3D animation with strip blending and 2D without, bakes to NLA tracks and strips, and includes an audio converter.

Key features

  • Lip-sync generated from audio files
  • Mapping to shape keys, bones or action sheets
  • Multi-object mapping and baking
  • NLA baking to tracks and strips
  • Built-in audio converter
