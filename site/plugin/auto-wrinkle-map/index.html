What Auto Wrinkle Map does

Auto Wrinkle Map is a Blender add-on by Hattiffnat that builds wrinkle maps controlled by bones. With one click it creates the setup, including the drivers and shader nodes that would normally take time to wire by hand.

The package is only about 10 KB, needs no special permissions, and supports Blender 4.4 and newer. It is released under the GNU GPL v3 or later. Character artists who want skin or cloth wrinkles to respond to facial or joint movement can use it to speed up this rigging step.

Key features

  • Creates bone-driven wrinkle maps in one click
  • Quick driver creation
  • Quick shader node creation
  • Supports Blender 4.4 and newer
  • Tiny package with no special permissions
