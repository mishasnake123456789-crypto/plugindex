What QuickNest Pro does

QuickNest Pro takes care of nesting operations that Premiere makes tedious. It un-nests sequences, either merging them into the parent or reverting something you nested before.

It can also batch nest clips, which comes in handy when stabilizing footage that runs at different frame rates. It requires Premiere Pro 15.0 or later. It saves time on timelines where nesting and un-nesting happen frequently. Nesting multiple clips at once can also keep effects like stabilization consistent across mixed sources.

Key features

  • Un-nest sequences
  • Merge or revert nested sequences
  • Batch nest clips
  • Helps stabilize mixed frame rate clips
  • Premiere Pro 15.0 and later
