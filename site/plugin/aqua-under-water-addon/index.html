What Aqua Under Water Addon does

Aqua Under Water Addon helps Blender artists build submerged environments. It creates water surfaces whose color, transparency and waves can all be tuned, and offers presets for oceans, rivers and lakes.

Underwater lighting tools change the color, intensity and direction of lights, while fog and atmospheric effects add a feeling of depth. Particle support places water particles or floating dust. A free diver model is included to use in scenes, along with documentation from EdenWeby 3D.

Key features

  • Water surfaces with color, transparency and waves
  • Ocean, river and lake presets
  • Underwater lighting controls
  • Fog and atmosphere for depth
  • Particle support and free diver model
