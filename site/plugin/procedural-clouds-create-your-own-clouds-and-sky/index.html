What Procedural Clouds - Create Your Own Clouds & Sky does

Procedural Clouds, from Suzanne studio, creates cloud formations and complete skies using geometry nodes. Everything is generated procedurally, so no image libraries or simulations are required, and the results are rendered with Cycles.

Individual clouds or entire skies can be customized, giving control over shape and atmosphere. Environment artists can design a specific sky for a scene instead of settling on a generic HDRI.

Key features

  • Clouds, skies and atmospheres from geometry nodes
  • Fully procedural creation
  • Customizable individual clouds or full skies
  • Rendered with Cycles
