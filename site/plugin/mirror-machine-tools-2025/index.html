What Mirror Machine Tools 2025 🙂⃤🙂⃤ does

Mirror Machine Tools 2025 speeds up symmetrical modeling in Blender by deciding the mirror direction from where your cursor hovers. This removes the guesswork about axes that usually accompanies auto-symmetrize operations.

A dedicated toolset sits at the top of the Mirror Machine N-panel for predictable results, and in Edit Mode a Shift+Alt+Q shortcut lets you hover over the source side and confirm. More than a hundred and twenty hard-surface demo assets are included, and you can import your own. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux with both Cycles and Eevee. Hard-surface modelers get faster symmetry.
