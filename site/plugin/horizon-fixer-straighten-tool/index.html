What Horizon Fixer - Straighten Tool does

Horizon Fixer by Vakago Tools corrects shots that were filmed off level. Draw a horizontal reference line along the horizon, similar to the straighten tool in Lightroom, and the footage is rotated to match.

It scales and rotates the video automatically so no black bars appear at the edges, and it can stabilise the horizon when the tilt changes over time. It is available for After Effects and Premiere Pro. Editors rescuing handheld, drone or action camera clips can level them without manual keyframing.

Key features

  • Level footage with a drawn horizontal line
  • Automatic resize and rotation to avoid black bars
  • Stabilises a drifting horizon
  • Available for After Effects and Premiere Pro
