What Bevel Lite does

Bevel Lite is a script by Jon Seagull that reworks the standard Bevel in 3ds Max with far fewer controls. It is shaped around chamfered extrusions, which the author finds is the task needed most of the time.

For modelers who find the full Bevel dialog more than the job requires, this simpler tool gets to the result with less fuss. It works with 3ds Max 4, 5 and 6, so it belongs to older versions of the software, and a demo video is available. It is free.

Key features

  • Reworked Bevel with fewer controls
  • Streamlined for chamfered extrusions
  • Works with 3ds Max 4, 5 and 6
  • Demo video available
