What Midi Piano Motion Tools Pro Max does

Midi Piano Motion Tools turns a MIDI performance into a playing piano. Key motion, hammer strikes and pedal movement are animated automatically with realistic rhythm.

It covers the full 88-key range from MIDI note 21 to 108, handles sixteen MIDI channels with color management, and can generate audio. Hammers sync to the notes, and pedals show suspension, softness and sustain. Musicians and video makers creating piano visualizations skip hand keyframing.

Key features

  • Key, hammer and pedal animation
  • Full 88-key range
  • 16-channel MIDI with color management
  • Audio generation
