What Social Beam does

Social Beam connects After Effects with Instagram so finished videos can be posted without leaving the app. It exports an mp4 and publishes it as an Instagram Post in three clicks.

Stories and Reels are not supported. It requires a free Dropbox account and a Facebook Business account, works on M1 Macs and macOS Monterey, and supports After Effects CC 2018 through 2023. It saves the step of exporting, uploading and posting separately, which helps creators publishing regularly.

Key features

  • Exports mp4 files
  • Posts to Instagram in three clicks
  • Instagram Posts only, not Stories or Reels
  • Works on M1 Macs and Monterey
  • Needs Dropbox and Facebook Business accounts
