What Hatchliner does

Hatchliner is a Blender add-on for giving a scene a drawn, anime-like appearance. It ships with 27 presets in four categories, Lines, Inks, Manga and Shadows, shown in a preview interface, and it works with the Eevee render engine.

Styles can be applied to a single mesh or specific materials, including meshes with several material slots, and can either overwrite or merge with existing materials. A collection exclusion list lets you apply a style across the whole scene while skipping chosen items. Stylized renderers and illustrators can use it to reach a hand-inked look quickly.

Key features

  • 27 presets across Lines, Inks, Manga and Shadows
  • Apply per mesh, material or whole scene
  • Overwrite or merge with existing materials
  • Collection exclusion list
  • Preview interface for styles
