What AEJuice Chrome Effect does

AEJuice Chrome Effect supplies metallic animated graphics that you preview as gifs and import individually through the Pack Manager. It can also be downloaded as a project file for After Effects, as MOGRT files or as video files for offline use.

The Edit tab lets you change text, colours and other controls, and it remembers your changes per animation, reapplying them on every new import. Your own fonts work in most After Effects and Premiere Pro versions. Setup is automatic: images and videos are resized into placeholders, most required fonts are installed, and blending modes and mattes are applied. Compositions stay open with flexible controls for those wanting to customise further.

Key features

  • Preview as gifs and import only what you need
  • After Effects project, MOGRT or video downloads
  • Edit tab remembers changes per animation
  • Use your own fonts
  • Automated placeholder, font and matte setup
