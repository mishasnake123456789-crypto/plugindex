What Light Man Pro Addon does

Light Man Pro, by Grafocrate Art, is a Blender add-on that replaces manual light placement with a library of ready-made rigs. Choices span product renders, character highlights, dynamic scenes and abstract looks across eight categories.

Once a setup is loaded you can adjust the key, fill and back lights live, changing color and power with immediate feedback. Environment helpers include several floor shapes, day and night skies, ceilings and volumetric fog. It speeds up look development for artists who want a convincing lit scene without building every rig from scratch.

Key features

  • 100 lighting setups across 8 categories
  • Real-time key, fill and backlight controls
  • Floors in circle, square, pentagon, stepped and L shapes
  • Day and night sky options
  • Ceilings and volumetric fog
