What Motion Sounds does

Motion Sounds is a Blender add-on by Jan-Hendrik-Müller that automates sound effect placement from animation. It watches armature bones and, when one crosses a Z threshold you set, inserts a sound clip into the Video Sequence Editor at that frame.

Volume can follow speed, so faster motion plays louder, and sounds can be chosen at random or singly from a folder, with volume randomization for variety. Default sounds are included from version 0.9.0. It is practical for footsteps and impacts in character animation.

Key features

  • Detects bones crossing a Z threshold
  • Inserts clips into the Video Sequence Editor
  • Speed-based volume mapping
  • Pick one sound or a random one from a folder
  • Volume randomization and default sounds
