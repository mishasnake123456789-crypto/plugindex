Open-source Blender add-on for creating a dolly zoom effect in the viewport and with cameras.

What Dolly Zoom does

Dolly Zoom is a Blender extension by JLBöhm for producing the classic dolly zoom, where the camera moves while the focal length changes to keep the subject the same size and warp the background. It is listed under the 3D View and Camera categories.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Dolly zoom for the viewport and camera
