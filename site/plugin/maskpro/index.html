What MaskPro does

MaskPro by Zubair Khalid puts common mask operations into one After Effects panel. Masks on a layer can be selected in a click, then copied to other layers with or without keyframes, or deleted from all selected layers.

It can pre-compose layers based on their masks or bounding box, freeze and defreeze masks, convert between shapes and masks with one button, build a matte around the layer, and split masks into separate layers. It helps artists who work with masks across many layers.

Key features

  • One-click mask selection and copy/paste with keyframes
  • Delete masks from selected layers
  • Pre-compose by masks or bounding box
  • Freeze and defreeze masks
  • Convert between shapes and masks
