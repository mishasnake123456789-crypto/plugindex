What Rubble It does

Rubble It, from Blender Bits, fills Blender scenes with wreckage and clutter. Debris types include bricks, wood, trash, scrap metal and dust, which makes it helpful for ruined environments, alleys and construction sites.

It renders in Cycles and Eevee, supports Blender 4.0 through 5.0, installs as a regular add-on and is compatible with extensions. It is released under the GPL license, with documentation and a demo video provided.

Key features

  • Scatters bricks, wood, trash and scrap metal
  • Dust included
