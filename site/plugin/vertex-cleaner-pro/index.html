What Vertex Cleaner Pro does

Vertex Cleaner Pro from Arcaneforge targets riggers and animators who deal with weight painting and vertex group upkeep in Blender. It is meant for meshes carrying many bones and many groups, where manual cleanup becomes tedious.

The toolset covers merging weights, searching deeply through groups, manipulating values and making easy edits. The developer provides documentation, and the add-on supports Blender 3.0 through 5.2. Anyone inheriting a rig with a cluttered group list can use it to consolidate and tidy skinning data.

Key features

  • Weight merge
  • Weight deep search
  • Weight manipulation
  • Easy weight editing
  • Works with Blender 3.0 to 5.2
