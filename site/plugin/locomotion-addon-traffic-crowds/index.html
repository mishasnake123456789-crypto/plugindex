What Locomotion Addon - Traffic, Crowds does

Locomotion Addon brings ready-made movement systems to Blender through geometry nodes. Seven presets cover crowds, insects, birds and basic car traffic, populating scenes with life without hand-keyframing each figure.

The presets are: a crowd on a path, random crowd movement on a surface, insects on a path and on a surface, birds on a path, insects flying around an object, and a simple traffic system. Animations loop endlessly, settings live in the modifiers tab, and you can bring in your own models to follow the paths. Environment and architectural visualization artists get busy scenes quickly.

Key features

  • Seven presets for crowds, insects, birds and cars
  • Path-based and surface-based movement
  • Endlessly looped animation
  • Settings editable in the modifiers tab
  • Use your own models
