Brings 3D camera projection and UV unwrap/rewrap of Alembic geometry to After Effects.

What Wrap It does

Wrap It adds camera projection to After Effects compositing. It loads Alembic files, flattens 3D geometry into UV space, and can wrap textures back onto the models, which makes texture extraction and 3D integration far more direct.

Animated geometry is supported, and processing can use the GPU or the CPU through Advanced Settings. It works with native After Effects 3D layers. A separate OFX version exists for hosts such as DaVinci Resolve and Autodesk Flame, so compositors on other platforms are covered too.

Key features

  • Loads Alembic files
  • Unwraps geometry to UV space and rewraps textures
  • Supports animated geometry
  • GPU or CPU processing selectable
  • Works with native After Effects 3D layers
