What Texture Mixer does

Texture Mixer, by MoonRaider, brings a layer-based approach to combining PBR textures in Blender, along with tools for managing the textures you use. It is categorised under Workflow.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.
