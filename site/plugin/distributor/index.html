Distributes Blender objects continuously or discretely with control over position, rotation, scale and circular layouts.

What Distributor + does

Distributor + by Fantisal places objects across a scene according to rules you set. Continuous or discrete modes decide how spacing between objects is handled.

Position settings include spacing, random positions and offsets on the X, Y and Z axes, plus a choice of axis direction. Rotation and scale can also be offset, and objects can be arranged in a circle. It is helpful for rows, fences, columns, decorative patterns and other repetitive layouts.

Key features

  • Continuous or discrete distribution modes
  • Spacing, random positions and axis offsets
  • Rotation and scale controls
  • Circular arrangements
  • Axis direction across +/- X, Y, Z
