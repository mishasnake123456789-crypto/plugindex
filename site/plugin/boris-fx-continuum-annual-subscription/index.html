What Boris FX Continuum Annual Subscription does

Continuum is a broad collection of effects from Boris FX for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. It handles everyday finishing tasks such as repairing footage, stylizing it, adding film grain and looks, building transitions and compositing green screen shots.

Mocha tracking is built into every effect, so tracked masks and attachments don't require leaving the plugin. AI tools tackle blurry shots and skin touch-ups, and machine learning models include Deinterlace ML, Face ML, License Plate ML, Motion Blur ML and Upres ML. Beat Reactor syncs effects to music, and the Custom FX Editor adds quick preset browsing, Pixel Chooser and colour management.

Key features

  • AI restoration and retouching tools
  • Mocha tracking inside every effect
  • Beat Reactor animates effects to music
  • Film looks, grains and drag-and-drop transitions
  • Custom FX Editor with Pixel Chooser
