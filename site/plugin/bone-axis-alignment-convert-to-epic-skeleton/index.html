What Bone Axis Alignment (Convert To Epic Skeleton) does

Bone Axis Alignment is a Blender add-on for riggers who need their skeletons to match the Epic or Unreal Engine convention. Mismatched bone axes are a common headache when moving characters between Blender and a game engine, and this tool addresses that step.

It can flip bone axes in Edit Mode, reads the bone tree to build alignment groups automatically by hierarchy, and creates IK bones. It suits anyone building a rig directly onto an Epic skeleton or converting an existing rig to a UE skeleton. Documentation is included and the add-on is released under the GPL.

Key features

  • Flips bone axes in Edit Mode
  • Builds alignment groups from the bone hierarchy
  • Creates IK bones
  • Targets Epic and UE skeleton conventions
