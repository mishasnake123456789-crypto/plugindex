What Easy Card Animation does

Easy Card Animation is a Blender toolkit for card designers and 3D artists. Drop in a design, choose a preset, and render using animation presets and Geometry Nodes tools.

Thirty non-destructive, editable presets cover fans, waves, curves, floating cards, tuck case reveals and circus sequences, and nine Geometry Nodes tools include Cards Fan, Cards On Curve, One Card and more. Good for trading card, playing card and packaging promos. Key capabilities include 30 non-destructive animation presets and 9 Geometry Nodes tools.
