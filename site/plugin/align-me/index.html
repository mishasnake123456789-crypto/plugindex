What Align Me does

Align Me makes camera and light placement straightforward. It positions a camera or light around the surface of an imaginary sphere centered on an object and points it at that object.

The tool prioritizes simplicity, which suits beginners who find orienting lights or cameras around a subject awkward. Documentation and a demo video are included. It supports Blender 2.8 through 2.93 and is released under the MIT license. Among its capabilities are aligns cameras or lights to an object, placement on an imaginary sphere and beginner-friendly operation.

Key features

  • Aligns cameras or lights to an object
  • Placement on an imaginary sphere
  • Beginner-friendly operation
  • Blender 2.8 to 2.93
