What ✨ Glare does

Glare is an effect addon from Chromelabs that works in the viewport and in final renders with Cycles and EEVEE. One click adds a set of lens effects at the tail of your compositor chain, and you may place custom nodes ahead of or behind it.

Fog, Shimmer, Rays, Ghosts, Chromatic, Reflect, Streaks, Glare and Bloom modules are available. This lets artists add cinematic optical looks to renders without building the node networks themselves.

Key features

  • One-click compositor effect setup
  • Bloom, streaks, ghosts, rays and fog modules
  • Works with Cycles and EEVEE
  • Real-time viewport preview
  • Custom nodes can go before or after
