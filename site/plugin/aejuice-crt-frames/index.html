What AEJuice CRT Frames does

AEJuice CRT Frames is a bundle of overlays that mimic old television sets and VCR playback. Layer one over a photo, video or design and it picks up the scanlines, glow and wear associated with analog hardware.

The 26 clips come as 4K H.264 files at 30 fps, supplied with transparent or black backgrounds so they blend in most editing programs, including After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro. The AEJuice Pack Manager gives fast previews, drag and drop import and favourites. Editors working on throwback montages or music videos can apply the look in a few clicks.

Key features

  • 26 vintage CRT and VCR overlays
  • 4K H.264 at 30 fps
  • Transparent or black backgrounds
