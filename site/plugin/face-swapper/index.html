What Face Swapper does

Face Swapper from Pixel Sorter Studio replaces faces in footage using AI models that run on your own graphics card. Nothing needs to be sent over the internet, which suits studios that must keep material on local hardware.

It is powered by three HyperSwap models and includes a Render Swap Mask option. The models supplied can be used commercially. The plugin is under continuing development, with new models and host support added over time, and works in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro.

Key features

  • Face swapping on a local GPU
  • Three HyperSwap models
  • Render Swap Mask
  • Models licensed for commercial use
  • No internet connection needed
