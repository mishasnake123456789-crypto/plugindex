What Flexiscatterpro does

FlexiScatter Pro is a Blender add-on for distributing objects without placing each one by hand. You can target surfaces, whole collections or specific objects as the scatter area.

Density and distribution controls tune how many items appear and how they spread, collision detection keeps instances from overlapping unrealistically, and randomization options vary the result. It suits environment artists filling terrain with rocks, plants, debris or props. Key capabilities include target surfaces, collections or objects and adjustable density and distribution.

Key features

  • Target surfaces, collections or objects
  • Adjustable density and distribution
  • Collision detection between instances
  • Randomization options
