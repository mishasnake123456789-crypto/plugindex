What Simple Align Tools does

Simple Align Tools helps line up objects in Blender relative to a target object. Three operations match position, rotation or scale, avoiding the manual typing of values or repeated snapping steps.

Align Position can use the minimum, maximum, center or origin as the base, Align Rotation matches the target's rotation on the local axis, and Align Scale matches its size. It works across Blender releases from the 2.8x and 2.9x series, through 3.0, up to 4.2, and includes documentation. Layout artists and modelers arranging parts will find the controls clear and quick.

Key features

  • Align Position by min, max, center or origin
  • Align Rotation on the local axis
  • Align Scale to the target
  • Blender 2.8x to 4.2
