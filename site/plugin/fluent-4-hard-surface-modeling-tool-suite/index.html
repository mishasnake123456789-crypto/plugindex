What Fluent 4 - Hard Surface Modeling Tool Suite does

Fluent 4 from CG Thoughts is a hard surface modeling toolkit for Blender. Its workflow centers on non-destructive boolean cuts, so shapes stay editable instead of being committed to the mesh.

A cutter add-on shapes and carves geometry, with documentation included. It installs as an extension covering Blender 3.6 through 5.0 and released under the GPL. Its models are suitable for rendering in Cycles, Eevee, Arnold, Octane or V-Ray. Concept artists and product designers building mechanical parts and sci-fi props can iterate quickly.

Key features

  • Non-destructive boolean modeling
  • Cutter add-on for carving geometry
  • Documentation included
  • Blender extension for 3.6 through 5.0
