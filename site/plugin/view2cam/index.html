Creates a Blender camera from your viewport angle, or records viewport navigation as camera keyframes.

What View2Cam does

View2Cam turns whatever you are looking at into a camera. One click makes a camera that matches your viewport position, rotation and lens, in both perspective and orthographic views.

It can also record orbit, pan and zoom navigation in real time as keyframes, giving you a camera animation from a simple fly-through. Sample rate and frame step are adjustable to control how dense the keys are. Animators and visualizers blocking out camera moves can do it by simply moving around.

Key features

  • One-click Camera From View
  • Perspective and orthographic modes
  • Records orbit, pan and zoom as keyframes
  • Adjustable sample rate and frame step
