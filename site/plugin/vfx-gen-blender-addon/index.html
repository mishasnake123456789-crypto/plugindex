What Vfx Gen Blender Addon does

VFX GEN supplies a collection of more than fifty-five procedural visual effects for Blender, built with Geometry Nodes and optimized textures.

Effects include fire, explosions, sparks, rain, snow, dust, bubbles, fireworks, energy beams and lightning strikes. A clean N-panel brings each into the scene with one click, and they remain customizable. Artists needing quick atmospheric or action effects can drop them in rather than building from scratch. The feature set spans 55+ procedural VFX assets and fire, rain, snow, sparks, lightning and more.

Key features
