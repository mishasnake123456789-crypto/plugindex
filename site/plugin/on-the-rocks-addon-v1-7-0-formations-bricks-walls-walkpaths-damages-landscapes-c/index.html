What On The Rocks (Addon) V1.7.0 | Formations, Bricks, Walls, Walkpaths, Damages, Landscapes, Crystals, Creations does

On The Rocks is a Blender plugin built on Geometry Nodes for natural and architectural detail. It creates rock formations, brick walls, damage, fractures, landscapes and crystals.

Twelve tools and modifiers, among them Pillar Formation, Rock Formation, Bricks Walls, Edge Damage and Surface Erosion, as well as Surface Fracture, Cell Fracture and Landscapes, can start from a freehand drawing, curves or an existing mesh, each with its own settings. Great for environment artists. Having natural and built forms in one add-on lets users create ruins, cliffs, cobbled paths and aged architecture without switching tools.

Key features

  • 12 Geometry Nodes tools and modifiers
  • Rock formations, walls and landscapes
  • Edge damage, erosion and fracture
  • Works from drawings, curves or meshes
