What NewBlue Stylizers Ultimate does

NewBlue Stylizers Ultimate packages 35 effects plugins for After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve; Avid Media Composer, Magix Vegas Pro and Corel editors are supported as well. It provides over 400 presets arranged in four sets.

Cartoonr Plus creates a comic or cel-animation look. Illuminate covers lighting and atmosphere with Light Rays, Neon Lights, Glow Pro, Starlight, RGB Shift and Metallic. Excite adds energy with Motion Blur, Earthquake, Zoom Blur, Ripple, Spin Blur and Warp, and Imagine turns footage into moving art. Editors seeking quick stylised looks have a lot to choose from.
