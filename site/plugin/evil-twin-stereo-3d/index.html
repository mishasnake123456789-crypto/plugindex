A stereoscopic 3D toolkit for After Effects that builds and maintains the right-eye comp from the left.

What Evil Twin Stereo 3D does

Evil Twin Stereo 3D simplifies stereoscopic work in After Effects by letting you control everything from the left-eye composition. One click generates or refreshes the right-eye comp as a clone.

For checking your work there is anaglyph viewing with live convergence, a wiggle flip using RAM Preview, and a viewer comp with checkerboard and interlaced modes. A Magic Expression links properties across eyes, Split FX gives each eye its own effect or mask, and a full S3D camera rig is included. Useful for S3D footage or adding depth to flat graphics.

Key features

  • One-click LR Clone for the right comp
  • Anaglyph viewing with live convergence
  • Magic Expression and Split FX for per-eye control
  • Stereo viewer comp with checkerboard and interlaced modes
  • Complete S3D camera rig
