What Facecap Basic Demo does

Facecap Basic Demo is a free AI facial motion capture add-on for Blender. It turns ordinary video into facial animation data, so no iPhone, TrueDepth sensor or mocap suit is required.

MP4, MOV and AVI files are processed to extract all 52 ARKit-compatible blendshapes along with eye and head tracking, and the animation data is mapped onto your character. Indie animators and creators can try facial capture cheaply. In practice that means works with MP4, MOV and AVI video and 52 ARKit-compatible blendshapes.

Key features

  • Works with MP4, MOV and AVI video
  • 52 ARKit-compatible blendshapes
  • Eye and head tracking
  • No iPhone or mocap hardware
