Developer: Evan Manning

FreeAfter Effects

After Effects plugin that makes any masked layer look like genuinely torn paper.

What Torn Paper does

Torn Paper converts a masked layer into a convincing ripped sheet. Edges can be shaped separately on the inner and outer side, with controls for roughness, jaggedness and notches, so tears look irregular and natural.

Paper fibres reach out from the tear with adjustable length, density and shadow. Fold marks can be placed anywhere and come with cracks and shadows on both sides. Dirt, smudges and dust each have independent settings, and the result holds up at any resolution without losing quality.

Key features

  • Separate inner and outer torn edge controls
  • Paper fibres with length, density and shadow
  • Fold marks with cracks and dual shadows
  • Independent dirt, smudge and dust controls
  • Resolution independent
