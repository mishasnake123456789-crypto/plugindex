Blender rigging add-on that generates a deform bone chain along the length of a hair strand.

What Hair Bone Generator does

Hair Bone Generator by morimoristudio creates a chain of deform bones that follows a hair strand, saving you from placing each bone manually when rigging hair. It is listed under Rigging.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Generates deform bone chains along hair strands
  • Reduces manual bone placement
