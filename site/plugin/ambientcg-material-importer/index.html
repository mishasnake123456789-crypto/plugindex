What AmbientCG Material Importer does

AmbientCG Material Importer, by Nino-Filiu, builds Blender materials from AmbientCG's free texture library in a single click, so you skip downloading and wiring maps by hand. It is listed under Import-Export and Material.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
