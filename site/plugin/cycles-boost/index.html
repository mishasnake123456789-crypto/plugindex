What Cycles Boost does

Cycles Boost is a bundle of render profiles for Blender aimed at shortening render times. It targets owners of RTX and GTX graphics cards as well as CPU-only machines, with separate profiles for CUDA, CPU (AMD and Intel), Optix and Eevee, each offered in Low, Medium and High tiers.

It includes a redesigned AI denoising option with Normal, Ultra and Extreme settings alongside the original Blender denoiser, plus optimizations for Optix, volumetrics and CUDA branched path tracing. A Lens Dirt node is added to the Optix profiles, and documentation is supplied. Artists wanting quicker Cycles output without tuning every setting by hand will benefit.

Key features

  • Profiles for CUDA, CPU, Optix and Eevee
  • Low, Medium and High tiers per profile
  • AI denoise with Normal, Ultra and Extreme settings
  • Optix and volumetrics speed optimizations
  • Lens Dirt node in Optix profiles
