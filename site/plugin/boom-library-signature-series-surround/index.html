What BOOM Library Signature Series - Surround does

BOOM Library Signature Series - Surround brings together every one of the Signature Series packs in a single bundle, including surround editions where they exist. The WAV files import into After Effects, Premiere Pro and any program that reads that format.

It holds more than 210,000 sounds in over 60,000 files, totaling above 2.1 TB, with themes such as cinematic effects, vehicles, medieval and science fiction. Audio is 24-bit at 48 kHz or higher and comes in Basics, Stereo and Surround variants. Many packs divide into Construction Kits and Designed Variants. UCS labelling and embedded metadata work with Soundly, Basehead and Soundminer. Trailer, film and game sound teams get a very broad starting library.
