Layer Sherpa

A tabbed After Effects panel that replaces the Align panel with alignment, anchor, positioning and layer tools.

What Layer Sherpa does

Layer Sherpa takes the place of Adobe's Align panel and expands it. Layers can be aligned to the comp, a selection or a target layer, with matching choices for anchor points.

Adjustments can be made in pixels, percent of layer or percent of comp, on one axis or across X, Y and Z, with increments, math operators, variables and save or restore. Utilities set parenting, in and out points, staggering, ordering and dimension matching, plus fades and scale or rotation steps. Hotkeys support the tabbed UI.

Key features

  • Align to comp, selection or target layer
  • Adjust in pixels or percentages on X, Y and Z
  • Math operators, variables and saved values
  • Parenting, in/out, stagger and ordering tools
  • Hotkey-driven tabbed UI
