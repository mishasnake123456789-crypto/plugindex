Pack of 365 gobo light textures for Blender, including animated and procedural ones, placed from the asset browser.

What King Of Light does

King of Light is a collection of gobos, textures that shape light to cast detailed patterns and shadows into a scene. Gobos are added with drag and drop through Blender's asset browser.

The pack includes 365 gobos, of which 72 are animated and 8 procedural. Softness, power and size are edited in the light properties, and procedural window, fan and edge gobos have sliders. Categories span animals, caustics, godrays, rain, trees and window combinations. It adds atmosphere to lighting setups quickly.

Key features

  • 365 gobos including 72 animated
  • Drag-and-drop through the asset browser
  • Softness, power and size controls
  • Procedural window, fan and edge gobos
