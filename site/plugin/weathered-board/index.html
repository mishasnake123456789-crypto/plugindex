What Weathered Board does

Weathered Board, by John-Singer, creates worn, weathered wooden planks in Blender that are meant to be 3D printed. It is listed under Add Mesh.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
