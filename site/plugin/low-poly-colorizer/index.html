What Low Poly Colorizer does

Low Poly Colorizer, by WASDCAT Games, assigns colors to faces using a palette picker and supports exporting to the Godot engine. It is listed under Material and Import-Export.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
