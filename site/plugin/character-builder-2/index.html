What Character Builder 2 does

Character Builder 2 from Pixcode builds custom characters in After Effects from swappable parts. More than 400 demo characters are included, either as starting bases or as examples for building from scratch.

Parts cover clothing, hair, beards, hats, glasses, ears, eyes, eyebrows, mouths, noses and more, and over 100 rigged characters span humans, animals and other figures. A character can be turned to six directions. It supports the Duik Bassel structure and Limber, giving animators of explainers and stories a quick cast.

Key features

  • 400+ demo characters
  • Interchangeable clothing, hair, face and accessory parts
  • 100+ rigged characters
  • Six viewing directions
  • Duik Bassel and Limber support
