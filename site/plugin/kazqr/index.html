Generates QR codes as raster images or DWG/DXF vectors, with logos, Unicode text and batch lists.

What KazQR does

KazQR makes QR codes for 3ds Max users in two forms: raster images in TIFF, JPEG, BMP, PNG and GIF, and vector DWG and DXF files for CAD systems. Logos can be placed in the code, covering up to 35 percent, and background and colors are free to choose.

Four error correction levels are available, and Unicode support covers Arabic, Japanese and other non-Latin scripts. Batch generation works from a prepared list, an Excel or Access table or a CSV file, and settings and text can be saved and loaded. Designers adding scannable codes to signage, packaging or drawings will find it convenient.

Key features

  • Raster output: TIFF, JPEG, BMP, PNG, GIF
  • Vector DWG and DXF export for CAD
  • Logo support up to 35% coverage
  • Four error correction levels
  • Batch generation from Excel, Access or CSV
