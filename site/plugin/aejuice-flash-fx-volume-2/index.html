What AEJuice Flash FX Volume 2 does

AEJuice Flash FX Volume 2 adds more than a hundred presets and effects to the Flash FX line. It is aimed at After Effects, Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro users who want ready-made flashes and bursts to punctuate cuts and transitions.

Content loads through the AEJuice Pack Manager, with quick preview, drag-and-drop import and favourites. After Effects CC 2015, Premiere Pro CC 2021 and Final Cut Pro 10.4.9 or newer are required, on Windows 7+ or Mac OS X 10.14+. As transparent or black-background videos the effects also fit editors such as Sony Vegas, Avid, DaVinci Resolve and Apple Motion, plus OBS, Filmora 9 and Photoshop.
