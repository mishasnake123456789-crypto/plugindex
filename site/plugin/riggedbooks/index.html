Blender add-on that builds rigged, animatable hard or soft cover books with page-flip animation and page-count presets.

What Riggedbooks does

Riggedbooks creates ready-to-animate books inside Blender. You choose a hard or soft cover and a page preset, and the add-on produces a rig that turns pages without the interleaving glitches that usually plague page-flip setups.

Presets range from 10 up to 100 pages, and cover height, width, thickness, spine curvature and materials can all be customized. Motion designers, product visualizers and scene dressers who need an opening book or flipping pages in a shot can skip building and rigging one by hand.

Key features

  • Hard cover and soft cover types
  • Page presets from 10P to 100P
  • Page turning without interleaving
  • Adjustable cover dimensions and spine curve
  • Customizable materials
