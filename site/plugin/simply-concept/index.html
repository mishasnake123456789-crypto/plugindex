What Simply Concept does

Simply Concept is for quickly roughing out ideas in Blender. You combine primitive shapes, or your own models, and see them blend together as one shape instantly.

Resolution can be adjusted to balance detail and performance, and afterward the concept mesh gets remeshed so it can go on to retopology, sculpting or further modification. Concept artists and designers exploring forms can iterate fast before committing to detailed modeling. The workflow is meant for early ideation, when speed matters more than polished topology and shapes may change many times.

Key features

  • Build from primitives or your own models
  • Real-time merging with shading preview
  • Adjustable resolution
  • Remeshing for retopology or sculpting
