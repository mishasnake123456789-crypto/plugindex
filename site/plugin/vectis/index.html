What VECTIS does

VECTIS from TOKI Projects is a pseudo-3D line generator for After Effects. It takes any path and renders a stroke that understands depth, so lines appear to recede and approach in space.

Stroke width adjusts automatically according to Z-depth and distance from the camera, which removes manual compensation when animating through a scene. No extra plugins are needed, and it works with After Effects 2023 through 2026. Motion designers can use it for flowing ribbons, wires and abstract line art with convincing perspective.

Key features

  • Pseudo-3D Bezier line generator
  • Depth-aware strokes from any path
  • Auto stroke scaling by Z-depth and camera distance
  • No additional plugins required
  • Works with After Effects 2023-2026
