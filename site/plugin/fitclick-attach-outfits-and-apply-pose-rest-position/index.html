What Fitclick - Attach Outfits & Apply Pose (Rest Position) does

FitClick attaches outfits to a rigged character in Blender without manual weight painting. Clothing, hair and accessories bind to the rig in one click, with no vertex group setup or cleanup, and it works with Rigify, ARP and custom rigs.

It detects the correct rig automatically when only one character is visible. The Pro Max tier adds a Smart Face Hair Binder that binds hair meshes only and keeps their shape keys, while the Pro and Pro Max tiers include a Smart Accessories System. It also supports applying a pose as the rest position. Character artists dressing models quickly will save many hours.

Key features

  • One-click binding of garments to a rig
  • No weight painting or vertex group cleanup
  • Automatic body detection
  • Smart Face Hair Binder in Pro Max
  • Works with Rigify, ARP and custom rigs
