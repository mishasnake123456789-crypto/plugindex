What Naked-Eye 3D does

Naked-Eye 3D, by NickolasM, is an After Effects script for previewing anamorphic, glasses-free 3D billboard content and preparing output for LED screens. It runs in a dockable ScriptUI panel with separate Camera Match and Unwrap modules.

Camera Match reads red, green and blue perspective guide lines to create a one-node camera. Section Chain Unwrap marks flat, corner and segmented screens as connected sections without gaps, using custom widths and a shared height. It builds individual unwrap comps plus a combined flat output, and Quick Rebuild updates results after corner points move. Only built-in features are used.

Key features

  • Camera Match from perspective guide lines
  • Section Chain Unwrap for flat, corner and segmented screens
  • Per-section widths with one common height
  • Automatic unwrap comps and combined flat output
  • Quick Rebuild after moving corner points
