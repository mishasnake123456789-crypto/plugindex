Blender add-on from Mets with quality-of-life tools for weight painting, including Symmetrize Weights.

What EasyWeight does

EasyWeight smooths out the weight painting process in Blender by gathering handy workflow tools in one add-on. One notable feature, Symmetrize Weights, mirrors weights across the two sides of a model and also deals with center groups.

It requires Blender 4.2 LTS or newer, installs without special permissions and is released under the GPL v3 or later. Documentation is available on the developer's site. Riggers and character artists skinning meshes can fix asymmetries and handle repetitive tasks more quickly.

Key features

  • Weight painting quality-of-life tools
  • Symmetrize Weights including center groups
  • For Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
  • No special permissions
