What Sketchn'carve does

Sketch N' Carve is a modeling add-on for Blender that turns Grease Pencil drawings into 3D meshes. Artists can model without touching a single vertex.

Boolean choices are Add, Remove, Intersect and Slice, each usable destructively or not. Modeling actions include Extrude with Subdivide, Randomize, Twist and Scale, and drawing helpers such as Join Strokes, Join Shapes and Concentric Shapes. Concept artists and illustrators block out objects by sketching. Because the drawn shapes stay editable until committed, ideas can be tried, adjusted and discarded quickly during blockout.

Key features

  • Sketch to geometry in one click
  • Add, Remove, Intersect and Slice booleans
  • Extrude, Randomize, Twist and Scale
  • Stroke and shape joining helpers
