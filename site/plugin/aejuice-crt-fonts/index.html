What AEJuice CRT Fonts does

AEJuice CRT Fonts is a footage pack that lets titles look as though they are glowing on an aging cathode-ray monitor. Rather than a font file, each character is a rendered clip with soft edges and chromatic aberration, ready for After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve or other editors.

There are 363 elements covering A to Z, digits 0 to 9 and special symbols, each isolated on its own looping clip against black at 2160 x 2160. Because characters are separate, you can stack and overlay them to spell anything. The AEJuice Pack Manager adds quick previews, drag and drop and favourites. Motion designers chasing a retro terminal vibe can build words in minutes.

Key features

  • 363 clips: letters A-Z, digits 0-9 and symbols
  • 4K, 2160 x 2160 looping clips on black
  • Blurred edges and chromatic aberration baked in
  • Pack Manager with preview, drag and drop, favourites
