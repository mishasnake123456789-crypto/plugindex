Blender add-on giving a non-destructive live preview of triangulated geometry converted to quads.

What Preview Quads does

Preview Quads, by maylog, shows how a triangulated mesh would look once converted to quads, live and without changing the mesh itself. It is listed under Mesh and Modeling.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Live triangle-to-quad preview
  • Non-destructive
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later
