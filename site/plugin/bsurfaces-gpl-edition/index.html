What Bsurfaces GPL Edition does

Bsurfaces GPL Edition is a retopology and surface modeling add-on for Blender. It is reached from the Edit tab of the 3D View sidebar while in Edit Mode, and it used to be bundled with Blender 4.1.

The package is about 31 KB, needs no special permissions and is licensed under GPL v3 or later. It works with Blender 4.3 and newer. Modelers who create clean topology over sculpts or scans, or who want to build meshes by drawing guides, can use it as a lightweight alternative to larger retopology suites.

Key features

  • Modeling and retopology in Edit Mode
  • Sidebar Edit tab access
  • Compact 31 KB download
  • For Blender 4.3 and newer
