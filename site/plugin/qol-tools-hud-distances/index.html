Shows a live on-screen distance between any two selected objects, lights, vertices or edges in Blender.

What Qol Tools: Hud - Distances does

QOL Tools: HUD - Distances displays a realtime measurement as soon as you select two items. They can be objects, lights, vertices, edges and more, and the readout appears on screen right away.

A lock mode pins the measurement to those two items so it stays while you work. It saves time compared with ruler tools when you just need to know how far apart things are. Because the figure updates as you move things, it works well for aligning parts and spacing objects precisely.

Key features

  • Realtime distance between any two items
  • HUD appears on selection
  • Lock mode keeps the measurement
  • Objects, lights, vertices and edges
