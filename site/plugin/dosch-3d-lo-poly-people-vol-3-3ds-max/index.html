What Dosch 3D - Lo Poly People Vol.3 - 3ds Max does

Volume three of Dosch Design's Lo Poly People collection brings another 100 human figures to 3ds Max. Each is a photo-real, fully textured low-polygon model built with 10,000 polygons.

Figures come in various poses, giving architectural and urban scenes a believable population without a heavy polygon cost. Visualization artists building plazas, offices or transport scenes can choose from the set to add scale and life.
