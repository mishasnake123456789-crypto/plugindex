What AEJuice Polygonal Animated Alphabet does

AEJuice Polygonal Animated Alphabet is an English typeface where each character is animated on its own. It includes all 26 letters, the numbers 0 to 9 and 10 additional animated elements.

Characters are dragged onto the timeline to spell out words and phrases, with selectable colours and customisable animation. Resolution can be resized at 30 fps, and the plugin interface comes in nine languages. The download contains After Effects project files and plugin files, 2.2 MB in total. After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve users making intros, title sequences, tutorials or kinetic typography can spell out text quickly.
