What AltView does

AltView offers fast viewport navigation in Blender through a library of 200 predefined views, covering standard front, top and side angles and diagonal ones. One hotkey does the switching.

The first press moves to the nearest preset ortho view, switches to orthographic projection and freezes rotation. A second press releases rotation and returns to perspective. This is particularly useful for people working with 3DConnexion devices, where accidental drift is common. No special permissions are required.

Key features

  • 200 predefined view angles
  • Single hotkey operation
  • First press snaps to nearest ortho view and locks rotation
  • Second press restores perspective
  • Suited to 3DConnexion users
