Builds brick walls in Blender with real geometry in many bond patterns, along straight, corner or curved paths.

What Ns Brick Wall Builder does

NS Brick Wall Builder generates physical bricks rather than texture tricks. Bond patterns range from standard and basketweave to irregular, English, Flemish, herringbone, quoin and course rubble, with presets you can load and edit.

Walls can run straight, turn corners or curve, and brick dimensions and materials can be customized. Architectural visualization artists and environment modelers can create detailed masonry quickly. Because every brick is geometry, close-up renders and displacement-free detail hold up better than flat textures.

Key features

  • Eight-plus brick patterns with presets
  • Straight, corner and curved paths
  • Custom brick dimensions
  • Unique materials per brick
