What Multiple Camera Render does

Multiple Camera Render is a Blender add-on by ivan-perevala for rendering from many cameras in sequence. When a camera is done, the next one starts, and it works for both stills and animations.

A preview option lets you check how the add-on will behave before committing to a full render. A select camera function makes a chosen camera the scene's active camera, and per-camera settings let you assign different render settings to each one. This saves time on multi-angle product shots or turntable-style setups.

Key features

  • Sequential rendering from multiple cameras
  • Works for images and animations
  • Preview option before a full render
  • Select camera makes it the active camera
  • Per-camera render settings
