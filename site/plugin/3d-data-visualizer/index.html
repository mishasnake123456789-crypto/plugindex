Blender add-on that converts spreadsheet data from Excel or CSV into animated 3D charts: lines, bars and side-by-side comparisons.

What 3D Data Visualizer does

3D Data Visualizer takes spreadsheet content and builds animated charts inside Blender. There are three modes: animated lines, animated bars, and comparison videos.

Camera movement and lighting are generated automatically, so the result is ready to render without further scene setup. It is a quick route for presenters, educators and motion designers who want eye-catching data stories without constructing every bar and curve by hand.

Key features

  • Loads spreadsheets in Excel or CSV form
  • Animated line graph mode
  • Animated bar graph mode
  • Data comparison video mode
  • Automatic camera movement and lighting
