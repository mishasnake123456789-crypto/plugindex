What Cinematic Compositor Addon does

Cinematic Compositor Addon gives Blender renders a filmic finish. It combines color grading, lens effects and film grain, working through compositing rather than requiring external software.

It is made by Extra 3D, available as a GPL-licensed Blender extension, and comes with documentation.
