What Soft Body 2 does

Soft Body 2 by MotionDesk makes squishy, deformable objects that collide with others inside After Effects. It takes shape layers, text and pre-comps, so logos and lettering can bounce and jiggle like jelly.

Two engines are offered. Quick includes a Wobble feature for automatic animation, and Advance, new in version 2, can also bend images and layers. Advanced settings tune the soft layer, and each soft body can have several colliders chosen through a multi-collider selector. Motion designers wanting playful physics without leaving the app get a direct route to it.

Key features

  • Quick and Advance engines
  • Handles shape layers, text and pre-comps
  • Multiple colliders per soft body
  • Automatic Wobble animation in Quick
  • Advance engine bends images and layers
