What Urbaniac! Streets, Roads And Highways does

Urbaniac! Streets, Roads And Highways is an asset set for building roadways in Blender. Modular pavement pieces cover sidewalk, street, intersection, highway and overpass sections.

The Early Access release has 106 assets, among them highway signs, traffic signals, barriers, lane markings and potholes. City builders and architectural visualization artists can assemble believable street scenes from these parts. The modular approach means pieces snap together into varied layouts, from quiet side streets to complex interchanges in a driving or city scene.

Key features

  • 106 assets in Early Access
  • Modular pavement for streets and highways
  • Elevated highway pieces
  • Signs, traffic lights and barriers
  • Road markings and potholes
