What Tickertools does

Tickertools provides ready-made animated text for motion graphics in Blender. Ten ticker styles are included, such as number counters, per-digit displays, timers, percentages, currency, slot-style word reveals, typewriter effects, countdowns, stepped values and sequential pop numbers.

Values, timing, formatting, bevel and extrusion are adjusted from the sidebar, and the text remains editable. Designers making dashboards, sports graphics, explainers or finance visuals can produce changing numbers without keyframing each digit. Because the text remains editable, changing a figure or format later is just a matter of adjusting a value.

Key features

  • Ten ticker styles
  • Counters, timers, currency and percentages
  • Typewriter and slot-style word reveals
  • Sidebar control of values, timing and bevel
