What [Addon] Pidgeon Tool Bag does

Pidgeon Tool Bag packages the Pidgeon Tools add-ons for Blender into one download meant to cover a whole rendering pipeline. It brings together the Super Image Denoiser, Super Fast Render, project handling, upscaling and render farm use.

The denoiser comes in a regular mode for stills and animation and a temporal mode for steadier animation, offering three quality tiers, control over individual passes and Multilayer-EXR handling. Other included tools optimize scenes to save render time, add camera control with color grading and effects, handle large-format renders such as posters, and upscale images with AI.

Key features

  • Super Image Denoiser with regular and temporal modes
  • Super Fast Render scene optimization
  • Super Advanced Camera with grading and effects
  • Large-format render and AI upscaling tools
  • Project management and render farm support
