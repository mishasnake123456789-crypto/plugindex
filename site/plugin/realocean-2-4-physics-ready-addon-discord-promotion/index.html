What Realocean 2.4 - Physics Ready Addon! Discord Promotion does

RealOcean 2.4, from 3dude, is a Blender addon for creating procedural oceans that interact with physics. It comes with 16 presets and works in both Cycles and Eevee, and it includes documentation.

The whole scene can be animated together with the ocean surface. Objects and the ocean react to each other, with adjustable wakes and waves, and a real-time foam mask shows foam as you work. It is suited to artists who need believable water for boats, shores or open-sea shots.

Key features

  • 16 ocean presets
  • Works in Cycles and Eevee
  • Physics for objects and the ocean
  • Adjustable wakes and waves
  • Real-time foam mask
