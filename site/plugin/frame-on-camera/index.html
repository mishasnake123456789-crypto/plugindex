Frame On Camera

Developer: KVD

FreeBlender
Download Free

Blender add-on that adds picture frames and look-through overlays such as keyholes or binoculars to camera renders.

What Frame On Camera does

Frame On Camera puts a border around the camera lens so a render looks as if it sits inside a picture frame.

Five frames are in the selector, and Look Through images mimic a keyhole, binoculars, rifle scope, open window or car window. You can use your own images, and multiple cameras can each carry a border, look-through filter or custom transparent image. The selected image stays attached to the camera.

Key features

  • Five picture frames
  • Look-through effects like keyholes and binoculars
  • Custom image support
  • Different overlays per camera
  • Image stays attached to the camera
