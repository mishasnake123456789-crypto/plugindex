What NewBlue Filters Ultimate does

NewBlue Filters Ultimate delivers four plugin collections to After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. In total there are 27 colour-enhancing filters and effects and more than 250 presets.

ColorFast 2 handles primary and secondary correction, white balance, colour masks and keyframeable four-point shape masks, with real-time vector, RGB parade, waveform and histogram scopes. Recreate offers Film Grain, Bleach Bypass, Day for Night, Duochrome, Old TV and Vignette; Recolor adds Color Replace, Gradient Tint and Selective Color; Refocus brings Tilt Shift, Dream Glow, Soft Focus, Light Leak and Fish Eye. Editors and colourists get film-style looks from one suite.
