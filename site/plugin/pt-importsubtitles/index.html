What pt_ImportSubtitles does

pt_ImportSubtitles by Paul Tuersley reads subtitle files and turns them into timed text layers in After Effects, and is also usable with Premiere Pro. Supported formats are Encore, SubRip SRT and WebVTT.

An optional black box can sit behind the text, with adjustable size, colour, opacity and an absolute vertical position. It builds a composition in the format suited to your output and includes Title Safe guides to help positioning. There are two routes for delivery: render with an alpha channel for use in your editing software, or place video beneath the subtitle layer in After Effects and render. Editors who need burned-in subtitles can save hours of typing.

Key features

  • Imports Encore, SRT and WebVTT
  • Optional backing box with size, colour and opacity
  • Absolute vertical position option
  • Creates a correctly formatted composition
  • Title Safe guides and alpha or burned-in output
