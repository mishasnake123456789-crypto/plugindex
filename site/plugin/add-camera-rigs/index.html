What Add Camera Rigs does

Add Camera Rigs, by WayneDixon, creates a camera rig in your scene and gives it a user interface so you can operate the camera from a panel rather than the bone level. It is listed under Camera.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
