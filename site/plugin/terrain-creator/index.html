Blender addon that combines procedural noise with drawing tools to place mountains and valleys, with water, erosion and foliage.

What Terrain Creator does

Terrain Creator generates landscapes in Blender from noise and then lets you shape them by drawing. Paint mountains and valleys onto the terrain itself rather than only adjusting noise values.

Presets cover a temperate landscape and a couple of desert types among others, and tools add water, erosion and foliage scattering. Environment artists can go from blank plane to a dressed landscape in one add-on.

Key features

  • Procedural noise plus drawing tools
  • Terrain presets
  • Water and erosion
  • Foliage scatter
