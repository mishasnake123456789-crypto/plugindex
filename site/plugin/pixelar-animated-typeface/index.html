What Pixelar - Animated Typeface does

Pixelar is an Animography typeface in which letters are assembled from fifty unique animated squares, giving a building-block style of animated pixel type. Motion is part of each letterform, so text arrives already moving.

It works with After Effects 2020 through 2024 and is useful for retro, gaming and digital-themed titles. Ordering four or more Animography typefaces earns a 25 percent discount, and a demo video is available. The blocky construction gives designers a flexible base for game-style intros, glitch titles and other digital looks.

Key features

  • Modular pixel-style letterforms
  • Characters from 50 unique animated squares
  • Built-in motion
  • Supports After Effects 2020 to 2024
  • 25% discount on four or more typefaces
