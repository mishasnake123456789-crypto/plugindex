What Wrinkle Deformer does

Wrinkle Deformer adds wrinkle detail to moving surfaces in Blender with a single click. Hand-sculpting wrinkle shapes for every pose is slow, and this addon from Blender Procedural generates them procedurally as the surface deforms.

You can adjust intensity, depth and distribution to suit the material, whether that is a face, a piece of clothing or skin. It works with both Cycles and Eevee and runs in Blender 4.0 through 4.5, with documentation included. Character animators and modelers who need believable folds without manual corrective work are the main audience.

Key features

  • One-click wrinkle generation
  • Adjustable intensity, depth and distribution
  • Suited to faces, clothing and skin
  • Works in Cycles and Eevee
  • Supports Blender 4.0 to 4.5
