Blender channel editor for Vertex Color data: preview, paint, gradients, randomize, convert and pack R, G, B and A channels.

What YL VertexColForge does

YL VertexColForge focuses on working with the individual channels of vertex colors or color attributes. A non-destructive preview powered by Geometry Nodes displays the red, green, blue or alpha channel in gray, and the previewed channel stays synced with the one being written.

Further features include painting, selection, gradients, randomization, conversion and packing. Game and stylized artists who store masks and data in vertex channels can edit them precisely.

Key features

  • Non-destructive single-channel grayscale preview
  • Channel fill, paint and select
  • Gradient generation and randomization
  • Conversion and packing of RGBA channels
