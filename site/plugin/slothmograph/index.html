Motion graphics system for Blender with cloners, effectors, MoSpline, MoText, fracture tools and animation presets.

What Slothmograph does

Slothmograph delivers a Cinema 4D-style motion graphics toolset in one Blender panel. Everything it generates is standard Blender data.

Thirteen cloner modes, including Grid, Radial, Honeycomb and Curve, distribute any object. Nineteen effectors modify position, scale, rotation and color non-destructively, stackable and maskable with eleven field types. MoSpline offers more than thirty 3D shapes with live thickness controls, and MoText, fracture tools and one-click animation presets are included. Artists who like layered, procedural motion graphics can build complex scenes while staying within Blender's native data.

Key features

  • 13 cloner modes
  • 19 stackable effectors with 11 field types
  • MoSpline with 30+ shapes
  • MoText and fracture tools
  • One-click animation presets
