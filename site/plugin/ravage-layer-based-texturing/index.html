What Ravage - Layer Based Texturing does

Ravage brings layer-based PBR texturing into Blender, in the style of Substance Painter, Quixel Mixer and ArmorPaint.

Included are workflow helpers, texturing tools that shortcut setups normally needing long node chains, and easier baking. Ravage systems can be mixed with your own nodes, or either used alone. Texture artists can stay in Blender for the full painting process. The approach will be familiar to anyone who has used dedicated painting applications, and it keeps texturing, masks and baking together inside the same Blender file.

Key features

  • Layer-based PBR texturing
  • Mix with your own nodes
  • Built-in texturing tools
  • Simplified baking
