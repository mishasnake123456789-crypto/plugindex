What AEJuice Audio Visualizer does

AEJuice Audio Visualizer supplies 20 animated scenes that react to music for lyric videos, podcasts and promos. They are managed through the Pack Manager, which provides quick previews, drop-in import and favourites.

Smart controls, batch export, editable fonts and universal expressions let you customise each scene. Right-to-left languages such as Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu are supported, output auto-resizes at 1920x1080 and 30 fps, and the interface comes in nine languages, English, Japanese, Spanish and Traditional Chinese among them. Editors who want an audio-reactive look without building one from scratch get ready-made options.
