What Emma does

Emma is an AI video editing assistant that lives inside Premiere Pro and takes over routine timeline chores. Instead of clicking through menus, you type what you want in a chat window and the assistant carries out the work.

Under the hood it can detect objects, recognise images and spot movement in footage, which lets it respond to requests that depend on what is actually on screen. Editors who spend much of their day on repetitive cleanup or assembly steps are the main audience, since the natural-language approach removes the need to learn scripts or panels for each small job.

Key features

  • Natural-language prompts for timeline tasks
  • Built-in chat interface
  • Object detection in footage
  • Image recognition and movement detection
