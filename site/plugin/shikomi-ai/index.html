An AI auto-editing plugin for Premiere Pro that structures footage by spoken content into an editable sequence.

What shikomi.ai does

shikomi.ai analyzes footage, recognizes speech and builds a structured edit, returned as a standard sequence you can change freely. It picks moments by what is said rather than only removing silences.

Four AI editor personalities, Storyteller, Methodical, Discoverer and Humanistic, can propose different approaches at the same time. Audio and subtitle data are deleted right after processing, and a free trial covers 30 minutes of raw footage with no expiry or credit card.

Key features

  • Speech-driven structural auto-editing
  • Four AI editor personalities
  • Multiple editors propose different perspectives
  • Standard, freely editable output sequence
  • Free 30-minute trial, data deleted after processing
