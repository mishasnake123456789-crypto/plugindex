What Slide Edge does

Slide Edge duplicates the selected edges and slides them inward or outward in Blender. It is built with retopology in mind but suits general modeling as well.

Nearby edges at connected poles slide along, boundary edges can be moved, and edges chosen at several places are handled at once. Sliding follows mouse movement for direct control. Retopologists tightening flow around poles and modelers refining borders can work with fewer manual adjustments.

Key features

  • Duplicates and slides edges inward or outward
  • Connected pole edges slide along
  • Handles boundary edges and multi-location selections
  • Mouse-driven sliding
