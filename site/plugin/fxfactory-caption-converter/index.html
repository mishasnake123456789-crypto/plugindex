What FxFactory Caption Converter does

FxFactory Caption Converter is a Mac application from Noise Industries for working with subtitles and captions, designed to work with Premiere Pro CC 2015 and later as well as Final Cut Pro X. It converts SRT and VTT files to iTT for Final Cut, and converts iTT back to SRT and VTT for sites such as Facebook and YouTube.

It flags incorrect or overlapping timecodes, detects file format and text encoding automatically so files made on Windows open properly, and can show two languages side by side for translation work. Editors who handle multilingual deliverables will use it most.

Key features

  • Converts SRT and VTT to iTT and back
  • Flags incorrect or overlapping timecodes
  • Auto-detects format and text encoding
  • Two languages shown side by side
  • macOS app for Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro X
