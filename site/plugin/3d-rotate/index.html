What 3D Rotate does

3D Rotate by anup-gupte brings a CAD-style way of rotating around a 3D reference in Blender, with visual snapping to help you land on exact orientations. It is categorised under Workflow.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
