What Advanced Boolean Toolkit does

Advanced Boolean Toolkit simplifies boolean operations in Blender. Four modes, Slice, Single, Multi-Object and Union, work from the active object.

One-click Difference, Union, Intersect and Advanced Slice actions are provided, along with modifier management and automatic organization into a dedicated collection. Hard-surface modelers can cut and combine shapes with less setup. Under the hood you get slice, Single, Multi-Object and Union modes, one-click Difference, Union and Intersect, modifier management and automatic dedicated collection.

Key features

  • Slice, Single, Multi-Object and Union modes
  • One-click Difference, Union and Intersect
  • Modifier management
  • Automatic dedicated collection
