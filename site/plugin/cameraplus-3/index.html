What Cameraplus_3 does

CameraPlus_3 is a Blender add-on that builds studio-style setups for product, industrial, e-commerce, motion graphics and cinematic work. Camera and light rigs lock to a target object, so switching the target moves the whole studio setup with it.

A Camera-to-View mode lets you reframe the shot in the viewport and then shifts the keyframes to the new angle once disabled. Camera distance, lighting presets and HDRI controls can be adjusted in real time, and presets are animatable. It is useful for anyone who needs repeatable, quick studio lighting and camera moves without assembling rigs from scratch.

Key features

  • Target-locked camera and light rigs
  • Camera-to-View with keyframe rewrite
  • Camera distance control
  • Lighting presets and HDRI controls
  • Animatable presets with live viewport updates
