What Agedfx 1.6 - Age Complete Scenes does

AgedFX is a Blender add-on from VICUBE Animation that weathers objects or entire scenes procedurally. It scans every material in the scene and layers on effects such as dust, dirt, scratches, cobwebs, moss and fog, working with any material built on the Principled BSDF.

Your original nodes stay untouched and are reconnected only where necessary. Dust settles on top with controllable inconsistency by viewing angle, edge dirt is fully supported in Cycles, and every object receives scratches and bumps. A metallic section adds imperfections that can also be rusted, so a clean scene can look lived-in quickly.

Key features

  • Detects all materials and applies procedural aging
  • Dust, edge dirt, scratches, bumps, cobwebs and moss
  • Works with any Principled BSDF material
  • Original nodes left intact
  • Metallic imperfections with rust
