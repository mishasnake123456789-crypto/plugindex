What Quad Sans does

Quad Sans is a typeface made for Blender that produces text as real mesh geometry rather than curves. Every letterform is built from quads, which keeps subdivision, deformation and sculpting predictable.

It works with Blender 4.5 through 5.2 and with render engines including Cycles, Eevee, Arnold, Octane and V-Ray. Robin Squares released it under the GPL license and includes documentation. A good fit for artists making 3D titles and logos who dislike messy triangulated text.

Key features

  • Text delivered as mesh geometry
  • Quad-only topology on all letterforms
  • Blender 4.5 through 5.2 support
  • Works with Cycles, Eevee, Arnold, Octane and V-Ray
  • GPL license
