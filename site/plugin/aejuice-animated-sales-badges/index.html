What AEJuice Animated Sales Badges does

AEJuice Animated Sales Badges is a template pack for promotional videos and sales pages. It holds 22 animated badges and 14 stickers, all built entirely from After Effects layers.

Colours are editable, animation speed can be adjusted, and each animation has detailed controls. In After Effects it works with the AEJuice Pack Manager for quick previews, drop-in import and favourites, and Premiere Pro is also supported. Marketers and editors making sale announcements, product spots or social ads can add discount and new-arrival call-outs in moments.

Key features

  • Built entirely with After Effects layers
