Automatically switches collection detail levels in Blender depending on animating, rendering or posing.

What Smart Simplify Pro does

Smart Simplify Pro keeps the viewport responsive by swapping detail levels for your collections as your task changes. When you animate, render or pose, it chooses the appropriate version for you.

That removes the repeated work of toggling heavy assets by hand. A global toggles panel manages the switcher states, and undo and redo are supported. Animators working in scenes full of dense geometry gain smoother playback while the final renders still use full-quality assets.

Key features

  • Auto LOD switching per collection
  • Modes for animating, rendering and posing
  • Global toggles panel
  • Undo and redo support
