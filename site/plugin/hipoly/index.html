What Hipoly does

HiPoly from Netron Systems automates repetitive high-poly modeling steps in Blender. Several operations that normally take multiple actions become shortcuts or one-click tools.

VertexHub creates a flat center point on a face with Ctrl+Alt+C, and VertexLink connects selected vertices to the last hub with Ctrl+Alt+V, adding only new edges. Snap Tools toggle vertex snapping with auto-merge and tuned settings, and Edge Operations select edges by a chosen sharpness angle. The aim is cleaner topology and shading.

Key features

  • VertexHub for flat center points
  • VertexLink without duplicate edges
  • Snap tools with auto-merge
  • Edge selection by sharpness angle
  • Keyboard shortcuts for common steps
