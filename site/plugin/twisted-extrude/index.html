What Twisted Extrude does

Twisted Extrude is a Blender add-on from Mark Kingsnorth that extrudes faces while twisting them, which makes curled geometry like pipes and tendrils, or more abstract effects. Its interactive interface behaves like Blender's standard extrude and the ZigZag Extrude tool.

You can rotate the extrusions and taper the effect, twisting can follow the face normal or local and global axes, and choose the number of side subdivisions. Faces can be extruded individually or as a group, and presets remember your parameters for later sessions.

Key features

  • Interactive twisted face extrusion
  • Rotation and taper controls
  • Twist along normal, local or global axes
  • Side subdivision control
  • Saveable presets
