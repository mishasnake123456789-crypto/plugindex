What Real Eye Motion does

Real Eye Motion gives characters lifelike eyes in Blender through a Geometry Nodes setup instead of hand-keyed animation. It works on any character with Eyelid and Eyeball bones, including Human Generator and Daz3D rigs.

Blinking occurs with a random possibility, a random look point drives the main eye movement, and subtle eyeball shaking adds a secondary motion. The look point can also follow an object in the scene. Documentation is included. Animators and character artists who want living, believable eyes during idle shots or crowd scenes will save a lot of keyframing.

Key features

  • Random blinking
  • Random look point for main eye movement
  • Subtle eyeball shake
  • Look point can follow a scene object
  • No keyframes required
