Blender interface utility with tab search, pinning and add-on management for a crowded N-Panel sidebar.

What Quick Tab does

Hugu's Works' Quick Tab helps when the N-Panel has too many tabs. Typing in the search bar narrows the tabs live and looks at panel labels inside tabs as well as tab titles.

Always Show mode copies panels dynamically so they remain visible on every sidebar tab, and pinning favorites keeps them at the top of results. Ctrl + Shift + T summons a separate search popup over the 3D Viewport, and add-ons can be managed with a click. Users with many add-ons locate tools faster.

Key features

  • Search that indexes internal panel labels
  • Always Show mode across tabs
  • Pinned favorites at the top of results
  • Search popup with Ctrl+Shift+T
  • One-click add-on management
