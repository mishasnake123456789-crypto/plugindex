What Synctools Pro For Sketchup does

SyncTools Pro is a SketchUp plugin for moving models between SketchUp and other 3D applications, including Blender, Maya and 3ds Max. It relies on a unified FBX-based exchange protocol and copies data in both directions.

SketchUp components and groups keep their hierarchy, and textures and basic material settings travel along. You can choose which elements to transfer, and units are matched automatically through smart scaling. A native SyncTools menu sits inside SketchUp. It suits architects and visualization artists who model in SketchUp but render or animate elsewhere.

Key features

  • Two-way sync with Blender, Maya and 3ds Max
  • Components, groups and hierarchy kept
  • Material and texture transfer
  • Selective transfer and automatic unit scaling
