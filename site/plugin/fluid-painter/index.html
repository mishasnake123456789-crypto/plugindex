What Fluid Painter does

Fluid Painter lets you paint liquids onto 3D surfaces in Blender by drawing a curve. It is built on Geometry Nodes and includes more than thirty presets that work in Eevee and Cycles.

Fluid presets cover general painting and leaks, while Fluid+ presets adapt to the surface underneath. A drop system finishes each shape with a droplet whose size, length and curve can be tuned and randomized. Basic and advanced parameters allow custom fluids. Motion graphics and product artists can add spills, paint, slime or blood without simulating.

Key features

  • Paint fluids along curves with Geometry Nodes
  • 30+ Eevee and Cycles presets
  • Fluid and surface-adapting Fluid+ presets
  • Drop system with adjustable shape
