What Automatic Duck Ximport AE does

Automatic Duck Ximport AE is a plug-in that brings Final Cut Pro X projects into After Effects. It reads .fcpxml files exported from Final Cut Pro X 10.1.2 or later and translates the timeline into an After Effects composition, for CS6 or CC.

The import runs through the Ximport AE dialog, where options can be set before the conversion starts. It operates on both Apple and Intel processors, and a QuickStart video is available as an introduction. Editors who finish cuts in Final Cut and then move to After Effects for graphics or compositing can use it to avoid rebuilding the sequence manually.

Key features

  • Reads .fcpxml from Final Cut Pro X 10.1.2 or later
  • Converts timelines into After Effects comps
  • Options set in the Ximport AE dialog
  • Supports Apple and Intel processors
