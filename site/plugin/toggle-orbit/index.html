Switches the 3D Viewport orbit method with a hotkey or header buttons in Blender.

What Toggle Orbit does

Toggle Orbit is a Blender plugin that changes the viewport's orbit method quickly. The author points to sculpting as a typical case, where people often change orbit styles.

The backtick key is mapped to an operator that toggles the method, and clashing hotkeys can be enabled or disabled in the add-on preferences. Buttons can also be added to the header. A small convenience for anyone with particular navigation habits. This keeps view navigation consistent with whichever task you are doing.

Key features

  • Backtick hotkey toggles orbit method
  • Header buttons
  • Hotkey conflicts configurable in preferences
  • Handy for sculpting
