What AEJuice CRT Backgrounds does

CRT Backgrounds offers 18 video overlays for projects that want a nostalgic, old-monitor appearance. They show patterns and space-themed elements on black backgrounds, with the glow and texture of a cathode-ray screen.

Clips are 4K at 30 FPS in H.264, and can be dropped into After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or other editors as videos with transparent or black backgrounds. Pack Manager features include fast preview, drag-and-drop import and favorites, with high-speed download.
