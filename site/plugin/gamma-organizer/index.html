What Gamma Organizer does

Gamma Organizer is a script by pixamoon for adjusting bitmap load gamma in 3ds Max, either across the whole scene or only on chosen bitmaps. Filters narrow the work to selected objects, a selected material or a bitmap type such as Normal Bump, VRay Normal Bump or Corona Normal Bump.

Its Normal Bump switching helps avoid V-Ray render crashes caused by merged models. Separate gamma and color space settings cover standard maps, VRayHDRI or VRayBitmap, and Arnold Image, and global input and output gamma can be changed. It supports V-Ray, Redshift, Octane and Arnold bitmaps, and the Pro version adds a listener showing maps with their slots, classes and locations.

Key features

  • Change load gamma scene-wide or on selected bitmaps
  • Filter by object, material or bitmap type
  • Normal Bump gamma switching for V-Ray and Corona
  • Separate settings for standard, VRay and Arnold maps
  • Supports VRay, Redshift, Octane and Arnold bitmaps
