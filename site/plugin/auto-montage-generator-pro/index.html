What Auto Montage Generator Pro does

Auto Montage Generator Pro, from Team Blender, assembles an edit for you. Configure the settings, click generate, and it lays out a montage from the video, image and audio clips you selected.

Random effects can be applied, including blur, brightness and contrast changes, color balance and speed variations, and transitions include crossfade, gamma cross and wipe. Clip duration, the number of clips and the starting channel are adjustable. It suits quick social edits, recaps and anyone who needs a rough cut fast.

Key features

  • Automated montage from selected clips
  • Random blur, brightness/contrast, color balance and speed
  • Crossfade, gamma cross and wipe transitions
  • Adjustable clip duration and clip count
  • Choose the starting channel
