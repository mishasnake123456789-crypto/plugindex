What Dosch 3D: Travel and Recreation does

Dosch 3D: Travel and Recreation collects 90 textured objects connected with trips and free time. The set includes suitcases, beach chairs, a kayak, camping equipment, a hammock, a sleigh, scuba gear and GPS or navigation systems.

Materials and textures can be freely adapted, scaling is adjustable, and elements can be switched on or off, made transparent and animated. Models are intended for illustrations, animations and visualizations in Cinema 4D. Travel brands, holiday advertising and infographic designers will find plenty of ready props.

Key features

  • 90 textured travel and recreation models
  • Suitcases, beach chairs, kayak, hammock, scuba gear
  • GPS and navigation systems
  • Editable materials, textures and scale
  • Animatable, switchable elements
