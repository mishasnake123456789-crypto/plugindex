What BOOM Library Cannons does

BOOM Library Cannons is a bundle for sound design in After Effects and Premiere Pro, built around 15 era-accurate cannons such as the 16-pounder, carronade and culverin. Recordings are 26-channel WAVs of blasts, impacts, flybys and mechanical handling noises.

The Construction Kit contains Foley impacts into water, sand, stone, wood, sails and dirt, with eight variations per cannon. The Designed pack adds five sets, Distant, Mechanic, Crisp, Loud and Soft, at six variations each. Shooting near a forest, a military base and an archery range gave natural outdoor tails. Files are 24-bit, 96 kHz WAV.

Key features

  • 15 era-accurate cannons
  • 26-channel blast, impact and flyby recordings
  • Foley impacts on water, sand, stone, wood and more
  • Designed pack with five sets
  • 24-bit, 96kHz WAV for Windows and Mac
