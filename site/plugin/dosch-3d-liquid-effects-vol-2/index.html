What Dosch 3D: Liquid Effects Vol.2 does

Dosch 3D: Liquid Effects Vol.2 offers 50 detailed static liquid models for advertising images of drinks, cosmetics, food and fruit, as well as presentations. They are meshes rather than simulations, so a splash or pour appears frozen in a chosen moment.

Scale is adaptable, and materials, textures and colors can be changed, with your own textures or labels applied. Elements can be switched on or off, set to varying transparency and animated. Product visualizers get a quick route to appetizing liquid shapes in Cinema 4D without running fluid simulations.

Key features

  • 50 detailed static liquid models
  • Aimed at drinks, cosmetics, food and fruit
  • Adjustable scale and lighting
  • Editable materials, colors and labels
  • Transparency and animation of elements
