What BTracer does

BTracer converts moving objects and particles into curves in Blender. A former member of the Blender 4.1 bundle, it is found on the Create tab of the 3D Viewport sidebar.

At roughly 17 KB it requires no special permissions, carries a GPL v2 or later license and targets Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. Motion designers who want trails, ribbons or path drawings of moving elements can generate them quickly. The generated curves are ordinary Blender objects, so they can be given thickness, materials or further animation afterward.

Key features

  • Converts objects and particles to curves
  • Animates the generated curves
  • Panel in Create sidebar tab
  • Lightweight download
