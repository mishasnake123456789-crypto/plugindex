What Blenrig 6 does

BlenRig 6 is a rigging system for characters in Blender that has been used on many of the Blender Open Movies.

An Interactive Rigging Assistant Guide leads through the process with text and images and takes over more than half of the work. Mesh Deform is a central deformation technique in the system. Riggers building high-quality characters can follow a structured workflow. Its guided approach makes advanced character rigs more approachable, offering studios and individual artists a route to production-quality deformation and controls.

Key features

  • Interactive Rigging Assistant Guide
  • Automates over 50% of rigging labor
  • Mesh Deform technique
