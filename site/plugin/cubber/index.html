Hard-surface shape generator for Blender that builds complex forms from selected faces or objects.

What Cubber does

Cubber generates intricate hard-surface forms from a mesh you already have. Select faces or an entire object, press "Cubber it", and a complex shape appears that you can then adjust.

Sliders cover every parameter, and a preset library is included: version 1.0 offers seven presets that get updated often. Inside Cubber sits Shaper, a complete add-on of its own for hard-surface and complex shapes. The tool works with both Cycles and Eevee, and the developer publishes parameter documentation on a website. It speeds up greebling and sci-fi detail work.

Key features

  • Generate shapes from selected faces or objects
  • Sliders for all parameters
  • Seven starter presets
  • Includes the Shaper add-on
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
