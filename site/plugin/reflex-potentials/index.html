What Reflex Potentials does

Reflex Potentials is an unusual research-oriented add-on from dartmeadow. It translates three natural buoyancies, Geological, Maritime and Aerospace, into software logic.

Buoyancy is represented as a shell surrounding connected data and data inputs, aimed at AI neural network research rather than conventional 3D art. The notes available give limited detail, so it will mainly interest experimental users exploring these concepts inside Blender.

Key features

  • Aerospace, Maritime and Geological buoyancies
  • Buoyancy modeled as a shell around data
  • Targeted at AI neural network research
