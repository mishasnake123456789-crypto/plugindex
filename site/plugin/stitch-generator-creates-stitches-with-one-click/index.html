What Stitch Generator - Creates Stitches With One Click does

Stitch Generator makes sewn seams on models quickly. Select edges, press Generate Stitch, and one of 50 handcrafted stitch styles, each shown with a preview, is built along them.

Options stay adjustable after generation. It works with Cycles and Eevee and is compatible with Blender 4.3 through 5.0 as an Extension. Documentation is included and updates are free for all users. It suits clothing, leather goods, plush toys and upholstery.

Key features

  • 50 previewed stitch styles
  • Generate from selected edges
  • Adjustable after creation
  • Blender 4.3 to 5.0 extension
