What Rotate Video Toolbar does

Rotate Video Toolbar, from Vakago Tools, is an extension for Premiere Pro and After Effects that handles clip orientation. Phone footage and mixed sources often arrive sideways, and this puts the fix a click away.

Select one or more clips from the timeline or composition and click a button to set the orientation, even across several clips at once. It opens as an extension through Window > Extensions > Rotate Video, and is compatible with Premiere Pro 15.0 or later and After Effects 18.0 or later.

Key features

  • Rotate video with toolbar buttons
  • Set orientation for several clips at once
  • Works on timeline or composition selections
  • Compatible with Premiere Pro 15.0+ and After Effects 18.0+
