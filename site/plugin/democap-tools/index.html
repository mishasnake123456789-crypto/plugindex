What DEMoCap Tools does

DEMoCap Tools is a Blender add-on from DragonDreams that brings motion capture made with DEMoCap into Blender. It imports both free captures and exported captures.

A live connection streams capture data from DEMoCap straight into Blender without importing it first. Correction bones are among the tools provided for refining imported motion. Importing captures requires the Drag[en]gine Import/Export extension. Animators working with DEMoCap will find it connects their capture sessions to scenes.

Key features

  • Imports free and exported DEMoCap captures
  • Live streaming from DEMoCap into Blender
  • Correction bones for imported motion
  • Needs the Drag[en]gine Import/Export extension for imports
