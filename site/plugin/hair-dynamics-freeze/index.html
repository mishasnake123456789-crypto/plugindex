What Hair Dynamics Freeze does

Hair Dynamics Freeze takes the position of hair physics at a chosen frame and bakes it, so you get a static hairstyle shaped by dynamics rather than a full simulation.

It works with Blender hair particles. A demo video and documentation are included, and it is released under the GPL.

Key features

  • Bakes hair physics to a specific frame
  • Freezes dynamics into a static shape
  • Works with hair particles
  • Demo video included
