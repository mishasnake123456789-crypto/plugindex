A silence cutter for Premiere Pro that finds quiet sections and removes them or marks them.

What Shhh does

Shhh scans footage for silence and either cuts it out or drops markers, which speeds up editing of interviews, podcasts and talking-head videos. The process is non-destructive, so original clips stay intact.

Simple and advanced settings tune the cuts. It can work across multiple clips and tracks, shows a waveform preview with zoom and pan, and has experimental nested sequence support with a replace nest option. It is intended to speed up the first rough pass on spoken material, leaving the finer judgment calls to the editor.

Key features

  • Automatic silence detection and removal
  • Non-destructive editing
  • Markers as an alternative to cutting
  • Multi-clip and multi-track cutting
  • Waveform preview with zoom and pan
