Blender add-on that aligns and distributes objects with six alignment and six distribution modes, like Illustrator.

What Quick Align does

Null Station's Quick Align brings Adobe Illustrator's alignment tools into the Blender 3D Viewport. Six alignment modes snap objects to the left, center horizontally, right, top, center vertically or bottom.

Six distribution modes space objects evenly horizontally or vertically. Smart Axis Detection works out the horizontal and vertical axes from the current view, and Manual Axis Control lets you override it. Layouts, kitbashing and set dressing become tidier in a few clicks.

Key features

  • Six alignment modes
  • Six distribution modes
  • Smart Axis Detection from the view
  • Manual Axis override
  • Illustrator-style precision
