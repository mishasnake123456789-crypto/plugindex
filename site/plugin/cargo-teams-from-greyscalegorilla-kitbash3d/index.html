What Cargo Teams from Greyscalegorilla + KitBash3D does

Cargo Teams combines the libraries of Greyscalegorilla and KitBash3D on one platform that teams use together. It reaches Cinema 4D as well as other 3D applications, including Blender, Unreal, Houdini and Maya.

The pool holds more than 20,000 KitBash3D models for environments, vehicles and props, plus over 3,500 Greyscalegorilla materials, textures and HDRIs, with World Kits and plugins on top. Access is centralized and licensing scales with the team, so studios can share workflows instead of passing files around. It is built for studios that need production-ready assets available to everyone.
