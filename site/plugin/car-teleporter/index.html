What Car Teleporter does

Car Teleporter is a Blender plugin from elmas that brings ready-rigged cars into your scene. Over 90 unique cars are included, with more to be added, all available through a simple menu.

Doors, hood and trunk open, and the cars are set up for physical animation. Documentation is included, and it runs on Blender 2.8 through 4.2. Quick vehicles for street scenes and animation. Instead of searching for and rigging vehicles yourself, you can add one from the menu and begin animating or staging scenes immediately.

Key features

  • 90+ rigged cars
  • Openable doors, hood and trunk
  • Set up for physical animation
  • Blender 2.8 through 4.2
