What Hardops 26: Lead does

Hardops 26: Lead, by TeamC, is a toolkit for hard surface work in Blender. It leans on a modifier workflow that can stay non-destructive or be made destructive when you choose.

Tools are reachable from menus, pies, the N-panel or the active tool, and pressing Q brings up a dynamically updating menu. It has a boolean cutting system, an interactive modifier dot system in the viewport and smart shapes for starting out. Mirroring is also covered. Concept artists and product designers can move from blockout to polished models quickly.

Key features

  • Non-destructive or destructive modifier workflow
  • Menu, pie, N-panel and tool access
  • Boolean cutting system
  • Interactive modifier dots and smart shapes
