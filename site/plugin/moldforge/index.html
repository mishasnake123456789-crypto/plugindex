FreeBlender

Blender add-on that turns a mesh into printable silicone mold parts with registration, splits and volume estimates.

What MoldForge does

MoldForge builds mold systems from a 3D model for casting. It produces watertight, registered parts ready for slicing and printing, and displays live estimates of volume and weight.

Choose from Tray/Open Pour, Direct Printed Mold or Silicone Pour Box molds. Splits register themselves through contoured or flat parting using cone keys or interlocking teeth, and multi-part splitting is supported. Makers and prop builders can go from a sculpt to a mold quickly.

Key features

  • Pour box, printed mold and open-tray mold types
  • Self-registering splits with cone keys or teeth
  • Multi-part splitting
  • Live volume and weight estimates
  • Watertight printable output
