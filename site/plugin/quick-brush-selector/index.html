What Quick Brush Selector does

Quick Brush Selector by sum studio offers a menu for picking brushes without browsing through the toolbar or asset shelf. You can customize the menu so your favorite brushes are always close.

It operates in sculpting, texture painting and Grease Pencil drawing, so the same habit carries across different kinds of work. Documentation and a demo video are provided, which helps artists who change brushes dozens of times per session.

Key features

  • Customizable brush selection menu
  • Usable in Sculpt mode
  • Usable in Texture Paint mode
  • Usable in Grease Pencil Draw mode
  • Documentation and demo video
