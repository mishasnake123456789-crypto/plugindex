What Npr Framerate Mixer does

NPR Framerate Mixer is a Blender plugin for stylized animation where characters move at a lower frame rate than the camera. It lets an object or rig animated on twos, threes or any rate sit comfortably with a camera moving on ones.

It produces an empty that stores the camera offset for the held frames. Parent your character or rig to that empty and the stepped look is preserved while the camera remains smooth. Useful for anime and other non-photorealistic projects.

Key features

  • Mixes 2s/3s character animation with 1s camera
  • Bakes camera offset into an empty
  • Parent rigs to the baked empty
  • Preserves stepped animation timing
