What Tracegenius Pro 4.7 - Advanced 2D Image To 3D Mesh Tracer does

TraceGenius Pro 4.7 converts 2D artwork into 3D geometry inside Blender. It uses Blender's built-in Grease Pencil tracing engine to extract outlines or faces from JPG, PNG and WebP files.

Tracing is tuned with color and brightness thresholds and an invert option. Shapes can then be extruded and beveled, and individual parts separated after generation. Auto-Material Setup unwraps the object and applies a PBR material, and Retrace updates the mesh without undoing work. Logo designers, signage makers and motion artists turn flat graphics into 3D quickly.

Key features

  • Traces outlines or faces from images
  • Color and brightness thresholds
  • Extrude, bevel and separate parts
  • Auto-Material Setup with PBR
  • Retrace without starting over
