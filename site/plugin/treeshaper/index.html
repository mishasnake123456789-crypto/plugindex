Tree generator for Blender with trunk, root and branch creation, grow zones and quad-based geometry in version 2.0.

What Treeshaper does

TreeShaper grows trees from a minimal interface, taking care of trunks, roots and branches so you do not build limbs by hand.

Grow zones and no-grow zones steer branches and roots into custom shapes, with dynamic regrowth to update the tree. Version 2.0 introduces a custom quad meshing system for quad-based topology, clean UVs and additional shaping controls. It suits environment artists and game developers who need varied, well-structured trees quickly.

Key features

  • Instant trunk, root and branch generation
  • Grow and no-grow zones
  • Dynamic regrowth
  • Quad-based geometry and clean UVs in v2.0
