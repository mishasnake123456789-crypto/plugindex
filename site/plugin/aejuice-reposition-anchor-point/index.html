What AEJuice Reposition Anchor Point does

AEJuice Reposition Anchor Point is an After Effects plugin that shifts anchor points across every selected layer in one go. It preserves existing keyframes, so animated layers are not thrown off.

Nine directions are available for placing the anchor, such as corners, edges and center, and it integrates with kBar. It supports After Effects CC 2015 through CC 2022 and newer. Setting pivots on many layers before rotating or scaling becomes a quick task.

Key features

  • Batch repositions anchors on selected layers
  • Works with existing keyframes
  • 9 placement directions
  • kBar integration
  • Compatible with CC 2015 and newer
