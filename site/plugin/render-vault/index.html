What Render Vault does

Render Vault is a camera and render management add-on that gathers render settings into one panel. You get one-click resolution presets, per-render choice of PNG, JPEG or EXR, and timestamped filenames that prevent overwriting earlier renders.

Per-camera overrides cover lens, resolution and denoise settings, and cameras can be added from the current view or refreshed in a list. Batch rendering runs across multiple cameras. It suits product shots, architectural views and any project needing many angles rendered with consistent control.

Key features

  • 1080p, 4K and Square resolution presets
  • PNG, JPEG or EXR output
  • Timestamped filenames
  • Per-camera lens, resolution and denoise overrides
  • Batch render across cameras
