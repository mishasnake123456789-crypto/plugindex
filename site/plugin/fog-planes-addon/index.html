What Fog Planes Addon does

Fog Planes Addon supplies Blender with ready-made fog for still renders and animations. It pairs thin 2D fog planes with a shader, giving atmosphere without heavy volumetrics, and it works in Cycles and Eevee.

Thirty planes are organized by area of use and can be switched quickly. The fog shader sits right in the add-on panel, modifiers subdivide the textures for depth and a sense of motion, and every shader change can be keyframed. A fog emitter allows manual motion or loops. It is aimed at environment artists building nature scenes.

Key features

  • 30 categorized fog planes
  • Fog shader built into the add-on panel
  • All shader changes keyframable
  • Fog emitter for motion and loops
  • Works in Cycles and Eevee
