What Blace 2 - AI Face Detection & Blurring does

Blace 2 is an AI plugin for anonymizing people in video. Apply it to a clip and faces are found immediately and blurred, which helps with privacy requirements for news, documentary and street footage.

It can also detect and blur whole human bodies, and it performs well even on computers without a dedicated GPU. Bit depths of 8, 16 and 32 are supported. The detection results can drive other effects, such as a custom blur you design yourself, and only a few settings are needed to control detection. It runs in After Effects and Premiere Pro.

Key features

  • Instant AI face detection and blur
  • Body detection and blurring
  • Good performance on CPU-only systems
  • 8, 16 and 32-bit support
  • Detections can drive other effects
