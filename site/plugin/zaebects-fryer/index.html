What Zaebects Fryer does

Zaebects Fryer is a single-purpose After Effects plugin that produces a fried, over-processed look. It does this by running its processing on an image again and again across several passes.

The first release, version 1.0, is free and runs on After Effects from CC 2019 through 2026. Because it concentrates on one effect, it is simple to apply to footage or stills when you want crunchy, degraded imagery for memes, glitch styles or stylized edits.
