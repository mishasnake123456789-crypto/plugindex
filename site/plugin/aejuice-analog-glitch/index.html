What AEJuice Analog Glitch does

Analog Glitch is a set of 70 animated glitch graphics with a retro, signal-interference feel. They are pre-rendered as 4K H264 videos at 3840 by 2160 and can be dragged onto a timeline and combined using blending modes.

They suit music videos, social posts and ads aiming for a Matrix or cyberpunk atmosphere. Works in After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and any other video editor. The AEJuice Pack Manager adds fast preview, drag-and-drop import and favorites, with quick downloads.
