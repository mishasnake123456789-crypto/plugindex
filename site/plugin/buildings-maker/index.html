A Blender add-on that randomly assembles buildings from libraries of bases, rooftops and add-on parts.

What Buildings Maker does

Buildings Maker generates many different buildings in Blender by combining parts you supply. Libraries hold bases, rooftops and pluggable addons, and the tool picks among them at random to create vertical structures, so a skyline or city block can be filled in quickly.

Bases repeat until the last floor, rooftops cap each building, and addons attach details. You can generate as many buildings or vertical elements as you wish, and the add-on works across Blender 2.9 through 4.2 with documentation. City builders and environment artists needing variety without modeling each tower will find it practical.

Key features

  • Random assembly from part libraries
  • Bases repeat to the last floor
  • Rooftops and pluggable addons
  • Any number of buildings
  • Blender 2.9 to 4.2
