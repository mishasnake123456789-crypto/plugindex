What Quick Palette Addon does

Quick Palette Addon brings palette management into Blender. You can build your own palettes and apply colors to materials, lights or other parts of the scene without leaving the program.

Built-in color collections are grouped by tone and mood, including Warm, Cool, Neutral, Dark, Light, Vibrant, Pastel and Muted sets. This helps artists establish a consistent color scheme quickly and experiment with moods. Key capabilities include built-in collections by tone and mood and apply colors to materials, lights and more.

Key features

  • Built-in collections by tone and mood
  • Apply colors to materials, lights and more
  • Create and organize your own palettes
