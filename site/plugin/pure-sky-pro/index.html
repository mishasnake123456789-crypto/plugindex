What Pure-Sky Pro does

Pure-Sky Pro from 3DVision builds skies, volumetric clouds and lighting environments in Blender. It works with Eevee Next and Cycles and arrives with a library of presets.

There are 21 sky presets, 22 cloud presets and 6 atmospheric fog presets, and custom presets can be exported and shared. Cloud types such as cumulus, cumulonimbus and altocumulus evolve into formations, and Hybrid Mode blends procedural skies with HDRI maps. Collision Mode lets clouds interact with objects. Landscape artists and filmmakers can build convincing weather.

Key features

  • 21 sky, 22 cloud and 6 fog presets
  • Realistic evolving cloud types
  • Hybrid Mode mixing procedural sky and HDRI
  • Collision Mode
  • Eevee Next and Cycles support
