What My Best Pie Menu Ever does

My Best Pie Menu Ever places shortcuts for frequently used tools behind a pie menu summoned with the W key in Blender. Favorite brushes, their parameters and common functions are collected in one radial layout.

The upper part of the menu is arranged by mode, while the lower part holds general utility functions. It covers Object, Edit, Texture paint and further modes, so the same key serves modeling, painting and sculpting. Artists who dislike hunting through menus can speed up daily work.

Key features

  • W-key pie menu
  • Upper section organized by mode
  • Utility functions in lower section
  • Covers Object, Edit and Texture modes
