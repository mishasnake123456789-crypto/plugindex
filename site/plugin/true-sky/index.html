Blender add-on that builds a to-scale Earth atmosphere with planet-scale scattering from street level to lunar orbit.

What True Sky does

True Sky creates an Earth atmosphere in Blender that can be flown through, from the ground all the way into space. The sky mesh moves with you so parallax and curvature stay consistent.

Rayleigh, Mie and ozone layers are all modeled with real-world coefficients, and one rig handles flights to space or the moon and back. Celestial objects include the Moon, planets, asteroid belts and a metal orb, with auroras and magnetosphere glows. Space and aerial scene artists get believable scale.

Key features

  • Planet-scale sky mesh
  • Rayleigh, Mie and ozone layers
  • Single rig from ground to moon
  • Moon, planets and auroras
