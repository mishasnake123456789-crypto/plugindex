What Realtime Cycles does

Cycles Y's Realtime Cycles extends Blender's built-in Cycles engine without changing your workflow. It introduces a realtime mode advertised as up to 15 times quicker than regular Cycles, along with a module for photon-mapped rendering.

Improved algorithms and smarter path tracing cut needless calculation in complex scenes and animations, and reworked light paths end low-impact paths early to reduce overhead. Artists wanting faster previews and renders can stay in familiar tools.

Key features

  • Realtime mode for Cycles
  • Photon-mapped rendering module
  • Smart path tracing optimizations
  • Early termination of low-impact paths
  • Fits the existing Cycles workflow
