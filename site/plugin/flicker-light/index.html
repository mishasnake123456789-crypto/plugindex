What Flicker Light does

Flicker Light brings natural or stylized flickering to any light source in a Blender scene. It works with point, spot, area and sun lights as well as emission materials, covering candles, neon signs, campfires and failing bulbs.

Parameters such as energy, intensity, speed and randomness can be customized, and keyframe animation support lets flickering lights sit in the animation timeline. Artists creating moody interiors, horror scenes or night environments can add convincing light instability without keying brightness by hand.

Key features

  • Flicker on point, spot, area and sun lights
  • Works with emission materials
  • Energy, intensity, speed and randomness controls
  • Keyframe animation support
