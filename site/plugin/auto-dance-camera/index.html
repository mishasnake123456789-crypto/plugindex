What Auto Dance Camera does

Auto Dance Camera produces cinematography for character dance animations automatically. Pick an armature and it creates a new active camera called Auto Dance Camera with a sequence of moves.

Shots come from 540 possible combinations, and only shots that suit the character's pose are kept. This is helpful for MMD-style and music-video creators who want varied, usable camera work without manually keying each angle.

Key features

  • One-click camera motion from the armature
  • 540 possible shot combinations
  • Only pose-appropriate shots are used
  • Creates a new active camera
