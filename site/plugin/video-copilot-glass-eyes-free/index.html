What Video Copilot Glass Eyes (Free) does

Glass Eyes lets you put artificial eyes onto footage in After Effects. Apply the effect to a blank solid, motion track the eye position, and adjust the look.

Controls include pupil size and color, custom pupils, googly eye speed and amount, and highlight rotation. People use it for creepy clips, comedy, giving objects personality, and playful edits of existing scenes. It is a free Video Copilot plug-in. The effect is simple to set up on a solid layer, so it works well for quick gags as well as more careful fixes.

Key features

  • Custom pupils
  • Pupil size and color controls
  • Googly eye speed and amount
  • Highlight rotation control
