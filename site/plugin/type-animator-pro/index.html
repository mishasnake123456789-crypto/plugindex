What Type Animator Pro does

Type Animator Pro generates animated 3D text effects. Enter your text, choose one of 11 animation styles, adjust the controls and click Build to create the setup in the scene.

Styles range from Typewriter and Pop In to Spiral Zoom and Letter Orbit, with styling controls such as font size. Motion designers can make titles quickly. In practice that means 11 ready-made text animations, build workflow in the scene and font size and styling controls.
