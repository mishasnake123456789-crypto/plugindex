What Pixel Lab Aura: Professional 3D Environments for Octane does

Pixel Lab Aura offers 13 ready-built 3D environments for Cinema 4D, prepared for Octane. They act as backplates for animations and product shots and arrive with lighting and texturing already dialed in at high resolution.

Each studio includes an Xpresso rig with sliders for lights, temperature, color and glows. The collection spans a variety of spaces, from lofts to more futuristic sets. Designers who want a polished backdrop for a product or character can open a scene and adjust it rather than constructing a set.
