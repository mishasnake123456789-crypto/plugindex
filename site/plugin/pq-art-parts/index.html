What PQ Art Parts does

PQ Art Parts from Peter Quinn is a collection of hand-made elements for After Effects and Premiere Pro. Transitions, frames, edges, single brush strokes, bubbles and lower thirds give layers a crafted, tactile look.

Each part was painted, inked or sketched in multiples, filmed at very high resolution and cleaned. Clips are sequenced at either 12 or 24 fps, and versions from CS6 to 2022/CC are supported. Editors and motion designers who want to move away from slick digital graphics can layer the pieces for organic texture.

Key features

  • Handmade transitions
  • Frames for customising any layer
  • Edges, brush strokes and bubbles
  • Lower thirds
  • 12 or 24 fps clips
