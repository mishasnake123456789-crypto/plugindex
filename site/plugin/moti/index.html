One-click After Effects tool that tags layers by role and generates promo, title and social animation keyframes.

What MoTi does

MoTi by Ti Nyain automates promo-style animation in After Effects. You tag layers once with roles such as KV, SV, Title, Badge, Caption, BG, BGElem and Btn, choose a motion style, and a single click writes the keyframes into the active composition.

Style groups cover key visuals (Fly In, Scale Pop, Slide Up, Burst, Zoom), taglines and badges (Wiggle, Hover, Heartbeat, Pop-up). Global controls handle speed, outro and background fade. Layered PSD or Illustrator files import through Smart Import, and the result renders from After Effects or Media Encoder.

Key features

  • Layer tagging with KV, Title, Badge, Caption and more
  • KV, Tagline and Badge motion style sets
  • Global speed, outro and background fade controls
  • Smart Import for PSD and Illustrator files
  • One-click Run generates animation in the active comp
