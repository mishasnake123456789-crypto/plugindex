What Easy Rb Pro 1.2.1 (Easy Rigid Body Physics) does

Easy RB Pro simplifies rigid body physics in Blender by organizing the workflow into panels. A fracture panel gives a shortcut into Blender's Cell Fracture add-on, which must be activated first, and an RB Emitter panel releases objects with rigid body physics and many options.

A Duplicate panel arranges objects as a grid, circle, cube or sphere pattern, while Physics and Kinematics panels manage the rigid body state. A single click links the selected objects with an elaborate constraint chain. The Bake panel bakes, deletes or resets simulations, and the Switch panel swaps visibility between an object and its fragments.

Key features

  • Cell Fracture access from a panel
  • Rigid body emitter with many options
  • Duplicate in grid, circle, cube or sphere
  • One-click constraint chains
  • Bake, delete or reset simulations
