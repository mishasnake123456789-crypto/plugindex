What Lumibrush does

LumiBrush turns lighting into a painting action. Sweep a brush over the scene and genuine light shows up along the stroke, complete with shadows, falloff and bounce.

Results render live in both Cycles and EEVEE, and each stroke remains fully adjustable afterward. Documentation is included. Lighting artists who prefer an intuitive approach to placing lights may find it faster. Painting light this way makes it easy to try highlights and accents where they are needed before committing to a final setup.

Key features

  • Brush-based light painting
  • True shadows, falloff and bounce
  • Live in Cycles and EEVEE
  • Adjustable strokes after painting
