Widens or tightens the spacing of selected objects in 3ds Max, such as a row of fence posts, by scaling their relative positions.

What Position scaler does

From Chuggnut, Position scaler changes the gaps between selected objects in 3ds Max while leaving the objects untouched. It scales the relative positions of the selection, so a layout can be stretched apart or pulled closer together.

A typical job is moving already placed fence posts further apart. It operates on the current selection and is offered at version 2.3.5, free to download from the developer's page. Anyone adjusting repeated elements, such as columns, lamps or trees, can use it instead of moving each item by hand.

Key features

  • Scales relative positions of selected objects
  • Works on the current selection
  • Spreads or tightens repeated elements
  • Version 2.3.5
