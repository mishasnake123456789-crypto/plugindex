What Handy Surface Detail does

Handy Surface Detail gives hard-surface and costume artists a library of more than 100 small meshes used to decorate larger forms. They are sorted into four groups: net or grid, armor and cloth decoration, architecture, and boolean hard surface.

They are spread over a target surface using Blender's internal Tissue add-on, and a helper script is included to simplify the setup. It is a quick way to add intricate surface complexity to props and characters.

Key features

  • Over 100 detail meshes
  • Net/grid, armor, architecture and boolean categories
  • Uses the internal Tissue add-on
  • Helper script included
