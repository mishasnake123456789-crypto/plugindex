What BOOM Library Monsters & Beasts does

BOOM Library Monsters & Beasts combines animal recordings with human elements and props to create roars, hisses, bellows, screeches and moans. It supports After Effects, Premiere Pro and other WAV-capable software.

The Construction Kit has 737 files with 4,637 sounds at 24-bit, 192 kHz, which allows heavy pitching without losing detail. The Designed package offers 320 sounds covering huge to small creatures, each with attack, death, idle, pain and scream in four variations. A bundle brings both together, around 14 GB. PDF and XLS content info is supplied.

Key features

  • Construction Kit with 4,637 sounds at 192kHz
  • Designed set of 320 creature sounds
  • Attack, death, idle, pain and scream per creature
  • Huge, large, medium and small creature classes
  • Bundle of both editions
