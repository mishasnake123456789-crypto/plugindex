What Intersection of a Line and a Sphere - MAXScript function does

The function, written in MAXScript for 3ds Max, calculates line and sphere or circle intersections using an approach from Paul Bourke. The result is computed from the line and sphere parameters.

The discriminant tells the story: below zero means no intersection, zero means tangent at one point, and above zero gives two points. For a segment, u values reveal whether it lies outside or inside the sphere. For circles, the four points must have no three in a line and must not lie in one plane. It is aimed at scripters needing geometry calculations in their own tools.

Key features

  • Line and sphere intersection from Paul Bourke's method
  • Discriminant identifies no hit, tangent or two intersections
  • u values distinguish segments inside or outside the sphere
  • Circle case with stated point conditions
