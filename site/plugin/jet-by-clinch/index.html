What Jet by Clinch does

Jet by Clinch is an extension for After Effects aimed at advertising production. It converts an ordinary project into a fully dynamic template, either video or HTML5, so that content can be swapped without rebuilding the composition.

It also lets you organise and preview creative templates on the Clinch platform from within the app. Version 2.0.1 brought a redesigned interface and a new Text Source editor, and the extension needs After Effects 17.7 or later. Ad designers and production teams who deliver many variations of one creative will find it keeps the template work in a single place.

Key features

  • Converts a project to a dynamic video or HTML5 ad template
  • Manage and preview templates on the Clinch platform
  • Redesigned interface in version 2.0.1
  • New Text Source editor
  • Requires After Effects 17.7 or newer
