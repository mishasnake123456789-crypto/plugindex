Blender add-on for managing scene lights with light groups, IES texture previews and quick IES-ready light creation.

What Easylight does

Easylight organizes and speeds up lighting in Blender, working with both Cycles and Eevee. It creates and edits light groups, so power, switching, color and temperature change for several lights at once, even emissive mesh objects.

Its management system builds on Blender's own collections, so lights can be selected, moved or deleted by drag and drop, even ones not made with the add-on. An IES Light Manager loads a library of IES files, generates previews and applies one with a click, and quick add creates IES-ready lights. Frame-based animation of group settings is still in development.

Key features

  • Light groups for power, toggle, color and temperature
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
  • IES library manager with generated previews
  • Quick add of IES-ready lights
  • Collection-based light management
