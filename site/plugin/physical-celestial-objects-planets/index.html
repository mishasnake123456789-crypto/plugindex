What Physical Celestial Objects - Planets does

Physical Celestial Objects - Planets is a self-contained Blender tool for assembling planets at true scale with high visual quality. It is designed to stay responsive in the viewport, so you can work with large planetary bodies without long waits.

The planets work in Cycles as well as Eevee, and the add-on supports Blender 3.3 through 5.0. It ships as a Blender Extension with documentation and is released under the GPL license. Space scene creators, science visualization artists and sci-fi concept designers can use it to place convincing worlds in their shots.
