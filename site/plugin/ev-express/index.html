What Ev Express does

Ev Express is a Blender add-on from CGOnFire that speeds up presenting a model in EEVEE. A single click on a thumbnail places 11 lights, a backdrop and probes, so a studio-style scene is ready quickly.

Two light rigs use tuned settings for shadows and contact shadows, and all lights have adjustable color and size, and the color presets draw on Greg Zaal's HDRIs. There are 32 large thumbnails, smoke is added when a preset includes it, and volumetrics can be enabled in two button presses with an adjustable color.

Key features

  • 32 thumbnails placing 11 lights, backdrop and probes
  • Two light rigs with tuned shadow settings
  • Adjustable light color and size
  • Color presets inspired by Greg Zaal HDRIs
  • Volumetrics in two button presses
