What Browzmaster Pro does

BrowzMaster Pro lets you browse 3D models and assets stored on your hard drive from inside Blender. It works offline and imports with a click.

Supported formats include OBJ, FBX, ABC, GLB, GLTF, STL, SVG, BVH, PLY and USD. Folders are scanned to locate models, and you can select several, even of different types, to import together. After import, management options let you select and delete what was brought in. It suits artists with large local model libraries.

Key features

  • Offline browsing of local 3D assets
  • Ten common formats supported
  • One-click import per model
  • Multi-select import of mixed types
  • Folder scanning and post-import management
