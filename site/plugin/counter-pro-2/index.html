What Counter Pro 2 does

Counter Pro 2, by Md Mahadi, produces animated number counters inside After Effects. Counting graphics turn up in statistics, countdowns, finance videos and infographics, and this tool sets them up without building expressions manually.

The tool is at version 2.1, which fixed its license framework, and it supports a broad range of After Effects releases from CC 2014 through 2025. A demo video is provided to show how the counters behave. It is a practical pick for motion designers who regularly animate figures.
