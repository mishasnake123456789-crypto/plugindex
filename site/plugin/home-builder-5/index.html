What Home Builder 5 does

From AndrewPeel, Home Builder 5 is an add-on for modeling houses architecturally in Blender. It is distributed through Blender's extensions platform and targets Blender 5.0 and newer.

Builds exist for Windows on x64 and Arm, macOS on Intel and Apple Silicon, and Linux. Installation is by dragging into a Blender window or from disk in preferences, and the license is GPL v3 or later. Current version 5.1.0 was updated in February 2026. It is listed as free.
