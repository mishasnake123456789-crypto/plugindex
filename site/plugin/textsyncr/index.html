What textsyncr does

textsyncr is a tool by Joachim Holler for turning spoken audio into timed markers in After Effects. You import a spoken track, paste its transcription, and the tool aligns the words to the audio as layer markers.

The markers can drive subtitles, lyrics and the Monkey tools, and automatic captions can be generated and controlled. Speech analysis works for English only. Subtitles or lyrics take just a few clicks, making it useful for videographers who caption interviews, voiceovers and music videos.

Key features

  • Full-featured speech analysis for English
  • Aligns transcription to audio as layer markers
  • Generates and controls automatic captions
  • Markers usable by other tools
  • Subtitles or lyrics in a few clicks
