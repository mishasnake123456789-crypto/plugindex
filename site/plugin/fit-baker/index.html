What Fit Baker does

FIT Baker, short for Fast Image Texture Baker, simplifies baking material inputs in Blender. It takes the inputs of a Principled BSDF, Bump or Displacement node and renders them to image textures.

You choose resolution and samples, with presets for quick setup, and the images are saved internally. Baking can replace node connections or leave them alone, and it works on objects with a single material slot. It is part of the FIT-Exporter batch export add-on.

Key features

  • Bakes Principled BSDF, Bump and Displacement inputs
  • Resolution and sample settings with presets
  • Optional node connection replacement
  • Images saved inside the file
