What Polymorph does

Polymorph is Rob Barrett's tool for After Effects that handles layer replacement. If you built a layout with placeholders, you can swap those shape layers, text layers, footage or nulls for any other layer in the comp without redoing the setup.

It can also attach one layer to another instead of replacing it. This is useful when rigs or animations were designed around a stand-in and the final artwork arrives later. The tool is compatible with After Effects 2025 and 2026, and a demo video is available on the developer's page.

Key features

  • Swap any layer for another
  • Attach one layer to another
  • Works with shapes, text, footage and nulls
  • Compatible with After Effects 2025 and 2026
