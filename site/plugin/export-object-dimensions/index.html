What Export Object Dimensions does

Export Object Dimensions, by rafaeloliveiraz, writes out the measurements of objects in your Blender scene, including their volume, to plain text, CSV or JSON. That makes it easy to hand dimensions to spreadsheets or other tools. It is listed under Import-Export.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
