What Camera Shift Sync does

Camera Shift Sync is a Blender plugin for perspective cameras. When you move a camera, the shift values normally stay put and your framing drifts; this add-on recomputes shift_x and shift_y in real time so the frame center remains locked to a target plane fixed in the world.

Camera position controls travel along the local axes of the target plane or toward and away from it, and a Plate Transform mode lets you edit the plate itself. It helps with architectural views, matched backplates and any shot that needs controlled perspective.

Key features

  • Real-time shift_x and shift_y recalculation
  • Move along plane axes or radially to the target
  • Plate Transform editing mode
  • Keeps framing locked to a world-fixed plane
