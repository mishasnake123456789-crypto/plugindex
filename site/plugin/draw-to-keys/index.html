What Draw to Keys does

Draw to Keys, by TheMultiX-PGDM, turns lines drawn with Blender's annotation tool into keyframes, so you can sketch a motion path and have it become animation. It is listed under Animation.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.1.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Annotation strokes become keyframes
  • Sketch-based animation

  • Requires Blender 5.1.0 or newer
