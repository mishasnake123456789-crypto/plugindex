What Outils de sécurité des scènes 3ds Max does

Listed under a French title, this Autodesk tool detects and removes known malicious third-party scripts such as CRP/ADSL, ALC, ALC2, PhysXPluginMfx and MSCPROP from 3ds Max startup scripts and scene files, whatever their loading method.

It stops scenes from being saved in a damaged state and asks for confirmation before cleaning. Silent mode is supported along with other non-interactive scenarios. A notify-me-about-updates option for the Autodesk App Store is on by default. Version 2.0.0 was rewritten in C++, and 2.1.1 added IT management features, so it suits supported 3ds Max installations, including educational and indie ones.

Key features

  • Removes known malicious scripts from startups and scenes
  • Prevents saving of damaged scenes
  • Confirmation before cleaning
  • Rewritten in C++ in version 2.0.0
  • IT management features added in 2.1.1
