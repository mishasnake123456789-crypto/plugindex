Blender add-on that builds an easy panel interface for character outfits, hair, morphs and custom properties.

What MustardUI does

MustardUI creates a friendly control panel for human character models inside Blender, with no Python knowledge needed. Everything is configured from a panel in the program.

Outfit support mixes pieces from different outfits, with masks and armature layers switching automatically on the body and hair. Hair can be turned on or off, including mesh, particle and curve hair with their modifiers. Any Blender property can be added with a right-click, and morphs are supported. Character creators sharing models with other animators make them simple to operate.

Key features

  • Panel built inside Blender, no Python
  • Mix outfit parts with automatic masking
  • Hair toggles for mesh, particle and curve
  • Add custom properties via right-click
  • Morph support
