What Simple Fire Scatter does

Simple Fire Scatter lets you cover a selected object with flames without building a simulation. You pick it up in the Fire Scatter tab of the 3D View sidebar and choose a preset.

Presets such as Flame 1 and Flame 2 apply with one click, and density, randomness and rotation sliders adjust the look. Start and stop buttons control the animation. Artists who need quick burning props or stylized fire will get results fast.

Key features

  • Applies fire to a selected surface
  • Multiple flame presets
  • Density, randomness and rotation controls
  • Start and stop animation buttons
