What N-Panel Organizer - Clean Panels does

N-Panel Organizer tidies the crowded sidebar in Blender by changing how you reach installed add-ons. It offers four ways of access, covering icon filtering, pinned dropdown panels and pie menus.

Focus Panels sort add-ons into categories with their own icons that toggle from the viewport. A pie menu hops between categories, and the filtering method introduced in 3.0 operates separately from workspace filtering. Drop Down Panels pin categorized tabs to the top bar. Users with many add-ons gain a cleaner, faster interface.

Key features

  • Focus Panels with icon categories
  • Pie menu to switch categories
  • Filtering independent of workspaces
  • Drop Down Panels pinned to top bar
