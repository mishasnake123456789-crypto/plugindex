What 3Dc Polish - Claypolish does

3DC Polish refines and deforms meshes. A single click improves edge definition, tightens creases and smooths surfaces, and a toolkit adds inflation, noise and texture displacement.

Iterative edge refinement runs 1 to 10 passes and sharpens edges without losing volume. A Preserve Details slider keeps fine surface detail, and curvature detection handles convex and concave regions independently. Noise displacement offers four patterns including Perlin and Fractal. It suits sculpt cleanup and hard-surface polish.

Key features

  • Edge refinement in 1 to 10 passes
  • Preserve Details slider
  • Separate convex and concave handling
  • Noise and texture displacement
