Assetify

Developer: Nino

FreeBlender
Download Free

Blender add-on that bakes textures, unwraps UVs and exports assets for game engines, web, AR and 3D printing.

What Assetify does

Assetify prepares Blender assets for use elsewhere. It automates baking of alpha, normal, metallic, roughness and diffuse textures, with Unreal Engine 5 and Unity presets, and bakes multiple materials together or separately.

Exports go to FBX, OBJ, GLTF/GLB and STL. Version 2.1 recreates non-Principled shader setups for baking, and version 2 handles rigged, physics, shape key, geometry node and particle animations, including texture sequences. Asset Mode and Collection Mode manage hierarchies, and automated UV unwrapping and packing cover static and procedural content.

Key features

  • Baking for diffuse, roughness, metallic, normal and alpha
  • Unreal Engine 5 and Unity presets
  • FBX, OBJ, GLTF/GLB and STL export
  • Animation and geometry nodes baking
  • Automated UV unwrapping and packing
