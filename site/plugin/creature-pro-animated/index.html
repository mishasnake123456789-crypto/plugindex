What Creature Pro Animated does

studioromanrec's Creature Pro Animated delivers creatures that are already animated, ready to drop into Blender projects and saving the work of modeling and animating them.

A demo with three creatures is available, though some features are limited, along with a YouTube video. Documentation is included, and the add-on belongs to the Studioromanrec Blender Add-Ons Bundle under the GPL. Short films, game prototypes and motion graphics scenes can gain life quickly. Trying the limited demo first shows how the creatures look and move before you commit to the full set.
