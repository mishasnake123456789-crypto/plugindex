What Light It Up Pro - Turn Dull Blender Scenes Into Cinematic Masterpieces In 1 Click does

Light It Up Pro puts ready-made lighting rigs in Blender. It targets users who want good lighting without placing every lamp by hand, for characters, products, animation and cinematic renders.

More than 25 presets span Cinematic, Portrait, Studio, Product and Environment styles. Your own setups can be saved and loaded, lights auto-point at the subject, and the entire rig can be moved or rotated by parenting to an empty. The workflow is non-destructive, and it works in Eevee and Cycles with no dependencies.
