A Blender add-on that creates seamless looping animation through modifiers instead of keyframes.

What Loop Master does

Loop Master makes repeating animation in Blender without keyframes. You add a Loop Master modifier to an object, camera, material, light, rig or Geometry Nodes setup, and the motion cycles seamlessly in about two clicks.

Several loop modifiers can be stacked and combined, each with individual timing, speed and easing control. Progressive loops keep building over time, while recursive loops repeat the same cycle back to the start. It is a natural fit for motion designers making looping social content, backgrounds and product turntables who want editable loops.

Key features

  • Loop modifier on objects, cameras, lights and materials
  • Works without keyframes in two clicks
  • Stack and combine multiple loops
  • Timing, speed and easing controls
  • Progressive and recursive loop types
