What Djh Bake Batcher does

DJH Bake Batcher trades fine control for speed. It bakes standard PBR maps (base color, roughness, metallic, AO, normal) all at once with a single click.

Collection-based baking transfers from a high-poly collection to a low-poly one, aligned by matching origin empties so objects need not be stacked. Single-map baking covers non-PBR needs. It is a quick route for game asset texturing. Highlights include one-click full PBR bake, high to low poly collection baking and origin-empty alignment.

Key features

  • One-click full PBR bake
  • High to low poly collection baking
  • Origin-empty alignment
  • Single-map baking
