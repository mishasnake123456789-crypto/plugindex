Paints objects into a Blender scene with the mouse and lets rigid body physics settle them under gravity and collisions.

What Drop Cast Painter does

Drop Cast Painter places items by painting. Objects fall into position as you stroke, reacting to gravity and collisions as they land.

You choose any mesh as the ground, which becomes a Passive Rigid Body automatically, and objects get random rotation. Suitable for piles, clutter and natural arrangements that would take ages to place by hand. The physical result is natural looking, since objects bounce and settle instead of simply being placed.

Key features

  • Real-time painting of objects
  • Rigid body physics response
  • Choose any mesh as ground
  • Automatic random rotation
