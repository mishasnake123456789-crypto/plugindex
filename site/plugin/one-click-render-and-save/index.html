What One Click Render & Save does

One Click Render & Save collects shortcuts for common render tasks in a side panel. Automatic saving writes out images, videos and image sequences without setup or a compositor, including viewport renders.

Every render gets a unique timestamp so nothing is overwritten and naming conflicts are avoided. A 60 FPS switch adjusts the frame range and time stretching in one click. Useful for artists who produce lots of test renders and do not want to name each one.

Key features

  • Auto-save for images, videos and sequences
  • Unique timestamp on every render
  • One-click 60 FPS switch
  • Works for viewport renders
