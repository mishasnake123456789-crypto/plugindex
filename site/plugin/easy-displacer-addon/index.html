What Easy Displacer Addon does

Easy Displacer Addon streamlines displacement workflows in Blender, where the standard modifier takes many steps. Surface detail is applied in no more than three clicks.

Ten presets include Nose, Voronoi and Marble, auto subdivision prepares the mesh, and a resolution value up to 15 sets detail, with a Subdivide button for extra. Custom displacement maps can be imported on the Texture tab, and an Apply button converts the object to an editable mesh for export.

Key features

  • 10 displacement presets
  • Displacement in three clicks or fewer
  • Auto subdivision and resolution up to 15
  • Custom displacement map import
  • Apply to editable mesh for export
