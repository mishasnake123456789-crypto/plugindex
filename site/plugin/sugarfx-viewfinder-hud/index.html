What SUGARfx Viewfinder HUD does

SUGARfx Viewfinder HUD bundles three filters for creating viewfinder-style graphics in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro, plus Final Cut Pro X and Motion. Binoculars simulates a pair of binocular views.

Target draws attention to the point of focus in your message, and OSD simulates the on-screen display of a video or still camera. The filters combine so a heads-up display can be as simple or as complex as you like, with a streamlined interface and an online manual one button away. Editors making surveillance, found-footage or sci-fi sequences can add convincing overlays.

Key features

  • Binoculars viewfinder filter
  • Target filter for focus emphasis
  • OSD camera display filter
  • Combinable for simple or complex HUDs
  • Online user manual
