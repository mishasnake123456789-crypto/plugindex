What Surgeon DCTL does

Surgeon DCTL, made by Nikita Petrovich, is a straightforward image-analysis helper for DaVinci Resolve. It is meant to be used together with Resolve's own scopes, giving colorists an extra way to inspect what is happening in a shot.

It is delivered as a DCTL file and works with Resolve versions 16 through 21, so it fits both older and current installations. The initial release, version 0.1.0, appeared in May 2024. It suits anyone who likes to check and diagnose their footage carefully before and during grading rather than relying on eyes alone.

Key features

  • Image analysis aid for DaVinci Resolve
  • Designed to complement the built-in scopes
  • Distributed as a DCTL
  • Compatible with Resolve 16 through 21
