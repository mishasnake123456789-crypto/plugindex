What Path Selection Mode 2 does

Path Selection Mode 2, from Terrible Junk Show, adjusts how path points are selected in After Effects. Working with masks and shape paths often involves fiddly selection, and this tool offers a choice of click styles.

Points can be selected with a single click or a double click. Version 2.0 added the double-click mode and a release function. It runs on After Effects 2020 through 2026, and is aimed at users who edit paths frequently and want their selection behavior to match their habits.

Key features
