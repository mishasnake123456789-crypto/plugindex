What Decal Forge Add-On + Library does

Decal Forge lets you stamp decals directly onto any 3D model with one click, projecting them in place. It includes a built-in library of over 500 decals, scratches, dirt and grunge.

Decals can be layered, partly erased, flipped and rotated without baking or node setup, with real-time work on the surface and full brush control. A quick way to add wear and detail. Highlights cover one-click decal stamping and 500+ decals, scratches and grunge.

Key features

  • One-click decal stamping
  • 500+ decals, scratches and grunge
  • Layer, erase, flip and rotate
  • No baking or nodes
