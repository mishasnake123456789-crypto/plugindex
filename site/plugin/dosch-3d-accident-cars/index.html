What DOSCH 3D: Accident Cars does

Accident scenes are the subject of DOSCH 3D: Accident Cars, a set of ten detailed and fully textured crash-damaged cars for Cinema 4D and several other formats. Each can serve as a base for your own creations.

Scale can be changed, materials and colors edited, and your own textures or labels applied. Parts may be switched off or made transparent, and they can be animated one by one or all together. Safety campaigns, insurance visuals, film previs and illustrations are natural uses.

Key features

  • Ten fully textured accident car models
  • Editable materials, colors and labels
  • Adjustable scaling
  • Switchable, transparent elements
  • Part or whole-model animation
