What Parallaxer does

Parallaxer prepares layered artwork for 2.5D camera moves. Bring in an Illustrator or Photoshop file and the plug-in sets up a 3D scene ready for animation, instead of you spacing out every layer by hand.

Autoscale keeps each layer's apparent size constant as it is moved in depth. Three scene scale presets cover small rooms, big rooms and exteriors, and Regroup, Flatten and Expand buttons reorganize layer groups. A dockable panel keeps the controls handy. Illustrators and motion designers can use it for depth-rich illustrations and photo animations.

Key features

  • One-click 3D scene from layered files
  • Autoscale holds layer size while moving in depth
  • Small Room, Big Room and Exterior presets
  • Regroup, Flatten and Expand buttons
  • Dockable interface
