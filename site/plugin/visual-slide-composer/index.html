What Visual Slide Composer does

Visual Slide Composer, made by MotionDesk, is a plugin for After Effects that builds a finished video from slides in just a few clicks. It acts as a visual alternative to assembling everything in the timeline.

The plugin contains many pre-made slides and transitions in current styles, slides can be swapped easily, and it detects Animation Composer transitions. This helps presentation-style videos, promos and social content come together quickly. Editors who need a fast route from content to a polished slideshow can lean on it.

Key features

  • Complete videos from slides in a few clicks
  • Many pre-made slides and trendy transitions
  • Swap slides easily
  • Detects Animation Composer transitions
