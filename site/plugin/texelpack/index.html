Packs UVs in Blender by texel density, normalizing resolution across selections with several packing algorithms and overlays.

What Texelpack does

TexelPack is built around consistent texture resolution. Each island is scaled to its object's real 3D surface area, including object scale, at a target density in pixels per meter.

Packing modes include MAXRECTS, Guillotine and Shelf, and islands pack by their real shape. Viewport overlays preview density and distortion before baking. Game artists can keep texel density uniform across many assets. The overlays let you spot stretched or inconsistent areas before baking rather than discovering them afterwards.

Key features

  • Uniform texel density in pixels per meter
  • MAXRECTS, Guillotine and Shelf packing
  • Packs by actual island shape
  • Density and distortion overlays
