What Livemesher - Arrange Your Parts Before Sculpting does

Livemesher builds on the remesh modifier so you can see what a group of objects will look like when merged before committing. One button creates the preview, and you can switch back to adjusting the parts individually.

Remesh mode spreads voxels equally across the parts, although tiny details may be lost even at high density. Union mode keeps the topology of the parts better but needs subdivided parts with matching density. Everything stays non-destructive until you apply, which suits blocking out sculpts from separate pieces.

Key features

  • One-button remesh preview
  • Remesh and Union modes
  • Switch back to tweak parts
  • Non-destructive until applied
