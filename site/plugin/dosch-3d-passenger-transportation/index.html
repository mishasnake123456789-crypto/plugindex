What Dosch 3D: Passenger Transportation does

Passenger Transportation is a Cinema 4D pack from Dosch Design with 24 detailed, fully textured vehicles. The range starts with a small two-wheel trailer and a luxury RV and extends to commuter trains, high-speed trains and a maglev monorail.

Each model can be scaled and recolored, textured with your own images or labels, and broken into elements that are toggled on or off or made transparent. Parts or entire vehicles can be animated. Transport visualizations, travel advertising and infographic scenes can draw on the collection.

Key features

  • 24 detailed textured vehicle models
  • Trailer, luxury RV, commuter and high-speed trains, maglev
  • Editable scale, materials and colors
  • Switchable elements with transparency
  • Animatable parts or whole vehicles
