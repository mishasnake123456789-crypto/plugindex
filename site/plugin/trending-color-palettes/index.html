What Trending Color Palettes does

studioromanrec's Trending Color Palettes collects 192 palettes popular in 2023. Rather than inventing color schemes, pick the one that suits and use it in your work.

It fits characters, vehicles, spacecraft and other subjects. The add-on works with Blender 4.0 and the 3.x series, is GPL licensed, and includes documentation and a demo video. Handy for artists wanting harmonious colors fast, especially in stylized scenes. Picking from a fixed set also helps keep a series of images or a whole project visually consistent.
