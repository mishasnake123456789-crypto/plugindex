What FST ForgePolish does

FST ForgePolish cleans up retopologized hard-surface meshes in Blender, removing irregularities before beveling and subdivision. It polishes whatever is selected, or the entire mesh when nothing is, and edges relax along their own flow with masks, locked endpoints and corners respected.

Face Sets divide the mesh into protected regions from edge angle, sharp marks, UV seams, bevel weights or material borders. A Polish Brush works locally in the viewport, and two modes trade controlled cleanup against a tighter, sharper result. The interface supports thirteen languages.

Key features

  • Polishes selected faces, edges or the whole mesh
  • Face Sets protect regions by angle, seams or materials
  • Local Polish Brush
  • Standard and Tension First modes
  • Thirteen interface languages
