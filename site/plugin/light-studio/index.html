What Light Studio does

Light Studio is a Blender addon from Bproduction for lighting 3D packshots in Cycles. It includes 37 ready-to-use lights, all customizable, divided across three styles: HDR light derived from HDR photographs, reflectors that open up dark areas, and procedural light (which does not work in Eevee).

A Change Light function swaps between lamp styles automatically so you can compare options quickly, and Position Reflexion lets you paint your lights where you want reflections to fall on glossy products. The aim is studio-style product lighting without building the setup manually.

Key features

  • 37 customizable ready-made lights
  • HDR, reflector and procedural light styles
  • Change Light function to compare styles
  • Position Reflexion for painting reflections
  • Designed for Cycles packshots
