What Section Analysis Tool does

Section Analysis Tool by Morbidmod slices through objects in Blender so you can look inside and analyze their internal geometry. It suits checking wall thickness, hidden cavities and assemblies. It is listed under 3D View, Mesh and Modeling.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
