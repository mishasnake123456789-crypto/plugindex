What Autosave+ Pro - Smart Auto Saving & Crash Prevention does

Smart AutoSave+ Pro protects work with automatic, numbered and named backups sorted by type. Each timer tick it computes a scene hash and compares it to the previous one, saving only when something actually changed.

Checkpoints and manual saves can upload silently to Google Drive or Dropbox after one-time authentication, using a background thread. It reduces the damage of crashes and keeps backup folders from filling with identical files. Highlights include change-based autosave, numbered, named backups by type and google Drive and Dropbox upload.
