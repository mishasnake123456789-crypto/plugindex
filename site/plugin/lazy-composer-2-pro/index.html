What 🔖 Lazy Composer 2 Pro does

Lazy Composer 2 Pro is a Lazy3D post-processing add-on for the Blender compositor. It puts cinematic color, lens treatments, grain and finishing touches into a single control panel, working non-destructively.

A preset grid with thumbnail previews applies looks in one click, and you can save custom presets for projects or clients. The color section covers three-way grading, saturation, vibrance, tint, temperature, contrast, exposure, sharpening, clarity, dehaze, film fade and gamma. The lens section offers distortion, chromatic aberration, fisheye, fringe, edge blur and lens dirt.

Key features

  • Preset grid with thumbnails and custom presets
  • Full color section with three-way grading
  • Lens distortion, chromatic aberration and dirt
  • Film grain and finishing controls
  • Non-destructive compositor workflow
