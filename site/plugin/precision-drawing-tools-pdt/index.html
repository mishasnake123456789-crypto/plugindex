What Precision Drawing Tools (PDT) does

Precision Drawing Tools (PDT) supports exact construction work in Blender. Its panel sits in the 3D View sidebar, and it was one of the add-ons bundled with Blender 4.1 before becoming a standalone offering with limited support.

The download is about 48 KB, needs no special permissions and is released under GPL v2 or later. It runs on Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. Modelers, architects and engineers who need to place vertices and edges at exact coordinates or distances can do so without relying on eyeballing or snapping alone.
