What STRUCTURA does

STRUCTURA is an asset organizer for After Effects from Eunomia Logic. It uses fuzzy matching and a dictionary-based approach to understand what each item is, then sorts the whole project into a professional folder structure at once.

What sets it apart is care about what not to disturb: renders, dynamic links and complex rigs are shielded from the reorganization. Artists inheriting disorganized files or preparing projects for a team will find it handy. It supports After Effects CC 2019 through 2026.

Key features

  • Context-aware project organization
  • Fuzzy logic and dictionary-based sorting
  • Instant professional folder hierarchies
  • Protects renders, dynamic links and rigs
