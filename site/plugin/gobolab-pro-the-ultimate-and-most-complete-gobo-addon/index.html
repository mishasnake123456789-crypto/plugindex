What Gobolab Pro - The Ultimate & Most Complete Gobo Addon does

LabSages' Gobolab Pro makes gobos, the cutouts placed in front of a light to shape it, in Blender. A library of presets includes animated and static categories, procedural water caustics and abstract designs.

Transformations, color values, blurriness and image extension are adjustable. Procedural animation presets such as Breeze, Strong Wind, Raindrops and Storm let you set speed and duration. Gobos can be blended together, and your images and videos can be imported. Lighting artists add moving shadows and dappled light easily.

Key features

  • Animated and static preset library
  • Procedural water caustics
  • Wind and rain animation presets
  • Gobo mixing
  • Custom image and video import
