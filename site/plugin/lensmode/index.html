What Lensmode does

LensMode applies camera lens character to Blender renders without you building compositor nodes by hand.

Fifteen presets deliver professional lens looks in one click, and settings for distortion, color fringing, vignetting and anamorphic squeeze can be tuned. The effects are applied in the compositor after rendering, so render times are not slowed, and the original render stays intact for a non-destructive workflow. Adjusting the look afterward needs no re-render, which helps when comparing options.

Key features

  • 15 one-click lens presets
  • Distortion, color fringing and vignette controls
  • Anamorphic squeeze
  • Compositor-based, applied after render
  • Non-destructive
