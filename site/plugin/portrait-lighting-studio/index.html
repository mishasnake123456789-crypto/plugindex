What Portrait Lighting Studio does

Portrait Lighting Studio builds lighting for portraits using traditional photography setups. Nine presets are included: Rembrandt, Split, Loop, Butterfly, Clamshell, Short, Broad, Rim and Cross.

Lights are positioned relative to the camera and adapt automatically when it moves, parametric controls allow adjustment, and it works in Eevee and Cycles with no external dependencies. Character and portrait artists can reach flattering lighting quickly. The feature set spans 9 classic portrait lighting presets and camera-relative lights follow the camera.

Key features

  • 9 classic portrait lighting presets
  • Camera-relative lights follow the camera
  • Parametric controls
  • Eevee and Cycles support
