What Fader DCTL does

Fader DCTL by Nikita Petrovich is a color-processing effect for DaVinci Resolve that produces a film-style fade. The whole effect is driven by a single slider, so cinematic transitions can be created and adjusted quickly.

It works with DaVinci Resolve 19, 20 and 21, is available through aescripts, and a demo video is provided. It is listed as free. Editors who want a classic fade look without building a node setup or keyframing multiple parameters can drop it on a clip and move the slider to taste.

Key features

  • Film-style fade effect
  • Single-slider control
  • Works with DaVinci Resolve 19, 20 and 21
  • Demo video available
