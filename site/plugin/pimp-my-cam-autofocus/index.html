What Pimp My Cam - Autofocus does

Pimp My Cam automates focus and adds camera movement. The camera locates the object at the center of view and adjusts focus live, without targets or drivers.

Focus speed and update rate are adjustable, and handheld shake and drift range from subtle to action-style. Gives animations a documentary or filmed feel. Together, these features add a more natural camera feel without manual keyframing or rig setup. Those wanting both realistic focus pulls and handheld character will find the settings approachable.

Key features

  • Autofocus on the middle object, no drivers
  • Adjustable focus speed and update rate
  • Handheld shake and drift
  • Subtle to intense motion
