What Outlinerflow Pro - Sync, Isolate & Colorize does

OutlinerFlow Pro is a Blender add-on for taming the Outliner in large scenes. It is useful for technical artists, riggers presenting rigs, and anyone preparing a scene for review.

Auto Sync links viewport selection and the Outliner with selectable scopes, and a double-click isolates the collections related to the selected object or the one under the cursor. Objects can be colored by collection in Individual, Collection Exclusive or Global modes, with randomize and reset options for folder colors. Force Sync collapses and focuses the Outliner, and settings can be stored as presets.

Key features

  • Configurable Outliner and viewport selection sync
  • Double-click isolation of related collections
  • Three modes for coloring objects by collection
  • Preset saving for highlight settings
