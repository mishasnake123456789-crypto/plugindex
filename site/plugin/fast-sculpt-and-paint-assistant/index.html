What Fast Sculpt And Paint Assistant does

Fast Sculpt And Paint Assistant, by sum studio, is built to keep your hands in place while sculpting or painting in Blender. Operations that normally call for reaching across the keyboard are gathered under a single key.

It supports Blender versions 3.1 to 5.0, is released under the GPL and ships with documentation and a demo video. Sculptors and texture painters who switch between tools constantly can use it to maintain rhythm and reduce fatigue.

Key features

  • Many sculpt and paint operations on one key
  • Less keyboard finger movement
  • Supports Blender 3.1 to 5.0
  • Documentation and demo video
  • GPL licensed
