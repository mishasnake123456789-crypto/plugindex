What Alt Tab Easy Decals - Add Images & Decals To Anything does

Alt Tab Easy Decals puts images and decals on Blender models without increasing the vertex count. Images can be placed, scaled and rotated to fit the object.

It works in Cycles and Eevee, supports Blender versions 4.0 through 4.5 and includes documentation. Artists adding labels, logos, stickers or damage marks can do so without complicated UV edits. Keeping decals separate from the base mesh also keeps the model light, which suits scenes with many labelled or marked objects.

Key features

  • No extra vertices added
  • Place, scale and rotate images
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
  • Supports Blender 4.0 through 4.5
