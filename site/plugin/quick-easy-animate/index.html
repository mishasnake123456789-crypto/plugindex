What Quick Easy Animate does

Quick Easy Animate is a Blender plugin for procedural animation. It makes F-curve modifiers such as Noise and Sin Generator easier to use, so you do not need to place each keyframe.

The Quake Easy Animate panel includes an animate button producing random rotation or location in a plane you choose, and noise-style keyframes provide imprecise motion such as wobbling. Ideal for adding life to props and lights. Since it builds on modifiers rather than keys, the motion is easy to tweak or remove, suiting ambient movement and quick motion graphics.

Key features

  • F-curve modifier workflow
  • Random rotation or location on XY, YZ or ZX
  • Noise-style keyframe generation
  • No manual keyframing
