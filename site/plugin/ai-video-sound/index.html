What AI Video Sound does

AI Video Sound uses AI to turn picture into audio. It analyzes video and produces realistic sound effects that are relevant to what is on screen and aligned with the timing.

It offers a video-to-audio workflow, with capabilities listed as AI audio, sound effects and music. It is helpful for editors who have silent footage and want a first pass of sound. It can help fill silent footage with a believable first layer of sound before finer sound design begins.

Key features

  • AI audio generated from video
  • Semantically relevant sound effects
  • Audio synchronized to the picture
  • Video-to-audio workflow
