What Shot Engine does

Shot Engine is a Blender plugin for managing shots in a scene. Shots can be created, renamed, duplicated, deleted, reordered and hidden, so a sequence stays organized within one file.

Custom cameras can be assigned to each shot, with an object to follow or track, and shots can be blended for transitions. Speed and duration are adjustable, and shots can be previewed or rendered one at a time or in batches. Useful for animated shorts and previs.

Key features

  • Create, duplicate and reorder shots
  • Cameras with follow or track targets
  • Blend between shots
  • Preview or render individually or in batches
