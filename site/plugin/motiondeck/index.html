Builds animated camera paths, transitions, text motion and lighting in Blender from empties placed as shots.

What Motiondeck does

Motiondeck turns empties into shots. Position empties as targets, set a hold time in frames for each, add text and layouts, then click Motion It to build the sequence.

The camera builds a path through the targets automatically. Transitions between them can be smooth, using four interpolation curves, or other styles, and text animation and lighting are generated as well. Motion designers making titles, explainers or product reels in Blender can block out an entire sequence in minutes.

Key features

  • Empty targets act as shots with hold times
  • Automatic camera path
  • Smooth transitions with interpolation curves
  • Generated text animation and lighting
