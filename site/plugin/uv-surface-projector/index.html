What UV Surface Projector does

UV Surface Projector, by Jackal, wraps curves and meshes onto a surface using its UV mapping, so drawn shapes follow the form of the target. It is listed under Mesh and Modeling.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Projects curves onto UV-mapped surfaces
  • Projects meshes onto UV-mapped surfaces
