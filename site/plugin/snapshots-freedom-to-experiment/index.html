What Snapshots - Freedom To Experiment does

Snapshots brings a version-control approach to Blender so you can experiment without fear. It captures either the whole project or targeted pieces such as objects, materials, node trees and actions, letting you branch, compare and revert non-destructively.

Its property-level discovery pulls specific settings from an earlier iteration without overwriting the entire scene. A Recover Deleted Data tool retrieves lost geometry, materials, Grease Pencil data and node groups, mapping them back to their original collections. It is aimed at artists who iterate heavily and want a safety net.

Key features

  • Full-project and partial snapshots
  • Pull individual properties from past versions
  • Branch, compare and revert
  • Recover deleted geometry, materials and node groups
