What 3D Paper Jam does

3D Paper Jam from Froggy Team produces a crumpled, folding paper effect in After Effects with full controls. Four Jam modes offer different looks, and the geometry parameters are animatable.

It includes a two-sided option and pseudo 3D shading, plus a motion blur hack for smoother movement. Layers do not have to be pre-composed, and transparency is linked to the Control layer. Designers can use it for transitions or text and graphics that scrunch up like paper.

Key features

  • Four Jam modes
  • Animated geometry parameters
  • 2 Sided option
  • Pseudo 3D shading and motion blur hack
  • Works without pre-composing
