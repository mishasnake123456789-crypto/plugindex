What Render Raw - Color Grading & Effects does

Render Raw gives Blender an approachable grading interface. Sliders resembling those in photo editors appear in the Properties Editor or 3D Viewport, and the add-on sets up the Raw view transform automatically.

Tonal controls span exposure, gamma, contrast and the white, highlight, shadow and black points, along with sharpening, texture and clarity, and a curves panel is available. Color controls include temperature, tint, color boost and saturation. Camera effects are included too. 3D artists can polish final renders without leaving Blender.

Key features

  • Lightroom-style sliders
  • Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows controls
  • Temperature, tint and saturation
  • Curves panel and camera effects
