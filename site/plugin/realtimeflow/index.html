What Realtimeflow does

RealTimeFlow recreates the look of moving water by using real-time simulation inside Blender. It builds on dynamic paint and effectors to generate realistic water effects within a scene.

The interface is straightforward, so you can set up ripples and surface interaction without a complex fluid simulation pipeline. Documentation is included to help with setup. It is aimed at artists who want believable water motion that responds quickly and is simple to adjust.

Key features

  • Real-time water simulation
