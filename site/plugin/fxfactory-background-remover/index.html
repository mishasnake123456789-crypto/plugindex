What FxFactory Background Remover does

This Noise Industries tool detects the people, animals and objects that are the focus of a video and separates them from the background. It runs in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and in Final Cut Pro X and Motion.

Output can be the subject alone, the background alone, or a black-and-white mask for matte tracks. A defocus feature blurs the background, backgrounds can be swapped, and filters can target specific areas. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel. Editors working with footage not shot against a green screen can isolate subjects without manual rotoscoping.

Key features

  • Automatic subject detection
  • Clean key without green or blue screen
  • Subject, background or mask output
  • Defocus blur and background swapping
  • Apple Silicon and Intel support
