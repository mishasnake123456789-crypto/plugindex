What Zoetrope GEODE does

Zoetrope GEODE is a pair of tools for After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve. GEODE FX wraps video around 3D mesh models such as planes, arcs, open boxes and dodecahedrons, while GEODE TRANS creates fades and wipes from the alpha channel of a 3D model.

Light position sets a transition's direction, and softness runs from a crisp wipe to a gentle dissolve. Scale, position and rotation can be keyframed or auto-animated, and a vertex distortion option produces glitchy abstract warps. Both plugins have preset systems and OpenGL acceleration, helping editors move beyond stock transitions.

Key features

  • Maps video onto 3D mesh models
  • Thirteen model-based transition shapes
  • Light direction and softness controls
  • Vertex distortion for abstract glitches
  • Preset saving and sharing
