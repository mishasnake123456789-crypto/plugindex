What Knitting Generator Addon does

Knitting Generator Addon creates procedural knitted looks in Blender, avoiding the need to hand-model yarn and stitches.

It runs on Blender 4.1 through 4.5, in both Cycles and Eevee, is released under a GPL license and includes documentation. Artists dressing characters, props or textile scenes can add woven yarn detail more easily. A procedural approach means yarn density and pattern can be changed after setup, avoiding a rebuild if the garment design changes.

  • Procedural knitting generation
