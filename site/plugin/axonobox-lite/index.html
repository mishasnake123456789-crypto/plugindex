What Axonobox Lite does

Axonobox Lite, by vivimade, adds CAD-like drawing of lines, rectangles and circles directly on your mesh, supported by ten object snap types. It is listed under Mesh.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Line, rectangle and circle tools
  • 10 object snaps
