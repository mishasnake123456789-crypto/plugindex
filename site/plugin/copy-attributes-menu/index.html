Blender add-on that adds attribute-copying operators to the Ctrl-C menu in the 3D viewport.

What Copy Attributes Menu does

Copy Attributes Menu gives Blender a quick way to transfer properties from one object to others. It appears when you press Ctrl-C in the 3D viewport, and was previously among the add-ons shipped with Blender 4.1.

Operators include light and shadow linking, hotfixed in version 0.6.3. The download is just over 8 KB, needs no special permissions and is licensed under GPL v3 or later. It works with Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. Scene builders who regularly match settings across many objects will save repetitive clicking.

Key features

  • Attribute copying from Ctrl-C
  • Light and shadow linking operators
  • Compact download
  • For Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
