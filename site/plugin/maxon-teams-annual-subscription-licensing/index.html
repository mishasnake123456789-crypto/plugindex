What Maxon Teams Annual Subscription Licensing does

This is a one-year, named-user subscription plan for creative teams that use Maxon software alongside After Effects and Premiere Pro. It gives studios a single place to buy and manage seats for Cinema 4D, Redshift, ZBrush and Red Giant's tool collections.

Licences are controlled centrally through the Teams account dashboard, formerly the ORG account, and Teams Support and Training are part of the deal. Cinema 4D seats include Cineversity, Team Render, and all upgrades during the term, while any plan with Cinema 4D adds five Team Render nodes and two Command Line nodes. Early renewal opens 60 days before expiry.

Key features

  • Named-user seats managed from the Teams dashboard
  • Cinema 4D, Redshift, ZBrush or Red Giant options
  • Teams Support and Training included
  • 5 Team Render and 2 Command Line nodes with Cinema 4D
  • Renew up to 60 days early
