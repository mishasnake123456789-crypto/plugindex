What Procedural Outlets and Switches does

Procedural Outlets and Switches builds wall fixtures in 3ds Max with a few clicks. Frames hold one to five sockets and come in three designs, with socket types such as blank, one to three switches, rotary dimmer, temperature sensor and a 220V power socket.

Placed within a certain distance, a fixture snaps to the wall, and holes are cut for each socket you add. Mounting height is measured from the floor or surface underneath, with presets of 20, 90 and 135 cm or a value of your own. Cable accessories use one of two plug styles, and Put Down mode lays the cable on the surface beneath. Color presets or the color picker are available. Interior visualization artists save modeling time.

Key features

  • One to five sockets per frame in three frame designs
  • Blank, switch, dimmer, sensor and 220V socket types
  • Automatic wall snapping and hole cutting
  • Height presets of 20, 90 and 135 cm or custom
  • Cable options with Put Down mode and color choices
