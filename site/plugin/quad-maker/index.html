What Quad Maker does

Quad Maker collects straightforward mesh operations for manual retopology in Blender. It works as a hotkey-driven workspace alongside native mesh editing.

Tools include Add Vertices for dropping points, Fill Quads for filling points and neighboring geometry with quads, Extend Borders for extruding borders in single, loop or edge-ring fashion, and Quick Cut. Retopology artists converting sculpts into clean production meshes can work faster. Its operations are aimed at artists who prefer to place geometry themselves but want fewer repetitive steps than native tools require.

Key features

  • Add Vertices for quick point placement
  • Fill Quads from points and geometry
  • Extend Borders as single, loop or ring
  • Quick Cut tool
  • Hotkey-driven workflow
