What Photoshop To Blender Plugin does

The Photoshop To Blender Plugin by Matronius brings layered artwork into Blender. It consists of an export script for Photoshop and an import add-on for Blender, and runs on PC and macOS.

Normal, Shape and Text layers are all imported, keeping the original z-order. Shapes and text become flat curves, while normal layers become textured curves. Each Photoshop group turns into a Blender collection. The layers travel in one compact .psl zipped file, which suits asset creation, concept design and texture layering.

Key features

  • Imports Normal, Shape and Text layers
  • Preserves layer z-order
  • Groups become Blender collections
  • Single compact .psl file, PC and macOS
