What Constructor Addon does

Constructor Addon is a Geometry Nodes generator that creates procedural animations in Blender. It is built on the node system and produces a variety of animated effects.

It supports Blender 4.0 through 4.5, works with Cycles and Eevee, is GPL licensed and includes documentation. Useful for motion designers wanting quick animated build-ups. Because the effects are driven by nodes, they can be adjusted and combined, giving motion designers a flexible source of ready animated content for titles, backgrounds and abstract visuals.

Key features

  • Geometry Nodes powered generator
  • Variety of procedural animations
  • Blender 4.0 through 4.5
  • Cycles and Eevee support
