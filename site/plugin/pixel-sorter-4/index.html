What Pixel Sorter 4 does

Pixel Sorter 4 creates the stretched, streaky look of pixel sorting, a staple of glitch art and digital artifact styling. It runs as a plugin in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro.

Constraint masks restrict where sorting happens, while stretch mode and noise add further variation, and separate settings cover animation and the overall look. A simple mode offers a less intimidating interface for newcomers. One universal license is valid in every supported host, which also include Media Encoder, Final Cut Pro, Nuke or NukeX, and Photoshop.

Key features

  • Pixel sorting for glitch and artifact looks
  • Constraint masks, a stretch mode and noise
  • Animation and look settings
  • Simple mode with a lighter interface
  • Single licence covers all supported hosts
