What One-Click Fixes does

One-Click Fixes packs a large library of small repair and setup actions into organized panels, so you do not have to dig through menus. They span geometry, UV, rigging, simulation and scene management.

Geometry fixes include merging loose vertices, recalculating flipped normals, triangulating or converting to quads, and selecting or deleting loose or non-manifold parts. Many more tasks are included. Generalists and technical artists who run the same cleanup routines repeatedly can shorten them to single clicks.

Key features

  • Over 100 one-click operators
  • Geometry, UV, rigging and simulation fixes
  • Normals, doubles and non-manifold cleanup
  • Organized into panels
