What Blender 4-1 Vertex Color & Vertex Alpha Gradient Maker And Visualizer does

This Blender add-on builds vertex color and vertex alpha gradients on mesh objects and shows them in the viewport. Gradients can be linear, reflected or radial along the X, Y or Z axis.

Start percentage, gradient percentage and radial offsets are adjustable in 3D space, with both global and local transforms. You can write to the R, G and B channels, custom color values, alpha or custom alpha values, and erase specific channels. It is helpful for game assets, shader masks and stylized shading that rely on vertex data.

Key features

  • Linear, reflected and radial gradients
  • Color and alpha on any axis
  • Adjustable start, spread and radial offset
  • Per-channel writing and erasing
