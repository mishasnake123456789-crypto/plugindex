What Quick Turnaround Camera Addons does

Quick Turnaround Camera Addons is a small Blender tool for presenting finished models. One click creates a camera that circles the subject.

Rotation speed and frame count set the animation length, and you can switch between objects and angles. The controls appear in the viewport sidebar. This is the developer's first version, with more updates planned, and documentation is included.

Key features

  • Turnaround camera in one click
  • Adjustable rotation speed and frame count
  • Switch between objects and angles
  • Sidebar panel in the viewport
