What Weight Wizard - Paint Weight Maps With Ease does

Weight Wizard is made for character artists and riggers who spend too long fixing skin weights in Blender. It aims to produce cleaner, more dependable weights with fewer rounds of repetitive painting and manual touch-ups.

The focus is on predictable behaviour around joints and areas that move a lot, which is where bad weights show up first. It supports Blender 4.5 through 5.1 with Cycles and Eevee, and documentation is included. If you rig characters regularly, it is meant to shorten the path from a bound mesh to a deformation you can trust.

Key features

  • Weight painting workflow for skin weights
  • Predictable results around joints and high-motion areas
  • Less manual cleanup and repeated painting
  • Built for character artists and riggers
  • Supports Blender 4.5 to 5.1
