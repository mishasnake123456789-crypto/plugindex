Blender add-on that adds noise-based wiggle to rotation and location with adjustable strength, speed and influence.

What The Wiggle Add-On does

The Wiggle Add-On brings quick procedural movement to objects. With a click, noise is applied, and you control strength, speed and influence, separately for rotation and location.

It works through delta transforms, so the wiggle sits on top of any existing animation without overwriting keyframes. Motion designers can add life to props, text and cameras, such as hand-held shake or floating drift, without setting keys.

Key features

  • One-click noise movement
  • Adjustable strength, speed and influence
  • Separate rotation and location control
  • Layered on delta transforms
