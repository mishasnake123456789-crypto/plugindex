What foxReveal does

foxReveal from foxScripts is an After Effects script for animating the reveal of paths and other animations quickly. A few clicks replace what would otherwise be manual trim path work.

Version 1.2.0 improves speed and supports After Effects from CC 2018 up to 2026, along with macOS Tahoe 26. It also fixes an issue with shapes imported from Overlord. Motion designers drawing lines, icons and diagrams can get reveals in seconds.

Key features

  • Reveals paths and animations quickly
  • Speed improvements in v1.2.0
  • Works with After Effects CC 2018 through 2026
  • macOS Tahoe 26 compatible
  • Fix for shapes imported from Overlord
