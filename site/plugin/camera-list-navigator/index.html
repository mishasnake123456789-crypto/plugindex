3ds Max script for stepping through every scene camera with arrows or a dropdown, with automatic timeline range detection.

What Camera List Navigator does

Camera List Navigator makes moving between cameras in a 3ds Max scene fast. One button grabs the active camera node of the current viewport, and a readout gives the camera total and the one you are on.

Arrow buttons walk through the cameras alphabetically, and a dropdown selects and switches in one click, also pointing the active viewport at whichever camera is picked in the Explorer. Auto Range reads a camera's first and last keyframes and adjusts the timeline to fit, and Auto Select can choose the camera node as you navigate. A layer filter lists layers holding cameras, with a Refresh button after scene changes.

Key features

  • Step through cameras sorted by name with arrows
  • Dropdown for direct selection and viewport switching
  • Camera count and current camera display
  • Auto Range sets timeline from camera keyframes
  • Layer filter with Refresh button
