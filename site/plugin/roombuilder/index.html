What RoomBuilder does

RoomBuilder generates simple interior rooms in 3ds Max with one click. Walls, floor, ceiling, plinths and cornices are created together, which gives visualizers a fast starting shell for interior scenes.

Rectangular rooms take a length, width, height and a floor level on the Z axis. For other layouts, splines are picked to describe the shape, shown in a list and marked red in the viewport, and a Delete button removes a wrong pick. Dimensions can also come from AutoCAD contours. Checkboxes decide whether the floor, ceiling and other details are included. Walls are single-layer and have no thickness.

Key features

  • One-click walls, floor, ceiling, plinths and cornices
  • Rectangular rooms from length, width, height and floor level
  • Custom room shapes from picked splines
  • Works with AutoCAD contours
  • Checkboxes to include or skip floor, ceiling and details
