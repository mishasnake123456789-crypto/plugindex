What Nuclear Explosion does

Nuclear Explosion, from studioromanrec, provides a ready explosion effect for Blender. It is assembled using Cycles render settings together with a compositing setup shown in the product screenshot.

A demo video and documentation are included. It works with Blender 3.4, 3.5, 3.6 and 4.0, and is released under the GPL.
