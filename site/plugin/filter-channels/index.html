What Filter_Channels does

Filter_Channels is a Blender add-on by Carles-V that works inside the Graph Editor. It lets you isolate the X, Y or Z curves of translation, Euler rotation, quaternion rotation and scale, and it supports both classic Actions and Slot actions, which arrived in Blender 4.4.

Buttons for hiding and unhiding curves serve as an alternative to the default keymaps, with Shift-click adding to the selection and Ctrl-click removing from it. Quaternion filtering is off by default and can be switched on in the preferences. No special permissions are required.

Key features

  • Filters X, Y, Z channels for translation, rotation and scale
  • Supports Actions and Slot actions (Blender 4.4+)
  • Hide and unhide buttons for curves
  • Shift and Ctrl click to add or remove from selection
  • Quaternion filtering optional in preferences
