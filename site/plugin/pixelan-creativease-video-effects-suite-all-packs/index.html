What Pixelan CreativEase Video Effects Suite (ALL Packs) does

Pixelan CreativEase Video Effects Suite (All Packs) brings eleven plugins to After Effects and Premiere Pro, arranged in four packs plus extras. They offer hundreds of adjustable film, blur, cartoon and motion options.

The Color pack has PosterWise and ChromaWarp 2; Blur has OrganiBlur and BlurPro; Depth has RiseShine and FlowTexture 2; Grain has GrainPlus 2 and SpatterFlex. Extras include 400 more spices, the free KaleidElastic effect, and StepMotion and StepTime. CreativEase 3 adds an interface for high-resolution monitors, speed optimization and graphics-chip rendering.
