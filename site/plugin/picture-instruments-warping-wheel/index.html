What Picture Instruments Warping Wheel does

Picture Instruments Warping Wheel reinvents color wheels for After Effects and Premiere Pro. Its Color Warp technology bends colors smoothly in the color space, which greatly reduces compression artefacts such as blocking and banding.

Separate wheels handle shadows, mids and highlights, with range and smoothness controls, and a Master Wheel tints or adjusts the entire image. Protect Skin Tones shields faces from unwanted tints, with Boost and Skin Tone Hue controls. It suits graders working with compressed footage who want skin protected without drawing masks.

Key features

  • Color Warp reduces banding and block artefacts
  • Separate shadow, mid and highlight wheels
  • Protect Skin Tones, Boost and Hue controls
  • Range and smoothness controls per wheel
  • Master Wheel for overall adjustments
