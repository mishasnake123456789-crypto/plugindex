3ds Max modeling script that generates quilted and Chesterfield upholstery in diamond or square patterns with buttons.

What Quilted & Chesterfield Script does

This script creates quilted and Chesterfield-style surfaces in 3ds Max, the tufted look seen on sofas, poufs, armchairs and ottomans. Two pattern types are available, diamond and square, and the model is built in a single click.

Buttons and lines can be added to the pattern, and the work can be driven by polygon or vertex selections. It also assigns V-Ray and Corona materials automatically, complete with diffuse and bump maps, so the result is ready to render. Furniture visualizers and interior artists can use it to avoid the long process of hand-modeling and pinching every tuft.

Key features

  • Diamond and square quilting patterns
  • Button Add and Line Add options
  • Polygon and vertex selection
  • Automatic V-Ray and Corona material with diffuse and bump maps
  • Single-click model creation
