What VSE Playback Speed does

VSE Playback Speed, by Andreas-Linnartz, improves how the video sequencer plays back, aiming for smoother speed and detection of pauses. It is listed under Sequencer.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
