What Live Midi Visualizer - Real-Time Midi Controller Add-On does

Live MIDI Visualizer reacts to MIDI controllers inside Blender. Connect a device, press keys, pads, knobs or buttons, and matching objects respond as you play.

It supports piano keys, drum pads, knobs and buttons, and generates a visual MIDI controller layout in the scene. Animated visuals respond in real time. Performers, motion designers and music visualization creators can use it for live demonstrations and recorded visuals. Under the hood you get real-time MIDI visualization in the viewport and piano keys, drum pads, knobs and buttons.

Key features

  • Real-time MIDI visualization in the viewport
  • Piano keys, drum pads, knobs and buttons
  • Generated controller layout
