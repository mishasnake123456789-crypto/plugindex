What Pixflow TypeToon does

Pixflow TypeToon is a typography toolkit for After Effects featuring 64 colorful animated titles in a retro, cartoon-like 3D style. Text can be written in and animated without building the look from scratch.

Each title loops, and backgrounds switch on or off with a single click, producing transparent text animations when you need an overlay. Finished text can be exported out of After Effects at Full HD, which makes it suitable for video posters, logo reveals and general video titles. It gives editors a quick route to playful, vintage-flavored lettering.

Key features

  • 64 colorful animated retro titles
  • 3D-style lettering
  • Looping text animations
  • One-click background on or off
  • Full HD output
