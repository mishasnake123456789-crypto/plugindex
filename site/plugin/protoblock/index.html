Snap-to-grid level blockout tool for Blender with primitives, CSG booleans, kit pieces and FBX export.

What Protoblock does

ProtoBlock bundles blockout tools into a single sidebar tab for level designers. Grid snapping preserves your existing snap settings and restores them afterwards.

Eight primitives are tuned for level design: cube, cylinder, wall, pillar, ramp, floor, arch and stairs. CSG booleans offer Union, Difference and Intersect with auto-apply, kit pieces are reusable, and FBX export includes placeholder collision. Speeds up greyboxing for games. Under the hood you get grid snap with settings backup and 8 level-design primitives.

Key features

  • Grid snap with settings backup
  • 8 level-design primitives
  • Union, Difference and Intersect booleans
  • FBX export with placeholder collision
