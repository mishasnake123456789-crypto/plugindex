Blender add-on that connects the vertex under the cursor when you press J, without selecting a second vertex.

What Quick J ( Connect Vertex Path ) does

Quick J streamlines the connect-vertex-path operation in Blender. Point at a vertex and tap J to join to to it, with no need to click a second vertex first.

The hover radius is adjustable, and after each operation all points except the hovered one are deselected by default (this can be turned off). Modelers cutting edge flow and retopologizing meshes can speed up repetitive connecting steps. Eliminating the second click matters when you connect many points in a row, as in retopology or cutting detail into panels.

Key features

  • Hover and press J to connect
  • Adjustable hover radius
  • Deselects other points after operation
  • Option to disable auto deselect
