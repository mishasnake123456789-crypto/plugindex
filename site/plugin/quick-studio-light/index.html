What Quick Studio Light does

Quick Studio Light speeds up lighting setup in Blender. Activate Lighting Mode with Shift+R, hover over an object, and the light moves automatically to illuminate that spot.

Spacebar enters a precision adjustment mode, with left click to confirm and right click or Esc to cancel. A light parameters panel opens with Ctrl and right mouse, showing standard settings and values from the light's nodes. Selection works by clicking the area a light illuminates. Product and portrait artists place studio lighting by sight.

Key features

  • Lighting Mode on Shift+R
  • Hover to place lights
  • Precision Adjustment Mode
  • Select lights by clicking lit areas
  • Light parameters panel
