Blender add-on that displays rigid body constraint limits in the viewport.

What Visual limits does

Visual limits, by xbodya13, makes rigid body constraints easier to set up in Blender by drawing their limits in the viewport instead of leaving you to guess from numbers.

It is a lightweight add-on of about 2.6 KB, written entirely in Python, for Blender 4.3 and newer. It needs no special permissions, is released under GPL v3.0 or later, and can be installed by dragging it in or from a file.
