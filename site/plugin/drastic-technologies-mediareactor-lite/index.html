What Drastic Technologies MediaReactor Lite does

Drastic Technologies MediaReactor Lite is a PRM plug-in for After Effects, Premiere Pro and Media Encoder. It is an optimized version of the MediaReactor suite offering the most requested import and export formats.

It reads camera raw formats such as Phantom Cine, Panasonic VRW, Canon RMF and CRM, Kinefinity KRW, Codec CDX and Magic Lantern MLV directly, with no transcoding step. MOV files open without QuickTime installed, including uncompressed YCbCr and Cineform. On export it writes MOV and MP4 including ProRes, plus Sony XAVC-S MP4 and MPEG-2.

Key features

  • Reads Phantom, Panasonic, Canon, Kinefinity and Magic Lantern raw
  • Reads MOV without QuickTime installed
  • Writes MOV and MP4 including ProRes
  • Writes XAVC-S MP4 and MPEG-2
  • Works in Premiere Pro, After Effects and Media Encoder
