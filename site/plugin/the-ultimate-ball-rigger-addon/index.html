What The Ultimate Ball Rigger Addon does

The Ultimate Ball Rigger from CGDive builds a bouncing-ball rig in Blender without manual bone setup. Select a ball model, click Generate Ball Rig, parent your mesh, and you are ready to animate.

The rig is sized to match the model and works with a single ball or several meshes. It includes the usual controls: root, center of gravity, rotation, and squash and stretch at the top and bottom, with defaults suited to 2D-style bounces. The stretch direction can be adjusted. A short tutorial is provided, and Blender 4.2 or newer is needed.

Key features

  • One-click ball rig generation
  • Rig sized to the selected ball model
  • Adjustable squash and stretch direction
  • Root, center of gravity and rotation controls
  • Short tutorial included
