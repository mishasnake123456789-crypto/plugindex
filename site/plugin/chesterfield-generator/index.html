What chesterfield generator does

Chesterfield Generator by mgh3d reshapes mesh vertices into the classic tufted Chesterfield look. It works only on editable poly nodes and offers two modes: convert all vertices of a picked mesh, or convert pre-selected vertices arranged in a diamond formation.

Buttonhole depth and aperture control the size and spacing, and wrinkle amount around the buttons and button size can be set. Optional seams between buttonholes have adjustable depth and width, seam tape width can be specified, and a subdivision setting decides the level of the final geometry. It suits artists who are comfortable preparing a base mesh with suitable topology for sofas and headboards.

Key features

  • Convert whole mesh or pre-selected diamond vertices
  • Adjustable buttonhole depth, aperture and button size
  • Wrinkle amount around buttons
  • Optional seams and seam tape with set widths
  • Subdivision level for final geometry
