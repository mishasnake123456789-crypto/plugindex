What Helium + HeliumX Lite Free does

The bundle combines HeliumX Lite's basic 3D toolset with HeliumX AI inside After Effects. The aim is to feel natural for After Effects users who want 3D without leaving the app.

The tools import 3D models, extrude and animate text and paths, add procedural primitives such as cubes, spheres and planes, and create a 3D page scroll. On the AI side, Text to Image and Image to Image generate pictures from prompts or a starting frame, and style presets and ControlNet keep prompts short and results controlled. Seamless texture output and import of custom SDXL models are available. Everything comes at no cost.

Key features

  • Procedural primitives and 3D page scroll
  • Text to Image and Image to Image
