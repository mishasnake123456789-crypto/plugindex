What Stop Motion Animator does

Stop Motion Animator by Copperhead converts fluid 3D animation into the choppy, handmade feel of claymation or LEGO films. It reduces keyframes, changes interpolation to Constant and can add procedural noise for slight imperfection.

Step Frames drops the animation to a lower rate, such as on twos or threes, and Phase Offset staggers objects so they do not all update on the same frame. This gives artists a quick way to stylize existing animation instead of re-keying it.

Key features

  • Step Frames to animate on twos or threes
  • Constant interpolation applied automatically
  • Phase Offset to de-synchronize objects
  • Procedural noise for handmade wobble
