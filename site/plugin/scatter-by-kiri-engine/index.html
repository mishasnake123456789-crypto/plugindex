What Scatter By Kiri Engine does

Scatter by KIRI Engine offers three ways to populate a Blender scene. Point and Shoot lets you place hero assets exactly where you want them, Simple Scatter fills a mesh or curve through a non-destructive Geometry Nodes modifier, and Physics Scatter drops objects using the Rigid Body engine so gravity and collisions decide where they land.

During playback you can push things around with wind, vortex fields and gravity toggles in real time. A preview gallery and loader handles KIRI Asset Packs, and one free pack was included at launch. It is free, documented and released under the GPL license.

Key features

  • Point and Shoot mode for manual hero placement
  • Simple Scatter via a Geometry Nodes modifier
  • Physics Scatter using Rigid Body dynamics
  • Live wind, vortex and gravity control during playback
  • Asset Pack preview gallery and loader
