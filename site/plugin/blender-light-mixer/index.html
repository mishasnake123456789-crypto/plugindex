What Blender Light Mixer does

Blender Light Mixer gathers all scene lighting into a single control panel, so you do not have to hunt through the outliner. It is meant for arch-viz, cinematic, game and product lighting in Cycles.

It detects point, area, spot and sun lights, the HDRI world and emission materials, each with intensity and color controls. Tints are non-destructive, lights can be soloed or muted, setups saved as presets, and HDRIs swapped in one click. Instances are grouped, and a compositor setup with denoise works with Light Groups.

Key features

  • Intensity and color control for every light
  • Solo and mute with swatches
  • Lighting presets and one-click HDRI swap
  • Instance-aware grouping, Light Group compositing
