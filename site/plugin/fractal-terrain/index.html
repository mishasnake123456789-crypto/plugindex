What Fractal Terrain does

Fractal Terrain creates landscapes entirely inside After Effects. It produces three-dimensional terrain from fractal noise and draws it with ray-casting, so mountains and valleys can be made without leaving the compositing environment.

Because the work runs on the GPU, results come quickly, which suits motion designers and VFX artists needing backdrops, abstract topography or title-sequence scenery. The plugin supports After Effects 2020 through 2026. It is a practical choice for title sequences, map-style graphics and sci-fi backdrops where a landscape is needed quickly.

Key features

  • 3D terrain from fractal noise
  • Ray-casting based landscape rendering
  • GPU-accelerated processing
  • Procedural generation inside After Effects
