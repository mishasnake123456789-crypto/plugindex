Blender add-on that selects mesh regions by diffuse texture colour for editing, face sets, material masks and retopology help.

What Edit By Colour By Kiri Engine does

Edit By Colour from KIRI Engine lets you click a colour in an object's diffuse texture and turn it into a selection on the mesh. A threshold value fine-tunes how much similar colour is included, and selections can be smoothed, grown, shrunk or masked.

Selected areas can be duplicated, split off or deleted, converted to face sets for sculpting, or exported as shader attributes to mask materials. There is also a patch-based texturing workflow that bakes part of the mesh, replaces regions and re-bakes into a single texture, plus automatic quad loops around a colour selection to help with manual retopology. It suits scanned or high-poly meshes.

Key features

  • Pick colours from the existing diffuse texture
  • Smooth, grow, shrink or mask selections
  • Duplicate, split or delete selected regions
  • Convert selections to face sets or shader attributes
  • Quad loops around selections to assist retopology
