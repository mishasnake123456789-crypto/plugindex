Blender add-on that animates whole collections of mesh and curve objects with presets, timing controls and procedural effects.

What Vectart Animation does

Vectart Animation animates every object in a Blender collection with one click. Timing is conducted like an orchestra, using delay modes, overlap, randomness and a Musicality control, and 15 presets include Domino Fall, Elastic Pop, Bounce Drop, Spiral In and Typewriter.

A Geometry Nodes mode adds five procedural effects with auto-keyframed inputs: Wave Deform, Noise Displacement, Dissolve, Scatter Grow and Smooth Inflate. Collision avoidance prevents overlaps, keyframes can be copied, pasted in reverse and cleared, and animation types combine scale, rotation, location and extrude.

Key features

  • Collection-wide animation in one click
  • 15 presets such as Spiral In and Domino Fall
  • Delay, overlap, randomness and Musicality timing
  • Five geometry nodes effects
  • Collision avoidance
