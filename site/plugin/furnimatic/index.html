A Blender add-on for parametric furniture built by typing dimensions and picking materials, handles and appliances.

What Furnimatic does

Furnimatic creates fully parametric cabinets and furniture in Blender from numbers and a few choices. Enter the dimensions, select materials, handles, profiles and built-in appliances, and the model updates, which suits kitchen and interior visualization.

Corner cabinets can be generated in L, U and G shapes. Door millings, furniture legs and a handle system with custom positions are created automatically, LED lighting can be placed at chosen distances, and animations are generated in several styles. Furniture templates and material combinations can be saved for reuse. Interior designers and archviz artists benefit most.

Key features

  • Parametric cabinets from typed dimensions
  • L, U and G corner cabinets
  • Automatic door millings, legs and handles
  • Automatically generated animations
  • Reusable furniture and material templates
