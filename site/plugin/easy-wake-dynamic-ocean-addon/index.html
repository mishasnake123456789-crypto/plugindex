What Easy Wake - Dynamic Ocean Addon does

Easy Wake, from Blender Procedural, brings dynamic ocean simulation to Blender. Pressing the Add Dynamic Ocean button in its panel creates an ocean scene that includes wakes from ships moving across the water.

It works with Cycles and Eevee on Blender 4.0 through 4.5, ships with documentation and is released under the GPL license. It suits artists building maritime scenes who want a quick setup instead of assembling simulation parts themselves.
