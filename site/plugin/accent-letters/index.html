What Accent Letters does

Accent Letters, by Roshan-Tiwari, makes it simple to enter accented characters in Blender text objects, with support for the letters used in 55 languages. It is listed under User Interface.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
