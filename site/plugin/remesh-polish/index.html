Blender modifier that remeshes and smooths high-poly mechanical parts non-destructively while preserving their hard edges.

What Remesh + Polish does

Remesh + Polish adds a modifier to Blender that rebuilds the surface of a mechanical object and smooths it without losing its crisp edges. It is intended for high-poly bolts, nuts and machined parts that will serve as sources for baking.

Because it is a modifier, the original stays intact and settings can be revised later. Place it at the end of the stack, tune the parameters, and the output is a clean high-poly ready for bake workflows. It requires manifold geometry, runs with both Cycles and Eevee, and comes with documentation.

Key features

  • Non-destructive remeshing and smoothing modifier
  • Preserves hard edges on mechanical shapes
  • Designed for bake-ready high-poly meshes
  • Works with manifold geometry only
  • Runs with Cycles and Eevee
