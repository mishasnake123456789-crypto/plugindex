What Bricksculpt - Sculpt With Bricks does

BrickSculpt is an extension for the Bricker add-on in Blender, built for people who want to hand-tune a brick model after it has been generated. Instead of regenerating the whole build, you can push and pull individual bricks with brush-style tools.

A draw brush lays down bricks along your stroke and blends them into the existing model, while a cut brush removes them plate by plate. Merge and split brushes combine or break apart pieces, and a paintbrush recolors bricks. The result is a more sculptural way to refine mosaics and brick characters.

Key features

  • Draw brush that merges new bricks into the model
  • Cut brush that takes bricks out plate by plate
  • Merge and split brush for combining or separating parts
  • Paintbrush for recoloring individual bricks
