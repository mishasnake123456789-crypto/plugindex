What Rocket Lasso Slicer does

Rocket Lasso Slicer divides a mesh with a series of parallel cuts and gives back splines, flat sheets, extruded geometry or alternating layers with bevels. It is designed to work with Cloners and other MoGraph tools for parametric modeling and futuristic interface elements.

A Smooth parameter produces clean organic curves, while Expand inflates or deflates the slices relative to the original mesh. A built-in animated Rate setting offsets and loops the cuts. Motion designers can create layered, contour-style looks from existing models without sculpting each slice manually.

Key features

  • Parallel cuts output splines, sheets or extrusions
  • Alternating layers with bevels
  • Smooth parameter for organic curves
  • Expand inflates or deflates slices
  • Animated Rate offsets and loops cuts
