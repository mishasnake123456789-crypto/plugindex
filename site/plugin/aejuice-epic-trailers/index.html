What AEJuice Epic Trailers does

AEJuice Epic Trailers is a collection of cinematic opening titles in the style of blockbuster trailers. Twenty pre-made designs can be applied in one click and dragged into position.

Each has in and out animations, and typography and text effects can be modified, including with your own font and in any language. The Pack Manager adds quick preview and favourites, with fast downloads. Editors and motion designers making trailers for films, video games or shows can reach a dramatic look without building each title from scratch.

Key features

  • 20 customisable pre-made designs
  • One-click application and drag-and-drop placement
  • In and out animations
  • Your own font and any language
  • Pack Manager with preview and favourites
