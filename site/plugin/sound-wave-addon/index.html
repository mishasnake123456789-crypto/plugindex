Blender add-on that animates from audio, from a lip-syncing talking mouth to music visualizers.

What Sound Wave Addon does

studioromanrec's Sound Wave Addon converts sound into motion. Record a voice clip and assign it to the tool, and the character's mouth moves in step with the speech. Audio visualizations are possible too.

Other objects can be animated by sound, an audio file can be assigned to any selected key, and audio can be added to existing keys, replacing them, from anywhere on the timeline. Cycles and Eevee are both supported. A fit for music visualizers and quick talking characters.

Key features

  • One-button talking character
  • Sound-driven animation of objects
  • Audio visualization creation
  • Assign audio to any key
  • Cycles and Eevee support
