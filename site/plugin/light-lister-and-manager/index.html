What Light Lister And Manager does

ZA Lighting Manager gathers many lighting tasks into one Blender panel, helping when large scenes, product renders or cinematic shots become messy.

The Light Lister selects, solos and toggles lights and adjusts color, power, exposure and size. Light linking is simplified with Include and Exclude lists, a target picker aims lights, and Match Camera Exposure analyzes the view brightness. Lights can be animated over a frame range, an emission scanner finds glowing objects, and a Light Mixer offers channels and atmosphere presets such as Neutral, Sepia, Warm and Cool.

Key features

  • Light list with solo, toggle and adjustments
  • Simplified light linking
  • Light target picker and exposure matching
  • Emission scanner and atmosphere mixer
