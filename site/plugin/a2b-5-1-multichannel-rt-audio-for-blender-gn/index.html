What A2B 5.1 Multichannel Rt Audio For Blender Gn does

A2B converts live audio into attributes you can use inside Geometry Nodes, in real time and without baking, keyframes or drivers.

Input can come from a microphone, system sound or external sources. FFT bands are configurable at 8, 16 or 32 with a custom frequency range from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, and the data is written into a Geometry Nodes attribute where the first value holds volume and the rest hold bands. Motion designers building music-reactive visuals get an immediate link.

Key features

  • Real-time audio from mic, system or external input
  • 8, 16 or 32 FFT bands, 20 Hz to 20 kHz
  • Writes audio data to a Geometry Nodes attribute
  • No baking, keyframes or drivers
