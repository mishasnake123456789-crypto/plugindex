FreeBlender

Blender add-on with two timeline buttons: bind a camera to a marker on the current frame, or delete that marker.

What Quick Cam does

Quick Cam adds two small buttons to Blender's timeline panel for camera switching. One binds cameras to markers on the current frame, and the other removes the marker there.

Because the buttons sit among the other timeline controls, building a multi-camera edit takes fewer menu trips. It is released under the GPL license and includes documentation. Artists cutting between cameras in animation will find it speeds up the setup. Anyone cutting between several cameras on a timeline will find the markers quicker to manage.

Key features

  • Bind cameras to markers on the current frame
  • Delete the marker on the current frame
  • Buttons placed in the timeline panel
  • GPL license
  • Documentation included
