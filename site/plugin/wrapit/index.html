Blender add-on that shrink-wraps selected objects with a stretched, crumpled plastic film effect.

What Wrapit does

Wrapit from Blender Guppy creates the look of plastic wrap tightly covering objects. It works on several selected meshes at once and is intended for geometry with extruded details, where the film stretches across the protrusions and wrinkles in between.

The operator is found in the Shift+A menu under the mesh submenu in the 3D viewport, and its settings let you reach different looks. Product visualizers and still-life artists can use it for packaged goods and protective covers.

Key features

  • Plastic film look on mesh objects
  • Handles several selected objects at once
  • Accessible from Shift+A mesh submenu
  • Adjustable settings for different looks
