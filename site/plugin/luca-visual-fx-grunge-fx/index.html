What Luca Visual FX Grunge FX does

Grunge FX from Luca Visual FX combines two plugins in one package for After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve: Grunge FX Overlays and Grunge Transitions.

Overlays provide a range of animated textures, while transitions use animated, often complex grungy textures designed for cutting between clips. Being generator-based, the built-in controls can be combined with extra effects or additional motion controls, and the generous preset library can be customised freely. Editors and motion designers wanting a rough, gritty finish for intros, music videos and edits can drop these in quickly.

Key features

  • Grunge FX Overlays and Grunge Transitions in one package
  • Variety of animated textures
  • Transitions with complex grungy textures
  • Generator-based so extra effects can be combined
  • Many customisable presets
