What Isograd Learning Lab does

Isograd Learning Lab is a learning platform that now offers an Adobe Premiere Pro course next to Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign. Content follows Tosa certification objectives and supports self-paced study.

Students get pre and post assessments, flash cards, short videos, eBooks, challenges and practice tests, with a built-in Immersive Reader. Instructors can manage groups and customize curriculum and learning plans, and the platform is LTI compliant. It serves both self-directed learners and classroom or remote teaching programs.

Key features

  • Tosa pre and post course assessments
  • Self-paced courseware with progress tracking
  • Flash cards, videos, eBooks and practice tests
  • Immersive Reader and 24/7 resources
  • Group management, LTI compliant
