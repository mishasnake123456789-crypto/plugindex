What C4D2Blender does

C4D2Blender simplifies moving a scene from Cinema 4D into Blender. It finds the most recently exported file automatically and brings it in, so you skip browsing for it.

Modifiers and materials are restored, and duplicate names such as Mesh.001 or Material.002 are cleaned up. Collections are organized smartly, with color tags to help identify them. Artists who work in both applications, or who are migrating projects from Cinema 4D, can avoid a lot of manual cleanup after every transfer.

Key features

  • Auto-detects the newest Cinema 4D export
  • Restores modifiers and materials
  • Cleans duplicate names like Mesh.001
  • Collection organization with color tags
