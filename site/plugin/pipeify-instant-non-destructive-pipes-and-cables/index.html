Blender add-on that turns a cylinder into editable pipe and cable runs with bends and welded joints via Geometry Nodes.

What Pipeify - Instant Non-Destructive Pipes & Cables does

Pipeify builds pipe systems in Blender from a simple base cylinder, using Geometry Nodes and stacked modifiers, so everything remains adjustable in real time.

Bend segments come in 45, 90, 135 and 180 degree presets or any custom angle, with bending in the ZY plane or XZ plane. Radius, segments, angles and path direction can be changed at any time. Smart Welding merges vertices at joints for clean connections. It suits industrial scenes, sci-fi props and cabling.

Key features

  • One-click base and bend segments
  • 45, 90, 135 and 180 degree presets plus custom
  • Non-destructive modifier workflow
  • Bending planes in ZY and XZ
  • Smart Welding at joints
