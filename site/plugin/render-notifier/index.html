Blender add-on that messages you on Telegram when a render or image sequence finishes or is cancelled.

What Render Notifier does

Neeraj Lagwankar's Render Notifier lets you leave your computer during a long render. A Telegram bot sends an alert to your phone when Blender finishes.

It reports single images, image sequences and cancelled jobs, and sends stats for all frames when a sequence completes. Per-frame notifications can be switched off so only the final message arrives. Freelancers and overnight renderers get peace of mind. This is particularly handy when a render farm, remote machine or overnight job runs without anyone watching the screen.

Key features

  • Telegram notifications via a bot
  • Single image and sequence alerts
  • Alert on cancelled renders
  • Final stats for image sequences
  • Option to disable per-frame messages
