What FX Texturator-800 DCTL does

FX Texturator-800 DCTL supplies a large collection of over 2000 grayscale pixel textures inside DaVinci Resolve. The textures are designed to be combined with your footage so creative effects can be achieved without leaving the application.

It is delivered as a DCTL and works with DaVinci Resolve 16 through 21. The listing shows version 0.1.0, released on May 20, 2024 as the initial release, and a demo video is on YouTube. Colorists and editors seeking stylized pixel patterns, overlays and texture mixes can use the library as a source of creative material.
