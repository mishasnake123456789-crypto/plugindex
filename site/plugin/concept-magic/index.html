What Concept-Magic does

Concept Magic turns Blender viewports into polished concept renders using cloud AI. It was made by Chipp Walters, who also created KIT OPS.

One-click Style Injectors drive ComfyUI-based workflows within Blender, no local GPU is needed, and access is credit-based without subscriptions. It works with Cycles and Eevee scenes. Concept artists and designers can produce stylized imagery from rough blockouts. Because processing happens in the cloud, it is accessible on modest hardware, and the credit system means you pay only for the renders you make.

Key features

  • Cloud AI rendering, no local GPU
  • One-click Style Injectors
  • ComfyUI-based workflows in Blender
  • Credit-based, no subscription
