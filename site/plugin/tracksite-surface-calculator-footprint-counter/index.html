What Tracksite Surface Calculator + Footprint Counter does

The Tracksite Surface Calculator + Footprint Counter helps paleontologists analyze fossil track sites in Blender.

Trace a track-bearing surface by extruding vertices into a polygon and the add-on computes its area and perimeter. Footprints are counted from vertices placed on each print, which suits sites with hundreds or thousands of tracks. It runs on Blender 3.0 through 4.1, includes documentation and is distributed under a Creative Commons license. The measurements support documentation and quantitative analysis of fossil sites.

Key features

  • Surface area and perimeter calculation
  • Polygon tracing by extruding vertices
  • Footprint counting with vertices
  • Scales to thousands of footprints
  • Blender 3.0 to 4.1
