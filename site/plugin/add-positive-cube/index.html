Blender add-on that inserts a cube with its origin at a corner, useful for scaling from the ground or a wall.

What Add Positive Cube does

Add Positive Cube adds a cube whose origin sits at its corner. That makes it simple to scale with the origin staying on the floor or against a wall.

It is reached through the 3D View's Add Mesh menu. Version 1.1.0 deselects other objects before adding the cube, matching normal behavior. Architectural and layout modelers blocking out walls and floors benefit. A corner origin makes dimension changes predictable, since scaling expands the cube away from the floor or wall instead of from its center.

Key features

  • Cube with corner origin
  • Ideal for scaling from the ground
  • Add Mesh menu access
  • Deselects other objects (v1.1.0)
