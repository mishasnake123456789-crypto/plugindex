What Snapline does

Snapline speeds up placing objects in Blender. Its real-time snapping and alignment tools match one object's position, rotation and orientation to another with minimal effort.

Version 2.0.0 added a Helper Offset parameter. It supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer, needs no special permissions, and installs by drag and drop or from disk. It is made by Berun.

Key features

  • Real-time snapping and alignment
  • Matches position, rotation and orientation
  • Helper Offset parameter
  • Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
  • Drag-and-drop installation
