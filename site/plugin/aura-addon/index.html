What Aura Addon does

Aura Addon applies a glowing aura effect to video, aimed at creators working on cartoons, comics and ads. It is part of the Studioromanrec Blender Add-Ons bundle.

It supports Blender 3.5, 3.6, 4.0, 4.1, 4.2 and 4.3, is GPL-licensed and comes with documentation and a demo video. It offers a quick stylized energy look. You get aura effect on video, blender 3.5 to 4.3 and documentation and demo video, along with the rest of the feature set.
