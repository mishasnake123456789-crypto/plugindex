What Randomize_Bones_Pose does

Randomize_Bones_Pose makes unexpected poses for armatures in Blender with a single click, useful for characters and particle-like animation.

It randomizes rotation, location and scale of the bones on the selected armature, either adding to the current transform or replacing it, and keyframes the new values automatically. Motion designers can generate varied poses quickly. Randomness can serve as a starting point for crowd variation, abstract motion or particle-like effects, which the artist can then refine with manual edits.

Key features

  • Random rotation, location and scale
  • Add or replace current transform
  • Automatic keyframes
  • One-click operation
