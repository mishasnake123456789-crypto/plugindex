What Boom Library Crowds - War & Battle does

Boom Library Crowds: War & Battle holds 3,935 sounds in 251 files, around 42 GB, recorded by male and female actors. Emotions include agony, moaning, fear, pain, celebration, fighting, begging, death, protest, work and war cries. It suits After Effects, Premiere Pro and other audio and video software.

Single highlight voices sit alongside large crowd background washes, so scenes can be built from foreground and ambience. It is designed to pair with BOOM weapon libraries like Medieval Weapons and Historical Firearms. Files are 24-bit, 96 kHz WAV with extensive metadata and PDF and XLS content lists.
