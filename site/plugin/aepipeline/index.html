What aePipeline does

aePipeline, from Eric at VFX Tools, is a pipeline tool for After Effects artists handling VFX shots. It builds shots, tracks versions, queues renders and produces mattes with a few clicks.

AutoComp assembles comps from plates and pairs a reference QuickTime with its plate even when names differ. QueueUp manages render settings, output paths and routing with a filename safety check. matteMaker prepares matte shots, accounts for the slate and renders alpha-only ProRes 422. A New Client Wizard covers slates, folders, handles and CDL paths, and conditional rules allow things like a different LUT for night shots. Less Mode shrinks the interface.

Key features

  • AutoComp builds comps and matches reference QuickTimes
  • QueueUp render settings with filename safety check
  • matteMaker renders alpha-only ProRes 422 mattes
  • New Client Wizard for slates and folders
  • Conditional rules such as night-shot LUTs
