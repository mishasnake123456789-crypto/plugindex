What Camera Orbit Render (Addon) does

Camera Orbit Render takes turntable-style views automatically. The camera may circle the 3D cursor, one object, the midpoint of several objects, or a focus object you define.

It creates a focus empty at the center of the selection, or at the cursor if nothing is selected, then renders the chosen number of images straight to disk. Handy for product views, photogrammetry-style references and asset previews. Rendering straight to disk means a full set of angles can be produced without touching the render window.

Key features

  • Orbit around cursor, object or group center
  • Custom focus object
  • Focus empty created automatically
  • Renders a defined number of images to disk
