What Puzzleurprint - 3D Printing Assembly Toolkit does

Puzzleurprint helps 3D printing hobbyists and makers whose models are bigger than their printer bed. It generates connector objects that slice a model into smaller pieces and add the elements needed to fit them back together after printing.

A parameter system lets you tune connectors, recall settings from existing ones and copy them between connectors, while on-screen gizmos adjust most values live. Sixteen planar connector shapes create jigsaw-style joins, with a stopper option for a flat back cut, and a 3D puzzle cut works on sculpts along the z direction. Cylinder inlays are another joining option.

Key features

  • Connector objects that cut models into parts
  • Parameter recall plus copy and paste between connectors
  • Gizmos for adjusting values on the fly
  • 16 planar jigsaw-style connector shapes
  • 3D puzzle cutting for sculpts
