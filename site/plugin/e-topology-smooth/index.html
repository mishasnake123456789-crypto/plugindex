What E Topology Smooth does

E Topology Smooth is a Python-only Blender add-on for refining the surface flow of meshes. It analyzes continuity from G0 up to G4 and applies several curve fitting algorithms to produce smoother topology.

It runs on Blender 5.1 and newer, with builds for Windows x64 and Arm, macOS Intel and Apple Silicon, and Linux. Install by dragging it into Blender or from disk in preferences. It is released under GPL v3 or later.

Key features

  • G0 to G4 continuity analysis
  • Multiple curve fitting algorithms
  • Topology smoothing in Blender
  • Builds for Windows, macOS and Linux
  • GPL v3 or later
