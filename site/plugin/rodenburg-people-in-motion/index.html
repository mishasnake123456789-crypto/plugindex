Character animation tool for Cinema 4D with drag-and-drop rigged people, a Walk Designer and crowd path controls.

What Rodenburg People in Motion does

Rodenburg People in Motion animates human characters in Cinema 4D. Rigged figures from DAZ 3D (Genesis 2 or 3), Renderpeople, AXYZ Design and Autodesk Character Generator can be dropped straight in, and a rig builder adds a standard joint rig to other models.

Walk Designer creates walk cycles and actions such as climbing stairs or sitting down, while automatic IK controls cover head, chest, hips, knees, feet, elbows and hands. Crowds are directed with staircases, elevators, escalators, seats, curves and walls. It runs alongside the older version, keeping existing projects usable.

Key features

  • Drag and drop rigged characters from several sources
  • Rig builder for standard joint rigs
  • Walk Designer with stairs and sitting actions
  • Automatic IK for head, hips, feet and hands
  • Crowd control with stairs, escalators and seats
