What Deform to Corrective does

Deform to Corrective, by a.a.andrusenko, captures the deformed result of a mesh and stores it as a corrective shape key that sits ahead of the rig. That makes pose-specific fixes easier to build. It falls under Workflow.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.0.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Saves evaluated deformation as a shape key
  • Live pre-rig corrective
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later
  • Requires Blender 5.0.0 or newer
