What Softbody Simulator does

Softbody Simulator, by Shuhan, brings secondary motion to chains and bones in Blender. The Chain Simulator generates a chain and adds dynamics in five steps, while the Bone Simulator adds dynamics to bones in three, with any bone settable as ROOT.

The model and curve are backed up automatically, controllers can be added to the chain at any point, and parameters can be modified and the chain re-simulated at any time. A simulation can be removed or the chain deleted with one click, and a demo curve is included. Animators wanting swinging chains, tails or hair-like parts will find setup quick.

Key features

  • Chain Simulator in five steps
  • Bone Simulator in three steps
  • Automatic backup of model and curve
  • Re-simulate or remove with one click
  • Add controllers at any point
