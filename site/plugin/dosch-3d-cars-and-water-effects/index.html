What Dosch 3D: Cars & Water Effects does

Dosch 3D: Cars & Water Effects is a set of 96 water effect models from Dosch Design, built for Cinema 4D and many other applications. They are delivered in numerous formats, including Cinema 4D R13 and up, 3DS, FBX, OBJ, 3ds Max 9+, Lightwave, Artlantis, KeyShot, Modo, Collada, VRML and DXF.

Scale, materials, colors, transparency and animation can all be tailored, and custom textures and labels are supported. Because they are static meshes, the effects can be placed in a scene for a frozen-splash look without running a fluid simulation, which is helpful for product and automotive imagery.

Key features

  • 96 water effect 3D models
  • Cinema 4D R13+ and many other formats
  • Editable materials, colors and transparency
  • Custom textures and labels supported
  • Animatable elements
