What Scale Print Camera - Pdf & Exact Scale does

Scale Print Camera sets up an orthographic camera whose view matches a chosen paper size at a chosen scale, such as 1:50 or 1:100, tied to real paper size and DPI.

Paper formats include ISO A4 to A0, ANSI and ARCH, or custom millimeter sizes, and a true-size PDF can be exported. Useful for architects, engineers and designers producing technical drawings from 3D models. Highlights cover exact-scale orthographic camera and ISO, ANSI, ARCH and custom paper sizes.

Key features

  • Exact-scale orthographic camera
  • ISO, ANSI, ARCH and custom paper sizes
  • DPI-matched setup
  • True-size PDF export
