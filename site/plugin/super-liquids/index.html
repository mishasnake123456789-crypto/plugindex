What Super Liquids does

Super Liquids, from Motion Design School, builds hand-drawn-style liquid trails in After Effects. You draw the elements, apply them to an object and produce custom liquid animations frame by frame.

It works on both still and moving objects and runs in horizontal and vertical directions. Your own custom liquids can be added to the library. Everything is built from shape layers, and shape modifiers are supported, so the animations stay editable.

Key features

  • Frame-by-frame liquid trail animation
  • Static and moving objects
  • Horizontal and vertical directions
  • Extend with your own liquids
  • Shape layers with modifier support
