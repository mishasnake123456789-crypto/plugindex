What Cloudhaven does

Cloudhaven from R Animation Studios makes atmospheric effects with Geometry Nodes. It creates procedural clouds, lightning and tornadoes with intuitive controls.

Tornado shape, intensity and movement can be adjusted, results export to VDB in one click, and a library of more than 600 high-resolution VDB clouds is included. Skies and storm scenes can be assembled quickly. Among its tools are procedural clouds with Geometry Nodes, procedural lightning and tornadoes and one-click VDB export.

Key features

  • Procedural clouds with Geometry Nodes
  • Procedural lightning and tornadoes
  • One-click VDB export
  • 600+ high-resolution VDB clouds
