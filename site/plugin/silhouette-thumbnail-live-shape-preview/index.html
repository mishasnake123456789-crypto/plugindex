What Silhouette Thumbnail - Live Shape Preview does

Silhouette Thumbnail adds a real-time silhouette preview to the Blender viewport. It lets you check shape, pose and readability while modeling, sculpting, posing or animating.

The preview is independent from the viewport, so your camera, render settings, materials, lighting, transforms and shading stay untouched. Character and concept artists can judge silhouettes without toggling modes. The feature set spans live silhouette thumbnail in the 3D View, viewport-independent preview and leaves camera, materials and settings alone.
