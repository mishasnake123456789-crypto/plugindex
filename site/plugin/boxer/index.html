What Boxer does

Boxer, developed by .nook, adds an automatic box behind text in After Effects. A single click creates it, and it responds as the text changes.

A streamlined interface provides five quick starting points, and sliders for offset and trim plus individual corner controls refine each box. It is compatible with After Effects 2023 through 2026. Designers making captions, lower thirds, labels and UI-style titles can generate and tweak boxes far faster than drawing and matching shapes by hand.

Key features

  • One-click dynamic text boxes
  • Five quick starting points
  • Offset sliders
  • Individual corner controls and trim sliders
  • After Effects 2023 to 2026
