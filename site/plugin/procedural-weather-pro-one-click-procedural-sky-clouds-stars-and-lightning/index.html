What Procedural Weather Pro - One-Click Procedural Sky, Clouds, Stars & Lightning does

Procedural Weather Pro creates an entire sky inside Blender's World shader using nodes alone, with no image textures. One click builds the setup, and six presets cover sunny day, overcast, golden hour, thunder, starry night and dark night.

Master sliders control elevation and strength, and a blend dial fades toward the next preset. An advanced section has sub-panels for sky, clouds, sun, moon, stars, lightning, god rays and shadows. Clouds drift, stars twinkle and lightning flashes are driven by the frame number, so they animate without keyframes. Archviz exteriors, storm cinematics and stylized night scenes are natural fits.

Key features

  • Six presets from Sunny Day to Dark Night
  • Elevation, strength and preset blend controls
  • Procedural moon, star field and thunderstorm lightning
  • Frame-driven cloud drift and star twinkle without keyframes
  • Advanced sub-panels including god rays and shadows
