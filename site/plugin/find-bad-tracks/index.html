What Find Bad Tracks does

Find Bad Tracks is a Blender add-on by Walles for clean-up of motion tracking data. When the majority of tracks move in one direction and a single track strays the other way, the add-on flags that outlier for human review.

Bad tracks are listed by badness score; a click selects the track in the clip editor and moves to the frame where it behaves worst. A separate list shows pairs of tracks that come close to each other during their lifetimes, and clicking a pair moves to a frame where they overlap. It uses the median as the midpoint, which resists outliers.

Key features

  • Highlights tracks that move differently
  • Ranked list of bad tracks by badness score
  • Click to select and jump to the worst frame
  • Duplicate Tracks list for overlapping pairs
  • Median-based comparison resilient to outliers
