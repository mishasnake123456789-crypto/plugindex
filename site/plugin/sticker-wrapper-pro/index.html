Blender add-on that scatters PNG stickers across meshes with wear, random sizes and transform tools.

What Sticker Wrapper Pro does

Sticker Wrapper Pro places stickers and decals procedurally in Blender. Point it at a folder of PNGs, choose a count and scatter mode, and generate.

Area-weighted or uniform modes keep stickers flush and natural on curved, organic or hard-surface meshes. Wear & Damage uses noise to fade, tear and erode edges, and transform tools rotate, scale, flip and offset selected stickers. A min and max scale range ensures sizes differ, and unlimited images can be loaded.

Key features

  • Procedural scatter placement
  • Wear & Damage edge erosion
  • Rotate, scale, flip and offset tools
  • Random size range
  • Loads PNGs from a folder
