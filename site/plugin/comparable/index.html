Blender add-on that flips visibility between two similar objects or collections from a sidebar for fast comparison.

What Comparable does

Comparable helps you spot differences between near-identical versions of a model. Select two objects or collections, then press the switch button in the sidebar panel to toggle which one is visible.

A reset button makes both visible again, and an Affect Render option extends the switching to the final render. It's handy for comparing retopology against a sculpt, before and after edits, or alternate design options.

Key features

  • Switch button toggles visibility between two items
  • Reset button restores both
  • Affect Render option
  • Works with objects and collections
