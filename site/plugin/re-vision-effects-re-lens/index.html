What RE:Vision Effects RE:Lens does

RE:Lens, by RE:Vision Effects, handles 360 and 180 VR as well as fisheye footage inside After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. It converts between rectilinear, equirectangular, fisheye, super fisheye, super wide-angle, mirror ball and parabolic mirror projections.

The spherical stabiliser tracks global camera orientation and offers an editable stabilisation profile. Super fisheye can go to 360 panoramic in a single step, 360 can be converted to 2D with an animatable point of view and graphics projected back, and fisheye can be straightened with distortion and chromatic fringing corrections. High-resolution panoramas and virtual camera moves are supported. Valuable for VR editors and lens-correction work.

Key features

  • Conversion between many lens projection formats
  • Spherical stabiliser for 360 and fisheye
  • Super fisheye to 360 in one step
  • 360 to 2D with animatable point of view
  • Distortion and chromatic fringing correction
