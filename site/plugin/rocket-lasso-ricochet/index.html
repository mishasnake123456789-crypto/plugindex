What Rocket Lasso Ricochet does

Rocket Lasso Ricochet draws a path that bounces off geometry the way a laser beam would. It can fill a model's volume with hundreds of thousands of bounces, wrap around objects, or detect collisions from a distance.

The entire route is precalculated, so there is no frame dependency and no waiting on the timeline. The result plugs into generator objects, volume building, Hair, Sketch & Toon and third-party renderers. It suits motion designers creating abstract line art, light-ray effects and procedural shapes in Cinema 4D.

Key features

  • Spline bounces off surfaces like a laser
  • Fills volumes with huge numbers of bounces
  • Precalculated path, no frame dependency
  • Works with generators, volumes and Hair
  • Compatible with third-party renderers
