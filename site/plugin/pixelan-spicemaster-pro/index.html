What Pixelan SpiceMASTER Pro does

Pixelan SpiceMASTER Pro makes soft, organic transitions between two clips in After Effects and Premiere Pro, and can animate other video effects inside a single clip. More than 400 customizable transitions can be adjusted for position, direction, speed and softness.

It offers 600 OrganicFX and over 1,000 spice geometries in all. A MIXER control combines geometries with a luminance dissolve for directional film dissolves, and soft effects suit titles, logos and picture-in-picture. Controls are individually keyframable with Bezier curves and real-time previews, with GPU rendering and thousands of savable presets.

Key features

  • 400+ soft organic transitions
  • 1,000+ spice geometries and 600 OrganicFX
  • MIXER blends geometry with luminance dissolve
  • Effects for titles, logos and PIP
  • GPU-accelerated with Bezier keyframing
