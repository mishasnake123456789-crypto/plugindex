What JB3D Labeler does

JB3D Labeler is a Blender add-on for putting artwork onto surfaces as stacked material decals. It also handles labels that wrap fully around a bottle, can or similar product shape.

It suits product visualization artists who would otherwise assemble label materials and UV placement by hand. Because the decals live in the material setup, graphics can be layered and adjusted without redoing the whole shader.

Key features

  • Layered material decals
  • Full 360-degree label wrapping
  • Built for product visualization
  • Works inside Blender materials
