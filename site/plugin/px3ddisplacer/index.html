What px3DDisplacer does

px3DDisplacer is a scripted utility plugin that reshapes Editable Poly surfaces in 3ds Max, pushing vertices with another geometric object. Instead of reading a grayscale displacement map, it follows the real volume of the displacer, so the surface takes on its form.

The workflow is simple: position the displacer object against the surface, then press the apply button. It ships as one script file, px3DDisplacer.ms, and supports 3ds Max 5.1 to 9.0. It suits modelers who want to impress shapes into terrain or other surfaces with objects they can place and move interactively.

Key features

  • Displaces Editable Poly vertices with any geometry object
  • Uses real geometry rather than grayscale maps
  • Surface adopts the displacer's volume shape
  • Apply button after positioning the displacer
  • Single px3DDisplacer.ms script, 3ds Max 5.1 to 9.0
