What Print2CAD 2025 KI - PDF nach DWG Bidirektional does

Print2CAD 2025 KI converts PDF files into DWG or DXF drawings, transferring vectors, lines, arcs and pixel graphics. AI vectorization turns scanned or real images into CAD elements using edge recognition, and OCR reads PDF text, including text stored as raster or lines and blueprint scans with corner recognition.

Output covers DWG and DXF versions 14 and 2000 to 2025, with an integrated postprocessor, viewer and plot functions, plus a PDF viewer with distance and area measurement. Coordinates can be calibrated with reference points, and batch and command-line modes convert thousands of files in one run at no extra charge.
