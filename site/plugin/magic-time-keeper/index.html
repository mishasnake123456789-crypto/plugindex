What Magic Time Keeper does

Magic Time Keeper, from Digital DiLeo, is a time tracker built into Blender. It logs how long you actively work on a scene and stores the total inside the .blend file, so the figure travels with the project.

Counting stops by itself once you have been idle for five minutes, and picks up again when you click. The running total appears in hours, minutes and seconds within the Scene Properties panel. Freelancers who bill by time or anyone curious how long a piece took will find it helpful.

Key features

  • Logs active working time in the .blend file
  • Pauses after five minutes of inactivity
  • Resumes on your next click
  • Total shown in the Scene Properties panel
  • Time persists across sessions
