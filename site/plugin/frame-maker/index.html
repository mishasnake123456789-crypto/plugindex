3ds Max script that builds picture frames from selected images, matching each image's ratio, with profiles, colors and glass.

What Frame Maker does

Frame Maker quickly creates picture frames in 3ds Max from selected images. Each frame is sized to the correct ratio of the imported file, and many frames can be made together in just a few clicks.

There are 18 modern and classic frame profiles and 12 colors, split between six solid finishes and six wood textures. Glass can be a box, a plane or omitted. Corona, V-Ray and Standard materials are supported. It saves interior visualizers from modeling and texturing each frame by hand when decorating walls with art or photographs.

Key features

  • Frames sized to each image's ratio
  • Multiple frames created at once
  • 18 modern and classic profiles
  • 12 colors including six wood textures
  • Box glass, plane glass or none, with Corona, V-Ray and Standard materials
