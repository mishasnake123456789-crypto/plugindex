What Rbc does

RBC from aka studios builds realistic vehicle rigs in Blender through an intuitive interface. Setup can use presets, a custom configuration or a QuickRig approach in the Pro tier.

A collisions panel is in both free and Pro versions, while the FX panel and advanced setup, tuning and controls are Pro features. The free tier offers a single car rig with limited options. Documentation is provided. Animators and game artists can get driving simulations without constructing physics from scratch.

Key features

  • Physics-based vehicle rigs
  • Presets, Custom and QuickRig types
  • Collisions panel in free and Pro
  • FX panel and tuning in Pro
