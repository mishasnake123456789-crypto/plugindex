What Hair Particle To Hair Card Generator does

This Blender add-on turns hair particle systems into hair cards, the flat strips commonly used for real-time characters. The conversion happens in a single click and produces clean curve-based cards.

Density and card thickness are adjustable, so you can balance performance against coverage and match the character. A default hair material is applied automatically for instant feedback, and since the cards are curves, they stay editable for refinement instead of forcing a restart. It targets game-ready and optimization workflows and works with both Cycles and Eevee.

Key features

  • One-click particle to hair card conversion
  • Adjustable density and card thickness
  • Editable curve-based output
  • Default hair material applied automatically
