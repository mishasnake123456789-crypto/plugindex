What Genesis: Light Generator does

Genesis: Light Generator makes lighting setups in Blender by applying color harmonies, which helps artists reach pleasing palettes without choosing each light's hue by hand. It offers six color modes: Range, Complementary, Triad, Monochromatic, Split Complementary and Analogous.

Lights can be arranged in three layouts: a full sphere around the object, a top sphere above the horizon, or a spiraling grid overhead. Point, Sun, Spot, Area and Plane lights are supported, the last using an emission shader on a mesh plane, and more than eight settings allow fine tuning. Lighting and lookdev artists exploring moods will find it a fast starting point.

Key features

  • Six color harmony modes
  • Sphere, Top Sphere and Grid layouts
  • Point, Sun, Spot, Area and Plane lights
  • 8+ settings for hand tuning
