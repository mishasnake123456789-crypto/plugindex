What BOOM Library Seasons of Earth - European Winter - 3D Surround does

BOOM Library Seasons of Earth: European Winter (3D Surround) delivers cold-season ambiences recorded in the field with a Schoeps ORTF-3D Surround Set. Content spans snow, ice, water and wildlife including wolves, ravens, foxes and birds.

The eight-channel layout has lower L, R, Ls, Rs and upper Lh, Rh, LSh, RSh channels, with dedicated stereo mixes of the same material. The set is 244 files at 88.4 GB, or 366 files at 110.5 GB with stereo. Files are 24-bit, 96 kHz WAV with metadata, PDF and XLS info, aimed at Atmos, VR and stereo mixes for TV, online and mobile.
