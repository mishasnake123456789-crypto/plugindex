What miauu's Modifiers' Gizmo Aligner does

Modifiers' Gizmo Aligner by miauu orients the gizmos of the Symmetry, Slice and Mirror modifiers in 3ds Max. Instead of rotating them by hand, you pick three points in the scene and the gizmo lines up with that plane.

For Symmetry there is an Align to Working Pivot's Plane option for the XY, XZ or YZ plane, and the Slice modifier can align to the currently active grid. Autodesk 3ds Max and 3ds Max Design from 2013 to 2026 are supported, and there is a demo video. Modelers working on tilted or non-axis-aligned geometry will find it saves effort.

Key features

  • Aligns gizmos of Symmetry, Slice and Mirror modifiers
  • Pick three points in the scene
  • Symmetry plane aligned to working pivot XY, XZ or YZ
  • Slice plane aligned to the active grid
  • Works with 3ds Max 2013 through 2026
