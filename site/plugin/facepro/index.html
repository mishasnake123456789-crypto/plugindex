AI portrait retouching and face masking plugin for After Effects and Premiere Pro.

What FacePro does

FacePro uses AI to find faces and retouch them in After Effects and Premiere Pro. It handles tasks like blemish removal and skin smoothing, applying the effect through a face mask so the rest of the picture stays as it is.

Four mask modes are on offer: None, AI Mask, Skin Color and External. Version 1.1.0 improved the smoothing filter. The plugin works with versions from 2020 through 2026 of both host applications, and comes from vimager.

Key features

  • AI face masking
  • Blemish removal and skin smoothing
  • Mask modes: None, AI, Skin Color, External
  • Improved smoothing filter in 1.1.0
  • Versions 2020 to 2026
