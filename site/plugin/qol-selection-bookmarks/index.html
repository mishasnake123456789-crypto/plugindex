Bookmarks the current selection from an N-Panel in Blender and recalls objects or components later.

What Qol Selection Bookmarks does

QOL Selection Bookmarks remembers what you had selected. Press Add in the N-Panel and the current selection is stored for later recall.

The add-on detects what is selected and creates the right type of bookmark, covering objects such as meshes, lights, curves and metaballs, as well as vertices, edges and faces. Built-in management lets you extend or remove items in a bookmark. Modelers and riggers who keep returning to the same parts will save time.

Key features

  • Add button in the N-Panel
  • Automatic bookmark type detection
  • Objects, vertices, edges and faces
  • Extend or remove bookmark items
