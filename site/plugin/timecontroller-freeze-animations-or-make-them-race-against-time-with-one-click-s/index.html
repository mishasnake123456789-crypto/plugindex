What Timecontroller: Freeze Animations Or Make Them Race Against Time With One Click. (Slow And Fast Motion) does

Time Controller changes the speed of existing animation in Blender without touching the original keyframes. It works for objects, character rigs, cameras and other animation types.

Presets give 0.25x, 0.5x, 2x and 4x speeds, and a custom playback speed is available for other values. The timeline range and playback settings sync automatically, and you see the result instantly with no baking or rendering. It suits slow-motion, speed ramping, time-lapse and stylized effects.
