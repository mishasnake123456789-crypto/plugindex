What ALIBI Music Extension does

The ALIBI Music Extension puts a large audio library in the Premiere Pro workspace. Its catalogue holds over 300,000 music and sound effect files, with albums and curated playlists that keep growing.

Results can be filtered by mix type, cutdowns, genre, tempo and more. Any file, including stems, downloads straight into the project, and licensing is handled through the extension. It suits editors who need to find and clear music without leaving the timeline.

Key features

  • 300,000+ music and effects files
  • Filters for mix type, cutdowns, genre and tempo
  • Download tracks and stems into Premiere
  • Albums and curated playlists
  • License through the extension
