What BL Perspective Transform does

BL Perspective Transform, by usrname0, lets you pin the corners of a strip to distort its perspective directly in the video sequencer preview. It is listed under Sequencer.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 5.0.0 or newer.
