Blender add-on that highlights viewport-selected objects and bones in the Outliner automatically.

What Quick.Highlight does

Quick.Highlight, from EasyAddons, makes the Outliner follow what you select in the 3D viewport. It is useful in busy scenes where finding the selected item in a long list wastes time.

Matching entries are highlighted, including objects that belong to several collections. Expansion happens only once per selection change to keep things light, and there are options to expand all selected objects, expand bones in Pose Mode, and cap the number of objects expanded, with a default of 10.

Key features

  • Outliner highlights viewport selection
  • Handles objects in multiple collections
  • Pose Mode bone expansion
  • Configurable expansion limit
