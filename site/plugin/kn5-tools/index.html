What KN5 Tools does

KN5 Tools, by Gwarny, imports Assetto Corsa KN5 files, both cars and tracks, straight into Blender. It is listed under Import-Export.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.0.0 or newer.
