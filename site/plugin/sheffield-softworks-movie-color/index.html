What Sheffield Softworks Movie Color does

Sheffield Softworks Movie Color applies stylised film looks in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and also in Final Cut Pro X and Motion through FxFactory on Apple Silicon and Intel. More than 50 presets are included, and you can design and save your own.

The plugin produces two tinted images and blends them into the original using masks that act as adjustable stencils. A Pre-Processing section handles saturation, brightness and contrast, and there is a Bleach Bypass setting. Looks can be adjusted scene to scene or used as starting points. Editors who want a graded feel without leaving their editing system will enjoy the quick workflow.

Key features

  • 50+ presets with custom look saving
  • Two tinted images mixed with masks
  • Pre-Processing for saturation, brightness and contrast
  • Bleach Bypass setting
  • Runs via FxFactory on Apple Silicon and Intel
