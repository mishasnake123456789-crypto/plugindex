What Render-Pro does

Render-Pro, from Apex Assets, is a Blender tool for lighting and rendering. It works with both Cycles and Eevee and supports Blender 4.2 through 4.5.

Documentation and a demo video are included, and it is released under the GPL license. Suitable for artists who want a quicker route to polished lighting and renders. It is aimed at artists who want an organized approach to lighting and output rather than setting everything up from scratch every time.

Key features

  • Lighting and rendering tool
  • Cycles and Eevee support
  • Blender 4.2 through 4.5
  • Documentation and demo video
