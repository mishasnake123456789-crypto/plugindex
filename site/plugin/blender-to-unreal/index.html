What Blender To Unreal does

Blender To Unreal is a Blender add-on from B3dHub that sends content to Unreal Engine with just a few clicks. It covers meshes and objects, animations, collisions, sockets and LODs, and exports in batches over FBX.

Documentation is included, and it is a Blender extension-compatible add-on distributed under the GPL license. Game developers, architects and 3D artists can use it to avoid hand-configuring FBX settings and naming conventions for each asset.

Key features

  • Exports meshes, animations, collisions, sockets and LODs
  • A few clicks per export
  • Batch export support
  • FBX export
  • Documentation included
