What Muzzle Flash Fx Pro does

Muzzle Flash FX Pro makes gunfire effects without node setups. Point an emitter, press FIRE, and an animated flash appears with sparks landing at the hit point, baked to keyframes.

The emitter is set up once, then each shot is a single click. Smart target tracking locks the gun onto an object and follows it as it moves. VFX artists and game animators can add gunfire to scenes quickly. In practice that means one-click FIRE for each shot and muzzle flash with impact sparks.

Key features

  • One-click FIRE for each shot
  • Muzzle flash with impact sparks
  • Target tracking on moving objects
  • Baked to keyframes
