What Ref-Pro Image Referencing Made Easy does

Mr. Steve 3D's RefPro avoids flipping between Blender and an image viewer by giving references their own workspace inside the 3D view, designed for 2D and 3D reference modeling.

Bring in whole folders, several chosen files or a single picture, and lay them out vertically, horizontally or in a grid with adjustable padding. One toggle shows or hides the reference space, and flip and align commands mirror images and match your modeling orientation. Suits modelers who work from numerous photos.

Key features

  • Custom reference workspace
  • Import folders, selections or single images
  • Vertical, horizontal and grid layouts
  • Single toggle for the reference space
  • Flip and align tools
