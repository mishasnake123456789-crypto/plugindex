Blender add-on that builds cinematic camera moves by joining hand-placed shot cameras with a smooth generated path.

What Cinespline does

mazen lawand's CineSpline plans camera animation as a series of shots. Place the shot cameras where you want them, and the add-on generates a smooth path through them.

An Offset setting governs timing, a single click makes the camera look at any object, and the spline can be edited or rebuilt whenever you like. Release 1.4 added a Smart Target System, in which a selected object becomes the Look At target, or a default target is used. Directors of fly-throughs and product reveals can draft moves quickly.

Key features

  • Manual shot camera placement
  • Auto-generated smooth camera path
  • Offset-based timing
  • One-click focus on any object
  • Smart Target System (v1.4)
