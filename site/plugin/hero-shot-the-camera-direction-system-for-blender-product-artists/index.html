What Hero Shot: The Camera Direction System For Blender Product Artists does

Hero Shot gives product artists ready-made camera moves in Blender.

Ten tuned rigs cover slow reveals, turntable orbits, crash zooms and bolt cameras, each deployable in one click and customizable in range, easing and timing in real time. A motion style section picks an easing or custom curve, and the Director's desk sequencer stacks shots, sets lengths and switches between them. It suits product artists who need polished camera work quickly and consistently.

Key features

  • Ten pre-built camera rigs
  • Customize range, easing and timing live
  • Motion style and custom curves
  • Director's desk shot sequencer
  • No manual keyframes
