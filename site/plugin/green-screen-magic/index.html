What Green Screen Magic does

Digital DiLeo's Green Screen Magic takes backgrounds out of images, videos and image sequences inside Blender. Any color can be keyed out, not only green, and the result updates live as you change settings.

Low and high thresholds plus softness shape the key. Image sequence mode recognizes and processes sequences automatically, and video support covers popular formats. VFX artists and editors needing quick keys without a separate compositing program can use it.

Key features

  • Key out any color
  • Live preview of keyed content
  • Low and high threshold with softness
  • Automatic image sequence recognition
  • Video format support
