What Zeeks Auto UV Unwrap does

Zeeks Auto UV Unwrap, by Ezekiel-AA, unwraps UVs automatically, reduces stretching along seams and includes a seam picker for choosing where cuts go. It is listed under UV, Modeling and Pipeline.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
