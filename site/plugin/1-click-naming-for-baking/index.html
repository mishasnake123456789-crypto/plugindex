What 1 Click Naming For Baking does

This Blender add-on removes the tedious renaming step that comes before baking. It looks across the scene, labels low poly and high poly models according to baking conventions, and files them into matching collections.

With one button the scene is ready for Marmoset Toolbag or Substance. The low and high poly sets go out as two separate FBX files, and shortcut buttons launch Marmoset straight from Blender. Texture artists who bake normal maps from many assets will save the most time, and the developer includes documentation.

Key features

  • One-button naming of low and high poly models
  • Auto-sorts objects into collections
  • Writes separate FBX files for low and high poly
  • Buttons that open Marmoset from Blender
  • Documentation included
