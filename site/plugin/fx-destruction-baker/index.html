What Fx Destruction Baker does

FX Destruction Baker guides the creation of destruction-ready meshes and prepares them for Unity, Unreal Engine, trailers and cinematics. It walks through fracturing, gravity and force setup, glue constraints and rigid body baking.

Fracture generation includes break frame control for timed destruction, and forces cover explosion, pull, directional and vortex styles. VFX artists can produce collapses and explosions more predictably. In practice that means fracture generation with break frame control and explosion, pull, directional and vortex forces.

Key features

  • Fracture generation with break frame control
  • Explosion, pull, directional and vortex forces
  • Gravity and glue constraints
  • Rigid body baking
