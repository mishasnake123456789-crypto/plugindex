What Iris: Addon does

Iris is a Blender add-on that turns 3D projects into presentations. You build cinematic slides and dynamic lighting setups from a single panel, for showing models, teaching or pitching to clients.

It can showcase 3D models, animations and visual designs, works with the Cycles and Eevee render engines, and includes documentation. Designers who want to present inside Blender can skip exporting to other software. In practice that means cinematic slides for 3D presentations and dynamic lighting setups.

Key features

  • Cinematic slides for 3D presentations
  • Dynamic lighting setups
  • Single panel workflow
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
