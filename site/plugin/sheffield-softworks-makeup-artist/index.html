What Sheffield Softworks Makeup Artist does

This beauty-retouching kit from Sheffield Softworks for After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and it also works in Final Cut Pro X and Motion. It centres on Smooth Skin and Cover Up.

Smooth Skin removes blemishes and other features by replacing them with surrounding skin using an adjustable diffusion algorithm, limited by a chroma key to chosen skin tones and by a mask to a specific area. Smooth Skin Express builds a mask from standard skin tones. Cover Up applies virtual foundation to reduce shine, shadow or redness and can alter eye, lip or clothing colour. Version 3.5 rewrote both plug-ins, using AI assistance for skin tone processing.

Key features

  • Smooth Skin blemish removal with diffusion
  • Chroma key limits processing to skin tones
  • Smooth Skin Express with cutout mask
  • Cover Up virtual foundation
  • Version 3.5 rewrite with AI-assisted skin processing
