What Easy Comp Fx does

Easy Comp Fx adds post-processing through the compositor with a click. More than 20 stylized presets include Glitch, Halftone, Toon, Paper, Outline and Glare, and over 40 PNG-based LUTs offer color looks with a blend slider.

A thumbnail gallery shows previews, effects and LUTs are added or removed in one click, and the viewport preview works with Eevee Next and Cycles. It spares you from building compositor node trees by hand.
