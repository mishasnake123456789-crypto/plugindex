Blender asset library of baked and unbaked particle, cloth and rigid body simulations.

What Quick Realism does

Quick Realism is a library of ready-to-use simulations for Blender, so effects can be dropped into a scene instead of set up from scratch. No add-on installation is needed, since it works as an asset library.

Baked versions are quick drop-ins and unbaked ones allow precise customization. The contents include 8 particle simulations such as falling leaves, ground leaves, dust and moths, 4 paper and bag rigid body sims with weak and strong wind, and 11 cloth sims including banners, curtains, flags and plastic wrap.

Key features

  • Baked and unbaked versions
  • 8 particle sims including leaves and dust
  • 4 paper and bag rigid body sims
  • 11 cloth sims such as flags and curtains
