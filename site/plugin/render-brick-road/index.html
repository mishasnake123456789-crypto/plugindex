Shows an interactive history timeline in the Blender 3D viewport so you can see each edit and jump back to any step.

What Render Brick Road does

Render Brick Road tracks your modeling history visually. A strip along the viewport's lower edge shows every operation as a colored node: diamonds for mesh operations and circles for transforms and modifiers.

Each category has its own color, and you can jump to any step. It helps you retrace a modeling path and understand how a mesh got to its current state. It is meant for understanding and revisiting your modeling steps, not just stepping backwards through undo.

Key features

  • Viewport timeline of every edit
  • Diamonds for mesh ops, circles for transforms
  • Color per operation category
  • Jump to any step
