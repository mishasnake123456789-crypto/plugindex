What KLIPY: GIFs, Clips and Stickers does

KLIPY brings a huge library of GIFs, clips, stickers and memes into After Effects. Instead of downloading files from a browser and importing them, you search inside a native panel and click an asset to add it.

Inserted items are optimized automatically and placed in the active composition. Search helps find a specific reaction or emotion, and the panel is lightweight. It is useful for motion designers and editors making personal projects such as dynamic motion graphics or fan edits who want quick access to reaction material.

Key features

  • 10 million+ GIFs, clips and stickers
  • Click to insert into the active comp
  • Automatic optimization of assets
  • Search by reaction or emotion
  • Lightweight native panel
