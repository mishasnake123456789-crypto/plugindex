Blender add-on that reflects the active image along its X or Y axis by copying one half onto the other.

What Mirror Image does

Mirror Image, by R4V3N, applies symmetry to images in Blender. It flips one half of the picture and stamps it over the opposite side along the X or Y axis.

This fits artists painting directly on textures with symmetrical UVs. It requires Blender 4.2 LTS or newer, needs no special permissions, is released under GPL v3.0 or later and installs by drag and drop or from disk.

Key features

  • Mirror along X or Y axis
  • Copies one half over the other
  • Suited to symmetrical UV painting
  • Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
  • GPL v3.0 or later
