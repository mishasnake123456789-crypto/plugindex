What Better Randomizer Panikradius does

Better Randomizer is a Blender plugin built on Randomize Transform. It keeps all parameters in the N-panel, so adjustments are easy to reach.

A snapping option, quick creation of duplicates and single-click keyframes on location, rotation and scale expand what the standard tool offers. Good for scattering props and generating variation. The combination of controls makes it quicker to produce natural-looking variation in scattered props, debris and repeated architectural elements.
