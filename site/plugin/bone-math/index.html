What Bone Math does

Bone Math, by Xury46, provides operators and math functions for rigging in Blender. Its first release solves a tedious task: working out the pole angle for an IK bone chain.

The operator is reached by right-clicking the pole angle field of the IK constraint. The add-on is only about 4.7 KB, needs no special permissions, runs on Blender 4.5 LTS and newer, and is licensed under GPL v3.0 or later.

Key features

  • Automatic IK pole angle calculation
  • Right-click on the pole angle field
  • Math functions for bones and rigs
  • Compact 4.7 KB add-on
  • Blender 4.5 LTS and newer
