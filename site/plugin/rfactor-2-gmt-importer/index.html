What rFactor 2 GMT Importer does

rFactor 2 GMT Importer, by haunetal1990, reads rFactor 2 GMT files and brings them into Blender as meshes, handy for modders working on that sim. It is listed under Import-Export.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
