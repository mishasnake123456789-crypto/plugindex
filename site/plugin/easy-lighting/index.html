What Easy Lighting does

Easy Lighting simplifies how lights are placed and managed in Blender scenes. HDRI integration includes intensity and rotation controls, and a Dynamic Sun Sync keeps the sun aligned with the HDRI.

Lights can be dropped above objects or aligned to the viewport, and ground projection comes with customizable shadow-catching materials. Pre-configured interior, exterior and studio style setups give a quick starting point, along with studio light controls. Product and architectural renderers who want fast, repeatable lighting will find it useful.

Key features

  • HDRI intensity and rotation controls
  • Dynamic Sun Sync
  • Quick light placement over objects
  • Ground projection with shadow catcher
  • Interior, exterior and studio presets
