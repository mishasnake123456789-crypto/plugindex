Splits the Blender camera view into tiles, renders them separately and reassembles a very high-resolution image.

What Camsplitter 2 does

CamSplitter 2 lets ordinary hardware produce huge renders by dividing the camera view into tiles. Each tile renders on its own and is then stitched into one seamless image.

Resolutions such as 8K and 16K or higher become practical on standard machines. Tile and high-res heatmaps visualize triangle density and resource usage, and scene analysis tools help find the objects that drain memory. Archviz and print artists rendering very large images avoid running out of memory.

Key features

  • Tile-based render and automatic reassembly
  • 8K, 16K and higher on standard hardware
  • Tile and High-Res heatmaps
  • Scene analysis tools
