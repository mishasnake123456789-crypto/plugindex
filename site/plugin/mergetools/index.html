Mergetools

Developer: Paul Thien Store

FreeBlender

Geometry Nodes boolean toolkit for Blender that merges, cuts and fillets meshes non-destructively and adds procedural welds.

What Mergetools does

Mergetools combines selected meshes into a single live result while keeping the originals intact and hidden from render. A difference mode subtracts cutters from the base, and you can swap which object plays which role at any time.

Junction controls in the N-panel handle pre-smooth, fillet radius, tension, bulge and subdivision. Procedural weld beads follow the true intersection edges with size, detail and noise settings. Materials can be cross-faded across the junction, with a seam attribute for dirt or wear effects. Presets can be saved, and the final result bakes to real geometry with UVs and material slots.

Key features

  • Non-destructive merge of two or more meshes
  • Difference mode with swappable base and cutter
  • Junction and fillet controls in the N-panel
  • Procedural weld beads along intersection edges
  • Material blend across the junction and bake to geometry
