What Transmitter does

Transmitter, from Leyero, bridges the Motion controls and the Transform effect. It sends keyframes from Motion to Transform, resets Motion in one click, and lines up anchor point and position.

Interpolation can be added to the animation, shutter angle can be set for motion blur, and specific Transform effects are targeted by renaming them to tr. Right-clicking an element shows a description of what it does. Editors who prefer the Transform effect to Motion, for its shutter and interpolation options, will find it a handy bridge.

Key features

  • Sends Motion keyframes to Transform
  • One-click Motion reset
  • Adds interpolation
  • Aligns anchor point and position
  • Sets shutter angle for motion blur
