What GrabDrop does

GrabDrop lets editors fetch media from the web without leaving After Effects or Premiere Pro. You paste a source, choose what to download, and the file lands in your project workflow.

It covers video, audio and images, allows a custom download location, and can take just a chosen range of a clip rather than the whole thing. Batch downloads speed up bigger jobs, custom flags and parameters give advanced control, cookies are supported for sources that need them, and a history keeps track of earlier downloads. It was made by Leyero.

Key features

  • Downloads video, audio and images
  • Choose the download folder
  • Grab only a selected clip range
  • Batch downloading
  • Custom flags, cookies and history
