What Realistic Sky System For Blender 2.8 And Newer (Sale -50% Off) does

Natural Sky is a photorealistic sky setup for Blender 2.8 and 2.9, built from shaders instead of scripts. It is made for the Cycles render engine and does not support Eevee.

A full, animatable 24-hour cycle is keyed through the time parameter, with customizable sun and moon position and size. Volumetric clouds can move, and stars and fog are adjustable. It is designed for large exterior environments up to 64 square kilometers. Several setup options are offered, including importing models into the blend file. Landscape and exterior artists will get changing daylight without a script.
