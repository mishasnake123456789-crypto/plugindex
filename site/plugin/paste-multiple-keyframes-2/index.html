Copies and pastes keyframes from several After Effects layers at once, with loop support.

What Paste Multiple Keyframes 2 does

Paste Multiple Keyframes 2 removes the limitation of moving keyframes one layer at a time. It copies animation from several layers together and pastes it onto matching targets, and pasting is faster than in the previous version.

A loop checkbox repeats each property based on its own time range, and there are options for copying keyframe colors, moving the time slider to the end of the pasted keys and selecting them afterward. Unchecking interpolation pastes values without their easing. Animators who reuse motion often benefit most.

Key features

  • Paste keyframes from multiple layers together
  • Loop each property by its own time range
  • Copy and paste keyframe colors
  • Auto-select pasted keyframes
  • Optional interpolation pasting
