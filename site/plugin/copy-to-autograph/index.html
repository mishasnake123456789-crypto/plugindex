Copies selected Blender objects to the clipboard as Autograph JSON so you can paste them straight into Maxon Autograph.

What Copy to Autograph does

Copy to Autograph is a small bridge between Blender and Maxon Autograph. It takes whatever you have selected in your Blender scene and places it on the clipboard in the JSON structure that Autograph understands, so no export files or manual conversion are needed.

Run the operator in Blender, switch to Autograph and press Ctrl+V. Scene layout, lights, animated objects and cameras all travel across, with cameras keeping their position, rotation, focal length, sensor size and shift, and depth of field settings. It suits artists who block out 3D in Blender but finish in Autograph.

Key features

  • Copies selection to the clipboard, no intermediate files
  • Transfers layouts, lights and animated objects
  • Cameras keep focal length, sensor and depth of field
  • Paste into Autograph with a single Ctrl+V
