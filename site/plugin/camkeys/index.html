Drives shapekeys and object properties from the camera angle, with forced-perspective results bakeable to keyframes.

What Camkeys does

CamKeys links shape keys and properties to where the camera is looking, so models can change shape depending on the view. Record values from as many camera positions as you like, then let the addon blend between them.

Forced Perspective lets you assign a custom field of view per object and use falloff curves to move parts nearer or further away, producing cheat-based illusions. The final results can be baked into keyframes. This helps motion designers and stylized animators craft view-dependent effects.

Key features

  • Capture CamKeys or CamProps at many camera angles
  • Drives shapekeys and object properties
  • Forced Perspective with custom FOV per object
  • Falloff curves
  • Bake results to keyframes
