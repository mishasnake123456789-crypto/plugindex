Blender add-on that reverts themes from version 5.0 and later back to 4.5 LTS and older styles.

What Theme Reverter does

Theme Reverter, by byebyeLAN, takes the interface themes introduced in Blender 5.0 or later and reverts them to how they looked in 4.5 LTS and earlier versions. It is listed under System.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Reverts 5.0+ themes to 4.5 LTS
  • Works with older versions
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later
  • Requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer
