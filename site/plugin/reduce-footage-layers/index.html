What Reduce Footage Layers does

Reduce Footage Layers is a script by jfcg that tidies After Effects timelines by dealing with inactive footage layers. It works non-destructively, so projects can be slimmed without permanent loss.

It displays a list of unused footage that has been replaced with solids and another showing what footage has been removed, so you can check the changes. It supports After Effects 2025 back to CC 2017, and a demo video is available.

Key features

  • Non-destructive cleaning of inactive footage layers
  • List of footage replaced with solids
  • List of footage removed
  • Works from CC 2017 through 2025
  • Demo video available
