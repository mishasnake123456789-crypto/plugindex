Pixel art effects for Blender via the Shader Editor with optional baking, or globally in the Compositor.

What Pixelator Maker does

Pixelator Maker creates pixel-art looks in two ways: a shader-based effect in the Shader Editor, or a global effect in the Compositor. Pixel size, color steps and saturation are adjustable.

Texture baking stores the effect into optimized images, node indexing controls several objects or styles by index value, and a viewport toggle switches the composited result on and off. It suits retro-styled renders and games. Notable points: shader or Compositor workflow, texture baking and index-based multi-object control.

Key features

  • Shader or Compositor workflow
  • Texture baking
  • Index-based multi-object control
  • Pixel size, color steps and saturation
