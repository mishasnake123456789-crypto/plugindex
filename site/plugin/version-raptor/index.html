Automatically bumps version numbers in project, comp and sequence names in After Effects and Premiere Pro.

What Version Raptor does

Version Raptor takes the tedium out of saving new versions of your work. It reads the numbering in a project, composition or sequence name and raises it, handling styles such as project-v01 to project-v02, comp_0001 to comp_0002, or a name with initials after the number.

Deep Versioning goes further by duplicating every child comp in After Effects, or nested sequences in Premiere Pro, and repointing parent comps and sequences to the new copies. A standalone .jsx script with icons lets it run from KBar and similar launchers. It is compatible with the 2025 and 2026 releases of both applications.

Key features

  • Raises the number in project, comp and sequence names
  • Deep Versioning duplicates child comps or sequences
  • Relinks parent comps and sequences automatically
  • Standalone .jsx script for KBar and similar tools
  • Works with 2025 and 2026 releases
