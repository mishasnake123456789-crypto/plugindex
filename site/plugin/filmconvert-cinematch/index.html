What FilmConvert CineMatch does

FilmConvert CineMatch is aimed at editors who shoot with several cameras and want the footage to look consistent. Using sensor matching, it recognizes more than seventy camera makes and models and gives each a REC.709 conversion at the press of one button.

Dedicated camera packs let it translate between log styles such as S-Log2/3, C-Log, BRAW, V-Log and RED IPP2. RAW-like controls follow each sensor's behavior, and built-in false color guides assist with setting exposure. It works inside DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. Multi-camera shoots, corporate projects and run-and-gun productions get matched footage with little manual grading.

Key features

  • Sensor matching for 70+ camera models
  • One-click Rec.709 transforms
  • Conversion between common log formats
  • RAW-like controls per sensor
  • False color guides for exposure
