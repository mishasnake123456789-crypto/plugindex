What AEJuice Basic Premiere Pro does

AEJuice Basic Premiere Pro is a beginner course delivered on Teachable and taught by Chris Brooker. It runs roughly two hours and follows the editing process from shooting and importing to rendering.

Lessons cover masks, animated text, Essential Graphics and Mogrts, time-lapse, slow motion, green screen and multi-camera editing, dynamic linking, sound design, color correction and grading, and export settings. Progress is tracked and lessons play on any device. It is aimed at beginners, and little or no prior experience is needed to follow along.

Key features

  • Shooting, importing and basic editing
  • Masks, text animation and Mogrts
  • Time-lapse, green screen and multicam
  • Sound design, color correction and grading
  • Export settings guidance
