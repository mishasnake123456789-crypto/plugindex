What Separation No Anxiety does

Separation No Anxiety condenses a fiddly multi-step routine into one action. Pressing the button splits every loose piece of a mesh into its own object and then resets each piece's origin so it can be edited independently.

It supports Blender 3.0 through 4.0, is released under the GPL license and ships with documentation. Modelers who regularly break apart merged meshes, imported models or multi-part objects will notice the speed-up. Because the origins are reset at the same time, each separated part can be rotated or scaled around its own center straight away.

Key features

  • Separates all loose meshes at once
  • Resets origin on every piece
  • Blender 3.0 through 4.0
  • GPL license with documentation
