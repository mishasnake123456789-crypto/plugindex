Packs the main sculpting tools into one eight-slot radial pie menu in Blender, with brush radius and strength controls.

What Sculptpie does

SculptPie condenses the tools you reach for most into a single pie menu. Eight slots offer essential sculpting tools, so you spend less time hunting through toolbars and panels.

Brush radius, strength and direction are adjustable live, and the menu itself toggles between Object and Sculpt mode. A preferences panel lets you map shortcuts and tune the behavior. It suits sculptors who like to keep their hands near the tablet and the screen uncluttered.

Key features

  • 8-slot radial pie menu
  • Real-time brush radius, strength and direction
  • Switch between Object and Sculpt mode
  • Preferences panel for shortcuts
  • Keeps sculpting UI uncluttered
