What EmojiKit does

EmojiKit is a collection of animated emoji for After Effects from Breton Brander. It saves motion designers from animating common expressions themselves, and the assets are friendly to Lottie export for web and app use.

The emoji appear as thumbnails in a panel, so you browse visually and drop what you need into a composition. It supports After Effects versions 2020 through 2025, and a demo video shows how it works. Social video creators, app designers and explainer makers can use it to add personality quickly.

Key features

  • Animated emoji library
  • Lottie-friendly assets
  • Thumbnail browsing panel
  • Supports After Effects 2020 to 2025
