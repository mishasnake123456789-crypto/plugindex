What Moglyph FX 4 does

Moglyph FX 4 from Fremox clones and animates glyph shapes and text procedurally in After Effects, following the approach of Cinema 4D's Mograph. It works through ordinary text layers instead of a plugin, which keeps the timeline light and clean and allows as many cloner setups per comp as you like.

Cloners and effectors remain regular text layers, adjustable through text animator properties and expressions, and tools such as Animation Composer, Text Evo and Type Jazz can be layered on top. Version 4.5 rewrote the Radial Cloner, and the set includes a Rings Cloner, Hash mode, TextStyle Effector, Aim constraint and PaintBrush. Setups save as presets.

Key features

  • Cloners and effectors built from normal text layers
  • Unlimited cloner setups per comp
  • Radial Cloner rewritten in v4.5, plus Rings Cloner
  • Hash random characters, TextStyle Effector and Aim
  • Savable presets
