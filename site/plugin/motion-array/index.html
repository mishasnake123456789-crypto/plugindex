What Motion Array does

Motion Array is an extension that connects Adobe apps to the Motion Array marketplace. Editors can look for templates, stock video, photos and royalty-free music without switching to a browser and manually importing files.

After signing in to a Motion Array account inside the panel, you can search and browse assets that suit your software, then download and import them with a single click. Both video and photo assets are covered, and the extension works in After Effects and Premiere Pro, with Photoshop also mentioned as a supported host. It suits anyone who regularly pulls stock material into projects.

Key features

  • Search and browse the library from inside the app
  • One-click download and import
  • Templates, stock video, photos and music
  • Sign in to your account within the panel
  • Shows assets compatible with your software
