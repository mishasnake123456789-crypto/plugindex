What Light Juggler does

Light Juggler gathers all lights in a Blender scene into one collapsible panel so you do not have to hunt through the Outliner to change them.

Solo mode isolates a light, or several, to show its exact contribution. Large icons toggle viewport and render visibility separately, and power and color controls sit under each entry. An instant three-point setup generates key, fill and rim lights. It speeds up lighting iteration for look development.

Key features

  • One-click solo of one or more lights
  • Single collapsible panel for all lights
  • Separate viewport and render toggles
  • Power and color controls per light
  • Instant 3-point setup
