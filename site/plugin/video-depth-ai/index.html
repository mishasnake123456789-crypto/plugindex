Blender add-on that builds animated 3D geometry out of video using AI depth maps, with a bonus text-to-image generator.

What Video Depth Ai does

Video Depth AI produces animated 3D geometry from video footage in Blender. One-click processing creates full 3D displacement guided by depth maps estimated from the clips.

Processing can be local, requiring an NVIDIA GPU, or via an external service, depending on setup. A bonus text-to-image generator is included. Artists wanting to give footage dimensionality for parallax shots or experiments can start quickly. The depth-based approach works best for stylized or experimental results rather than precise reconstruction of real scenes.

Key features

  • One-click animated geometry from video
  • Depth-map driven displacement
  • Local or external processing
  • Bonus text-to-image generator
