What BOOM Library Seasons of Earth - European Autumn does

BOOM Library Seasons of Earth: European Autumn captures forest and countryside ambiences recorded as pure field audio, with no Foley or synthetic building. A Schoeps ORTF-3D surround rig gives the recordings height and depth, and a stereo version is included in the 3D Surround set.

Seasonal wildlife appears throughout, such as woodpecker knocks, raven calls and migrating cranes. There are 124 unique sounds, following BOOM metadata standards, in 24-bit, 96 kHz WAV for Windows and Mac. It suits sound designers building immersive 360-degree or stereo environments for TV drama, documentaries and games.
