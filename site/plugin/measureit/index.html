Blender add-on from antoniov for adding measurements and dimensions to objects in the 3D View.

What MeasureIt does

MeasureIt gives Blender tools for measuring objects and displaying those measurements in the viewport. Its controls are grouped in the sidebar of the 3D View under the View tab.

Version 1.8.4 fixed problems caused by API changes in Blender 5.0, and the add-on requires Blender 5.0 or newer. It needs no special permissions and is released under GPL v3 or later. Architects, product designers and 3D printing users who must verify or annotate dimensions will find it helpful.

Key features

  • Measurement tools in the 3D View
  • Sidebar View tab panel
  • Version 1.8.4 fixes Blender 5.0 issues
  • Requires Blender 5.0 or newer
