What BAO Mask Avenger 2 does

BAO Mask Avenger 2 exposes mask points as animatable controls inside After Effects. Each vertex, and optionally its tangents, can be keyframed, eased, driven by expressions or placed in 3D space.

It relies on the built-in masking engine, so it runs quickly without needing a separate Mask Renderer. Included scripts link points to nulls or tracker points through pick-whip, Mask Reader returns position and orientation along a path, and Shape Avenger brings the technique to shape layers. A Freeze mode lets you share projects with people who lack the plugin.

Key features

  • Per-vertex and optional tangent control
  • Keyframes, easing, expressions and 3D space
  • Scripts linking points to nulls and tracker points
  • Mask Reader for position and orientation data
  • Shape Avenger for shape layers
