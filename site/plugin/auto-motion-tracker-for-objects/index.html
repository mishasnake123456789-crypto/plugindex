What Auto Motion Tracker For Objects does

Auto Motion Tracker For Objects lets text, images or other items follow movement in a shot without visiting After Effects. It takes the keyframes of a tracked Mask Path and turns them into position, scale and rotation keyframes in a Transform effect.

Version 1.2.0 added footage stabilization using the tracked mask. It supports Premiere Pro 2020 through 2025, giving editors a simple tracking route for callouts and labels. Because tracking stays in the editor, quick callouts, labels and blurs follow moving subjects without leaving the timeline.

Key features

  • Mask Path keyframes to Transform keyframes
  • Makes text or images follow motion
  • Works inside Premiere Pro
  • Stabilization from version 1.2.0
  • Supports Premiere Pro 2020 to 2025
