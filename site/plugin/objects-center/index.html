What Objects Center does

Objects Center is a Blender add-on that handles a repetitive task for people who prepare 3D models for sale. It works out the common bounding box of several selected objects and then repositions the whole group at the center or top of the world.

It copes with models built from multiple sub-objects, keeping their relationships intact. The add-on is packaged as a Blender Extension for versions 4.2 through 5.0, notes support for Blender-Internal, Cycles and Eevee, and ships with documentation under the GPL license. Anyone who needs consistent placement before rendering or exporting assets will find it saves time.

Key features

  • Computes the common bounding box of a selection
  • Moves the group to world center or top
  • Handles models with many sub-objects
  • Blender Extension for 4.2 through 5.0
  • Documentation included, GPL license
