What Just Panels does

Just Panels is an addon and asset pack from Gabe for building panel surfaces quickly in Blender, with hard surface modeling in mind. It pairs a purpose-made addon with a collection of more than 300 assets.

Contents range from template and detailed panels to decals, slice panels and screw heads, and there are versions prepared for KIT OPS 2 (PRO and FREE), MESHmachine and DECALmachine. The bundled addon lets you make panels of your own without needing those other tools, using the default Alt+K hotkey. A recent update added 3D printable panels, and documentation and a Discord server are available.

Key features

  • 300+ panel, decal and screw head assets
  • Assets for KIT OPS 2, MESHmachine and DECALmachine
  • Standalone addon for creating custom panels
  • Alt+K default hotkey, customizable
  • 3D printable panels included
