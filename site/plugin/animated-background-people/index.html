What Animated Background People does

Animated Background People fills Blender scenes with moving people, aimed at archviz, film and game artists. Characters are browsed in an N-panel thumbnail grid with search and filters for gender, animation type, theme and resolution.

You can import at the 3D cursor, click to place characters, or draw a curve and fill it. Formations include School Class, Queue, Stadium Crowd, Concert Crowd and Nightclub, and Look At and Auto-Walk add behavior. The library has 80 characters with 8 animations each in 7k and 800-triangle versions, with texture sharing for faster renders.

Key features

  • Thumbnail browser with search and filters
  • Fill Curve and several crowd formations
  • Look At and Auto-Walk behaviors
  • 80 characters in two resolutions
