Replaces materials across Blender meshes in Active Material or Sync Material mode.

What Mat Switcher does

Mat Switcher updates materials on many meshes at once. Active Material Mode replaces the material on all meshes that share the selected mesh's material, across all objects or only selected ones.

Sync Material Mode improves linked materials by updating every mesh with a linked material whenever a new one is applied. It supports Blender 3.0 through 4.4 under a GPL license. Useful for swapping looks in scenes with many repeated materials.

Key features

  • Active Material Mode
  • Sync Material Mode for linked materials
  • All or selected objects
  • Blender 3.0 to 4.4
