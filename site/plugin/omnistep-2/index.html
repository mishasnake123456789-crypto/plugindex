Modular first-person controller add-on for Blender with physics-based movement, capsule collision and optional scripting.

What Omnistep 2 does

Omnistep 2 lets you walk through Blender scenes in first person, with physics-based movement and capsule collision detection. Optional scripting supports interactive animation.

Movement offers control over gravity, speed, acceleration, friction, air control and damping for stairs and uneven terrain, and a fly mode adds fully physics-based mouselook movement. Useful for prototyping, walkthroughs and scene exploration. The movement settings can be tuned to feel heavy, floaty or precise, making it a flexible sandbox for testing level designs and interactive ideas right inside Blender.

Key features

  • First-person physics movement
  • Gravity, friction and air-control settings
  • Fly mode with mouselook
  • Capsule collision detection
  • Optional scripting
