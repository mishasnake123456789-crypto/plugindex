What Smart Key Reverse 2 does

Smart Key Reverse 2 is a keyframe tool from Creative Motion for flipping animation backwards. Rather than manually moving each key, you click once and the timing of a complex animation is reversed.

Several reverse types are available, including reverse at a time, reverse within the work area, and reverse from in point to out point. It can act on all properties or just the selected ones, and a keyframe counter is built in. The design is compact with flexible settings, and Kbar2 and keyboard shortcuts are supported.

Key features

  • One-click keyframe reversal
  • Reverse at Time, Work Area and In-to-Out modes
  • All or selected properties
  • Built-in keyframe counter
  • Kbar2 and shortcut support
