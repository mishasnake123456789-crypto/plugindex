What Light It Up does

Light It Up, from Rasheed-Shaikh, provides studio lighting setups for Blender that you can keep tuning once they are in the scene, rather than fixed rigs you have to accept as they are. It is listed under Lighting.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
