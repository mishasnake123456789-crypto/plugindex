What Advanced Ocean Modifier does

Made by Modicolitor, Advanced Ocean Modifier produces animated water surfaces in Blender from one click. Twelve ocean presets act as starting points, with adjustable resolution, size, wave scale, alignment and simulation duration, plus a pointiness setting to remove artefacts.

Four water-and-foam materials are included, where foam and water can each be tuned separately, with extra foam options. You may add oceans until your computer runs out of headroom. Float objects follow the wave motion, while static objects generate waves on the surface, which gives boats and debris believable interaction.

Key features

  • 12 ocean presets with adjustable parameters
  • Four water and foam materials
  • Multiple oceans per scene
  • Float objects that follow the waves
  • Static objects that create waves
