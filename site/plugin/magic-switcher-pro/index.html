What Magic Switcher PRO does

Magic Switcher PRO is a Script-Fighter utility for After Effects that flips whole categories of project content with one click. It is helpful when heavy effects slow previews and you want them disabled everywhere, then restored later.

Layer styles, effects and adjustment layers can all be switched across the entire project, and audio can be muted and unmuted project-wide, with only previously enabled audio restored. It is compatible with After Effects 2023 back to CC 2015.3. Version 2.5 introduced a smaller design for the PRO edition, and 2.6 moved to the latest aescripts licensing system.

Key features

  • Toggle layer styles, effects and adjustment layers project-wide
  • Project-wide audio mute and unmute
  • Restores only previously enabled audio
  • Compatible with After Effects 2023 to CC 2015.3
