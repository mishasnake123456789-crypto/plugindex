What BOOM Library Cinematic Elements: Fire and Water does

BOOM Library Cinematic Elements: Fire and Water collects thousands of recordings of the two elements, from campfires, crackling flames and flamethrowers to splashes, rivers, waves and underwater movement. It suits After Effects, Premiere Pro and any host that reads WAV files.

The Construction Kit offers clean source recordings for building your own sounds, while the Designed collection supplies ready-made effects, mostly in small, medium and large versions, and sweeteners to enhance them. A bundle edition includes both. Files are 24-bit, 96 kHz for Windows and Mac. Trailer sound designers can also use the elements on creatures and heavy impacts.

Key features

  • Thousands of fire and water effects
  • Clean Construction Kit source recordings
  • Designed sounds in small, medium and large variants
  • Sweeteners for layering
  • 24-bit, 96kHz WAV
