After Effects easing extension with multi-point curves, one-click easing copy and velocity graphs.

What AccelCurve does

AccelCurve from grape design goes beyond two-handle easing in After Effects by supporting curves with three or more points. It analyzes the easing of selected keyframes and applies it to others in one click, though some properties such as paths are not supported.

Midpoints are added with Shift-click and removed with Alt-click, and intermediate keyframes are generated to preserve the curve shape. Twenty-four presets with three- and four-point curves are included, and custom presets can be grouped, reordered, renamed and exported. Velocity and acceleration graphs, absent in After Effects, are provided, with horizontal or vertical layouts.

Key features

  • Easing curves with three or more points
  • One-click easing analysis and copy
  • 24 multi-point presets
  • Custom preset groups with import/export
  • Velocity and acceleration graphs
