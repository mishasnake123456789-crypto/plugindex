What Blendurro - Churro Generator Addon does

Blendurro is a playful Blender add-on that converts a curve object into a churro with a single button click. It is built on geometry nodes and suits artists making stylized food or dessert assets.

Parameters let you change churro thickness, the dough and sugar materials, and the number of ridges. Stylized shaders in two different genres come with it, and the full edition adds the two shader sets seen in the showcase images. Documentation covers installation.

Key features

  • Converts any curve into a churro in one click
  • Built on geometry nodes
  • Adjustable thickness, ridges and materials
  • Stylized shader sets included
