What Blender Addon | Bone_Rename does

Bone_Rename, from VFX Grace, renames many items at the same time. It works on bones, objects, materials and vertex groups, so naming conventions can be enforced across a whole rig or scene.

You can add or change prefixes and suffixes, replace or delete specific characters and apply presets, including custom ones designed for fast bone naming. Riggers preparing characters for game engines or motion capture retargeting will appreciate the shortcut.

Key features

  • Renames bones, objects, materials and vertex groups in bulk
  • Prefix and suffix editing
  • Replace or delete characters
  • Built-in and custom name presets
