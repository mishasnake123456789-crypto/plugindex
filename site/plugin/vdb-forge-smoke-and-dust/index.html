What Vdb Forge - Smoke & Dust does

VDB Forge - Smoke & Dust, from CGI Forge, is a Blender add-on and asset pack that supplies ready-made smoke, dust and steam volumes. A standalone add-on loads the VDB files into your scenes, saving you from running simulations yourself.

The pack holds 104 VDB assets and renders in Cycles and Eevee. It works with Blender 4.0 up to 5.1, including on macOS, comes with documentation and is GPL licensed. Artists can drop in atmospheric volumes for shots needing haze, explosions' aftermath or rising steam.

Key features

  • 104 VDB assets of smoke, dust and steam
  • Standalone loader add-on
  • Renders in Cycles and Eevee
  • Supports Blender 4.0 to 5.1
  • macOS compatible with documentation
