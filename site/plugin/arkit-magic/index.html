What Arkit Magic does

ARKit Magic is a Blender add-on for facial rigging. You designate a Master Mesh as the driver source, connect secondary objects with one click, and automated drivers link their blendshapes.

Animate a blend shape on the Master Mesh and every linked mesh follows, and shapes can be edited along the timeline. It is useful for characters split into several meshes such as head, eyes and teeth. In practice that means master Mesh as driver source and one-click linking of secondary meshes.

Key features

  • Master Mesh as driver source
  • One-click linking of secondary meshes
  • Automated drivers for synchronized motion
  • Edit shapes along the timeline
