What Audio Exporter does

Audio Exporter is a Blender add-on by Dinesh007 for the Video Sequence Editor. It writes audio strips either one file each or as one merged file, with your choice of codec, sample rate, bit rate and channel layout.

Codecs on offer: Vorbis, PCM, MP3, MP2, FLAC, AC3 and AAC, with high-quality and compressed output options, and channel layouts go from mono and stereo up to 5.1 and 7.1. Naming and paths are managed, with auto-numbering that prevents overwrites. It is found under the Sequencer View menu, in the Audio section.

Key features

  • Separate or combined file export
  • Vorbis, PCM, MP3, MP2, FLAC, AC3, AAC
  • Sample rate and bit rate control
  • Layouts from mono to 7.1
  • Auto-numbering prevents overwriting
