What Synctools Pro For Houdini does

SyncTools Pro connects Houdini and Blender with a copy-and-paste workflow. It helps FX artists hand off baked simulations for shading and rendering, and modelers who prototype in Houdini and finish in Blender.

The same Ctrl+Shift+C and V shortcut works in both directions. You choose whether to transfer meshes, materials, lights, cameras, armatures or baked animation, units are matched automatically, and hierarchy and naming are kept. An Octane material mode and a Houdini shelf are included, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Key features

  • Bidirectional copy and paste between apps
  • Selective transfer of scene element types
  • Automatic unit matching and hierarchy preservation
  • Optional Octane material sync
