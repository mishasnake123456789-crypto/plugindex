What Easy Collection Visibility Keyframes does

Easy Collection Visibility Keyframes saves the repeated steps involved in animating whether a collection is shown. In Blender you would normally keyframe the viewport and render toggles separately, which is fiddly across many collections.

Here one click inserts keys for both. This helps when building reveal sequences, scene swaps or layered animation where groups of objects must appear and disappear on specific frames.

Key features

  • One-click visibility keyframes
  • Covers viewport and render toggles
  • Works on whole collections
