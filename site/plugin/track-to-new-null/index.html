What Track to New Null does

Track to New Null from Vankul is a single-button panel for After Effects. It takes tracking data from the built-in Motion Tracker or Mocha AE and writes it to a freshly created null layer.

By default the null is parented to the source layer, and a Parent to layer toggle lets you turn that off to get a free null instead. Works in After Effects 2024 to 2026, on Windows and macOS. Compositors and motion designers who attach graphics to tracked footage skip the manual steps of creating nulls and copying data.

Key features

  • One-button panel
  • Writes tracking data to a fresh null
  • Reads Motion Tracker and Mocha AE
  • Optional parenting to the source layer
  • After Effects 2024 to 2026 on Windows and macOS
