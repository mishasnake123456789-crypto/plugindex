Blender add-on for projecting and adjusting UVs from the 3D viewport with gizmos and a modal handler.

What Quick Uv Pro does

Quick UV Pro manages UV mapping without leaving the 3D viewport in Blender. Gizmos and a modal handler drive the texture on selected faces so you can rotate, scale, stretch and move it in the manner of 3ds Max.

Cube, cylinder and sphere projections are included, along with real-size Cube, Plane and World gizmos, and a one-click unwrap that straightens islands. A pie menu on the B key handles projection, adds image or IES textures to Cycles lights, and Alt+M tweaks materials from the viewport. It suits architectural visualization and furniture.

Key features

  • Cube, cylinder and sphere projection
  • Real-size Cube, Plane and World gizmos
  • Modal rotate, scale, stretch and move of UVs
  • One-click unwrap that straightens islands
  • Pie menu on B and Alt+M material tweaks
