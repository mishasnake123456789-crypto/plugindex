What Renamer2 does

Renamer2 is a naming utility from Mouse2Crew for After Effects projects with lots of compositions. Renaming them manually is slow and error-prone, so the tool lets you assemble a naming scheme from blocks and apply it automatically.

Blocks are arranged in a dedicated part of the interface, and each one has its own settings for fine adjustment. This suits editors and motion designers who deliver many versions or follow strict naming conventions. The current release is 1.0.2 from February 2024, covering After Effects CC 2019 through 2024.

Key features

  • Rename compositions automatically
  • Block-based naming scheme builder
  • Per-block settings for fine control
  • Dedicated area to arrange blocks
  • Supports After Effects CC 2019 to 2024
