What Alt Tab Studio Pro does

Alt Tab Studio Pro gathers productivity tools for Blender in one add-on, including lighting presets, a Lighting Manager and a Material Manager.

More than 200 lighting presets are included, along with quick popup tools, camera recording, a camera settings panel, fast render previews, Asset Master and diagnostics for blend files. Freelancers and studios can reduce repetitive setup across projects. The suite is aimed at saving time on routine setup, such as lighting a scene, managing materials or previewing a render, across many different kinds of projects.

Key features

  • 200+ lighting presets with manager
  • Material Manager
  • Camera recording and settings panel

  • Blend file diagnostics
