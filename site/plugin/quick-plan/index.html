Blender add-on that generates architectural 2D plans with dimensions, walls, columns and annotations, exporting to SVG.

What Quick Plan does

Douripo's Quick Plan produces architectural drawings in Blender. Quick Dimension adds measurements automatically along all four sides of the base mesh.

Quick Wall creates walls and columns from edges and vertices, extrudes solid faces into 3D objects, and uses Freestyle-marked edges for inward walls and columns. Quick Annotation adds notations based on area. Drawings export to SVG across many Blender versions. Architects and designers move quickly from model to plan.

Key features

  • Automatic dimensions on all four sides
  • Walls and columns from edges and vertices
  • Area-based annotations
  • SVG export
  • Wide Blender version support
