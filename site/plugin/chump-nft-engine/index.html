What Chump NFT Engine does

Chump NFT Engine from Block Chumps handles the layered artwork behind NFT collections from within After Effects. It combines trait layers to generate individual pieces and produces the metadata that accompanies them.

Artwork is exported as PNG files, and the whole process stays inside After Effects. It supports versions 2021 through 2023, and is aimed at artists and developers launching collections. It lets those working in After Effects handle both the visuals and the data side of a collection in one tool.

Key features

  • Assembles and generates collection artwork
  • Generates metadata alongside images
  • Runs entirely in After Effects
  • PNG export
  • Supports 2021 through 2023
