What 1 Click Sync - Blender 3Dsmax Bridge does

1 Click Sync handles exports and imports between Blender and 3ds Max with a single click. It also imports V-Ray lighting setups into Blender and provides a live link.

Options cover format, modifiers, exporting selection or animation, and smoothing groups, with Alembic and OBJ supported. Documentation is available. It suits visualization artists who split work across both applications. You get one-click transfer both ways, V-Ray lighting import and live link, along with the rest of the feature set.
