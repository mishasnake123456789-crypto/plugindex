What Animates Suite does

Animates Suite gathers everyday animation helpers into one panel, built for posing, pushing keys and tidying curves in the viewport.

GPU-batched onion skinning supports custom frame ranges, opacity falloff and per-bone colors, and copes with over 100 ghost frames. The Breakdowner makes weighted in-betweens with location, rotation and scale toggles, live visual feedback and tangent correction, for both bones and objects. Key Amp strengthens or softens f-curve values, alongside retiming and motion path tools.

Key features

  • GPU onion skinning with 100+ ghost frames
  • Weighted Breakdowner for bones and objects
  • Key Amp for f-curve values
  • Retiming and motion paths
