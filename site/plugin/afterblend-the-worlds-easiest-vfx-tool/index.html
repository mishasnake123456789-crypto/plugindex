What Afterblend - The Worlds Easiest Vfx Tool does

Afterblend moves After Effects tracking data into Blender with copy and paste. Two button clicks transfer the track, and then you can import characters in formats such as FBX, XPS or MDL and animate inside your footage.

Because it copies data instead of exporting, it works with trial versions of After Effects. Compositors and animators can add 3D elements to tracked shots. In practice that means copy and paste AE track data and works with After Effects trial versions.

Key features

  • Copy and paste AE track data
  • Works with After Effects trial versions
  • Import FBX, XPS or MDL characters
  • Animate inside footage
