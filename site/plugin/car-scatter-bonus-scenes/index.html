Blender add-on for placing vehicle models in scenes from an icon library, with bonus road and parking lot environments.

What Car Scatter + Bonus Scenes does

Mamba CG's Car Scatter populates Blender scenes with vehicles. Cars are chosen from a two-column panel with preview images and Add Car buttons, then placed at the 3D cursor in one click and then repositioned with Grab.

The library is open to your own cars and icons. Bonus scenes include Cars on the Road, with a textured road, mountains, water reflections and city details, and a Parking Lot scene. Cityscape and archviz artists can fill streets fast.

Key features

  • Icon-based car selection
  • One-click placement at the 3D cursor
  • Customizable car library
  • Cars on the Road bonus scene
  • Parking Lot bonus scene
