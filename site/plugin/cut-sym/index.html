Blender add-on that cuts a mesh object into two symmetric halves along a chosen axis.

What Cut Sym does

Cut Sym, by Wiffzackius, splits a selected mesh in half at the axis you choose. It is a small, focused tool for Blender that produces two symmetric halves.

It runs on Blender 4.2 LTS and newer, is written entirely in Python, needs no special permissions, and is distributed under GPL v3.0 or later.

Key features

  • Splits a mesh into two halves
  • Choose the cutting axis
  • Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
  • Pure Python
  • GPL v3.0 or later
