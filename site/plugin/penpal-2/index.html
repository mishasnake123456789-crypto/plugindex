What Penpal 2 does

Animatable's Penpal 2 adds vector editing to After Effects, filling a gap in its native toolset. It is free to use.

It supports After Effects CC 2018, CC 2019 and the 2020 to 2026 releases, and a demo video is available. Motion designers who regularly adjust shapes and paths, but would otherwise jump to Illustrator, can edit within their composition.
