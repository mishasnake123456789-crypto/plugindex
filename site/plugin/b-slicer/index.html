Blender add-on that slices objects into flat sections with registration dowels and exports SVG for cutting.

What B.Slicer does

B.Slicer cuts any Blender object into layers for laser cutting, CNC or packed layouts. A bisect slicing engine handles dense meshes reliably, with an optional legacy Boolean slicer in Advanced settings.

Three modes control slice placement, while thickness, spacing and axis stay adjustable. A manifold check reports mesh health before slicing. Slices can be flattened or packed efficiently, an interactive 2D and 3D tool places registration dowels, and one click exports SVG with organized layers. Objects are sorted automatically into Original, Sliced and Flattened collections.

Key features

  • Bisect slicing engine with legacy Boolean option
  • Extremity, Middle Slice and Middle Spacing modes
  • Manifold check with viewport report
  • Flatten and packed flatten for fabrication
  • One-click layered SVG export
