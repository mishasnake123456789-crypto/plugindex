What World Generator - Procedural Landscapes (Blender does

World Generator brings four procedural landscapes to Blender: arid desert, snow-capped mountains, canyon and island. All are built on Geometry Nodes, so changes appear in real time.

You can adjust the terrain geometry, explore the materials made from masks for each landscape, and render the scene. It's a quick starting point for backgrounds, environments and establishing shots. With four distinct terrains available and adjustable settings, it covers many backdrop needs without sculpting land by hand or hunting for scanned assets.
