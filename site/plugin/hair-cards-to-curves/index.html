Blender add-on for converting Fibermesh to curves, sorting by length and placing hair cards on guides.

What Hair Cards To Curves does

Hair Cards To Curves collects tools that simplify attaching hair cards to guide curves in Blender. It is geared toward real-time character hair workflows.

A Fibermesh Converter changes ZBrush-exported Fibermesh into separate curve objects for use as guides. Sort Hair by Length distributes cards and guides into 2 to 10 collections and appends the length range to each name. Place Cards on Guides uses a random seed to put linked duplicate cards on matching guides, and cards can be adjusted globally or one by one.

Key features

  • Fibermesh to curve conversion
  • Sort hair into 2-10 length collections
  • Seeded card placement on guides
  • Linked duplicate cards
  • Global and individual card adjustment
