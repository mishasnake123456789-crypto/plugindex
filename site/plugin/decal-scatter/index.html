A Blender add-on that scatters randomized decals over any mesh procedurally with one click.

What Decal Scatter does

Decal Scatter places decals on meshes procedurally in Blender, handling jobs like bullet holes on an object, leaves on a street, craters on a planet or repeating designs. A single click distributes randomized decals across the surface.

It works with Eevee and Cycles and includes PBR and non-PBR decals with diffuse, roughness and normal maps. Example scenes show a leafy street, a bullet hole, money on a street and a seamless design, and extra decals and scenes include a moon crater and a poster cylinder. Environment and prop artists adding wear and detail will find it fast.

Key features

  • One-click procedural decal scattering
  • Eevee and Cycles support
  • PBR and non-PBR decals with maps
  • Example scenes included
