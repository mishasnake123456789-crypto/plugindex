Blender add-on in the PGT toolkit for building customizable crystals with cracking, fracturing and detailing operations.

What Pgt Crystal Generator does

PGT Crystal Generator, by Jonas Mangelschots, belongs to the Procedural Generator Toolkit family and creates crystal meshes inside Blender. Crystal Base Creation lays the foundation of a design, after which operations can add cracks and refine their depth and complexity.

Fracturing and detailing operations extend the shapes, and a few experimental tools are included. Fantasy, game and environment artists can produce varied gemstones and rock crystals procedurally.

Key features

  • Crystal base creation
  • Crack formation and crack detailing
  • Fracturing and detailing operations
  • Experimental tools
  • Part of the PGT ecosystem
