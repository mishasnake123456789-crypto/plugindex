What Alt Tab Easy Fog does

Alt Tab Easy Fog adds ready-made volumetric materials to Blender scenes. A one-click automatic fog setup gives instant atmosphere, and more than thirty-five presets cover different looks.

Volumetrics add depth, mood and visible light beams to renders. The add-on supports Blender 4.0 through 4.5, ships with documentation and is released under the GPL. Environment artists and lighting designers who find volume shaders fiddly can start from a preset and tweak it.

Key features

  • 35+ volumetric material presets
  • One-click automatic fog
  • Atmospheric depth and lighting effects
  • For Blender 4.0 through 4.5
