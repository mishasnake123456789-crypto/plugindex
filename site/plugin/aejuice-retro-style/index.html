What AEJuice Retro Style does

AEJuice Retro Style is a training course of about two hours led by Cameron Pierron. Learners recreate four scenes from the promo, building each one from beginning to end in After Effects to capture an 80s retro atmosphere.

The lessons also cover finding references and inspiration, plus tips for refining a professional workflow. Course materials are included, and plugins such as Deep Glow, Red Giant Universe AV Club, Zaebects Signal and Boris FX Sapphire S_LensFlare are used to enhance the look. It is aimed at intermediate users.

Key features

  • Recreate four retro scenes start to finish
  • Techniques for an 80s retro look
  • Reference gathering and inspiration tips
  • Workflow improvement advice
  • Course materials included
