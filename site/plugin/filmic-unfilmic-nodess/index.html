What Filmic Unfilmic Nodess does

Filmic Unfilmic Nodess solves a common compositing problem: footage that looks washed out after a Filmic render is placed over video. These node groups convert between Filmic and Standard color spaces inside the Blender compositor.

You can comp a Filmic 3D scene onto video without the whites turning gray, or bring an image or video into a 3D scene and filter it against Filmic. It can be used alongside the Greenscreen Within Eevee Pro add-on. It is supplied as a node group add-on with documentation.

Key features

  • Filmic to Standard conversion nodes
  • Keeps whites from graying in comps
  • Brings footage into Filmic scenes
  • Pairs with Greenscreen Within Eevee Pro
