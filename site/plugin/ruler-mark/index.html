What Ruler Mark does

Ruler Mark, by Владимир-Владимирович-Корнилов, extends Blender's native Measure tool, letting you edit rulers and stamp marks at their ends. It is listed under 3D View and Modeling.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
