What Creation Effects: Space Effects does

This After Effects template is for making your own cosmos. It bundles scenes for Earth and Moon, the Sun, a black hole, a spiral galaxy, nebulae, comets and shooting stars, star fields, wormholes and a whole solar system.

The Black Hole, Solaris and Terra templates are included, plus many additional bodies. Planets from Mercury to Neptune are ready to render, with rings on the outer three and lighting and shadows that follow the Sun's position. Fifteen lens flares attach to the Sun, a 360 degree star background is included, and every body has a slider-based control layer. There are 28 pre-animated comps and a video tutorial, and no plugins are required.

Key features

  • Earth, Moon, Sun, black hole, galaxy, nebulae and solar system
  • Planets from Mercury to Neptune with live lighting
  • 15 lens flares and a 360 degree star background
  • Slider-based control layer for each body
  • 28 pre-animated comps and tutorial
