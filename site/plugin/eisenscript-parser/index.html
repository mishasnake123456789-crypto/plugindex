What Eisenscript Parser does

Eisenscript Parser brings the EisenScript grammar from Structure Synth into Blender. Short recursive rules grow into elaborate 3D forms, all within the editor and without coding.

More than 350 formulas are included, from abstract art to architectural patterns. Results are ordinary Blender objects, so they work with shaders, Geometry Nodes or physics. You choose a formula, generate it, and tweak a single value to see variations. It is good for generative artists exploring fractals and procedural structures.

Key features

  • Parses EisenScript into Blender geometry
  • 350+ pre-packed formulas
  • Output works with shaders, Geometry Nodes and physics
  • No coding needed
  • Tweak values to explore variations
