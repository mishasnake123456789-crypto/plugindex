What Audio Notifier does

Audio Notifier gives Blender audible alerts for success, cancel and warning events, by Lauloque. Out of the box it signals when renders and baking processes finish or are canceled, so you can leave long jobs running and listen for the result.

The default sounds ship under a CC0 license and are credited to IENBA. Version 1.0.5 added a developer mode to help with bug reports, and notification registering has been improved to address bugs. The add-on suits anyone who renders overnight or works in another window.

Key features

  • Sound alerts for success, cancel and warning
  • Notifies on render and bake completion
  • Default CC0 sound files included
  • Developer mode for bug reports
  • Improved notification registering
