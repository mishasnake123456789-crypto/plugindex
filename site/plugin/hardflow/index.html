Free open-source hard-surface toolkit for Blender 4.2+ with draw-to-cut booleans, snapping, direct modeling, decals and kitbash.

What Hardflow does

Hardflow packages the main hard-surface modeling tasks into one add-on. Draw cutters shaped as a vent, arc, star, slot, n-gon, polygon, circle or box, then apply them in Knife, Face, Intersect, Join, Make, Slice or Cut modes from Object or Edit Mode.

Precision snapping, direct modeling tools, curves, decals and kitbash assets are part of the kit. It is free and open source and requires Blender 4.2 or newer, helping product and sci-fi modelers work quickly.

Key features

  • Draw-to-cut boolean shapes
  • Seven modes from Cut and Slice to Intersect and Knife
  • Precision snapping and direct modeling
  • Curve tools, decals and kitbash assets
