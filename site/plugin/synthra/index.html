Blender add-on that builds multi-view camera rigs and exports datasets for Gaussian Splatting.

What Synthra does

Synthra automates the capture step of a Gaussian Splatting pipeline in Blender. Instead of positioning cameras and rendering angles by hand, it handles the whole job.

It places a multi-view camera rig around your subject, batch-renders every angle with Cycles or Eevee and exports a training-ready dataset in one click. The output targets Nerfstudio, Postshot and similar tools, relevant for NeRF and 3D scanning work. It is a GPL Blender extension with documentation, updates and support.

Key features

  • Automatic multi-view camera rig
  • Batch rendering of every angle
  • One-click dataset export
  • Built for Nerfstudio and Postshot
