What Fresh Trim does

Fresh Trim is a layer trimming script from Freshmograph for After Effects. Rather than dragging layer edges or using keyboard shortcuts one by one, you get a panel of buttons that does the trimming for you.

Fifteen modes include Trim to CTI, Trim to Comp, Trim Comp to Selection, Trim to Work Area and Trim Work Area to Selection. The panel layout is customizable, with a chosen number of rows and dynamic icons, and Kbar support lets you call features from your own setup. On macOS, the Command modifier trims only the in-point. It works with After Effects 2023 through 2026.

Key features

  • Fifteen trim and move modes
  • Customizable rows and dynamic icons
  • Kbar support
  • Command modifier trims in-point on macOS
