What Simple Audio Visualizer does

Simple Audio Visualizer makes objects dance to sound in Blender. A panel in the Graph Editor sidebar can build cubes from an audio file or drive objects of your own from the track's spectrum.

Each cube's height tracks the loudness of its frequency band along an exponential frequency scale. Options include the number of cubes, an intensity multiplier and Mirror Mode, and you can choose which transform channels respond. Motion designers and musicians make visualizers fast.

Key features

  • Generate cubes from audio
  • Animate selected objects to spectrum
  • Cube count, intensity and Mirror Mode
  • Choice of transform channels
