What MB Liquid Glass does

MB Liquid Glass produces translucent, refractive panels in the style of recent glassy interface design. It is a GPU-accelerated effect for After Effects from Mark View.

Glass refraction, blur, highlight and shadow are all adjustable. It has a built-in rounded rectangle, supports shape lists, and can take its form from a matte layer or from the layer it is applied to. That flexibility makes it useful for interface mockups, promos and motion graphics where a glass look is needed.

Key features

  • GPU-accelerated rendering
  • Adjustable refraction, blur, highlight and shadow
  • Built-in rounded rectangle
  • Shape list support
  • Matte layer or self layer shapes
