What Physical Starlight And Atmosphere - Sky does

Physical Starlight and Atmosphere is a sky system for Blender that renders atmospheric scattering in real time. It works in both Eevee and Cycles and bases its results on photometric measurements and analytic air mass calculations.

The sun can be defined through temperature in Kelvin, output in watts per square meter per steradian and a limb darkening factor. Stars and clouds can be procedural or texture based, with dynamic lighting, and a binary sun system lets you build alien worlds. Environment artists and lighting specialists get believable daylight, dusk and night skies.

Key features

  • Real-time Eevee and Cycles rendering
  • Physically defined sun from Kelvin and radiance
  • Procedural or textured stars and clouds
  • Binary sun system for alien skies
