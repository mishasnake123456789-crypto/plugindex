What Nebulosity does

Nebulosity by irrealix generates volumetric space clouds and smoke inside After Effects. Density is visualised with a GPU raymarching algorithm, then coloured through several techniques.

The base shape can be a box or sphere, or custom layers in Cut or Extrude mode. Density is built from up to three noise layers using Volume, Spiral and Inverted FBM scattering, each animated through an Evolution slider, and can be mixed with layers acting as masks and ramps. Emission colour comes from a solid colour, a layer or a gradient distributed by density, radially or by noise. Feather Ramp smooths the volume edges. Space and atmosphere artists get soft, deep forms.

Key features

  • GPU raymarched volumetric rendering
  • Box, sphere or custom layer bounding shapes
  • Up to three noise layers with three scattering types
  • Emission from colour, layer or gradient
  • Ramp masking with feathered edges
