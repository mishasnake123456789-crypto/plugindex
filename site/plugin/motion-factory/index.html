What Motion Factory does

Motion Factory is a Pixflow extension that gives motion designers and editors a store of ready-made elements right in their workspace. It offers LUTs, VFX elements, transitions, text and title graphics without switching to a browser.

The library holds upwards of 10,000 premium video templates and LUTs, a similar number of graphic templates, and 2,500-plus sound effects, with templates up to 8K. New material is added every week. It runs in After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop and Illustrator, and targets filmmakers, YouTubers, freelancers and post-production teams.

Key features

  • Built-in store for LUTs, VFX, transitions and titles
  • Premium video templates and LUTs by the thousand
  • 10,000+ graphic templates, 2,500+ sound effects
  • Templates up to 8K resolution
  • Weekly content updates
