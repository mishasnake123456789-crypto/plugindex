What rmKitUV does

rmKitUV is a Blender add-on by Timothee-Yeramian for UV editing that gathers transform, island, projection, hotspot and alignment tools. It brings modal operators, pie menus and dedicated panels to the UV Editor and 3D View.

Transform tools resize, rotate, mirror and fit UV selections with precision, and Falloff UV Transform applies gradient-based changes. Island tools scale islands relative to one another or to material sizes and normalize texel density so texture resolution is uniform. Projection in world space handles planar mapping and aligning UVs to grids.

Key features

  • Resize, rotate, mirror and fit UV selections
  • Falloff UV Transform
  • Island scaling and texel density normalization
  • World-space planar projection and grid alignment
  • Modal operators and pie menus
