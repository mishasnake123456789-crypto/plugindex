What Color Shift does

Color Shift, made by Haligonian, is a plugin for After Effects that recolors footage by mapping its hue, intensity and other values onto a gradient. The result is a completely different palette built from the colors you choose.

It works with Colorama Browser from synthetick and its more than 400 presets, and your own .ffx presets can be used as well. The plugin runs on After Effects 2023 through 2026. It suits stylized grading, duotone-like looks and psychedelic treatments for music videos and title sequences.

Key features

  • Remaps hue and intensity to a gradient
  • Creates new color palettes for video
  • Works with Colorama Browser's 400+ presets
  • Supports custom .ffx presets
