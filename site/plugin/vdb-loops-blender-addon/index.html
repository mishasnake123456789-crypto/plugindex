What Vdb Loops - Blender Addon does

VDB Loops offers quick access to a categorized gallery of loopable VDB sequences, including smoke, fire, clouds and fog, plus a loop function that stretches a sequence over the full length of your animation.

You can add as many sequences as needed and a readout shows how many frames the entire VDB sequence currently has. It saves simulation time on volumetric effects. Day to day, that covers categorized loopable VDB gallery, loop to full animation length and unlimited sequences.

Key features

  • Categorized loopable VDB gallery
  • Loop to full animation length
  • Unlimited sequences
  • Frame count readout
