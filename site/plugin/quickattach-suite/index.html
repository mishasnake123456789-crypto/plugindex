What QuickAttach Suite does

QuickAttach Suite attaches large numbers of objects into one Editable Poly in 3ds Max while guarding against running out of memory. A normal click performs a quick merge with Undo support, whereas Shift+Click starts Heavy Mode for massive object counts.

Under the hood it relies on an optimized Editable Mesh routine and turns the outcome back into a clean Editable Poly. During heavy runs it skips the Undo buffer and performs step-by-step garbage collection and texture cache clearing. Smart filtering skips groups, helpers, cameras, lights and broken CAD data. A progress bar does not freeze, a statistics report appears in Heavy Mode, and a warning shows above 15 million polygons.

Key features

  • Click for Undo-supported merge, Shift+Click for Heavy Mode
  • Optimized mesh algorithm that returns a clean Editable Poly
  • Memory protection with incremental garbage collection
  • Skips groups, helpers, cameras, lights and broken CAD data
  • Poly count safety warning above 15 million
