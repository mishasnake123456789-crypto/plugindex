What Ignitefx Flame Generator does

IgniteFX Flame Generator creates flames procedurally with Geometry Nodes, producing realistic fire that renders in Eevee and Cycles. It requires Blender 4.0 or newer.

It integrates into existing projects and comes with documentation. It suits VFX and animation shots that need fire without a full simulation. Notable points: geometry Nodes flames, realistic fire and eevee and Cycles rendering. The package also offers blender 4.0+. It is meant for artists who want to work faster inside Blender.
