What Storyliner does

Storyliner is a Blender add-on from WerwackFx that edits stories non-linearly and in real time within the 3D View. It covers storyboarding, camera management and rendering, and is aimed at artists, directors and studios. It is offered in four editions.

Shots are created quickly from scene cameras and combined into sequences that can be edited and played back non-linearly, including edit variations and live visualization. Hybrid edits blend storyboard shots with shots driven by scene cameras. Grease Pencil sketching works per shot, with continuous drawing from one shot to the next.

Key features

  • Quick shot creation from scene cameras
  • Non-linear sequence editing and playback
  • Hybrid storyboard and camera shots
  • Grease Pencil sketching per shot
  • Edit variations visualized in the 3D View
