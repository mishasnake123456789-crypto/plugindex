Blender add-on that applies detailed surface presets to objects in one click, with a custom map option.

What Surface Detail Addon does

Surface Detail Addon gives Blender objects intricate surfaces quickly. Pick one of over 40 presets, select your object and apply. It is aimed at hard surfaces, and a Custom Map tab lets you paint your own texture, which opens the door to organic shapes, mountains and ground.

A resolution value scales detail up to a maximum of 10, and a Subdivide button goes beyond that limit. A UV option helps multi-sided primitives like cubes. Results stay viewport friendly and can be exported. Documentation is included, with more features planned.

Key features

  • 40+ surface presets
  • Custom Map tab for painted textures
  • Resolution value up to 10 plus Subdivide
  • UV option for multi-sided primitives
  • Viewport friendly and exportable
