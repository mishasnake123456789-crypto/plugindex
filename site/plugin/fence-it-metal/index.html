What Fence It! - Metal does

Fence It! - Metal creates metal fencing in Blender with a procedural, parametric method, so lengths and details change without remodeling. Styles include chainlink, ornamental and wrought iron, covering needs in environment design, architecture and visualization.

The fences are modular and render in both Cycles and Eevee. The add-on works with Blender 4.0 through 5.0, is compatible as a Blender Extension, includes documentation and is distributed under the GPL. Artists who need quickly adjustable perimeters for scenes, games or archviz projects will find it saves a lot of repetitive modeling.

Key features

  • Chainlink, ornamental and wrought iron styles
  • Procedural, parametric construction
  • Modular fence sections
  • Cycles and Eevee support
  • Blender 4.0 to 5.0
