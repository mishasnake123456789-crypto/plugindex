What Keyshot Connector does

Keyshot Connector from Digizuite links Adobe applications to the KeyShot Digital Asset Management platform. Users can search and browse the library from inside After Effects and Illustrator, then insert images, graphics and other rich media without switching windows.

Filters let you move through folders and assets, and any item can be previewed alongside its metadata. A check-in and check-out process prevents accidental overwrites and keeps everyone on current files. Setup is quick. It is aimed at marketing teams, video and motion graphics specialists, and photographers who keep their media in KeyShot.

Key features

  • Search and browse KeyShot from Adobe apps
  • Insert images, graphics and rich media
  • Preview assets with metadata
  • File check-in and check-out
  • Quick installation and configuration
