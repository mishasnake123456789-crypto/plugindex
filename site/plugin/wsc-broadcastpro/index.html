What WSC BroadcastPro does

WSC BroadcastPro puts a WSC Sports library inside Premiere Pro, so sports editors can pull automated clips and highlights without switching tools. Clips appear as games unfold, and a real-time play-by-play feed updates during live events.

Assets are indexed and tagged by action, player and rating, with searching by sport, league, team and period. Compilations, recaps and top-play packages include thumbnails and markers, and each asset links to a high-resolution version. Source patching presets control audio channel routing. It is built for fast-turnaround sports content.

Key features

  • Automated clips and highlights
  • Play-by-play feeds tagged by action, player and rating
  • Search by sport, league, team and player
  • High-resolution assets ready for sequences
  • Source patching presets for audio routing
