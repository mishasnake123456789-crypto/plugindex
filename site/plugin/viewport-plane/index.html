What Viewport_Plane does

Viewport Plane adds precision aids for modeling. The work plane snaps to faces, edges or vertices and shows color-coded axis guides, with optional movement constraints.

Symmetry on X, Y or Z is measured from the plane and mirrors as you extrude, scale or move, with an adjustable blend. View alignment tools complete the set. Architects, modelers and digital artists working with exact coordinates get more control than the default tools offer.

Key features

  • Work plane aligned to faces, edges or vertices
  • Live symmetry on X, Y or Z
  • Adjustable symmetry blending
  • View alignment tools
