What Ramp Generator does

Ramp Generator by TFMSTYLE populates ColorRamp nodes automatically. Select a ColorRamp and generate colors in any of the node editors.

You can choose between 2 and 32 colors, randomize positions, use complementary or random colors, greyscale or stripes, and sample colors from an image. Artists exploring palettes get variants quickly. Instead of manually adding stops and picking colors one by one, you can produce complete gradients instantly and then refine the ones that look promising.

Key features

  • Works in four node editors
  • 2 to 32 colors
  • Complementary and random colors
  • Greyscale and stripes
  • Sample colors from an image
