Blender add-on that turns a curve, text or mesh into a neon sign or light with one Make Neon button.

What Neoner does

Neoner creates neon signs, lamps and light pointers in Blender from existing objects. A Make Neon button in the N-panel converts the active curve, text object or mesh into a glowing neon tube.

The glow color, tube thickness and tube position are adjustable, with options to include holders and choose how many and for adding a background. Motion designers, game artists and set builders can produce believable neon lettering and shapes without modeling every tube.

Key features

  • Make Neon button in the N-panel
  • Works with curves, text or meshes
  • Adjustable color, thickness and location
  • Holder count and background options
