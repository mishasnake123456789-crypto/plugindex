What Outliner Reorder does

Azerilo's Outliner Reorder puts you in charge of how items are arranged in the Outliner. Blender normally sorts them automatically, which can make a large scene hard to read.

With this tool you move objects into the order that makes sense for your project, working inside current scenes and collections. It is lightweight and simple to use, comes with documentation and supports Blender 3.6 through 5.0. Anyone organizing a busy hierarchy will appreciate the tidier structure.

Key features

  • Manual reordering of objects in the Outliner
  • Works with existing scenes and collections
  • Replaces automatic sorting with your own order
  • Lightweight and intuitive
  • Supports Blender 3.6 through 5.0
