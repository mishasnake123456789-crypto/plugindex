What AI Video Bokeh Extension does

The AI Video Bokeh Extension isolates the foreground subject and blurs everything behind it, mimicking shallow depth of field. Three styles are offered: Gaussian for classic soft focus, Motion for a sense of movement, and Smooth Camera for a dreamy feel.

After installation it shows under Window > Extensions in Premiere Pro. Wedding, vlog and short film editors can add a cinematic touch. It brings a shallow depth-of-field style to footage shot with larger depth of field or on phones.

Key features

  • AI subject detection
  • Gaussian blur for soft focus
  • Motion blur for dynamic movement
  • Smooth Camera blur for a dreamy look
  • Appears under Window > Extensions
