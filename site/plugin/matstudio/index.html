Blender add-on that renders custom material previews in the Asset browser using a chosen object, camera and world.

What Matstudio does

MatStudio generates and assigns custom preview images for materials inside Blender's Asset browser. Mark materials as assets, choose a target object, camera and world environment, then render.

Preview resolution is adjustable, and the output goes to a folder you choose, defaulting to a material_previews folder next to the .blend file. Studio Lighting renders previews with a collection of real lights, backdrops or extra geometry. It helps asset libraries look consistent and professional.

Key features

  • Custom material previews in the Asset browser
  • Choose object, camera, world and resolution
  • Configurable output folder
  • Studio Lighting with real lights and backdrops
  • Works with materials marked as assets
