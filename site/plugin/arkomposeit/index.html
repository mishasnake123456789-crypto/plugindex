Blender add-on that turns ARKit shape keys into over a hundred ready facial emotions and speech expressions.

What Arkomposeit does

Nabat's ARKomposeIt reads the shape keys on a character and, in a few clicks, maps them to ARKit conventions; pattern recognition copes even with custom rigs.

It then offers more than 100 prepared expressions: basic emotions like Joy and Anger, speech visemes, fantasy and cartoon looks. Adjusting a setting updates the face in real time, and you can edit and compose expressions of your own. Facial animators and mocap users get a quick path to lively characters.

Key features

  • Auto-maps shape keys to ARKit standards
  • 100+ built-in expressions
  • Speech visemes and emotions
  • Real-time facial feedback
  • Edit and compose new expressions
