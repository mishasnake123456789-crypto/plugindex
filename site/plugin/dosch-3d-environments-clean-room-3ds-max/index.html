What DOSCH 3D - Environments - Clean Room - 3ds Max® does

DOSCH 3D - Environments - Clean Room provides five models and scenes of clean rooms for 3ds Max. They depict pristine, sterile spaces that give the impression of a contamination-free environment.

Detail covers flooring, walls, ceilings, lighting and equipment, with an eye toward pharmaceutical, medical, semiconductor and scientific research projects. The scenes can be added to 3ds Max projects without modeling from scratch. This is version 1.0.0, an initial release, useful for industrial visualization and explainer animation.
