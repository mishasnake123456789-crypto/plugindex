What Variations does

Variations is an After Effects script from .nook for producing alternate versions of compositions. Delivering one design in several sizes or lengths is a common request, and this makes that repetitive work faster.

It can generate new variations with changed sizes, timings and more, or modify existing comps rather than duplicating them. The Name and Location sections can be switched off, name replacement options sit in a segmented control for quick access, and the side help sheet closes when you click elsewhere. It works with After Effects 2022 through 2026.

Key features

  • Generates comp variations with new sizes and timings
  • Modifies existing comps directly
  • Optional Name and Location sections
  • Segmented control for name replacement
  • Works with After Effects 2022 to 2026
