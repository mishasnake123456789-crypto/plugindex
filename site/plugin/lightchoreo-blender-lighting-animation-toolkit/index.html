What Lightchoreo - Blender Lighting Animation Toolkit does

Lightchoreo creates moving lighting effects by applying a preset directly to a light. More than ten options include flicker, fade, strobe, RGB cycle, pulse, orbit, swarm and sweep.

It works with Spot, Point, Sun and Area lights in both Eevee and Cycles. There is no need to set keyframes or write a script; apply an effect and press play. Motion designers and VFX artists making stage lighting, sirens, candle flicker or club visuals can get results quickly.

Key features

  • 10+ animation presets
  • Works with Spot, Point, Sun and Area lights
  • Eevee and Cycles compatible
  • No keyframes or scripting
