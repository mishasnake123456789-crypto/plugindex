What Knotus -The Masic Of Knots Blender Add-On does

Knotus is a Blender add-on by H3LB4 that builds strings and knots out of an asset library, with preset shapes and materials. It can build tricky knots, slip knots, icicle hitches, and pipes made from edge polygons.

The library includes over 100 knots and preset string shapes. Free Tube makes a pipeline from edges in one click, Edit Knot Mesh reshapes objects, and Mesh Copy Attribute pastes changes onto several objects. Free colouring materials come in 1K to 4K texture sizes, and Auto Displace chooses the Displace Modifier under EEVEE and another method under Cycles.

Key features

  • 100+ knots and preset string shapes
  • Free Tube builds pipes from edges
  • Edit Knot Mesh with attribute copying
  • Materials with 1K to 4K textures
  • Auto Displace switching by render engine
