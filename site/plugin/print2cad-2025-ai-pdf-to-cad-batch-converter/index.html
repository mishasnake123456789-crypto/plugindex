What Print2CAD 2025 AI - PDF to CAD Batch Converter does

Print2CAD 2025 AI is a separate converter, listed for 3ds Max, that changes 2D and 3D PDF files into fully editable DWG or DXF drawings. Vectors, lines, circles, arcs, surfaces, splines, text and pixel images are all transferred.

CADGPT AI vectorizes PDF raster images and assists with text recognition and coordinate calibration, while OCR recognizes text and intelligently detects layers, circles, arcs and line types. Extra conversions include TIFF and JPEG vectorization, HPGL to DWG, 3D scans to DWG, and PDF export to image formats. Batch conversion and five interface languages are supported.
