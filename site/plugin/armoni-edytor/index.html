What ArmonI Edytor does

ArmonI Edytor runs ArmonI Studio's AI editing engine from inside Premiere Pro. Drop in your rushes, describe the edit, and a sequence is generated in your active project, which you can refine and re-cut like any other.

A music-driven mode cuts to a track's beat for teasers and reels, and aspect ratios of 16:9 or 9:16 are offered. The engine analyzes video and audio for faces, motion, beats, dialogue and emotion. It is built on UXP, and media is encrypted in transit and at rest, then deleted automatically.

Key features

  • Upload, prompt and generate a timeline
  • Music-driven cutting to the beat
  • 16:9 and 9:16 options
  • Analyzes faces, motion, dialogue and emotion
  • Encrypted cloud processing with automatic deletion
