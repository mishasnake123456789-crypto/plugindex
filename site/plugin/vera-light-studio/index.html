Blender add-on that lights models from tap-to-apply presets, with mesh lights and volumetric fog options.

What Vera Light Studio does

Vera Light Studio sets up lighting for a model with a tap. Choosing a preset places lights automatically from the center of the viewport, in groups for rim, top and side light.

Mesh lights and a one-button volumetric fog suit sci-fi scenes. You can add and recolor lights around the object, change the world color and scene exposure, switch between studio assets, and move or rotate single lights. The subject can sit on the Vera Platform plane, or with a transparent or black backdrop.

Key features

  • Tap-to-apply lighting presets
  • Rim, top and side light groups
  • Mesh lights and one-click volumetric fog
  • Editable colored lights
  • World color and exposure controls
