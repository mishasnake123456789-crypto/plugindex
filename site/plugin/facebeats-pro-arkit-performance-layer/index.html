What Facebeats Pro - Arkit Performance Layer does

FaceBeats Pro adds an emotional performance layer to facial animation in Blender. With one click it generates twelve emotions from ARKit blendshapes and lays them over existing capture, using automatic ramp-in, hold and fade-out with clean keyframes.

The tool introduces asymmetry through A/B builds, left and right splits and staggered timing, plus micro-expression pulses and slight randomization. Eye tools cover darts, long looks, slow blinks and a blink scheduler. A driver-safe neutral bake protects the rig, and timing, intensity and speed can be set globally or per emotion. It targets iPhone capture, Faceit bakes and ARKit 52 rigs on Blender 4.5 and later.

Key features

  • One click creates 12 emotion layers plus eye keys
  • Automatic ramp, hold and fade timing
  • Asymmetry, micro-expressions and organic randomization
  • Eye darts, slow blinks and blink scheduler
  • Driver-safe neutral bake
