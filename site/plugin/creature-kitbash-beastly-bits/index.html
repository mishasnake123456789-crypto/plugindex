What Creature Kitbash - Beastly Bits does

Creature Kitbash - Beastly Bits is a Blender asset pack from Dragonboots Studios holding over 224 parts such as heads, torsos, limbs, claws and wings. The designs draw on mammals, reptiles and humanoid forms, making them suited to assembling new creatures.

Instant Asset Manager integration drops the parts straight into Blender, and mirroring and placement tools help position and align pieces. With one click, parts combine by boolean or remesh tools into a single mesh ready for sculpting. It gives concept artists and creature designers a head start.

Key features

  • 224+ creature parts
