What MoDeck.io Upfront Subscription does

MoDeck.io makes After Effects templates editable online, so colleagues or clients can change them without opening the desktop app. A drag-and-drop editor handles the edits, and mogrt properties can connect to spreadsheet data to produce many videos at once.

A free desktop app syncs through a chosen folder. You can customize the editor's colors and logo, share templates by link, embed the editor in your own site and send notification emails from your address. Higher tiers include unlimited API access. This is the upfront subscription.

Key features

  • Online drag-and-drop template editor
  • Mogrt properties linked to spreadsheet data
  • Free desktop sync app
  • Custom branding, link sharing and embedding
  • Works with CS5 through 2026 and Media Encoder
