What Keyword Marker does

Keyword Marker turns a transcript search into visible markers. Import an SRT file, type the words you care about, and the plugin places markers wherever they are spoken, handling hundreds in a single batch.

Every keyword hit can be previewed with its timecode before anything is committed. Markers may sit on the main timeline or on the clips so they travel with the media, and colors help separate sections. Sequence and SRT validation keeps placement frame-accurate, and all markers can be cleared at once. Interview editors and documentary teams can use it to navigate long spoken content.

Key features

  • Batch-add hundreds of markers from SRT keywords
  • Preview each match with its timecode
  • Markers on the timeline or on clips
  • Color-coded markers
  • Clear all markers in one step
