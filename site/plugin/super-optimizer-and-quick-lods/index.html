What Super Optimizer & Quick Lods does

Super Optimizer & Quick Lods examines Blender scenes and helps trim them down, working non-destructively whichever render engine you use.

The Scene Analyzer lists vertex counts and materials by object or collection, with Object and Collection view modes and threshold filters that flag high, medium or low poly assets. A one-click LOD generator creates two auto-decimated levels with clean naming and grouping, and can batch process collections. It also detects orphaned data.

Key features

  • Scene Analyzer for mesh stats
  • Object and Collection view modes
  • Threshold flags for poly levels
  • One-click two-level LOD generation
  • Orphaned data detection
