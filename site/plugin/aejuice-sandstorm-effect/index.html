What AEJuice Sandstorm Effect does

AEJuice Sandstorm Effect converts a still picture into sand that blows and billows away, all inside After Effects. It is built from your own images in a few clicks and needs no additional plugins, pre-renders or third-party apps.

The number of particles, turbulence and the distance particles travel are all adjustable. It supports After Effects CC 2015 through CC 2024 and newer on Windows and Mac, including M1 and M2 machines. Photo disintegration looks for intros, reveals and dramatic transitions are quick to produce.

Key features

  • Sand effect generated from a still photo
  • Control over particle count
  • Adjustable turbulence
  • Adjustable particle distance
  • No extra plugins or pre-renders needed
