What NDOF Viewport Switcher does

NDOF Viewport Switcher, by ChrisPrenn, lets you leave fixed views such as orthographic or camera view just by moving an NDOF (3D mouse) device. It is tagged under User Interface and 3D View.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features
