What Kekit does

keKit is a general-purpose toolkit for Blender by developer kjell. It gathers many scripts, tools, macros and pie menus into a single add-on aimed at speeding up and customizing everyday modeling work.

The design is modular, so individual parts can be switched on or off to suit your needs. It deliberately ships without preset shortcuts, leaving key assignment to you, and offers extensive options. Documentation, a wiki and a release log are available. It suits modelers who want a broad set of helpers rather than one narrow tool.

Key features

  • Scripts, tools, macros and pie menus in one add-on
  • Modules can be enabled or disabled
  • No preset shortcuts, you assign your own
  • Documentation, wiki and release log
