What Power Tools - Productivity Toolset does

Power Tools from aPOP adds helpers to three areas of Blender that otherwise need a lot of repeated clicking. They are aimed at multi-object and content-creation work.

The Arrange Modifiers panel lines up the modifiers of every selected object at once and can shift them up or down across the whole selection. NLA helpers pick strips under the playhead, add a track above the selected one, remove empty tracks and copy strips. Animators and editors handling many objects save steps.

Key features

  • Arrange modifiers on all selected objects
  • Move modifiers up or down across objects
  • NLA strip and track utilities
  • Video Sequence Editor enhancements
