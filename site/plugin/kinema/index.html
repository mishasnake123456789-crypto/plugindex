Blender add-on that builds animation-ready robot rigs with correct IK from URDF or MJCF files.

What Kinema does

Kinema, by ebgenius, imports robot descriptions in URDF or MJCF format and produces rigs with correct inverse kinematics, ready for animation. It is tagged under Import-Export, Rigging and Animation.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and MIT, and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Imports URDF files
  • Imports MJCF files
  • Rigs with correct IK
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later and MIT
