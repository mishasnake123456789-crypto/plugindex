What Realmotion Pro does

Realmotion Pro is a Blender add-on from Wee Learn From Mobile aimed at character animation. It combines a built-in library of over 450 animations with more than 1,700 motion capture clips, all accessible directly from the 3D viewport.

You can retarget, blend, reverse and smooth animations without opening the Graph Editor or NLA Editor, and push actions to the NLA when you want to. It works with AutoRig Pro, Rigify, Mixamo and Daz Genesis rigs, can retarget from Cascadeur rigs, and lets you apply several library animations to one or more characters, saving your own results back to the library.

Key features

  • 450+ built-in animations and 1700+ mocap clips
  • Tweak, blend, reverse and smooth from the viewport
  • Compatible with AutoRig Pro, Rigify, Mixamo and Daz characters
  • Retargets from Cascadeur rigs
  • Apply animations to multiple characters
