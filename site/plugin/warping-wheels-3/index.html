What Warping Wheels 3 does

Warping Wheels 3 from Picture Instruments is a grading plugin for After Effects and Premiere Pro based on colour wheels. Skin tones are protected while you grade, and a dedicated skin wheel edits them without secondary masking.

Separate wheels cover sky blue, plant green and film contrast. A Look Generator creates new looks at the push of a button, presets and LUT export work across hosts, and processing is the same for photo and video. Before and after comparison, reset, undo and redo are available, and Apple Silicon is supported, along with FxPlug4 for FCPX. Colourists wanting natural-looking grades get fast results.

Key features

  • Colour wheels with skin tone protection
  • Dedicated skin, sky, greenery and film contrast wheels
  • Look Generator
  • Cross-host presets and LUT export
  • Before/after comparison and Apple Silicon support
