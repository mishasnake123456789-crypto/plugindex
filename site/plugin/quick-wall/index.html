Quick Wall

Developer: Douripo

FreeBlender

A Blender add-on that creates walls and columns from a plane in one click, plus a Shear Wall generator.

What Quick Wall does

Quick Wall generates architectural walls in Blender from a plane base mesh with a single click. The standard generator builds both wall and column from input variables, giving a fast blockout for building interiors and exteriors.

A Shear Wall option creates just the wall and works at any angle, which suits non-orthogonal layouts. Documentation is included, compatibility reaches from Blender 2.79 up to 4.5, and it is distributed under the GPL. Architects and arch-viz artists planning floor layouts will find it quicker than extruding every wall by hand.

Key features

  • Wall and column from a plane in one click
  • Shear Wall generator for any angle
  • Input variables control the result
  • Blender 2.79 to 4.5
  • GPL licensed
