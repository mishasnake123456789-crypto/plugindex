What Decal - Industry Standard does

Decal - Industry Standard adds surface detail in Blender without altering the underlying material or geometry. Think dirt, damage, bullet holes and labels, layered over an existing model for extra realism.

Decals are projected onto textures with no Z-fighting or clipping, using triplanar or planar mapping. A shader-type system allows baking decals into textures, and the normal maps of the target and the decal are blended together. It works with Blender Internal, Cycles and Eevee. Documentation is included for setup.

Key features

  • Projection with no Z-fighting or clipping
  • Triplanar or planar mapping
  • Shader types for baking decals
  • Blends target and decal normal maps
  • Works with Blender Internal, Cycles and Eevee
