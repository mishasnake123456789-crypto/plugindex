Blender retopology add-on that converts Annotate strokes into quad topology and wraps it to the surface by simulation.

What Retopowrap does

RetopoWrap lets you sketch the edge flow you want with Blender's Annotate brush and turns those strokes into clean quad-based topology.

Quad generation follows the strokes and keeps the mesh editable and aligned to surface contours. A real-time simulation auto-smooths spacing so quads stay even, and simulation-based snapping wraps the new mesh onto the surface in a controlled way. It suits character and organic retopology where you want to dictate flow.

Key features

  • Annotate-brush driven workflow
  • Flow-aware quad generation
  • Real-time auto-smooth for even spacing
  • Simulation-based snapping to the surface
  • Editable output geometry
