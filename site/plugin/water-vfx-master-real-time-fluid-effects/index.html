What 🔖Water Vfx Master: Real-Time Fluid Effects does

Water VFX Master is a Blender addon by Lazy3D for building water effects. Its real-time simulators react immediately to changes, with no baking required, and physics and collision detection let water interact with other elements in the scene.

A Waterfall Generator covers everything from a small trickle to a large cascade, while the Water Cascade Simulator adds multi-layer effects with controls over flow dynamics, collision behavior and environment integration. Presets can be customized and saved to your asset library, so favorite setups can be reused.

Key features

  • Real-time simulators with no baking
  • Physics and collision detection
  • Waterfall Generator from trickle to cascade
  • Multi-layer Water Cascade Simulator
  • Customizable presets saved to the asset library
