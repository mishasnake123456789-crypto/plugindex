What Polyshell Builder does

Polyshell Builder, by Christian-Faur, turns a low-poly model into a set of flat panels you can print and glue into a shell. It is listed under 3D View, Mesh and Import-Export.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.5.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Low-poly mesh to printable panels
  • Panels designed to glue together
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later
  • Requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer
