What Second Life-style camera does

Second Life-style camera is a viewport navigation add-on by miunau that recreates the free-look camera of Second Life in Blender. You hold Alt and use clicks and drags to focus, orbit, zoom and pan.

A click with Alt held focuses on whatever is beneath the pointer; Alt-drag orbits sideways and zooms up and down; Alt+Ctrl-drag orbits the focus point without zooming; and Alt+Ctrl+Shift-drag pans. Releasing the modifiers lets the camera keep moving toward its target. It is written entirely in Python and needs no special permissions.

Key features

  • Alt-click to focus on the item beneath the pointer
  • Alt-drag orbits and zooms
  • Alt+Ctrl-drag orbits without zoom
  • Alt+Ctrl+Shift-drag pans
  • Pure Python, no special permissions
