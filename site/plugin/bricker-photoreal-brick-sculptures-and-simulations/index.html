What Bricker - Photoreal Brick Sculptures & Simulations does

Bricker turns 3D meshes into brick models in Blender in one click. It can also brickify keyframed animation, physics, armatures and fluid, smoke and fire simulations.

Brick type can be bricks and plates, studs, cylinders, cones or custom objects, with controls for materials, detail level, shell thickness and brick scale. Animators and content creators can build brick-style characters and effects without placing each element. Rendered results aim for a convincing plastic-brick look, suited to product style imagery, animation and playful visual effects.

Key features

  • Mesh to brick model in one click
  • Brick types including plates, studs and custom
  • Animation and simulation brickification
  • Shell thickness and detail controls
