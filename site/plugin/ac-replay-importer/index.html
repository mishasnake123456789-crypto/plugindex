What AC Replay Importer does

AC Replay Importer reads .csv files produced by the companion AC Replay Parser and applies Assetto Corsa replay data to objects in Blender, so recorded driving can be re-rendered as animation.

Location and rotation drive a car body and wheels, and telemetry such as RPM, speed and damage is stored as custom properties. Drivers use that data to animate gauges, lights and materials, with custom functions ac_lut and ac_interpolate_discrete for gauges and discrete properties. Framerate and starting frame are adjustable, and the tools sit in the Animation tab of the sidebar.

Key features

  • Applies replay motion to car body and wheels
  • Telemetry stored as custom properties
  • Driver-based gauges, lights and materials
  • Adjustable framerate and start frame
  • Custom driver functions for lookups
