What Speedtimize does

Speedtimize is a collection of workflow helpers for Blender, covering optimization checks and presentation setup. It suits artists organizing scenes and preparing turntable renders.

Mesh tools select similar edge loops and color heavy meshes by face or vertex count, with checks for quads, tris, ngons and normals. A checker material inspects UVs. Render setup offers resolution presets, frame range, transparency, motion blur and persistent data, plus backdrops, neutral lights, a 360 degree camera rig, clay and wireframe overrides. A bundled LowHigh Renamer manages _low and _high suffixes.

Key features

  • Mesh checks and heavy-mesh visualization
  • UV checker material
  • Render setup, backdrops and 360 camera rig
  • Bundled LowHigh Renamer
