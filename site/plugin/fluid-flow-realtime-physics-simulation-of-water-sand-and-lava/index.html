What Fluid Flow - Realtime Physics Simulation Of Water, Sand & Lava does

Fluid Flow runs liquid and granular simulation in Blender without any baking. It is built with Geometry Nodes, so water, sand and lava react in the viewport as you adjust sliders, giving immediate feedback.

A Simulation Type toggle sets the material behavior: water flows downhill, sand piles up and settles, and lava cools and solidifies. Sliders control speed, cooling and emission, with further settings for solver iterations, grid resolution, slope angle, cool rate and heat radius. Artists wanting quick flowing effects for motion design or environments will appreciate the interactive workflow.

Key features

  • Real-time water, sand and lava in the viewport
  • Simulation Type toggle
  • Per-material behavior such as piling and cooling
  • Sliders for speed, cooling and emission
  • No baking, built on Geometry Nodes
