What Refine tracking solution does

Refine tracking solution improves a camera solve in Blender by changing the weight given to individual tracks. It is found inside the Clip Editor at Tools, Solve, Refine Solution.

It supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer, is released under GPL v2 or later and needs no special permissions. It was originally bundled with Blender. Match-movers and compositors tuning a solve can use it for more accurate camera motion. Down-weighting unreliable tracks and re-solving is a standard method for lowering solve error without deleting useful footage data.

Key features

  • Refines solution via track weight
  • In Clip Editor under Solve tools
  • Supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
  • No special permissions
