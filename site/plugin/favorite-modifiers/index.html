A Blender add-on that customizes the Add Modifier menu so your most used modifiers stay at hand.

What Favorite Modifiers does

Favorite Modifiers reduces scrolling in Blender's Add Modifier menu. You choose which modifiers appear, so the few you use daily are directly accessible while the long default list stays out of the way.

The add-on is compatible with Blender 4.0 through 4.5, distributed under the GPL and documented by the developer. It suits modelers and technical artists who keep reaching for the same bevel, mirror, subdivision or array modifiers and want a shorter, personalized list that speeds up routine modeling steps.

Key features

  • Choose which modifiers appear in the menu
  • Avoids scrolling the full modifier list
  • Compatible with Blender 4.0 to 4.5
  • GPL licensed, documentation available
