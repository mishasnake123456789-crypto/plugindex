What Pixel Lab MockUp Volume 1: Minimalist Design does

Pixel Lab MockUp Volume 1 provides 10 fully textured and lit studio scenes for showing off designs. The process is simple: open a scene, place your design on the devices and render.

Render setups are included for Octane in Cinema 4D, Octane Standalone, Redshift in Cinema 4D and Corona in Cinema 4D, and a Photoshop file prepared at 6000x4335 resolution is part of the pack. Lighting and texturing are high resolution. App designers and brand studios can present interfaces and packaging attractively without building scenes.

Key features

  • 10 lit and textured mockup studios
  • Open scene, add design, render
  • Octane, Redshift and Corona render options
  • Photoshop file at 6000x4335
  • High-resolution lighting and texturing
