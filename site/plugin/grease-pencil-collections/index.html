Folder-style organization for Blender Grease Pencil layers with collapsible groups.

What Grease Pencil Collections does

Grease Pencil Collections brings folders to Grease Pencil layers. Create a folder, assign layers to it by selecting a layer and choosing its location, then collapse groups you do not need to touch.

It keeps long layer stacks, such as background layers, out of the way while you draw. The add-on is GPL-licensed, comes with documentation and supports Blender 2.83 through 4.2. It benefits 2D animators and illustrators using complex Grease Pencil scenes.

Key features

  • Folder-based layer organization
  • Assign layers to folders
  • Collapsible groups
  • Blender 2.83 to 4.2
