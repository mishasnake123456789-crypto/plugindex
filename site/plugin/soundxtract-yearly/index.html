What SoundXtract - Yearly does

SoundXtract - Yearly is a subscription plugin for After Effects and Premiere Pro that helps video follow music. It generates beat markers from elements like beats, drums and instrumentals and aligns footage to them.

Video clips can be placed and aligned to the audio, effects and keyframes can be tied to sounds for music-reactive visuals, and markers are placed on both clips and the sequence. It also handles cuts and solos automatically for multicam footage. Editors and motion designers building rhythmic montages and music videos benefit most.

Key features

  • Beat markers from drums, beats and instrumentals
  • Places and aligns clips to audio
  • Links effects and keyframes to sounds
  • Automatic cut and solo for multicam
  • Works in After Effects and Premiere Pro
