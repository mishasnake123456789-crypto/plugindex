What Corner Sharpener 2D & 3D does

Corner Sharpener tidies corners on subdivided models in Blender. With the subdivision modifier, corners tend to round off, and this add-on automatically creates supporting edges and removes face edges, work normally done with the knife tool.

It works best on convex surfaces and needs the selected vertex, or the selected vertex of edges, to have exactly three connected edges. A 2D Sharpener runs in Vertex Mode, with a separate 3D version. Hard-surface and product modelers who want crisp corners without extra topology fuss will find it saves repeated manual cuts.

Key features

  • Sharpens corners under the subdivision modifier
  • Creates supporting edges automatically
  • 2D and 3D sharpeners
  • Best on convex surfaces
