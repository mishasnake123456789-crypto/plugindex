What Auto Upscale Addon does

Auto Upscale lets you render at a lower resolution and enlarge the result afterward, shortening iteration time. An automatic mode works hands-free, and a manual mode allows fine-tuning.

Scale factors are 2x, 3x, 4x and 8x, a method option favors either speed or quality, and Resolution Sync matches render resolution to the chosen factor. It suits artists with limited hardware who still need high-resolution output. Among its capabilities are scale factors 2x to 8x, automatic and manual modes and speed or quality method choice.

Key features

  • Scale factors 2x to 8x
  • Automatic and manual modes
  • Speed or quality method choice
  • Resolution Sync
