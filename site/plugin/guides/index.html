What Guides does

Guides by Rendertom is an After Effects script for managing guides in bulk. It can set Lock Guides, Snap to Guides and Show Guides across several compositions and copy and paste guides between compositions or layers.

Guides can be saved to reuse later or share with a team, cleared across compositions, rotated and moved horizontally or vertically. New guides can be placed at the edge or center of a comp or layer, wrapped around the edges, or generated as a grid with a guide every X pixels or the area divided into X pieces.

Key features

  • Lock, snap and show settings across comps
  • Copy and paste guides between comps or layers
  • Save, apply and clear guides
  • Rotate and move guides
  • Add edge, center and grid guides
