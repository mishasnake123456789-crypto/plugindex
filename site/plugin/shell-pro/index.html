3ds Max modifier that creates a shell around models without self-intersections and with chamfered or beveled edges.

What Shell Pro does

Shell Pro thickens models into a shell inside 3ds Max without the self-intersections the standard Shell modifier can introduce. Surface details are preserved very well, so intricate forms hold up under thickening.

The edge of the shell can be chamfered, filleted or beveled, giving finished-looking borders without extra modeling steps. It lives in the modifier stack and covers many 3ds Max releases starting with 2013. Hard-surface and product modelers who need clean, thick walls on curved or detailed meshes will appreciate it.

Key features

  • Shells models without self-intersections
  • Preserves surface details
  • Chamfer, fillet and bevel options for the shell edge
  • Works as a modifier in the stack
  • Works with 3ds Max from 2013 onward
