What Dosch 3D - Lo Poly People Vol. 2 - 3ds Max does

Lo Poly People Vol. 2 is the second volume in Dosch Design's series of low-polygon human figures for 3ds Max. It offers 100 fully textured models of photo-real people, each built from 10,000 polygons.

The figures stand in a variety of poses, which makes the set practical for populating architectural renderings, street scenes and presentations without the weight of high-resolution characters. Artists who need crowds or background extras can place them straight into a scene.
