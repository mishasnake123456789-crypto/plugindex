Blender add-on that applies captured real-world camera shake to cameras, with layering and animated influence.

What Camera Shakify does

Camera Shakify makes digital cameras feel handheld. Shakes recorded from real cameras are applied through a panel in the camera properties, adding natural movement to otherwise perfectly smooth shots.

You can choose from several shake types, use a single one or stack several, and animate the influence of each over time. It requires Blender 4.4 or newer. The code is released under GPL v3 or later and the shake data under CC0. Motion designers and animators wanting documentary-style or action-camera feel can add it quickly.

Key features

  • Captured real-life shake library
  • Layer multiple shakes
  • Animate shake influence
  • Panel in camera properties
