What Pose2Sim Blender does

David PAGNON's Pose2Sim Blender links a research-grade motion analysis pipeline with Blender. It imports camera calibration from a .toml file, markers from .trc or .c3d, and OpenSim content: .osim models, .mot motions and .mot forces.

With that data a character can be rigged using markerless kinematics. Camera tools and other utilities are bundled, and you choose between a quick or a full install. Researchers, biomechanics specialists and animators using video-based capture can inspect results visually.

Key features

  • Imports calibration from .toml
  • Imports .trc and .c3d markers
  • Imports OpenSim models, motions and forces
  • Rigs characters with markerless kinematics
  • Quick and full install options
