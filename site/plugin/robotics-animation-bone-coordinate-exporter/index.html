What Robotics Animation Bone Coordinate Exporter does

Robotics Animation Bone Coordinate Exporter turns armature motion into spreadsheet-style data. It exports coordinates along with Euler and quaternion rotation values for animated bones.

The CSV files are intended for robotics, simulation and other external pipelines that need pose data from Blender animation. Engineers and animators programming robot motion can design movement visually and take the numbers to their controllers.

Key features

  • CSV export of armature animation
  • Coordinates, Euler and quaternion values
  • For robotics and simulation pipelines
