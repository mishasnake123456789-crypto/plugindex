What Polyline does

Polyline from Motion Boutique draws vivid, fully controllable animated lines made of polygon segments in After Effects. It builds on the stock Corner Pin and CC Power Pin effects.

It has been updated for Apple Silicon and works with After Effects from CS5.5 through 2024. The tool is discontinued and sold at a price you name yourself, so do not expect ongoing developer support. One limit to note is that total line length is capped by the 30,000 pixel maximum composition size. Motion designers wanting geometric line graphics can still use it.

Key features

  • Colourful, controllable animated polygonal lines
  • Apple Silicon compatible
  • After Effects CS5.5 through 2024
  • Uses Corner Pin and CC Power Pin
  • Length limited by 30,000px comp size
