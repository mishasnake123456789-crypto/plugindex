What Cinematic Flight Spin does

Cinematic Flight Spin adds aileron rolls to flying objects. Using presets, it bakes 360-degree spins onto any flight curve or path with a click.

The number of rolls, spin duration and fade-in and fade-out timing are adjustable, keyframes can be baked or deleted, and you can design and save your own spins as presets. Useful for planes, drones and spacecraft shots. Key capabilities include preset-based aileron rolls and adjustable roll count, duration and fades.

Key features

  • Preset-based aileron rolls
  • Adjustable roll count, duration and fades
  • Bake and delete keyframes
  • Save custom presets
