What Fade Up RTL does

From Sam Mageram comes Fade Up RTL, a tool for After Effects that animates multi-line text with a rising fade. It supports both right-to-left and left-to-right scripts, which many text animators handle poorly, and it also works with paragraph text.

Animation can run by character, word or line, with adjustable in and out transition timing, and the timing can be defined either by time or by speed. The smoothness of the fades is controllable. The workflow is to select layers, set parameters and click Update, making it practical for subtitles, Arabic or Hebrew titles and quote cards.

Key features

  • Animates by character, word or line
  • Adjustable IN and OUT transition timing
  • Time-based or speed-based animation
  • Fade smoothness control
  • RTL, LTR and paragraph text support
