What Wrap In Plastic does

Wrap In Plastic is a Blender add-on that places a plastic film around any object you select, handy for packaged-product looks and similar scenes. Instead of modeling the cover by hand, you generate it and adjust it afterward.

Thickness, smoothness through subdivision levels, and a light color tint over the clear material are all adjustable. To cover several objects in one wrap, join them first. The result works with Blender Internal, Cycles and Eevee. Documentation and a video tutorial come with the add-on.

Key features

  • Wraps a selected object in a plastic cover
  • Adjustable thickness and subdivision smoothness
  • Color tint over transparent plastic
  • Works in Blender Internal, Cycles and Eevee
