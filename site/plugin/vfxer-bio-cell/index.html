What VFXER Bio Cell does

VFXER Bio Cell is a collection of 50 organic models for sci-fi VFX, medical and scientific visualization. Subjects include DNA, virus cells, blood cells, neurons, bacteria and other microorganisms, all stylized and prepared for Cinema 4D and Redshift.

Models drag and drop from the Cinema 4D asset browser, come with 4K textures and optimized Redshift shader settings, and are plug-and-play, so there is no modeling or shader setup to do first. They are a quick route to convincing microscopic scenes for explainers, titles and science content.

Key features

  • 50 organic stylized models
  • DNA, virus cells, blood cells, neurons, bacteria
  • Drag and drop from the asset browser
  • 4K textures and tuned Redshift shaders
  • Plug-and-play, no modeling needed
