What Armbwoods Brazil Pbr Woods does

ARMBWoods bundles twenty wood materials for architectural and game use. They come from South America, Brazil and other countries and are built on a specular/glossiness workflow.

Apply a material by selecting objects and assigning it. The goal is photorealistic hardwoods for floors, furniture and props. The set is aimed at realistic surfaces for both rendered interiors and real-time projects. Artists can drop in realistic timber on floors, doors, furniture and game props without authoring textures themselves.

Key features

  • 20 PBR wood materials
  • South American and Brazilian species
  • Specular/glossiness workflow
  • Applied by assigning to selected objects
