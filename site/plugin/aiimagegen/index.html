Generates AI images inside After Effects through Replicate and drops them into the active comp.

What aiImageGen does

aiImageGen connects After Effects to Replicate so you can create images without leaving the application. Developed by Clayton West, it accepts a text prompt, a reference image, or a capture of what is currently selected in your composition.

Finished images are downloaded, saved and placed into the active comp, already scaled and centered. Cost estimates are shown up front, and alerts appear for insufficient credits or prompts that get blocked. The bundled model options include Google Nano Banana Pro, Google Imagen 4 and FLUX 2 Pro. It suits artists who want quick concept art or backgrounds in their timeline.

Key features

  • Text prompt, reference image or comp capture inputs
  • Auto-downloads and places images in the comp
  • Cost estimates and credit or prompt alerts
  • Includes Nano Banana Pro, Imagen 4 and FLUX 2 Pro
