What Lineworks does

Lineworks lets artists decorate a character with drawn lines in Blender. Rigged strips and Grease Pencil strokes are added and conform to the character's surface wherever you place them.

A control rig is included, so the look of the lines can be adjusted and animated. It suits stylized and toon projects where hand-drawn accents, creases or outlines should stay attached to a moving body.

Key features

  • Rigged strips plus Grease Pencil strokes
  • Lines follow the character's surface
  • Includes a rig to control appearance
  • Placement-based attachment
