What Dosch 3D: Business Jet Details does

This Dosch Design model depicts a business jet with all its important details, fully textured and prepared for Cinema 4D. You can modify scale freely, change every material and color, and apply your own textures or livery labels.

Individual elements can be turned off or set anywhere between fully visible and invisible, and they can be animated one by one or all together. That flexibility suits corporate aviation visuals, charter advertising and technical illustrations, where the aircraft often has to match a particular client's colors.

Key features

  • Detailed textured business jet
  • Freely editable materials, textures and colors
  • Own textures and labels supported
  • Elements switchable and transparent
  • Animation per element or whole model
