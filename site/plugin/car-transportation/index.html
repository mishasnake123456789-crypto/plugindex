What Car Transportation does

Car Transportation from Bproduction supplies a managed library of rigged vehicles for Blender. Over seventy-eight models are included, from trucks and sports cars to motorcycles, planes, boats and tractors.

Each has an HD and a low-poly rigged version, the latter handy for backgrounds or scenes with many vehicles. More than seventy car paint shaders cover metallic, matte and shiny finishes, with an editor to create your own, and HDRIs and backplates are provided. It works in Eevee and Cycles. Archviz and advertising artists get quick scene dressing.

Key features

  • 78+ rigged HD vehicles
  • HD and low-poly versions
  • 70+ car paint shaders and editor
  • HDRIs and backplates
