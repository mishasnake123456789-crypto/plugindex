What Wiggle Bones does

Wiggle Bones is a Blender add-on by Hans_Xwh that adds physics simulation to pose bones, giving ropes, chains, tails and similar parts more convincing motion. The physics logic has been reworked to look more realistic.

Bones can collide with specified meshes or collections, with friction, bounce or stickiness options. Pinning uses a damped track constraint to hold a wiggling bone to a target so the other bones react. Library-linked overrides are supported, and a one-click bake converts the simulation to keyframes.

Key features

  • Physics simulation on pose bones
  • Collisions with meshes and collections
  • Pinning via damped track constraint
  • Library-linked override support
  • One-click bake to keyframes
