Blender add-on that passes the weight of the last-picked vertex to the other chosen vertices, even across objects.

What Mio3 Copy Weight does

Mio3 Copy Weight transfers vertex weights from the last selected vertex to the others in your selection. It also works between different objects, not only within a single mesh.

It sits alongside the standard weight panel and can also run as its own panel with a bare-bones interface. Riggers and character artists repairing skin weights can duplicate a known good value quickly to many points. Copying a known-good value is often quicker than repainting, especially when matching weights between a body and its clothing layers.

Key features

  • Copies last selected vertex weight
  • Works across different objects
  • Integrates with the standard weight panel
  • Standalone minimal panel option
