What Sound Prompter does

Sound Prompter from Blace Plugins creates sound effects, and optionally music, from the layers in your After Effects or Premiere Pro composition. The AI runs locally on your machine and syncs the sounds to what happens in the video.

Results are written as .wav files that are added to the project and stored on disk. It works with real footage, 3D renders, motion design and AI-generated clips. Positive and Negative Prompt inputs through linked masks shape or suppress keywords, and applying it to a subcomp or adjustment layer lets multiple layers contribute.

Key features

  • Generates SFX and optional music with local AI
  • Sounds synced to video content
  • Outputs .wav files into the project
  • Positive and Negative prompts via linked masks
  • Works on subcomps or adjustment layers
