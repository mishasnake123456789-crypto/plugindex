What Epidemic Sound does

The Epidemic Sound plugin puts the company's music and sound effects library inside the editing workspace of After Effects and Premiere Pro. Instead of auditioning tracks on a website, editors can search, preview and place audio without leaving their timeline.

Search works with detailed descriptions of the sound you want, and the plugin can suggest music or effects matched to chosen moments in your video. Tracks can be reshaped into custom versions by changing length, looping sections or including and excluding parts. Signed-in users see their playlists and Liked Tracks, and downloads follow your chosen audio format and location.

Key features

  • Describe a sound in detail to search for it
  • Suggestions matched to chosen video frames
  • Custom track versions with length and loop edits
  • Drag-and-drop previewed tracks to the timeline
  • Access to your playlists and Liked Tracks
