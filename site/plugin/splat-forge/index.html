What Splat Forge does

Splat Forge adds 3D Gaussian Splatting support to Blender. Captured scenes made of millions of splats can be displayed, animated and rendered in the viewport in real time.

Rather than using proxies or primitive stand-ins, it evaluates the genuine 3DGS rendering math in GLSL, so what you see follows the true method. It suits artists working with photogrammetry-style captures who want to bring them into a Blender scene. That makes it practical for previewing scanned captures, animating them alongside regular geometry, and judging how a splat scene sits within a larger Blender composition.

Key features

  • Native 3D Gaussian Splatting in Blender
  • Real-time viewing of millions of splats
  • Animate and render splats
  • True 3DGS equations in GLSL
