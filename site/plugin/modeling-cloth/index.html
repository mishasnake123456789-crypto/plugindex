What Modeling Cloth does

Modeling Cloth, by The 3D Advantage, is a cloth simulation engine that responds as you push it around. You can click and drag the fabric directly in the viewport, which gives sculpt-like control over folds and drape.

It can add wrinkles, shrink cloth tightly around objects and blend softbody behavior with cloth. Versions exist for Blender 2.79 and 2.8, and documentation is included. Garment and prop artists can pose fabric interactively instead of repeatedly running simulations.

Key features

  • Click-and-drag cloth manipulation
  • Wrinkle creation
  • Shrink cloth around objects
  • Mixes softbody and cloth effects
  • Versions for Blender 2.79 and 2.8
