Blender add-on inspired by ZBrush and 3DCoat sphere tools for building base shapes from Geometry Nodes spheres.

What Bspheres does

BSpheres brings the sphere-based blocking approach of ZBrush and 3DCoat to Blender. You assemble characters, creatures and other forms from spheres, giving a quick base for sculpting, prototyping and animation.

Geometry Nodes produce the spheres on Blender 2.93 and newer, while symmetry precision lets you work across an axis, and Shift+S scales spheres with undo and redo support. A ToMesh operator converts the result into a mesh, with support for the subdivision surface setting.

Key features

  • Spheres generated with Geometry Nodes
  • Symmetry precision across an axis
  • Shift+S scaling with undo and redo
  • ToMesh operator with subdivision support
