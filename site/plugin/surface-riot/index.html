What Surface Riot does

Surface Riot is a texturing add-on that keeps shader complexity out of sight. Decals, stackable material layers, material variation layers, paint tools and edge wear are all controlled through its interface.

The point is to let artists finish a surface, adding detail, wear and variety, without building nodes by hand. Documentation is included. Environment and prop artists who want believable surfaces without deep shader knowledge are the main audience. Material variation layers add visual interest so repeated surfaces do not look obviously copied.

Key features

  • Decals for surface detail
  • Stackable material layers
  • Material variation layers
  • Paint tools and edge wear
  • No node editor needed
