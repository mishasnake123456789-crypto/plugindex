What DOSCH 3D: Classic Cars does

Vintage automobiles come together in Dosch 3D: Classic Cars, with 18 detailed and fully textured models. They are supplied for Cinema 4D 8.5 and above and R15 through R24, plus FBX, 3DS, 3ds Max, Lightwave, OBJ, Maya, VRML, Softimage XSI and KeyShot.

Each car can be scaled, recolored and retextured, and parts can be hidden, made transparent or animated. They suit architectural visualizations, simulations and renderings that need period vehicles, for example nostalgic street scenes, museum displays or retro-themed advertising.

Key features

  • 18 textured classic car models
  • C4D 8.5+, R15-R24, FBX, OBJ, Maya, KeyShot and more
  • Editable scale, materials and colors
  • Hide, fade or animate elements
