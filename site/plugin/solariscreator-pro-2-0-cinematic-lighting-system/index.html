What Solariscreator Pro 2.0 - Cinematic Lighting System does

Solariscreator Pro 2.0 controls HDRI environments, the physical sky and sunlight as one system in Blender. This avoids building a world node network by hand.

One click builds a complete lighting rig, and you can move between natural daylight and cinematic modes. Integrated volumetric fog offers adjustable volume, density, height-based falloff, physically based scattering and anisotropy for light direction. HDRI and sun stay synchronized for coherent results. Outdoor, architectural and cinematic scenes can be lit and tuned within a single panel.

Key features

  • Unified HDRI, sky and sun control
  • One-click lighting rig
  • Daylight and cinematic modes
  • Volumetric fog with height falloff and anisotropy
  • Synchronized HDRI and sun
