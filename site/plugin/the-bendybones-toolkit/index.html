What the bendybones toolkit does

The bendybones toolkit is a compact rigging utility by Felipe-G. It converts bendy bones into ordinary bones and assigns handle types.

Select the bendy bones and press a button to convert them, or pick the handle bone first and the bendy bone second to set handles. Tools sit in the armature context menu, plus a sidebar item of their own. Riggers working with segmented limbs or tails can save repetitive steps. These small operations remove repetitive clicking when converting or reconfiguring chains of segmented bones.

Key features

  • Converts bendy bones to regular bones
  • Sets handle types
  • Armature context menu access
  • Dedicated sidebar item
