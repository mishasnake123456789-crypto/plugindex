Updated! Undo Plus

Developer: Bala

FreeBlender

A Blender add-on that adds undo and redo for viewport navigation positions like pan, orbit and zoom.

What Updated! Undo Plus does

Undo Plus gives Blender's viewport its own history. Navigation such as pan, rotation and zoom is tracked automatically in the background, so you can return to the exact view you had when working on faces, vertices, edges or objects.

A capture delay of 0.4 seconds waits until you stop moving, so one gesture equals one undo step. Ctrl+Alt+Z moves to the previous position and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Z goes forward, and a Capture View Now button bookmarks a view manually. Modelers and sculptors who lose their framing after wandering around a model will appreciate it.

Key features

  • Automatic tracking of pan, orbit and zoom
  • 0.4 second capture delay, one gesture per step
  • Ctrl+Alt+Z and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Z navigation
  • Manual Capture View Now button
