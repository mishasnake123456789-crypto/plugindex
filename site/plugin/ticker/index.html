What Ticker does

Ticker is a KiwiVFX plugin for creating scrolling news-style tickers and crawls in After Effects. Content comes from starter templates in CSV, TSV or JSON, so updating text means editing a table instead of the composition.

It offers full right-to-left support, separate colors and bold settings for title, text and separators through a color picker, and animation types such as overlap, in both bottom-to-top and top-to-bottom directions, plus parabolic movement. Tooltips display minimum, maximum and default values. Broadcast and social video editors can generate a ticker in two clicks.

Key features

  • Ticker in two clicks
  • CSV, TSV and JSON templates
  • Full right-to-left support
  • Separate colors for title, text and separators
  • Overlap and parabolic animation types
