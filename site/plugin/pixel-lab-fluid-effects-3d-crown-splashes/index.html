What Pixel Lab Fluid Effects: 3D Crown Splashes does

Pixel Lab Fluid Effects: 3D Crown Splashes is a pack of 25 unique splash animations that arrive as Alembic caches. Each carries the characteristics of a real fluid splash and comes in regular-speed and slow-motion variants.

Because the format is Alembic, the files load into Cinema 4D and into Blender, Octane, Redshift, Arnold, V-Ray, Houdini and other packages. Artists can add liquid impacts to product shots and beverage visuals without simulating them, saving time on heavy fluid calculations.
