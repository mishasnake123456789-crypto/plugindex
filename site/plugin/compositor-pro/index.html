What Compositor Pro does

Compositor Pro is a library of well-made node groups for the Blender compositor, intended to lift render quality with little effort. Complex looks like lens flares, halation and film grain apply in one click.

More than 45 nodes cover bloom, ghosts, haze, vignette, color-grading operators and sharpening, plus utilities including a variable clamp, thresholds, custom mix modes and a screen-space UV texture. Nodes can be inserted via the N tab or a pie menu, which comes with a picture gallery, favorites appear in the pie, and quick mix and process space features are included. Documentation is provided.

Key features

  • 45+ effect and utility nodes
  • Bloom, flares, halation, grain and grading
  • N tab and pie menu with visual gallery
  • Favorites and quick mix
  • Fast process space switching
