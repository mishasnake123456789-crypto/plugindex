What IFC Mesh Importer does

IFC Mesh Importer, by KIRANKUMAR-R, brings Industry Foundation Classes data into Blender, including the geometry, materials, metadata and the original hierarchy. It is listed under Import-Export.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
