What Gizmodal Ops does

Gizmodal Ops merges the gizmo-based workflow known from other 3D packages with Blender's modal approach. Tap a key and the matching transform, rotation or scale gizmo appears.

The add-on listens to your keystrokes and switches between methods accordingly, so you can alternate fluidly without selecting tools from the toolbar. Artists moving from other applications find manipulation more intuitive.

Key features

  • Combines gizmo and modal workflows
  • Tap a key to show a transform gizmo
  • Switches between move, rotate and scale
  • Free add-on
