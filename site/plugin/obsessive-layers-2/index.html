What Obsessive Layers 2 does

Obsessive Layers 2 is a layer-trimming toolkit for After Effects. Rather than dragging in and out points by eye, you click a button and layers are cut to the first or last keyframe, marker, track matte, parent or child, or to a selected layer.

Holding ALT flips many behaviors, such as inverting a selection or trimming mattes to layers. Additional modes trim at zero opacity or scale and within the composition bounds. The developer has discontinued it in favour of Fresh Trim.

Key features

  • Trim to keyframes, markers or mattes
  • Trim to parent, children or selection
  • Trim to visibility and comp bounds
  • ALT modifier inverts or alters behavior
  • Discontinued in favour of Fresh Trim
