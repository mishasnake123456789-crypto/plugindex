What Mobo Super Suite does

Mobo Super Suite from Motion Boutique collects seven of the studio's After Effects tools into a single package. Instead of buying and installing each separately, motion designers get the whole set together.

Included are Newton 4, Connect Layers PRO, Pastiche 2, Plotter, Mobo_Utils, AutoStereogram and LayerGenerators, covering physics, layer connections, pattern work, utilities and generators. The bundle is compatible with After Effects versions from 2020 up to 2026, and a demo video is available to show what the collection can do.
