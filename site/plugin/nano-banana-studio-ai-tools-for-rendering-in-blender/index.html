What Nano Banana Studio - Ai Tools For Rendering In Blender does

Nano Banana Studio is a post-render add-on that sends a finished render or image to AI models to produce new variants. The original layout, objects and camera angle are kept, so only the look changes. It needs a billing-enabled Google Gemini API key.

Three models are offered, with output from 512px to 4K depending on the choice. Twelve visual styles use reference images and prompts, and presets cover Render, Retouch, Color, Roles, Action and BG Fix, with saved custom presets. It accepts images, render results and video frames, and includes gemstone and jewelry presets.

Key features

  • Three AI models, up to 4K output
  • 12 curated visual styles
  • Presets for render, retouch, color and background fix
  • Works with images, render results and video frames
