What Quick Rotate does

Quick Rotate is a Blender add-on for turning the active object without hunting through panels. A popup menu lets you choose the X, Y or Z axis and switch between local and global orientation.

You can type any angle, such as 30, 90 or 123.45 degrees, and a semi-transparent ghost preview in light blue shows the result before it is applied. A Reset Rotation button undoes the change, and settings are customizable. It suits modelers who place parts at exact angles.

Key features

  • Popup rotation menu
  • X, Y, Z axis and local or global toggle
  • Custom angle input
  • Live ghost preview
  • Reset Rotation button
