What Uv.Scatter does

Uv.Scatter distributes textures and decals across Blender materials using shaders alone. There are no geometry nodes or particle systems involved, so it stays lightweight and works at material level.

It adds parallax, blur and shadows to the scattered elements, works in Cycles and Eevee, and includes a free CC0 HDRI background texture from HDRI Haven plus documentation. Artists adding dirt, leaves, stains or decorative details to surfaces without extra geometry will find it an economical way to build variation.

Key features

  • Scatters textures and decals in shaders
  • Parallax, blur and shadows
  • No geometry nodes or particles
  • Cycles and Eevee support
  • Free CC0 HDRI included
