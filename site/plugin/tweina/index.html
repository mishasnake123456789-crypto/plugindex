What TweiNa does

TweiNa is a panel that pushes layered art from Photoshop and Illustrator straight into After Effects. Any number of layers can be sent, smart object position, scale and rotation are retained, and imported comps are cropped to fit their contents.

Vector layers become shape layers and text layers turn into editable After Effects text. Multiple artboards are treated as one big comp. To reduce clutter, it can convert groups into smart objects, delete unused Photoshop assets and reuse a layer after precomposing.

Key features

  • Imports any number of Photoshop layers
  • Keeps smart object transformations
  • Converts vectors to shapes and text to editable text
  • Auto-crops imported comps
  • Reduces layer counts
