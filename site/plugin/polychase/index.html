What Polychase does

Polychase is a free, open-source tracking add-on for Blender that takes inspiration from KeenTools GeoTracker. It follows camera or object movement in video using optical flow and PnP solving, assisted by your input.

A 3D Pin Mode places and manages pins on geometry, trajectories can be refined with bundle adjustment, and camera parameters, including focal length estimation, can vary. VFX artists tracking heads, props or camera moves get a no-cost option inside Blender.

Key features

  • 3D pin mode on geometry
  • Camera and geometry tracking
  • Bundle adjustment refinement
  • Variable camera parameters and focal length estimation
