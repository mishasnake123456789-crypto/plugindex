What Mixshaders does

Mixshaders by Derek Wang moves node setups between materials in Blender and blends shaders for you in the Shading workspace.

The Add ActiveMat to ScnMat operator copies selected nodes and their links from one material to another, placing them above the target's topmost nodes. It builds structured directories for procedural materials with hierarchical subfolders, and it mixes shaders automatically, calculating the right place to append nodes. The goal is to keep reusable material libraries organized and quick to assemble.

Key features

  • Copies nodes and links between materials
  • Places nodes above target nodes
  • Hierarchical directories for procedural materials
  • Automatic shader mixing
  • Works in the Shading workspace
