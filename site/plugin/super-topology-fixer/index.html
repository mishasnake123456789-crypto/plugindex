What Super Topology Fixer does

Super Topology Fixer is a Blender plugin for cleaning up polygons. In Edit Mode it takes the selected faces that have four or more vertices and divides them into smaller faces made of three or four vertices.

Vertex coordinates stay exactly where they were and normals remain intact, so the shape of the model is unchanged. Cleaner topology can make Subdivision Surface modifiers behave better. It is convenient for tidying imported or boolean-generated meshes that arrive with awkward faces.

Key features

  • Breaks large faces into triangles or quads
  • Works on selected faces in Edit Mode
  • Keeps vertex positions and normals
  • Helps Subdivision Surface results
