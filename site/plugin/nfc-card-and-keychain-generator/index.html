Blender add-on for designing 3D-printable NFC chip housings as cards, dots or keychains with logos and QR codes.

What NFC Card and Keychain Generator does

NFC Card and Keychain Generator creates printable holders for NFC chips inside Blender. Choose a card, dot or keychain shape and tune the height, edge bevels, corner rounding and any magnet slots you want with hexagonal or circular cutouts.

SVG logos can be imported as objects on the case, with an inset option to sit them flush for multicolor prints. A built-in QR generator places links, vCards, Wi-Fi details or text. The NFC cutout can be disabled for solid items. The finished design exports as an STL ready for printing.

Key features

  • Card, dot and keychain shapes
  • SVG logo import with inset option
  • Built-in QR code generator
  • Magnet slots with auto-adjusted height
  • STL export for printing
