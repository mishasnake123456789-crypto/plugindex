What Hair Card Studio (Hair Card Creator Addon does

Hair Card Studio is a menu-driven way to produce hair cards in Blender. You shape the strands with amount, points, root and tip thickness and length, and add variety with clumping, spread, hairline shape, frizz, curl and roughness.

Material controls recolor all hair at once, make gradients from root to tip and adjust gloss and sheen. Lighting source size, location and intensity can be set, and rendering presets run from Low to UltraHigh with an optional transparent background. Normal, Specular, AO and Alpha maps are output, and presets can be saved.

Key features

  • Strand shape, frizz, curl and clump controls
  • Root-to-tip color gradients
  • Low to UltraHigh render presets
  • Normal, Specular, AO and Alpha map output
  • Savable presets
