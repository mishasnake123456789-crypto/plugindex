What Physical Motion Blur does

Physical Motion Blur, by K_Naoki, controls Blender's motion blur the way a real camera does. Instead of an abstract shutter value, you work with shutter angle, measured in degrees, or shutter speed, measured in seconds.

A mode setting switches between Angle and Speed, the frame rate field shows and sets the scene's FPS, and the Camera Shutter Control panel sits under Properties, Render, Motion Blur. Blender's standard motion blur must be enabled first. It supports Eevee and Cycles for film and photorealistic stills.

Key features

  • Shutter Angle in degrees
  • Shutter Speed in seconds
  • Angle and Speed mode switch
  • Frame Rate field
  • Eevee and Cycles support
