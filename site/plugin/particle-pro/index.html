What Particle Pro does

Particle Pro is a preset collection from LetsMotion for building particle effects in After Effects. It is powered by Stardust, which is required and has to be purchased separately.

Presets are grouped into categories, including shockwaves, and a later update added a category with 40 presets. It works with After Effects from CC 2019 through 2025, and a demo video is available. Users who already own Stardust can reach polished particle looks without setting up emitters and forces by hand.

Key features

  • Preset-based particle effects
  • Categories including shockwaves
  • Powered by Stardust (required)
  • Compatible with CC 2019 to 2025
