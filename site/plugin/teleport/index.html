Stores up to five labeled timeline positions in Premiere Pro and jumps back to them instantly.

What Teleport does

Teleport lets you drop anchors in the timeline and return to them later. Record the playhead's timecode, give it a name, and a click brings you back to that exact frame, which beats scrolling through a long sequence.

As many as five locations can be held at once, covering both clip and sequence anchors. The panel can be laid out horizontally, vertically, or in tiny versions of each, and right-clicking in Tiny View resets anchors without using screen space. Elgato Stream Deck support is available through a free add-on. Editors who bounce between sections of a cut will appreciate it.

Key features

  • Five stored timeline locations
  • Labeled anchors for clips and sequences
  • Four layouts including tiny horizontal and vertical
  • Right-click reset in Tiny View
  • Stream Deck support via free add-on
