Lets you build and play slide-style presentations directly in the 3D View.

What Slides does

Slides turns Blender's 3D View into a presentation tool. You can set up slides and then play through them, moving from one to the next within the viewport.

This is useful for presenting models, scenes or lessons without exporting to another program. Teachers, speakers and artists reviewing work with clients can show 3D content in context while keeping everything live and editable.

Key features

  • Slide-based presentations
  • Playback inside the 3D View
  • No separate presentation app
