What Ghoster - Animation Version Visualizer does

Ghoster helps animators judge whether a revision is actually better. It records an animation sequence and shows it later as a semi-transparent overlay in the viewport while you work on the new version.

Keep up to three ghost layers at once, each with separate color, opacity and frame range, shown as solid, wireframe or silhouette. Comparing old and new takes in real time takes the guesswork out of timing and pose changes.

Key features

  • Records animation as a ghost overlay
  • Up to three independent ghost layers
  • Per-layer color, opacity and frame range
  • Solid, wireframe and silhouette modes
  • Real-time comparison in the viewport
