Blender add-on that adds Unreal and Unity style viewport flying with right mouse button plus WASD keys.

What Ap Navigation does

AP Navigation, by Alex Pearce, lets you move around the Blender viewport the way you would in a game engine. Hold the right mouse button and use WASD to travel forward, backward, left and right.

Several keymaps are included that share the WASD movement but differ in their other shortcuts, so you can pick the one that matches your habits. Level designers and environment artists coming from Unreal or Unity can feel at home right away.

Key features

  • RMB plus WASD fly navigation
  • Modeled on Unreal and Unity controls
  • Several included keymaps
  • Shared WASD movement across keymaps
