What Transcript Hacker does

Transcript Hacker builds on the transcripts that Premiere Pro already generates. Instead of scrubbing through footage, you search the spoken text for a word or phrase and see which clips contain it.

Results can be filtered, and matching clips are inserted straight into a sequence, so a stringout of every mention of a topic can be assembled in a few steps. It is aimed at interview, documentary and podcast editors who work from what people said and want to find and gather those moments quickly.

Key features

  • Works with Premiere Pro's native transcripts
  • Search transcripts for words or phrases
  • Filter transcript results
  • Insert clips from transcript matches
  • Assemble stringouts automatically
