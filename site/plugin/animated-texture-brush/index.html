What Animated Texture Brush does

Animated Texture Brush lets a texture brush step through the frames of an image sequence as you paint. Instead of stamping the same image repeatedly, each dab can use a different frame.

This adds variety and a hand-made feel to painted details, such as scattered marks, foliage or surface wear. It is aimed at texture painters and sculptors who want varied strokes without switching brushes.

Key features

  • Image sequence frames as brush
  • Frame cycling while painting
  • Adds variation to strokes
