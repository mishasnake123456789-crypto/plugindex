What Smart Adapt - Recurring Subscription does

Smart Adapt is an extension for producing a composition in many resolutions. Build a list of screens, organize them into groups and subgroups, and include or exclude any of them with checkboxes.

Each screen can be resized by width or height and given its own alignment, with prefixes and suffixes for naming. Lists can be exported and imported for later jobs. Duplication can happen automatically from the closest resolution or manually from an assigned master composition. This listing is the recurring subscription.

Key features

  • Groups and subgroups of screens
  • Resize by width or height with alignment
  • Prefix and suffix naming
  • Export and import screen lists
  • Auto or manual master duplication
