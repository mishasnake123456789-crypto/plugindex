Color-pairing plugin for After Effects and Premiere Pro that remaps colors and exports a 65-point Cube LUT.

What Color Llama does

Color Llama lets you create pairs of swatch and target colors to turn one color into another, working in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Change Color offsets hues, while Replace Color substitutes them, and blend modes (Smooth, Sharp, Posterized) control the falloff.

Target hue, saturation, lightness and alpha handles refine each pair, and LUT Softness blends targets in 3D color space. The result exports as a 65x65x65 Cube LUT usable in most video and animation software. A Shift+R toggle shows the original for comparison. It suits grading, shot matching across cameras, brand-color matching, keying and stylized looks.

Key features

  • Swatch-to-target color pairs
  • Smooth, Sharp and Posterized blend modes
  • LUT Softness post-process
  • 65x65x65 Cube LUT export
  • Show Original Colors toggle (Shift+R)
