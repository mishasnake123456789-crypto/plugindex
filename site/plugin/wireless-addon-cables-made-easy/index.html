What Wireless Addon - Cables Made Easy does

Wireless Addon is a Blender plugin for building cables quickly. You draw a curve, pick a cable type, and the curve becomes a finished cable with the right plugs on each end, which saves the usual hours of manual modeling and bending.

Twelve common cable styles come built in, covering electrical, audiovisual and computer connections. You can add your own cable parts and plug designs, and further libraries are released over time. It is handy for product visualization, architectural scenes and any desk or rack setup that needs convincing wiring.

Key features

  • Converts any curve into a cable in one click
  • Twelve built-in electrical, A/V and computer cable types
  • Custom cable and plug parts can be added
  • Improved material management
  • Extra libraries released periodically
