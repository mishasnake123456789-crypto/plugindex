A cloner and effector toolkit for After Effects that arranges and animates layers in grids, arrays and lines.

What MographAE does

MographAE brings Cinema 4D-style mograph thinking to After Effects. Three cloners, Grid, Array and Linear, arrange layers into a grid, a radial array or a line, and five effectors then animate them.

Field affects properties by proximity with remappable easing, randomness, delay and field shape. Step works by layer index for sequential motion, Inheritance morphs position and rotation between cloners, Target repels layers and points them toward it, and Noise applies a random pattern. It works with After Effects 2022 through 2025. Motion designers creating intricate repeated animations get a flexible, procedural setup.

Key features

  • Grid, Array and Linear cloners
  • Field effector with remappable easing
  • Step and Inheritance effectors
  • Target and Noise effectors
  • After Effects 2022 to 2025
