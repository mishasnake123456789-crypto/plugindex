What Unitypackage Importer does

Unitypackage Importer, by UTAGE, opens .unitypackage archives in Blender and brings in their meshes, materials and textures. It is listed under Import-Export.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.
