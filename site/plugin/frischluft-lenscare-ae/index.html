Depth-of-field and out-of-focus blur effects for After Effects and Premiere Pro, driven by a depth buffer.

What Frischluft Lenscare AE does

Lenscare adds camera-style focus effects as a 2D post-process in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Its physically based algorithms blur each pixel by how far away it sits, giving believable focus falloff on 3D renders or footage with a depth pass.

The Depth of Field effect reads a depth buffer, while Out of Focus is a faster constant-radius blur that accepts a custom-drawn lens shape and handles background distortion for semi-transparent areas. Aperture settings imitate different cameras, which shows most in highlights, and a brightness boost keeps bright regions from dulling. Focus can be checked without a full scene re-render. Multi-Frame Rendering and Apple Silicon are supported.

Key features

  • Depth of Field driven by a depth buffer
  • Out of Focus blur with custom-drawn lens
  • Aperture controls and highlight brightness boost
  • Focus testing without re-rendering
  • Multi-Frame Rendering and Apple Silicon support
