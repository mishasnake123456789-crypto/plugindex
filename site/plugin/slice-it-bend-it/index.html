What Slice-It Bend-It does

Slice-It Bend-It is a Blender addon by Chipp Walters for slicing and bending meshes with simple, what-you-see controls. It cuts a mesh in even increments along the X, Y or Z direction, and can slice on several axes at once in either object or local mode.

Bending works on meshes such as text and other complex objects, and a new SLICE modifier powers the cuts. It is available in the Tools tab of the 3D viewport's N panel, and documentation is included. It helps when a logo, word or model needs to curve smoothly around a shape.

Key features

  • Even slicing along X, Y or Z
  • Multi-axis slicing in object and local mode
  • Bends text and complex meshes
  • New SLICE modifier
  • Tools N panel with documentation
