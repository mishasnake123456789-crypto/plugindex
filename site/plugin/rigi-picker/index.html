A Blender bone-selection add-on for posing Rigify rigs, with IK, FK and Tweak bone pickers.

What Rigi Picker does

Rigi Picker simplifies selecting controls on Rigify rigs in Blender. From a picker you can choose IK, FK and Tweak bones for the arms, legs, torso and fingers, instead of searching for tiny bones in the viewport.

Selection is smooth, with Shift for multi-select and Alt to deselect. There is a button to switch FK to IK and back, the list can show only bones of the currently displayed layer, and a user bone picker adds chosen bones, even non-Rigify ones, as buttons. It opens from the 3D View sidebar, a pie menu (W) or a popup (Ctrl+W), which benefits character animators.

Key features

  • Pick IK, FK and Tweak bones
  • Shift multi-select and Alt deselect
  • FK to IK and IK to FK switch
  • User bone picker for custom buttons
  • Sidebar, pie menu and popup access
