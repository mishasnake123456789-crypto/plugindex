Blender add-on that renders chosen subcollections one at a time, enabling each and its cameras in turn.

What Render Subcollections does

Render Subcollections automates batch rendering by collection. For every subcollection you select, it enables the collection, renders whichever cameras it contains, then disables it again before moving to the next.

Subcollections are picked from the scene tree in a panel. This suits product visualization, variants and multi-shot scenes where different content must be rendered separately without toggling visibility by hand.

Key features

  • Activates, renders and deactivates each subcollection
  • Renders the cameras within each collection
  • Selection from the scene tree
  • Panel-based control
