What Spiral Maker does

Spiral Maker is a small script for creating spirals out of a mask path. Click once and the pattern is made, saving time compared with drawing and adjusting curves manually.

You can add a stroke or fill, bake and unbake the result, open or close several mask paths together, and reverse their direction. It is a handy generator for backgrounds, hypnotic loops and decorative graphics by Aaron Ecthelion. The script is a handy shortcut for loaders, abstract backgrounds and other graphics built around spiral shapes.

Key features

  • One-click spiral creation
  • Add a stroke or fill
  • Bake and unbake patterns
  • Open or close multiple mask paths
  • Reverse path direction
