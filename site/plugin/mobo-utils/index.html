What MOBO_Utils does

MOBO_Utils from Motion Boutique is a bundle of minimal, single-purpose tools that fill gaps in After Effects. It includes MultiDuplicate, PasteImage, SelectSameProperties, ColorPalette, CopyColor, Color Transfer and CollectFonts.

Other items are AddPathVertices for masks and shapes, an Arrow effect with customizable drawing, and BakeAllExpressions and RemoveAllExpressions. GIF_Export is an output module that writes GIF files directly from After Effects, and APNGExport handles APNG output. Each tool is native and focused on one job.

Key features

  • MultiDuplicate, PasteImage and SelectSameProperties
  • Color Transfer, ColorPalette and CopyColor
  • CollectFonts and AddPathVertices
  • GIF and APNG export
  • Bake and remove all expressions
