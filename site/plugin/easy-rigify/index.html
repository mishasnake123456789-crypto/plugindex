What Easy Rigify does

Easy Rigify takes the tedious alignment step out of Rigify. Place visual markers on your character, run auto-detection, and the add-on builds a configured metarig aligned to your markers.

Detection covers spine, hips, knees, feet and arm and leg chains, plus fingers and step-by-step face detectors including teeth and tongue. You can generate a Human rig without face or with face, and it works with standard Rigify human rigs out of the box. Character artists can reach a working rig in far less time.

Key features

  • Detects spine, limbs, hands and feet
  • Face detectors including teeth and tongue
  • One-click Human metarig with or without face
  • Bones aligned to placed markers
  • Compatible with standard Rigify human rigs
