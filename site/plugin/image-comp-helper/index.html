What Image Comp Helper does

Image Comp Helper shows composition guides in the active 3ds Max viewport to help frame renders. This updated edition by Itaipava extends the original imageCompHelper by Christoph Bülter with new guides and controls.

The golden spiral works in portrait as well as landscape and can be unlocked, zoomed and moved. Drop-down menus offer common aspect ratios, a toggle switches orientation, and right-clicking a button rotates the spiral or triangle guides. A Diagonal Thirds guide was added. Artists setting up cameras for stills and animation can check balance before rendering.

Key features

  • Golden spiral for portrait and landscape
  • Common aspect ratio drop-downs
  • Portrait/landscape toggle
  • Right-click rotation of spiral and triangle guides
  • Diagonal Thirds guide
