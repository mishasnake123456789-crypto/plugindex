What Axis Addon does

Axis Addon helps position objects precisely, particularly around round holes and cylindrical parts. In edit mode it builds an axis from either a selected plane or three points picked on a circle, then adjusts the 3D cursor to match.

The plane option places an axis at the weighted center, perpendicular to the face. The three-point option finds the circle's center and puts the axis perpendicular to it. You can send the cursor to either end of the axis and then align a new object to it with the cursor-based align function, which suits mechanical and hard-surface modeling.

Key features

  • Axis through a plane's weighted center
  • Axis from three points on a circle
  • 3D cursor placed at either axis end
  • Object alignment to the axis direction
