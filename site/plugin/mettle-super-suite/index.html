What Mettle Super Suite does

Mettle Super Suite packs Flux, Mantra V2 and Freeform Pro Suite together for After Effects and Premiere Pro. Together they cover volumetric fractals, 360 degree effects and 3D transformations.

After Effects is supported from CS6 through the 2026 release, while Premiere Pro is supported from CS6 through the 2023 release. A demo video is available. Artists who work with immersive or abstract visuals and wish to own all three plugins can buy them as a single set.
