What Boinx Software FxTiles does

FxTiles from Boinx Software brings several 3D tile effects to After Effects and DaVinci Resolve. Images are cut into tiles or fragments that flip or shatter across 3D space, with flexible animation settings.

Flip 3D and Shatter 3D are filters, while the transitions include Flip & Reveal, Shatter & Rebuild, a version with fade, and Shatter & Reveal in two variants. The plugin needs the free FxFactory host, on Apple Silicon or Intel. Video and post-production professionals wanting dramatic scene changes or title breakups can use them across FxFactory-supported hosts.

Key features

  • Flip 3D and Shatter 3D filters
  • Flip & Reveal transition
  • Shatter & Rebuild transitions, with and without fade
  • Shatter & Reveal in two variants
  • Requires free FxFactory host; Apple Silicon and Intel
