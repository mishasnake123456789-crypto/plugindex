What C.Mats does

chery's C.Mats is a set of 90 procedural materials plus two add-ons for Blender. Surfaces include fabric, wood, metal, marble and car paint, and there are 15 planet materials, real and imagined.

The Dirtify 2.0 add-on provides realistic weathering. Because everything is procedural, no texture files or resolution limits come into play. Documentation is included, Blender 3.0 to 4.3 is supported, and the license is GPL. Archviz, product and space-scene artists get a ready library.

Key features

  • 90 procedural materials
  • Fabric, wood, metal, marble and car paint
  • 15 planet materials
  • Dirtify 2.0 weathering add-on
  • Blender 3.0 to 4.3
