What TextBox 2 does

TextBox 2 is a native After Effects plugin that places a shape behind text and keeps it sized as the words change. It applies directly to the text layer, with none of the usual parenting, mattes or expressions. That makes it practical for lower thirds, subtitles, captions and text-in and text-out animation.

Shapes can be auto, square, circle or polygon. Expansion can be set per side, including negative values, with individual corner rounding. The box can be transformed independently, with gradients, borders, dashes and Trim Paths. Vertical, horizontal and paragraph text work, along with motion blur and 8, 16 and 32 bpc.

Key features

  • Shape updates automatically with the text
  • Auto, square, circle and polygon shapes
  • Per-side expansion and corner rounding
  • Gradients, borders, dashes and Trim Paths
  • Works with paragraph and vertical text
