What AEJuice Instagram Stories does

AEJuice Instagram Stories is a pack of story animations for After Effects, and it can be used in Premiere Pro as well. Templates are applied with one click or by dragging them into the project.

In and Out animations are included, along with options for building typography and text effects. Resolution can be resized, at 30 frames per second. The Pack Manager adds fast previews, favorites and quick downloads. Social creators can assemble polished vertical content without designing every frame.
