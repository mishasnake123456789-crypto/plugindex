Blender add-on by dr.sybren that stops scene playback from looping, with a toggle in the timeline header.

What Unlooped does

Unlooped makes Blender playback halt at the end of the frame range rather than restart. A toggle button sits in the timeline header.

The loop setting is stored for each workspace tab. The add-on needs no special permissions and works with Blender 4.2 LTS and newer under GPL v3 or later. Animators reviewing a shot once, or editors syncing playback to audio, avoid unwanted looping. It is a small convenience, but it removes a recurring annoyance when checking a single pass of an animation.

Key features

  • Prevents playback looping
  • Toggle in timeline header
  • Setting stored per workspace tab
  • For Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
