Blender add-on that applies procedural tearing and aging to meshes in real time, with materials assigned to the deformed areas.

What Babel 2.0: Addon does

Babel 2.0 gives models a worn, torn look procedurally. Mesh deformation updates live, so what you see in the viewport is the final effect.

With Smart Material Assign, materials land only on the deformed portion of the mesh, giving artists extra control. It is made by Egret Studios LLC and suits props such as fabrics, paper and weathered objects where damage needs to be created quickly and adjusted easily.

Key features

  • Real-time mesh deformation
  • Procedural tear and aging effects
  • Smart Material Assign for deformed areas
  • Live viewport preview
