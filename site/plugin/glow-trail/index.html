Generates glowing trails behind moving objects in Blender, with real-time or frame-based modes and NLA baking.

What Glow Trail does

Glow Trail is a Blender add-on that draws luminous trails following objects through a scene. Resolution controls how dense the trail segments are, and it works in either real-time or frame-based mode, depending on whether you want interactive previews or precise animation control.

Glow intensity, color and size scaling are adjustable, and NLA baking makes the effect portable. Motion designers can use it for light streaks, energy trails and stylized motion accents.

Key features

  • Trails follow any object
  • Real-time and frame-based modes
  • Adjustable glow intensity, color and size
  • NLA baking for portable effects
