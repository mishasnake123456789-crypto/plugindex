What Burner Font Family does

Burner Font Family from Highground is a graffiti typeface built to be stacked, aimed at After Effects and Premiere Pro. It delivers aggressive, modern wildstyle letterforms that combine into complex burner pieces.

Each of the five styles has 155 letters, numbers, symbols and flourishes, with fill, outline, fade and detail layers that fit together consistently and stay legible. Every style has three full alphabets plus initial and terminal glyphs and other embellishments. A Burner Color style uses OpenType full-color vector capabilities to merge four styles into one color typeface, usable as it is or broken apart and recolored.

Key features

  • 155 glyphs in each of five styles
  • Fill, outline, fade and detail layers
  • Three alphabets with initial and terminal glyphs
  • OpenType color version combining the styles
