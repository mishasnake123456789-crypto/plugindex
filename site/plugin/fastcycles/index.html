Blender add-on that switches Cycles between Draft, Medium and High render presets in one click.

What Fastcycles does

Fastcycles saves you from adjusting Cycles settings by hand. A single-click interface switches between three optimized render presets.

Draft gives a very fast rough with good quality, Medium delivers high-quality results in little time, and High uses high-end settings for the best final renders. It is a Blender extension with documentation.

Key features

  • One-click Cycles preset switching
  • Draft, Medium and High presets
  • No manual Cycles configuration
  • Blender extension
