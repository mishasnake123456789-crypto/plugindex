What FlyWiz - Vray Flythrough animation wizard does

FlyWiz is a wizard for 3ds Max users who render architectural flythroughs with V-Ray. Preparing such an animation normally involves a series of manual settings; this tool takes over that sequence and applies its changes directly within the engine.

Each required step can be run and rendered individually, giving control over the process, or the entire chain can be launched with a single click. It targets older releases, covering Max 9 SP1 up to Max 2010, and the developer's page hosts a demonstration video. Artists who produce walkthrough animations in V-Ray will find it removes repetitive preparation before rendering begins.

Key features

  • Automates V-Ray flythrough animation setup
  • Applies changes directly in the engine
  • Run and render each step on its own
  • One-click option to execute the whole process
  • Supports 3ds Max 9 SP1 through 2010
