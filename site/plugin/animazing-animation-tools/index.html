What Animazing - Animation Tools does

Animazing is a collection of small animation helpers for Blender that remove everyday friction from keyframe work. Rather than installing several separate tools, you get Root Bone Reverser, Auto Frame, Animation Scrubber, Share Keyframes, Precision Transform and Timeline Arranger together.

Root Bone Reverser counter-animates the root so a character stays in place, ready for in-place export. Auto Frame zooms the curve view to your working area through a pie menu or side panel, and Animation Scrubber jumps between keyframes by moving the cursor, with adjustable sensitivity. Character animators who spend long hours in the timeline and graph editor gain the most.

Key features

  • Root Bone Reverser for in-place animation export
  • Auto Frame with Frame All and Frame Selected
  • Animation Scrubber to move between keyframes
  • Share Keyframes, Precision Transform, Timeline Arranger
