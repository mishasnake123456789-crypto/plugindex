What Addon Generator - Convert Geometry Nodes Into Add-Ons does

Addon Generator packages your Geometry Nodes work for distribution. It takes any node group and produces a ZIP that installs as an add-on, with no coding needed.

The interface maps the group's exposed parameters into a live N-Panel automatically, and one-click Append applies the group as a modifier on the active object. It suits creators who want to share or sell node tools, and teams that want easy access to internal setups.
