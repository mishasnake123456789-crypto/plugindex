What BOOM Library SFX Destruction - Designed does

BOOM Library SFX Destruction (Designed) offers pre-made sounds of materials breaking and straining, including metal, wood, glass, ice, rock and buildings, for After Effects and Premiere Pro. It is aimed at users who prefer finished effects to building their own.

There are over 300 ready-to-use sounds in more than 70 files, each in four versions, plus large destruction compositions. Audio is 96 kHz, 24-bit WAV. Soundminer metadata with detailed keywords makes finding the right crash or creak quick.
