A Blender add-on that places a controllable wind system in the scene with savable presets.

What Windy does

Windy adds believable wind to a Blender scene, which is useful for moving grass, trees, cloth and other vegetation in architectural and environment renders. A single button creates the wind system at the 3D cursor, and another removes it again completely.

Controls for wind strength, turbulence strength, and frequency strength and scale shape the intensity and speed of the motion. Several wind setups can be saved as presets, then switched live or animated over time. Everything lives in a dedicated Windy tab in the sidebar, so setup stays quick for archviz artists and animators.

Key features

  • Create Wind at the 3D cursor
  • Strength and turbulence controls
  • Frequency strength and scale for motion speed
  • Savable presets that can be switched or animated
  • Dedicated Windy sidebar tab
