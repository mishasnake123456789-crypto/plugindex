A customisable subtitling plugin for Premiere Pro that syncs text by phrase.

What SubMachine does

SubMachine produces subtitles inside Adobe Premiere Pro and lets you customise their look. It syncs the text phrase by phrase, so the captions match speech.

Licensing comes as a subscription for ongoing use or a perpetual lifetime option. It works with Premiere Pro 2022 through 2026, and a demo video is available. Editors preparing videos for different audiences can use it. This makes it an option for editors who regularly deliver subtitled content and prefer a one-time purchase over a subscription.

Key features

  • Customisable subtitles
  • Phrase-based text syncing
  • Subscription or perpetual license
  • Supports Premiere Pro 2022 to 2026
  • Demo video available
