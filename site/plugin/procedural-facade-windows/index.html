What Procedural Facade Windows does

Procedural Facade Windows creates architectural windows in 3ds Max quickly. Each window has one to four segments, and each segment can be Normal, Fixed or Tilt, with mullion positions, setbacks and handles all adjustable. Autosize fits a window to its opening.

Swing and hinge direction can be flipped, sashes set to open the same way, and the opening animated. Internal or external glass, sills and drainage caps can be added or removed, and a transom window can be set per segment. Colors can follow RAL or be custom, the sill has a galvanized steel texture, seven wood types are offered, and textures range from 1K to 8K with Real-World Map Size support. Architectural visualization artists benefit most.

Key features

  • 1 to 4 segments with Normal, Fixed or Tilt types
  • Adjustable mullions, setbacks and handles
  • Animated opening with flippable swing and hinge
  • Optional glass, sills, drainage caps and transoms
  • RAL or custom colors, seven wood types, 1K to 8K textures
