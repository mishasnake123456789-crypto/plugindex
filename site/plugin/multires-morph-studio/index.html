What Multires Morph Studio does

Multires Morph Studio, by LowPolyPotato, converts the detail from a Multires sculpt into shape keys while leaving the original intact, so sculpted changes can be blended or animated. It is listed under Sculpt.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
