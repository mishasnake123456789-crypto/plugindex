Blender add-on that layers camera shake on top of existing animation using presets like Handycam and Explosion.

What Ultimate Camera Shake Generator does

RUIDO VFX's generator adds shake without disturbing your camera moves: it uses a delta transform setup, so the shake sits above the main animation and leaves position and rotation untouched.

There are five adjustable presets: Soft, Handycam, Explosion, Persecution and Spaceship. Frequency governs how quickly the direction changes, and Time Jitter introduces random variation in timing. Handy for action sequences, handheld looks and impacts, and the shake can be edited or removed anytime.

Key features

  • Non-destructive delta transform shake
  • Five presets including Handycam and Explosion
  • Frequency control
  • Time Jitter for random timing
  • Layered over existing camera animation
