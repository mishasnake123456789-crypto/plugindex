Blender toolkit for light and shadow linking through a viewport pie menu, for Cycles and EEVEE on 4.2+.

What Ray Link does

Ray Link moves light linking and shadow linking in Blender into a pie menu workflow. It supports Cycles and EEVEE from Blender 4.2.

An eyedropper links and unlinks objects using visual guides and overlays, and operators link, unlink or exclude the selection. Solo With Linked focuses on selected objects and their lights or hides all other lights. Add Linked Lights creates lights linked and parented to objects, and Link Groups connect whole collections to chosen lights.

Key features

  • Eyedropper linking with overlays
  • Link, unlink and exclude operators
  • Solo With Linked
  • Add Linked Lights parented to objects
  • Collection Link Groups
