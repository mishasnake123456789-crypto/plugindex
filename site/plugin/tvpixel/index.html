What TVPixel does

TVPixel simulates the subpixel structure of a display. It pixelates footage into red, green and blue elements the way a television or LCD would show them up close, and works in After Effects and Premiere Pro.

A custom pixel gap control sets how much space separates the elements. After Effects gets 32-bit floating point processing and Multi-Frame Rendering support, while Premiere Pro runs natively on Apple Silicon. It's a quick way to add a screen-within-a-screen or retro monitor look.

Key features

  • R, G, B pixel pattern like a TV or LCD
  • Custom pixel gap controls
  • 32-bit float in After Effects
  • MFR support in After Effects
  • Apple Silicon support in Premiere Pro
