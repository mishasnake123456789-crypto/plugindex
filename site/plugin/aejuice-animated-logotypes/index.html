What AEJuice Animated Logotypes does

AEJuice Animated Logotypes is a set of logo reveal templates. Drag your logo into the placeholder, apply the animation in one click, and then adjust the text and colours to finish the sequence.

Each template has In and Out animations, short and long versions, and editable colours for background, elements, text and logo. Compositions are resizable and run at 30 fps in After Effects, and Premiere Pro 2021 MOGRT files are included. The Pack Manager provides fast preview and favourites. Editors needing quick branded intros or outros can produce them without building an animation.

Key features

  • One-click apply and drag-and-drop logo placement
  • In and Out animations
  • Editable colours for every part
  • Short and long versions
  • Premiere Pro 2021 MOGRT support
