What MonkeFrames Mocap does

MonkeFrames Mocap, by sirkingbinx, reads motion capture files produced by MonkeFrames and brings them into Blender as animation, so captured performances can be used on your rigs. It is listed under Animation and Import-Export.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
