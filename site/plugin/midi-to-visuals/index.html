What MIDI To Visuals does

MIDI To Visuals, by Patochun, turns musical data into animation inside Blender. A MIDI file is read and its notes and instruments are mapped to visual sequences, so the picture follows the music exactly.

Six styles are offered: bar graphs, piano-roll style strips, falling waterfalls, fireworks, fountains and light shows. Animation follows note on and off events, velocity, track colors and timing, and MIDI formats 0, 1 and 2 are supported. It uses node-based materials, particle systems and Geometry Nodes, and can sync audio from MP3 with compositing effects.

Key features

  • Six visualization styles from bars to fireworks
  • Syncs to note events, velocity and track colors
  • MIDI formats 0, 1 and 2
  • Uses node materials, particles and geometry nodes
  • MP3 audio sync and compositing effects
