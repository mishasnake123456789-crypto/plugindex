What rdg::graphPaper does

rdg::graphPaper is a 3ds Max texture that generates graph paper procedurally. Instead of using an image, it constructs the grid from horizontal and vertical Gradient Ramps, which manage both the tiling and the lines.

Colors are defined by the top mix or the output map, depending on the material version. It is distributed as an .mzp package. The author describes it as a proof of concept for generating and manipulating procedural materials, so it appeals both to people who want a graph paper look and to those studying how procedural materials can be assembled in 3ds Max.

Key features

  • Procedural graph paper texture
  • Horizontal and vertical Gradient Ramps control tiling and lines
  • Colors set by top mix or output map
  • Distributed as an .mzp package
  • Proof of concept for procedural material generation
