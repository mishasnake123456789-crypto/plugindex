What Blendsync - Export To Unreal / Substance Painter And Back does

BlendSync is a Blender extension from Mangoframes that handles round trips that link Blender with Unreal Engine and Substance Painter. It removes much of the manual exporting and importing involved in moving a model through texturing and into a game engine, and back again for edits.

It works with both Cycles and Eevee and supports Blender 4.4 through 5.1. The extension is released under the MIT license, and comes with documentation and product updates plus support from the developer. It is useful for game artists who iterate between all three tools.

Key features

  • Asset transfer to Unreal Engine and Substance Painter
  • Round trip back into Blender
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
  • Supports Blender 4.4 through 5.1
  • MIT licensed extension
