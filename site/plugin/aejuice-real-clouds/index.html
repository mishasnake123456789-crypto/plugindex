What AEJuice Real Clouds does

Real Clouds is a footage library of clouds filmed in different weather and in various seasons, intended for replacing skies in movies, CGI, music videos and television. It works in After Effects, Cinema 4D, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and many other editors.

The collection has 23 timelapse clips in 6K of 10 seconds each, 31 fast clouds in 4K of 5 seconds, 31 real-time clouds in 4K of 10 seconds and 28 timelapse clouds in full HD running 10 to 50 seconds. Files are H264 with bitrates up to 240 Mbps.

Key features

  • 113 captured cloud clips
  • 6K and 4K timelapse and real-time options
  • Fast clouds and full HD timelapses
  • H264 up to 240 Mbps
  • Recorded in varied weather and seasons
