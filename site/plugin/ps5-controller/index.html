Blender add-on that lets a PS5 gamepad navigate the viewport, move objects and control cameras.

What Ps5 Controller does

PS5 Controller adds native gamepad support to Blender, so artists and animators can move around and manipulate scenes with sticks and buttons instead of mouse and keyboard.

The left stick positions objects and the right stick rotates them, with axis-based navigation and adjustable deadzone. Circle jumps to camera view and L1 returns to normal perspective, and cameras can be controlled directly. Speed, turn sensitivity, boost multiplier and custom speed presets are all adjustable. It is a comfortable option for reviewing scenes away from the desk.

Key features

  • Dual stick control for movement and rotation
  • Adjustable deadzone
  • Button mapping for camera view and perspective
  • Direct camera object control
  • Custom speed and sensitivity presets
