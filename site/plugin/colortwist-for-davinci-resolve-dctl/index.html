What ColorTwist for Davinci Resolve (DCTL) does

ColorTwist is a look-development kit for DaVinci Resolve made of four DCTLs from Nx Color. Together they let a colorist push specific colors around, rather than moving the whole image with broad controls.

Nx ColorWarp moves one hue toward another, Nx Saturation and Nx Luma adjust the strength and brightness of any chosen color, and Nx Density deepens colors for a filmic feel. Version 2.0 builds on the shared DCTLs Common library and works on macOS, Windows and Linux. The bundle runs in Resolve 16 through 20, which suits grading teams on mixed setups.

Key features

  • Nx ColorWarp for precise hue-to-hue shifts
  • Nx Saturation per hue or color
  • Nx Luma for brightness of selected colors
  • Nx Density for filmic color depth
  • Resolve 16 through 20 on macOS, Windows, Linux
