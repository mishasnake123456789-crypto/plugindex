What Eye Animation does

Eye Animation, by Affe_mit_Waffe, simplifies animating a character's eyes in Blender. From panels in the Dope Sheet, it keyframes looks, blinks, winks and squints over frames you choose.

In Pose Mode an eyedropper button saves the eye-bone positions for each direction, and those positions are reused when keys are set. Timing presets for fast, medium and slow behavior shape how quickly an action plays, giving animators a head start on lively eyes.

Key features

  • Keys looks, blinks, winks and squints
  • Panels available in the Dope Sheet
  • Eyedropper saves eye-bone positions per direction
  • Fast, medium and slow timing presets
