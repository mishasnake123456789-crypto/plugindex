What Chaos Corona 9 does

Chaos Corona 9 is an unbiased and biased photorealistic renderer for 3ds Max, built around ease of use. Interactive LightMix lets you rebalance lighting without re-rendering, even during rendering, and Chaos Scatter creates hundreds to millions of objects such as trees, rocks and grass.

The Chaos Cosmos library provides materials, objects, HDRIs and scatter presets, and denoising can cut render times by up to 75 percent. Interactive rendering reduces test renders, with frame buffer post-processing and fast caustics. Version 9 adds Procedural Clouds, Corona Pattern geometry tiling and out-of-core rendering for Corona Bitmaps.

Key features

  • Interactive LightMix for lighting changes
  • Chaos Scatter for trees, rocks and grass
  • Chaos Cosmos assets and HDRIs
  • Denoising to cut render time up to 75%
  • New Procedural Clouds and Corona Pattern
