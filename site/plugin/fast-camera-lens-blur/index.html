What Fast Camera Lens Blur does

Fast Camera Lens Blur by TumoiYorozu produces out-of-focus blur and bright glow in After Effects and Premiere Pro. It is built for speed and works natively on Apple Silicon Macs, with a notarized macOS build.

Bokeh shape can be chosen, including 12-sided and 16-sided options, giving different aperture characters. Multi-Frame Rendering is supported in After Effects. Compatibility covers After Effects CS6 through 2025 and Premiere Pro CS6 through 2024, so older installations are included as well as current ones.

Key features

  • Combined blur and glow
  • Native Apple Silicon (M1) support
  • Selectable shapes including 12-gon and 16-gon
  • Multi-Frame Rendering in After Effects
  • After Effects CS6-2025, Premiere Pro CS6-2024
