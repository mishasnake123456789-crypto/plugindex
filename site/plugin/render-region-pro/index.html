Controls Blender's render region from the viewport, with snapshot comparison by wipe slider and one-click lookdev toggles.

What Render Region Pro does

Render Region Pro makes region rendering easier to manage. Draw and resize a region directly in the viewport, and in camera view it locks to the camera frame and follows it automatically.

Up to ten snapshots can be stored and compared against the live result with a wipe slider. Six one-click toggles switch lookdev features such as Subdivision, Hair, Instances and a Clay override. Lighting and shading artists testing changes on part of the frame can iterate faster.

Key features

  • Draw and resize a region in the viewport
  • Auto-follow of the camera frame
  • Up to 10 snapshots with wipe-slider comparison
  • Six one-click lookdev toggles
  • Clay override and more
