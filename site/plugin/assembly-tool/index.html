What Assembly Tool does

Assembly Tool gathers a wide range of scripts for 3ds Max into a single place, so artists do not have to hunt through many separate utilities. Its scope runs from smart object transformation and scene assembly to modeling helpers and unwrapping aids.

Further sections cover texture and material handling, bulk renaming of scene objects and materials, plus layer, group and pivot management. For environment work there are tools for painting objects into a scene, arrays, scattering and randomization, plus optimization and physics helpers. It suits artists who prefer one collection covering the routine chores of building and organizing scenes rather than a single specialized function.

Key features

  • Smart transform and manipulation of objects
  • Scene assembly, modeling and unwrapping helpers
  • Texture and material tools with batch renaming
  • Layer, group and pivot management
  • Object painting, scattering, arrays and randomization
