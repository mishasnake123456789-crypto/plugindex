After Effects tool that adds safe-zone guides for social videos, ads and thumbnails using platform presets.

What Safest Zone does

Safest Zone places safe-zone guides in After Effects compositions so titles and key graphics stay clear of interface overlays. It includes built-in presets for popular social platforms, which means a guide is a few clicks away.

For client-specific or unusual delivery formats you can build reusable custom presets, rename them inline, color their names and reorder them by dragging. Several safe-zone variants are available, with quick preview and apply options plus bulk styling and updating of existing guides. It suits anyone laying out vertical video, ads and thumbnails.

Key features

  • Built-in presets for popular social platforms
  • Reusable custom presets
  • Multiple safe-zone variants
  • Quick preview and apply
  • Bulk styling and updating of guides
