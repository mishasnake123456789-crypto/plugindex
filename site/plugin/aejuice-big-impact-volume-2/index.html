What AEJuice Big Impact Volume 2 does

Big Impact Volume 2 provides 102 ready-made impact animations for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. Each is a short clip, under a second, rendered at 3840 by 2160 in horizontal orientation.

They are delivered as ProRes 4444 MOV files with RGB and alpha, in 30 and 50 FPS options. The After Effects range covers CC 2015 to CC 2024 and Premiere Pro is supported. A standalone Pack Manager works without any video editor, and its interface is available in nine languages including English, Japanese, Spanish and Korean.

Key features

  • 102 pre-rendered impact animations
  • 4K, under one second each
  • ProRes 4444 with alpha channel
  • 30 and 50 FPS options
  • Standalone Pack Manager in nine languages
