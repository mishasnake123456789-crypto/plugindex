What Generation Ai: State-Of-The-Art 3D Generator does

Generation AI adds AI-based 3D creation to Blender. It produces meshes automatically and can generate images and corresponding meshes in different styles with little effort.

It is distributed as a Blender extension under the GPL license for Blender 4.3 and 4.2, with documentation and a demo video. It is suited to quick concept models, blockouts and experimentation when you want to explore ideas without modeling every piece manually. Notable points: AI mesh generation inside Blender, image and mesh generation in multiple styles and blender 4.2 and 4.3 extension.
