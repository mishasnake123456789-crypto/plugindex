What A.N.T.Landscape does

A.N.T.Landscape generates terrain meshes in Blender from procedural parameters. It used to be bundled with Blender 4.1 and is now offered as it stands, with limited support, for Blender 4.2 LTS and newer.

Its controls sit in the 3D viewport sidebar under the Create tab, and a displace function accompanies the landscape generator for modifying existing meshes. The add-on needs no special permissions and is distributed under GPL v2.0 or later. Environment artists wanting quick mountains and rough ground as a starting point can generate them in moments.

Key features

  • Generates landscape meshes
  • Includes a displace function
  • Found in sidebar Create tab
  • For Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
