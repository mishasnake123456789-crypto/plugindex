What FanFilmFX Blaster Bolt does

FanFilmFX Blaster Bolt is a plug-in built on Noise Industries' FxFactory that supplies laser-bolt style visuals. It works in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and also in Final Cut Pro X and Motion.

It runs on both Apple Silicon and Intel processors, and the requirements are the same whether you use the free FxFactory or the paid FxFactory Pro. A demo video is available. Fan filmmakers and editors adding space-gun shots or sci-fi action to footage can apply it in the host they already use.
