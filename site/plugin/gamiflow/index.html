Blender add-on that generates low-poly, high-poly and game-ready meshes non-destructively, renamed for Substance Painter.

What GamiFlow does

GamiFlow automates game asset preparation in Blender. Mark edges, faces or modifiers that should be optimised out, then create baking meshes using the Make Low and Make High buttons.

Output is triangulated, optimised and named to follow Substance Painter conventions, all without destroying the source model. Game artists bake detail from high to low poly with less manual duplication and cleanup. Consistent naming also reduces errors when textures are painted and re-imported, because meshes match across tools.

Key features

  • Make Low and Make High buttons
  • Tag edges, faces or modifiers for removal
  • Triangulated and optimised output
  • Names follow Substance Painter conventions
