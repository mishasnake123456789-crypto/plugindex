Blender add-on from MACHIN3 for flexible poly and NURBS curve editing, including non-destructive Blendulate radii.

What Curvemachine does

CURVEmachine adds powerful editing to Blender's curves. Its Blendulate tool creates, edits or undoes radii, arcs and non-circular blends in an essentially non-destructive way.

Further tools drop a one-point poly curve at the cursor to extrude from, flick-symmetrize a spline across the object origin or cursor, knife-cut points into splines or split them, and merge to the last selected point or center. Modelers using curves for wires, rails, trim or logos get cleaner shapes.

Key features

  • Blendulate for radii, arcs and blends
  • Single-point curve at the cursor
  • Flick Symmetrize across origin or cursor
  • Knife cut and merge for splines
