What LumiMakr Video does

LumiMakr Video is a plugin for After Effects and Premiere Pro that builds high dynamic range looks by stacking images captured at the same exposure. The aim is to hold on to information in both highlights and shadows of a scene.

The resulting visuals are more balanced and detailed, and the method is meant to work under many lighting conditions. It is pitched at videographers and amateur creators who want a better dynamic range without elaborate grading setups.

Key features

  • HDR through same-exposure image stacking
  • Retains bright and dark detail
  • More balanced, detail-rich visuals
  • Works with After Effects and Premiere Pro
