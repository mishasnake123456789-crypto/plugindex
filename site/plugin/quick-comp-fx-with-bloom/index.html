What Quick Comp Fx With Bloom does

Quick Comp Fx With Bloom puts post-processing in a simple panel in Blender's Render tab, so effects are enabled with checkboxes instead of a hand-built compositor graph. Parameters can still be adjusted afterwards.

It offers bloom with threshold, strength and size that work in the viewport and the final render, color correction for exposure, gamma, contrast and saturation, chromatic aberration with separate RGB channel control, and sharpening with strength and radius. A cartoon-style Kuwahara filter smooths while preserving edges. Artists wanting finishing touches on renders quickly will find it convenient.

Key features

  • Bloom with threshold, strength and size
  • Color correction controls
  • Chromatic aberration per RGB channel
  • Sharpen and Kuwahara cartoon smoothing
