What Survey Points Exporter - Geodetic & Construction Workflow does

Survey Points Exporter serves surveyors and construction teams who need coordinates from a model. It writes mesh vertices and the locations of Empty objects as real-world coordinates.

Axis orientation can be set to East, West, North or South, reference point offsets applied, and an optional Z-axis rotation added. Output is TXT or CSV, taken from the active mesh or selection. It makes it practical to carry setting-out points from Blender into survey tools.

Key features

  • Exports vertices and Empty locations
  • Custom East/West/North/South axes
  • Reference point offsets and optional Z rotation
  • TXT or CSV output
  • Real-world coordinates
