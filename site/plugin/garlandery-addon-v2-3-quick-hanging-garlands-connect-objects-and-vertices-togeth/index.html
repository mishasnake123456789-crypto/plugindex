What Garlandery (Addon) V2.3 | Quick Hanging Garlands | Connect Objects And Vertices Together, Control Them From Outside does

Garlandery connects objects, or selected vertices on them, with hanging curve garlands in Blender. The garlands stay attached as objects move, making strings of lights, bunting, ropes and cables easy to arrange.

Eleven geometry nodes modifiers are available from a side panel for fast access and combining. The workflow is non-destructive, so applied properties can be changed later, and it works with any geometry nodes modifier that modifies a curve, beyond those included. Scene dressers, festive scene creators and archviz artists will find it more efficient than sculpting sag by hand.

Key features

  • Stay in place when objects move
  • 11 geometry nodes modifiers in a side panel
