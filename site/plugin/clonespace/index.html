What CloneSpace does

CloneSpace from grape design is an After Effects plugin that takes one layer and multiplies it across three-dimensional space. It runs on the GPU for responsive previews.

Copies can be laid out in flexible grids, varied with randomization tools, and offset with time controls. Cropping and depth of field are included for added realism. Motion designers can use it to build walls of images, repeating patterns and deep, layered arrangements without duplicating layers by hand.

Key features

  • Duplicates layers throughout 3D space
  • Flexible grid layouts
  • Randomization tools for variation
  • Time control for clones
  • Cropping and depth of field
