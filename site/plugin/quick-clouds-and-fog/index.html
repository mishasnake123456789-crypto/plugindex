What Quick Clouds & Fog does

Quick Clouds & Fog helps artists build atmospheric effects in Blender without crafting volumes from scratch. It ships with more than eighty presets applied in one click, plus ten ready-made scenes useful for learning how the effects are put together.

You can make anything from a single cloud to a whole array, along with fog types such as cosmic nebulae. Three noise types can be layered, and a global mask adds extra control. It is designed for the EEVEE engine and works in animated scenes, with a documentation section offering detailed instructions. Landscape, space and environment artists get atmosphere quickly.

Key features

  • 80+ one-click presets
  • 10 ready-made training scenes
  • From single clouds to cloud arrays and nebulae
  • Three layered noise types and a global mask
  • Designed for EEVEE and animation
