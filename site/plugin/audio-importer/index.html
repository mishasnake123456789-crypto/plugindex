What Audio Importer does

GhostVertex's Audio Importer brings music and sound files into Blender and bakes them into ten frequency ranges that every node editor can use.

An All Frequency Node displays the bands, and individual ones are reached via minimum and maximum sliders. The audio file is placed in the video sequencer for playback, and the timeline end is adjusted to the length of the sound. Motion designers can drive materials, geometry and animation from music.

Key features

  • Bakes audio into 10 frequency ranges
  • All Frequency Node display
  • Min and max sliders per frequency
  • Available in all node editors
  • Adds audio to the sequencer
