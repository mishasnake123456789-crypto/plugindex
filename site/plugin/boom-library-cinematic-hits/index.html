What BOOM Library Cinematic Hits does

BOOM Library Cinematic Hits is a royalty-free collection aimed at trailers, teasers, presentations and IDs, usable in After Effects, Premiere Pro or any software that handles WAV files. Material includes crashes, drums, cymbals, metal, wood and rumbles shaped into hits, impacts, sub booms and endings.

The Construction Kit has more than 1,800 source sounds left as raw as possible for designing hits from nothing, while the Designed edition is ready to use. A bundle contains both at a reduced price. Every file carries embedded metadata, and the pack belongs to the Cinematic Series, so it can stand alone or be combined with others.
