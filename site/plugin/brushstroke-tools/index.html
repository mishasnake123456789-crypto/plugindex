What Brushstroke Tools does

Brushstroke Tools gives Blender a painterly 3D look by creating and managing layers of procedural brushstrokes. Developed by Blender Studio, it supports stylized looks for production assets.

Fill layers flood a surface with strokes, while Draw layers place strokes as curve geometry directly on a mesh. A detailed Geometry Nodes system generates everything, and Fill layers follow flow curves, or edges and faces you mark to steer or interrupt the strokes. Stylized animation and illustration artists can produce hand-painted renders with full control.

Key features

  • Fill and Draw brushstroke layers
  • Procedural Geometry Nodes generation
  • Flow curves and marked edges guide strokes
  • Layer management and editing
