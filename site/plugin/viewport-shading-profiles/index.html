What Viewport Shading Profiles does

Viewport Shading Profiles lets you store how your viewport looks and recall it later. The profiles are found on the View tab of the 3D viewport.

Each captures lighting, color, contour and wireframe settings, and you get an instant preview of how models appear under the different setups. Profiles can be organized across scenes, projects or purposes. Modelers and presenters who flip between clay, flat and detailed views will save repeated tweaking.

Key features

  • Create, organize and switch profiles
  • Lighting, color, contour and wireframe settings
  • Instant preview
  • Per-project customization
