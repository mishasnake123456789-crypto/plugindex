What TypeMonkey 3D does

TypeMonkey 3D is an add-on from Ebberts + Zucker for people who already use TypeMonkey for kinetic typography. It takes the text created by TypeMonkey and converts it to three dimensions within seconds.

The environment light source is set automatically. TypeMonkey must be installed, and After Effects 2024 or later is required, with 2024, 2025 and 2026 listed as compatible. It lets animated type gain depth and lighting without rebuilding the project from scratch.

Key features

  • Converts TypeMonkey text to 3D in seconds
  • Automatic environment light source
  • Requires TypeMonkey
  • Needs After Effects 2024 or later
