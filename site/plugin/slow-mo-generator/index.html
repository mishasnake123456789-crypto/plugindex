Blender add-on that creates slow-motion character animation as a new Action without altering your keyframes.

What Slow-Mo Generator does

Retiming animation by editing keys is risky. Slow-Mo Generator, available from the Render Properties tab, leaves your source animation alone and writes the slowed result into a fresh Action named with a _SlowMo suffix.

Enter the range to affect and a speed factor, such as 0.3 for a third of normal speed, and set how many frames ease in and out. A smoothstep remap makes tempo changes flow without sudden jumps, and one click performs the bake. Fits action beats, impact moments and dramatic emphasis.

Key features

  • Non-destructive bake to a _SlowMo Action
  • Start frame, end frame and slow factor
  • Customizable transition in and out
  • Smoothstep time remapping
  • One-click bake
