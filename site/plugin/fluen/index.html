Creates timed captions from sequence audio and translates caption tracks into more than 50 languages in Premiere Pro.

What Fluen does

Fluen adds AI-based captioning and subtitle translation to Premiere Pro. Audio from the sequence is transcribed in the cloud into accurately timed captions, and the spoken language is detected automatically.

Existing or new caption tracks can be translated into more than 50 languages, with segmentation and cue timing adjusted for each. Settings cover characters per line, lines per caption, reading speed and speaker labels, and a custom glossary keeps names, brands and technical terms consistent. Editors who deliver video in several languages can handle captions without leaving the project.

Key features

  • Cloud transcription with language detection
  • Translation into 50+ languages
  • Controls for line length, reading speed and speakers
  • Custom glossary for names and terms
  • Works on new or existing caption tracks
