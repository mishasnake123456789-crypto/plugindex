What AEJuice Countdown does

AEJuice Countdown is a ready-made countdown sequence for After Effects, Premiere Pro and other editors. In the Adobe apps you can edit text layers, place a logo in the placeholders and recolour it with Color Controls; for other editors it's supplied as video with a transparent or black background.

It works at up to 4K and adjusts automatically for horizontal, square, vertical and in-between formats. Any font can be used, and most animations install fonts for you, and the AEJuice Pack Manager gives fast preview, download, import and favorites.

Key features

  • Editable text, logo placeholders, colour controls
  • Video versions for other editors
  • Up to 4K
  • Auto-adjusts to horizontal, square and vertical
  • Works with any fonts
