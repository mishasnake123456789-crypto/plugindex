What G-Mesh - Grease Pencil Retopology does

G-Mesh turns Grease Pencil strokes into a clean mesh. Draw your retopology lines over a model; the add-on places a point wherever two strokes cross, joins those points with edges and then fills the gaps with faces.

This drawing-based approach feels natural for artists who prefer sketching topology flow over placing vertices one at a time. It was created by Ryan Gonzalez and suits character and organic retopology work.

Key features

  • Draw retopology with Grease Pencil
  • Points added at stroke crossings
  • Automatic edges and face filling
  • Optimized mesh output
