What Flatcut does

Flatcut, by ITAMOTO-SHINTARO, prepares wood parts for fabrication by exporting them as G-code or SVG for laser and CNC machines that cut in two dimensions. It is tagged under Import-Export and Object.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 5.0.0 or newer.

Key features

  • G-code export
  • SVG export
  • For laser and CNC 2D cutting
