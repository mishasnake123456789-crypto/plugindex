What LayerLUT Exporter does

LayerLUT Exporter, from NT Productions, creates lookup tables out of the look applied to a layer in After Effects. If you have graded footage with effects, you can capture that look as a LUT and reuse it elsewhere.

It works with After Effects versions from CC through 2025. Version 1.1 fixed a major bug that occurred when exporting a LUT without full resolution, and a demo video is available from the developer. Colorists and editors can use it to move grades between applications.

Key features

  • Exports LUTs from any layer
  • Compatible with After Effects CC through 2025
  • Version 1.1 fixes lower-resolution export bug
  • Demo video available
