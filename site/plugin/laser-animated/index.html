What Laser Animated does

Blender 4.2 dropped the Bloom effect many artists relied on for glow. studioromanrec's Laser Animated download packs two add-ons that address this: the laser tool itself, plus a Bloom add-on that brings the effect back.

Bloom can accompany the lasers or be used separately, and both operate in EEVEE as well as Cycles. The package is marked compatible with the Blender extension format and has documentation. Good for sci-fi beams, neon highlights and any shot that needs a soft glow.
