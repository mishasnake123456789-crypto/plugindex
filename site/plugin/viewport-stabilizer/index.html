What Viewport stabilizer does

Viewport stabilizer is a Blender add-on by Yaroslav_Yushk that holds the viewport fixed on a chosen 3D point while you work. It is built for matchmovers and others dealing with tracked shots, where moving camera shots make precise placement difficult.

You can stabilize on the 3D Cursor, on the pivot of the active or a specified object, or on selected vertices if enabled in the settings. One click finds the center of an object or selected vertices and creates an Empty there. Alt+C toggles stabilization, and the controls are in the View tab of the 3D View sidebar.

Key features

  • Stabilizes on the 3D Cursor, object pivots or vertices
  • Creates an Empty at an object or vertex center
  • Alt+C toggle, reassignable
  • Controls in the 3D View sidebar's View tab
  • Aimed at matchmove and tracked footage
