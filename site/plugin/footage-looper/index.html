What Footage Looper does

Footage Looper, by Leyero, repeats a clip with a single click, avoiding the work of copying and trimming segments over and over. Use it to extend short clips, backgrounds or b-roll to the length you need.

The initial 1.0.0 version came out in November 2024, it is free, and it supports Premiere Pro 2021 through 2025. A demo video is available. It is useful for extending short clips such as backgrounds or b-roll to fit a longer shot.
