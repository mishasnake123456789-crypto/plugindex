What Tenji P-Fab:Japanese Braille does

Tenji P-Fab, by Kaname-Endo, places Japanese braille on 3D models by projecting text onto their surfaces, which suits tactile and printed objects. It is listed under Add Mesh.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.
