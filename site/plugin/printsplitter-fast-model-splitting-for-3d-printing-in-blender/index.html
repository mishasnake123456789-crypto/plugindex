Blender add-on that cuts large meshes into evenly sized parts along chosen axes so they fit a printer's build volume.

What Printsplitter - Fast Model Splitting For 3D Printing In Blender does

PrintSplitter divides oversized models into printable sections without manual boolean work. In the 3D View sidebar you choose how many cuts to make and along which axis, check the slicing planes in a live preview, then generate the parts.

Cuts can run along X, Y or Z, with multi-axis slicing, and are spaced evenly from the object's bounding box. Overshoot boolean cuts help keep the results reliable. It suits sculptures, mechanical parts, cosplay props and terrain pieces that are bigger than a resin or FDM printer's build limits.

Key features

  • Even splitting into multiple printable parts
  • Cuts along X, Y or Z with multi-axis support
  • Live preview of cut locations
  • Bounding-box alignment for even spacing
  • Overshoot boolean cuts for reliable results
