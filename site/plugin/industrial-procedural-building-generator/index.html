What Industrial Procedural Building Generator does

This add-on from Quad Lab generates complex industrial architecture from a plain base shape, so repeated parts such as pipes and structural details do not have to be modeled by hand.

Controls live in the N-Panel and nothing is baked in, so reshaping the base mesh updates the whole structure. The generation follows your own silhouette or blockout. It is suited to environment artists building factories, mechanical structures and sprawling cityscapes where large quantities of believable detail are needed.

Key features

  • Procedural non-destructive workflow
  • Controls in the N-Panel
  • Builds detail around custom blockouts
  • Industrial buildings, machinery and city layouts
