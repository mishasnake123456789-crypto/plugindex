What Zoetrope FLOW does

Zoetrope FLOW is a generative design effect for After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve. It takes whatever video sits in your timeline and wraps it onto smoothly curving shapes in 3D space. It grew out of the earlier FOLD plugin.

Motion can run automatically through built-in behaviors or be driven by a looping manual slider. Sliders for resolution, growth, complexity and scale reshape the form, and three raster modes decide how the source maps across it. Designers and live visual artists use it for abstract backdrops and eye-catching motion pieces.

Key features

  • Auto or manual looping animation
  • Resolution, growth, complexity and scale sliders
  • Direct, Tiled and Streaked raster modes
  • Per-axis 3D rotation and translation
  • All parameters keyframeable
