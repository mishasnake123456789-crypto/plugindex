A Blender add-on for rectangular nesting of cabinet furniture parts, producing a full cut layout in one click.

What Procutter does

ProCutter optimizes how parts are laid out on sheet material, specifically for cabinet and furniture design done in Blender. From a model, it produces a full cut of all parts with one click and takes into account the position of textures on each part, so grain direction is respected.

Offcuts are calculated on nesting layouts, either automatically, inside a specified area or edited by hand with the mouse, and offcuts can be cut first in a new order. Layouts can be edited quickly with the mouse, including rotation. Furniture designers and woodworkers planning material use will benefit.

Key features

  • Full cut of all parts in one click
  • Respects texture positions on parts
  • Offcut calculation, automatic or manual
  • Priority cutting of offcuts
  • Quick mouse layout editing and rotation
