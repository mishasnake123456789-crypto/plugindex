What QuickPortals does

QuickPortals, by Marcos Medel, creates portal transitions in After Effects in a few clicks. Instead of building ring and distortion effects manually, you choose a style and apply it to your footage.

Three styles are included: NEON, SIGIL and PIXEL, each with its own presets and complete customization. It is compatible with After Effects 2024 and 2025, and a demo video is available. Editors can use it for magical, sci-fi or gaming-flavored scene changes.

Key features

  • Three portal styles: NEON, SIGIL and PIXEL
  • Presets for each style
  • Fully customizable options
  • Portal transitions in a few clicks
