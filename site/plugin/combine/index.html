What Combine does

Combine is a small Blender add-on for people who merge objects often. Rather than pressing a series of keys to finish the operation, a single click joins the selected objects into one.

The add-on comes with documentation and is distributed under the GPL license. It supports Blender 3.3 to 4.3 and is part of the developer's Blender add-on bundles. Modelers who repeatedly assemble parts into single meshes, such as when preparing assets for export or boolean work, can save a little time on every merge.

Key features

  • One-click merging of objects
  • Replaces the usual key sequence
  • Documentation included
  • GPL licensed
  • Works with Blender 3.3 through 4.3
