What AeJuice CRT Effect does

AeJuice CRT Effect gives modern video the appearance of an old television or computer monitor. A set of adjustable options lets you reach the classic aesthetic in just a few clicks.

It runs in After Effects CC 2015 or newer and Premiere Pro CC 2021 or newer, and works with Final Cut Pro 10.4.9 or later. Other editors, including Sony Vegas, DaVinci Resolve, Avid and OBS, can use the transparent or black-background video versions. It works on Windows 10+ and macOS 10.14+, including M1 and M2, and before-and-after demo videos are available.
