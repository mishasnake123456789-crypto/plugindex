Replaces Blender's Q menu with a mode-aware pie menu for modifiers, modeling tools and materials.

What Quick Pie & Favorites does

Quick Pie & Favorites swaps the default Q Quick Favorites menu for a customizable pie that changes depending on whether you are in Object or Edit mode.

Smart Modifiers pops up Subdivision, Mirror and Bevel instantly, and a modeling group gives fast access to Extrude, Inset, Loop Cut and Knife. Smart Materials can produce checker patterns, emission shaders or random pastel tints, and an existing material can be reassigned to new objects with one click. A good fit for modelers who prefer gestures to menus.

Key features

  • Mode-aware pie menu on Q
  • Smart Modifiers: Subdivision, Mirror, Bevel
  • Extrude, Inset, Loop Cut and Knife access
  • Smart Materials: checker, emission, pastel colors
