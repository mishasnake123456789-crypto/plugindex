Generates TMT steel reinforcement models in Blender from predefined specifications, with naming schemes and batch processing.

What Akku does

Akku automates the creation of TMT (Thermo-Mechanically Treated) steel reinforcement models, a common need in structural and architectural visualization. Models are produced from predefined specifications rather than modeled bar by bar.

User-defined naming schemes keep large projects consistent, and batch processing generates many models in one go. It is aimed at engineers, architects and 3D modelers who need rebar geometry quickly and in an organized form. The predefined specifications also help keep naming and dimensions consistent across large structural scenes.

Key features

  • Automated TMT reinforcement generation
  • Predefined specifications
  • Custom naming schemes
  • Batch processing
  • For engineers, architects and modelers
