What Advanced Mask Editor 2 does

Advanced Mask Editor 2 by Nataworkstudio gives After Effects users a panel that lists every mask set on the selected layer. From there you can merge mask paths, mirror and replicate them, and open or close paths with ease.

It also works on animated masks: two can be merged into one, or an animated element copied, flipped or mirrored. Sixty animated presets are included. Artists who rely heavily on masks for roto, reveals and shapes get a central place to manage them.

Key features

  • Panel listing all mask sets on a layer
  • Merge mask paths
  • Mirror and replicate paths
  • Open and close paths easily
  • 60 animated presets, works with animated masks
