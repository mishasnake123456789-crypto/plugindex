What Quad Splitter does

Quad Splitter, by Rezuwen, speeds up splitting quads and certain n-gons, which helps when you need to adjust edge flow without rebuilding an area. It is listed under Mesh.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Fast topology splits
  • Supports quads and certain n-gons
