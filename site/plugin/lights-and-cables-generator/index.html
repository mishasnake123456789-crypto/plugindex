What Lights And Cables Generator does

Lights And Cables Generator uses Geometry Nodes to build strings of lights, wires and veins. It works in Blender 4.0, 4.1 and 4.2+, in both Cycles and Eevee.

Subdivision level, number of lights, light size and random light counts are adjustable, lights can be randomly rotated and wires randomized on XYZ. Wire radius, scale and twist are set directly, and a custom object selector allows your own bulbs or parts. Environment artists wanting tangled cables or festive strings avoid hand modeling.

Key features

  • Lights, cables and veins in one generator
  • Controls for light count, size and randomness
  • Wire radius, scale and twist
  • Custom object selector
