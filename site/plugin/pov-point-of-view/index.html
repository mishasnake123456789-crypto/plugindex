What Pov: Point Of View does

POV: Point Of View lets you explore a 3D world from the inside in Blender. You move through it with controls you can adjust.

Gravity, jump height, walk speed and view height are configurable, so you can experience the scene at different scales, from a small animal to a giant. Documentation and a demo video are included.

Key features

  • First-person scene exploration
  • Adjustable gravity and jump height
  • Walk speed and view height
  • Experience scenes at different scales
