What Elastic Comp Changer does

Elastic Comp Changer by Mathieu Glatigny is an After Effects script for lengthening or shortening a composition without breaking its timing. Plus and minus buttons add or remove frames from the current duration.

Keyframes after the playhead can be offset, as can layer and comp markers, and changes can apply to locked as well as unlocked layers, including keyframes in inactive layers. Layer start times after the playhead can be modified, In and Out points can move while keeping start times, and an options window adjusts behaviour. Nested pre-comps are updated too. Animators retiming a finished piece will avoid manually dragging every keyframe.

Key features

  • Add or remove frames with + and - buttons
  • Offset keyframes and markers after the playhead
  • Handles locked layers and inactive layer keyframes
  • Modify layer start times and In/Out points
  • Updates nested pre-comps
