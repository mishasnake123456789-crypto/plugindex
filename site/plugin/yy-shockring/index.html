What YY_ShockRing does

YY_ShockRing is a ring-drawing plugin by Andrew Yang for After Effects. It covers shockwaves, impact pulses, HUD circles and any design that needs a clean or soft-edged ring.

Center point, radius, expansion for thickness, feather for edge softness and opacity are all adjustable. Two color settings tint the outer and inner parts, and blend modes include None, Normal, Add, Stencil Multiply and Multiply+Alpha. A scatter parameter adds noise to the ring, and smooth interpolation is available instead of linear blending. It supports Multi-Frame Rendering and runs natively on Apple Silicon.

Key features

  • Center, radius, thickness, feather and opacity
  • Two colors for outer and inner areas
  • Multiple blend modes including Add and Multiply
  • Scatter noise parameter
  • Multi-Frame Rendering and Apple Silicon support
