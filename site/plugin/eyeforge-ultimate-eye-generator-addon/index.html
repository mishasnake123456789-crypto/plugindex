What Eyeforge (Ultimate Eye Generator Addon does

EyeForge builds character eyes in Blender with plenty of control. Thirty iris types cover Realistic, Stylized and Anime looks, each with ten presets that switch in one click.

Eye color uses a slider or custom gradient, with sclera color and vein intensity adjustable, as are iris edge hardness, bump detail and displacement. Pupils can be reshaped and resized, the iris and cornea bulge adjusted, and an oval iris button gives anime proportions. Fake shadows and ten fake reflections live in the shader, while the lacrimal caruncle and tear line arrive as their own objects. It works with Eevee and Cycles from the N panel.

Key features

  • 30 iris types across three styles
  • Color gradients and vein controls
  • Pupil, iris and cornea shape settings
  • Fake shadows and reflections in the shader
  • Eevee and Cycles support
