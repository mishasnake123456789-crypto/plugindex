What Red Giant - Annual Subscription does

Red Giant is MAXON's family of plugins for motion graphics, particles, light effects and colour work. The annual subscription covers After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Cinema 4D and Autograph, with listed support for the 2025 and 2026 Adobe releases and Resolve 19 and 20.

Trapcode Particular provides over 355 presets and audio-driven particles, Form adds more than 150, and Mir builds fractal terrains. Shine adds volumetric light that respects a 3D camera, while 3D Stroke offers 40 preset shapes and Tao draws geometric forms and ribbons. Magic Bullet colour tools sit in the same bundle.

Key features

  • Trapcode Form, Mir terrains and Shine volumetric light
  • 3D Stroke with 40 preset shapes, plus Tao
