A Blender procedural add-on for brick, stone and masonry walls aimed at architecture and ArchViz.

What Wall It does

Wall It generates walls procedurally in Blender, covering brick, stone and masonry styles, and architectural elements such as fences and construction. The parametric approach lets walls be altered without remodeling.

It is a documented, GPL-licensed Blender extension supporting Blender 4.0 through 5.0 and works with both Cycles and Eevee. Architectural visualization artists, game environment modelers and anyone building buildings or boundaries will find it quicker than assembling each brick or stone by hand.

Key features

  • Procedural brick, stone and masonry walls
  • Covers fences and construction elements
  • Cycles and Eevee support
  • Blender 4.0 to 5.0 extension
  • GPL licensed with documentation
