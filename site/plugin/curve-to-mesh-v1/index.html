What Curve To Mesh_V1+ does

NENGHUO Studio's Curve To Mesh_V1+ lets you sketch a curve in Blender and get geometry out of it, using Geometry Nodes under the hood. The studio highlights Chrometype and Acid Graphics, the swirly metallic lettering styles, as its strongest use.

A tutorial video covers installation and use, and documentation is included. Support spans Blender 3.2 to 4.3. Poster designers and motion artists who build bold abstract or typographic forms can shape them by drawing instead of box modeling.

Key features

  • Geometry Nodes based curve modeling
  • Build meshes by drawing curves
  • Suited to Chrometype and Acid Graphics
  • Tutorial video included
  • Blender 3.2 to 4.3
