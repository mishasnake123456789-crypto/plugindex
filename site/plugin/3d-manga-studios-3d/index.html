What 3D Manga Studios / 3D漫画スタジオ does

3D Manga Studios, also titled in Japanese, prepares Blender scenes for manga and comic-style cel rendering. Its goal is to take a creator from camera setup through to screen tones with minimal manual configuration.

One click builds an organized collection hierarchy for effects, characters, background, lighting and camera. Camera tools handle perspective or orthographic switching, resolution presets and safe-area guides. A three-point light rig comes with intensity control and light linking. The Manga Tone Shader and Solidify-based outlines apply to selected objects, and tone textures and AO masks load from the Asset Browser. It suits beginners and doujin or indie creators.

Key features

  • Auto-built production collection structure
  • Camera presets, safe areas and layout guides
  • One-click three-point lighting with light linking
  • Manga tone shader with outline generation
  • Asset Browser texture loading
