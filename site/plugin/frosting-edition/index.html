Scatters instances in Blender with rigid body physics for natural gravity, collisions and settling, in four modes.

What Frosting Edition does

Frosting Edition distributes objects by letting them fall and settle instead of placing them randomly. It uses real rigid body simulation, so instances respond to gravity and collisions and end up resting naturally.

Four modes are available: preset, brush, volume grid and tweak. It works with both Cycles and Eevee. This approach suits piled objects such as sprinkles, rocks, leaves or toppings, where a physically plausible arrangement looks more convincing than a random scatter.

Key features

  • Physics-based scattering with rigid bodies
  • Preset, brush, volume grid and tweak modes
  • Gravity, collisions and natural settling
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
