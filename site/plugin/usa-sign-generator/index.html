What Usa Sign Generator does

USA Sign Generator, from GS3D Studios, converts flat sign images into 3D traffic signs inside Blender. Geometry Nodes do the construction, and the add-on handles the setup of images, materials, scale and posts.

You choose the image from the add-on panel and then fine-tune each sign through its Geometry Nodes modifier, adding posts, adjusting scale or changing materials. It works with Cycles and Eevee. Regulatory signs are currently included, with more planned, making it handy for street scenes, driving simulations and architectural visualization.

Key features

  • Turns 4K images into procedural 3D signs
  • Automatic setup of nodes, materials, scale and posts
  • Image selection from the add-on panel
  • Per-sign tweaks in the Geometry Nodes modifier
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
