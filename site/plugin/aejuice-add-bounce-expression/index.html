What AEJuice Add Bounce Expression does

AEJuice Add Bounce Expression is an After Effects script that adds bouncing motion to whichever properties you have selected. Instead of pasting and editing expression code, you run it once and the overshoot behavior is applied.

Amplitude, frequency and decay can be controlled and saved for reuse, and the script integrates with kBar for quick launching. It works with After Effects CC 2015 through CC 2022 and newer. Animators can use it to give position, scale or rotation a lively spring-like finish.

Key features

  • Adds a bounce to selected properties in one click
  • Adjustable amplitude, frequency and decay
  • Settings can be saved
  • kBar integration
  • Works with CC 2015 and newer
