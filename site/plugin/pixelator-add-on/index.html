What Pixelator Add-On does

Pixelator Add-On converts models into a pixelated look entirely procedurally, with full control over the appearance of the pixels. Setup is simple and intuitive.

Your own voxel assets can be instanced through a custom geometry option, giving finished-looking results. Supported renderers include Cycles, Eevee, Arnold, Octane and V-Ray. Game artists, motion designers and stylized render makers who want a retro or blocky look from regular 3D models can use it.

Key features

  • Instant mesh-to-pixel-art conversion
  • Fully procedural and customizable
  • Instance your own voxel assets
  • Simple setup
  • Works with Cycles, Eevee, Arnold, Octane and V-Ray
