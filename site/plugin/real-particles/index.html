What Real Particles does

Real Particles is a viewport-driven particle module for Blender with six tools built around an emitter setup. Particle Pile emits disk particles each frame with custom collisions so they build up into piles.

Reactor Particles spawn secondary particles on impact, sticky particles attach to surfaces, and further tools cover curve following, clusters and Voronoi collapse. Effects artists get interactive particle behaviors without complex simulation setups. In practice that means particle Pile with custom collisions and reactor particles spawning on impact.

Key features

  • Particle Pile with custom collisions
  • Reactor particles spawning on impact
  • Sticky and curve-following particles
  • Clusters and Voronoi collapse
