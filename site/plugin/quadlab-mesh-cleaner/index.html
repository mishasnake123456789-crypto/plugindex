What Quadlab Mesh Cleaner does

QuadLab Mesh Cleaner prepares dirty geometry for the steps that follow, such as remeshing, export or manual retopology. It begins with analysis, showing diagnostics for stray geometry, fragments, holes, boundaries, non-manifold areas and degenerate faces.

Basic CleanUp removes loose parts, detached fragments and small islands, merges by distance, dissolves degenerate geometry and recalculates normals. Trimming and repair tools complete the toolkit. It is aimed at artists handling photogrammetry scans and rough sculpts.

Key features

  • Visible diagnostics for holes, fragments and non-manifold geometry
  • Basic CleanUp for loose parts and small islands
  • Merge by distance and degenerate dissolve
  • Trim and repair tools
  • Prepares meshes for remeshing or retopology
