Blender add-on that builds keyframed turntable camera animations with elevation steps and hold frames.

What Turntable Camera does

Turntable Camera adds a panel in Blender that generates a keyframed orbit around your subject. It is meant for model demonstrations, sprite sheet and flipbook creation, and product gallery renders.

Rotation steps, start and end angles and offsets are adjustable, elevation can be stepped or linear, and hold frames pause on each position for multi-angle sprites. A root empty lets the rig move away from the origin, Range to Scene matches the render range to the animation, and visualization options display the path.

Key features

  • Auto-generated keyframed orbit
  • Rotation steps with custom start and end angles
  • Stepped or linear elevation
  • Hold Frames for flipbooks and sprites
  • Range to Scene sync
