What Krotos Studio does

Krotos Studio is a sound design tool that works as both a desktop application and a plugin for DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. Rather than searching for a finished sound effect, you shape and perform sounds in real time, then drag the result to your timeline.

Hundreds of presets span Foley, ambiences and whooshes, and the output is royalty-free. Performances can be controlled by mouse, MIDI controller or automation across hundreds of parameters, and multi-channel output lets you pull stems. You can import your own sounds, save presets, and use AI to build a personalised ambience from an image or prompt.

Key features

  • Hundreds of presets: Foley, ambiences, whooshes
  • Perform sounds live and drag to the timeline
  • Mouse, MIDI or automation control
  • Multi-channel stem output
  • AI ambience preset from an image or prompt
