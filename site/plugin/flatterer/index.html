Blender add-on that flattens a 3D model into 2D shapes and writes them to SVG for laser cutters boxes and enclosures.

What Flatterer does

Flatterer converts a 3D model into a group of flat shapes and writes them out as SVG, aiming at laser-cut boxes, enclosures and similar parts designed in Blender.

Two Boolean Cut operators subtract the selected objects from the active one, or the active one from every selected object ones, and the Boolean Cut modifiers are placed ahead of any Solidify modifier. Makers and product designers can prepare cut files without leaving their modeling environment.

Key features

  • Exports flat mesh shapes to SVG
  • Two Boolean Cut operators
  • Boolean modifiers placed before Solidify
  • Aimed at laser cutting designs
