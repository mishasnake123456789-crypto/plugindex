What Overcast HQ Video Review and Collaboration does

Overcast HQ is a panel that connects Premiere Pro to the Overcast cloud video platform. Originals or proxies are kept in your own cloud account and can be shared with colleagues and clients for review.

Technical workflows such as transcoding, subtitle generation and image recognition can be automated. Taxonomy values can be applied to many bin files in one action; cloud assets update immediately through bulk edit, while local files receive taxonomy during upload or project sync.

Key features

  • Store originals or proxies in your cloud account
  • Share with colleagues and clients
  • Automate transcoding, subtitles and image recognition
  • Bulk taxonomy edits across selected files
