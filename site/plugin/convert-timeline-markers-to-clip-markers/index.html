What Convert Timeline markers to clip markers does

Convert Timeline markers to clip markers is a free EditingTools.io utility for Premiere Pro. Markers placed on the sequence timeline stay behind when clips are moved or reused, and this tool relocates them onto the clips themselves so they travel with the media.

It copes with timeline markers, existing clip markers and source markers, and handles markers on audio clips too. Editors who annotate rough cuts on the timeline and later need those notes attached to footage will save time over recreating them one at a time. Premiere Pro 15.0 or later is required.

Key features

  • Transfers timeline markers onto clips
  • Handles clip and source markers
  • Works with markers on audio clips
  • Requires Premiere Pro 15.0 or later
