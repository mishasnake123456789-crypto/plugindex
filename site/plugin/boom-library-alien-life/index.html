What BOOM Library Alien Life does

BOOM Library Alien Life is a sound pack for After Effects and Premiere Pro projects that need otherworldly creatures. It offers snarls, shrieks, growls and chirps at different levels of agitation, along with squishy, slimy, scratchy and crunchy prop sounds.

A Construction Kit of organic source recordings, with no humanoid sounds, lets designers layer elements from different species. The designed side has 12 curated bundles across giant, large, medium and small creatures. Files are 24-bit WAV at 96 to 192 kHz for Windows and Mac and work in many editors and DAWs.

Key features

  • Exotic animal vocalizations in varied states of agitation
  • Slimy, squishy, scratchy and crunchy prop effects
  • Construction Kit of organic, non-humanoid sources
  • 12 designed bundles by creature size
  • 24-bit WAV at 96-192kHz
