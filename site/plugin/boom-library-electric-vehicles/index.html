What BOOM Library Electric Vehicles does

BOOM Library Electric Vehicles gathers 1,396 WAV recordings of electric cars, a scooter, a motorbike and a monowheel, for use in After Effects, Premiere Pro and other tools. Vehicles range from city EVs to a Tesla Roadster.

Onboard audio uses up to 12 channels, including electromagnetic microphones in the cabin, engine bay, trunk and bumpers, with a 3D ambisonic driver perspective. Exteriors are recorded as approach, away, cornering and start/stop passes. Maneuvers cover steady driving, acceleration, deceleration and reversing, and handling sounds include doors, wipers and charging. Format is 24-bit, 96 kHz.

Key features

  • 1,396 recordings of EVs, scooter, motorbike and monowheel
  • Up to 12-channel onboard mics, including electromagnetic
  • Approach, away, cornering and start/stop exterior takes
  • Doors, wipers and charging handling sounds
  • 3D ambisonic driver-position mix
