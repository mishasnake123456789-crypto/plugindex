What Copy Face does

Copy Face, by Blace Plugins, is an AI-driven face swap plugin for After Effects built on blace.ai. It transfers one face onto another in video, and it handles both photorealistic people and stylized characters.

Processing happens entirely on your own machine and is video-native, which avoids the flicker that frame-by-frame swaps often show. Two models are offered: Instant, the default for fast realistic faces and available on macOS and Windows, and Style for stylized subjects. A face-verification system is included as a safeguard against misuse of the technology.

Key features

  • Copies one face onto another
  • Realistic and stylized face support
  • Video-native, flicker-free processing
  • Instant and Style models
  • Face verification safeguard
