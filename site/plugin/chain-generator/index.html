What Chain Generator does

Chain Generator from Liryc Creative Design Studio builds metal chains inside Blender. It includes 60 photorealistic parts and 16 high-quality materials, with a nod to fashion trends such as jewelry looks.

It is built on geometry nodes for open-ended customization, and every part is made at real-world scale. Jewelry, accessory and hardware visualizers can assemble convincing chains without modeling each link.

Key features

  • 60 chain part types
  • 16 materials
  • Geometry nodes customization
  • Real-world scale parts
