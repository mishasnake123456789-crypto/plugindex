What Interlaced Glitch does

Interlaced Glitch is a Benzostudio plugin that gives After Effects footage the worn look of old video cameras. It reproduces true interlacing, scanlines and motion imperfections, from early 2000s camcorders to soft, lo-fi tape visuals.

Controls are not limited to presets, so every aspect of the effect can be fine tuned. The plugin supports Multi-Frame Rendering for efficient performance and works with After Effects 2022 through 2026. It suits nostalgic music videos, found-footage styles and retro title treatments.

Key features

  • Real interlacing and scanlines
  • Motion imperfections from old cameras
  • Full control beyond presets
  • Multi-Frame Rendering support
