Blender add-on for stop motion with keyframe shortcuts, frame skipping and onion skinning.

What Stopmagic does

Aldrin's Art Factory's Stopmagic assists artists who build stop motion poses in Blender. Customizable shortcuts insert keyframes and move around the timeline.

You can hop forward or backward by a set number of frames, with automatic keyframing if you like, and jump to the next or previous frame that holds a keyed mesh. Experimental onion skins display earlier and later frames of the active object in colors you choose. Animators after a stepped, clay-like feel work more tactilely.

Key features

  • Customizable keyframe shortcuts
  • Frame skipping with auto keyframing
  • Jump to keyed frames
  • Experimental onion skins
  • Adjustable onion skin colors
