What Mycel does

Mycel is an N-panel add-on from Sporemind aimed at keeping Blender scenes tidy. Its renamer covers objects, meshes, materials, modifiers, bones and collections, with Find/Replace, Prefix/Suffix, Sequential and Regex modes.

It can act on a selection, the active collection or the whole scene. The same panel adds colour tagging for collections, visibility snapshots you can restore later, and a tool for fixing duplicate names. Studios and solo artists dealing with messy files will find it a convenient single stop.

Key features

  • Batch rename in four modes including Regex
  • Targets objects, meshes, materials, modifiers, bones, collections
  • Collection colour tagging
  • Visibility snapshots
  • Duplicate-name fixing
