A Blender add-on that locks a bone by recomputing the root bone position, handy for pinning feet in mocap.

What Inverse Lock Bone does

Inverse Lock Bone fixes foot sliding in motion capture work in Blender. Instead of keeping the root where it is, it locks a chosen bone in place and recalculates where the master or root bone must be to make that happen.

Four lock types are provided: Head, Tail, Center and Entire. The first three recompute the root location, while Entire also recomputes the locked bone's rotation. The result is keyed on every frame, and those keys can be thinned out in the Graph Editor. It runs in pose mode from the bone properties. Animators cleaning up mocap will find it effective.

Key features

  • Lock types: Head, Tail, Center and Entire
  • Recalculates master or root bone location
  • Entire mode also recomputes rotation
  • Keyframes written per frame, decimate in Graph Editor
  • Runs in pose mode
