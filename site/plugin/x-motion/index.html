What X-Motion does

X-Motion is an extension for After Effects and Premiere Pro, delivered through Creative Cloud. It generates AI images, video clips, music and voiceovers from inside the host app and places the results directly onto the timeline.

It also gives access to free motion graphics templates and unlimited use of TextFlow. Work syncs through a Vidimize account, so editors, motion designers and other creative professionals can pick up assets across sessions. The goal is to cut the trips between separate generation sites and the editing project.

Key features

  • AI image, video, music and voiceover generation
  • Direct import onto the timeline
  • Free motion graphics templates
  • Unlimited TextFlow access
  • Cloud sync via Vidimize account
