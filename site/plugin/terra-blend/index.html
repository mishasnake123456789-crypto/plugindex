What Terra Blend does

Terra Blend, by Blender Procedural, merges two objects into a smooth transition in Blender. It was designed for integrating objects with terrain, such as a rock sitting on uneven ground or a building in a complex landscape.

The interface is simple, it handles many kinds of objects and surfaces, and it renders in Cycles and Eevee. It supports Blender 4.0 through 4.5 and fits game assets, animations and 3D artwork.

Key features

  • Blends any two objects smoothly
  • Made for placing objects on uneven terrain
  • Simple interface
  • Cycles and Eevee support
  • Blender 4.0 through 4.5
