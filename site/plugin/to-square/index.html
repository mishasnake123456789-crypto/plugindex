Blender Edit Mode add-on that turns selected vertices into squares with three alternative methods.

What To Square does

To Square is a Blender add-on by Jean-Yves-Grosbeau that squares up selected vertices right in Edit Mode. It is available from the Sidebar or the context menu and works with any selection.

Three methods are offered: Multi makes one square per selected loop, Around forms squares from the vertices ringing the selection, and Mono produces one square out of everything selected. The square's center can come from the geometric center, the bounding box center or the 3D cursor, and the start corner can be chosen from a vertex, the cursor or a projection.

Key features

  • Multi, Around and Mono squaring methods
  • Works with any selection
  • Center from geometry, bounding box or 3D cursor
  • Selectable start corner
  • Sidebar and context menu access
