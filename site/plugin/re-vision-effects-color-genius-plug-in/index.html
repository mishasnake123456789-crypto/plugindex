What RE:Vision Effects Color Genius (Plug-in) does

Color Genius is a RE:Vision Effects plugin offered for After Effects and Premiere Pro, from CS6 through 2026, and for a broad group of OFX applications including Nuke, Resolve, Fusion Studio, Baselight, Natron, Silhouette, Scratch, HitFilm and Vegas Pro. The same tool can therefore follow you from one application to another.

A Start Analyze button sits at the top of the parameter list, and OFX colour space management is supported. It runs on macOS 12 or later and on Windows. Recent releases added SGO Mistika and Autograph 2026 support.
