What Procedural Posters and Paintings does

Procedural Posters and Paintings decorates interior scenes in 3ds Max. It places framed images instantly with a height control, aimed at architectural visualization.

Twelve ready-made composition presets arrange multiple frames on a wall, and images, frames and passe-partout can be randomized. The package contains 72 high-resolution portrait and landscape images, your own pictures work too, and finished compositions can be stored as presets. Visualizers dressing walls in apartments, offices or hotels can fill them quickly without modeling and texturing each frame.

Key features

  • Instant frame placement with height control
  • 12 composition presets
  • Randomization of images, frames and passe-partout
  • 72 included high-resolution images
  • Support for custom images and saved presets
