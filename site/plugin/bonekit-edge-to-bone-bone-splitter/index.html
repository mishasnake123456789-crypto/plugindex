What Bonekit - Edge To Bone + Bone Splitter does

BoneKit pairs two lightweight rigging helpers. Edge to Bone creates aligned bone chains from selected mesh edges, automatically recognizing connected chains and handling several at once.

Quick Bone Split subdivides selected bones and redistributes weights automatically, so skinning is not lost. Useful for ropes, tails, hair and other segmented parts that need quick bone chains. Together they cover two common jobs when building a skeleton from existing geometry. Riggers working from sculpted or scanned characters benefit when bones must follow existing edge flow.

Key features

  • Edge to Bone builds aligned chains
  • Detects multiple connected edge chains
  • Quick Bone Split subdivides bones
  • Automatic weight redistribution
