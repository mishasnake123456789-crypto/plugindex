Archive It

Developer: Flame_Python

FreeBlender
Download Free

Blender add-on that duplicates selected objects into named archive slots and cycles through them in organized subcollections.

What Archive It does

Archive It helps keep earlier versions of objects without cluttering the Blender viewport. It makes structured subcollections and stores duplicates in them.

Selected objects are copied into automatically named archive slots, optionally stamped with a time in a custom format. You can then cycle through the slots one at a time to review them. It works with Blender 3.0 through 4.5 and is GPL licensed. Good for comparing design iterations.

Key features

  • Duplicate objects into named archive slots
  • Optional timestamps in names
  • Cycle through slots one at a time
  • Structured subcollections
  • Works with Blender 3.0 to 4.5
