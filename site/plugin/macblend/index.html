What MacBlend does

MacBlend by Manuel-Houben helps with color accuracy by letting you calibrate the colors in a Blender scene against a Macbeth chart. It sits in the Compositing category and is tagged for pipeline use as well.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
