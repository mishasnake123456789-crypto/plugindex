What Image To 3D does

Image To 3D, by CGMatter, converts pictures into geometry by tracing outlines in Blender. Unlike methods that require an SVG, it reads the image directly, which makes turning a logo or silhouette into a model fast.

The rule is simple: black areas are kept and white areas are discarded. Settings can still be modified after the conversion, so you can adjust the result rather than starting over, which suits motion designers and modelers working from flat artwork.

Key features

  • Turns a bitmap into a 3D model
  • Traces outlines without needing an SVG
  • Black areas are retained; white areas are dropped
  • Settings editable after conversion
