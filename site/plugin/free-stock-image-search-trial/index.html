What Free Stock Image Search Trial does

This is a limited trial of Vakago Tools' stock image search panel. It looks up pictures from Pixabay inside After Effects and Premiere Pro, so you can test the workflow before buying the full product.

Functionality is restricted and the trial expires after 30 days. It installs under its own entry at Window > Extensions > Free Stock Image Search - TRIAL, and works with After Effects 18.0 and Premiere Pro 15.0 or newer, along with Photoshop, InDesign and Illustrator.
