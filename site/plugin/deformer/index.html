What Deformer does

Deformer is a Blender plugin that shapes objects through the influence of a second object. Move the Deformer Object near a mesh and the surface reacts, which lets you sculpt or carve without traditional tools.

Standard and Normal deformer types are available, with a Normalize mode for the Standard type. Deformation is live while you move the object, and the influence field can be adjusted with a remap control. It suits quick dents, bulges and organic adjustments.

Key features

  • Influence radius deformation from another object
  • Standard and Normal deformer types
  • Live updates as the deformer moves
  • Remap control for influence range
