Blender add-on for flying through a scene with the Dolly Rig from Add Camera Rigs to find framing, with camera and aim modes.

What Camerafly does

Camerafly lets you navigate with a Dolly Rig camera in Blender, from the Add Camera Rigs community extension. Pick the Dolly Camera, press fly and look for a framing.

Camera Mode is a regular fly mode much like Blender's walk navigation, and Aim Mode circles the aim bone to help locate an angle on your subject. Cinematographers and layout artists can explore compositions interactively. Flying a rig interactively feels closer to operating a real camera, which can produce more natural compositions than numeric adjustment of camera values.

Key features

  • Fly with the Dolly Rig
  • Camera Mode like walk navigation
  • Aim Mode orbits the aim bone
  • Interactive framing
