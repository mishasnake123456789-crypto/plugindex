What Weight Shaper does

Weight Shaper is a Blender addon from Makatopia that speeds up weight painting. You pose the character, move vertices until the deformation looks right, and then run Optimize Weights so the add-on generates weights that support those corrections.

An Influencing Bones list controls which bones may share weight, and results are normalized so the total stays within 1. Advanced settings expose learning rate, convergence threshold and iterations for the optimization. Multiple bones can be added to the list at once, which suits characters whose joints need precise corrective shaping.

Key features

  • Pose, move vertices, then optimize
  • Influencing Bones list for weight splitting
  • Normalized weights capped at a total of 1
  • Learning rate, convergence and iteration settings
  • Add multiple bones at once
