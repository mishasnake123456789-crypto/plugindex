FreeBlender

Blender add-on for clicking screws onto surfaces or face corners, with offset sliders and 18 included screw assets.

What Screw Placer does

Screw Placer lets you add screws to a model by clicking on the surface in the 3D View. It is aimed at hard-surface work where fasteners need to land in the right spots quickly.

A face corner mode drops a screw at a corner in one click, while normal and side offset sliders push it in, out or diagonally. The hotkey defaults to Shift+E and can be changed. Placed screws are gathered into a collection, and a compact N-Panel keeps controls handy. Eighteen screw models are marked for the Asset Browser.

Key features

  • Click-to-place screws on any surface
  • One-click face corner placement
  • Normal and side offset sliders
  • Customizable hotkey, Shift+E by default
  • 18 screws ready for the Asset Browser
