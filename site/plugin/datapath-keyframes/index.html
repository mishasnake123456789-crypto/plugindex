What Datapath Keyframes does

Datapath Keyframes makes loops out of ordinary keyframes. Supported data types include floats, integers, booleans, percents, angles, colors and enums, and any animatable property can be driven.

Three repeat methods are offered: Reset, Pingpong and Incremental, with custom clamps and reverse options. Targets can be shape keys, material colors, geometry node values and more. Motion designers building continuous loops, counters or cycling effects avoid cumbersome modifiers. Setup is quick, which suits repeated elements in loops.

Key features

  • Wide data type support
  • Reset, Pingpong and Incremental repeat methods
  • Custom clamps and reverse options
  • Works with shape keys, materials and geometry nodes
