What FxFactory Face Blur does

Face Blur from FxFactory finds human faces in footage and hides them in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, also working in Final Cut Pro and Motion. Many faces can be treated at once, and each one is tracked independently for relative size and tilt.

You can blur or pixelate every face or limit the effect to a single one. Custom graphics act as shape masks, with bright areas defining what remains visible, and output inversion makes vignettes that follow all faces. There are 14 presets that can be shared across apps or users. The bundled Object Blur plugin tracks and conceals any object and can be stacked with Face Blur.

Key features

  • Automatic face detection, blur and pixelation
  • Independent tracking of each face
  • Blur all faces or just one
  • Custom shape masks and inverted output
  • 14 presets; included Object Blur plugin
