What Stylized Hair Pro does

Stylized Hair Pro is a node-driven based add-on for creating stylized hairstyles without hand-sculpting every lock. It plugs into Blender's hair curves system and can also adapt to bézier curves and ordinary paths.

Controls cover the shape of the hair curves, the generated geometry, materials and decorative ornaments, so a style can be adjusted non-destructively after it is built. Character artists working on anime, toon or otherwise stylized figures can iterate on looks quickly because the setup stays procedural.

Key features

  • Procedural Geometry Nodes hairstyle creation
  • Works with Blender hair curves
  • Adapts to bézier curves and paths
  • Control over shape, geometry and materials
  • Ornament options
