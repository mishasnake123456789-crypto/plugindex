What Parametric Primitives does

Parametric Primitives expands Blender's standard mesh menu with shapes that stay editable through settings. Ten primitives are offered: Box, Capsule, Cog, Cylinder, Drop, Pyramid, Sphere, Spring, Star and Tube.

Inputs are unit-aware and accept both metric and imperial values, and each object keeps its own controls, so you can revisit dimensions later. Some shapes double as others, for instance the cylinder can act as a cone and the pyramid as a cake. It suits modelers who want precise basic forms without extra modifier setups.

Key features

  • Ten parametric primitives in the Add Mesh menu
  • Metric and imperial unit-aware inputs
  • Per-object adjustable controls
  • Cylinder doubles as a cone, pyramid as a cake
  • Includes Cog, Spring, Star and Tube
