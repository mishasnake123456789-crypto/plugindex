What Lazy Bones does

Lazy Bones is an experimental rigging toolkit for Blender made by Blue Nile 3d. It is released under the GPL license and includes documentation. Version 2 is currently in a testing phase.

It is compatible with Blender 4.3 up to 5.0, and later updates are included with the purchase. It is aimed at Blender users who like trying out new rigging approaches.
