What Logo Reveals Pro does

SkywayVisuals' Logo Reveals Pro is a ready-made pack for adding branded intros to video projects without outside plugins. It holds twelve animated reveals with a clean, minimalist style.

Colors and fonts are customizable, and the fonts are supplied. Rendering is possible in Full HD (1920x1080) or 4K (3840x2160). A selection of royalty-free music from TunePocket.com is bundled, and a tutorial in the documentation section walks through the steps. Content creators who need polished openers quickly can start here.

Key features

  • Full HD and 4K resolutions
  • Customizable colors and fonts
