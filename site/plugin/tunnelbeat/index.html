Builds a BPM-synced infinite tunnel animation in Blender, with driver-based rotation tied to your tempo and a camera.

What Tunnelbeat does

TunnelBeat creates a rhythmic tunnel for music visuals. After you type a tempo and confirm, it creates the tunnel and a camera, and links the rotation to the beat through drivers rather than keyframes.

Any BPM can be used, speed can be slow, medium or fast relative to the beat, and gridline thickness is adjustable. VJs, music video makers and motion designers can have a synced loop in seconds. Since the rotation is driven by the tempo, changing the BPM immediately retimes the whole animation.

Key features

  • Driver-based rotation locked to BPM
  • Any tempo supported
  • Slow, medium or fast scroll speed
  • Adjustable gridline thickness
  • Camera placed automatically
