What One-Click Wear Generator does

One-Click Wear Generator, from Lazy3D, produces aged and lived-in looks quickly. The effect is fully procedural and can be customized, and it works with Cycles. EEVEE is also supported, though limited by that engine.

A .blend file is included for use from the asset browser. Prop and environment artists can add edge wear, dirt and signs of use without hand painting textures.

Key features

  • Weathered, worn looks within a few clicks
  • Procedural throughout, with adjustable settings
  • Cycles support, limited EEVEE support
  • Included .blend for the asset browser
