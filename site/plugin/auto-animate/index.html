What Auto-Animate does

Auto-Animate is a Blender add-on that speeds up the reuse of motion. Instead of rebuilding keyframes for each object, you capture an animation once and add it again whenever it is needed, which suits repeated movements across a project.

It combines an animation generator with a Save Animations feature. The Essentials edition covers the generator and saving, while the Premium edition includes every feature. Documentation comes with it, and it is compatible with Blender 3.6 through 5.2. Motion designers and animators who want a small library of reusable moves will benefit most.

Key features

  • One-click saving of animations
  • One-click adding of saved animations
  • Built-in animation generator
  • Essentials and Premium editions
  • Works with Blender 3.6 through 5.2
