What Auto Active Camera Switcher does

Auto Active Camera Switcher removes the extra step of setting a camera active in Blender. Selecting a camera makes it the scene's active camera.

The feature can be enabled or disabled from the Sidebar's View tab or with a camera icon along the top of the viewport, and the default state is configurable. Artists managing many cameras for lookdev or multi-shot layouts can compare views quickly. Turning it off is just as easy, which keeps full manual control when a scene needs a particular camera held active.

Key features

  • Selecting a camera activates it
  • Toggle from viewport icon or Sidebar View tab
  • Quick enable and disable
  • Configurable default state
