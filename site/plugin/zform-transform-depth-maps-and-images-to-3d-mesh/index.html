What Zform - Transform Depth Maps And Images To 3D Mesh does

zForm turns existing depth maps and the images or videos that go with them into 3D meshes in Blender. It handles single images, sequences and video frames, and can create explorable 360 degree worlds from one 2D image.

PNG, JPG and JPEG files import along with their depth maps. Mesh properties such as subdivision, smoothing and depth strength are customizable. Concept artists, photogrammetry-adjacent users and VR creators can quickly turn flat imagery into 3D scenes.

Key features

  • Images and videos with depth maps become meshes
  • Image sequences and video frames
  • Subdivision, smoothing and depth strength
  • 360 degree worlds from a single image
