Smooths triangle meshes using graph-Laplacian spectral filtering.

What FourierMesh does

FourierMesh applies spectral smoothing to triangle meshes in Blender. It uses the graph Laplacian of the mesh, a technique similar to a Fourier transform on geometry, to filter out high-frequency noise.

This approach can clean up scans or noisy surfaces while preserving broader shape, and gives more control than simple averaging. Technical artists and researchers interested in geometry processing can experiment with it directly in the viewport.

Key features

  • Graph-Laplacian spectral smoothing
  • Works on triangle meshes
  • Removes high-frequency noise
