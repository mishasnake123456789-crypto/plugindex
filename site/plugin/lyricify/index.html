Creates lyric videos in Blender with Whisper transcription and physics-based falling text animation.

What Lyricify does

Lyricify automates lyric video production. It transcribes audio with OpenAI's Whisper from MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC or M4A files, detects the language among more than 99 and trims initial silence.

Lyrics then fall in with gravity-based physics, complete with materials, lighting and bloom. Musicians and video makers can get a styled lyric animation without manual timing. Under the hood you get whisper transcription from common audio formats, language auto-detection and gravity-falling lyric animation.

Key features

  • Whisper transcription from common audio formats
  • Language auto-detection
  • Gravity-falling lyric animation
  • Materials, lighting and bloom included
