What Lapineige's Tools V2.9 - Add-Ons Compilation To Make Your Life Easier does

Lapineige's Tools collects ten small add-ons in one package for simpler installation. They were written from user requests and cover mirroring, render borders, cameras, origins and other everyday tasks.

AutoMirror readies a mesh using a mirror modifier, Precise Render Border Adjust refines border regions, and Multi-Camera Render handles multiple cameras. A grab bag for modelers and renderers. Because the tools are small and focused, they can be enabled individually, and each addresses a specific irritation in daily Blender use.
