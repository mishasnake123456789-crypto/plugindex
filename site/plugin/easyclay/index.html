Applies a scene-wide clay material in Blender non-destructively while preserving displacement, normals and opacity.

What Easyclay does

Easyclay overrides every material in a scene with a neutral clay look, which is ideal for reviewing form without color distractions. Setup, update and removal each take one click, and the original materials remain untouched.

Displacement, bump, normal and opacity maps are kept so cutouts and surface detail still read correctly. Toggles can keep emission materials lit and glass transparent. Look-dev artists, reviewers and portfolio builders get quick clay renders.

Key features

  • One-click scene-wide clay override
  • Non-destructive setup and removal
  • Keeps displacement, normal and alpha maps
  • Toggles for emission and glass
