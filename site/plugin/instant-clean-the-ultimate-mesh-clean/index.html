What Instant Clean - The Ultimate Mesh Clean does

Instant Clean automates routine mesh maintenance in Blender. One click runs a cleanup routine that prepares a model for rendering, 3D printing or export to a game engine.

Cleanup routines are stored as customizable presets. Single Execution runs each step on its own and reports what changed; steps can also be placed in Quick Favorites. Mesh Repair handles non-manifold geometry, flipped normals and broken edges, and Fix Normals deals with face orientation. Artists who receive messy imported models can standardize them quickly.

Key features
