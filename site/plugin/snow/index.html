Blender add-on that covers selected objects with geometry-based snow in one click, with footprints from any object.

What Snow does

Snow, by Sahin Ersoz, builds real geometry snow on the objects you select in Blender instead of relying on texture tricks. One click adds the layer, and you then tune it to match the look you want.

Height, direction, resolution and bulge are adjustable, and a simple snow option gives faster feedback. Footprints or other impressions can be generated from any object or collection, which is useful for winter scenes where characters or vehicles disturb the surface.

Key features

  • One-click geometry-based snow
  • Adjustable height, direction, resolution and bulge
  • Simple Snow mode for quick previews
  • Footprints from any object or collection
