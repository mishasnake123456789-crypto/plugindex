What Forge: Cad Precision Modelling In Blender does

Forge adds CAD-style precision modeling to Blender. NURBS and BRep geometry let you design hard-surface parts with fillets and live booleans, working with true surfaces rather than only polygons.

A zero-retopology conversion produces a quad mesh from the CAD model, and STEP and IGES files can be imported and exported to exchange data with engineering software. Documentation is included, along with product updates and support from RenderCraftStudio. Product designers, hard-surface artists and anyone moving parts between CAD tools and Blender will find the precision useful.

Key features

  • NURBS and BRep modeling in Blender
  • Fillets and live booleans
  • Zero-retopology quad mesh conversion
  • STEP and IGES import and export
