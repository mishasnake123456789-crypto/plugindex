Review and proofing plugin that brings colleagues' comments into After Effects and Premiere Pro in real time.

What VERI does

VERI integrates review into Adobe Creative Cloud so feedback appears while you work. Colleagues' comments on improvements and modifications arrive in real time, and you can check and edit them inside the application.

Reviewers are assigned for proofing, and the process starts when the working file is uploaded. It links to Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Premiere Pro and After Effects, and can be downloaded from the VERI site or the Adobe Creative Cloud marketplace. Teams that proof design and video work together can keep revisions in one place.

Key features

  • Adobe Creative Cloud integration
  • Real-time comments from colleagues
  • Check and edit comments in the app
  • Assign reviewers for proofing
  • Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Premiere Pro and After Effects
