What Camera Nodes Fx does

Camera Nodes Fx offers a visual node graph for controlling cameras in Blender. Every animatable property appears as a socket, so you connect, mix and layer cinematic effects instead of setting keyframes or drivers.

Seventy-eight nodes are organized into eight categories: Camera, Input, Transform, Motion, Moves, Animation, Mix and Math. Motion is procedural and updates when a socket value changes, with real-time viewport feedback, and effects stack additively through the graph. Camera animators, motion designers and previs artists who like a node-based approach will enjoy the flexibility.

Key features

  • Node graph with every camera property as a socket
  • 78 nodes in 8 categories
  • Procedural motion, no keyframes or drivers
  • Real-time viewport updates
  • Effects stack additively
