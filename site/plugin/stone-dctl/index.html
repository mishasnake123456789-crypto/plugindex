DaVinci Resolve DCTL that shapes curves with sliders, adjusting middle gray, RGB channels and luminance without a graph.

What Stone DCTL does

Stone DCTL brings curve adjustment to DaVinci Resolve through sliders instead of dragging points on a graph. It lets you move the middle gray point while preserving the shape of the curves, which keeps contrast relationships intact.

RGB channels can be adjusted separately, and the Luminance curve has its own control. Blend sliders set the power of each curve, presets are provided for the middle gray point, and the curve shape is smooth. An RGB Curve Visibility slider changes the brightness of the overlay. Colorists who prefer precise, repeatable numeric control over free-form curve editing will find it comfortable.

Key features

  • Adjust middle gray while keeping curve shapes
  • Separate RGB channel adjustment
  • Luminance curve control
  • Blend sliders for curve power
  • Middle gray presets and RGB curve overlay visibility
