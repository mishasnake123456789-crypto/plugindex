What Lollipop Characters does

Xane Studios' Lollipop Characters pairs an add-on with a library of 18 stylized rigged characters, each having a wardrobe and utilities, plus a cat rig and creation tools. One click in a sidebar add-on brings every asset into the scene.

Two clicks bind objects together, for example clothes onto characters, buttons onto clothes, and tattoos onto characters. Garments stay pinned to the characters and animate without cloth rigging, and their textures and materials are adjustable. It gives animators ready-to-pose figures.

Key features

  • 18 stylized character rigs plus a cat rig
  • One-click import from the N-menu
  • Two-click binding of clothes, buttons and tattoos
  • Garments pinned without cloth rigging
  • Adjustable textures and materials
