What Two-Point Perspective does

Two-Point Perspective makes architectural-style cameras in Blender with vertical lines kept parallel.

It swaps the active camera for a copy using two-point perspective, converting from three-point, with manual setting of the target angle. The new camera is named after the original. Architectural visualizers get cleaner verticals. Keeping verticals straight is a common requirement in architectural renders, and this approach avoids converging lines without manually tuning camera shift. It is a quick fix for scenes where tilted cameras create unwanted convergence.

Key features

  • Replaces active camera with a two-point copy
  • Converts three-point cameras
  • Manual target angle
  • New camera named after original
