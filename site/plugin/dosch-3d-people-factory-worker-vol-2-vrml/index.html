What DOSCH 3D: People - Factory Worker Vol. 2 - vrml does

DOSCH 3D: People - Factory Worker Vol. 2 is the second volume in the People series and holds 10 photo-real, fully textured human models of factory workers. The files are supplied in VRML format for use in 3ds Max.

They are ready-made figures to place on production lines, workshop floors or industrial sites. Visualization and training animation artists can add believable workers to scenes without sculpting characters, keeping attention on the machinery and layout.
