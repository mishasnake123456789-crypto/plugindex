Blender surface patching toolkit that builds quad surfaces from curves and meshes.

What Curviblend does

CurviBlend creates clean quad-based surfaces from curves and meshes through a non-destructive workflow. It suits hard-surface modeling, game assets, 3D printing and architectural visualization.

Objects are organized into groups, and loop finding generates the shortest closed loop of Bezier curves connecting them. Patching works from a group or any selection of two or more curves, with a fast bilinear (G0) or tangent-smooth bicubic (G1) option. Boundary control, curve rectification and snapping are included, and settings update live through a modal workflow in the N-Panel.

Key features

  • Group-based non-destructive workflow
  • Automatic closed-loop curve finding
  • Bilinear G0 or bicubic G1 patches
  • Live modal settings with boundary control
