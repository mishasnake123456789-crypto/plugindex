What BAO Perspective Changer does

BAO Perspective Changer is a native After Effects plug-in for altering image perspective cleanly. Described as a corner pin with extra power, it calculates two distortions before sampling the image, so definition is not lost.

Input and output perspective areas are defined with four selection and four transformation points, and perspective changes by dragging points without any camera. Perspectives can vary over time on video or animated comps, and points can be driven by expressions for 3D interaction. A script converts masks to perspective and back. It is GPU accelerated with OpenGL and includes a dockable controller panel and user guide.

Key features

  • Two distortions computed before sampling
  • Four selection and four transformation points
  • Time-varying perspectives
  • Expression-controlled points
  • Script converts masks to perspective and back
