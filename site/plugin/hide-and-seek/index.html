Lists every hidden object in Blender and lets you toggle, preview or isolate them.

What Hide & Seek does

Hide & Seek solves the problem of objects that vanish from a scene and are hard to find. Press Refresh and the add-on lists every hidden object in a clear panel, so you do not have to dig through the Outliner.

Lists can be narrowed by which kind of hiding applies: viewport visibility, viewport disable or render visibility. An item can be peeked at or briefly unhidden straight from the list, leaving its stored setting untouched, or isolated to concentrate on it. Handy for cleaning up inherited files.

Key features

  • Refresh lists all hidden objects
  • Filters by viewport and render visibility
  • Preview without permanent changes
  • Isolate objects from the list
