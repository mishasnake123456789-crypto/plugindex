What Baga Grove - Evermotion does

Baga Grove is a GeoPack for Blender, created with Evermotion, that scatters vegetation by drawing. Paint where you want assets to appear and they arrange themselves into a believable grove, putting smaller items near the edges and larger ones toward the center.

You can build and save your own groves, add your own assets from the Asset Browser or with J > Scatter, and use the BagaPie GeoPack system. To keep the viewport usable there are camera culling, a proxy option with simplified models, and a setting to show only a percentage of particles.

Key features

  • Draw-based scattering with automatic size arrangement
  • Create and save custom groves
  • Use your own assets through the Asset Browser
  • Camera culling to skip off-screen objects
  • Proxy models and reduced particle display
