After Effects plugin that attaches layers to shape paths and guides them as the shape animates.

What Wayfinder does

Wayfinder by Paul Conigliaro attaches artwork to shape layers in After Effects and keeps it traveling along the path even when the shape is complex or animated. Draw-on and trail effects are the main applications.

Several layers can follow each other along one path, and intricate routes can be built from shape modifiers such as twist and zig-zag. A Leader control on the main shape trims or offsets, with attached layers following, while a Follower control positions each layer individually. An option hides the helper calculation layers it creates.

Key features

  • Attach layers to animated shape paths
  • Draw-on and trail effects
  • Multiple layers following each other
  • Leader and Follower controls
  • Option to hide calculation layers
