What 3D Modeler does

3D Modeler from MotionCodes builds three-dimensional models in After Effects by extruding layers. Solids, shapes, text and transparent layers can all be pushed into volumes, forming cubes, origami shapes, geometric forms and pyramids.

To extrude a surface you confirm a depth and offset and click generate. Masks let you carve or alter selected surfaces, while beveling and chamfering use simplified controls for color, depth and rotation angle. Surfaces can be textured individually or in groups with cut-out templates, and an Origami Cube style includes an opening controller and a Wired Mode.

Key features

  • Extrudes solids, shapes, text and transparent layers
  • Cubes, origami shapes, pyramids and geometric forms
  • Mask-based carving and surface changes
  • Bevel and chamfer with simple controls
  • Individual or grouped surface texturing
