What Armature Deform Controls does

Armature Deform Controls, by Jim Kroovy, helps when skeletons from other DCC applications don't behave well in Blender. It creates new bones that indirectly drive the original bones of the source armature, with orientations that suit rigging and animation in Blender.

Compatibility with imported assets is maintained, and animation can be baked between control and deform bones whenever needed. Documentation is included, and it is part of the B.L.E.N.D add-on suite. Animators and riggers who work with game-ready or Mixamo-style skeletons will find it makes them far easier to pose.

Key features

  • Control bones that indirectly drive original bones
  • Blender-friendly bone orientations
  • Bake between control and deform bones
  • Keeps imported asset compatibility
