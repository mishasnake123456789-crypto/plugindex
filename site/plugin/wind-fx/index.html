What Wind Fx does

Wind Fx, made by Blender Procedural, generates dynamic wind in Blender. One click sets up wind in your scene, and the setup is node-based, so you can inspect and adjust how it works.

It works with Cycles and Eevee across Blender 4.0 to 4.5, comes with documentation and is released under the GPL license. It is intended for artists who want grass, foliage or cloth to sway without building a wind system by themselves.

Key features

  • Dynamic wind generator
  • One-click setup
