What HERAW does

HERAW is a panel that gathers project details in one place within After Effects and Premiere Pro. It synchronises with your project automatically and keeps comments, task status and search close at hand.

The timeline can be adjusted to fit the picture, specific comments are displayed, and replies can be sent without leaving the panel. Task status is managed and synchronised, and a built-in search engine locates items. Editors and artists working on team productions who want less switching between tools will find review details stay beside the work.

Key features

  • Automatic synchronisation with the project
  • Timeline adjustment to fit the picture
  • View and reply to comments in the panel
  • Task status management
  • Built-in search engine
