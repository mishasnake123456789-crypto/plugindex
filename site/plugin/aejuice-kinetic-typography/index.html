What AEJuice Kinetic Typography does

AEJuice Kinetic Typography is a collection of animated text designs ready to take on your own words. Everything is built from After Effects layers, so the text stays editable and customisable.

Built-in animations vary in style, and the typography resizes automatically and works in any colour or style. Four Google Fonts (Passion One, Montserrat, Barlow and Big Shoulders Display) are used, though your own can replace them. Downloads are fast. Video editors looking for punchy animated titles and captions in After Effects or Premiere Pro can customise and apply them quickly.

Key features

  • Built entirely from After Effects layers
  • Replaceable editable text
  • Variety of customisable animations
  • Auto-resizable typography
  • Uses four Google Fonts or your own
