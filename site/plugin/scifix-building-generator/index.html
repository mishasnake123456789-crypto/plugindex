What Scifix - Building Generator does

Scifix is a Geometry Nodes toolset that produces procedural sci-fi towers. A sidebar panel spawns a fully detailed tower at the 3D cursor, from foundations up to roof antennas.

A Space Fill city generator populates a defined area with varied towers, designs change by seed, and emissive night lighting controls light up the skyline. Environment artists can build futuristic cityscapes quickly. Highlights cover instant Add System at the 3D cursor and space Fill city generator.

Key features

  • Instant Add System at the 3D cursor
  • Space Fill city generator
  • Seed-based design variation
  • Emissive night lighting controls
