What FlexiMist - Diffusion Plugin For DaVinci Resolve (DCTL) does

Traditional diffusion filters soften highlights and give images a gentle haze, but they cost money and are hard to change after the shoot. FlexiMist from Nx Color recreates that dreamy glow digitally as a DCTL inside DaVinci Resolve.

Because it runs on the GPU, the effect can be previewed and adjusted in real time on Resolve 18 or 19. Filmmakers and content creators use this kind of look for commercials, music videos and narrative scenes where a mistier, more cinematic mood is wanted. Updates are free and the license is perpetual, so it can stay in a long-term grading toolkit.
