Automates scene audio in Blender with ambience, motion-driven and animation-triggered speakers.

What Speaker Overhaul - Automated Sound Design does

Speaker Overhaul builds sound design from three parts. The Master Speaker handles ambience, with multiple variations or effects playing at different locations, frames, pitch and volume.

Motion Speakers respond to an object's movement along X, Y and Z, correcting speed and pitch as it travels, and the Animation Speaker triggers sound by frame. It reduces manual placement of speakers in animated scenes. Among its capabilities are master Speaker for ambience, motion-driven speakers with pitch correction and frame-based Animation Speaker.

Key features

  • Master Speaker for ambience
  • Motion-driven speakers with pitch correction
  • Frame-based Animation Speaker
  • Variation by location, pitch and volume
