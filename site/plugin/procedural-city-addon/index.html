What Procedural City Addon does

Procedural City Addon, from EdenWeby 3D, creates urban scenes using geometry nodes. A button click and a few adjustments produce a city, and everything remains procedural for further editing.

It ships with more than seven building presets, such as War Zone and Modern City, and includes an easy fog effect driven by noise. Artists making backdrops, flyovers and sci-fi skylines can build large environments quickly.

Key features

  • One-click geometry nodes city
  • Over seven building presets
  • Procedural and editable
  • Noise-based fog
