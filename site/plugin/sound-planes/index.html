Sound Planes

FreeBlender

Blender add-on that places sounds on the sequencer automatically when moving objects intersect user-defined planes.

What Sound Planes does

Sound Planes uses planes as triggers in a Blender scene. Position them in the areas you want to monitor, and when an object passes through a plane the add-on adds a sound to the sequencer at that moment.

Motion detection is described as running efficiently, and the add-on needs no special permissions. Animators can sync footsteps, impacts and other effects to the action without manually placing audio strips.

Key features

  • Planes act as motion triggers
  • Automatically adds sounds to the sequencer
  • Efficient motion detection
  • No special permissions
