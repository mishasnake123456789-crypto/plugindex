What Actioncast does

Actioncast lets you animate many rigs with a single choice. Create skeleton groups, add armatures to them, pick an action from a dropdown, and every armature in the group updates immediately.

You can make unlimited groups, add or remove armatures with one click, and manage more than one group at a time. The N-panel interface is clean. It fits crowd and background characters or multi-character scenes where many rigs share the same motion.

Key features

  • Unlimited skeleton groups
  • One-click add or remove of armatures
  • Action dropdown applies to the whole group
  • Multiple groups managed at once
