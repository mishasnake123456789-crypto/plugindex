What Fire-Wish does

Fire-Wish is a preset pack from Holapro for adding fire effects without simulating. It includes pre-rendered and VDB fires, explosions and bursts, along with trees, smoke and particles.

Presets provide high quality and alpha channels and work with both Eevee and Cycles. Documentation and a demo video are included. A handy shortcut for effects shots. Using ready-made assets keeps scenes light and speeds up work on explosions, campfires and similar effects where simulation time would otherwise be high.

Key features

  • Pre-rendered and VDB presets
  • Fires, explosions and bursts
  • Alpha channels included
  • Eevee and Cycles compatible
