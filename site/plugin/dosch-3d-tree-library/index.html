What DOSCH 3D: Tree Library does

Dosch 3D: Tree Library supplies textured trees in Cinema 4D 8.5 and above, R13 and V-Ray formats. Each can be scaled, recolored, animated and edited to fit a scene.

Materials, textures and colors are freely editable, and you may apply your own textures or labels. Individual elements can be switched on or off, given any transparency from fully visible to invisible, and animated separately or as a whole. Scene lighting is yours to choose. Architectural visualizers and landscape artists can place or modify trees quickly.

Key features

  • Fully textured 3D tree models
  • Cinema 4D 8.5+, R13 and V-Ray formats
  • Editable materials, textures and colors
  • Per-element transparency
  • Animation of elements or whole tree
