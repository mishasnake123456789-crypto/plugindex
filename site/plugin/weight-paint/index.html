What Weight Paint ++ does

Weight Paint ++ is a Blender plugin for fixing weight painting problems. One click finds vertex groups that are not symmetrical and weight islands that have become disconnected.

You can repair left and right symmetry per group or across every asymmetric group. A Weight Islands panel calculates discontinuous weights and jumps to the smallest or next island, while further panels clean up groups and transfer weights. Riggers will find it useful for polishing skinning on characters.

Key features

  • Detects asymmetric vertex groups
  • Left/right symmetry repair
  • Weight Islands panel for stray weights
  • Group cleanup and weight transfer
