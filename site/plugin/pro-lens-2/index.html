What Pro Lens 2 does

Pro Lens 2, rebuilt from the ground up, applies lens effects to a Blender camera. Controls live in the Camera Properties panel, where the effect can be added or removed and its strength adjusted.

It offers natural vignetting, focal length and ratio changes that keep the lens elements lined up, auto brightness correction to prevent a darkened image, and bokeh designed around an f/2.8 aperture. Documentation is included. Photographers and 3D artists chasing realistic, camera-like renders can add imperfections that suggest real glass.

Key features

  • Lens effect panel in Camera Properties
  • Strength slider
  • Natural vignetting
  • Auto brightness correction
  • Bokeh based on f/2.8
