What AEJuice Liquid Effect does

AEJuice Liquid Effect produces fluid, dynamic reveals for logos and short pieces of text in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Colours can be customised to suit your brand.

The pack is applied by drag and drop through the AEJuice Pack Manager, which provides fast preview, download, import and favourites. It requires After Effects CC 2015 or newer or Premiere Pro CC 2021 or newer, on Windows 10+ or macOS 10.14+, including M1 and M2. Motion designers wanting a splashy intro or sting can drop one in and recolour it.
