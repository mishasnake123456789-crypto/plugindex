What Astroview - View Constraint Tool does

Astroview helps keep the part of a model you are working on in front of you in Blender. It is a modal operator that moves the viewport to defined focus points, countering the way your working area drifts out of perspective during modeling or sculpting.

The target point can be changed instantly, orbiting and navigation are smooth, and the view auto-zooms toward the target while you orbit. Documentation is included. Modelers and sculptors who spend their time circling around forms will find fewer manual pans and zooms are needed.

Key features

  • Jumps the viewport to focus points
  • Instant target point change
  • Smooth orbiting
  • Auto-zoom to the target
  • Documentation included
