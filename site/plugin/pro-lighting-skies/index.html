What Pro-Lighting: Skies does

Pro-Lighting: Skies, made by Blender Guru, supplies ready-to-use outdoor lighting for Blender. Instead of tuning sun lamps by hand, you apply a sky and get lighting modeled on real-world conditions.

The skies are of HDR quality and optimized for Blender, and documentation is included. Hobbyists, product visualizers and beginners learning to light exteriors can get believable daylight results fast and then fine-tune the look as needed. Because the lighting is built around HDR-quality skies, it replaces the guesswork of tuning sun and fill lamps with a ready, consistent starting point.

Key features

  • One-click outdoor lighting
  • HDR-quality skies
  • Real-world accurate lighting
