What Multicam does

Multicam is a Blender add-on from Team Blender for managing many viewpoints of one scene. With a single click it generates as many cameras as you need and distributes them along paths.

Placement can follow existing curves, circles for orbiting shots, or straight lines for parallel setups. Cameras orient themselves along the path, can aim at a target object, and allow full control over rotation and position. You can then render stills or animations from each camera, which suits product turntables and architectural review.

Key features

  • Generate any number of cameras in one click
  • Place along curves, circles or lines
  • Automatic orientation or target object
  • Full rotation and position control
  • Render stills or animations from every camera
