Imports a folder of images into Blender's Video Sequence Editor as a scaled sequence using Scale to Fit or Aspect Fill.

What Quick Image Scale does

Quick Image Scale is a Video Sequence Editor add-on that loads whole folders of pictures as a sequence. Images are scaled to match your scene resolution as they arrive.

Two modes are available, Scale to Fit and Aspect Fill, and Ctrl+Shift+I triggers the import. It takes only a couple of clicks. Editors building slideshows, animatics or timelines from stills avoid scaling each strip by hand. The pairing of two scaling modes means portrait and landscape stills can share one timeline without distortion or manual resizing.

Key features

  • Imports a whole folder as a sequence
  • Scale to Fit and Aspect Fill modes
  • Images scaled to scene resolution on load
  • Ctrl+Shift+I shortcut
  • Two-click workflow
