What ProtoTech's 3DPDF Exporter For 3ds Max® -Trial does

This trial of ProtoTech's 3DPDF Exporter writes 3ds Max solid and wire bodies into interactive 3D PDF files viewable in free Adobe Reader. Face-level colors are supported, so exported models keep their appearance.
