What Zoetrope PATTR does

Zoetrope PATTR is a pattern-based effect from Zoetrope Software, distributed through FxFactory, for After Effects and Premiere Pro. It offers more than 120 seamless tiling designs that can be used as backgrounds, masks or transition drivers.

The same library powers three uses: a background generator for building intricate patterned backdrops, a luma mask effect, and an alpha channel transition. You add the chosen component to your timeline and pick a style. The effect is also available for Final Cut Pro and Motion, so teams working across editors can keep a consistent look.

Key features

  • 120+ seamless tiling pattern styles
  • Background generator
  • Luma mask effect
  • Alpha channel transitions
