What Pixel Lab C4D Tower Tool does

The Pixel Lab C4D Tower Tool builds cityscape buildings in a couple of clicks. It replaces the older Building Kit with a faster rig and offers around 200,000 possible random buildings out of the box.

There are 96 preset buildings, 400 panels and 129 spline profiles to combine, controlled through Panel Random, Spline Random or full manual adjustment. More than 50 bonus models add detail, and 30 tileable 4K materials are included. It speeds up prototyping of different designs and is included in the Pixel Lab Mega Pack. Useful for motion designers filling skylines.

Key features

  • About 200,000 possible random buildings
  • 96 presets, 400 panels, 129 spline profiles
  • Panel Random, Spline Random or manual control
  • 50+ bonus detail models
