Blender add-on for shooting animated laser beams with impact explosions on chosen frames.

What Combat Laser does

Combat Laser fires laser beams in the Blender timeline without manual keyframing. It is meant for action scenes, sci-fi films, game cinematics and VFX.

One click places a beam on the frame you choose, auto-aim locks onto any scene object, and impact explosions track moving targets. Beam and explosion color, brightness, size, length, duration and fade are adjustable, 15 presets such as Sci-Fi Blue, Rail Gun and Plasma Cannon are included, and the last shot can be deleted in one click. It works with Cycles and EEVEE.

Key features

  • One-click laser shots on a chosen frame
  • Auto-aim system
  • Impact explosions that follow moving objects
  • 15 built-in presets
