What Breeze does

Breeze is aeHamid's plugin for After Effects that builds wave-style animation. It suits flags, flowing lines, water-like movement and other bending graphics where a gentle ripple is needed.

Waves can travel in four directions, and a two-way option allows movement both ways. Several presets give quick starting points for different wave types. It can run in Mask mode, where multiple breezes can sit on a single layer's masks, or in Overall mode affecting the entire layer. It works across a range of recent After Effects versions.

Key features

  • Wave movement in four directions
  • Mask and Overall modes
  • Several wave presets
  • Two-way breeze option
  • Multiple breezes on one layer's masks
