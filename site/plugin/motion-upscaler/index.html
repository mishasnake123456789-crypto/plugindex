What Motion Upscaler does

Motion Upscaler by Rezi Chemia is an AI upscaling plugin for After Effects that increases resolution without cloud services. It runs locally, with no subscriptions or tokens needed, and accepts PNG sequences carrying alpha mattes, batches of stills and video clips.

Three modes cover single or sequence, multi-image and video inputs, with 2x, 3x and 4x scale factors. The model is tuned for 3D renders, anime, cartoons and motion graphics, and alpha channels are fully preserved. It is compatible with After Effects 2020 through 2026, making it a practical way to rescue low-resolution assets.

Key features

  • Single/Sequence, Multi-Image and Video modes
  • 2x, 3x and 4x upscale factors
  • Tuned for 3D renders, anime and motion graphics
  • Preserves alpha in PNG sequences
  • Runs locally without subscriptions
