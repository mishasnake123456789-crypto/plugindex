What Rename It does

Rename It, by Kabansky, handles the naming of many clips at once. Select clips on the timeline and apply a prefix, a suffix, the date and time, or a running number, with optional extra spacing.

It can also sort clips. It supports Premiere Pro 2020 through 2026, and helps editors keep sequences orderly when working with big batches. A single pass keeps naming consistent, which matters on edits containing hundreds of clips.

Key features

  • Batch renaming of selected timeline clips
  • Prefix and suffix support
  • Date and time insertion
  • Numbering with spacing options
  • Clip sorting
