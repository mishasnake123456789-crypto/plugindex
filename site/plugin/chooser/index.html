Selects or deselects sequence clips by condition: graphics, offline media or disabled clips.

What Chooser does

Chooser is a REDDESK extension that refines a clip selection in Premiere Pro by condition. Pick one of three conditions, graphics, media offline or disabled clips, then press Select or Deselect.

Select keeps the clips meeting the condition and drops the rest from the selection, while Deselect does the opposite and removes the matching clips. It works on the clips you have selected in the sequence, so it is useful for finding all offline media at once or excluding titles from a bulk operation. It supports recent Premiere Pro releases.

Key features

  • Three conditions: graphics, offline, disabled
  • Select keeps matching clips
  • Deselect removes matching clips
  • Works on the current sequence selection
