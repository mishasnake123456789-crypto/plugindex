Blender add-on that puts 3D Viewport navigation controls in the sidebar View tab.

What 3D Navigation does

3D Navigation moves viewport navigation tools into the sidebar of Blender's 3D View, under the View tab. It used to be bundled with Blender 4.1 and is now provided as it is, with limited support.

Version 1.3.0 introduced custom view directions and a redesigned interface. It runs on Blender 4.2 LTS and newer, needs no special permissions and is released under GPL v3 or later. Tablet and touch users, or anyone who prefers clickable navigation, can orbit, pan and snap views without keyboard shortcuts.

Key features

  • Navigation controls in sidebar View tab
  • Custom view directions in 1.3.0
  • Redesigned interface
  • For Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
