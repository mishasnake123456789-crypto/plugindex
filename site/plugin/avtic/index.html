What Avtic does

Avtic tackles the tedious back-and-forth of client revisions in DaVinci Resolve. Feedback notes are turned into timeline markers, along with matching assets, in only three clicks, so changes can be tracked right where they belong in the edit.

Assets from a chosen folder are dropped in automatically based on the selected marker, and finished videos are detected so they can be exported in two clicks. A batch export process handles several deliverables together. The interface is localized into Spanish, Russian and Hindi, and the plugin works with Resolve 18, 19 and 20. Freelance editors handling many revision rounds will benefit most.

Key features

  • Client notes become timeline markers
  • Automatic asset placement from a selected folder
  • Detects finished videos for two-click export
  • Batch export process
  • Spanish, Russian and Hindi localization
