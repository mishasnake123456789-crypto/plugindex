Fills Blender scenes with animated falling leaves, leaf heaps and individual leaves through an easy interface.

What Autumn Elements does

Autumn Elements helps build fall scenes quickly. Animated falling leaves can be added with one click and will react to collisions with objects in the environment.

The pack also supplies leaf piles and single leaves, including nine variants detailed enough for close-up camera shots. It runs in both Cycles and Eevee, and its assets are lightweight enough for weaker computers. Environment artists and VFX makers creating seasonal atmosphere will find it saves hours of scattering geometry by hand.

Key features

  • One-click animated falling leaves
  • Leaves collide with scene objects
  • Leaf piles and nine close-up single leaves
  • Works in Cycles and Eevee
  • Optimized for modest hardware
