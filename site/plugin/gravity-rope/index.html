What Gravity Rope does

Gravity Rope is a Blender add-on from IK3D that simulates ropes, cables, chains and pendulums live in the viewport. Sketch a rope, hit Play, and it collides, respond to wind, stretch and snap, all calculated on the GPU with a WGPU and XPBD solver.

Every frame is cached while it plays, so you can scrub back and replay it without manual syncing. Collisions include self-collision, and wind and force fields are supported. Eight ready-made Geometry Nodes skins cover rope, chain, phone cable, ribbon, beads and a braided sleeve, with controls for twist and cross section.

Key features

  • Real-time GPU rope, cable and chain simulation
  • Collisions, self-collision, wind and force fields
  • Stretch and breakage
  • Instant replay of cached frames
  • Eight Geometry Nodes skins
