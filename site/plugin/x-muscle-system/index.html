Blender physics-based tissue simulator for muscles, fat and skin deformation driven by volume preservation.

What X-Muscle System does

X-Muscle System builds muscular systems and organic tissue in Blender and simulates how skin deforms as the body moves. The simulation keeps the volume of muscle, fat and bone constant.

Flexor and extensor muscle types have animatable size, properties and controllers, and X-Mirror mirrors shape, properties and behavior. A Muscle Pinning Manager and Bendy Muscles keep tissue following the bone chain of a rig, and armatures and custom rigs including Rigify are supported. Character animators and creature designers can create believable anatomy.

Key features

  • Flexor and extensor muscle types
  • X-Mirror for symmetrical setups
  • Muscle Pinning Manager and Bendy Muscles
  • Works with Rigify and custom rigs
