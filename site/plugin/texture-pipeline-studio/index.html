What Texture Pipeline Studio does

Texture Pipeline Studio, by Gihan-Maddumage, assigns PBR textures to materials from folders organised by resolution, and lets you swap between levels of detail. It is listed under Material, Pipeline and Node.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.
