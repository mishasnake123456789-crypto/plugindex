Creates Plexus-style effects in Blender from points, lines and animated deformations on objects or collections.

What Atomic does

Atomic generates abstract network visuals inspired by Plexus. Points are spread over the surface or vertices of objects and collections, with adjustable density and a choice of uniform or normal-based spread.

Lines connect nearby points to form networks, with limits such as a maximum distance and a number of connections per point, and animated deformations bring the structure to life. It suits technical animations, abstract backgrounds and motion design. The look comes together quickly and can be adjusted live, making it practical for titles, backgrounds and explainer animations.

Key features

  • Points on surfaces or vertices
  • Density and distribution control
  • Dynamic line connections with distance limits
  • Connections-per-point limit
  • Animated deformations
