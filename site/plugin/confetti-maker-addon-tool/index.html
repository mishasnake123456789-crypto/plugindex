What Confetti Maker Addon Tool does

Confetti Maker V1 produces celebratory confetti in Blender in seconds. It suits logo reveals, product promos, YouTube intros, award graphics and countdowns.

Choose one of nine effects, such as Floating Down, Side Cannon Burst, Golden Award Rain or Finale Explosion, and the add-on builds the confetti system, camera, lighting and background in a 350-frame scene. Colors come from a custom pick or presets, roughness, metallic and size are adjustable, curved pieces improve close-ups, and a reset button cleans up.

Key features

  • Nine built-in confetti effects
  • One-click scene, camera and lighting setup
  • Color palettes, material and size controls
  • Quick reset and cleanup
