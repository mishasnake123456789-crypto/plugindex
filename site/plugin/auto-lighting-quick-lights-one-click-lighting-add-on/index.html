What Auto Lighting - Quick Lights (One-Click Lighting Add-On) does

Auto Lighting, Quick Lights, creates a rig of lights around a target in one click. Area and Spot lights are supported, and the Light Count and Light Distance controls update in real time.

Every light tracks the active object automatically, and per-light controls on selection adjust energy, size and more. Handy for quick product or model lighting without placing and aiming each light individually. Key capabilities include one-click surround rig and live Light Count and Distance.

Key features

  • One-click surround rig
  • Live Light Count and Distance
  • Auto Track To on the target
  • Per-light energy and size controls
