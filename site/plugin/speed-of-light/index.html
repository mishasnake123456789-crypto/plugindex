Blender add-on that builds studio lighting presets with real-time viewport editing and a backdrop.

What Speed Of Light does

Speed Of Light creates professional lighting for product renders, photography-style shots and character presentations in Blender. Ready presets let you start quickly, with editing directly in the viewport.

Rotation, height, spread, brightness and color temperature in Kelvin update live. Lights follow the selected object through constraints, or can be baked in place after setup, and an automatic backdrop supports studio-style renders. Three lighting presets are included, in a redesigned interface.

Key features

  • Lighting presets for product and character renders
  • Real-time rotation, height, spread, Kelvin control
  • Lights follow selected object, or bake in place
  • Automatic studio backdrop
