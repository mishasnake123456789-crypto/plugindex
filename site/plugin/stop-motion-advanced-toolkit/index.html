What Stop-Motion Advanced Toolkit does

Stop-Motion Advanced Toolkit helps build stop-motion animations, simulations and textures in Blender. Objects in a collection play back at stepped frames in Ping-Pong, Reverse, One Shot or Repeat modes, with probability controls.

Onion skinning, UDIM texturing per frame and tools that convert existing animation to stepped stop motion are included. It suits animators chasing a handmade, claymation look. The tools extend from posing and timing to texturing, which suits creators who want a complete stop-motion workflow within Blender.

Key features

  • Stepped frame playback modes
  • Onion skinning
  • Per-frame UDIM texturing
  • Convert animation to stepped stop motion
