Automated maze generator for Blender with size, complexity, visualization, lighting and animation options.

What Maze Gen does

Maze Gen creates mazes in a few clicks and offers options for shaping the result. Size and complexity range from simple layouts to very intricate ones.

The maze can be shown as grid tiles, extruded geometry or high-resolution assets, with instancing for walls and paths. Lighting and animation options, plus an included pack of wall and path elements, round out the add-on. It suits game prototypes, puzzle visuals and motion graphics.

Key features

  • Automatic maze generation
  • Adjustable size and complexity
  • Tile, extruded or high-res visualization
  • Lighting and animation options
  • Included wall and path elements
