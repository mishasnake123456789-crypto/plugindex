What AEJuice Big Impact Volume 3 does

Big Impact Volume 3 is a large library of ready-to-use disaster elements for filmmakers and editors. More than 410 assets are included, many shot from multiple perspectives, covering nuclear bombs, particle and massive explosions, shockwaves of fire and smoke as well as ground and air, plus meteors, dirt blasts and impact clouds.

It also provides dynamic dust and fire plus high-quality PNGs of clouds, lights, terrain and lava. Everything is delivered in Apple ProRes 4444 MOV format at 4K, usable in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and other editing software.
