What TypeToon does

TypeToon is a Pixflow typography toolkit that provides 64 colorful animated titles for After Effects. The titles use retro, cartoon-like letterforms with a three-dimensional appearance, ready to drop into a project.

They are set up for Full HD resolution output. The package is 135 MB, supports After Effects from CC 2015 through 2025, and was last updated in March 2025 at version 1.6.3. It suits promos, intros and social videos that need playful, eye-catching text without building the animation from scratch.
