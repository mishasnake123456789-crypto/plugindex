What Mettle Mantra does

Mettle Mantra provides 360-degree, VR and AR effects for After Effects and Premiere Pro. Styling tools work on flat footage as well as monoscopic and stereoscopic 360 content in over/under or side-by-side form.

Mantra ObjX imports Alembic, FBX, OBJ, glTF and USDZ, edits materials and exports to USDZ and glTF for Adobe Aero and Apple Reality Composer. Spherify makes 3D spheres from 360 media, and Graphix extrudes 2D graphics into 3D. A node-based panel merges effects without scripting, audio-reactive effects are available, and Globe Preview shows results on a sphere. The 3DNA engine supports Apple Silicon natively.

Key features

  • 360, VR and flat stylization effects
  • ObjX imports 3D models and exports USDZ and glTF
  • Spherify and Graphix for spheres and extruded graphics
  • Node-based panel, no scripting needed
  • Audio-reactive effects and Globe Preview
