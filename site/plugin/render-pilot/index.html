What Render Pilot does

Render Pilot is a Blender add-on that gathers the steps of preparing and launching a render into a single sidebar. Instead of hopping between properties tabs, you set up, test and render from one place.

It works with Cycles, Eevee and Blender's internal renderer, and covers a wide span of releases from 3.6 to 5.1. It is available as a Blender Extension under the GPL license, and documentation is included. Artists who switch between engines depending on the job are the ones likely to get the most from it.

Key features

  • Setup, test and render from one sidebar
  • Works with Cycles, Eevee and the internal renderer
  • Supports Blender 3.6 through 5.1
  • Blender Extension compatible
  • Documentation included
