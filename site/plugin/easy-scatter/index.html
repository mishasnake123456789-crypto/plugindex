Scatters objects around the Blender 3D cursor with a click or paints them along a stroke, with surface snapping.

What Easy Scatter does

Easy Scatter fills scenes with objects from an N-panel. Scatter Objects drops items around the 3D cursor on click, and Stroke Scatter lays them out live along a path you draw in the viewport.

Surface Snap uses raycasting to put copies on any chosen mesh, optionally aligned to the surface normal. Good for grass, rocks, props and set dressing where hand placement would be slow. The raycast and normal alignment options make objects sit naturally on uneven or sloped surfaces.

Key features

  • Click-based scatter around the 3D cursor
  • Stroke Scatter along a path
  • Surface snap using raycasting
  • Align to normal option
