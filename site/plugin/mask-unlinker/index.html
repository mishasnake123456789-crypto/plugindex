Detaches a layer's masks from its transforms in After Effects so you can move the layer alone.

What Mask Unlinker does

Mask Unlinker lets a mask stay put while its layer moves, which normally forces you to build a separate matte layer. Select a mask, press Unlink, move the layer, and press Link to reconnect.

It works with masks on 3D layers, supporting Z rotation and X/Y position, warns when a mask has feather points, and includes KBar support with icons and instructions. A quick-select icon grabs the mask of the selected layer. It needs After Effects 2018 or later.

Key features

  • Unlink and relink masks from layer transforms
  • Quick mask selection for the selected layer
  • Works on 3D layers
  • Warns about feather points
  • KBar support
