A Blender add-on for saving camera and viewport angles as bookmarks and switching between them instantly.

What Qol Tools: Camera Bookmarks does

Camera Bookmarks lets you save the angles you like in Blender and return to them without keyframing. One click detects whether you are in the 3D view or a camera and stores a matching perspective or camera bookmark, including nearly all of its settings, while animations are left untouched.

Restoring a bookmark switches camera types and brings back every stored setting, regardless of perspective type. A bookmark can be updated to a new angle with a button press, and all bookmarks are saved in the file. Artists comparing compositions, product shots and layouts will move between views faster.

Key features

  • One-click perspective or camera bookmarks
  • Stores nearly all camera or view settings
  • Restore switches camera types automatically
  • Update a bookmark to a new angle
  • Bookmarks saved in the file
