What Hide 'n Work : Save Hidden Objects And Collections In Selections does

ACP's Hide 'N Work manages what is shown or hidden in a Blender scene. Helper operators hide and reveal objects and collections in the viewport and in renders.

Visibility setups are saved as named HideSnapshots collected in one place. Make as many as you want, rename them by double clicking, and switch by clicking the eye icon beside the one you want. Complex scenes with different working states, such as modelling, lighting and rendering, become easier to organize.

Key features

  • Hide and show helper operators
  • Named HideSnapshots
  • Rename by double click
  • Switch with an eye icon click
  • Viewport and render visibility
