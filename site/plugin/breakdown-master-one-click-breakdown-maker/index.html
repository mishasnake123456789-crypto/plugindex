Blender add-on that animates a scene's objects into a build-up breakdown sequence with one click.

What Breakdown Master - One Click Breakdown Maker does

ZEG's Breakdown Master creates a build-up animation of your scene from a sidebar panel in the 3D View. A single click makes objects appear one after another.

Animate just the selection or everything, and choose which object types take part, such as Mesh, Curve, Light and Camera. Timing uses presets or custom values for frames between and per object, with styles like Pop or Fall Down and offsets for location, rotation and scale. Great for showing how a model was assembled.

Key features

  • One-click breakdown animation
  • Choose object types to include
  • Preset or custom timing
  • Pop and Fall Down styles
  • Offsets for location, rotation and scale
