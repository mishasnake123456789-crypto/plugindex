What Randomatic 2 does

Randomatic 2, by Romain Cousin, produces random and wiggling movement on 2D, 3D and color properties in After Effects. It is for artists who want organic variation without hand-keying every value, and version 2.12 runs from CS6 to After Effects 2026.

A Transfer function lets a controller be moved outside its original composition, and applying it to Position properties switches to relative mode automatically. A Stretch function scales keyframe timing and keeps the selected keyframes selected afterwards. Keyframe color labels are preserved, which helps in busy timelines.

Key features

  • Random and wiggle on 2D, 3D and color properties
  • Transfer function for controllers across comps
  • Automatic relative mode on Position
  • Keyframe Stretch keeps selection
  • Works from CS6 through 2026
