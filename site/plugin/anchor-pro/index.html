Plugin for After Effects and Premiere Pro that repositions anchor points relative to a clip, sequence or selection.

What Anchor Pro does

Anchor Pro by Leyero manages anchor point placement in After Effects and Premiere Pro. The anchor can be moved relative to the clip itself, the whole sequence, or a group of selected clips, and dragging gives precise manual control.

Alpha Mode handles clips with transparent backgrounds and can ignore drop shadows. A position can be saved to align other clips, and existing animation is kept while the anchor moves. In After Effects it supports the Transform effect, separated dimensions, 3D layers and Spell Book integration. Keyboard shortcuts are customizable.

Key features

  • Anchor relative to clip, sequence or selection
  • Drag the anchor for precise control
  • Alpha Mode that can ignore drop shadows
  • Keeps existing animation while moving
  • Customizable keyboard shortcuts
