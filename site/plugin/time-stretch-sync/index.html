Blender add-on that keeps the playhead, frame ranges and markers aligned when Time Stretching is changed.

What Time Stretch Sync does

Time Stretch Sync solves a common mismatch in Blender, where Time Stretching in output settings changes timing but leaves the timeline out of step. A new Sync section appears beneath Output > Frame Range > Time Stretching.

The playhead stays at the same relative frame while stretching is active, and the timeline and preview range start and end frames follow the stretch values, as do markers. Editors retiming sequences avoid recalculating positions manually.

Key features

  • Sync section under Time Stretching
  • Playhead keeps relative position
  • Timeline and preview ranges follow stretch
  • Markers synced to stretch values
