Blender add-on listing all scene cameras in the viewport for quick viewing, switching and light editing.

What Cameralist does

CameraList gives direct access to every camera in a Blender scene from the 3D viewport, so you can change shots without leaving your working view.

A list shows scene cameras, with Next and Previous operators that can be given custom shortcuts. The active camera name is displayed, a local camera toggle switches between local and scene modes, and camera name and background settings can be edited in the interface. Documentation is included. Useful for scenes with many shots.

Key features

  • Scene camera list with quick switching
  • Next and Previous operators
  • Active camera name display
  • Local camera toggle
  • Edit camera name and background
