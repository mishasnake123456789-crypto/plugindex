Offers a quick editor-switching menu in Blender, with toggles for toolbar, sidebar, header and area splitting.

What Cgp: Editor Switch does

Cgp: Editor Switch provides an alternative to Blender's default editor-type dropdown. Its menu presents editors in a different layout, so changing from the 3D Viewport to the Shader Editor or timeline takes fewer steps.

Quick toggles show or hide the toolbar, sidebar and header, and another option splits the current area without grabbing the corner of an edge. Documentation is included. Users who frequently rearrange their workspace layouts will find it speeds things up.

Key features

  • Editor-switch menu
  • Toolbar and sidebar toggles
  • Header toggle
  • Quick area split
