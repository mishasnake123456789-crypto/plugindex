What CineBrush does

CineBrush is an Nx Color plugin that gives DaVinci Resolve footage a hand-painted appearance. It is aimed at editors and colorists who want stylized, illustrated shots rather than a purely photographic finish.

The tool works with Resolve 20 and 19 and runs on macOS, Windows and Linux, so a team on mixed machines can share the same look. Version 2.0 added alpha support, letting the effect sit cleanly over transparent layers, along with performance improvements. A demo video is available from the developer for anyone who wants to preview the result before trying it.

Key features

  • Painterly look for DaVinci Resolve footage
  • Runs on macOS, Windows and Linux
