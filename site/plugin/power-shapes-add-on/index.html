What Power Shapes Add-On does

Power Shapes expands Blender's primitive menu with thirty extra shapes designed for low poly modeling and quick blockouts. They range from cubes and cones to geospheres and torus variants, giving you more starting forms than the default set.

How each shape is added is configurable: it can be added to the scene or replace the current selection, and the selection can be kept or swapped for the new primitive. A transform setting activates the gizmo immediately after adding, and an adjustable scale sets the initial size. Game artists and prop modelers blocking out forms will work faster.

Key features

  • 30 new primitives
  • Add to scene or replace selection
  • Gizmo activates on creation
  • Adjustable initial scale
