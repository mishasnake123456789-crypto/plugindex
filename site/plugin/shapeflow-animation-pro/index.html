Animation add-on for Blender where you change a mesh's shape over time with sculpt and deformation tools.

What Shapeflow Animation Pro does

ShapeFlow Animation Pro animates by reshaping rather than rigging. Poses and deformation states come directly from edited and sculpted mesh shapes.

Multiple shader materials can be assigned to selected faces of a single mesh through Blender's material slot system, and it works alongside shape keys and other native tools. Motion designers animating blobs, characters or morphing logos can skip building a rig for every movement. Because shape changes are keyed over time, even complex deformations stay easy to revise.

Key features

  • Sculpt-based animation workflow
  • Poses from mesh edits
  • Multiple materials on selected faces
  • Works with native Blender tools
