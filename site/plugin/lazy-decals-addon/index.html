What 🎁Lazy Decals Addon does

Lazy3D's Lazy Decals Addon makes sticking graphics onto models simple. Import a decal, drag it, and it hugs the surface by itself; a single click finishes the setup.

Built-in grunge and wear masks add realistic scuffing, and custom roughness and normal maps are accepted. It runs with Cycles and Eevee, and documentation is provided. Prop and hard-surface artists adding labels, stickers and dirt avoid building shader networks for each one.

Key features

  • Automatic surface snapping
  • One-click decal setup
  • Built-in grunge and wear masks
  • Custom roughness and normal maps
  • Cycles and Eevee support
