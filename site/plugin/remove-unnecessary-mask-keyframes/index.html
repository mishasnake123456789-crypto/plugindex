What Remove Unnecessary Mask Keyframes does

Remove Unnecessary Mask Keyframes tidies masks tracked with the built-in mask tracker, which writes a keyframe on every frame. The script reviews them and deletes those whose shape is too similar to the one before.

The result is a lighter, easier-to-edit animation without losing the motion. It is free and compatible with After Effects CS6 through 2025, and suits rotoscopers and compositors. Fewer keyframes make later edits to a tracked mask quicker, and the script costs nothing to try.

Key features

  • Cleans mask tracker keyframes
  • Compares each keyframe to its predecessor
  • Adjustable by similarity threshold
  • Supports CS6 through 2025
  • Free
