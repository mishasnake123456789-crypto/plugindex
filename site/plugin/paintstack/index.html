Paintstack

Developer: MarkSeam

FreeBlender

Adds layer-based texture painting to Blender with blend modes, masks and export of standard PBR maps.

What Paintstack does

Paintstack brings a layer stack to Blender's texture paint mode. Color, transparent, bump, roughness and subsurface layers are painted in the viewport, and each connects straight to the Principled BSDF.

Layers combine through six blend modes (Mix, Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Soft Light, Add) with opacity per layer, update live, and accept masks. When finished, standard PBR maps are exported from the visible stack. Texture painters who miss layer workflows from dedicated paint tools will find it familiar.

Key features

  • Color, transparent, bump, roughness and subsurface layers
  • Six blend modes with per-layer opacity
  • Layer masks
  • Connects directly to Principled BSDF
  • Exports PBR maps from the visible stack
