Sends finished Premiere Pro sequences to Verbit for captions, subtitles and localization and returns the results.

What Verbit does

Verbit connects Premiere Pro to its captioning services. Editors submit a completed sequence directly from the application and carry on working while the captions, subtitles or localization are produced.

Status is tracked in Premiere Pro, and finished captions return to the plugin automatically. Other offerings include transcription, translation and audio description, with AI. The plugin is free to install, but a Verbit subscription is needed. It helps teams that need accessible and localized versions of their videos.

Key features

  • Submit sequences to Verbit from Premiere
  • Captions, subtitles and localization
  • Track submission status in Premiere
  • Captions delivered back automatically
  • Transcription, translation and audio description
