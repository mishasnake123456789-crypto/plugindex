What Creature Kitbash - Stylized Animals Vol. 1 does

Creature Kitbash - Stylized Animals Vol. 1 supplies a collection of 195 body parts, including heads, torsos and limbs, designed in an exaggerated cartoon manner. You assemble them to invent new creatures quickly.

The parts load through Asset Manager integration, and mirroring and placement tools help position them. A single click merges the pieces using boolean or remesh tools into one mesh ready for sculpting. It can also be combined with other Creature Kitbash packs for greater variety.

Key features

  • 195 cartoon-style body parts
  • Asset Manager integration
  • Mirroring and placement tools
  • Combine into one mesh via boolean or remesh
  • Works with other Creature Kitbash packs
