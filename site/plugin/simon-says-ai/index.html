What Simon Says AI does

Simon Says AI offers speech-to-text, captioning, subtitling and translation for Premiere Pro editors. Results are frame-accurate, with a timecode attached to every word, and AI helps find meaningful soundbites.

Markers can be added to clips, and captions or translated subtitles to sequences. A visual transcript and subtitle editor lets you invite teammates, and output can be exported for Adobe and other NLEs. Support covers one hundred languages. Transcripts can be exported to Adobe tools and other editing software, which keeps collaboration flexible.

Key features

  • Transcription, captions, subtitles and translation
  • 100 languages
  • Timecode on every word
  • Markers, captions and translated subtitles in Premiere
  • Team transcript and subtitle editor
