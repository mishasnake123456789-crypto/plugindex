What Center Focus Depth Of Field Add-On does

Center Focus Depth Of Field is a small add-on that updates the active camera's focus distance so it matches whatever sits at the center of the frame. It follows as you move the camera.

A Smooth Transition option makes focus changes more gradual. Options are switched on from a panel in the camera's Object Data tab. Photographers and animators who like autofocus-style behavior will avoid fiddling with a focus object. It can be switched off whenever you want manual control again.

Key features

  • Focus distance follows the frame center
  • Real-time updates
  • Smooth Transition option
  • Controls in the camera Object Data tab
