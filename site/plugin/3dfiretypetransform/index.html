What 3Dfiretypetransform does

3Dfiretypetransform is a Blender add-on that converts every 3D file in a folder to a format of your choice. It spares you from opening and exporting each model manually.

On Blender 2.8x and newer it can output OBJ, FBX, GLTF and X3D, while the 2.79x version adds 3DS in place of GLTF. English editions exist for Blender 2.8 and 2.79, and a Chinese edition for 2.8. Documentation and a demo video are provided, covering Blender 2.79 through 3.0.

Key features

  • Batch converts a folder of 3D files
  • OBJ, FBX, GLTF and X3D export on Blender 2.8x+
  • OBJ, FBX, 3DS and X3D on Blender 2.79x
  • English and Chinese editions
  • Documentation and demo video
