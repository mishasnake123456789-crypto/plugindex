What Center On Interest Addon does

Center On Interest solves a small but annoying problem in Blender: framing a selection normally changes your zoom or view angle. This add-on recenters on the selected object, vertex, edge or face while leaving perspective and zoom as they were.

That stability makes it easier to carry out precise serial operations on one element after another. The default hotkey is Ctrl+Shift+F. It is marked as Blender extension compatible, includes documentation and is distributed under the GPL license.

Key features

  • Centers on objects, vertices, edges or faces
  • Keeps perspective and zoom unchanged
  • Default hotkey Ctrl+Shift+F
  • Extension-compatible
  • Documentation included
