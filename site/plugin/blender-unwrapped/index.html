What Blender Unwrapped does

Blender Unwrapped is a stats tracker for Blender, in the spirit of a yearly recap. It records how much time you spend in the app across versions, in each file, and on rendering, and it even counts default-cube deletions.

It generates a colorful stats image you can share with friends and keeps a daily streak counter. A fun way to look back on your Blender habits. Among its tools are time tracked across Blender versions and per-file and rendering time.

Key features

  • Time tracked across Blender versions
  • Per-file and rendering time
  • Shareable stats image
  • Daily streak counter
