What Blender To Unity does

Blender To Unity, made by B3dHub, simplifies getting assets out of Blender and into the Unity engine. Instead of configuring export settings by hand each time, you send objects, animations, colliders and level-of-detail variants across with a handful of clicks.

Anyone who regularly shuttles models between the two programs, from game developers to architects, is the target audience. The add-on works with Blender versions 3.3 up to 5.1, is delivered as a Blender extension, includes documentation and is released under the GPL.

Key features

  • Meshes, animation, colliders and LOD levels exported
  • Few-click export workflow for Unity
  • Supports Blender 3.3 to 5.1
  • Packaged as a Blender extension
  • Documentation included
