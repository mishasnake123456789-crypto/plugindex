Geometry Nodes arrow generator for Blender with six setups and 20+ preset arrows for technical animation.

What Arrowify does

Arrowify creates arrows for technical 3D animations that show direction, flow, heat exchange, air pressure or wind. It ships six Geometry Nodes setups: UV Arrows, Simple Arrows, Morph Arrows, Curve Flow Arrows, Area Arrows and Array Arrows, each giving a different look.

More than 20 preset arrow styles are provided, such as chevron, pointer, double-headed and curved, and you can use your own objects as arrows. It works on curves and on mesh lines. It is a good fit for explainers, engineering visualizations and infographic-style animation.

Key features

  • Six Geometry Nodes arrow setups
  • 20+ preset arrow styles
  • Custom arrow shapes supported
  • Works on curves and mesh lines
