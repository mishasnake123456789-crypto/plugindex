Blender add-on that builds a lit world from Blender's bundled HDRIs with one click, with blur, flat background and rotation.

What Quick World does

Quick World gets you a lit environment fast by using the HDRIs that already ship with Blender. Choose one and click Generate World.

It gives feedback messages during generation and offers a blur effect with adjustable intensity. A flat background option replaces the HDRI for camera rays with a chosen solid color, and the HDRI can be rotated. No external files are needed. It suits quick renders, lighting tests and previews.

Key features

  • Uses HDRIs bundled with Blender
  • One-click world generation
  • Adjustable HDRI blur
  • Solid color background for camera rays
  • HDRI rotation
