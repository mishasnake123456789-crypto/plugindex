What Greyscalegorilla for Teams - Renewal does

Greyscalegorilla for Teams - Renewal continues team access to Greyscalegorilla's Cinema 4D tools and assets for studios. An admin manages who can use what through the Teams Dashboard, and a single bill covers multiple licenses, payable by credit card, purchase order and other methods.

Studios get ongoing high-priority customer support with a dedicated account manager, plus onboarding help and account management. Unlimited render-only licenses are included for a growing studio. It is relevant for production companies that have outgrown individual licenses and need centralized administration.
