What Camera Caddy does

Camera Caddy puts camera management in a single place inside Blender's N-Panel. New cameras are added and switched from the same interface, which keeps shot setups easy to follow.

Focal length can be set per camera, depth of field adjusted for precise framing, and transitions between angles can be animated. Documentation is included. Animators and visualization artists cutting between several viewpoints will appreciate not having to dig through the outliner.

Key features

  • Add, switch and manage multiple cameras
  • Per-camera focal length
  • Depth of field adjustment
  • Animated camera transitions
