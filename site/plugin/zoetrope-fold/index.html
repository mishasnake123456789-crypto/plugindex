What Zoetrope FOLD does

Zoetrope FOLD is a generative tool for After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve that turns video from your timeline into constantly folding three-dimensional geometry. Forms can stay simple or grow elaborate.

Animation is either automatic, using built-in behaviors, or manual through a seamlessly looping slider per axis. Resolution, growth, complexity and pull change how the shape tessellates. Direct, Striped and Streaked raster modes control how footage covers the surface. It appeals to architecture visualizers, graphic designers, live performers and motion artists.

Key features

  • Auto behaviors or looping manual animation
  • Resolution, growth, complexity and pull controls
  • Direct, Striped and Streaked raster modes
  • X, Y and Z rotation and translation
  • Every parameter can be keyframed
