What Autokroma PlumePack does

Autokroma PlumePack shrinks a Premiere Pro project to what the edit actually needs. It copies, trims or transcodes the used portions of media into a new consolidated project and replaces the links smartly.

Dependent files such as proxies, After Effects assets, sidecars and XML are copied too. Three optimization modes are offered, a Toolbox adds trimming, unmerging and track reordering, and presets and a queue handle batches. A free mode applies to projects under 60GB.

Key features

  • Copy, trim or transcode only used media
  • Copies proxies, sidecars, XML and After Effects assets
  • Smart Replace in the new project
  • Three optimization modes
  • Free mode for projects under 60GB
