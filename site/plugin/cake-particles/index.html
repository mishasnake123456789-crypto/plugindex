What Cake_Particles does

Cake_Particles is a Blender add-on by scaryplasmon that converts particle simulations into keyframed animated objects. That makes particle effects usable in game engines and other software that cannot read Blender's particle systems.

Meshes, Grease Pencil objects, metaballs and cameras are all supported, offers a bake-step option that keys only every n frames, and manages bakes through collections. The full simulation can be exported to FBX, with quick particle export to GLB. A progress bar for baking was added in version 5.0.0.

Key features

  • Bakes particles to keyframed objects
  • Meshes, Grease Pencil, metaballs and cameras
  • Bake step to key every n frames
  • Collection-based bake management
  • FBX and GLB export
