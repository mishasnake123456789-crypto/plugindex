What Smartevo does

Smartevo is a plugin for Premiere Pro and After Effects that surfaces your team's annotations in the workspace where you edit. Feedback no longer lives in a separate window or email thread that has to be checked manually.

Keeping comments next to the timeline reduces back-and-forth and the risk of missing a note. It works with version 14.0 and later of both apps, and provides a language selection setting, improved in version 1.1.1. Teams that review and annotate video together are the natural fit.

Key features

  • Team annotations inside the editing workspace
  • No switching screens to read feedback
  • Fewer lost comments
  • Premiere Pro and After Effects 14.0+
  • Language selection
