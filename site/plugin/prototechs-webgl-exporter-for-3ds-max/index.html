What ProtoTech's WebGL Exporter For 3ds Max® does

ProtoTech's WebGL Exporter converts solid bodies from 3ds Max directly to HTML, producing interactive 3D models that open in common browsers. The models can be embedded in web pages for online viewing, sharing designs without special software.

Material export includes colors and textures at the face or body level. Precision values let you trade file size against quality, while tessellation can be set at the body or face level. Supported versions are 3ds Max 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. Designers, product teams and archviz artists can use it to put models online.

Key features

  • Solid bodies exported directly to HTML
  • Face and body-level colors and textures
  • Precision values for size and quality
  • Tessellation at body or face level
  • 3ds Max 2023 through 2026
