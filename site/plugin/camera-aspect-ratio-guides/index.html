Blender add-on that overlays composition guides, aspect ratios and safe areas on the camera view.

What Camera Aspect Ratio Guides does

This add-on draws live composition overlays on Blender's camera viewport. Guides include harmonious triangles, center cross, diagonals, golden triangle, golden ratio and rule of thirds, helping you frame shots with intention.

Several aspect ratio presets can be shown at once, with custom width and height, optional thirds and center lines, and a passepartout for letterbox bars; buttons give instant 4:3, 1:1, 9:16 and 16:9. Safe areas such as action safe and title safe are included, and the guides can optionally be burned into renders through non-destructive compositor integration.

Key features

  • Rule of thirds, golden ratio and other composition guides
  • Multiple aspect ratio presets at once
  • Passepartout and one-click common ratios
  • Action and title safe areas
  • Optional render burn-in via compositor
