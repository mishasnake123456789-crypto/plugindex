What Animation Composer Pro does

Animation Composer Pro lets artists build a personal library inside After Effects and Premiere Pro. Your own After Effects compositions, MOGRT files, footage and audio files can all be browsed, previewed and added from a single panel.

Composition previews appear instantly, MOGRTs can be imported straight from the panel, and hover-scrub previews are rendered automatically for footage. Pitch shifting works with your audio files, and large sound effect libraries can be browsed and previewed. It is aimed at artists who maintain their own template collections or big audio libraries.

Key features

  • Browse and preview your own comps instantly
  • Import MOGRT files directly
  • Automatic hover-scrub footage previews
  • Pitch shifting for your own audio
  • Browse sound effect libraries
