What 3ds Max 场景安全工具 does

This Autodesk scene security tool, whose listing is in Chinese, targets known third-party malicious scripts in 3ds Max, such as CRP\ADSL, ALC, ALC2, PhysXPluginMfx, MSCPROP and their variants. It finds them in startup scripts and scene files however they load.

It helps stop 3ds Max from saving scenes corrupted by those scripts, asks for confirmation before cleaning anything, and also works silently in non-interactive cases like command-line rendering and batch scripts. Actions are logged to Max.log and the MAXScript listener, and you can keep working even if you decline cleanup. An optional notice announces new versions.

Key features

  • Removes listed malicious scripts from startup and scene files
  • Helps prevent saving of corrupted scenes
  • Asks for confirmation before cleaning
  • Silent mode for batch and command-line use
  • Logs to Max.log and the listener
