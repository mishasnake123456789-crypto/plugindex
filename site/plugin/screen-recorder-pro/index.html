Offline Blender add-on that takes screenshots, records the screen and builds optimized GIFs without external software.

What Screen Recorder Pro does

Screen Recorder Pro captures images and video straight from the Blender interface.

GIFs use multi-pass compression with a custom 128-color palette, diff-based frame updates and Bayer dithering to prevent banding. Video encoding rounds dimensions to even numbers and forces yuv420p for broad playback. Region capture is pixel-perfect, and an audio scanner detects devices. It works offline. Tutorial authors and bug reporters can capture work without installing separate software, and the files are optimized for sharing and playback on various systems and browsers.

Key features

  • Screenshots, recordings and GIFs
  • Multi-pass GIF compression
  • Compatible video encoding
  • Pixel-perfect region capture
  • Audio device scanner
