What Motioncraft - Quick Animation & Camera Tools does

Motioncraft is a compact Blender add-on that creates keyframed motion with a few clicks, helping with logos, UI pieces, product shots, props and camera work without hand-setting every key.

Pick an effect such as Bounce, Spin, Pop In, Shake, Slide, Fade or Noise Location and apply it to selected objects or a whole collection. Duration, strength, randomness, loops, offsets and easing are adjustable, and a second effect can be chained on. Camera helpers rely on Track To for following, front, side and top views and distance. Presets include Logo Bounce and UI Pop, and baking tools finish the job.

Key features

  • Library of ready motion effects
  • Works on meshes, text, lights and cameras
  • Timing, easing, randomness and loop controls
  • Effect chaining
  • Camera follow tools and bake options
