What SoundXtract does

SoundXtract detects musical elements and uses them to drive editing in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Beats, drums and instrumentals produce markers, and clips can be placed and aligned against those cues.

Video effects and keyframes can be tied to sounds so visuals pulse with the music. Markers work on both clips and the sequence, and multicam footage gets automatic cut and solo handling. Editors and motion designers making music videos, edits and beat-synced content can save much of the manual marker work.

Key features

  • Beat markers from beats, drums and instrumentals
  • Place and align clips to musical cues
  • Link effects and keyframes to sounds
  • Markers on clips and sequences
  • Automatic cut and solo handling for multicam
