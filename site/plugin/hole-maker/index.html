Blender add-on that cuts holes of exact diameter and depth at a chosen vertex, with optional countersink.

What Hole Maker does

Hole Maker, by Tiemen's addons, adds precise holes to a mesh. Pick a vertex as the center of the hole, then use Make Hole(s) in Edit Mode to cut it at the diameter and depth you specify.

Direction can follow either the vertex normal or the 3D cursor, and an optional countersink adds a second hole whose top and bottom diameters are set independently. It is useful for mechanical parts, enclosures and product models where hole sizes must be accurate.

Key features

  • Exact diameter, depth and position
  • Direction by vertex normal or 3D cursor
  • Optional countersink with separate diameters
  • Edit Mode operation
