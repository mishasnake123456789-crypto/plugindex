What Rig UE5 Exporter does

Rig UE5 Exporter simplifies sending an armature from Blender to Unreal Engine 5. It prepares the rig and writes it out in a way that suits that engine.

Game artists who build characters in Blender can use it to avoid repeated trial and error with export settings. It keeps the handoff between modeling and engine as direct as possible.

Key features

  • Exports rigs for UE5
  • Prepares armatures for the engine
  • Reduces export trial and error
