Blender add-on that saves camera states as snapshots and animates or renders them with one camera.

What Quick Snapshot Camera does

Quick Snapshot Camera stores complete camera setups as reusable snapshots on a single camera. Product visualization, archviz and storyboard artists can switch viewpoints without managing many cameras.

Snapshots capture position, rotation, focal length, lens shift and depth of field. Keyframes can be generated from the list with set spacing, a turntable generator uses a focus tracker point, and static or timeline render modes include per-snapshot toggles. Whole setups save to a .qsc file. Documentation and a tutorial scene are included.

Key features

  • Snapshots of full camera settings
  • Keyframe and turntable generation
  • Static or timeline render modes
  • Save and load .qsc setups
