What Fade It does

Fade It is Matin Kazerooni's tool for After Effects that adds fades to layers without hand-keying opacity. It is meant for everyday projects where quick, consistent fades are needed.

A variety of fade options can be chosen, covering both fade-ins and fade-outs. The latest version updates the license framework and fixes compatibility with After Effects 2025. It works with After Effects 2020 through 2025, suiting editors and motion designers who add fades constantly.

Key features

  • Fade In and Fade Out transitions
