What QLE (Quick Lighting Environment) does

QLE adds a quick lighting rig to Blender from a panel in Properties > Scene. A single click creates a world, four area lights, a target empty and a backdrop.

The world is called QLE World, with strength 0.25 and a Blackbody color of 5454. Left light is orange at 3800, Fill and Back are matched at 5454, and Right is blue at 20,000. All lights connect to an Empty called Lights_Target through Track To constraints, so aiming them is simple. Product shots and quick tests start from a balanced setup.

Key features

  • One-click world, lights and backdrop
  • Four area lights with set Blackbody values
  • Lights_Target empty with Track To
  • Panel in Properties > Scene
