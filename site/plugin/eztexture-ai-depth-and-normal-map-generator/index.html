What Eztexture - Ai Depth & Normal Map Generator does

EzTexture converts flat images into AI-generated depth and normal maps without leaving Blender. It suits 3D artists, game developers and concept designers who need quick surface detail.

Generation is one click through an EXE/ONNX backend, with no coding or node setup. Results can be applied as a modifier or shader displacement in Cycles and Eevee, with invert options and automatic subdivision and smoothing. The panel previews the original, depth and normal maps, and a non-destructive toggle is included.

Key features

  • AI depth and normal map generation
  • Modifier or shader displacement output
  • In-panel previews
  • One-click, non-destructive operation
