What Carver does

Carver offers several tools for cutting into or building objects in Blender's 3D View. It shipped with Blender 4.1 and now continues as a separate release with limited support.

The tools are invoked through Ctrl, Shift or X in the viewport. The add-on is about 28 KB, needs no special permissions and is released under GPL v3 or later, for Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. Hard surface modelers who prefer quickly drawing cuts get a lightweight option.

Key features

  • Multiple carve and create tools
  • Invoked via Ctrl, Shift or X
  • Lightweight 28 KB download
  • For Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
