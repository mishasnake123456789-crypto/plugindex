What ReScanX does

Plugin LyfeX makes ReScanX, a scan reconstruction effect for After Effects and Premiere Pro. It reprocesses footage so it looks as though it were drawn by electronic beams, producing results like phosphor contours, terrain maps, ghostly traces or monitoring screens.

Beams and scans have detailed controls, timing and display settings can be adjusted, and a built-in preset library offers starting points. Editors and motion artists producing sci-fi interfaces, tech titles or experimental visuals can rework ordinary clips into an electronic aesthetic.

Key features

  • Phosphor, terrain-map, ghost and surveillance looks
  • Detailed beam and scan controls
  • Timing and display adjustments
  • Built-in preset library
  • Works in After Effects and Premiere Pro
