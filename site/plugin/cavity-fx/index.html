Blender add-on that brings solid-mode cavity shading into rendered output for EEVEE and Cycles with one click.

What Cavity Fx does

Cavity FX carries the cavity shading seen in Blender's solid viewport into material rendering and final images. It works in both EEVEE and Cycles.

Activate it with a button, then tune Crease and Edge intensity sliders with instant viewport feedback. You can preview the effect or bake it to an animation, using a realtime mode that refreshes every second or a manual interval. World and screen space are supported, and a compositor-ready node group manages temporary files and nodes automatically.

Key features

  • One-click activation with Crease and Edge sliders
  • Works in EEVEE and Cycles
  • Preview or bake to animation
  • Realtime or manual update interval
  • Compositor-ready node group
