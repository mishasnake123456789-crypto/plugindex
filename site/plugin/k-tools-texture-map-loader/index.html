What K-Tools: Texture Map Loader does

K-Tools: Texture Map Loader, by Robert-Kezives, takes the effort out of hooking up a full set of texture maps to a shader material in Blender. It is listed under Material.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.
