Quick muscle helper for 3ds Max: put a bone, or a cylinder carrying a Flex modifier, across two picked points.

What Muscle Setup does

Muscle Setup is a small 3ds Max script for building simple muscle-style rigs. With two points picked, it creates either a bone or a cylinder that already has a Flex modifier applied between them.

It installs by drag and drop as an .mcr macroscript and appears under the Toke Scripts category. It is listed at version 1.00. Character riggers who want a fast way to add secondary deformation, such as bulging or jiggling forms, can use it as a starting point instead of building each muscle manually.

Key features

  • Creates a bone or cylinder between two points
  • Cylinder comes with a Flex modifier applied
  • Drag-and-drop installation
  • Found under the Toke Scripts category
  • Delivered as an .mcr macroscript, version 1.00
