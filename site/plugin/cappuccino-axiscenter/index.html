What Cappuccino-Axiscenter does

Cappuccino-Axiscenter, from Caffuchino, borrows ideas from the axis-centering approach used in Cinema 4D. It is the opening entry in the Cappuccino series.

The goal is to give artists a pivot and origin experience similar to what they know from Cinema 4D, so repositioning an object's origin feels familiar. It is available as a Blender extension for version 4.4, includes documentation and is released under the MIT license. People moving between Cinema 4D and Blender are the most likely audience.

Key features

  • Cinema 4D-inspired origin control
  • Familiar pivot workflow for C4D users
  • First add-on in the Cappuccino series
  • Blender extension for version 4.4
  • MIT license with documentation
