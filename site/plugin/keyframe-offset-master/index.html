What Keyframe Offset Master does

Keyframe Offset Master offsets the timing of copies of the same animation so they follow one another. It works on objects, armatures and other animated items.

Offset modes include Linear, Exponential, Ease In/Out, Random and Custom, and ordering can follow name, reverse order, X, Y or Z position, distance from the origin, active object or cursor, numeric suffix, or chance. A backup system protects the original keys. Motion designers get waves and domino effects quickly.

Key features

  • Six offset modes
  • Ten ordering options
  • Works with objects and armatures
  • Backup system
