What Asset Shelf Shortcut Display does

Asset Shelf Shortcut Display, by The-Loose-Spirit, puts the hotkey for an asset right in the asset shelf, so you do not have to remember or look up shortcuts. It is listed under User Interface.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.0.1 or newer.
