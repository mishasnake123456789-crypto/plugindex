What FxFactory Lines does

FxFactory Lines provides animated lines and arrows to point out details or add graphic flair. It targets After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and also Final Cut Pro X and Motion.

Lines and arrows are customisable, and hand-drawn styles are available in Premiere Pro, After Effects and Motion as well as Final Cut Pro. Presets exist for Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro, Motion and After Effects, and Final Cut Pro also gets custom line templates with animated multi-point paths.
