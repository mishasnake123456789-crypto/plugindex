What Test Pilot : Aerodynamics Test Simulator does

Test Pilot lets you watch airflow around a design inside Blender. It is aimed at drones, planes and RC vehicles, and can also be used on projects that are not for 3D printing, such as car designs.

Fluid and smoke colors and viewport coloring can be adjusted, density and temperature are controllable, and custom fans and turbines can be created and viewed in motion. A resolution setting keeps it usable on low-end computers. Hobbyists and designers get a visual sense of flow before building anything.

Key features

  • Visual aerodynamics inside a 3D environment
  • Adjustable fluid, smoke and viewport colors
  • Custom fans and turbines
  • Density and temperature controls
  • Resolution setting for low-end PCs
