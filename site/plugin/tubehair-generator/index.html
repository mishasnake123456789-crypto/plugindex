What Tubehair Generator does

Tubehair Generator turns geometry you have modeled into hair curves. It accepts tubes, curves or mesh strips and converts them with one click.

Hair roots are found automatically without edge selection, strands are recalculated and snapped to the surface without altering the original, and Hair Sculpt Mode opens straight away. Character artists can build styled hair shapes manually and then use them as grooming curves. Under the hood you get one-click conversion to Hair Curves and automatic root edge detection.

Key features

  • One-click conversion to Hair Curves
  • Automatic root edge detection
  • Strands snapped to the surface
  • Opens directly in Hair Sculpt Mode
