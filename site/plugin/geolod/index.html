Generates UV-safe LOD chains in Blender with cleanup, named collections and Weighted Normals.

What Geolod does

GeoLOD creates levels of detail from the active mesh in one click. It makes 1 to 10 LODs, stored in a BaseName_LODs collection with objects named BaseName_LOD1 and so on, a LOD_Level custom property and an optional LOD0 copy.

Topology is cleaned with UV awareness before reduction, and a Weighted Normals modifier is added to each LOD. Game artists get tidy, consistent LOD sets without UV damage. In practice that means 1 to 10 LODs in one click and named LOD collection with custom property.

Key features

  • 1 to 10 LODs in one click
  • Named LOD collection with custom property
  • UV-aware cleanup before reduction
  • Weighted Normals on every LOD
