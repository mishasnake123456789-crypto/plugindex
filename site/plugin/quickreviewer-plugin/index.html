What QuickReviewer plugin does

The QuickReviewer plugin links Adobe Creative Cloud apps, including Premiere Pro, to an online proofing platform. Designers send work for review and approval directly, avoiding export steps and email chains.

You can create folders, upload files, view proofs with reviewer comments, and upload revisions with version history tracked. Unlimited reviewers can be given access without needing Creative Cloud licenses, and email templates, teams and SMTP are configurable. The platform also syncs PDF scrolling and comment jumping with InDesign.

Key features

  • Export and upload directly to QuickReviewer
  • View proofs with reviewer comments
  • Revision uploads with version history
  • Unlimited reviewers without CC licenses
  • Customizable email templates and teams
