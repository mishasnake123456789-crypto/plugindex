What Prism 2 does

Prism 2 makes After Effects labels easier to manage. It displays colour swatches beside label names and lets you colour project items as well as layers, with a Colour Masks button for mask work.

Toggles skip locked or unmatched layers and masks, colour by delimiter suits character rigs, and the starting label colour can cycle. Shift-click shortcuts move label banks to the top or bottom, or set active labels to all. A fix helps custom label colours load on non-English installs.

Key features

  • Colour swatches beside label names
  • Colour project items and masks
  • Skip locked or unmatched layers and masks
  • Colour by delimiter for character rigs
  • Shift+click shortcuts for label banks
