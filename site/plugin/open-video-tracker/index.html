What Open Video Tracker does

Open Video Tracker is a Blender add-on by Haseeb Ahmed that analyzes footage using photogrammetry methods to recover camera and scene information from video. It targets artists who need to match 3D content to live-action plates.

It covers Blender releases from 3.6 to 4.5 and can be used with Blender Internal, Cycles and Eevee as render engines. The add-on is distributed under a GPL license, is flagged as extension compatible, and ships with documentation.

Key features

  • Photogrammetry-driven video tracking
  • Blender 3.6 through 4.5
  • Works with Internal, Cycles and Eevee
  • GPL licensed, extension compatible
  • Documentation included
