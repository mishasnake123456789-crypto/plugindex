What Real Ease does

Real Ease is a grape design tool for creating natural-feeling motion in After Effects. Starting from two keyframes, it adds an intermediate keyframe that shapes the movement around forces of acceleration and deceleration rather than relying on generic ease curves.

You can control the length of animation near top speed and interrupt constant-velocity motion in the middle. A smooth animation preset is included, and animation curves can be copied and pasted between properties. Animators who want realistic, weighty movement without hand-tweaking graph editor handles will find it quick.

Key features

  • Smooth animation from two keyframes
  • Control of time near top speed
  • Focus on acceleration and deceleration
  • Smooth animation preset
  • Copy and paste animation curves
