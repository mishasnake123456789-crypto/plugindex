What Dronecam (Drone Simulation Addon) does

DroneCam lets you fly an FPV drone inside Blender in real time with a gamepad or controller, then render the flight. Physics follow real-world formulas for drag, gravity and thrust.

Your inputs are keyframed automatically as you fly and cached for playback. There are four flight modes: Acro, 3D, Horizon and Angle, and controls can be mapped to any joystick axis or trigger, with Actual Rates style sensitivity settings. It is an intuitive way to produce believable drone shots.

Key features

  • Real-time FPV flight in the viewport
  • Drag, gravity and thrust physics
  • Automatic keyframe recording
  • Acro, 3D, Horizon and Angle modes
  • Custom axis and rate mapping
