What Flip Aspect Ratio does

From Aris Melachroinos comes Flip Aspect Ratio, a script for After Effects that turns a landscape comp into portrait, or the reverse, by swapping its width and height. It is handy when you need a vertical version of horizontal work for social platforms.

Compositions can be picked by clicking in the timeline or by selecting several in the project panel. A settings panel changes the scaling method, and the old comp can be fitted to the new comp's width or height. It has been tested on After Effects CS6 and later.

Key features

  • Swaps width and height in one click
  • Works on multiple selected comps
  • Configurable scaling method
  • Fit old comp to new width or height
