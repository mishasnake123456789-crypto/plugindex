What Metahuman Dna Importer does

MetaHuman DNA Importer rebuilds downloaded MetaHuman characters in Blender from their DNA files, recreating meshes, materials, skeleton and facial controls.

Head and body meshes import with selectable texture resolution and LOD0 to LOD7. Skin, eyes, teeth and groom materials get live HSV, tint, roughness, SSS and specular sliders. Real-time Rig Logic drives corrective shape keys as you pose, with a facial control board and region-grouped panels. It also supports 4D sculpting, round-trip export to UE5 and Rigify rigs.

Key features

  • DNA-based mesh, skeleton and control import
  • LOD0-7 and texture resolution choices
  • Live material sliders
  • Real-time Rig Logic face rig
  • UE5 round-trip and Rigify support
