What Bone info does

Bone info is a utility from Chuggnut that works inside the Skin modifier of 3ds Max. Select some vertices, press the button, and the bone assignments and weights for each vertex are read out.

It also reports whether vertex weights are normalized, which is a common source of trouble. The purpose is to help find the cause of blending problems in skinned meshes, where a vertex may be influenced by unexpected bones or have weights that do not add up. Riggers and character artists debugging deformations gain a clear view of the data.

Key features

  • Runs inside the Skin modifier
  • Reads bone assignments and weights per selected vertex
  • Works from a vertex selection and one button
  • Reports weight normalization
  • Helps diagnose blending problems
