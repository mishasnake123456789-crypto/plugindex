A font management panel for After Effects that previews, searches, organises and applies fonts with sample text.

What Font Previewer does

Font Previewer by Alex Lee shows your installed typefaces in a panel, rendered with sample text you can edit. Instead of changing fonts one by one on a layer, you scan the list and apply your pick directly.

Search by keyword, add fonts to favourites and manage folders to keep large collections tidy. Preview controls include size, style and tracking, and sample text can be saved and loaded. Three colour themes (dark, default, light) and wide or compact layouts fit different workspaces. Designers choosing between many fonts for titles and lower thirds will find comparisons much quicker.

Key features

  • Previews with editable sample text
  • Keyword search of the font list
  • Favourites and font folders
  • Size, style and tracking preview controls
  • Dark, default and light themes with two layouts
