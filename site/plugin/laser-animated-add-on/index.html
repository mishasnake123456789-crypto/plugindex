What ❤️ Laser Animated Add_On does

The Laser Animated Add_On adds an animated laser beam to Blender scenes, with settings you can adjust. It runs under EEVEE as well as Cycles, with documentation included.

It supports Blender 2.93 through 5.0. Owners get a free upgrade to version 2, and version 3 packages the laser and Bloom add-ons together. A quick pick for sci-fi, stage and light-show shots.
