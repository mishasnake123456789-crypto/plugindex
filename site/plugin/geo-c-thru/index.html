Makes non-selected objects transparent or wireframe in the Blender viewport as your selection changes.

What Geo-C-Thru does

Geo-C-Thru changes how objects display based on what you select. Non-selected objects can turn transparent or wireframe on the fly, so you can see the part you are working on.

Transparency of selected and non-selected objects is controlled independently, wireframe overlays have an opacity slider, and presets plus viewport shading controls are also included. It is helpful for inspecting interiors and tightly packed models. Changing visibility automatically as you pick objects keeps your attention on the active part without manual hide and show steps.

Key features

  • Independent transparency controls
  • Wireframe overlay with opacity slider
  • Preset system
  • Viewport shading controls
