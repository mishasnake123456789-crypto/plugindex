What Snapshot Next - One Click Copies And Version Control does

Snapshot Next saves versions of your objects without destructive operations. One click copies the selected object into a dedicated backup collection.

A Next button starts a fresh version folder at a new milestone, such as moving from blockout to detailing, and backups are hidden automatically.
