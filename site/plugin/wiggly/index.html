What Wiggly does

Wiggly is a lightweight Blender add-on by YONK for creating noise-based, loopable animations with lattices. A lattice is auto-sized to fit your selected object, and its resolution can be tuned for finer or coarser deformation with real-time feedback.

One click adds looping noise animation controlled by intensity, speed and seed sliders, with separate X, Y and Z intensity for per-axis control. Objects can also interact with the lattice using physics. It suits motion designers wanting jelly-like or organic wobble without keyframing.

Key features

  • Lattice auto-sized to the selected object
  • Adjustable lattice resolution with live feedback
  • Loopable noise animation in one click
  • Intensity, speed and seed sliders
  • Per-axis XYZ intensity and physics interaction
