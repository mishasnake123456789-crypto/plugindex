3ds Max automation utility that extracts downloaded asset archives, builds folder structures and renames files into a consistent library.

What PolySorter does

PolySorter organizes the 3D assets you download so a library stays clean and production-ready. With one click it extracts nested zip, rar and 7z archives straight into the library and builds a structured layout, placing textures, lights and proxies in their own sub-folders.

Unwanted promotional PDFs, URLs and redundant text files are removed, and files and their preview images are renamed consistently. Helper features for 3Dsky recognize numeric IDs, route categories to folders and fetch preview images by ID. A whole download queue can be processed in one session. It handles filenames only; models and textures never leave your computer.

Key features

  • Extracts nested zip, rar and 7z archives with one click
  • Structured folders for textures, lights and proxies
  • Clean-up of promotional and redundant files
  • Consistent renaming of files and preview images
  • Batch processing with local-only operation
