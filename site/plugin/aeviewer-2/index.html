What AEVIEWER 2 does

AEVIEWER 2 is a browser panel from MotionLand for After Effects and Premiere Pro. It lets you look through projects, presets and media files and bring them into your work without leaving the host application.

Items can be previewed, applied or imported with one click, or simply dragged in. When you use an FFX preset, the panel works out what kind it is and creates the matching layer first, and animated previews are supported for the default After Effects presets. Comps get generated previews too. The PRO tier adds EXR sequence preview and import, SVG import and a resizable large thumbnail view.

Key features

  • One-click preview, apply and import
  • Drag-and-drop import with a delete option
  • FFX type detection creates the right layer
  • Generated previews for comps
  • PRO: EXR sequences, SVG import, big thumbnails
