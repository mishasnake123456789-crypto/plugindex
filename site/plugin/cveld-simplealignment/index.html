Aligns and distributes selected Blender objects along X, Y and Z with several distribution modes.

What Cveld Simplealignment does

Cveld Simplealignment lines objects up on any combination of axes. You can pick one axis or several, and the relative positions of objects are preserved when aligning.

Distribution options include Linear with even spacing and automatic calculation, Center (Average) with Start, End and Spread alignment, and Gaussian with adjustable parameters. Layout artists arranging props, seating or repeated elements can place them neatly in seconds. Because relative positions can be kept intact, you can tidy a layout without losing its overall arrangement.

Key features

  • Align along X, Y and/or Z
  • Preserves relative positions
  • Linear distribution with automatic spacing
  • Center and Gaussian modes
