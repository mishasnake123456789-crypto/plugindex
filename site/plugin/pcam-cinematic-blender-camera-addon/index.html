What Pcam Cinematic Blender Camera Addon does

PCam brings a more filmmaker-friendly approach to cameras in Blender. A rig can be created with one click, and lens, tilt, focus and f-stop are adjusted visually, with real-world lens presets available.

The viewport or rig can be locked to avoid accidental changes, and the camera or its target can be parented to any object or bone. Click Focus and Walk Mode add auto-keyframes, and a Vertigo effect is included. Animators and cinematographers planning shots in 3D get framing and focus tools in one place.

Key features

  • One-click modular camera rigs
  • Visual lens, tilt, focus and f-stop control
  • Lock viewport or rig; parent to objects or bones
  • Click-to-focus and walk mode that key themselves
  • Vertigo effect
