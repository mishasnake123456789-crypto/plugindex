Compositor-based Blender add-on that turns clean renders into VHS tape, CCTV or camcorder footage.

What Vhs Generator does

RUIDO VFX's VHS Generator brings analog video decay to Blender renders through a Compositor node system. Pick one of three looks: classic VHS, a security camera feed, or a 1990s camcorder, each with its own presets and adjustable parameters.

There are fifteen presets overall, from fairly clean to heavily degraded. Scanlines, interlacing and lens curvature supply the CRT character, and moving static grain plus digital noise can be adjusted for size and speed. Suited to found-footage scenes, music videos and retro title sequences.

Key features

  • Classic VHS, CCTV and 90s Camcorder modes
  • 15 presets, five per mode
  • Scanlines, interlacing and CRT curvature
  • Animated grain and digital noise
  • Compositor node based
