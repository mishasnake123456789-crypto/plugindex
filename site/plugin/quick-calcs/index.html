Four small in-app calculators for converting frames to time, estimating render time, working out aspect ratios and simple sums.

What Quick Calcs does

Quick Calcs saves trips to a separate calculator while you plan a Blender project. It adds four small tools directly in the application.

A frames-to-time converter works in both directions, a render time calculator estimates the total for an animation, an aspect ratio calculator derives ratios from a resolution, and a simple calculator handles everything else. Documentation is included. Animators estimating deadlines and artists setting up output sizes are the likely users.

Key features

  • Frames to time conversion both ways
  • Total animation render time estimate
  • Aspect ratio from resolution
  • Built-in simple calculator
