Blender add-on bundling hard-surface operators: advanced bevel, booleans, paneling, quick cloth and retopology helpers.

What Handy Toolset does

Handy Toolset gathers many daily modeling operators into one Blender add-on. A supercharged bevel adds angled bevels, reorders a bevel, bevels selected edges or outward faces, and handles boolean objects. A simplified boolean pie menu comes with simple boolean operators and a modal mode.

Panel tools turn faces into panels and sub-panels, Quick Cloth and Simple Plate convert faces or objects into cloth or thick plates, and Stickywrap shrinkwraps an opening onto a surface. Extras include viewport toggles, a minimalist retopology panel, pivot shortcuts and face separation with normals intact.

Key features

  • Bevel operator with angled and boolean support
  • Boolean pie menu and modal operators
  • Face-to-panel and intersect panel tools
  • Quick Cloth and Simple Plate
  • Stickywrap and viewport display toggles
