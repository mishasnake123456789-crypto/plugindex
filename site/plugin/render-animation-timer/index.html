What Render Animation Timer does

Omega VFX Ltd's Render Animation Timer measures how long animation renders take in Blender. It averages time per frame and displays an expected finish time inside Blender.

Version 0.0.2 introduced energy cost estimates in dollars and kilowatt-hours, and one click starts capturing render data. Freelancers can quote jobs more accurately and plan machine time, and studios see render cost across projects. Having numbers on hand makes it simpler to compare hardware, settings and optimization attempts over time.

Key features

  • Render time estimation
  • Per-frame average time
  • Estimated finish time
  • Energy cost in dollars and kWh
  • One-click activation
