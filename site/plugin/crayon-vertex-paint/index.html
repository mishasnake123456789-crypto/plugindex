What Crayon: Vertex Paint does

Crayon is a Blender plugin that treats vertex colors as an organizing system. Painting a color on objects or faces becomes a way to tag them, and the tags can then drive materials and Geometry Nodes.

You can select objects or faces according to color, material, or the presence of vertex paint, and map colors across a selection. It is a quick approach for marking regions without creating many vertex groups.

Key features

  • Vertex colors as grouping labels
  • Usable in materials and Geometry Nodes
  • Select by color, material or paint presence
  • Color mapping across selections
