What Kyokaz's Toolbox does

Kyokaz's free toolbox covers camera and keyframe management in Blender, along with render utilities. A Quick Camera pie menu adds cameras, binds shot markers and copies camera or shot properties.

New cameras pick up default settings, and eight favourites can sit in the pie. A Camera & Shot List shows frame range and total frames per shot. Keyframe baking per step and viewport render options complete it. Animators and cinematographers handling many shots save setup time.

Key features

  • Quick Camera pie menu
  • Default camera settings
  • Favorites for up to 8 cameras
  • Camera & Shot List with frame ranges
  • Per-step keyframe baking
