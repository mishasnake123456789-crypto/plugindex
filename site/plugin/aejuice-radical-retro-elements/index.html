What AEJuice Radical Retro Elements does

Pre-rendered shapes that evoke 1970s graphic design make up AEJuice Radical Retro Elements. More than 100 animated pieces are delivered as 3840x2160 H.264 videos, ready to drag onto a timeline.

Blending modes let you combine elements with each other or with footage. They work in After Effects, Premiere Pro and other video editors, and the AEJuice Pack Manager offers preview, drag-and-drop import and favourites. Creators making music videos, social media content and other projects with a throwback feel can build backgrounds and overlays quickly.
