What ToothPaste does

ToothPaste, by Sergio Ribero, brings vector artwork into After Effects by pasting it straight from the clipboard. Copy an element from a design application, paste it into the composition, and it arrives as After Effects layers.

Raster images embedded in SVGs are supported, and the layer structure stays close to the original design file. Groups can be pasted as precomps, text remains editable by default, and strokes in text are supported. It works with After Effects from CC 2014 through 2026, saving designers time over exporting and importing files.

Key features

  • Pastes SVGs from the clipboard
  • Supports embedded raster images
  • Layer structure close to the original design
  • Groups can paste as precomps
  • Editable text with stroke support
