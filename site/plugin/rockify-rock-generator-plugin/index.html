What Rockify - Rock Generator Plugin does

Rockify creates rocks in Blender from any low-poly primitive shape, made for film, games and other CG projects.

One click produces a realistic rock with natural cliff faces. Presets, remeshing into triangulated real-time topology for engines such as Unreal, and Substance Painter smart materials are included. Environment artists can fill terrain quickly. Real-time friendly topology and smart materials make the output suitable for game environments as well as film-quality scenes and quick background dressing.

Key features

  • One-click rock generation
  • Natural cliff faces
  • Remesh to real-time topology
  • Substance Painter smart materials
  • Rock presets
