What Meshed - Animated Typeface does

Meshed is an animated typeface from Animography that brings its Meshed Display serif into After Effects. The letterforms are a rational serif built on strong vertical stems and fine horizontal hairlines, giving headlines a sharp, high-contrast look.

Designers can use it to set titles and lower thirds that already carry motion, rather than animating each character by hand. It supports After Effects 2020 through 2024, and version 1.0 was released in December 2023.

Key features

  • Rational serif with strong vertical contrast
  • Heavy vertical stems against delicate hairlines
  • Built-in animation for titles and text
  • Works with After Effects 2020 to 2024
