Blender add-on for fast boolean operations that remains quick with many objects, supporting destructive and non-destructive use.

What Booltron does

Booltron speeds up boolean work in Blender, especially when a scene involves many objects. It can operate destructively or non-destructively and also works with Curve and Text objects.

Modifier results can be baked, several adjustments help produce cleaner booleans, and the add-on automatically checks for non-manifold geometry. The interface is translated into English, French, Italian, Russian and Spanish. Hard surface modelers and 3D printing users who rely on booleans will get reliable cuts with fewer errors.

Key features

  • Fast booleans with many objects
  • Destructive and non-destructive modes
  • Works with Curve and Text objects
  • Automatic non-manifold check
  • Five interface languages
