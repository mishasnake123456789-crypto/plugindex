What Chaos Corona Premium does

Chaos Corona Premium is a renderer for Cinema 4D that runs entirely on the processor and supports both unbiased and biased rendering. An optional AI denoiser uses a compatible NVIDIA GPU to clean up results.

The premium tier includes access to more than 18,500 3D assets and materials, plus AI features through Veras, such as AI-enhanced scenes, Nano Banana 2 images and AI videos in quantities that depend on the tier. Free learning resources are also provided. Archviz artists and product visualizers who like a straightforward CPU workflow will find it appealing.

Key features

  • Entirely CPU-based rendering
  • Unbiased and biased modes
  • Optional GPU AI denoiser on NVIDIA
  • 18,500+ 3D assets and materials
  • Veras AI scenes, images and videos
