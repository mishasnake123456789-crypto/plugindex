What BackSpin Font Family does

BackSpin Font Family by Highground draws on the look of vintage hip-hop fliers and breakdancing memorabilia. The three fonts suit After Effects and Premiere Pro titles that need chunky, blockbuster graffiti letters.

Styles include a bold solid fill, thick offset outlines, and a combo college style with an inset fill. Each carries 96 characters, with full alphabets, numbers, symbols and punctuation, plus arrows, end pieces, splatters and other artwork for embellishing text. The characters were produced through an algorithm using seven automated distortion and transformation steps.

Key features

  • Three styles: solid, offset outline and college inset
  • 96 characters per style
  • Arrows, end pieces and splatter artwork
  • Old school hip-hop flier inspired letterforms
