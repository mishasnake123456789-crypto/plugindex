A Blender add-on that matches a face on one object to a face on another with parallel and snap edge modes.

What Face And Edge Align does

Face And Edge Align snaps one object against another by selecting faces. In a single click, any face is matched to a face on a different object, and normals are oriented automatically so the two surfaces make contact.

It works with any mesh geometry, from simple cubes to complex imported assets. Parallel Edge Mode keeps edges parallel while preserving face alignment, and Snap Edge Mode overlaps edges to form joints. Hard-surface modelers, kitbashers and anyone assembling parts will find it far quicker than rotating and moving objects by eye.

Key features

  • One-click face-to-face matching
  • Automatic normal orientation for contact
  • Works on any mesh geometry
  • Parallel Edge and Snap Edge modes
