What AEJuice History Timeline Constructor does

AEJuice History Timeline Constructor is a set of scenes for telling a story chronologically in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Dates, quotes, photos and videos can all be customised.

Scenes come as headlines with text, photos with text, text-only, videos with text, mixed scenes and transitions. It is made of 100% After Effects, auto-resizes to any resolution, and runs through the AEJuice Pack Manager for quick previews, drag and drop and favourites. Output suits Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and Snapchat. Documentary and explainer editors can produce a history timeline without designing each scene.

Key features

  • Headline, photo, text, video and mix scenes
  • Included transitions
  • Auto-resize to any resolution
  • Built entirely from After Effects layers
  • Pack Manager with preview and favourites
