Blender add-on that overlays composition guides and rulers in the 3D Viewport and Video Sequence Editor.

What B Guides does

B Guides puts framing aids on top of your work in Blender. The rule of thirds, golden ratio, golden spiral and golden triangles, radial symmetry, diagonals, a grid and perspective lines can be drawn in the 3D Viewport and over the Video Sequence Editor preview.

Each 3D camera can hold its own guide configuration, kept separate from VSE settings. Diagonal angle, line counts for vanishing points and full rotation and position for generic guide lines are adjustable, integrated rulers measure, and colors, opacity and thickness can be changed. Guide setups can be saved as presets.

Key features

  • Rule of thirds, golden ratio, spiral and more
  • Overlays in 3D Viewport and VSE preview
  • Per-camera guide configurations
  • Integrated rulers
  • Preset saving and loading
