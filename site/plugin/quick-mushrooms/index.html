A Blender add-on with 15 animatable mushroom models for forest scenes, plus a royalty-free photo pack.

What Quick Mushrooms does

Quick Mushrooms populates forest scenes in Blender with fifteen unique mushroom models, available as single specimens or grouped clusters. They use high-resolution textures and detailed geometry, and are fully animatable.

Elements can sway in the wind or glow, covering both daytime forests and glowing night forests. A 1.44 GB pack of real mushroom photographs from multiple angles is included, royalty-free and free of copyright restrictions, for custom texturing. Environment artists and illustrators creating fantasy or woodland scenes will have ready assets without sculpting from scratch.

Key features

  • 15 unique mushroom models
  • Single mushrooms or grouped clusters
  • Wind sway and glow animation
  • Day and glowing night forests
  • 1.44 GB royalty-free photo pack
