What iZotope Nectar Elements does

Nectar Elements is the simplest edition of Nectar 4, supplied for DaVinci Resolve. It gives newcomers to vocal mixing a compact set of tools plus guidance, so a voice can sound finished without deep audio knowledge.

iZotope Relay links the plugin to other iZotope products. An AI-driven Vocal Assistant proposes a starting setup that can be edited, Voices makes layered parts from a preset, and Backer builds background singers in eight styles or from an acapella you bring. Tone and loudness can be matched to a reference, key detection is built in, and wet/dry options help blend effects. Podcasters and home producers are the natural audience.

Key features

  • Vocal Assistant recommends a starting mix
  • Voices module for layered vocals
  • Backer builds background singers
  • Tone and loudness matching
  • iZotope Relay hub
