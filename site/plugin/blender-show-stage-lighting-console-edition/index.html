What Blender Show Stage Lighting (Console Edition) does

FAFIG STUDIO's Blender Show Stage Lighting (Console Edition) ties Blender to professional lighting desks. Through an Artnet connection, it works with any console that sends DMX, such as Grand MA2, Tiger and Pearl.

GDTF fixture files can be imported with their model, position, size, gobo pattern, channel details and presets, although gobo and macro programming does not carry over. Lamps can be controlled and tuned independently, arranged and recorded. Lighting designers can use it to visualize shows and render scenes.

Key features

  • Artnet connection to DMX consoles
  • GDTF fixture file import
  • Independent control of individual lamps
  • Arrange and record lamp data
  • Works with Grand MA2, Tiger and Pearl
