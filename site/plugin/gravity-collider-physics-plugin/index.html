What Gravity Collider Physics Plugin does

Gravity Collider adds physics to bones in Blender rigs quickly. Secondary motion for skirts, hair, armor, chests and keychains comes from collision boxes strung together like a bead necklace.

The boxes adjust easily, weight painting is simple, and it is light and scales with the model. IK-based physics can be gradually turned off and on for full control, and results export to game engines. Game character artists get dynamic accessories without heavy simulation.

Key features

  • Bone physics in two clicks
  • Bead necklace collision boxes
  • IK-based physics with adjustable influence
  • Exports to game engines
