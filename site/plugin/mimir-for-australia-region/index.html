What Mimir for Australia region does

Mimir for Australia region, from Mjoll, connects Premiere Pro and After Effects to a cloud media library holding everything from deep archive to ongoing live material. Editors can find and import clips without leaving the application.

Media may be brought in as a proxy or at full resolution, and captions generated through transcription services can be imported into Premiere Pro. AI-driven metadata tagging helps with discovery, and sequences cut in proxy can be rendered centrally into high-resolution files. It offers PAM, DAM and MAM functionality, which suits teams handling large media collections.

Key features

  • Search and import in proxy or full resolution
  • Access from deep archive to live material
  • Imports transcription-generated captions
  • AI-driven metadata tagging
  • Central render of proxy-edited sequences
