What Mat Clear Restore does

Mat Clear Restore strips every material from a Blender scene and brings them back with a click. It is useful for clay renders where you want to judge form and lighting alone.

It supports material overrides and emission light settings, and is reached from the Material section of the Properties Panel after installation. It works with Blender 4.2, 4.1, 4.0, 3.6 and 3.5 and is GPL licensed and marked Blender Extension compatible.

Key features

  • Removes all materials at once
  • Quick restoration
  • Supports clay renders and overrides
  • Emission light options
  • Works with Blender 3.5 to 4.2
