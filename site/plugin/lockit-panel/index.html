What Lockit Panel does

Lockit Panel, from Ambient Recording, brings on-set logging into the edit. Upload the file from the NanoLockit Logger, click synchronize, and your recorded clips are matched with markers created during the shoot.

The panel shows which clips were logged as good or bad, based on the red or green buttons pressed on the Logger. It saves assistant editors from relying on paper notes. That helps assistants find the takes that were flagged during recording without listening through everything.

Key features

  • Works with the NanoLockit Logger
  • Automatic matching after synchronize
  • Shows clips logged as good or bad
  • Reads on-set red and green button markers
