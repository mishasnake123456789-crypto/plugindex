What Bl To Mar--Blender To Marmosettoolbag4 Plugin does

Moving assets from Blender to Marmoset Toolbag 4 for baking involves a lot of housekeeping. Bl To Mar handles the transfer in one click and adds utilities inside Blender to prepare the scene.

Renaming tools can add or remove suffixes, work out names on the fly and batch-rename; visibility can be toggled by suffix; and quick bake groups are assembled from scene collections by attaching or detaching matching low and high objects. The destination path is set in the panel, and export follows saving the blend file.

Key features

  • One-click export to Marmoset Toolbag 4
  • Batch rename with suffix handling
  • Toggle visibility by suffix
  • Quick baking groups from collections
  • Export path set in the panel
