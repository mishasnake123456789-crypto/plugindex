What Gradient8 does

Gradient8 from grape design generates smooth multi-point gradients in After Effects. You can place as many as eight points, give each its own radius, and tune how strongly the colors interpolate between them.

Color mixing can happen in RGB, HSV or OKLCH, and the blend method is either Smooth or Gaussian. Animatable noise adds movement and grain to the result. The interface is intentionally minimal, so motion designers can create mesh-like backgrounds and colorful washes without stacking several gradient effects.

Key features

  • Up to eight gradient points
  • Per-point radius and interpolation strength
  • RGB, HSV or OKLCH color spaces
  • Smooth or Gaussian blend methods
  • Animatable noise
