What Krotos Video to Sound does

Krotos Video to Sound adds sound design to a cut by analyzing a part of your video. Select a region, pick a type of effect, and matching sounds are placed and synced on the timeline.

It works with real recordings made by sound designers rather than generative AI. Effects can be swapped for others without losing sync. Current categories include Ambiences, Whooshes, Risers, Impacts and Cloth, and Footsteps are listed as coming soon. Editors who need to add effects quickly to rough or finished cuts can save a lot of manual placement.

Key features

  • Automatic sync of effects to a chosen region
  • Choose the type of sound to add
  • Swap effects while keeping sync
  • Real recordings, not generative audio
  • Ambiences, whooshes, risers, impacts and cloth
