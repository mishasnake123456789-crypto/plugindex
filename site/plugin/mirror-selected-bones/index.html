Blender add-on that copies skin weights of chosen bones and their vertex groups across a symmetrical model to the other side.

What Mirror Selected Bones does

Mirror Selected Bones, by Martin Brockelmann, copies skin weighting from one half of a symmetrical character to the other, limited to the bones you choose. That avoids mirroring the entire weight set when only a few bones need fixing.

It works with a single bone or several at once and carries your working state over to the pose bone on the opposite side. Riggers painting weights on characters will find it quicker than repainting both halves by hand.

Key features

  • Mirrors weight paint data for selected bones
  • Targets the matching vertex groups
  • Works with one or more bones
  • Carries working status to the opposite pose bone
