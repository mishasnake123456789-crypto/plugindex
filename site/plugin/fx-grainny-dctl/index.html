What FX Grainny DCTL does

FX Grainny DCTL turns footage into a graphic-novel look in DaVinci Resolve, using grain shading and high-contrast treatments. The look is built from Posterization and Pixel Mix settings, with a Steps slider where fewer steps create a stronger, more stylized result.

Blend sliders control how visible the grain and texture are, and Colorize works best when posterization Steps are five or fewer. Source Contour adds outlines, and their color can come from HSV parameters, color picker controls or by inverting the image within the contour area. Hovering shows tooltips, and you apply the effect by putting NP FX Grainny on a clip or node.

Key features

  • Posterization and Pixel Mix core look
  • Steps slider for stylization strength
  • Grain and texture blend sliders
  • Source Contour with customizable color
  • Colorize option and hover tooltips
