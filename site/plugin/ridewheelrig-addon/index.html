What Ridewheelrig Addon does

RideWheelRig creates a driveable vehicle rig from your meshes. Pick the meshes and press Build to get a functioning setup.

It offers real-time ground tracking over any terrain, suspension, steering and banking, and supports both four-wheel and two-wheel vehicles. Animators can drive cars and bikes along paths without hand-rigging. The rig is aimed at animators who need convincing vehicle motion without building a custom setup. Wheels follow the terrain while the body reacts, so driving shots look grounded with minimal effort.

Key features

  • One-click rig build
  • Ground tracking on any terrain
  • Suspension, steering and banking
  • 4-wheel and 2-wheel vehicles
