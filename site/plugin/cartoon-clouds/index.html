What Cartoon Clouds does

Roman Tursunov made Cartoon Clouds, released by studioromanrec, for creating stylised clouds in Blender. Its author describes it as simple, fast and convenient.

Blender 3.1 through 4.3 are supported, and it comes with documentation and a demo video under the GPL license. It also belongs to a thirty-three product Studioromanrec bundle of Blender add-ons. Suited to children's animation, stylised skies and game backgrounds. Because the clouds are produced inside Blender, they can be placed, scaled and animated like any other object.
