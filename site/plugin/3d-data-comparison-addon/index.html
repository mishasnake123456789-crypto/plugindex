What 3D Data Comparison Addon does

The 3D Data Comparison Addon creates animated bar chart videos in Blender. Provide a CSV file and it generates the bars, text and pictures and prepares a scene ready to render.

Bar textures, floor textures, fonts and HDRI lighting can be customized. An animated camera travels from one data point to the next, and settings such as camera zoom and text size remain adjustable after generation. A built-in protection system prevents deleting the watermark or essential camera objects.

Key features

  • CSV-driven bars, text and pictures
  • Custom textures, fonts and HDRI lighting
  • Camera animation between data points
  • Adjustable zoom and text size
  • Scene ready for rendering
