What Content Browser does

Content Browser by DMZScripts scans any folder on your machine for 3D models, max files, materials and HDRIs, then groups matching files into assets. It detects preview images, LODs, mapping channels and resolutions on its own and builds tag filters from them.

Assets are dragged into the viewport or the Slate Material Editor, or imported into Slate and Compact editors, with automatic filepath relinking for merges. You can pick LODs, resolutions and variations and import several assets together. Afterwards it can retarget files, rename objects, put them on a new layer, add a skylight and drop objects onto surfaces. The resizable, dockable window suits libraries from MegaScans, Poliigon and PolyHaven.

Key features

  • Scans folders and groups files into assets
  • Auto-detects previews, LODs, channels and resolutions
  • Drag and drop into viewport or Slate
  • Multi-asset import with LOD and variation choice
  • Post-import renaming, layers, skylight and surface placement
