What Procedural Building Generator does

Procedural Building Generator creates buildings with Geometry Nodes. Dimensions, layouts and styles are changed via the modifiers, and randomness adds variety.

A library of pre-made doors and windows can be placed, and you can create your own. It works well for city scenes and background architecture without modeling each structure. Because everything is driven by modifiers, whole blocks can be varied quickly, which suits city backdrops and architectural exploration. It is useful for quick massing studies as well.

Key features

  • Adjustable dimensions, layouts and styles
  • Pre-made doors and windows
  • Custom door and window support
  • Randomness for variation
