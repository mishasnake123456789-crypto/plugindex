What Wiggle 2: RTX Edition does

Wiggle 2: RTX Edition is a fork of the Wiggle 2, kept up by the community, bone physics add-on, updated by Chamiseul for recent Blender releases. It is tuned for complex, high-density bone rigs.

Multi-Bone Sync applies settings changes to all selected bones at once, and the add-on is designed to avoid viewport lag on long hair strands. Bake Result creates a new Sim layer. Animators adding secondary motion to hair and accessories benefit.

Key features

  • Multi-Bone Sync
  • Tuned for dense bone rigs
  • Avoids viewport lag on long strands
  • Bake Result to a new Sim layer
