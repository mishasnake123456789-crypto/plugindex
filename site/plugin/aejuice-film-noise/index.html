What AEJuice Film Noise does

AEJuice Film Noise is a collection of ready-made overlays that give clean digital footage an analog feel. It contains 31 clips of film grain, noise and dirt, rendered as 4K MP4 files at 3840 by 2160.

They are dropped over footage and combined using blending modes, so they work in After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and other video editors. Inside AEJuice's Pack Manager you get fast preview, drag-and-drop import and favorites, and downloads are quick.

Key features
