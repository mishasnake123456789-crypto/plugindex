What Locked Camera Protector does

Locked Camera Protector deals with a subtle trap: a camera locked to the view moves whenever you navigate, which can overwrite its position without you noticing. This add-on makes such a camera conspicuous.

When you open a file, old or downloaded, it alerts you right away and is deliberately hard to miss. If enabled while you are already editing, it behaves more gently and stays out of the way. It protects framing work in shared or inherited scenes.

Key features

  • Highlights cameras locked to the view
  • Alerts on opening a file
  • Deliberately hard to miss
  • Gentler behavior when enabled mid-edit
