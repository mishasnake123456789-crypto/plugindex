What Quick Procedural Terrain & Water Addon does

The Quick Procedural Terrain & Water Addon from EdenWeby 3D creates landscapes inside Blender by combining GPU-accelerated terrain shaders with CPU-based geometry nodes. It adds a Quick Terrain panel to the 3D view after installation.

Start from one of seven presets, including Desert, Ice Land, Wet Land, Mountain and Tundra, then adjust terrain size, shape and detail, colors and textures, or load your own displacement map. World controls handle lighting, weather, sun position and skybox, while water settings cover size, depth, color and gentle animated waves.

Key features

  • Seven terrain presets from desert to tundra
  • GPU terrain shaders combined with geometry nodes
  • Custom displacement map support
  • World settings for sun, weather and skybox
  • Adjustable animated water with waves and currents
