What Pixel Lab Aura: Professional 3D Environments for Redshift does

This edition of Pixel Lab Aura targets Redshift users in Cinema 4D. It contains 13 professional environments aimed at animations and product shots, built for high-resolution renders.

Scenes include Loft, Containers, Museum, Modern, Wave, Race and SciFi Tunnel, among others. An Xpresso rig in every studio provides sliders to modify lights, temperature, color, glows and other properties. Artists can start from a finished set and tune the mood without lighting a room by hand, which suits product visuals and short animated pieces.
