What Animoxtend: Ai Animotion & Generation Toolbox does

AnimoXtend combines animation tools with AI-assisted workflows in Blender. It aims to cut repetitive animation and production tasks through automation and batch processing.

Text to Model builds a model from a prompt and imports it automatically. Text to Motion searches by description, including bilingual search, and applies motion to a chosen armature. Retargeting offers auto bone mapping, auto T-pose, quick retargeting and part-specific retargeting, and motion blending uses keyframe or NLA in-betweens.

Key features

  • Text to Model with auto-import
  • Text to Motion with bilingual search
  • Retargeting with auto bone mapping and T-pose
  • Keyframe and NLA motion blending
  • Batch processing and optimization
