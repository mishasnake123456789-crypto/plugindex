What Color Extractor does

Color Extractor is a Blender add-on for pulling colors out of reference images and putting them to use. It serves texture artists, modelers and designers who build palettes.

An eyedropper samples colors with duplicate filtering, and threshold-based filtering removes near-identical entries from the list. Selected colors can be averaged into one, applied as materials to the active object, or kept in a favorites list. Random colors can be assigned to selected objects or faces. The panel appears in Scene properties, the image editor and the viewport.

Key features

  • Eyedropper extraction with duplicate filtering
  • Color list, threshold filtering and favorites
  • Average multiple colors into one
  • Apply colors as materials or randomly to faces
