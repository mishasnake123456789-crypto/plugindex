A Blender add-on that adds or removes wireframes on many objects at once in Cycles and Eevee.

What Easy Wireframes does

Easy Wireframes manages wireframe looks for several objects from one place in Blender. Select the objects and add or remove wireframes in one action, instead of building the same shader setup on each.

Different wireframes can be given per material by manually unlinking the material drivers. Most common wireframes come preconfigured, though certain materials need manual configuration. It works in Cycles and Eevee, and documentation is included. Technical illustrators, archviz artists and those making clay or blueprint-style renders will find it speeds up the look.

Key features

  • Control wireframes of multiple objects together
  • Add or remove wireframes on a selection
  • Per-material wireframes by unlinking drivers
  • Most common wireframes preconfigured
  • Cycles and Eevee
