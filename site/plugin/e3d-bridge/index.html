What E3D Bridge does

E3D Bridge, by DominicSmith, exports an OBJ, its MTL and textures in a single click, prepared for use in Element 3D. It is listed under Import-Export.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • One-click OBJ export
  • MTL and texture export
