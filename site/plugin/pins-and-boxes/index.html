What Pins & Boxes does

Pins & Boxes, from mamoworld tools, helps build layouts that react to changing text in After Effects. Elements can be aligned and attached to any point of a text layer's bounding box, so labels and icons follow their content.

Boxes grow and shrink along with their content, which is a common need for lower thirds and speech bubbles. Position and anchor control effects provide the adjustments, disconnected pins can be parented, and expressions are universalized to work with foreign-language versions of After Effects. It supports versions from CC 2015 through 2026.

Key features

  • Attach elements to text bounding box points
  • Boxes that resize with content
  • Position and anchor control effects
  • Parentable disconnected pins
  • Works in non-English After Effects
