Layer management by groups for After Effects, with collapsible groups and precomp conversion.

What Workflower does

Workflower by Constantin Maier gives After Effects a way to group layers the way many illustration apps do. Groups and sub-groups can be created, collapsed and expanded, keeping crowded timelines manageable on Windows and Mac.

Whole groups can be selected, moved, parented, disabled, soloed, locked and relabelled, duplicated, given group opacity or turned into group mattes. Groups convert to and from precomps, comp items in the project panel can be changed in batch or pre-rendered, and custom shortcuts live in a scrollable panel. KBar support arrived in version 1.0.4. Artists with layered scenes get one panel for tidy organisation.

Key features

  • Groups and sub-groups with collapse and expand
  • Move, parent, solo, lock and relabel groups
  • Group opacity and group mattes
  • Convert groups to and from precomps
  • Batch attribute changes and pre-rendering
