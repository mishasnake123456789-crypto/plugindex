Blender add-on that uses a selection to reset a mesh's rotation in several different ways.

What Smart Rotation Reset does

Smart Rotation Reset, by Pazelock, lets you pick part of a mesh and use it as the reference for resetting rotation, with more than one way of doing it. That is useful when an object's orientation is off and you want it aligned to a face or edge you choose. It is listed under Mesh.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
