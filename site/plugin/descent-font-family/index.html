What Descent Font Family does

Descent Font Family from Highground is a graffiti typeface designed to read vertically, available for After Effects and Premiere Pro as well as many other Adobe applications. It is built from four fonts.

Descent Classic offers stackable fill and outline styles, giving up to six variations per word or phrase. The Contextual style uses an alternating pattern of specialized, interlocking letters, adding twelve more variations. Classic and Contextual letters are compatible, so they can be mixed and matched. It suits designers working on graffiti or gangster-style lettering.

Key features

  • Vertical reading graffiti style
  • Classic style with stackable fill and outline
  • Contextual style with interlocking letters
  • Classic and Contextual can be mixed
