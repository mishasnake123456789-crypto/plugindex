What Earth-Sun-Moon Simulator does

The Earth-Sun-Moon Simulator is an educational Blender add-on for exploring how the three bodies interact. You can follow the Moon's orbit and phases from many points of view, from space or the ground.

It shows seasons and local solar eclipses cast by the Moon's shadow, and location presets let you jump to places on Earth. Teachers, students and astronomy fans can experiment with the system interactively. Under the hood you get real-time Moon orbit and phases and local solar eclipse from space or ground.

Key features

  • Real-time Moon orbit and phases
  • Local solar eclipse from space or ground
  • Seasons visualization
  • Location jump presets
