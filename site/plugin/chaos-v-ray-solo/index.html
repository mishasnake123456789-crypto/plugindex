What Chaos V-Ray Solo does

Chaos V-Ray Solo brings V-Ray to Cinema 4D for freelancers and independent artists. It delivers physically accurate, photoreal rendering and simulation, and is tuned for both animation and still images.

Rendering scales across the CPU, the GPU or both, with distributed rendering and optimizations for heavy scenes. Real-world cameras, effects and lighting come with the engine, and up to 15,000 high-quality 3D assets and materials are included. It is a fit for artists making architectural, product and design visuals who want a mature production renderer.
