What Backgrounds 2 does

Backgrounds 2 from Mister Horse is a collection of animated backdrops for titles, interviews and general video work. Every background was created from scratch for this version.

In Premiere Pro the pack works through the free Premiere Composer plugin, which drops each background into the sequence as a MOGRT. It installs alongside the earlier Backgrounds pack without replacing it. Compatibility covers After Effects 2021 and 2022, and Premiere Pro 2020 through 2022. Editors who need fresh, ready-to-use backdrops can add them without building them.
