What Flexible Radial Array does

AR Utilities' Flexible Radial Array builds circular arrangements that stay as editable objects in Blender. Copies may be plain duplicates or instances, spread by total count or relative step.

Multi-object methods are Relational, Sequential or Random Sequential. Radii interpolate singly, linearly or smoothly, with X/Y scaling for ovals, and Angle Total yields full circles, partial arcs or spirals. Per-object transforms and helixes widen the options. Modelers making clocks, fences, columns and decorative rings get precise control.

Key features

  • Copies or instances
  • Count or relative step distribution
  • Relational, Sequential and Random Sequential methods
  • Radius interpolation and oval scaling
  • Arcs, spirals and helixes
