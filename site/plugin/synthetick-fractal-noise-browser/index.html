What synthetick Fractal Noise Browser does

The Fractal Noise Browser from synthetick lets you flip through noise looks instead of fiddling with sliders. It ships with over 250 presets in nine curated categories; each applies as a Fractal Noise or a Turbulent Noise effect, flagged with FN or TN badges.

The resizable, scrollable panel shows thumbnails and animated previews. A random seed can be reshuffled, and evolution runs by expression or as a seamless loop. User Presets store your own settings, and application settings decide whether an existing noise effect gets replaced or a new one is added. Artists building textures, clouds and backgrounds can start quickly.
