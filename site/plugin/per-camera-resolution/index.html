Blender add-on that lets each camera have its own resolution and updates the scene resolution when cameras switch.

What Per-Camera Resolution does

Per-Camera Resolution removes the limit of one resolution per Blender scene. Each camera can have its own, and the scene follows when you change cameras.

Switching happens manually or through timeline markers, and settings sit in the camera data properties under the Camera panel. A Render Animated Resolution operator renders animations using the per-camera values. Editors cutting between different aspect ratios or formats in one shot sequence avoid manual changes.

Key features

  • Resolution stored per camera
  • Auto-update on camera switch or marker
  • Settings in Camera data properties
  • Render Animated Resolution operator
