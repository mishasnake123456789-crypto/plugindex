What Drive Them All - Colors does

Drive Them All - Colors focuses on color drivers. Colors are grouped into Teams, and as many colors can be added as needed.

Capture a color, apply a driver in two clicks, and rename Teams or Colors at any time. Remove Driver detaches drivers and keyframes, Remove Color deletes a slot along with its drivers, and entire Teams can be deleted. Helps artists drive color schemes from one place. Because the controls live together, large color schemes can be switched or tuned without hunting through properties.

Key features

  • Colors organized into Teams
  • Two-click driver application
  • Rename Teams and Colors
  • Remove Driver, Remove Color and delete Team
