What Creation Effects: Creation VHS Effects does

Creation VHS Effects turns clean video into worn tape or a bad TV signal in After Effects. Eleven finished presets range from subtle ageing to unwatchable, and a single Intensity control adjusts all effects together or each can be tuned separately.

There are 48 VHS and TV glitch effects modelled on real artefacts and added by copy and paste, 21 TV static overlays, looping tracking clips, 23 sound effects such as VHS hum and interference, and 17 short glitch transitions with audio. 4K and HD versions are included, no plugins are needed and it works in any language version of After Effects.
