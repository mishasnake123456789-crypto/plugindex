What AEJuice Cyberpunk HUD 2 does

AEJuice Cyberpunk HUD 2 follows the first Cyberpunk HUD with all-new dark, neon graphics for After Effects and Premiere Pro. It was made entirely in After Effects and resizes automatically to any resolution.

Contents include 50 stories, 57 dark backgrounds, 30 neon text preset animations and 38 HUD elements, among other assets. Premiere Pro users get a MOGRT version with full controls and a media placeholder feature for swapping images and videos in stories. Fonts, styles and colours are editable, and the Pack Manager offers preview, drag and drop and favourites. Suitable for social platforms, ads, TV, events and cybersport content.
