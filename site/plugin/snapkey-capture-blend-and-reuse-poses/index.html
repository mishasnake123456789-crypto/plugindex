What SnapKey - Capture Blend and Reuse Poses does

SnapKey, by Ciaran Duffy, lets After Effects animators save a setup spanning multiple layers as a single Snap. That could be a character pose, a layout state or a group of settings, and it can be recalled whenever needed.

Snaps can be blended together for in-between states, with advanced blending and key animation, and saved states remain fast and editable. It is useful for switching poses, testing variations or building motion systems. It is compatible with After Effects 2025 and 2026.

Key features

  • Captures multi-layer setups as one Snap
  • Recall saved Snaps any time
  • Blend between animation states
  • Advanced blending and key animation
  • Saved states stay editable
