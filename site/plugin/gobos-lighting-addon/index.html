What Gobos Lighting Addon does

Gobos Lighting Addon brings gobo effects to Blender lighting. Gobos are patterned cutouts that shape light, and ten presets are included for projecting dappled and patterned illumination.

Size, rotation and position can be changed manually, with further controls available in the Properties panel, and your own images, textures or video clips can be brought in and turned into gobos. Color of the light can also be altered. It helps with moody interior and stage-style lighting.

Key features

  • 10 gobo texture presets
  • Bring in your own images, textures or video
  • Adjustable size, rotation and position
  • Light color control
