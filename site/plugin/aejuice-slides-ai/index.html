What AEJuice Slides AI does

AEJuice Slides AI is a slideshow template collection containing over 100 slide compositions grouped into ten styles, such as Wedding Touches, Elegant Photos, Sunlit Photos, Wooden Gallery and Modern Canvas. A slideshow AI creator tool comes with it.

One click combines a chosen music track with your photos. Full compositions live in After Effects, which is the best place to build a complete slideshow; the Premiere Pro version handles individual slides only. The templates have a realistic look and are cleared for commercial use, so photographers and editors can produce presentable slideshows fast.
