Blender add-on that fills building exteriors with parallax interiors and rooms for architectural visualization.

What Nterior Facade Addon does

Nterior Facade Addon adds believable interiors behind windows in architectural renders. Select a plane of quads, with each quad acting as a room, and place hundreds of parallax rooms with two clicks while the grid stays parametric through array modifiers.

Rayportal projection in Blender 4.2 with Cycles places a true 3D scene inside each polygon, supporting animated objects and lights without baking. Other features include Quick Facade, glass mode, animated people, a switchable curtain shader and a pack maker for your own content. Over 100 shaders ship with free future updates.

Key features

  • Two-click parallax rooms from a quad plane
  • Rayportal real 3D interiors in Cycles
  • Quick Facade for parametric facades
  • Glass mode and animated people
  • Pack Maker and 100+ shaders
