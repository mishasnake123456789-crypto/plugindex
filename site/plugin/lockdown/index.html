Warp and planar tracking for After Effects that also sends 3D tracking data to Cinema 4D and Blender.

What Lockdown does

Lockdown tracks surfaces that bend and flex as well as flat ones inside After Effects. Once a surface is stabilized, you can clean up or paint within the stabilized composition and the work links back onto the moving footage.

The Paint Link workflow supports frame-by-frame animation and even Photoshop's AI generative fill on moving shots, and a Composite Brush offers a colour selector and modifier. Tracking data can go to Cinema 4D as a C4D file, or reach Blender and other 3D apps through Alembic, so objects can be attached in 3D and the mesh relit.

Key features

  • Tracks warping and planar surfaces
  • Cleanup and paint inside a stabilized comp
  • Paint Link workflow incl. Photoshop generative fill
  • Composite Brush colour selector and modifier
  • Tracking data out to Cinema 4D, or Blender via Alembic
