What Blender Ai Library Pro does

Blender AI Library Pro links Blender with online AI services so you can produce assets from text or image prompts. It needs a working internet connection.

It supports text-to-3D models, image-to-3D conversion, text-to-image, text-to-PBR material and text-to-HDRI generation. Artists wanting quick concept assets, placeholder models or lighting maps can experiment without leaving the scene. Results depend on the connected online services, so output quality and availability follow whatever those providers offer at the time.

Key features

  • Text-to-3D model generation
  • Image-to-3D conversion
  • Text-to-image generation
  • Text-to-PBR material generation
  • Text-to-HDRI generation
