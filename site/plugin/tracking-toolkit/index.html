What Tracking Toolkit does

Tracking Toolkit connects OpenXR hardware to Blender. You can view and record tracked devices inside the program, with NLA integration and SMPTE timecode support to keep takes organized and in sync.

It handles camera and object tracking and can capture the movement of actors for motion capture. Trackers that are detected can be turned into scene objects. Virtual production and previs teams can use VR hardware as a capture source.

Key features

  • View and record OpenXR devices
  • NLA integration and SMPTE timecode
  • Tracking of cameras and objects
  • Actor motion capture
  • Add detected trackers to the scene
