What Textyle Premiere does

Textyle Premiere from MotionDesk is an easy interface for animating titles in Premiere Pro. More than 550 title animations in 8 categories are available, together with over 30 styles in 4 categories, both with free updates.

Every animation and style has full settings, and in and out animations are separate with duration control. Editors can quickly assemble polished text without building keyframes themselves. It speeds up title work, especially for editors who reuse the same looks across multiple videos.

Key features

  • 550+ title animations in 8 categories
  • 30+ title styles in 4 categories
  • Full settings for animations and styles
  • Separate in and out with duration control
