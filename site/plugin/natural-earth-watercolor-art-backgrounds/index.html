What Natural Earth Watercolor Art Backgrounds does

Natural Earth Watercolor Art Backgrounds is a Graphic Spirit pack of 33 painted textures. Each is a 6000x4000 pixel JPG at 300 dpi, large enough to crop, scale and animate over in Premiere Pro or other editors.

The washes use organic hues and natural, earthy colors in an abstract artistic style, which suits eco-minded themes. The images also work in Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign, and a user guide PDF comes with the download. Designers and editors needing soft handmade-looking backdrops for titles, lower thirds or branded visuals can use them directly.
