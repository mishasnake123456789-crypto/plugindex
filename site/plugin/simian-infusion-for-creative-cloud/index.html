What Simian Infusion for Creative Cloud does

Simian Infusion for Creative Cloud puts Simian's review and collaboration service for video inside Premiere Pro and After Effects. Feedback no longer has to be copied between a browser and the timeline.

Comments and annotations are pulled into the application and appear as markers synced to the timeline, and replies or revisions can be posted from the same place for the team to see. Cuts, scenes, effects or graphics sequences can be uploaded to Simian project folders, and Premiere Pro exports can use the entire sequence, in/out range or work area. Editors, designers, VFX artists and assistants who review through Simian will work faster.

Key features

  • Pull comments and annotations into the app
  • Timeline markers synced with feedback
  • Reply and revise from inside the application
  • Upload cuts and sequences to Simian projects
  • Export the whole sequence, in/out range or work area
