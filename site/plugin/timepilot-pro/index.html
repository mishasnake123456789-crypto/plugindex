Timeline navigation panel for After Effects with jump modes, a live radar track and trimming tools.

What TimePilot Pro does

TimePilot Pro is Adolfochs's extension for After Effects that speeds up moving around a timeline. Four navigation modes jump between cuts, keyframes or markers, while a Hybrid mode weighs all three and lands on the closest relevant point.

A live radar track lets you click anywhere to teleport to a timecode and shows composition markers as dots. Trimming tools include Trim In/Out, Move to CTI that keeps internal timings, and Trim to Content. Work area controls, frame shifting in single or ten-frame steps, and marker management with protected regions and name search round it out for editors and animators.

Key features

  • Cuts, Keys, Markers and Hybrid jump modes
  • Live radar track with click-to-teleport
  • Trim In/Out, Move to CTI and Trim to Content
  • Work area fitting and jump controls
  • Marker quick-add, search and clear
