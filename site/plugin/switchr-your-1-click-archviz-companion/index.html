What Switchr - Your 1-Click Archviz Companion does

SwitchR speeds up architectural visualization work in Blender by bundling frequent chores into single clicks.

One-click materials come with over 30 CC0 textures, and a Smart Wizard names empty shaders, selects the mesh and applies materials together. Mesh repair fixes bad imports by recalculating normals and clearing sharp edges. You can set origins to bottom, center or top, toggle HDRIs, and add fuzz, scratches and fingerprints. Documentation is included. It is aimed at archviz artists who want finished-looking interiors with minimal manual setup.

Key features

  • 1-click materials with 30+ CC0 textures
  • Smart Wizard for material assignment
  • Mesh repair for bad imports
  • Origin to bottom, center or top
  • HDRI toggle and realism details
