What Motion Camera Addon does

EdenWeby 3D's Motion Camera Addon animates cameras procedurally. Effects compute each frame as you play and can be toggled at will.

Seven shake presets (Stable, Phone, Head POV, Car Chase, Rough Ride, Extreme, Cinematic) are provided, and translation, rotation, per-axis amplitude and speed are set independently. The camera can orbit an object at a set radius and height while tracking it, with procedural zoom. Third-person follow, tracking and dolly zoom add more variety for action scenes and showcases.

Key features

  • Seven camera shake presets
  • Orbit around any object
  • Third-person follow and tracking
  • Dolly zoom effect
  • Real-time per-frame calculation
