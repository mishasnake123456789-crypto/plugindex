What Blender Addon | Projectcheck does

Projectcheck inspects models before delivery and displays problem areas clearly so you can judge quality at a glance. It looks for isolated points, edge vertices, doubles, boundaries, n-gons, intersecting and overlapping faces, non-planar polygons, degenerate polygons and poles with six or more edges.

On the UV side it detects overlapping vertices, overflowing UVs and proportion problems, and it also checks normal direction, missing textures and non-manifold geometry. It is helpful for QA before handing assets to a client or game pipeline.

Key features

  • Detects n-gons, doubles, non-manifold geometry
  • UV overlap, overflow and proportion checks
  • Normal direction and missing texture checks
  • Clear visual display of issues
