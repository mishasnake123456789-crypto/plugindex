What AEJuice All Access Yearly does

All Access Yearly is a subscription to the full AEJuice catalogue, a large body of motion graphics packs and tools for After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Cinema 4D. It runs on Windows 10 or later and macOS 10.14 or later, including Apple M1 and M2.

After Effects support spans CC 2015 to CC 2024 and above, and Premiere Pro from CC 2021. Older CS6, CC 12 and CC 2014 versions can open the After Effects projects without the plugin, as provided. Because many assets come as transparent or black-background videos, they also work in CapCut, Filmora, Final Cut, Nuke, OBS and other editors. Requirements are listed per product.

Key features

  • Access to the AEJuice product library
  • Windows 10+ and macOS 10.14+, M1/M2 included
  • After Effects CC 2015 to CC 2024+
  • Videos for CapCut, Final Cut, Nuke, OBS and others
  • Per-product system requirements
