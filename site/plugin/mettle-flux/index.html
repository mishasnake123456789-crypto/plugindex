What Mettle Flux does

Mettle Flux creates swirling, glowing fractal flame forms inside After Effects and Premiere Pro. Output works for traditional video as well as 360/VR in monoscopic and stereoscopic over/under or side-by-side formats.

The FLUX Preset Panel applies animations in one click, a Globe Preview Panel lets you work in 360 degrees, and controls for Force Field, Mutations, Iterations, Evolutions, Lights and Symmetry allow deep customisation. Rendering is volumetric and GPU accelerated. The native After Effects 3D camera is supported, and a built-in camera serves both hosts. Artists producing abstract backdrops and space visuals will find it striking.

Key features

  • Volumetric fractal flame generation
  • Traditional and 360/VR output
  • FLUX Preset Panel and Globe Preview
  • Force Field, Mutations, Iterations, Evolutions, Lights, Symmetry
  • GPU-accelerated with built-in camera
