What Surface Psycho does

Surface Psycho brings non-destructive NURBS surface design to Blender through Geometry Nodes modifiers, edited in real time.

Surfaces and curves can be trimmed, projected, interpolated and connected with continuity control, and export to STEP and SVG is supported. Industrial and product designers who need CAD-like surfaces inside Blender can model precise forms. Working with NURBS surfaces in Blender is attractive for designers who want smooth, precise curvature and the option to send results to CAD systems.

Key features

  • Non-destructive NURBS modeling
  • Trim, project, interpolate and connect
  • Continuity control
  • STEP and SVG export
