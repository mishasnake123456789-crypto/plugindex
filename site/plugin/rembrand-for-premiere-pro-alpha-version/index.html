What Rembrand for Premiere Pro - Alpha Version does

Rembrand for Premiere Pro (alpha) lets creators send video projects to Rembrand directly from Adobe Premiere, for use with its virtual product placement service. A free Rembrand account is needed to upload.

Version 0.9.5 reduces upload size by encoding the video before uploading. It works with Premiere Pro 9.0 and later. As an alpha release, it is aimed at early adopters and creators exploring product placement for their videos. A free Rembrand account is required.

Key features

  • Upload projects to Rembrand from Premiere
  • For virtual product placement
  • Requires a free Rembrand account
  • Encodes video before upload (0.9.5)
  • Premiere Pro 9.0 and later
