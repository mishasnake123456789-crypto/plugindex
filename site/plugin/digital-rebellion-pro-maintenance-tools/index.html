What Digital Rebellion Pro Maintenance Tools does

Digital Rebellion Pro Maintenance Tools gathers utilities for keeping edit systems healthy, covering Premiere Pro, Avid and Final Cut projects. Autosave Manager archives or deletes autosaves by age and size.

Project Repair tries to revive projects that won't open, Corrupt Clip Finder locates bad media, and Media Salvage extracts video and audio from damaged clips into a new file. Crash Analyzer reads crash logs and suggests solutions, Compressor Repair fixes the Qmaster link, and System Toolkit offers optimization tweaks.
