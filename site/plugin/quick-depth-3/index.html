What Quick Depth 3 does

Quick Depth 3 by Baskl.ai estimates depth from ordinary video and produces a depth map for use in After Effects. Rendering is local, either on GPU or CPU, on Windows and on Macs with Apple Silicon.

A Time Consistency mode smooths results from frame to frame, and Quality Boost processes tiles to retain more detail. It is up to twice as fast as Quick Depth 2 with improved quality. Compositors can use the maps for fake depth of field, fog, relighting or 2.5D camera moves without needing 3D renders.

Key features

  • Depth map from any footage
  • Local GPU or CPU rendering
  • Windows and Apple Silicon Mac support
  • Time Consistency mode
  • Quality Boost tile processing
