What Hedge It does

Hedge It is a Blender plugin from Blender Bits for vegetation. It focuses on hedges, bushes, branches, leaves and vines, fitting nature, garden and architectural visualization themes.

It works with Cycles and Eevee on Blender 4.0 through 5.0, is available as an extension and add-on, and is GPL licensed with documentation. A quick way to dress gardens and exteriors. Gardens, parks and building exteriors benefit most, as the add-on supplies leafy shapes that would otherwise take a long time to model manually.

Key features

  • Hedges, bushes, leaves and vines
  • Cycles and Eevee support
  • Blender 4.0 through 5.0
  • GPL licensed with documentation
