What PlumePack does

PlumePack shrinks and consolidates Premiere Pro projects. It trims and transcodes media, copies dependent files such as proxies, After Effects assets, sidecars and XML, and smart-replaces media in a new project, saving disk space.

Three modes optimize differently: Optimize, Minimize File Number, or one file per timeline clip. Tools also trim or transcode selected clips, unmerge merged clips and reorder tracks. Presets, a batch queue, background processing and integrity checks with a final report are included.

Key features

  • Trim, transcode, archive and organize media
  • Copies proxies, After Effects assets and sidecars
  • Three optimization modes
  • Presets and batch queue
  • Integrity checks with detailed report
