What Tetrominode does

Tetrominode is a complete Tetris game that runs inside Blender as an add-on. Visuals are produced with Geometry Nodes handling instancing and Shader Nodes handling materials, with smooth animation including line-clear transitions.

Every tetromino can have its own material, color and bevel, or share a style. A built-in AI with 1-ply and 2-ply search can play automatically, there is procedural 8-bit music and sound effects, and sessions can be recorded and baked into keyframed animation. More than twelve languages are supported.

Key features

  • Fully playable Tetris in Blender
  • Customizable materials, colors and bevels
  • Built-in AI player
  • Chiptune music and sound effects
  • Record and bake sessions to keyframes
