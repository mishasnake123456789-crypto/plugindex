What Vakago Stock Search Pro does

Vakago Stock Search Pro finds free stock material from within the application. Images, vector graphics, music and videos come from sources like Pixabay and ccMixter, and a few clicks add them to a document, video project or animation.

Images and videos download at full resolution, suitable for print and 4K footage, and files can be used in both personal and commercial work. It plugs into Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects and Premiere Pro. Editors needing quick assets avoid browser detours.
