What Baketool does

Baketool from Cogumelo Softworks reorganizes Cycles baking into a more coherent process. It copes with individual assets or entire scenes, PBR map output, and detail transfer from dense to light meshes.

Multi Job and Multi-Pass baking sets objects, devices, sizes and samples across jobs for a single run. PBR output includes Metallic and ID Map channels absent from Blender's native baking, with per-pass color space handling. Results can be individual textures or a joined atlas, and Bake to Target covers high-to-low transfers. Game artists save repetitive setup.

Key features

  • Multi Job and Multi-Pass baking
  • Metallic and ID Map channels
  • Per-pass textures or atlas
  • Bake to Target
