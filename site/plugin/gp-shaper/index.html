Blender add-on that turns a hovered Grease Pencil stroke into a clean ellipse, arc, rectangle, polyline or line.

What Gp Shaper does

pullup's Gp Shaper cleans up hand-drawn Grease Pencil lines. In draw mode, hover over a stroke and press a shortcut to replace it with a regular shape.

Targets include ellipse or circle, arc, rectangle or square, polyline and straight line. C and Alt+C convert directly or open a pie menu to pick the shape, and V and Alt+V provide further shortcuts. 2D animators and storyboard artists can draw loosely and tidy up afterwards.

Key features

  • Hover and convert strokes
  • Ellipse, arc, rectangle, polyline and line targets
  • C and Alt+C shortcuts
  • Pie menu shape picker
  • Works in draw mode
