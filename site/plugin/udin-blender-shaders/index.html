What Udin Blender Shaders does

Udin Blender Shaders is a material library for Blender that works in both EEVEE and Cycles. Its materials are built purely from math, so their quality does not depend on image resolution or graphics memory.

There is no need to unwrap UVs; if you prefer your own, pushing the projection slider to 1 uses them. Shader families include materials such as car paint, carbon fibre and plastic. This is a convenient shortcut for product and automotive visuals that need convincing surfaces without managing texture files.

Key features

  • Works in EEVEE and Cycles
  • Purely procedural, resolution independent
  • No UV unwrapping needed
  • Optional custom UVs via projection slider
  • Families like car paint, carbon and plastic
