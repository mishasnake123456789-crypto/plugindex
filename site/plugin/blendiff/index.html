What BlenDiff does

BlenDiff, by vishydaperry, compares two versions of a .blend file and shows what differs, which helps in review and debugging. It is tagged under Pipeline and Development.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Compares versions of a .blend
  • Shows what changed
