What AEJuice Whiteboard does

AEJuice Whiteboard offers over 200 animated elements for creating whiteboard-style videos in After Effects. Colors and styles, including sticker, modern and scale, can be edited, and strokes and markers resize automatically.

Customizable settings include Marker Angle, BG Width, Stroke Width, Marker Size, Sticker, Marker Color and BG Color. The Pack Manager enables fast preview, drag-and-drop import and favorites, with very fast rendering and quick downloads. It suits digital tutorials and explainers, and can also be used in Premiere Pro.

Key features

  • 200+ animated whiteboard elements
  • Editable colors and styles
  • Automatic stroke and marker resizing
  • Marker angle, size and background settings
  • Pack Manager with preview and favorites
