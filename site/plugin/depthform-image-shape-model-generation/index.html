What Depthform - Image Shape Model Generation does

DepthForm converts images into 3D geometry. Pick an image shape from a folder browser, then choose Depth for bake-ready meshes or Form to build a mesh.

Depth mode offers Extrude or Push In effects, and scale, rotation, extrude, tilt and bevel are adjustable. Useful for logos, emblems, icons and relief details built from flat artwork. Under the hood you get generate models from image shapes, depth and Form operations and extrude or Push In effects.

Key features
