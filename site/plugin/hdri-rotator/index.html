What HDRI Rotator does

HDRI Rotator, by 1P2D, lets you move and rotate HDRIs, shadows and lights directly in the Blender viewport rather than through node values. It is listed under Lighting.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

  • Adjust shadows and lights from the viewport
