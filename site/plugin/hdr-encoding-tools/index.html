What HDR Encoding Tools does

HDR Encoding Tools, by Theanine3D, encodes and compresses HDR images and vertex colors in Blender, shrinking data for baked results. It is tagged under Bake and Material.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
