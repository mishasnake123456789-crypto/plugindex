What Quick Finger Collision does

Quick Finger Collision adds collision awareness to finger bones. Fingers stop when they touch a mesh instead of passing through, so hands close around props quickly while posing.

A grab function is included, along with documentation. It is useful for animators posing hands holding tools, cups and handles. Among its capabilities are collision-aware finger bones, stops on mesh contact and grab functionality. The package also offers documentation included. It is meant for artists who want to work faster inside Blender.

Key features

  • Collision-aware finger bones
  • Stops on mesh contact
  • Grab functionality
