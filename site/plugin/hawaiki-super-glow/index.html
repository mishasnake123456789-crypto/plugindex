What Hawaiki Super Glow does

Hawaiki Super Glow adds highly controllable glow to footage in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, plus Final Cut Pro X and Motion. It uses an exponential glow algorithm made of six independently adjustable elements.

What glows is isolated and refined using alpha, luminance, channel operations, matte tools and an HSV keyer. Spread, falloff and intensity are adjustable, colour options include chromatic aberration, and blending and compositing choices are provided. High Quality, Fast and Super Fast modes balance quality and speed, and the plugin is fully HDR compatible. Editors and compositors can add bloom to lights, titles or highlights with precision.

Key features

  • Six independently adjustable glow elements
  • Alpha, luminance and HSV keyer isolation tools
  • Spread, falloff and intensity controls
  • Glow colour options with chromatic aberration
  • HDR compatible with three processing modes
