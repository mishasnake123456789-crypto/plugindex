What Vybe does

Vybe is a procedural animation tool from Orrin Zucker for After Effects. Instead of keyframing every move, it uses waveforms to generate motion, which produces varied and sometimes unexpected results.

The approach suits exploratory work, such as looping backgrounds, lively icons and abstract visuals, where happy accidents are welcome. It works with After Effects 2024, 2025 and 2026, and version 1.01 updates the licensing framework. A demo video is available.

Key features

  • Waveform-driven procedural animation
  • Varied, often surprising results
  • Compatible with After Effects 2024 to 2026
  • Version 1.01 with updated licensing
