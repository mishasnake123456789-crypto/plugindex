What Vampro Voice Generator Text-to-Speech does

Vampro Voice Generator Text-to-Speech produces narration in Premiere Pro without recording anyone. You type or paste a script, choose a voice profile and tone preset, and the plugin generates the audio.

Speed and pitch can be adjusted to refine the delivery. The result is imported at the playhead on the timeline or into the Project Bin. Previously generated voice clips can be loaded again to revise the script, voice, tone, speed or pitch. It gives editors and content creators a fast way to add voiceover to explainers, social posts and drafts.

Key features

  • AI voiceovers from text
  • Multiple voice profiles, genders and tone presets
  • Speed and pitch controls
  • Import at the playhead or into the Project Bin
  • Reload clips to revise script or voice
