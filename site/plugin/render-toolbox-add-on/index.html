What Render Toolbox / Add-On does

Render Toolbox is a Blender add-on from Split Studios that centralizes common rendering tasks. You can add and control HDRIs, store camera bookmarks, paint lighting onto models and apply camera effects such as glare and film grain.

HDRI rotation, strength and ground level are adjustable, with options for X and Y background orientation. Camera bookmarks hold full transforms and settings without keyframes. Painting lighting directly on a model lets you steer highlights and draw attention to key areas interactively.

Key features

  • Add and control HDRIs with rotation and ground level
  • Camera bookmarks without keyframes
  • Paint lighting directly on a model
  • Glare and film grain camera effects
  • Background orientation options
