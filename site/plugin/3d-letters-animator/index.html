What 3D Letters Animator does

3D Letters Animator from MotionDesk helps create three-dimensional titles whose letters move in distinctive ways. More than 120 letter animations in six categories give a head start, and everything can be customized.

Six ready-made materials come with it, five designed for the Element 3D plugin and one that works without any plugin. Any font can be used, and delay and direction control how the letters appear. Materials, lighting, orientation and wiggle effects are all adjustable. It is compatible with After Effects 2024 and 2025.

Key features

  • 120+ letter animations in 6 categories
  • 6 ready-made materials, one plugin-free
  • Works with any font
  • Adjustable delay and direction
  • Customizable lighting, orientation and wiggle
