What iZotope Velvet does

iZotope Velvet is a de-esser for Premiere Pro that divides audio into two channels, Sibilance and Tonal. Each can be treated separately, covering de-essing, de-clicking and reshaping of vocal tone.

A Sibilance Learn button finds the offending frequencies and configures the 6-band dynamic EQ automatically. Dynamic Tone Shaping offers Lift and Tame knobs, eight target profiles and frequency sliders, and a De-Click fader removes mouth sounds, pops and clicks while a counter shows the repairs. Either channel can be soloed for checking. Dialogue editors and voiceover producers get a quick way to tame harsh recordings.

Key features

  • Separate Sibilance and Tonal channels
  • Sibilance Learn sets up a 6-band dynamic EQ
  • Lift and Tame tone shaping with 8 target profiles
  • De-Click fader with repair counter
  • Solo either channel
