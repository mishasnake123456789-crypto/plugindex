What AI Color Match Pro does

AI Color Match Pro measures a clip against a reference frame and then applies one overall grade so different shots feel like they belong together. It is meant for sequences filmed on multiple cameras or in changing light, and runs in After Effects and Premiere Pro.

Exposure, contrast, white balance and more are matched, with an adjustable strength control. Analysis happens locally on your GPU so footage stays on your machine. Pro adds 16 and 32 bits per channel for high-fidelity results and exports the match as a .cube LUT for other software. It supports Windows and Mac, Intel or Apple Silicon.

Key features

  • Matches a reference frame's look
  • Adjustable match strength
  • Runs locally on the GPU
  • Pro: 16/32bpc and .cube LUT export
  • Windows, Intel and Apple Silicon Macs
