A Blender add-on for switching between up to eight workspaces with a pie menu and hotkeys.

What Quick Workspace Toggle does

Quick Workspace Toggle makes changing workspaces fast in Blender. Instead of clicking tabs along the top, you open a pie menu or press assigned shortcuts, set up in the add-on tab.

Up to eight workspaces can be listed in the preferences panel, mapped to fixed positions on the pie: left, right, bottom, top and the four corners. A main workspace can optionally be defined, and ordering follows numeric and alphabetic names, so renaming a workspace changes its place. Artists who move between modeling, sculpting, shading and animation layouts will save clicks.

Key features

  • Pie menu and keyboard shortcut switching
  • Up to 8 workspaces
  • Fixed pie positions for each workspace
  • Optional main workspace
  • Ordering by workspace name
