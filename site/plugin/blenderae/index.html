What BlenderAE does

BlenderAE from Salt Media links Blender with After Effects. It exports objects and data from Blender, including vertices, 3D layer transforms, cameras and lights, and keeps layers in sync between the two programs.

A glb (glTF) quick exporter delivers 3D objects to After Effects 23.0 or later. Sync covers objects, vertices, faces, lights and cameras, with no new layers made in After Effects; vertex support captures cloth, ocean, soft body and rigid body simulations, and rotation can use orientation, XYZ values or vertex normals. It connects automatically, handles camera shift, and installs as an extension in Blender 4.2 or later, or as an add-on in Blender 3.6 LTS up to 4.2. Hybrid 3D and 2D artists benefit.

Key features

  • Export objects and data from Blender to After Effects
  • glb quick exporter for After Effects 23.0+
  • Syncs objects, verts, faces, lights and cameras
  • Vertex support for cloth, ocean, soft and rigid body
  • Extension for Blender 4.2+, add-on for 3.6 LTS to 4.2
