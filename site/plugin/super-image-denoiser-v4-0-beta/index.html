Blender addon that denoises renders through compositor nodes with three quality levels and selectable render passes.

What Super Image Denoiser V4.0 + Beta does

Super Image Denoiser adds denoising to a render through the compositor with a single click on the Add Super Denoiser button. After that you select a quality level and decide which render passes get cleaned.

Three qualities are offered: Standard, equivalent to OpenImageDenoiser, High, a compromise between speed and quality, and a higher setting for the best result. Artists with grainy low-sample renders can clean them up without leaving Blender.

Key features

  • One-click setup with compositor nodes
  • Three denoising quality levels
  • Standard level matches OpenImageDenoiser
  • Choose which render passes to denoise
