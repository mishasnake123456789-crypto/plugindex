Rock Scatter

FreeBlender

Scatters rocks and stones across Blender surfaces, with density painted in weight paint mode.

What Rock Scatter does

Rock Scatter populates terrain and other surfaces with stones. A paint tool relies on Blender's weight paint view, so you simply brush over the areas you want covered.

Density follows the paint color, with redder zones receiving more rocks. The built-in library has 12 single rocks and 7 combination rocks or stone clusters, and setup is intentionally easy. Documentation is included, and the add-on works with Blender 2.8 through 3.5. It is a quick way to add natural clutter to landscapes, riverbanks and environment scenes.

Key features

  • Paint placement using weight paint
  • Density driven by paint color
  • 12 single rocks and 7 combo rocks
  • Documentation included
