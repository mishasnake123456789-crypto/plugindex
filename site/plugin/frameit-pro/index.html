A Blender add-on that builds frames with mullions and panels in one click, with optional materials.

What Frameit Pro does

Frameit Pro generates window-style frames, complete with mullions and panels, from polygon geometry. It works on any polygon object, or on selected faces in Edit Mode, which makes it handy for windows, doors, screens and architectural paneling.

Size, depth and offset can be adjusted, and the frame, mullions or panels can each be excluded. Bevels apply to all elements together or individually. Cycles or Eevee materials can be added, reusing earlier ones or creating new ones each time, and an option preserves the original geometry. Architectural and interior modelers building repeated frame details will work faster with it.

Key features

  • One-click frames with mullions and panels
  • Adjustable size, depth and offset
  • Exclude frame, mullions or panels
  • Bevel all elements or each separately
  • Cycles or Eevee materials, original geometry kept
