What Dosch 3D: Shop Clothing does

Dosch 3D: Shop Clothing is aimed at retail visualization. It provides detailed, fully textured models of POS shelves, promotional displays and presentation props such as those used in stores selling clothes, attire and accessories.

Scale adapts to your layout, materials, textures and colors can be freely changed, and your own textures or labels can be applied. Elements can be switched on or off, faded to any transparency and animated, individually or all together. Store designers and advertising artists can mock up shop interiors in Cinema 4D without modeling fixtures.

Key features

  • POS shelves, displays and presentation props
  • Fully textured clothing-store fixtures
  • Editable scale, materials and colors
  • Own textures and labels supported
  • Elements switchable, transparent and animatable
