What Klash does

Klash brings collaboration to the editing apps. Teams can gather comments on a project, drop markers and see all feedback gathered in one overview, all connected to the KLASH platform.

It promises real-time work with zero latency, so cutscenes and videos can be finished while reviewers respond. It runs inside After Effects and Premiere Pro. Production teams that currently chase notes through email or chat can keep review discussion tied to the footage itself.

Key features

  • Add markers
  • Single overview of all feedback
  • Real-time collaboration
