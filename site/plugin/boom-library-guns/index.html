What BOOM Library Guns does

BOOM Library Guns offers sound effects for 17 modern firearms: six pistols, seven rifles and four shotguns, among them the AK47, Colt M4, Beretta M9 and Ithaca M37. It is built for After Effects, Premiere Pro and other WAV-capable software.

The Designed collection has over 350 ready-to-use sounds in four style sets, Light, Crispy, Realistic and Larger than Life, plus handling sounds. The Construction Kit supplies more than 2,600 sources as 12-channel multitrack recordings, over 9 GB, remastered with improved stereo imaging and updated metadata. Files are 24-bit WAV up to 192 kHz.

Key features

  • 17 firearms including AK47 and Colt M4
