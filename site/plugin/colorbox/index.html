What Colorbox 🔴🟠🟡🟢🔵🟣 does

Colorbox, a color toolkit for Blender, deals in palettes, hex codes and color schemes: it swaps palettes, lets you drag colors into place, picks colors out of images and makes color ramps from palettes.

The Swapper changes palettes while you explore combinations, and Color Scheme Maker builds mathematical schemes inside Blender and applies them to the current scene. The Selector and Color Picker take samples from any image, and a loaded image can be boiled down to its dominant colors as a palette. It supports color-driven design in lookdev and motion work.

Key features

  • Palette Swapper for exploring combinations
  • Drag and drop colors from palettes
  • Color Scheme Maker with mathematical schemes
  • Sample colors from any image
  • Turn palettes into color ramps
