After Effects script that crops a precomp to its Region of Interest without moving the layer in the main comp.

What CropROI does

CropROI is an After Effects script by Aris Melachroinos that trims a precomposition down to its Region of Interest. Normally, cropping a precomp shifts how its layer sits in the parent composition; this script keeps the position unchanged.

Add a Region of Interest inside the precomp, select the precomp layer, and press the CropROI button. It works with Adobe After Effects CS6 and later. It is useful for reducing render area and keeping heavy comps efficient.

Key features

  • Crops a precomp to its Region of Interest
  • Keeps layer position unchanged
  • One-button operation
  • Uses an ROI set inside the precomp
  • Works with CS6 and later
