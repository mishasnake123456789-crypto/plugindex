A Blender add-on that offsets a selected bezier spline in 3D space toward the cursor or by a set distance.

What Curve Offset In 3D Space does

Curve Offset In 3D Space creates parallel copies of bezier splines in Blender curve edit mode, positioned in three dimensions. The offset can head toward the cursor location, pass exactly through the cursor point or be a specified distance.

A steps value applies multiple offsets in one go, and offsetting through a precise 3D point currently works for straight segments only. It runs from the W-key menu or the Ctrl+R hotkey and is released under the GPL. Aimed at users of Blender 2.7x, it helps modelers building rails, outlines and parallel paths.

Key features

  • Offsets bezier splines in 3D
  • Toward or through the cursor, or by distance
  • Steps value for multiple offsets
  • W-key menu or Ctrl+R hotkey
  • GPL licensed
