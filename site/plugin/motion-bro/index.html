What Motion Bro does

Motion Bro is a preset browser that lives inside After Effects and Premiere Pro. Instead of building common transitions, overlays and animated scenes from scratch, editors pick a ready-made item from a large library and drop it onto the timeline.

Every preset has a video preview, so you can judge it before applying. Once added, the preset stays editable, and its animation speed can be adjusted live. Transition presets come with matching sound effects, and the library also covers graphic elements and face tools. Both free and premium packs exist, and they are downloaded separately as needed.

Key features

  • Library of 10,000+ presets across transitions, overlays and scenes
  • One-click application to the timeline
  • Video preview for every preset
  • Adjustable animation speed in real time
  • Transitions paired with sound effects
