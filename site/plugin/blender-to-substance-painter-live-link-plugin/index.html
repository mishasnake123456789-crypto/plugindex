What Blender To Substance Painter Live Link Plugin does

This plugin creates a two-way, live bridge linking Blender and Substance Painter. Geometry, materials and textures travel both ways in a full PBR round trip, with a single click to connect and sync.

Normal, roughness, metallic and base color channels are transferred, and multiple models move at once with each object receiving its own texture set. Texture set material sync keeps materials mapped. It spares texture artists repeated exports and imports.

Key features

  • Bi-directional PBR transfer
  • Base color, normal, roughness and metallic
  • Multi-model transfer with separate texture sets
  • One-click connect and sync
