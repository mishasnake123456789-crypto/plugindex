What AEJuice Light Leaks does

AEJuice Light Leaks supplies 62 real light leak clips filmed on a RED camera in 4K. Laid over a shot and set to a blending mode, they add the warm, flaring washes of colour associated with film stock and vintage lenses.

They work in After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and other editors by drag and drop, and the AEJuice Pack Manager provides fast preview and favourites. Built-in digital controls let you tweak colour and intensity, and a single-user licence covers unlimited videos and clients. Editors who want organic-looking flares for transitions and mood can apply them in moments.

Key features

  • 62 leaks filmed on a RED 4K camera
  • Drag and drop into supported editors
  • Blend with footage using blending modes
  • Controls for colour and intensity
  • Pack Manager with preview and favourites
