What Stylized Water Shaders And Generator does

Casey_Sheep's add-on is a procedural set mixing water shader materials with Geometry Nodes generators, producing stylized water surfaces, underwater terrain and water effects that are animated and ready to render.

The water shaders animate the surface with stylized waves, foam and ripples, and expose stylization and speed controls. An Underwater Terrain Generator adjusts height, scale and detail, and a Fish School Generator provides lightweight animated background fish. It suits stylized game scenes and animations.

Key features

  • Animated stylized water shaders
  • Waves, foam and ripples with speed control
  • Underwater Terrain Generator
  • Fish School Generator
  • Fully procedural and ready to render
