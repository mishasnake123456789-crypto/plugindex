What Dosch 3D: LoPoly People Vol. 1 - 3ds Max does

LoPoly People Vol. 1 is the opening volume of Dosch Design's low-polygon human figure series, ready to load into Autodesk 3ds Max. The pack holds 100 fully textured models, each built at 10,000 polygons.

The people are posed in various positions, so scenes can feel populated without the cost of detailed characters. The release is version 1.0.0 of the collection. It suits architectural visualization, street scenes and presentation graphics that need quick, lightweight extras.

Key features

  • 100 fully textured low-poly people
  • 10,000 polygons per model
  • Figures in various positions
