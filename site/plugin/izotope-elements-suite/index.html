What iZotope Elements Suite does

iZotope Elements Suite packages four entry-level audio tools: Ozone Elements, Neutron 5 Elements, RX Elements and Nectar Elements. It covers mastering, mixing, repair and vocals in one collection, for audio work around Premiere Pro projects.

Ozone assists with mastering, Neutron acts as an AI mixing helper, RX includes four repair plug-ins and an improved De-hum, and Nectar's Vocal Assistant adapts to your voice. Repair Assistant tackles clicks, clipping, hum and voice noise. Plugins come in AAX, AU and VST3 (64-bit), on recent macOS and Windows 10 or 11.

Key features

  • Ozone Elements for assisted mastering
  • Neutron 5 Elements AI mixing assistant
  • RX Elements with four repair plug-ins
  • Nectar Elements Vocal Assistant
  • AAX, AU and VST3, 64-bit only
