What Krock.io does

Krock.io is a collaboration plugin that places video review inside the editing workflow. Instead of exchanging emailed notes, collaborators leave comments and annotations on exact frames without leaving After Effects or Premiere Pro.

Timecode marks specific moments, updates arrive in real time, and revisions are tracked so people know which version is current. It also supports approval of the video under review. Individual editors and larger teams alike can use it to keep feedback and picture together in one place.

Key features

  • Frame-accurate comments and annotations
  • Review and approval workflow
  • Timecode marking of specific frames
  • Real-time updates and revision tracking
  • Runs inside After Effects and Premiere Pro
