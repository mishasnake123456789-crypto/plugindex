What Vray ACES Convert does

Vray ACES Convert moves 3ds Max scenes from a Gamma Workflow to ACES OCIO. It processes V-Ray materials, VrayLight, displacement modifiers, VrayFur, Chaos Scatter and Forest Pack bitmaps automatically.

Standard bitmaps become VrayBitmap with sRGB or raw color spaces assigned, and .exr and .hdr files go to raw. In involved setups such as ColorCorrection, Multitex and Composite, maps are split apart from their instances, with no more than two versions of a bitmap made when possible. Batch options cover the selection, all objects, folders with or without subfolders, or chosen files, and .mat files are backed up and overridden or saved with an _ACES suffix. Settings are remembered between sessions.

Key features

  • Converts Gamma Workflow scenes to ACES OCIO
  • Handles V-Ray materials, lights, displacement, fur and Chaos Scatter
  • Automatic bitmap conversion with color space assignment
  • Batch conversion of selections, folders and files
  • Batch .mat conversion with backup or _ACES suffix
