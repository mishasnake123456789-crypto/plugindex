Blender add-on for managing light linking, choosing which objects or collections a light illuminates.

What Light Helper does

Light Helper manages light exclusion in Blender. Decide which objects or collections a lamp reaches, or which lamps reach an object, for light and shadows alike.

Objects with lights and materials with emission above zero are recognized automatically, and light and material exclusions can be created, including excluding a light along with what it illuminates. Lighting artists who want selective illumination without complicated setups save time. This is handy in scenes where one lamp should light only a hero object, or where a bright emitter should be kept out of a set of props.

Key features

  • Light Link and Object Link
  • Supports lighting and shadows
  • Automatic detection of emitters
  • Light and material exclusions
