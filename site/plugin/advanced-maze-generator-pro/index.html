What Advanced Maze Generator Pro does

Hand-modelling a maze is pointless when an algorithm can do it. blenderVfxMaster's Advanced Maze Generator Pro creates mazes in Blender using more than twenty methods, among them Recursive Backtracking, Kruskal, Prim, Wilson, Eller, Binary Tree, Sidewinder and Growing Tree.

Outlines go well beyond squares: circles, stars, hearts, spirals, rings, polygons, fractal and noise-driven layouts and custom silhouettes are all possible. Walls, floors, materials and grid size can be tuned, which serves game levels, puzzle art and abstract motion backgrounds.

Key features

  • Over 20 maze generation algorithms
  • Rectangle, circle, star, heart, spiral and custom shapes
  • Control over walls and floors
  • Material and grid dimension settings
  • Fractal and noise-based patterns
