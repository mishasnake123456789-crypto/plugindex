What Cinecast Projector does

Projecting video or a gobo-like pattern in Blender normally calls for a hand-wired node setup. Cinecast Projector from Shilo Labs creates a complete projector rig with the shader work already done, aimed at video projection, motion design visuals and creative lighting.

Quick Aim swings the rig toward whatever surface your cursor points at, and Auto-Shape Correction straightens projections distorted by steep angles; Corner Pin Control lets you drag the picture's corners in the viewport. Scale, position and rotation of the image are adjustable, and fifty seamless loops come built in.

Key features

  • Interactive Quick Aim to a surface
  • One-click auto-shape correction
  • Corner pin dragging in the viewport
  • Scale, position and rotation controls
  • Built-in seamless loops
