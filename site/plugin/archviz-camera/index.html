What Archviz Camera does

Archviz Camera deals with the details that matter in architectural stills. One click straightens converging vertical lines by adjusting a camera's rotation and shift.

A Create Camera From View button makes a camera inheriting the current viewport perspective, and a camera list lets you view, activate and rename cameras from one place. Render settings can switch per shot, and a Stream Deck profile is included for hardware shortcuts. Interior and exterior visualization artists get cleaner, faster framing.

Key features

  • One-click vertical correction
  • Create Camera From View
  • Camera list with activate and rename
  • Per-shot render settings
  • Stream Deck profile
