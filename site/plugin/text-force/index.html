What Text Force does

Text Force, made by Marco Sanasi, is a script for After Effects aimed at rapid-fire text sequences, the kind used in kinetic lyric and promo videos. It builds them from twelve parametric animation styles, including Slide, Zoom, Typewriter, Ping Pong and Rotational.

The animation can be synced to an audio track or voiceover, working fully automatically or with manual setup. A background generator adds text solids, each layer can use its own style, and setups can be saved and loaded as presets. Text separation and alignment tools are included, and Speech Mode works together with Layer Force.

Key features

  • 12 parametric fast-cut styles
  • Audio and voiceover sync modes
  • Text solid background generator
  • Per-layer style and saved presets
  • Text separation and alignment tools
