What Aeblink V1.1 ( Now Support Blender does

AeBlink is a Blender plugin paired with an After Effects script. One click carries the camera, nulls, solids and footage of an After Effects project into Blender, keyframes included.

Blender detects the export and imports automatically. Cameras keep position, rotation and focal length, solids become image planes, nulls become empties, and footage loads on its own. A smooth route for combining 2D and 3D work. This is especially useful for compositors and motion artists who plan shots in After Effects and want to extend them into 3D.

Key features

  • Auto-import when After Effects exports
  • Keyframed camera with focal length
  • Solids to image planes, nulls to empties
  • Footage auto-load
