Generates randomized, automatically labeled synthetic image data for computer vision work.

What Rendersynth does

Rendersynth turns Blender into a generator of synthetic training data. It randomizes scenes and produces images along with labels, which can be used to train and test computer vision models.

Researchers and developers who lack enough real photos can create varied examples with known ground truth. Working in Blender means physics and rendering are available for building realistic variation, and the labelling step is handled alongside the render rather than annotated afterwards.

Key features

  • Randomized scene generation
  • Automatic labels for images
  • Aimed at computer vision datasets
  • Uses Blender's renderer
