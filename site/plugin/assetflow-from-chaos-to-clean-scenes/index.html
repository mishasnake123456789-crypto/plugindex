What Assetflow - From Chaos To Clean Scenes does

Assetflow is a Blender add-on from wans7 for asset management and scene cleanup. It expands the F2 shortcut with extra menus while keeping its usual object-renaming role, and a right-click menu offers instant access.

Meshes can be moved into groups without using the M shortcut, single or multiple at a time. Group colors come from Blender's options and can be renamed, with mesh colors set to match automatically. Visibility control and access to transforms, modifiers and materials are included. Artists with messy scenes get a quicker route to order.

Key features

  • F2 menu extended with organization options
  • Right-click menu access
  • Group meshes without the M shortcut
  • Group colors with auto-matching mesh colors
  • Visibility controls
