What One-Click Split - Blender Add-On does

One-Click Split is a Blender add-on for slicing a mesh in two without a manual bisect routine. Choose an X, Y or Z axis in world or local space, or use a guide plane you can move and rotate to cut at any angle.

A cap toggle fills the cut surface or leaves it open, and a separate offset pushes the halves apart so you can inspect the result. It accounts for evaluated meshes, keeping unapplied modifiers in mind. The tools are found in the N-panel. Modelers preparing cutaways, assets for printing or split parts will find it quick.

Key features

  • Split along X, Y or Z, world or local
  • Movable, rotatable guide plane
  • Cap the cut or leave it open
  • Offset halves apart for inspection
  • Works with evaluated meshes
