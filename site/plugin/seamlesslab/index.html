Blender add-on that bakes procedural node patterns into seamless tileable PNG textures after a tiling preview.

What Seamlesslab does

Procedural patterns are resolution-independent but don't tile by default. kunyongChen's SeamlessLab converts node setups into repeating textures, first showing a preview on a grid of planes and then baking the outcome to PNG for materials.

Preview density ranges from 2x2 to 8x8, and baking sizes go from 64px up to 8K, with UV padding handled automatically. Your original nodes stay untouched. Noise and Voronoi textures are supported, with scale, roughness and distortion adjustable. Handy for texture artists who need seamless maps.

Key features

  • Converts procedural nodes to tileable patterns
  • Tiling preview from 2x2 to 8x8
  • Bakes PNG from 64px to 8K
  • Non-destructive workflow
  • Noise and Voronoi texture support
