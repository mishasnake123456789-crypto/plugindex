Blender facial animation add-on that builds slider controllers and generates the drivers for you.

What Wk Facerig does

Wk Facerig simplifies facial rigging in Blender. You place slider controllers in the viewport, connect shape keys, bones or object transforms, and the add-on writes all the drivers itself, including left and right asymmetry.

Controllers come in two-, three- and four-way types and presets exist for the mouth, eyes and brows; they can be grouped and locked. Shape Linking attaches existing shape keys in one pass, and symmetric sculpts are split into left and right halves automatically. Bone chains and object channels are handled through mapping with an influence ramp. Documentation and a tips section explain how to sculpt key expressions.

Key features

  • Viewport slider controllers with presets
  • One-pass shape key linking
  • Automatic left and right splitting
  • Bone and object mapping with influence ramp
  • Drivers generated automatically
