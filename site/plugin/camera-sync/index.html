What Camera Sync does

Camera Sync is a Blender add-on that gathers a few frequently needed camera operations in one convenient spot. It spares you from digging through menus during framing and layout.

You can toggle the camera view, allow viewport navigation while inside the camera view, and lock or unlock transformations on the selected camera so it does not move by accident. It is a small utility for anyone who often adjusts shots. Documentation is included and the add-on is GPL licensed.

Key features

  • Toggle camera view quickly
  • Enable navigation within camera view
  • Lock and unlock camera transformations
  • GPL licensed with documentation
