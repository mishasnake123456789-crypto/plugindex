What Geo Stacks Add-On does

Geo Stacks builds realistic piles in Blender using Geometry Nodes and instancing. The scene remains quick and responsive even with complex stacks.

Zero-Gap Instancing avoids floating objects, and automatic center correction aligns assets so piles feel grounded. Any collection can serve as the source. Procedural vertical control sets height, spacing and variation, Natural Variation adds controlled disorder, and Volumetric Masking shapes where the stack grows. It suits crates, books, plates and similar clutter.

Key features

  • Zero-Gap Instancing
  • Automatic center correction
  • Stacks from any asset collection
  • Height, spacing and variation controls
  • Volumetric masking
