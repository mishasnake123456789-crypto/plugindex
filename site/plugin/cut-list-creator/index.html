What Cut List Creator does

Cut List Creator is a Blender export add-on for woodworkers. Design furniture or another project in Blender, and it calculates the dimensions needed for each kind of timber.

The list is produced through File, Export, Cut List (CSV). You can export only selected objects with geometry, and group parts by collections to keep the list organized. A bridge between 3D design and the workshop. Because the list comes from your actual model, it stays accurate as the design changes, reducing errors when ordering lumber and planning cuts.

Key features

  • Exports a CSV cut list
  • Export selected objects only
  • Group parts by collection
  • Available from the Export menu
