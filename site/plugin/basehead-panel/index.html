What BaseHead Panel does

BaseHead Panel connects the BaseHead sound library tool with Premiere Pro. Communication runs in both directions, so editors and sound designers can search, audition and organize effects and music cues, then move them into the project.

Spot to Track and Xfer to Bin functions place sounds on the timeline or in bins, Follow Session Folder tracks work in progress, and Auto-Switching Paths handles locations. It runs on PC and Mac.

Key features

  • Bi-directional communication with BaseHead
  • Search and organize effects and music
  • Spot to Track and Xfer to Bin
  • Follow Session Folder and Auto-Switching Paths
  • PC and Mac
