What Cavendish Music search does

Cavendish Music search lets editors find production music from within Premiere Pro and bring it straight into the project. The catalogue holds more than 330,000 tracks organized by genre, style, length, mood and rhythm.

Results include vocals, instrumentals, alternate versions and stems, and a similar-tracks search finds related pieces. Tracks come in 60, 30 and 15 second cuts, can be downloaded several at once as WAV or MP3, and tested in your production.

Key features

  • 330,000+ production tracks
  • Filters for genre, style, length, mood and rhythm
  • Vocals, instrumentals, alternates and stems
  • Similar tracks search
  • WAV and MP3 downloads
