What Direktor 3D does

Direktor 3D packs the ingredients of a music video into one Blender add-on. It targets creators who want finished-looking scenes quickly, with pie-menu access and built-in tutorial links.

Contents include 14 customizable template scenes for cities, cars, characters or visualizers, 14 real-time geometry-node effects, and a logo maker with 12 text presets. There are 100 objects, 15 materials, a Light Studio with 20 presets, and 21 modern dance animations that can be applied to any 3D model.

Key features

  • 14 template scenes with procedural controls
  • 14 real-time geometry node effects
  • 3D logo maker, 100 objects, 15 materials
  • Light Studio and 21 dance animations
