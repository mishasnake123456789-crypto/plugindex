What Flexcam does

FlexCam is a smart camera for Blender that behaves like a camera operator. It tracks the action, frames shots and dynamically adjusts field of view instead of staying locked in place.

It works with Cycles and Eevee on Blender 4.4 through 4.5, is released under the GPL license and includes documentation. Useful for animators who want natural-looking coverage without manual keyframing. Because the framing reacts to the subject automatically, it can replace tedious manual camera animation for tracking shots and dynamic coverage.

Key features

  • Follows the action automatically
  • Dynamic field of view
  • Blender 4.4 through 4.5
  • Cycles and Eevee compatible
