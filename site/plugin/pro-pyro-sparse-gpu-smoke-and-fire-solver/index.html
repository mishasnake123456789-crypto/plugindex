What Pro Pyro - Sparse Gpu Smoke And Fire Solver does

CG_Flow's Pro Pyro runs fluid dynamics entirely on the graphics card to make volumetric fire and smoke in Blender, with an approachable interface in the n-panel.

Change heat, buoyancy or turbulence and the viewport responds instantly, so exploring needs no baking. A sparse design only allocates space where fire or smoke exist, letting sprawling effects use a fraction of the VRAM dense solvers require. A complete combustion pipeline sits underneath. Effects artists can iterate rapidly on explosions, campfires and chimneys.

Key features

  • Real-time GPU fluid solver
  • Sparse allocation lowers VRAM use
  • No baking needed while exploring
  • Heat, buoyancy and turbulence controls
  • N-panel interface
