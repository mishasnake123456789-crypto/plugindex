What MotionSpice 2 does

MotionSpice 2 is a motion toolkit from InovativeWorks for After Effects. It gathers over 110 presets in 15 categories that apply with one click, and also lets you turn your own work into a reusable library.

CompToAsset and LayerToAsset capture whole comps or chosen layers, including effects, keyframes and expressions. Smart Import offers Auto Color, Snap Drop and Asset Shuffle in multi and batch modes. Collections and tags organize everything, unused motions can be hidden, and hover previews show auto-generated GIFs. Multilingual search, history and exportable settings round it out, and it stays smooth with large libraries.

Key features

  • 110+ presets across 15 categories
  • CompToAsset and LayerToAsset saving
  • Smart Import with Auto Color and Snap Drop
  • Collections, tags and hover GIF previews
  • Multilingual search and settings export
