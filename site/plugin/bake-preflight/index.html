What Bake Preflight does

Bake Preflight, by Fortune.jp, checks your setup for typical baking errors ahead of time, so failed bakes are caught before they cost render time. It is listed under Bake.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Detects common baking mistakes
