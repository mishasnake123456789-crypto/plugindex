What Suzanne_Blender-Addon does

Suzanne is a collection of positioning and replacement helpers from Animation Store. Any number of objects can be snapped to the surface of another, aligning both position and rotation.

Several separate objects can be parented to a bone in one click, and any object can be replaced with another without losing its modifiers or constraints. Hook curve and random replace tools are also included. It supports Blender 3.0 through 3.6 under a GPL license, and suits set dressing and character prop attachment.

Key features

  • Snap multiple objects to a surface
  • Parent many objects to a bone in one click
  • Replace objects keeping modifiers and constraints
  • Hook curve and random replace
