What Lipsyncx - Automatic Lip Sync & Facial Acting does

LipSyncX creates an automatic lip-sync pass from loaded voice audio. It can transcribe the speech or accept dialogue text you enter, then builds visemes from words and phonemes.

A timeline editor lets you adjust words and timing, so animators can review and polish rather than start from scratch. Useful for character dialogue and facial acting. Its editable approach is meant to get a rough pass done fast while leaving final judgment to the animator.

Key features

  • Automatic lip-sync from voice audio
  • Transcription or manual dialogue entry
  • Visemes from words and phonemes
  • Timeline editor for words and timing
