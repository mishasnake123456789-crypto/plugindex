What DDSlideshow does

DDSlideshow is a Blender add-on by Michael-Lamertz that creates slideshows with the classic Ken Burns look, where each image slowly zooms and pans. It works in Blender's sequencer and needs just one button to build the result.

You can add optional intro and outro slides, for example with a studio logo, and an optional audio track. The range of zooming and the direction of panning are configurable. It is a quick way to turn a folder of pictures into a presentable video without opening a separate editor.

Key features

  • Ken Burns zoom and pan effect
  • Optional intro and outro slides
  • Optional audio track
  • Configurable zoom range and pan direction
  • One-button workflow
