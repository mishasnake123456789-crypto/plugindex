Blender add-on for managing HDRIs and world lighting with dome, sky and shadow catcher tools.

What Qhdri Pro does

QHDRI Pro handles environment lighting and HDRI libraries in Blender, with support for EEVEE, Cycles and Octane. It is meant for artists who keep many HDRIs and swap them often.

Three world modes are offered: HDRI, Dome and Procedural Sky, the last with presets for clouds and stars. HDRI assets can be organized with subfolders and several asset paths. The background can be replaced with a color, gradient or another HDRI, and a shadow catcher panel includes object reflections on the catcher plane.

Key features

  • HDRI, Dome and Procedural Sky modes
  • Organized HDRI libraries with subfolders
  • Background replacement by color, gradient or HDRI
  • Shadow catcher with reflections
