What Downtown Generator - Procedural City Tool does

Downtown Generator creates city scenes in Blender with a procedural approach. You extrude to make buildings and use loop cuts to divide the layout into streets, giving results immediately and keeping control over shapes, layouts and variation.

A layout system handles block spacing and overall structure, with adjustable street width, sidewalks and road shoulders. Street props such as billboards and lights are available, along with roof props and environmental details. Environment artists, game level designers and animators who need a believable urban backdrop will find it far quicker than modeling each block.

Key features

  • Extrude buildings, loop cut streets
  • Adjustable street width, sidewalks and shoulders
  • Block spacing layout system
  • Street and roof props
