What Lazy Composer: One-Click Film Emulation Addon does

Lazy Composer applies a finished compositing look in one click, giving renders a film-like feel and more cinematic depth. It works with both Cycles and Eevee.

Controls cover color correction, brightness and contrast, lens effects, glow and glare for highlights and bloom, and atmospheric light rays. Hallation recreates the soft red halos of vintage film, and a procedural grain adds texture. The film emulation can run in the real-time viewport compositor, so you see the look as you work and adjust it from one panel.

Key features

  • Color correction and image adjustments
  • Lens effects, glow, glare and light rays
  • Hallation for vintage film halos
  • Procedural film grain
  • Viewport compositor preview
