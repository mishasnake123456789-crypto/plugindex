What MassRename does

MassRename lets editors rename a whole selection at once. Pick the clips, type a naming pattern, and see a live preview of every resulting name before applying it.

A single click renames everything. In After Effects it works on both project items and composition layers. It's sold as a one-time purchase with no subscription, appealing to editors and motion designers who deal with piles of numbered clips. This is useful for tidying large bins of footage, and the preview prevents naming surprises before anything changes.

Key features

  • Batch rename in one click
  • Live preview of resulting names
  • Pattern-based naming
  • Project items and comp layers in After Effects
  • One-time purchase
