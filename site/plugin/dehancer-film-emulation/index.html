What Dehancer Film Emulation does

Dehancer Film Emulation brings analog film character to After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. Its creators, a mix of enthusiasts and scientists, modeled more than 60 photographic and cinema films, such as Kodak Vision 3, Portra 400, Cinestill 800, Ilford HP5 Plus and Kodachrome 64, each with Push and Pull.

The set adds 130+ camera log profiles, print films, photo paper looks and a grain engine with profiles from 8mm to 65mm. Halation, bloom, film breath, damage and overscan are included too, so colorists can build convincing film looks.

Key features

  • 60+ film stocks with Push/Pull
  • 130+ camera log profiles
  • Print film and photo paper emulation
  • Grain profiles for 8, 16, 35 and 65mm
  • Halation, bloom, film breath and damage
