What Compositing Line Art - Cla does

Compositing Line Art produces outlines in the compositor instead of through geometry, which allows it to cope with extremely heavy scenes. It is built for Blender 5.0 and later.

It copes with tens of millions of polygons; CLA Main Group and CLA AOV Group automate the technical setup, and transparency support gives cutouts such as leaves, lace and decals proper outlines. Style presets offer artistic looks, and an Ignore Material Normal toggle refines results. Useful for NPR, illustration and stylized rendering.

Key features

  • Handles tens of millions of polygons
  • CLA Main and AOV groups automate setup
  • Alpha support for leaves, lace and decals
  • Artistic style presets
