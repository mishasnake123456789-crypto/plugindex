Imports Grabyo VOD content, with multi-track audio, captions and markers, into Premiere Pro.

What Grabyo does

Grabyo connects Premiere Pro to a Grabyo account so editors can browse and bring VOD content into a project. Multi-track audio comes across, with track selection handled in Premiere Pro.

Captions arrive as editable data, and video markers, both AI-generated and manual, are imported as well. A Grabyo account is required. Because captions and markers arrive with the files, the content is ready to work with right after import. It spares editors from downloading files separately when content already sits in Grabyo.

Key features

  • Import VOD files from a Grabyo account
  • Multi-track audio clips
  • Editable captions data
  • AI-generated and manual markers
