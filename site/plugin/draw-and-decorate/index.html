Blender add-on for turning hand-drawn or digital artwork into designs, signs and inscriptions.

What Draw And Decorate does

Draw And Decorate lets you bring your own artwork into Blender scenes. Draw on paper and photograph it, or draw in Blender or another program, then add the result to the add-on.

The designs can then be used to decorate stages, cities, bars and similar scenes with signs and inscriptions. It works with Blender 3.1 through 4.3, includes documentation, and is GPL licensed.

Key features

  • Use drawings from paper or software
  • Create custom signs and inscriptions
  • Decorate stages, cities and bars
  • Blender 3.1 to 4.3
