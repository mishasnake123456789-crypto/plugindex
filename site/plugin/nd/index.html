What ND does

ND is a Blender add-on by HugeMenace for non-destructive hard surface modeling. It collects workflow improvements, operations and generators, and adds helpers for finishing work in outside programs such as Substance Painter and Marmoset Toolbag.

Tools work in both object and edit modes, with interactive modifiers and the ability to recall modifiers and alter their parameters. Boolean, bevel, extrusion and replication workflows are streamlined. Model packaging prepares material IDs, high and low poly versions and UVs, and scene management brings overlays and visualization modes. Behavior is customizable. Prop and weapon artists benefit.

Key features

  • Interactive modifiers and recall
  • Streamlined boolean, bevel and extrude workflows
  • Packaging for material IDs, high/low poly, UVs
  • Scene management and visualization modes
