What AI Relight Pro does

AI Relight Pro from Baskl.ai lets you add lighting to a shot after it was filmed, working inside After Effects and Premiere Pro. It analyzes the 3D properties of the footage so placed lights behave as though they are in the scene.

You control how light bounces along X, Y and Z directions, and the position and color of each light can be animated. The lights alone can be rendered when needed. It targets footage that was not lit properly and runs on Windows and on Apple Silicon Macs.

Key features

  • Customizable lights added in post
  • Understands the 3D properties of footage
  • Light direction control on X, Y and Z
  • Animatable light position and color
  • Option to render only the lights
