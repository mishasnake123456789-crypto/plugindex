What Decal Master - Add Decals Like Never Before - Ultimate Addon does

Decal Master helps Blender artists apply decals to surfaces. It controls placement, scaling and rotation, and decals can be blended and customized.

Damage effects and shading controls make labels, scratches and markings look worn, while normal stamps add finer detail. Free decal packs arrive through updates, and documentation is included. Hard surface and prop artists add realistic panel markings and surface wear easily. Artists can build up detail passes on hard surface models without painting every marking into a texture by hand.

Key features

  • Add, blend and customize decals
  • Placement, scale and rotation control
  • Damage and shading effects
  • Normal stamps and free decal packs
