What Mixamo Rig does

Mixamo Rig, by tyler.tofu, builds an animator-friendly control rig on top of the skeleton you import from Mixamo as FBX. It is listed under Import-Export and Rigging.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Control rig from Mixamo FBX skeleton
