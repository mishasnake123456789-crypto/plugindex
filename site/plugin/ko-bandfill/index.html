What KO BandFill does

KO BandFill generates stripe and band patterns procedurally inside After Effects. One effect covers a lot of ground, from animated backgrounds to transitions, gradients and shapes, all adjusted through parameters rather than drawn by hand.

Twenty geometries share a common logic, and Variation and Distortion controls broaden the range of results. Settings are organized in sections named Band, Fill, Range, Feather, Variation, Distortion and Rendering, giving a fast parametric workflow from a single control set. A Basic Version is included next to the full controls.

Key features

  • 20 band geometries
  • One effect for backgrounds, transitions and shapes
  • Variation and Distortion controls
  • Parameters grouped into seven sections
  • Basic Version included
