What Cassette Customizer does

Cassette Customizer drops a fully assembled cassette tape model into a Blender scene with one operator. Sidebar controls then let you style it live.

Five built-in decal designs appear as thumbnails, and label templates come with an included PSD source for all five graphics. Sliders adjust base color, roughness, transmission and procedural edge wear, and a Middle Round shape key distorts the label at value 1. Randomize shuffles color, finish, roundness and label, and the spools and film can be animated.

Key features

  • Append a ready cassette prop
  • Five decal designs with PSD templates
  • Color, roughness, transmission and edge wear sliders
  • Randomize and spool animation
