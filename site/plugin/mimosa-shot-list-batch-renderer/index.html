What Mimosa - Shot-List Batch Renderer does

Mimosa records the current view as a shot, saving camera, timeline frame, resolution and collection visibility together. A whole list of shots can then be rendered in one batch.

Collection visibility per shot lets you render variants such as furnished versus empty rooms or finish A versus finish B, and day or night setups are supported per shot. Architectural visualization and product artists can deliver many variations without manual setup.

Key features

  • One-click shot capture
  • Collection visibility per shot
  • Day and night settings per shot
  • Batch render of the full list
