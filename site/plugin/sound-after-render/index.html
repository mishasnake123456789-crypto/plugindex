What Sound After Render does

Sound After Render notifies you audibly when a render is done, so you can step away. Pick from more than 40 sounds in the sidebar under Tools.

A default sound for all scenes can be set in preferences. The add-on is built as a Blender Extension and comes with documentation. It is helpful for long renders you do not want to watch. Among its capabilities are 40+ notification sounds, settings in sidebar Tools tab and global default sound in preferences.

Key features

  • 40+ notification sounds
  • Settings in sidebar Tools tab
  • Global default sound in preferences
  • Blender Extension format
