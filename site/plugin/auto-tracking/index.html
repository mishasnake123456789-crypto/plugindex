What Auto-tracking does

Auto-tracking by mcd1992 speeds up VFX motion tracking in Blender. It adds a toolbar to the tracking viewport which runs Detect Features and follows it with a forward track for you.

It is aimed at footage that has no tracking markers in the scene and works together with Blender's built-in tools or other extensions. Compositors and VFX artists get a fast starting point for a camera solve without repeating the two steps manually.

Key features

  • Toolbar in the tracking viewport
  • Detect features then track forward
  • Aimed at marker-free footage
  • Works with built-in tracking tools
