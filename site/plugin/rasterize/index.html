What Rasterize does

Rasterize converts the picture in DaVinci Resolve into a visual pattern assembled from shapes, in the spirit of halftone and graphic poster treatments. It was published by Nx Color in May 2025 as version 1.0.

Shapes and dynamic effects are combined to produce stylized, graphic results from ordinary footage or stills. The plugin works on macOS, Windows and Linux and is compatible with Resolve 20 and 19. A demo video is available. Motion designers and editors can use it for title backgrounds, music visuals or any project that calls for a bold, abstract look.

Key features

  • Builds patterns from the source image
  • Uses shapes and dynamic effects
  • Cross-platform on macOS, Windows and Linux
  • Works with Resolve 19 and 20
