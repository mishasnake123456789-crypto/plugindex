What Light Control does

Light Control is a Blender add-on from Blender Control for adjusting lights right in the 3D viewport. Tools let you swing lights around a pivot, alter how far a light sits from it, and relocate lights and pivot together.

It operates inside one 3D viewport, fullscreen included. A free version offers the essential tools, while the full version includes all tools and free updates, and documentation is provided. It is practical for artists who position lights by eye.

Key features

  • Orbit lights around a pivot
  • Change distance to the pivot
  • Move lights and pivot
  • Works in one viewport, including fullscreen
  • Free essentials version and full version
