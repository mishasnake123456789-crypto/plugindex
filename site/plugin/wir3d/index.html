What WIR3D does

WIR3D is a plugin by mograph.tools that builds three-dimensional wireframe forms in After Effects. It starts from one shape layer and produces a wireframe object, saving the effort of modeling in a separate 3D application.

It works with After Effects 2020 through 2025 and is at version 1.2.1, last updated in January 2024. Each license includes two activations, so it can be used on two machines. A demo video is available, and the look suits technology graphics, HUDs and abstract motion design.

Key features

  • 3D wireframe generation from a shape layer
  • Works with After Effects 2020 to 2025
  • Two activations per license
  • Demo video available
