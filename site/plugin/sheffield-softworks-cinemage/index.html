What Sheffield Softworks Cinemage does

Sheffield Softworks Cinemage grades footage with two linked tints. A double hue ring sets each tint, and a pairing slider controls how far apart the hues sit, defaulting to 180 degrees.

Central tint rings set hue and saturation, with intensity growing as you drag outward. A Peek button shows the image without correction, and on-screen guides can be dimmed or hidden. Basic controls cover presets, Strength, Balance and Mix, while advanced ones offer Soften/Sharpen, blend modes, masking and luma or colour retention. Luminance or saturation masks blend the tints, and one tint can smooth while the other sharpens. Works in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro through FxFactory.

Key features

  • Paired tints with double hue ring
  • Pairing slider
  • Peek button and dimmable guides
  • Strength, Balance, Mix and preset controls
  • Soften/Sharpen, blend modes and masking
