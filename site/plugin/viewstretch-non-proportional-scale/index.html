What Viewstretch - Non-Proportional Scale does

ViewStretch brings the non-proportional scale workflow from Autodesk Alias into Blender. A horizontal drag scales along the view's X axis and a vertical drag along its Y axis.

It is fully non-destructive: mesh data never changes, and original transforms and parenting are restored exactly on reset. Designers can explore proportions quickly. Among its tools are single-gesture stretch, view-axis based scaling, non-destructive, exact reset and alias-style workflow. It suits designers who like to explore proportions interactively.

Key features

  • Single-gesture stretch
  • View-axis based scaling
  • Non-destructive, exact reset
  • Alias-style workflow
