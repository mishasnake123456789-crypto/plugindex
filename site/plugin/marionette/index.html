Snaps selected Premiere Pro clips into positions within an area based on each clip's size.

What MARIONETTE does

MARIONETTE helps lay out multiple clips on screen, such as grids or split screens. It places each selected clip at a position inside a chosen area, taking the clip's size into account, and can account for Crop effect values.

Three areas are available. Modifier-clicks shift the anchor point either within the clip or within the area, and several items can be placed together. It's a time saver for multi-panel compositions made in Premiere Pro.

Key features

  • Snaps clips within an area by clip size
  • Three area choices
  • Places multiple selected items together
  • Honors Crop effect values
  • Modifier-click anchor point adjustment
