What 3D Slicer Suite does

3D Slicer Suite is a Blender add-on for printing models bigger than your printer bed. You define the printer volume as block sizes, such as 20 or 30 centimeters, and the object is sliced into printable parts.

An intelligent pin system studies mesh walls, hollow objects included, to place alignment keys, and a tolerance setting makes parts fit together. Each piece is exported as its own STL in a batch. Makers and prop builders can assemble big prints from smaller pieces.

Key features

  • Grid slicing by printer volume
  • Alignment pins based on mesh geometry
  • Tolerance for fitting parts
  • Batch STL export per piece
