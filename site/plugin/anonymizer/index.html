What Anonymizer does

Anonymizer helps protect identities in footage. It finds faces in a scene and can replace each one with a randomly generated AI face, or hide them with automatic blurring or pixelization. It runs in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and Nuke.

The swap approach keeps faces looking natural in the frame, unlike a blur that draws attention to the redaction. Version 1.1.0 updated the swap model, and a demo video is available. It's relevant for documentaries, news and compliance work.

Key features

  • Swaps faces for random AI-generated ones
  • Automatic face blurring
  • Face pixelization
  • Works in After Effects, Resolve, Premiere Pro and Nuke
  • Updated swap model in 1.1.0
