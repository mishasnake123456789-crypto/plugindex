What IndiVideo does

IndiVideo, from Bluerush, lets motion designers build video templates in After Effects whose content changes per viewer. Text, imagery, charts and data fields can all be variable, so one design replaces many hand-made versions.

Conditional logic and dynamic content rules decide what each person sees, and personalised calls to action can be included. From a single motion framework it can generate thousands or even millions of tailored videos. After Effects 23.0 or later is required. Marketing teams and motion artists working on campaigns that need individual messages will find it removes repetitive rendering of variations.

Key features

  • Text, imagery, charts and data fields can be variable
  • Conditional logic and dynamic content rules
  • Personalised calls to action
  • Scales from thousands to millions of videos
  • Requires After Effects 23.0+
