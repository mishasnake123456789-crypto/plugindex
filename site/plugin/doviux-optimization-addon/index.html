Blender add-on that picks Subdivision and Multires levels per object from its size, camera distance and lens settings.

What Doviux Optimization Addon does

Heavy subdivision on objects the camera barely sees wastes render time. Doviux Optimization Addon works out suitable levels for you, weighing an object's size, vertex count, distance from the camera and camera properties like focal length and depth of field.

It handles both Subdivision and Multiresolution modifiers. Scene-wide behaviour comes from global variables, while custom properties give particular objects their own rules, and for animated shots the optimization can be baked into keyframes. Documentation is included. Dense scenes become lighter to render.

Key features

  • Automatic subdivision levels
  • Works with Subdivision and Multires modifiers
  • Considers focal length and depth of field
  • Global and per-object settings
  • Bakes optimization into keyframes
