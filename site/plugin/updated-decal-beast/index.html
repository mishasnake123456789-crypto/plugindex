Blender add-on for placing text and graphic decals on meshes, with TTF and OTF font loading in version 2.

What Updated! Decal Beast does

Bala's Decal Beast applies text and artwork decals to meshes in Blender. Version 2 can load any TTF or OTF font from your computer or the web, in addition to the bundled PNG fonts.

The library holds more than 25 graffiti PNG fonts, over 250 hand-drawn scribbles and 22 background shapes. Decals drag onto any mesh, including ones with multiple materials, and a layer system moves them forward or back, with arc and curve effects and custom spacing. Good for signs, graffiti and labels.

Key features

  • Load external TTF and OTF fonts
  • 25+ graffiti PNG fonts
  • 250+ scribble decals and 22 backgrounds
  • Drag-and-drop on multi-material meshes
  • Layering with arc and curve effects
