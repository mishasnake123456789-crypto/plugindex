What Simple Neon - Generator For Blender 4.0+ does

Simple Neon is a Geometry Nodes based tool for creating neon signs in Blender. A few clicks produce a glowing sign that you can customize to fit your scene.

It works on Blender 4.0 and 4.1 and is delivered as a Blender file you can open and use right away. A quickstart guide and documentation are included, and it was made with Cycles in mind. A quick way to add signage to city and bar scenes.
