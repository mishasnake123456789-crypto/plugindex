What Neuro3D Lab does

Neuro3D Lab, by Francisco-Angulo-de-Lafuente, sets up a virtual optical bench in Blender for working with diffractive optical neural networks. It is listed under 3D View and Import-Export.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
