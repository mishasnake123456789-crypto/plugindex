What Wirebomb - Professional, Fast And Easy Wireframing does

Wirebomb prepares wireframe renders with two methods: the Freestyle renderer or the Wireframe modifier. Both EEVEE and Cycles are fully supported, as is Blender's internal engine.

It is compatible with Blender 2.83, includes documentation and is GPL-licensed. It suits architectural visualization, technical presentations and stylized wireframe-over-solid looks. Among its capabilities are freestyle or modifier wireframes, EEVEE and Cycles support and blender internal engine support. Also on board: documentation included. It is meant for artists who want to work faster inside Blender.

Key features
