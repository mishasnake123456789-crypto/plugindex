What Essential Typography does

Essential Typography is a set of animated titles for After Effects and Premiere Pro made by Mister Horse. Premiere Pro users apply them via the free Premiere Composer plugin, which places each title in the sequence as a MOGRT.

Compatibility is listed for After Effects 2021 and 2022 and for Premiere Pro 2020 through 2022. A demo video is available. Editors wanting polished typography without designing animations can quickly add and edit titles.
