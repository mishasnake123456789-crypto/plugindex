A Blender kit that makes a character playable with third-person controls, a follow camera and Mixamo animations.

What Third Person Kit does

Third Person Kit turns Blender into a small playable testing ground. Drop in a character, press play, and you can walk, sprint, crouch, jump and idle, with room for one more action of your choice such as interacting.

Animations come from your own clips or from Mixamo, assigned in action slots with adjustable blend times, offsets, speeds and gravity. An over-the-shoulder follow camera offers optional collision, scroll-wheel zoom and a Tab-key free look mode. Game prototypers and animators who want to test characters in motion without leaving Blender will find it a quick sandbox.

Key features

  • Walk, sprint, crouch, jump and idle controls
  • Own clips or Mixamo animations in action slots
  • Adjustable blend times, speeds and gravity
  • Follow camera with collision, zoom and free look
