Blender side-panel add-on that lists, tags, filters and previews scene cameras and binds them to timeline markers.

What Viewfinder - Camera Management Tool does

Viewfinder helps keep scenes with many cameras under control. A side panel lists every camera with options to view it, select it, frame it, remove it or open its settings.

Cameras can be switched quickly, tagged, filtered and bound to timeline markers so shots change automatically during playback. Layout and previs artists who build multi-shot scenes in a single file will find it simpler than clicking through the outliner.

Key features

  • Camera list with view, select, find and remove actions
  • Quick camera switching
  • Tagging and filtering
  • Binding cameras to timeline markers
