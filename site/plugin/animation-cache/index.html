What Animation Cache does

Bowstone Studios' Animation Cache holds animation data in memory for smooth playback, previewing motion at the rate you pick without recomputing physics or simulations every pass.

Target speeds are 12, 24, 30 or 60 FPS, and Play from Cache replays without recalculation. Smooth keyframe interpolation can turn stepped keys into fluid curves. Caching takes a single click. Animators with heavy rigs and simulations get faster feedback. This makes it practical to judge timing and spacing at the real playback speed before committing to a final render.

Key features

  • Memory cache for playback
  • 12, 24, 30 or 60 FPS playback
  • Play from Cache without recalculating
  • Smooth interpolation of stepped keys
  • Single-click caching
