What Dosch 3D: Car Details - SUV does

Dosch 3D: Car Details - SUV reproduces a sports utility vehicle together with its critical parts and systems, making it useful for technical visualizations, animations and illustrations in Cinema 4D.

The model is fully textured and can be scaled to your needs. Materials, textures and colors can be changed, and you can apply your own textures or labels. Individual elements are switched on or off and set from fully visible to invisible through transparency, and they can be animated singly or as a complete model, which is handy for exploded views and cutaway presentations.

Key features

  • Fully textured SUV with key parts and systems
  • Adjustable scaling
  • Editable materials, colors and labels
  • Transparency control per element
  • Individual or whole-model animation
