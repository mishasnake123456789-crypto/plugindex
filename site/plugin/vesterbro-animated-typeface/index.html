What Vesterbro - Animated Typeface does

Vesterbro is a serif typeface created jointly by Black[Foundry] and Animography. The animated edition comes in two weights, while a static version is sold in twelve, so you can set body text in the same family.

It works with After Effects 2020 through 2024. Ordering four or more Animography typefaces saves 25 percent, and a demo video is available. A fit for editorial, film and brand titles. Having both animated and static weights available helps keep headlines and supporting copy visually consistent.
