What AEJuice Low Poly World Landmarks does

AEJuice Low Poly World Landmarks supplies 45 simplified models of well-known monuments and buildings around the globe. The full set totals 735,522 polygons, light enough to be used in games, AR and VR as well as animation.

Files come in six formats, .c4d, .max, .fbx, .obj, .stl and .3ds, and no plug-ins are required. Cinema 4D scenes are render-ready with Physical Sky. Travel videos, educational animations and stylized map sequences can draw on the pack instead of building each landmark from scratch.

Key features

  • Six formats including .c4d, .fbx and .obj
