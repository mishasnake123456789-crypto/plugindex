Blender add-on that grows generated ivy on a mesh object starting from the 3D cursor.

What IvyGen does

IvyGen creates climbing ivy on objects in Blender. Growth starts at the 3D cursor and spreads over a chosen mesh, giving walls, ruins and props an overgrown look.

Its panel is in the sidebar's Create tab under Ivy Generator. Installation is by drag and drop or from disk, no special permissions are needed, and it works with Blender 4.2 LTS and newer under the GPL v2 or later. Environment artists can dress old buildings and scenery with vegetation in a few clicks.

Key features

  • Generates ivy on a mesh
  • Starts at the 3D cursor
  • Panel in Create tab
  • For Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
