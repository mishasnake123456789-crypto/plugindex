What Music Motion does

Music Motion is a Blender plugin that reads an audio file and turns it into animation without nodes or scripting. It is meant for motion designers, indie creators and musicians making audio-reactive 3D scenes.

Volume, frequency and timing drive an object's position, rotation or scale, on any chosen axes. Separate tracks such as vocals, bass and percussion can control different elements, with per-track strength and frequency-range targeting for sharp or wave-like movement. A sidebar panel previews the effect in real time, and the result can be combined with other animation, shaders and lighting before rendering.

Key features

  • Audio-driven position, rotation or scale
  • Multi-track control with per-track strength
  • Frequency-range targeting
  • Real-time preview in the sidebar panel
