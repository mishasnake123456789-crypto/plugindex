What Moments Lab does

Moments Lab connects Premiere Pro to a centralized, AI-enriched media library. Editors search with plain text and advanced filters, matching the Moments Lab Media Hub, and drag frame-accurate clips into a bin.

Results can be specific moments or whole files, with AI-generated summaries, sequences, soundbites and labels, plus face recognition and highlights. Files may be stored on-premise or in the cloud, and local rushes can be added to an event for analysis and indexing.

Key features

  • Plain text and filtered search
  • Search for moments or whole files
  • AI summaries, soundbites and labels
  • Frame-accurate clips into the bin
  • On-premise and cloud storage
