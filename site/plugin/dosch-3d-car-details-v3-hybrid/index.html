What DOSCH 3D: Car Details V3 - Hybrid does

A neutral, unbranded hybrid sedan forms the basis of this Dosch Design model. Its important parts and systems are included, and every surface is textured, ready for technical visualizations, animations and illustrations in Cinema 4D.

Scaling is freely adjustable. Materials, textures and colors can be altered, and custom textures or labels applied. Elements can be switched on or off, adjusted from fully visible to invisible and animated individually or collectively. Educators and automotive illustrators can use it to demonstrate how combustion and electric components combine.
