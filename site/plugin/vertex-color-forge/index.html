What Vertex Color Forge does

Vertex Color Forge, by YLIANG, gathers tools for working with vertex colors in Blender: editing them, previewing the result and packing data into them. It is listed under Mesh and Paint.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
