Creates and updates architectural profiles with the Sweep modifier in 3ds Max, with built-in and custom profile libraries.

What Sweep Profile does

Sweep Profile from KSTUDIO makes moldings and other architectural profiles with 3ds Max's Sweep modifier far less fiddly. Profiles are created or updated in one click, and their dimensions can be set on the fly in a chosen unit.

It includes ready-made profiles, 10 in the free edition and 37 in PRO, and you can add your own from the scene or from a folder, optionally including subfolders. Favorites and a user library are supported, PRO can use selected edges from Editable Poly, and German and Japanese interfaces are provided. Interior and archviz artists will find it handy.

Key features

  • Create and update profiles in one click
  • Set dimensions on the fly in any unit
  • 10 profiles free, 37 in PRO
  • Add profiles from the scene or a folder
  • German and Japanese localizations
