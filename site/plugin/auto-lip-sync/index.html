What Auto Lip-Sync does

Auto Lip-Sync from mamoworld tools creates a mouth that moves according to your voice recording in After Effects. The mouth follows speech automatically, which saves manual keyframing for dialogue.

Tutorials show basic usage on a talking tree and application to a moving object in a talking car example, and a page collects all of them. It runs on After Effects from CS4 through the 2026 release, and a demo video is available. Animators making characters speak will save considerable time.
