What Mp2B Realtime Ai Tracking In Blender does

MP2B brings live AI tracking into Blender. Camera input is processed with Google MediaPipe models and streamed in, turning hands, face or body pose into tracking data for animation, rigging and procedural work.

Three specialized models cover hands, face and body pose, used one at a time, with real-time feedback as you move. Motion designers and performers can drive scenes with their own movement. The feature set spans hand, face and body pose models and live camera input via MediaPipe.

Key features

  • Hand, face and body pose models
  • Live camera input via MediaPipe
  • Real-time feedback
  • Data for animation and procedural setups
