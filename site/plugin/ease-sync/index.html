What Ease Sync does

Ease Sync lets animators keep timing consistent across many keyframes. You choose a set of keyframes, press Link, and they share the same easing, whether within one layer, a composition or the entire project.

Because a control curve drives the others, editing that easing updates everything linked to it, while keyframes remain editable. A single property can follow different easing for its In and Out animations, extra sync can be layered over existing control easing, and each synced keyframe receives a label. The interface is simple and minimal.

Key features

  • One-click linking of easing
  • Sync within a layer, comp or whole project
  • Edit the control curve to update all linked keys
  • Separate easing for In and Out animations
  • Labels on synced keyframes
