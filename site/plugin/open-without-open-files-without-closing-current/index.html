What Open Without: Open Files Without Closing Current does

Open Without launches additional Blender files in their own instances while your current project stays open. You can keep several files active and switch between them instead of closing and reopening.

It integrates into the Blender interface and installs through Edit > Preferences > Add-ons. Supported versions include Blender 4.3, 4.2, 4.1, 4.0, 3.6 and 2.93, among others. It is handy when comparing or copying between projects. Day to day, that covers opens files in separate instances, keep the current project active and integrated into the interface.
