What Bone Mirror Rename does

Fenutus3D's Bone Mirror Rename spares riggers from naming every pair twice. Rename one bone and its counterpart follows, guided by left and right markers in the names.

It can pair bones whose names are otherwise identical and ignores position, scale, rotation and any other transform. Documentation is included. Anyone building symmetrical skeletons for characters will cut typing and naming mistakes. Keeping names consistent also helps other rigging and retargeting tools that depend on left and right conventions.

Key features

  • Auto-renames the mirrored bone
  • Uses left and right name indicators
  • Matches otherwise identical names
  • Independent of transforms
  • Documentation included
