What Composition Guides does

Composition Guides draws framing systems in the 3D viewport to help align subjects and camera angles. Options include Rule of Thirds, Golden Ratio, Fifths, Golden Spiral, Golden Triangle, Diagonals and Harmonic Armature.

Overlays update in real time, so you can judge composition while adjusting the view. Photographers and 3D artists framing shots will find it useful. Among its tools are real-time viewport overlays and rule of Thirds, Golden Ratio and Fifths.

Key features

  • Real-time viewport overlays
  • Rule of Thirds, Golden Ratio and Fifths
  • Golden Spiral, Triangle and Diagonals
  • Harmonic Armature
