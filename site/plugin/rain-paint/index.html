Procedural erosion add-on for Blender that weathers any model in seconds, with a paintable effect area.

What Rain Paint does

LowLvlLiving's Rain Paint erodes models quickly. Paint in the area you want, and a dynamic area of effect makes the erosion fast and precise; a one-click swap toggles between local and model-wide erosion.

The simulations are designed to compute faster, and the interface stays simple while leaving room for customizing the result. Documentation is included. Environment and prop artists can age rocks, walls and terrain without sculpting weathering by hand.

Key features

  • Procedural erosion in seconds
  • Paint-in area of effect
  • Switch between area and model-wide erosion
  • Faster-calculating simulations
  • Simple, customizable interface
