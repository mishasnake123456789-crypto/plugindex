What FontReplacer does

FontReplacer is an After Effects tool for replacing fonts project-wide, even on layers with mixed fonts, keyframed text and expressions. Stacked source-to-target rules can be reused across projects, so a studio can apply the same swaps repeatedly.

Fonts can be sourced from Adobe, Google Fonts, Fontsource and Fontshare, or from a folder of your own licensed files, and installed in one click. It rewrites font names inside expressions, detects and repairs typos, collects used font files into one folder, exports a CSV of fonts by comp and layer, and keeps a replacement history for handoff.

Key features

  • Project-wide font swapping including expressions
  • Sources: Adobe, Google Fonts, Fontsource, Fontshare
  • Typo detection and repair per row
  • Collects used font files and exports a CSV report
  • Full replacement history for handoff
