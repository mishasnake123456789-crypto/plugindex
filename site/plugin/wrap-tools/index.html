What Wrap Tools does

Wrap Tools helps you make shapes follow the surface of other shapes in Blender. It includes a surface follow add-on updated for Blender 2.8, and tackles jobs that are awkward by hand, like wrapping a rope around a log.

The same approach lets you place thousands of rocks across uneven terrain so each one sits on the ground. Documentation is included and it is released under the MIT license. Modelers and environment artists who need objects to conform to curved or irregular geometry will get a faster and more natural result than manual placement.

Key features

  • Conforms shapes to other surfaces
  • Surface follow add-on updated for Blender 2.8
  • Wraps ropes around objects
  • Places thousands of rocks over uneven terrain
  • MIT licensed
