What Scenerunna - The Ultimate Camera, Lighting, And Prop Suite does

SceneRunna groups camera work, lighting and props into a single Blender add-on, so a shot can be set up from one panel. It suits artists building cinematic scenes in Cycles or Eevee.

Six camera rigs are offered: Arc, Crane, POV, Dolly zoom, Straight Line and Standalone, plus drawn-path guidance, a focus-distance plane and 15 camera shakes. A switcher supports resolutions up to 4K. On the lighting side there are 15 main lights and 8 rim lights that combine into 120 presets, along with fill lights, reflectors, atmosphere, palettes and color management settings.

Key features

  • Six camera rigs and 15 camera shakes
  • Camera switcher with live feedback
  • 120 combinable lighting presets
  • Volumetric atmosphere and color settings
