What Facial Hair Toolkit does

Facial Hair Toolkit is a Blender addon by young that supplies ready-made hair assets that shrink-wrap onto characters. Assets cover hairstyles, hair elements, eyebrows, beards and eyelashes, and you can alter their density, length and shape.

It also creates hair cards and bakes hair textures, and a custom hair mesh can be distributed along hair curves next to generated cards. Hair presets can be used as they are or made yourself, and hairs can be combined and saved with one click. It helps game and real-time artists add believable facial hair.

Key features

  • Pre-made hairstyles, beards, eyebrows and eyelashes
  • Density, length and shape controls
  • Hair cards creation and texture baking
  • Custom hair mesh on hair curves
  • Presets and one-click save
