What Ultimate Animation Suite does

Ultimate Animation Suite collects automated animation tools in a UAS panel on the 3D Viewport sidebar. Everything revolves around a camera target empty.

Camera moves include Orbit and a 360 Spin that finish one or two revolutions, plus Dolly, Zoom, Crane and Tracking shots that stay pointed at the target, and a Handheld Shake. Rigging tools and physics integration are bundled too. Animators who need polished camera work without hand-keyframing can set up shots in minutes.

Key features

  • Camera Orbit and 360 Spin
  • Dolly, Zoom, Crane and Tracking shots
  • Handheld Shake
  • Rigging tools and physics integration
