What Kit Ops Parallax does

KIT OPS PARALLAX builds virtual rooms out of one plane in Blender. The interior appears three-dimensional from different angles in real time, avoiding the cost of modeling full rooms.

Over 60 Loft-style assets come in a KPACK, works with Cycles and Eevee, and is recommended alongside KIT OPS 2 PRO. Artists filling building windows and background interiors can add depth cheaply. The effect is a cheap alternative to modeling interior geometry, which keeps render times and scene weight low.

Key features

  • Virtual rooms from a single plane
  • 60+ Loft-style assets in a KPACK
  • Works in Cycles and Eevee
  • Parallax in real time
