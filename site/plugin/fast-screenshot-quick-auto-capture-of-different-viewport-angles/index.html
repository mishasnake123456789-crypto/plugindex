What Fast Screenshot - Quick Auto Capture Of Different Viewport Angles does

Documenting a model from every angle by repositioning the camera manually takes ages. Salman Naseem's Fast Screenshot handles it with one click, removing the need for camera setup and for hiding objects by hand.

It shoots Front, Back, Left, Right, Top and Bottom views using Wireframe, Solid or Material Preview display. Pick from HD, Full HD, 2K and 4K presets or enter a size, then adjust zoom for framing. Lights, cameras and armatures can be hidden, and batch processing produces many shots together, handy for portfolios and asset sheets.

Key features

  • Six camera views, three display modes
  • HD to 4K presets plus custom size
  • Zoom factor for framing
  • Hide lights, cameras and armatures
  • Batch screenshot processing
