Collects and relinks all assets used by a Premiere Pro timeline and its nested timelines in one click.

What Smart Collect does

Smart Collect gathers the media a particular timeline needs. Select a timeline and the extension copies to disk the assets used only there, including those in nested timelines, then saves a copy of the project and relinks its clips to the new files.

It can also shrink a project by deleting unused assets and timelines. It runs on Premiere Pro from CC 2015 through 2025, helpful for archiving and handoff.

Key features

  • One-click collection and relinking
  • Includes nested timelines
  • Saves a relinked project copy
  • Deletes unused assets and timelines
  • Premiere Pro CC 2015 to 2025
