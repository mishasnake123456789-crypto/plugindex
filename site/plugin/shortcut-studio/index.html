What Shortcut Studio does

Shortcut Studio is a library manager for After Effects that gathers the snippets artists reuse most: expressions, scripts and presets. Instead of hunting through folders and old projects, everything lives in one central place.

It is aimed at motion designers who rebuild the same setups again and again and want a faster way to reach them. The initial 1.0.0 release works with After Effects 2022 through 2026, and a demo video is available so you can see the panel in action before adopting it.

Key features

  • Single library for expressions, scripts and presets
  • Central location for reusable assets
  • Works with After Effects 2022 through 2026
