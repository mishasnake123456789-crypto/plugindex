What Carousel XPress does

Carousel XPress from Tilles Motion is a script that creates a carousel in After Effects with one click. It offers five arrangements: Linear, Circular 2D, Circular 3D, Exponential and Path.

A Randomize Offsets window varies placement, and an About/Settings dialog includes a close button for Macs where the standard one may not appear. It works with After Effects from CS6 through the 2026 release. Designers can use it for rotating galleries, product showcases and repeated-item layouts without positioning every layer manually.

Key features

  • Creates a carousel in one click
  • Linear, Circular 2D, Circular 3D, Exponential and Path types
  • Randomize Offsets window
  • Mac-friendly settings dialog
