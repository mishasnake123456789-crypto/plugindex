What Blocksurfaces does

BlockSurfaces is a Blender add-on made for the blockout stage of building characters. Characters and creatures are assembled from connected forms, so you can study proportions and silhouettes, try poses without a rig, and produce meshes that are ready to sculpt.

Forms can represent torsos, limbs, horns, tails, tentacles and armor-like pieces. BlockMuscles turn branches into forms that mimic stretching, contraction and anatomical structure. Forms can be moved, rotated, scaled, edited and sculpted straight from the branches, and objects can be positioned along curves to make horns, tentacles, tails or repeating details.

Key features

  • Connected-form building for creatures and characters
  • BlockMuscles for stretch and contraction
  • Edit forms directly from branches
  • Place objects along curves
  • Generates sculpt-ready meshes
