What You're Auto Keying does

You're Auto Keying addresses a classic animation mistake in Blender: forgetting that Auto Keying is on and recording unintended keyframes. The add-on makes the state obvious by adding clear indicators across the interface.

Indicators appear on the outliner, the keyframe playhead, the preview viewport, pose mode outlines and the preview window header. Colors are adjustable in the add-on, the Auto Keying button sits at the far right of the header, and a Fetch Color button captures your theme defaults to return to when Auto Keying is off. Animators and riggers will avoid accidental keys.

Key features

  • Indicators in outliner, playhead and viewport
  • Adjustable colors
  • Auto Keying button in the header
  • Fetch Color captures theme defaults
