What Fake Liquid Pro does

Fake Liquid Pro creates the appearance of a drink being consumed inside a container, with none of the cost of a real fluid simulation. It was designed to stay light enough for modest computers.

It avoids complex geometry nodes and shaders, works with any object and any material, and renders in both Cycles and Eevee. That makes it practical for product shots, ads and quick scenes where the liquid only needs to look right. Documentation is included.

Key features

  • Drinking-liquid illusion with no simulation
  • Works with any object and material
  • No complex nodes or shaders needed
  • Compatible with Cycles and Eevee
