Blender add-on combining an Armature Picker panel with a Solo Pie menu for toggling bone collection solo states.

What Armature Pie does

Armature Pie speeds up work with rigs in Blender. ArmaturePicker helps you select one armature among many objects, found at View3D, Sidebar, ArmPie.

Filtering settings can be recorded to a preset and reused, other armature objects can be hidden when you pick one, and a Solo Pie toggles bone collection solo states using a pie menu. Animators in scenes with multiple characters can find and isolate rigs fast. Isolating collections with solo toggles makes it easier to focus on one part of a rig during animation or cleanup.

Key features

  • ArmaturePicker panel
  • Filter presets
  • Hide other armatures on selection
  • Solo Pie for bone collections
