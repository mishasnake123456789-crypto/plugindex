What Easy Sky does

Easy Sky makes working with HDRI lighting less fiddly. A plus button creates a preset and saves your custom setup for reuse.

A Load HDRI button browses your drive and assigns the chosen file to the World, and you can flip between presets to compare moods and lighting conditions. Rotation, strength and other settings are adjusted in a panel under World Settings. Lookdev and product artists testing many environments save time.

Key features

  • One-click HDRI preset creation
  • Load HDRI from disk to World
  • Instant preset switching
  • Rotation and strength controls
