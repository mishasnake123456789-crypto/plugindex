What Animation+ does

Anyone who spends hours in Blender's animation editors knows the interface can feel scattered. Animation+, by the Nyetei Team, concentrates on UI improvements that make that work less awkward.

It plugs into the Blender extension system and covers versions 4.5 to 5.0. A GPL license applies, documentation is provided and a demo video shows it in use. The benefit is incremental rather than dramatic, but animators in long sessions may appreciate the smoother layout.
