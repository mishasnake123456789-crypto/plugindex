What Startup Preset Manager does

Startup Preset Manager, by Gruff-Wright, lets you create your own startup files for Blender and reload them without restarting. It is tagged under Pipeline, Scene and System.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.
