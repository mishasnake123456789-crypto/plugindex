3ds Max MAXScript that applies the Match Camera to View behavior to lights so they take on the current viewport.

What MatchLightToView does

MatchLightToView by Martin Breidt extends a built-in feature of 3ds Max. The Match Camera to View command sets a camera to the current viewport, and this tool does the same for light objects.

Positioning a spotlight by lining it up with your viewport is far more intuitive than moving and rotating it with gizmos. The tool is a free MAXScript, works with 3ds Max 4, 5 and 6, and is version 1 from Martin Breidt's scripts page. Lighting artists can place lights where they can see the effect.
