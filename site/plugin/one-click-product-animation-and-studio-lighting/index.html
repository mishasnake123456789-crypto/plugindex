What One-Click Product Animation & Studio Lighting does

This add-on combines quick animation tools and a built-in three-point lighting rig for product visualization in Blender. It targets motion graphics and fast render workflows where setup time matters.

Effects include Bounce, Spin, Scale, Pulse, Shake, Slide, Fade, Pop In, Wiggle Rotation and Camera Shake. The lighting is a Key, Fill and Rim arrangement, and the camera setup offers auto-follow, follow path and front, side and top presets. A backdrop with a shadow plane is available, along with Product, Cinema and Beauty presets.

Key features

  • Ten animation effects such as Bounce, Spin and Pop In
  • Key, Fill and Rim studio lighting
  • Automatic camera follow and path options
  • Backdrop and shadow plane
  • Product, Cinema and Beauty presets
