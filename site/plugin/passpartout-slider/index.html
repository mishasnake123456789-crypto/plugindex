What Passpartout Slider does

Passpartout Slider by Costa lets you change a camera's passpartout opacity quickly in Blender. Hold P to adjust the alpha percentage and tap P to toggle it on or off.

It supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer, needs no special permissions and is licensed under the GNU General Public License. Artists composing shots can dim the area outside the frame without opening camera properties. Quickly tweaking this overlay makes framing decisions easier, since a darker surround helps you judge composition at the edges of the camera frame.

Key features

  • Hold P to change passpartout alpha
  • Tap P to toggle on or off
