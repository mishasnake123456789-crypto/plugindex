What timeinpixels Nobe OmniScope does

Nobe OmniScope brings broadcast-style monitoring tools to After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. It offers 19 types of scopes and accepts several inputs, so colourists and editors can check signal levels without hardware.

Quality control is covered by a QC Timeline and an Error Logger with CSV export. Colour management runs through OpenColorIO 2.3 with SDR, HDR PQ, HDR HLG and ST2084 presets. Control surfaces like StreamDeck and NDI 6 streaming are supported, Apple Silicon machines get GPU-accelerated real-time monitoring, and a screen capture source can scope anything on the desktop. It supports 12-bit signals and Dolby Vision workflows.

Key features

  • 19 scope types with multiple inputs
  • QC Timeline and Error Logger with CSV export
  • OpenColorIO with SDR and HDR presets
  • StreamDeck and NDI 6 support
  • Screen capture source; 12-bit and Dolby Vision
