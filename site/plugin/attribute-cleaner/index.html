What Attribute Cleaner does

Attribute Cleaner, by YLIANG, finds the attributes stored on meshes in a scene, selects the objects that carry them and removes unwanted ones in batches. It helps clear out leftover data from imported or procedural meshes. It is listed under Mesh.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Scans attributes across objects
  • Selects objects that use an attribute
  • Deletes attributes in batches
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later
  • Requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer
