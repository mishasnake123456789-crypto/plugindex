What Cheat Sheet does

Cheat Sheet is a Blender plugin that keeps your prompts close while you work. This third version allows you to record your own, so reminders match your projects.

A timer of chosen length notifies you visually and audibly when it is time for a break, and hovering over long text reveals more. A simple companion for long sessions. Having prompts beside your work can help with consistency, whether you use them for tasks, steps or reminders, and the timer encourages healthier habits.

Key features

  • Reference prompts always accessible
  • Record your own prompts
  • Break timer with visual and audible alerts
  • Hover to read long text
