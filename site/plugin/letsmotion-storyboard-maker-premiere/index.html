What Letsmotion Storyboard Maker Premiere does

Letsmotion Storyboard Maker is a Premiere Pro extension for planning shots without moving to another program. You can draw with a choice of brush colors and shapes, or import photos and edit them with filters, masks, cropping and scaling.

Every scene or camera setup holds an image, description, shot type, shot size, subject, frame rate, lens, duration, camera movements and sounds. When the board is ready, it can be applied back into Premiere Pro or exported in several formats for sharing. Directors, editors and previs teams can sketch ideas and keep production notes alongside the edit.

Key features

  • Draw in Premiere Pro with brushes and shapes
  • Per-shot camera, lens, size and duration fields
  • Photo import with filters, masks and cropping
  • Apply storyboards back into Premiere Pro
  • Export to shareable formats
