Blender rigging add-on that drives several bones from one controller without constraints.

What Proxy Control does

Proxy Control lets one master bone move many others in Blender while the rig hierarchy stays intact. It suits character and prop rigs, and creators who deliver or sell rigged assets.

A group of bones moves as a unit with per-bone influence, creating smooth curved motion for tails, tentacles and wires. Movement can be limited per axis, rigs can be organized with groups and folders, and _L and _R symmetry is generated automatically. Everything works in real time, and setups can be exported to a standalone script for sharing.

Key features

  • One master bone controls many bones
  • Per-bone influence and axis restriction
  • Groups, folders and automatic _L/_R symmetry
  • Export to a standalone script
