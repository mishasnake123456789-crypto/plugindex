What SUGARfx Revolve 360 does

SUGARfx Revolve 360 lets editors control which part of a 360 degree panorama the viewer sees. It works with equirectangular video or high-resolution images in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro, via FxFactory.

Framing can be keyframed, auto-animated or set with advanced timeline animation, using a built-in timeline with simple values and on-screen guides. Presets can be created and shared between hosts, projection options include flip and flop adjustments, and a built-in alpha matte supports transitions. Editors delivering flat video from 360 footage get precise control over the viewing angle.

Key features

  • Control framing of 360 equirectangular footage
  • Keyframe, Auto-Animation and timeline animation
  • Presets shareable between hosts
  • Built-in alpha matte for transitions
  • Flip and flop projection options
