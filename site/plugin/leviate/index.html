What Leviate does

Leviate, by vladpereverzyev, lets you control the Blender viewport or move objects through hand tracking, with input from a webcam or phone. It is listed under 3D View and User Interface.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Move the 3D view with your hands
  • Move objects by gesture
  • Webcam or phone input
