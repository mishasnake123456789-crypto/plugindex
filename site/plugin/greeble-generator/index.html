What Greeble Generator does

Greeble Generator is a Blender add-on for adding mechanical surface detail to sci-fi models. It scatters chosen greeble pieces randomly over a mesh, producing hundreds of shapes in seconds.

Each part aligns to its face normal without manual rotation and is parented to the target mesh, so moving the original carries the detail along. Scale can be uniform or per axis, offsets from the surface are adjustable, and the first material slot of the greeble can be copied to the target. It suits starship exteriors, interiors and rapid concepting.

Key features

  • Random scattering of parts over surfaces
  • Auto-alignment to face normals
  • Auto-parenting to the target mesh
  • Scale, offset and material copy options
