What Substyle does

Substyle generates animated subtitles right inside Premiere Pro. Editors can change individual words and their timing, which helps correct transcription and fine-tune the pacing of the captions.

Colors can be changed and several popular subtitle styles are offered. It needs Premiere Pro 25.0 or later, and suits social videos where eye-catching captions are expected. This is practical for social videos and shorts, where punchy captions that follow the speech are expected.

Key features

  • Animated subtitles inside Premiere Pro
  • Word-level text and timing edits
  • Color changes
  • Several popular caption styles
  • Premiere Pro 25.0 or later
