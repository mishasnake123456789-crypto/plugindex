What Blender Grid Units Addon does

Blender Grid Units Addon puts units on the grid letting you read sizes immediately rather than counting lines. Labels appear on both perspective and orthographic grids.

Several font themes are available, overlay settings adjust the display, and you can supply your own texture atlas for the labels. Useful for scale-conscious modeling and layout. Reading dimensions directly from the grid reduces guesswork when modeling to scale or lining up objects. It is a small aid for modelers who work to scale.

Key features

  • Unit labels on perspective and ortho grids
  • Font themes
  • Overlay settings
  • Custom texture atlas support
