Creates, edits, manages and bakes custom pivots for Blender objects and pose bones.

What Pivot Pro does

Pivot Pro lets you put a pivot exactly where the action happens, for objects and pose bones. Work stays non-destructive, so pivots remain editable, or they can be baked into the animation.

Pivots can be placed at contact points, the direction of a motion path can change during animation, and hand contacts and object interactions are handled. Animators gain flexible control over rotation points. Key capabilities include custom pivots for objects and pose bones and precise contact point placement.

Key features

  • Custom pivots for objects and pose bones
  • Precise contact point placement
  • Change motion path direction mid-animation
  • Edit or bake into animation
