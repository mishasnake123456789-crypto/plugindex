What K-Tools: Sync does

K-Tools: Sync keeps several 3D viewports in Blender looking at the same thing. One view acts as Master and others follow its navigation, which helps when reviewing a scene from several angles.

Real-time Sync updates at a configurable refresh rate, while Single Match syncs them just once. A Sync Options subpanel chooses what is shared, such as location, rotation and distance, per-viewport toggles are available, rotation can be locked and shading presets are included. Handy for modelers and reviewers.

Key features

  • Real-time sync from a Master viewport
  • Single Match one-time sync
  • Choose shared location, rotation and distance
  • Rotation locking and shading presets
