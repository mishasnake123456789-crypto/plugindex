What AEJuice Pixelate does

AEJuice Pixelate is a script for After Effects that turns selected layers into chunky 8-bit visuals with a single click. A strength control lets you decide how coarse the pixels should be.

It comes with kBar integration and supports After Effects CC 2015 through CC 2022 and newer. Retro-game graphics, censor blocks and glitchy transitions are easy to create, with no need to build a mosaic setup by hand.

Key features

  • 8-bit effect in one click
  • Adjustable strength
  • Applies to selected layers
  • kBar integration
  • Compatible with CC 2015 and newer
