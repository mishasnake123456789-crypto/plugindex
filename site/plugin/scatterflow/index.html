What Scatterflow does

Apex Assets' Scatterflow scatters objects using physics, helping with scene and asset building. Typical uses are foliage and other natural elements, plus rocks, rubble, trash, debris and decals.

Cycles and Eevee are both supported, along with Blender 4.2 to 5.0 as an extension-compatible add-on, and documentation is provided. Environment artists get items that settle naturally on surfaces under physics rather than floating or intersecting.

Key features

  • Physics-based scattering
  • Foliage, rocks, rubble, trash and debris
  • Decal scattering
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
  • Supports Blender 4.2 to 5.0
