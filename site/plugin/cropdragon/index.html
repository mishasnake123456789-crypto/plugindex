What Cropdragon does

Cropdragon, by 884tak, trims clips to the edges of their transparency. It applies the Crop effect along the alpha channel automatically, a job otherwise done by eye.

Support for Motion Crop arrived in version 1.1.0, and the current 1.1.1 was updated in March 2026. It works with Premiere Pro 2022 through 2026. It trims away the manual adjustment normally needed for cropping clips with transparent areas. It is a small timesaver for compositing graphics and cutouts with transparent edges.

Key features

  • Automatic Crop along the alpha channel
  • Motion Crop support from 1.1.0
  • Extension for Premiere Pro
  • Supports 2022 through 2026
  • Version 1.1.1, March 2026
