What NewBlue TotalFX Suite - Perpetual does

NewBlue TotalFX Suite - Perpetual is a broad post-production collection for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. It bundles 177 video plugins and over 1,500 presets, and also supports Avid Media Composer.

Titler Pro makes 3D-animated titles and lower thirds, integrating with Photoshop and After Effects. Stabilizer analyses motion with region of interest, strength and motion controls. Chroma Key Pro isolates green screen with sensitivity, range and spill settings, ColorFast corrects colour and balances shots, and 69 transitions with 861 presets span 3D, light, colour and motion styles. A further 27 colour filters include Bleach Bypass and Light Leak. Editors get one-stop coverage.
