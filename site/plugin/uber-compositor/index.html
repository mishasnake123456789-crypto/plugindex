What Uber Compositor does

Uber Compositor adds common post-processing effects to Blender renders: vignette, lens dirt, film grain, glare, bloom, exposure and color temperature.

Bloom and glare are resolution independent, scaled to the image instead of fixed pixel values. Artists wanting a finished look straight from Blender can avoid building compositor node trees by hand. Packaging these finishing touches in a single add-on makes it simple to apply a consistent photographic look to still frames and animation without wiring a complicated compositor graph.

Key features

  • Vignette, lens dirt and film grain
  • Glare and bloom
  • Exposure and color temperature
  • Resolution-independent bloom and glare
