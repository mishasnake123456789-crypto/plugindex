What SUGARfx MagiMoto does

SUGARfx MagiMoto, delivered through FxFactory, animates elements on screen without keyframes in After Effects and Premiere Pro. It can throw elements across the frame, spin them, scale them, and apply a subtle wiggle in chosen directions.

Single moves or combined moves and spins can be built in real time, with on-screen information showing how elements synchronize. Slide shows can be generated from stills or movies in a folder, and setups save as presets to share with colleagues. Editors get quick motion for transitions and slide shows.

Key features

  • Throw, spin, scale and wiggle without keyframes
  • Combined moves built in real time
  • On-screen synchronization feedback
  • Slide shows from folders of stills or movies
  • Savable and shareable presets
