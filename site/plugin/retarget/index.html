Blender add-on to move animation between rigs, convert characters to Rigify and organize actions, with common presets.

What Retarget does

Retarget from KBS-DEV moves animation from one rig to another in Blender. It includes presets for Mixamo, Unreal, Vroid, MMD, Daz and Auto Rig Pro, and you can add your own from the N-panel.

It can also convert characters to Rigify through its Bind to Active Metarig and Extract Metarig commands, which needs the Rigify add-on, and it helps manage actions. Animators reusing motion library clips on different characters can avoid re-keying.

Key features

  • Built-in rigs: Mixamo, Unreal, Vroid, MMD, Daz, Auto Rig Pro
  • Custom presets from the N-panel
  • Rigify conversion via metarig tools
  • Action management
