What Hyper Bar: Render Time Progress Bar & Dashboard does

Hyper Bar is a render monitoring add-on for Blender. During animation rendering it shows a progress bar along with estimates of how long remains and when the job should finish.

The dashboard also tracks elapsed time, frame count and average frame time, and keeps a recent render history. It works with Cycles and Eevee and is compatible with Blender extensions for versions 4.2 through 5.2. Anyone queuing long renders will find it easier to plan their time.

Key features

  • Progress bar with ETA and finish time
  • Elapsed time and average frame time
  • Frame count during renders
  • Recent render history
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
