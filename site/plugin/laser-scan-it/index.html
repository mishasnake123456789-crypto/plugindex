A Blender add-on that applies a procedural laser scan effect to the active material for sci-fi assets and holograms.

What Laser Scan It does

Laser Scan It injects a procedural scanning-laser shader into a material in Blender with one click. It builds the effect in the node editor, so it suits sci-fi props, holograms and scanning visuals without manual shader work.

The scan line can be sent along the X, Y or Z axis, or rotated around any axis by a user-defined angle for diagonal or radial sweeps. The laser color is chosen from the panel, and distortion with procedural noise and a strength slider adds flicker. Motion designers and VFX artists creating futuristic interfaces or product reveals will find it quick.

Key features

  • One-click laser scan node system
  • X, Y or Z axis scan line
  • Color picker for the beam
  • Distortion and noise for flicker
  • Rotation around any axis
