What Smartvideo does

Smartvideo is a small free extension from Adobe Exchange that brings markers created in the Smartvideo service into Premiere Pro, so annotations made there appear on your timeline. It's compatible with After Effects as well.

It needs Premiere Pro 14.0 or later, or After Effects 14.0 or later. The current release is version 1.0.3, updated in January 2023.

Key features

  • Imports Smartvideo markers into Premiere Pro

  • After Effects 14.0 and later
