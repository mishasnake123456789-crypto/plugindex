What MeltFlow Blur does

MeltFlow Blur comes from grape design and runs on the GPU inside After Effects. It smears an image outward from a chosen point, producing a melting, liquid look rather than a plain blur.

The affected area is centered on a point and can be adjusted through aspect ratio and rotation. Five turbulence maps break up the smear, turbulence itself can be animated, and grain noise adds texture. A wind setting steers the blur direction. Motion artists can use it for transitions, title treatments or stylized backgrounds that need a fluid distortion.

Key features

  • Blur centered on a chosen point
  • Five turbulence maps with animation
  • Grain noise control
  • Wind-driven blur direction
  • GPU accelerated
