3ds Max camera animation tool with eight cinematic moves driven by real-world speed or duration for Corona, V-Ray and standard cameras.

What CineMove does

CineMove animates cameras in 3ds Max using real-world numbers. Eight moves are included, among them Orbit variants of Pan and Tilt, plus Dolly, Truck, Pedestal, Zoom, Pan and Tilt, for CoronaCam, V-Ray Physical, Free and Target cameras.

Speed can be entered in kilometers per hour or degrees per second, or you can set a duration, and the script handles the math. Keyframes are created automatically with midpoint interpolation, and the logic is aware of targets. Orbit modes generate their own motion path splines, and movement can be centered or directional, starting or ending at the current position. It speeds up architectural walkthroughs and property videos.

Key features

  • Eight moves from Dolly to Tilt Orbit
  • Speed given in km/h, degrees per second or as a duration
  • Supports CoronaCam, V-Ray Physical, Free and Target cameras
  • Automatic keyframes with midpoint interpolation
  • Motion path spline generation for orbit modes
