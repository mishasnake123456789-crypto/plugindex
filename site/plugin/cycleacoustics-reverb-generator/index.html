What Cycleacoustics Reverb Generator does

Cycleacoustics Reverb Generator uses the Cycles render engine to follow the paths sound can take through a modeled space, then produces a reverb impulse response. It serves artists, music producers and filmmakers who want realistic room acoustics from a 3D scene.

Scenes use CycleAcoustics materials, and timing follows a changeable speed of sound so scale-accurate models give correct travel times. Output is stereo. The full edition adds psychoacoustic processing, realtime dynamic reverb, reverb on other materials, and wave interference and diffraction. Results can be saved as 32-bit RGBA EXR files.

Key features

  • Stereo impulse responses from 3D scenes
  • Adjustable speed of sound
  • Psychoacoustic processing in the full edition
  • EXR output for later impulse generation
