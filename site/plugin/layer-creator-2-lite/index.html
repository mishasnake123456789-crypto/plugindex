What Layer Creator 2 Lite does

Layer Creator 2 Lite is a Blender texturing add-on that works with image-based layers, handled in its own panel. Layers can be rearranged, reordered, deleted and saved.

You can blend layers using custom images or procedurally through built-in node settings, recolor them, and use procedural textures as textures or masks. It offers a layer-style approach to texturing familiar to painters, without leaving Blender. Among its tools are image-based layers to reorder, delete and save and blending by image or procedural settings.

Key features

  • Image-based layers to reorder, delete and save
  • Blending by image or procedural settings
  • Recoloring of layers
  • Procedural textures as textures or masks
