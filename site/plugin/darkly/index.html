What Darkly does

Darkly is a Blender add-on that sends what you see in the viewport, or through a chosen camera, into a Darkly layer. It acts as a bridge between Blender and that external tool.

This lets you keep working in 3D while the live image feeds into another application, without rendering and exporting stills each time. It is aimed at people who already use Darkly alongside Blender and want a tighter link between the two.

Key features

  • Streams the 3D viewport
  • Can stream a camera view
  • Feeds a Darkly layer
  • Avoids manual render exports
