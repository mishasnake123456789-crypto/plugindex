Compositing add-on for Blender that stacks AOVs, view layers and media as layers, After Effects style.

What LayerComp does

LayerComp brings After Effects-style, layer-oriented compositing to Blender. Instead of one filter laid over the screen, results from AOVs, View Layers, media and more are stacked as layers.

It offers adjustment and matte layers, each with several masks to control, and more than ten feature nodes whose effect values can be driven by a layer. Layers or effects can be copied and pasted between two compositors, and custom setups can be saved, shared, loaded and exported. Motion designers familiar with After Effects will feel at home.

Key features

  • Layer stack from AOVs, View Layers and media
  • Adjustment and matte layers with masks
  • 10+ feature nodes controllable by a layer
  • Copy and paste between compositors
  • Save, share, load and export custom setups
