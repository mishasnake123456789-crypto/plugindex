What Yanobox Motype does

Yanobox Motype generates motion titles in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, along with Final Cut Pro X and Motion from one installation. Text is auto-animated by character, word or line, with 3D effects on selected portions.

Text Motion Painting creates light trails, curved long shadows and flowing text. Characters can be extruded along motion paths, with trail length, colour gradients and fractal noise adjustable. Native Mac text layout supports font, style, colour, kerning, alignment and RTF import, and text auto-fits rectangular selections. 250 presets and templates arrive as Titles and Generators, and custom presets can be saved. Video makers get striking titles quickly.

Key features

  • Auto-animated characters, words and lines
  • Light trails, long shadows and flowing text
  • Extrusion along motion paths
  • Auto-fit text with per-unit controls
  • 250 presets and templates
