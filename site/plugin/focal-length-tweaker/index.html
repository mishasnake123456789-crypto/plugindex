Blender add-on for changing viewport and camera focal length by dragging the mouse, with a precision mode.

What Focal Length Tweaker does

Focal Length Tweaker lets you adjust focal length in Blender through mouse movement rather than typing numbers. It affects both the viewport and cameras, and the drag can run horizontally or vertically.

Holding Shift gives finer control when the keymap does not already include it. Ctrl+Alt+MMB is the default shortcut, which can be changed in the add-on settings or main keymap, and a double click of it toggles between orthographic and perspective. It works with Blender 4.2 LTS and newer.

Key features

  • Drag to change focal length
  • Precision mode with Shift
  • Horizontal or vertical drag direction
  • Ctrl+Alt+MMB default, remappable
  • Double click toggles ortho and perspective
