What Save Selection does

Save Selection, by Blenderbob, adds a simple export option to Blender. Under File, Export you will find a Save Selection (.blend) entry that writes only the objects you have selected into their own blend file.

It is a tiny add-on, about 3.6 KB and written entirely in Python, with no special permissions needed. It works with Blender 4.2 LTS and newer and is released under GPL v3 or later, which makes it a convenient way to split parts of a scene out for reuse.

Key features

  • Exports only the selected objects
  • Adds an entry under File, Export
  • Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
  • Tiny pure-Python add-on
  • GPL v3 or later license
