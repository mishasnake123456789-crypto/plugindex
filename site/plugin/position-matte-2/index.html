What Position Matte 2 does

Position Matte 2 is a compositing tool for working with position passes from 3D renders. Instead of asking for many separate matte passes or rotoscoping, you generate mattes from the position data already in your render.

Footage, shape layers or precomps can serve as mattes or projections. Layer mode offers tiling and offset, there is 3D world rotation with full control over rotation order, and Apple Silicon and multi-frame rendering are supported. VFX compositors integrating CG will benefit most.

Key features

  • Mattes from 3D position passes
  • Footage, shapes or precomps as mattes or projections
  • Tiling and offset in layer mode
  • 3D world rotation with rotation order control
  • Apple Silicon and MFR support
