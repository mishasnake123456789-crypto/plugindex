What Physics Gen does

Physics Gen, from Ace Of 3D, speeds up simulation setup in Blender by assigning randomized starting values. One button press can rework smoke and fire domains, fluid domains or particle settings, giving each attempt its own character.

It can also add random force fields. You may use it for quick experiments or set everything up manually, since every property stays adjustable. The controls appear in the N panel under Physics and in the object properties panel.

Key features

  • Randomizes smoke and fire domains
  • Randomizes fluid domain settings
  • Randomizes particle settings
  • Adds random force fields
  • Manual adjustment of every property
