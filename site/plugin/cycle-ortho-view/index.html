What Cycle Ortho View does

Cycle Ortho View, by revorT, steps through Blender's orthographic views one after another, so you do not need separate shortcuts for each. It is categorised under Workflow.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 5.0.2 or newer.
