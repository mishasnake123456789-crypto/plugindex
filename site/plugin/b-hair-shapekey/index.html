What B Hair ShapeKey does

B Hair ShapeKey, by Dinesh007, lets hair curves use shape keys in a way that still works when you sculpt their deformation. It is tagged under Animation and Sculpt.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.

  • Shape keys for hair curves
