What Rowbyte Bad TV does

Rowbyte Bad TV recreates the artifacts of a worn television in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Rather than asking you to build displacement maps, it derives the distortion from its parameters.

Vertical Sync offset gives the jumpy roll of old sets, scan lines vary in thickness for a DV tape appearance, and Manual Time Control and Horizontal Sync Control allow fine adjustment. This listing also notes compatibility with floating licenses.

Key features

  • Distortion generated from parameters
  • Vertical Sync offset jump
  • Variable-thickness scan lines
  • Manual Time and Horizontal Sync control
  • Floating license compatible
