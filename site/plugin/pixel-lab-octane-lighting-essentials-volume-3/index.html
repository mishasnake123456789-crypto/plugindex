What Pixel Lab Octane Lighting Essentials Volume 3 does

Pixel Lab Octane Lighting Essentials Volume 3 adds 10 studio scenes for Cinema 4D with Octane, each controlled by a full Xpresso rig for lights and colors.

Some scenes are quite specialized: Rim Studio uses included IES lights, Light Painting provides a customizable softbox, Endless Road is a seamless loopable animation with day or sunset options, and a Dance scene accepts a custom soundtrack. More studios are planned as free additions until the collection reaches 20. Octane users can build on these when lighting products or stylized animated scenes.

Key features

  • 10 customizable studio lighting scenes
  • Xpresso rig for lights and colors
  • Rim Studio with included IES lights
  • Endless Road loopable day or sunset animation
  • Free studios planned up to 20 total
