What Modifier Tools does

Modifier Tools provides quick commands for working with several modifiers together in Blender. It was among the add-ons bundled with Blender 4.1 and is now offered as it stands.

The actions are found in the Properties editor under Modifiers, grouped as modifier specials: show, hide and apply for the selected modifiers. The add-on works with Blender 4.3 and newer, is only 2.7 KB, needs no special permissions and is released under GPL v3 or later. Artists with long modifier stacks can toggle or finalize them in fewer clicks.

Key features

  • Toggle visibility of or finalize chosen modifiers
  • Located in Properties > Modifiers
  • Tiny 2.7 KB download
  • For Blender 4.3 and newer
