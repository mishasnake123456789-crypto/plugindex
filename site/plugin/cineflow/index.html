Lets you pilot a Blender camera live with WASD and the mouse, then turns the flight into smooth keyframes.

What Cineflow does

Cineflow gives camera animation the feel of piloting a drone. Fly the camera with WASD and the mouse, similar to a gimbal, and your performance is recorded while the animation plays.

When the timeline ends or you press Esc, smooth keyframes are generated. Gaussian smoothing, on-screen path overlays and snapshot-style editing help polish the take, and anchor tracking is also offered. It lets animators capture natural, flowing moves instead of placing keys one by one.

Key features

  • Real-time WASD and mouse camera control
  • Records motion into keyframes
  • Built-in Gaussian smoothing
  • Visual path overlays
  • Snapshot-based editing
