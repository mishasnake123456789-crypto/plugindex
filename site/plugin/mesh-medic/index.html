Diagnoses and repairs duplicate vertices, Ngons, loose geometry and flipped normals from the Blender N-panel.

What Mesh Medic does

Mesh Medic checks meshes for common problems and fixes them. A one-click diagnosis can cover one or many meshes and returns a combined report of duplicate vertices and faces, Ngons, zero-area faces, loose vertices and edges, unapplied scale and rotation, and flipped normals.

Issues can be fixed individually or all at once. It suits modelers cleaning imported or sculpted meshes before export or further work. Highlights cover one-click multi-mesh diagnosis and detects Ngons, loose geometry and flipped normals.

Key features

  • One-click multi-mesh diagnosis
  • Detects Ngons, loose geometry and flipped normals
  • Fix individually or all at once
  • Combined report
