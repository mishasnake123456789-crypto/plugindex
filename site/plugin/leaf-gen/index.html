What Leaf Gen does

Leaf Gen is a Blender add-on for making foliage. It produces leaf geometry with tidy retopology and pairs it with a procedural shading setup, so you do not need to model and texture each leaf by hand.

Environment artists and anyone populating plants or trees can use it to generate believable leaves quickly. The procedural approach makes it easy to vary appearance across a whole plant, avoiding repeated-looking foliage.

Key features

  • Leaf mesh generation
  • Clean retopology
  • Procedural leaf shading
