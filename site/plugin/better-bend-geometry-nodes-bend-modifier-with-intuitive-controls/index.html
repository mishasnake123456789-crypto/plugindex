What Better Bend - Geometry Nodes Bend Modifier With Intuitive Controls does

Better Bend is a Geometry Nodes modifier for bending geometry with fewer surprises. Pick the axis your mesh runs along, drag the bend amount, and the shape bends.

Axis choices map to Blender's internal orientation automatically, and Bend Style presets cover Both Sides, Plus Side and Minus Side. Start and End limits use real units and update automatically or can be overridden, and a pivot and rotation sub-panel moves the bend center in object-local space. It suits modelers who find Simple Deform awkward.

Key features

  • Axis selection mapped automatically
  • Bend Style presets: both, plus or minus side
  • Real-unit Start and End limits
  • Pivot and rotation sub-panel in local space
  • Geometry Nodes modifier
