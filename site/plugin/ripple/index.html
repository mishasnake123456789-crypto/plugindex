What Ripple does

Ripple, by Tristan-Lyons, adds or removes time in a Blender timeline and carries keyframes, NLA content and markers along with the change. It is listed under Animation.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.
