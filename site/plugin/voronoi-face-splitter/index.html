What Voronoi Face Splitter does

Voronoi Face Splitter breaks a mesh into pieces without altering the original. It works either as a viewport Geometry Nodes tool or as a group node placed inside a node tree.

Cell density, seed and split offset are adjustable, and UV integrity and face orientation are maintained so the fragments remain usable in production. Useful for shattered glass, cracked walls and destruction prep ahead of simulation. Because the source mesh is preserved, you can change cell settings at any time and compare different break patterns.

Key features

  • Works as tool or group node
  • Non-destructive fragmentation
  • Controls for cell density, seed and offset
  • Keeps UVs and face orientation
