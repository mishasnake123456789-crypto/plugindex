Blender add-on that places text-based graffiti on walls and objects, with presets, tags and shrink-to-surface.

What Graffiti Decal Addon does

EdenWeby 3D's Graffiti Decal Addon dresses Blender street scenes with spray-paint style text. Choose a preset, aim at a surface and shrink the lettering onto it, then convert to mesh and parent.

The font file holds over forty presets and eighty-two extra tags. Shrink To Target has an adjustable distance, colors and materials can be changed, and a Parent button attaches the graffiti to its target. Environment artists dressing urban backdrops will find it quick.

Key features

  • Over 40 presets and 82 extra tags
  • Shrink To Target with adjustable distance
  • Convert text to mesh
  • Parent button for the target object
  • Adjustable colors and materials
