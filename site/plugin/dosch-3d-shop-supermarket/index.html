What Dosch 3D: Shop Supermarket does

Supermarket furniture is the subject of Dosch 3D: Shop Supermarket. The pack contains detailed, fully textured shelves, refrigerated display cases and produce containers, each accompanied by models of the goods that stock them.

The models are scalable, and their materials, textures and colors can be freely changed, including with your own labels. Elements can be disabled or made transparent and animated, whether individually or as a unit. Retail visualizers, packaging designers and advertisers can set up convincing shop scenes in Cinema 4D without modeling every shelf and product.

Key features

  • Shelves, chillers and produce containers
  • Stock item models included
  • Adjustable scale
  • Editable materials, textures and colors
  • Elements switchable, transparent and animatable
