What Chamfer Tube does

lantlant's Chamfer Tube is a 3ds Max plugin script that adds a ChamferTube object to the creation options. It works on 3ds Max 9 and later, and the current version is 1.3.

The listing offers little detail beyond that, but the name points to a tube primitive with chamfered edges, which suits modelers who want a quick pipe or ring shape without chamfering it manually. It is free and installs as a plugin script. Users running 3ds Max 9 or a newer release can add it to their toolset.
