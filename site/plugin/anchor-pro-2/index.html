What Anchor Pro 2 does

Anchor Pro 2 is a utility from Leyero for repositioning anchor points in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Moving an anchor point without shifting the layer is a chore in both programs, and this panel takes the fiddly math out of it.

In After Effects it understands separated dimensions and 3D layers, and it can aim at a particular Transform effect instead of the layer's own properties. You can drag the anchor point by hand for fine placement, and the revised Alpha Mode lets you set an opacity threshold and skip drop shadows when it measures a layer's edges. Motion designers and editors who place, save and reapply anchors all day will get the most from it.

Key features

  • Targets a specific Transform effect
  • Separated Dimensions and 3D layer support in After Effects
  • Drag the anchor point for precise placement
  • Alpha Mode with opacity threshold and drop shadow skipping
  • Transform and Vector Motion modes with click shortcuts
