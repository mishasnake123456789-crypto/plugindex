What Kuwahara+ DCTL Plugin does

Kuwahara+ is a DCTL plugin from Nx Color that turns DaVinci Resolve footage into something resembling a painting. It is built on the Kuwahara filter, a non-linear smoothing method that lowers noise and keeps edges crisp while flattening areas of texture.

Since edges remain defined, subjects stay readable even when the surfaces inside them look brushed and simplified. It runs in Resolve 19 and 18, comes with a perpetual license bought through a single payment, and includes free updates and immediate access after purchase. Editors and colorists wanting a stylized, illustrated treatment for music videos or social content can apply it directly in the node graph.

Key features

  • Painterly effect based on the Kuwahara filter
  • Smooths noise while preserving edges
  • Works in DaVinci Resolve 18 and 19
  • Perpetual license with free updates
