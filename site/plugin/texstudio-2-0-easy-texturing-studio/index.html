What Texstudio 2.0 - Easy Texturing Studio does

Texturing in the middle of a cluttered scene makes it hard to judge a material. CG Seb's TexStudio 2.0 separates the object from everything else and shows it in a studio of your choosing.

Variants of the full material set can be kept and flipped between, and swapping studios or light sets takes a few clicks, revealing how a texture reacts to illumination. You can make your own studios and sets, then reuse or share them; one by CG Thoughts is included. Perfect for lookdev and texture artists.

Key features

  • Isolates an object in a texturing studio
  • Save and switch material versions
  • Swap studios and light sets quickly
  • Create and share your own studios
  • Includes a studio set by CG Thoughts
