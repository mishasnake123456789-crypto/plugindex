What LutLens does

LutLens is a color grading panel for After Effects built around a large, organized look library. It offers 25 curated categories holding more than 600 LUTs, so grading options can be previewed quickly instead of hunting through folders.

A before/after slider remembers its position when you change preview images, and bookmarks can be exported and imported to share favorites. The LUTs are stored in the blut format to keep the extension smaller. Version 2.0.0 added the Euro Classic, Mystic Haze and Travelogue categories, and small in-panel toast messages report what is happening.

Key features

  • 25 categories with 600+ LUTs
  • Before and after preview slider
  • Bookmark export and import
  • Compact blut LUT format
  • In-panel toast notifications
