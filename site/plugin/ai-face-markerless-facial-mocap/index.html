What Ai Face-Markerless Facial Mocap does

Ai Face is a markerless facial motion capture tool for indie animators working in Blender. It records a performance with an ordinary webcam at 30 fps, with no depth sensor or iPhone needed, then maps the performance onto a character that has Apple ARKit shapekeys.

It animates up to 24 of the 52 ARKit shapekeys and tracks head motion, eyes and expressions. Previews are real time with live recording, data can stream directly to Blender or be recorded for later, and output can be fine-tuned before applying. Streaming to Unity3D is supported through a bundled unitypackage.

Key features

  • Webcam facial capture at 30 fps
  • Up to 24 of 52 ARKit shapekeys
  • Head, eye and expression tracking
  • Live preview, streaming and recording
  • Streaming to Unity3D
