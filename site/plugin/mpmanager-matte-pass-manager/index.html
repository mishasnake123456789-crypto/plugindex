What MPManager (Matte Pass Manager) does

Written by colinsenner, MPManager builds layer-based matte passes in 3ds Max. It also works with xrefs, so scenes assembled from external references can still produce their mattes.

It comes as two MaxScript files, MPManager and MPManager_Functions, and needs 3ds Max 2008 or newer. A demo video is available. Compositors and lighting artists who need object or layer masks for post-production can use it to generate them without setting up each matte by hand.

Key features

  • Creates matte passes by layers
  • Works with xref scenes
  • Supports 3ds Max 2008 and later
  • Delivered as MPManager and MPManager_Functions MaxScript files
  • Demo video available
