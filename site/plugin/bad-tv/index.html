What Bad TV does

Bad TV produces the wobble and breakup of a poor television signal in After Effects and Premiere Pro. The distortion is generated from its own parameters, so there is no need to prepare displacement maps.

A Vertical Sync offset recreates the jumping picture of old sets, and scan lines of varying thickness provide a DV tape look. Manual Time Control and Horizontal Sync Control complete the set. Version 2.1 brought a performance update to both hosts.

Key features

  • Distortion from parameters, no maps needed
  • Vertical Sync offset for jumpy picture
  • Varied scan lines for DV tape look
  • Manual Time and Horizontal Sync control
  • Performance update in 2.1
