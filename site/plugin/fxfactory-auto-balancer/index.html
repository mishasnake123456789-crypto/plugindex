What FxFactory Auto Balancer does

Auto Balancer from Noise Industries is a colour balancing plug-in delivered through FxFactory. It works in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, as well as Final Cut Pro X and Motion.

It adjusts white point, temperature and tint to remove unwanted colour casts or to imitate different lighting conditions. Automatic and manual modes are offered, AI-assisted reference point selection targets the balance, and secondary corrections cover vibrance, exposure and gamma. Editors cleaning up footage shot under mixed lighting can get a neutral base before grading.

Key features

  • White point, temperature and tint adjustments
  • Automatic and manual modes
  • AI-assisted reference point selection
  • Secondary vibrance, exposure and gamma
  • Removes casts or simulates lighting
