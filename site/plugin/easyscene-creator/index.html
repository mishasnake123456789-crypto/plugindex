What Easyscene Creator does

Easyscene Creator builds scenes for product visualization and 3D product design. It supports Cycles, Eevee and Luxrender.

More than 20 GOBO lights are included, while Auto Generate plus camera, keying and animation tools come in the higher tiers. All add-on integrations are included, with documentation under a GPL license. Marketers and designers presenting products can set up lit scenes quickly. Starting from a prepared setup also keeps lighting and camera work consistent across a whole product range.

Key features

  • Product scene generator

  • Cycles, Eevee and Luxrender support
