What AEJuice CRT Elements does

AEJuice CRT Elements is a collection of ready-made footage that recreates the feel of old cathode-ray tube screens. It spans digital noise, geometric shapes and glitch bursts, and works in After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and other editors that accept video clips.

The 104 clips are rendered in 4K as H.264 at 24 fps, on transparent or black backgrounds so they can be layered over any shot. Importing is handled through the AEJuice Pack Manager, which offers quick previews, drag and drop, and a favourites list. Editors who want a retro overlay without building it from scratch can simply drop a clip onto the timeline and blend it.

Key features

  • 104 CRT clips: digital noise, geometry objects and glitches
  • 4K footage, H.264 at 24 fps
  • Transparent or black backgrounds
  • Pack Manager with preview, drag and drop and favourites
