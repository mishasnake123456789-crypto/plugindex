What Dog Fight Generator does

Dog Fight Generator automates flight-path animation for dogfights in the Blender 3D Viewport.

Single Mode produces one maneuver, while Combo Mode chains up to five moves with automatic transition bridges and role swaps between attacker and defender. Ten standard combat moves are included, such as the Immelmann Turn, Split-S, Pugachev's Cobra, Barrel Roll, High and Low Yo-Yo and Wingover. Because it automates the flight paths, you can focus on camera work, lighting and effects instead of hand-keyframing complicated aerial maneuvers, which suits films, games and motion graphics.

Key features

  • Single and Combo generation modes
  • Chain up to five moves
  • Automatic transitions and role swaps
  • Ten standard combat maneuvers
  • Procedural flight paths
