What Ies Light Architect Pro does

IES Light Architect Pro is aimed at architectural visualization and interior rendering in Blender. From a dedicated panel you choose a light profile, add the setup to your scene, and adjust settings such as intensity, colour temperature and placement.

The goal is to cut down on repetitive manual lighting setup. It works within Blender's normal lighting and rendering pipeline and is used with Cycles and Eevee. Typical jobs include interior visualization, archviz scenes, product renders and staged environments.

Key features

  • IES-style lighting profile workflow
  • Adjustable intensity, colour temperature and placement
  • Adds lighting setups from a dedicated panel
  • Used with Cycles and Eevee
  • Reduces repetitive manual light setup
