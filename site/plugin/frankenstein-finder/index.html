A dockable After Effects panel for browsing project hierarchy, renaming layers and auditing nested comps.

What Frankenstein Finder does

Frankenstein Finder helps artists get their bearings in large, deeply nested After Effects projects. A searchable tree displays every layer across all precomps with type icons, and a double-click jumps right to any of them.

The panel also includes a live transform inspector, Smart Rename with numbering, prefixes or suffixes, bulk transform copying, and remote text editing across nested comps. Isolate Mode hides everything except the selection, and Show All restores it. A deeper audit helps clear out unused assets from messy files.

Key features

  • Searchable tree of layers across nested comps
  • Double-click to jump to any layer
  • Smart Rename with numbering, prefixes and suffixes
  • Isolate Mode with Show All restore
  • Remote text editing and project auditing
