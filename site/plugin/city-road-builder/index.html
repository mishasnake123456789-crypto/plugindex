What City Road Builder does

City Road Builder is a Blender add-on from EdenWeby 3D for laying roads into a scene without modeling them from scratch. You choose a road type, run the setup, and then lengthen it with a repeat value or bend it along a curve.

Roads can follow included curve presets or one you draw yourself, and they can be dressed with sidewalks, barriers, street lights, grass, trees and fencing. An overpass option converts a road in a single step, and a built-in material editor covers roughness, wetness, puddles and bump strength. Junction tools and multiple roads per scene are part of the Pro version.

Key features

  • One-click road setup with adjustable length
  • Curve roads from presets or your own curve
  • Sidewalks, barriers, lights, trees and fences
  • Overpass conversion and junction system (Pro)
  • Built-in material editor with wetness and puddles
