What Navigation Puck Addon does

Navigation Puck Addon adds a small navigation menu for the Blender viewport, modeled on the puck in Sketchbook Pro. Orbit, pan, zoom, camera flip and similar view actions are reachable from the current editor context.

Hold V, drag toward an action and let go; the key can be remapped. A Shortcut Button mode shows a small icon that fades in near the cursor, while Menu Near Cursor mode opens the whole puck there. Placement, sizes and drag threshold are adjustable. It runs on Blender 4.2 LTS and newer under GPL v3.0 or later.

Key features

  • Orbit, pan, zoom and camera flip puck menu
  • Hold V and drag to choose an action
  • Shortcut Button and Menu Near Cursor modes
  • Adjustable sizes and thresholds
  • Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
