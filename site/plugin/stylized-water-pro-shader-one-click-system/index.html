What Stylized Water Pro Shader - One-Click System does

Stylized Water Pro Shader creates a complete water setup for Blender in one click. It makes or reuses a water object, assigns a stylized shader material and adds Geometry Nodes wave layers.

Helper collections drive foam for Static_Objects, ripples for Ripple_Points and floating behavior for Floating_Objects. Built-in presets can be extended with your own, and sidebar controls cover waves, surface, foam, ripples, floating and volume. It suits lakes, ponds, rivers and game-style scenes.

Key features

  • One-click stylized water setup
  • Geometry Nodes animated wave layers
  • Foam, ripple and floating object workflows
  • Built-in and custom presets
