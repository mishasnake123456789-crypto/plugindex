What Faux Lock does

Faux Lock from Poly Artboard solves a common annoyance in After Effects: adjustment layers, guides and mattes that swallow clicks meant for the layers beneath. It makes selected layers click-through in the Comp viewport while still fully selectable and editable in the Timeline.

Switching is automatic: selecting a target in the Timeline frees it, and clicking the viewport locks it again. A multi-target panel can add, lock, hide, select or remove many layers at once. A native real-time engine tests the cursor live on macOS universal and Windows, and a quick toggle pauses everything.

Key features

  • Click-through layers in the Comp viewport
  • Automatic click-driven lock switching
  • Multi-target panel for many layers
  • Native real-time cursor hit-testing
  • Quick on/off toggle
