What Shademix does

ShadeMix is a node management interface for Blender's Shader Editor. It acts as a combined mixer and switcher for building complex material layers from a single place.

Colors, floats, normal maps and full shaders can be mixed, with node setups and data conversions handled automatically in the background. Material artists avoid wiring and converting nodes by hand. Key capabilities include all-in-one mixer and switcher and mixes colors, floats, normals and shaders.

Key features

  • All-in-one mixer and switcher
  • Mixes colors, floats, normals and shaders
  • Automatic node setup and conversions
