What OnlyBlends.Mixer does

OnlyBlends.Mixer by Zulhakar is a playful extension that sets out to make Blender behave like a mixer of sounds. It is tagged under Node and Pipeline.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
