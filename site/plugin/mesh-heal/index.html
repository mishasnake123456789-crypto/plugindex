Blender add-on that erases selected faces and reconstructs the missing surfaces, edges and corners in one click.

What Mesh Heal does

Mesh Heal is a companion for hard-surface modelers in Blender. Select faces to remove, press F8, and it erases them and rebuilds the edges and faces as they should have been.

Corner Heal handles corners, un-bevel flattens beveled edges, and Fold and Bridge options add control. Cut faces can be erased whole or individually, and holes can be removed with faces restored on both sides. Material-based auto selection works from the 3D cursor position, an optional Boost mode is available, and three tutorial scenes are included.

Key features

  • Heal reconstructs erased areas
  • Corner Heal and un-bevel
  • Fold and Bridge options
  • Auto selection by material
  • F8 activation with optional Boost mode
