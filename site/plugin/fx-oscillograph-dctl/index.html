What FX Oscillograph DCTL does

FX Oscillograph DCTL draws graphs from the picture's own values across the frame, in the manner of a modulation effect. It runs on the Edit, Color and Fusion pages, with Y graph modes for Luminance, Maximum or Minimum.

Quantity sets how many graph rows are drawn, and the Y graph is colored by channel averages, with Y and RGB graphs toggled independently. Vertical graphs show average values of pixel lines. Scopes on BG places a luminance or RGB waveform behind the graphs as an artistic touch, and Background either reveals the input image or fills the backdrop. Gain, hue and saturation adjust each element, and Precision balances analysis against real-time playback.

Key features

  • Luminance, Maximum and Minimum Y graph modes
  • Adjustable number of graph rows
  • Scopes on BG waveform layer
  • Background reveal or fill color control
  • Per-element gain, hue and saturation with Precision setting
