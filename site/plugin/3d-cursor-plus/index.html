Blender add-on that adds axes, a gizmo, undo and redo, and cursor-relative operators to the 3D cursor.

What 3D Cursor Plus does

3D Cursor Plus expands what Blender's 3D cursor can do. It adds visible axes and a gizmo, brings undo and redo support to cursor changes, and provides operators that work relative to the cursor's position and orientation.

Two gizmo and axes presets can be used alone or together and toggled from a shortcut, panel button or pie menu. Colors, sizes, per-area visibility and behavior are adjustable, and a preset can be linked to the cursor. Modelers who rely on precise placement and pivots get a more controllable cursor workflow.

Key features

  • Axes and gizmo presets for the cursor
  • Undo and redo for cursor changes
  • Toggle via shortcut, panel or pie menu
  • Adjustable colors, sizes and visibility
  • Cursor-relative operators
