What Let's Flow - Add Multiple Fluid Simulation In Seconds!!-No Setup, No Baking does

Let's Flow supplies fluid simulations that are already baked, so you can add liquid motion to a Blender scene without configuring domains or waiting on a bake. Assets can be scaled, rotated, moved and animated, and the start time of the flow is adjustable.

Several simulations may run in the same scene, flow speed can be altered and the fluid material is changeable. The assets are light enough for modest hardware. The fluid assets work in Cycles and Eevee, though the materials are for Cycles only, and 7-Zip is needed to extract the files. Artists who need quick liquid effects will find it convenient.

Key features

  • Prebaked fluid assets, no setup or baking
  • Scale, rotate, move and animate
  • Adjustable flow speed and fluid material
  • Several simulations in one scene
  • Lightweight for modest hardware
