What SDVI Rally Access - Legacy does

SDVI Rally Access - Legacy helps media teams verify and modify content in Premiere Pro. It combines content with metadata on the timeline to guide you to the right moment and tracks each item as it is checked.

Supported tasks include assisted QC and fix, compliance and distribution versioning, content segmentation, caption review and validation, and manual metadata logging. Projects and EDLs can be exported with cloud render. It is the older version of the Rally Access panel, intended for teams in media supply chains.

Key features

  • Assisted QC and fix
  • Compliance and distribution versioning
  • Content segmentation
  • Caption review and validation
  • Project and EDL export with cloud render
