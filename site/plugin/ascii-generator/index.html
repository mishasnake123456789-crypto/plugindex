What ASCII Generator does

ASCII Generator from Motion Boutique converts a text layer into retro-style ASCII art. Select at least one text layer, pick an ASCII font, and click Generate to produce the result.

It is compatible with After Effects from CS5 through 2025, and a demo video is available. Designers can use it to give titles and text elements a terminal or hacker aesthetic without building character grids manually.

Key features

  • Converts text layers to ASCII art
  • Choice of ASCII font
  • One-click Generate
  • Works with After Effects CS5-2025
  • Demo video available
