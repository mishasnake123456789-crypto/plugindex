What Easy Block Saver (50% Off) does

Easy Block Saver targets the rough blocking stage of modeling. It records the state of your rough work in a form similar to Blender's own primitive meshes, so you can bring it back whenever needed.

Saved shapes are available for reuse at any time. It is listed for Blender 3.6 to 4.5, works with the Blender-Internal, Cycles, Eevee and Freestyle engines, and comes with documentation under the GPL. Modelers who repeatedly start from similar base forms may save time.
