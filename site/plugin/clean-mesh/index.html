What Clean Mesh does

Clean Mesh, from Cobox, tidies geometry in Blender for characters, environments and other meshes. It deletes redundant vertices, edges and faces while keeping the details that matter, and corrects normals.

Separate Vertex, Edges and Face menus target problems like floating vertices, stray edges and open faces, and a Normals menu reorients or resizes normals. Full Analyze fixes everything in one click according to your preferences, while Analyze, View and Correct let you inspect and preview before removing. Correction options include Open objects, Negative Normals, Positive Normals and Mesh Only.

Key features

  • Removes redundant vertices, edges and faces
  • Targeted Vertex, Edges and Face menus
  • Normals reorientation and sizing
  • Full Analyze in one click
  • Inspect and preview before correcting
