What FxFactory Light Show does

FxFactory Light Show delivers a sweeping wipe of light effects across your picture. It blends glows, spills, glints, distortions and environmental touches, and works in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, plus Final Cut Pro X and Motion.

The Filter version goes on a title or clip and animates automatically or by keyframes, and is the most flexible. The Transition version goes between clips, or at the start or end of a single one, in Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro. A Title version exists only in Final Cut Pro. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel and requires the free FxFactory download.

Key features

  • Linear wipe of glows, spills, glints and distortions
  • Filter version with automatic or keyframed animation
  • Transition version for clips
  • Title version in Final Cut Pro only
  • Requires free FxFactory
