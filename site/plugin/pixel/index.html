What ▩ Pixel does

Pixel is an AI-driven material add-on for Blender. It converts prompts, existing images or renders into materials you can keep editing, producing seamless textures and upscaled assets within the program.

Text to Material turns descriptions like wood, marble or fabric into textures, and PBR map generation outputs albedo, roughness, normal, height, metallic and ambient occlusion. A simplified layer editor allows masks and blending on images. Artists who need quick surface ideas can iterate without leaving Blender.

Key features

  • Text to Material from prompts
  • PBR maps: albedo, roughness, normal, height, metallic, AO
  • Seamless texture creation
  • Upscaling inside Blender
  • Simplified layer editor with masks and blending
