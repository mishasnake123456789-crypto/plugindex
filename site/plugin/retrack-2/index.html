What ReTrack 2 does

ReTrack 2, by Marco Sanasi, is a tracking toolkit for After Effects that works with data you already have. It combines tracked data of all types, re-tracks hard shots using selections, and adjusts poor tracks by working above the tracked keyframes.

Partial tracks are rebuilt automatically, and missing tracks can be estimated, including targets that move offscreen. A keys-based edit tool supports trimmed and shifted layers, perspective tracked masks are handled, and 3D camera motion can be transferred to 3D nulls. Tracked data converts directly, and complex tracking can be built from basic data.

Key features

  • Auto-rebuild partial tracked data
  • Estimates missing and offscreen tracks
  • Keys-based editing with trimmed layers
  • Perspective tracked mask support
  • Transfer 3D camera motion to nulls
