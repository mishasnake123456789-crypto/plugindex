3ds Max script that makes a work plane following the dominant screen-aligned axes, usable as a construction plane.

What miauu's Work Plane does

miauu's Work Plane creates a plane matching the two major axes closest to your screen view. In automatic mode, rotating the Perspective viewport makes the plane snap to the dominant XY, XZ or YZ orientation.

The plane can be locked and used for construction. There are several manual methods for setting a specific center and axis, including locking to a chosen major axis and position. It can snap to selected geometry or the polygons directly beneath the mouse, and alignment can be forced. Modelers who draw or place objects in perspective gain a flexible drafting surface.

Key features

  • Automatic plane following the screen rotation
  • Snaps to dominant XY, XZ or YZ
  • Lockable construction plane
  • Manual center and axis methods
  • Snap to geometry or polygon under the mouse
