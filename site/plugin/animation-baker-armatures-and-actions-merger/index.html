What Animation Baker : Armatures & Actions Merger does

Animation Baker combines several armatures and their NLA animations into one unified armature with a single baked action, handling bone prefixes to avoid naming conflicts.

It bakes all active NLA tracks, detects the animation range and generates one merged action. Useful for game pipelines that need a single skeleton and clip. Highlights cover merges armatures into one, bakes active NLA tracks, automatic animation range detection and bone prefix management. It keeps animated characters tidy before they are sent to another application.

Key features

  • Merges armatures into one
  • Bakes active NLA tracks
  • Automatic animation range detection
  • Bone prefix management
