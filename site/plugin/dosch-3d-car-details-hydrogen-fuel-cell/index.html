What DOSCH 3D: Car Details - Hydrogen Fuel Cell does

DOSCH 3D: Car Details - Hydrogen Fuel Cell models a car powered by hydrogen, complete with the parts and systems that define it. It is fully textured and made for technical visualizations, animations and illustrations in Cinema 4D.

Scaling can be adapted, and materials, textures and colors changed, with your own labels applied. Individual elements can be switched on or off or set to partial transparency, and animated individually or as a complete model. It is helpful for explaining fuel cell stacks, tanks and drive components in educational and marketing content.
