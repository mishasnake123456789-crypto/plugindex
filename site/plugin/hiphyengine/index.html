Unified physics solver for Blender covering rigid body, hair, cloth, soft body and MPM snow and sand, with no intersections.

What Hiphyengine does

HiPhyEngine simulates rigid body, hair, cloth and soft body in a unified, fully coupled solver that supports five material models. It guarantees intersection-free results, even with complex multi-layered collisions.

An MPM solver covers snow and sand, and finite element discretization with continuous collision detection supports the stability. It suits artists needing reliable interactions between different materials. Notable points: fully coupled multi-material solver, intersection-free guarantee and MPM snow and sand. The package also offers continuous collision detection.

Key features

  • Fully coupled multi-material solver
  • Intersection-free guarantee
  • MPM snow and sand
  • Continuous collision detection
