What ProtoTech's PARASOLID Importer for 3ds Max®-Trial does

This importer from ProtoTech lets 3ds Max open Parasolid files natively, so CAD-originated geometry can go into visualization, rendering or further editing without a conversion detour. Colors defined in the source model carry across.

The listing is the trial edition, which runs for fifteen days or thirty imports and needs no activation key. A full perpetual license includes a year of free updates, and an optional automation license allows imports to be triggered from your own plugin code. It installs on Windows 10 and 11 and works with 3ds Max 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027.
