What Sheffield Look Sweet II does

Four plug-ins from Sheffield polish troubled or mundane footage in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. It runs through the free FxFactory on Apple Silicon and Intel.

Mr. Fixit EZ borrows HDR photography to produce bright shadows and dark highlights, mixing them back without changing colour. Vibrance Plus controls saturation, raising chroma faster in low-saturation areas, with Pastel and Enhance modes. Bounce Fill simulates a coloured reflector, gold by default, with an optional invert clip. Super Glam sharpens highlights and softens shadows, either on the whole image or on luminance alone. A handy finishing kit for editors improving weak shots.

Key features

  • Mr. Fixit EZ shadow and highlight repair
  • Vibrance Plus with Pastel and Enhance modes
  • Bounce Fill with adjustable colour
  • Super Glam highlight and shadow shaping
