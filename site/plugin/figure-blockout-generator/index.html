What Figure Blockout Generator does

Figure Blockout Generator creates a full human blockout as regular mesh objects with one button. The figure arrives in its own collection, offering three geometry modes and two independent landmark systems.

Height, gender base and proportion type are adjustable, built around the Cranial Unit. The figure can be posed, used as a modeling base, or baked to a standard armature. Character modelers and illustrators who need accurate proportions as a starting point can save time before sculpting details.

Key features

  • One-button full figure blockout
  • Three geometry modes, two landmark systems
  • Height, gender base and proportion controls
  • Pose or bake to a standard armature
