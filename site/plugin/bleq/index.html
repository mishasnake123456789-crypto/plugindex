Blender extension that queues scene and camera combinations and renders them one after another automatically.

What BLeQ does

BLeQ, short for Blender Queue, handles batch rendering of several scenes and cameras without supervision. Put scene and camera pairs in the queue, launch the renderer, and the entries render one by one.

It manages the switch between scenes and cameras itself during the process. Artists producing multiple angles, shots or variations of a project can leave the machine working through the list instead of changing settings between jobs.

Key features

  • Queue of scene and camera setups
  • Sequential automatic rendering
  • Automatic scene and camera switching
  • Unattended batch operation
