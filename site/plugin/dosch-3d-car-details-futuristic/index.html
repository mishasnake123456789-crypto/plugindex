What Dosch 3D: Car Details - Futuristic does

Futuristic styling distinguishes this Dosch Design vehicle, a fully textured Cinema 4D car that includes its critical parts and systems. Like the other models in the series, it is intended for technical visualizations, animations and illustrations.

Materials, textures and colors can be freely changed, custom textures and labels applied, and the scale adapted. Elements are turned on or off and faded by transparency, and they can be animated individually or together. Concept visualizations, science-fiction scenes and technology presentations can make use of it without building a car from scratch.

Key features

  • Futuristic car with parts and systems
  • Editable scale, materials and colors
  • Custom textures and labels
  • Transparency control per element
  • Animation of parts or whole model
