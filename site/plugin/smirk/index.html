Blender add-on for dynamically carving facial features into a mesh, for animation and rigging.

What SMIRK does

SMIRK, by Krysidian, lets you carve facial features such as expressions directly into a mesh in Blender, with a link to Grease Pencil. It is tagged under Animation, Rigging and Grease Pencil.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.
