What Morganite - Animated Typeface does

Morganite is a condensed typeface from Animography with a sleek urban personality. The narrow letterforms remain legible while allowing more words to fit in limited space, useful for headlines and posters.

It comes with built-in animation for After Effects 2020 through 2026, and suits branding, titles and bold typographic layouts. It is made by Animography and is intended for graphic designers and motion artists who want a narrow face that still reads clearly in headlines or posters.
