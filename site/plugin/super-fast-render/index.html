What Super Fast Render does

Super Fast Render, from Pidgeon Tools, looks at how a scene behaves and then works out settings it expects to give quicker renders. That benchmark-based approach takes the guesswork out of balancing samples, tiles and other options by hand.

If you would rather skip the test, a selection of presets is available, and a one-click general optimization applies sensible changes across the scene. Documentation is included. It targets Blender 2.92, 2.93 and 3.0 and is released under the GPL, so it is best suited to artists on those versions who want shorter render times with little tweaking.

Key features

  • Benchmarks the scene to choose settings
  • Automatic scene optimization
  • Presets as an alternative to benchmarking
  • One-click general optimization
  • Works with Blender 2.92 to 3.0
