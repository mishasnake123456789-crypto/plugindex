What Boris FX Continuum Perpetual License does

Continuum is a large effects collection for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro, and this version is sold as a perpetual licence. It targets everyday finishing work: fixing soft or damaged footage, keying green screen, applying film looks and grain, and building transitions.

Each effect has Mocha tracking built in. AI tools handle restoration and skin retouching, with machine learning models for deinterlacing, faces, license plates, motion blur and upres. Effects can follow the beat of a music track, and the Custom FX Editor adds Pixel Chooser, quick preset browsing and colour management.

Key features

  • AI image restoration and retouching
  • Mocha tracking in every effect
  • Keying and drag-and-drop transitions
