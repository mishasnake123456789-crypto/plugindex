What Convert Rotation Mode does

Convert Rotation Mode is a Blender add-on by Lauloque that changes the rotation mode of selected bones while keeping existing animation and poses intact. Choose bones in Pose mode, pick the new mode, and convert.

The add-on scans through all keyframes on the chosen bones automatically, and it works on one or several bones at once. The panel can be placed in a sidebar tab of your choice, and a rotation modes cheat sheet helps you decide which mode to use. Clipboard access is requested for copying and pasting bone transforms and supports Blender 4.5 LTS and newer.

Key features

  • Changes bone rotation modes while preserving animation
  • Scans all keyframes of selected bones
  • Works on one or many bones in Pose mode
  • Customizable sidebar tab
  • Built-in rotation modes cheat sheet
