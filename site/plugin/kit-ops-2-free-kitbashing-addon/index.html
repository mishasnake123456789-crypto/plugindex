What Kit Ops 2 Free Kitbashing Addon does

Kit Ops 2 Free offers a kitbashing workflow for Blender aimed at quickly exploring hard surface designs. It works with INSERTS, which can cut into or add to existing objects, or form standalone objects of their own.

Because it is built on Blender modifiers, the results remain editable. KPACKS bundle collections of INSERTS, and a thumbnail view with batch browsing makes picking them fast. Rotation on insertion, mirroring, alignment and an auto-parent mode help placement. Concept artists and product modelers can use it to block out mechanical shapes rapidly.

Key features

  • INSERTS cut into or add to objects
  • Built on Blender modifiers
  • KPACK collections of inserts
  • Thumbnail browsing in batches
  • Mirroring, alignment and auto-parent
