What Hold My Cam does

Hold My Cam gives Blender cameras natural, lifelike motion without hand-keyframing every shake and drift. One click applies the effect.

It targets artists who want their shots to feel filmed rather than perfectly mechanical. The add-on works with Blender 4.0 and 4.1, includes documentation, is released under a Creative Commons license and provides free future updates to all customers. It is a quick way to add realism to cinematic or product shots.
