What Stornaway.io - The Interactive Video Editor does

This panel loads the Stornaway.io interactive video editor within After Effects and Premiere Pro. Creators can design and playtest interactive journeys without leaving their editing interface, previewing the interactivity next to Premiere sequences.

Experiences are built without coding from regular video, 360 VR video, images and graphics. After logging in, you can create, edit, upload and preview from the panel, then publish to the web and LMS platforms in a click. On certain plans, publishing through Unity extends to mobile, VR and Mixed Reality apps. It fits education, learning and marketing projects.

Key features

  • Stornaway.io editor inside Adobe apps
  • Design and playtest interactive journeys
  • No-code builds with video, 360 VR and images
