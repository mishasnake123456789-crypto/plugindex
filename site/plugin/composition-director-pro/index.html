What Composition Director Pro does

Composition Director Pro collects shot-framing aids in one panel. Live overlays show the Rule of Thirds, Golden Ratio and Fibonacci Spiral so you can judge composition while working.

It includes eight cinematic aspect ratios with letterbox, smart shot-size framing and fourteen camera angles. A Composition Score from 0 to 10 points out what is off in a shot. Filmmakers and render artists can refine camera work more systematically. Highlights cover rule of Thirds, Golden Ratio and Fibonacci overlays and 8 cinematic aspect ratios with letterbox.

Key features

  • Rule of Thirds, Golden Ratio and Fibonacci overlays
  • 8 cinematic aspect ratios with letterbox
  • 14 cinematic camera angles
  • 0-10 Composition Score
