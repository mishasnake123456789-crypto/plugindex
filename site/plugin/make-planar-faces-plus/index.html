What Make Planar Faces Plus does

Make Planar Faces Plus is a Blender add-on by Anton-Florey whose job is to make mesh faces planar. It requires Blender 5.1 or newer and is released for free under the GNU GPL v3.

There are separate builds for Windows, Linux and macOS, covering Intel and Apple Silicon, and installation works by drag and drop or from disk. The latest release is 1.1.0, and a demo video is available. It is intended for artists who need clean flat faces in their models.
