Cloud collaboration extension that shares footage, syncs review comments and transcribes media from Premiere Pro.

What TubeWay does

TubeWay connects Premiere Pro to a cloud platform for working with teams and clients. Raw footage can be shared through upload and download links, while reviewers leave frame-accurate feedback on the platform itself.

Cloud clips can be imported to the timeline, edits exported for review, and comments brought back as timeline markers. The extension also transcribes, subtitles and translates footage, and detects text, objects, shots and scenes, with a metadata search for finding material across a library. It is useful to remote teams who want review, transcription and media management in one panel.

Key features

  • Share footage with teams and clients via links
  • Import cloud clips and review comments to the timeline
  • Transcribe, subtitle and translate footage
  • Detect text, objects, shots and scenes
  • Metadata search for media management
