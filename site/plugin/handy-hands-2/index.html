What Handy Hands 2 does

Handy Hands 2 from Alfie Dawes lets After Effects artists produce hands with a click. Style buttons apply looks such as Simple Fill, Separated, Tapered Stroke and Advanced Stroke, so the same hand can match different illustration styles.

Management buttons swap left and right hands, rotate a hand 180 degrees, and add a linked hand. A three-fingered mode drops to three digits rather than four in some styles. The panel offers compact icon tabs or a detailed view with text descriptions. It supports After Effects 2020 through 2026.

Key features

  • Preset styles including Tapered and Advanced Stroke
  • Swap left/right and rotate 180 degrees
  • Add a linked hand
  • 3-fingered mode
  • Compatible with After Effects 2020-2026
