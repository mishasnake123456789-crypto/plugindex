What Drastic Technologies MediaReactor Workstation does

Drastic Technologies MediaReactor Workstation translates media files for After Effects, Premiere Pro and Media Encoder on macOS and Windows. It offers broad read and write access to formats and codecs so footage can move between applications without extra steps.

Supported ecosystems include Filmworkz Nucoda, Autodesk Flame, Smoke and Lustre, Assimilate SCRATCH, and Avid Media Composer, Symphony and NewsCutter. It converts on the fly across acquisition formats such as Weisscam, Phantom, Sony and ARRI, reads DPX, Cineon, ARI and DNG sequences, and carries metadata, multi-track audio, timecode and captions. Edit-while-recording is supported with a compatible recorder.

Key features

  • Interfaces for Premiere Pro, After Effects and Media Encoder
  • Works with Avid, Autodesk, Assimilate and Nucoda formats
  • On-the-fly conversion of camera acquisition formats
  • DPX, Cineon, ARI and DNG sequences with metadata
  • Live edit-while-recording
