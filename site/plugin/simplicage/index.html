Blender add-on that generates cloth-physics cages and collision boxes from meshes, bones or vertex groups.

What Simplicage does

Simplicage builds simple physics cages for models in Blender using its cloth physics. A cage can be created from a mesh, bone selection or vertex groups, giving secondary motion to clothing, hair or accessories.

Collision boxes can be generated in the same ways, and a presets system provides defaults for common uses. A tool manages the available cages and bakes them quickly. Complete documentation and a demo video are available. Character artists and animators setting up jiggle or cloth behavior get a simpler path than manual setup.

Key features

  • Physics cages from mesh, bones or vertex groups
  • Collision box generation
  • Presets for common uses
  • Cage management and quick baking
