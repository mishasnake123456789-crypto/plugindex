What Fastest Bake does

Fastest Bake aims to shorten the time spent waiting on bakes. It uses Blender's own settings to strike a balance between speed and quality, rather than adding a separate engine.

Settings are optimized automatically, so you need not tune samples and margins yourself, and a simple interface lets you pick the texture types to bake. Beginners and busy artists generating maps for games or lookdev will get acceptable results with fewer decisions.

Key features

  • Speed and quality balance via Blender settings
  • Automatic settings optimization
  • Streamlined texture type selection
