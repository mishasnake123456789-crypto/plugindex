What Wrap It! - Procedural does

Wrap It! - Procedural offers a procedural way to wrap objects in Blender. It is designed for use with both Cycles and Eevee and is distributed as a Blender Extension.

It works with Blender 4.0 through 5.0, is released under the GPL license, and ships with docs and a video demo. Artists can use it to cover products and objects without hand-building the wrapping geometry. Its procedural nature means the wrap can be adjusted after the fact, which is convenient for packaging mockups, gift boxes and other products that need covering.

Key features

  • Procedural wrapping approach
  • Cycles and Eevee support
  • Blender 4.0 through 5.0
