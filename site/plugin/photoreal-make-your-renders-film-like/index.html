What Photoreal - Make Your Renders Film-Like does

CGMatter's Photoreal makes clean CG feel like footage shot on film. It applies effects to every frame using procedural compositing.

Gate weave and flicker imitate the small shifts and flashes of a film gate. Film grain adds the texture of film stock, glare mimics lens flare from bright objects, lens artifacts include chromatic aberration and distortion, and vignetting darkens the corners. Filmmakers and motion designers can reach a photographic finish without external tools.

Key features

  • Gate weave and flicker
  • Film grain
  • Glare from bright objects
  • Chromatic aberration and distortion
  • Vignetting
