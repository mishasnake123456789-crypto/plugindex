What Pixel Lab Arnold Lighting Essentials does

Pixel Lab Arnold Lighting Essentials is a set of 20 studio lighting scenes made to render with Arnold in Cinema 4D. Every scene is supplied with a full Xpresso rig for changing lights and colors.

Setups cover product visualization, vehicles, jewelry, portraits, beverages and glossy tech, as well as cinematic and dramatic moods that include a foggy volumetric scene. Product and automotive artists can load a scene, swap in their model and adjust the rig to get polished lighting without building it from nothing.

Key features

  • 20 customizable Arnold studio scenes
  • Full Xpresso rig in every scene
  • Product, vehicle, jewelry, portrait and beverage setups
  • Cinematic and volumetric lighting options
