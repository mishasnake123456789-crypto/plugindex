What Circle Slicer does

Circle Slicer from ALINCO is a circular slicing tool for After Effects. You set up a composition with a 1:1 aspect ratio, place the content you want to cut inside it, and press a button to run the slicing.

A Make by shape option adds a controller for extra adjustment. It supports After Effects 2022, 2023 and 2024. Motion designers can use it to create radial, pie-like or ring-shaped breakups of images and graphics without cutting everything by hand.

Key features

  • Circular slicing of content
  • Works in 1:1 compositions
  • Button-driven operation
  • Make by shape option with controller
  • Supports After Effects 2022-2024
