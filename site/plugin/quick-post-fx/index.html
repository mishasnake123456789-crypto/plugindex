A Blender color grading add-on with 14 filmic presets built on the real-time compositor.

What Quick Post Fx does

Quick Post FX adds color grading to Cycles renders through Blender's real-time compositor. It sets up the necessary compositor nodes for you and provides fourteen filmic presets that imitate film stocks and well-known movie looks.

Beyond presets there are parameters for exposure, contrast, saturation, vibrance, brightness and hue, plus individual color channel control and a color balance slider to move the temperature warmer or cooler. Image filters including blur, vignette and lens distortion complete the look. Visualizers and animators wanting a graded finish without wiring nodes themselves will find it speeds up the final pass.

Key features

  • 14 filmic grading presets
  • Exposure, contrast, saturation, vibrance and hue
  • Color channel control and temperature balance
  • Blur, vignette and lens distortion filters
  • Uses the real-time compositor
