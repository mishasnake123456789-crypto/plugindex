What AEJuice Prism does

AEJuice Prism is a collection of light overlays for After Effects and Premiere Pro, made from footage shot with real lenses in 4K. It supplies refractions, reflections, flares and bent-light looks that would otherwise take a lot of fiddling to fake.

Effects are applied by dragging them onto a composition or sequence. The pack includes shard, kaleidoscope and broken-prism filters, and its manager offers quick previews, favorites and drag-and-drop import. Editors who want a dreamy, glassy light treatment on their footage can get there in a few clicks.

Key features

  • 50+ refraction, reflection and flare effects
  • Filmed with real lenses in 4K
  • Shard, Kaleidoscope and Broken prism filters
  • Drag-and-drop onto a comp or sequence
  • Pack Manager with preview and favorites
