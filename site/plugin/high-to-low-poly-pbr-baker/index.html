What High To Low Poly Pbr Baker does

High To Low Poly PBR Baker transfers materials from a detailed model to an optimized one. In the N-panel you set Base Name, Resolution, Margin and Extrusion, then bake all maps with one click.

It uses Blender's Selected to Active with extrusion, and bakes metallic by re-routing the metallic texture through emission. A fast option for game asset texturing. Highlights cover one-click bake of all maps and base Name, Resolution, Margin and Extrusion.

Key features

  • One-click bake of all maps
  • Base Name, Resolution, Margin and Extrusion
  • Selected to Active with extrusion
  • Metallic baked via emission
