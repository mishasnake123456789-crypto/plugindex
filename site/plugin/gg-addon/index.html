Builds optimized grass and tree systems on landscapes in Blender using camera culling and impostors to cut render cost.

What Gg Addon does

GG Addon, from Impedelse, scatters vegetation across terrain or other meshes in a few clicks while keeping scenes light. Camera culling is on by default, so objects outside the view are skipped to reduce render time.

Impostors combine with culling to lower the amount of geometry while preserving detail. A GG_Baker tab in the N-panel provides two-sided and four-sided templates using a selected-to-active bake workflow for creating the impostor textures. It benefits landscape artists who need dense foliage without overwhelming memory.

Key features

  • Grass and tree systems in a few clicks
  • Camera culling enabled by default
  • Impostors to reduce scene geometry
  • GG_Baker tab with 2-sided and 4-sided templates
  • Works on landscapes and other meshes
