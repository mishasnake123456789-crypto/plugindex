What Ziflow Creative Collaboration does

Ziflow Creative Collaboration lets creative teams send work for proofing from within After Effects and Premiere Pro. Rather than exporting a file and uploading it elsewhere, assets can be shared for review straight from the host.

You can check the status of an asset, work in Ziflow's asset viewer, and see comments update in real time. Comments can be viewed, answered and resolved without leaving Creative Cloud. It is built for agencies and brands who want a quicker feedback and approval cycle on video and other creative files.

Key features

  • Share assets for review from the host app
  • Check asset status in Creative Cloud
  • Collaborate in Ziflow's asset viewer
  • Real-time comment updates
  • View, reply to and resolve comments
