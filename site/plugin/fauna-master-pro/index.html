What 🦅Fauna Master Pro does

Fauna Master Pro adds living animals to Blender scenes through a combination of assets and simulation. Birds, insects, land animals and fish can be scattered, animated and directed.

A library of more than 150 species is included. Movement is simulated in real time and reacts to terrain, obstacles and rules, and needs neither baking nor caching needed. Simulation rules include options such as self-collision. Environment and wildlife scene artists can fill nature shots with convincing motion.

Key features

  • 150+ animal species
  • Real-time movement simulation
  • Responds to terrain and obstacles
  • No baking or caching
  • Birds, insects, land animals and fish
