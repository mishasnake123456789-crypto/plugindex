What Voxel Heat Diffuse Skinning does

Mesh Online's Voxel Heat Diffuse Skinning automates character weight painting in Blender and copes with meshes that are not watertight, a case where Blender's own automatic weights often struggle.

The mesh is turned into a solid statue using a ray-traced voxel grid, and heat is diffused through it to give natural vertex weights. This is combined with Blender's own heat map diffusion, which takes care of fine regions like fingers and toes, with a physically based energy attenuation algorithm. Riggers and character artists save time on skinning.

Key features

  • Skinning for non-seamless characters
  • Voxel heat diffusion with ray-traced grid
  • Combined with built-in heat map diffusion
  • Physically based energy attenuation
