GoPro's plugin for importing and reframing HERO, MAX and Fusion footage in After Effects and Premiere Pro.

What GoPro Reframe does

GoPro Reframe lets editors treat wide or 360 GoPro footage as a flexible source. Import HERO, MAX or Fusion clips, then choose the view you want after the fact, as if you were holding the camera.

Pan, tilt, rotate and zoom are animated with keyframes, and the amount of motion blur between keyframes is controllable. Lens curvature can be changed to alter the look, and the horizon can be levelled by hand for HERO and 360 material. Output presets match resolutions and aspect ratios commonly used for social sharing.

Key features

  • Imports HERO, MAX and Fusion footage
  • Keyframed pan, tilt, rotate and zoom
  • Motion blur control between keyframes
  • Lens curvature and manual horizon leveling
  • Social-ready resolution and aspect presets
