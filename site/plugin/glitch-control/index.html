What Glitch Control does

Glitch Control, from MEGAKRUNCH, is a modular effect plugin for producing classic glitch looks. Modules can be combined to build the type of digital breakdown seen in music videos and titles.

It runs in After Effects and Premiere Pro, supports Apple Silicon natively and works with Multi-Frame Rendering. Compatible hosts run from CS6 up to After Effects 2022 and Premiere Pro 2022. It is available at no charge, making it an easy addition for editors and motion artists who want glitch effects without a big setup.
