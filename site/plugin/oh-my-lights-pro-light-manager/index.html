What Oh My Lights - Pro Light Manager does

Oh My Lights is a light manager that puts the key settings for every light in one place. You can change power, color, shadow and visibility of spot, point, area and sun lights and select each by clicking its name.

All lights can be selected at once and moved into collections. Wires can be disabled, background transparency adjusted, and the render engine switched between Eevee and Cycles. Advanced parameters are also available. It speeds up lighting setup in scenes with many lights.

Key features

  • Power, color, shadow and visibility control
  • Select lights by name
  • Move all lights into collections
  • Eevee and Cycles switching
