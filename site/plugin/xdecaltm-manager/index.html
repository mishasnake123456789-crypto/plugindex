A Blender drag-and-drop decal manager that adapts decals to surfaces without UV maps, with procedural wear materials.

What Xdecal™ Manager does

Xdecal Manager lets you ornament and texture models in Blender with decals, without requiring UV maps. You drag a decal onto an object and a dedicated gizmo positions it, adapting its geometry to the surface beneath.

Any material or object can serve as a template pasted like a sticker. Included materials offer procedural effects such as damage and peeled paint, wear, bump and normal maps, and parallax, while the Decal Palette creates new decals. Hard-surface artists and environment texturers who want fast surface detail will find it quicker than UV-based texturing.

Key features

  • Drag-and-drop decals with a placement gizmo
  • Works without UV maps
  • Any material or object as a template
  • Procedural damage, peel, wear and parallax materials
  • Decal Palette
