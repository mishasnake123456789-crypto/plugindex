Brings Filestage review and approval comments into a panel inside After Effects and Premiere Pro.

What Filestage does

Filestage's panel lets editors and motion designers handle review feedback inside their Adobe application. You can start a review process from the app, and reviewers' comments appear in context in the panel instead of spreading across email and chat.

With playhead sync, selecting a comment moves the timeline to the matching moment in either application. In Premiere Pro, comments also show up as markers marking requested changes. The tool covers proofing and reviewing for video work.

Key features

  • Start reviews from Adobe apps
  • Comments shown in context in the panel
  • Playhead sync from comments
  • Comments as markers in Premiere Pro
  • Proofing and reviewing
