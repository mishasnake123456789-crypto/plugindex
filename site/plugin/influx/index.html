What Influx does

Influx is Autokroma's importer plugin for Premiere Pro, After Effects and Media Encoder. It teaches these applications a wide range of formats and codecs, saving the time, disk space and hassle of external transcoding.

Footage can be edited in Premiere Pro and processed in After Effects, projects from either can be exported through Media Encoder, and files can be transcoded straight from there. Audio opens in Audition in the new containers and codecs without re-encoding or remuxing, covering FLAC, Opus, Ogg Vorbis, DTS-HD Master Audio, Dolby AC-3, AIFF, MKA, WMA and Speex. Editors receiving mixed-source material benefit most.

Key features

  • Native importer for Adobe apps
  • Edit in Premiere Pro, process in After Effects
  • Export and transcode via Media Encoder
  • Edit audio in Audition without re-encoding
  • FLAC, Opus, Vorbis, DTS-HD, AC-3, AIFF, MKA, WMA, Speex
