What Math Art does

Math Art by dkrider adds mathematically defined shapes to Blender, including minimal surfaces, polyhedra and knots, so you can create geometric sculpture without modeling it by hand. It is listed under Add Mesh.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
