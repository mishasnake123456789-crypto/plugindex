What Rt Painter - Realtime Object Scatter & Physics Brush does

RT Painter lets you paint assets directly into a Blender scene. A modal operator spawns objects under the cursor as you click and drag.

Brush controls include radius, flow, density, spacing and pressure, with distance-based or time-based flow. Placement uses raycasts with optional alignment to surface normals and snapping, and physics settings let objects settle naturally. Scale, rotation, offset and materials can be randomized. Suits dressing environments with rocks, debris or props.

Key features

  • Realtime painting with a modal operator
  • Radius, flow, density, spacing and pressure brushes
  • Raycast surface placement with normal alignment
  • Physics-aware placement
  • Randomized scale, rotation, offset and materials
