Blender fluid simulation addon that gathers Mantaflow settings into one 3D viewport panel with quick-setup presets.

What Mantapro does

MantaPro builds on Mantaflow, Blender's standard fluid solver, and puts all of its settings in one 3D viewport panel so you do not hop between property editors. Simulation objects stay active, leaving domain, flow, effector and force always selected.

A Quick Setup preset system speeds up common scenarios, and extra simulation tools extend what the stock solver offers. Smoke, fire and liquid artists who iterate often will find the consolidated layout faster to tune.

Key features

  • Single viewport panel for all settings
  • Always-active domain, flow, effector and force
  • Quick Setup presets
  • Extra tools on top of Mantaflow
