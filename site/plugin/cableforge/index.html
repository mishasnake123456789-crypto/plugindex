Procedural cable bundle generator for Blender routing along curves or between objects with presets and zip ties.

What Cableforge does

CableForge builds cable bundles procedurally. Cables route along existing curves or automatically between two selected objects, with optional natural sag.

Cable count, radius, spacing and bundle thickness are adjustable with live editing, real-world presets are included, and zip ties and strain-relief end boots can be added. Great for electronics and sci-fi environments. Under the hood you get customizable cable count, radius and spacing, routes along curves or between objects and natural sag.

Key features

  • Customizable cable count, radius and spacing
  • Routes along curves or between objects
  • Natural sag
  • Zip ties and strain-relief boots
