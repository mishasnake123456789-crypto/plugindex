What Biped Helper + Picker does

Biped Helper + Picker sits beside the standard Biped panel in 3ds Max and fills in functions that panel lacks, while still giving quick access to its familiar tools. A separate Picker interface mirrors much of the main window so selecting body parts stays fast during animation.

Reference aids cover ghosted poses, a pose buffer, geometry snapshots and trajectory helpers, and keying, TCB and range controls are close at hand. It also covers transform application, footstep cleanup, loading and saving clips, layer management and IK constraint retargeting. Pose copy and paste, limb mirroring and finger or spine alignment round out the toolset for Biped work.

Key features

  • Dedicated Picker UI with partial part selection and IK limb pivots
  • Ghosting, pose buffering, snapshots and trajectory helpers
  • Keying, TCB and time range operations
  • Animation clip load and save, layers and IK retargeting
  • Pose copy and paste, limb mirroring, finger and spine alignment
