What Bagapie does

Bagapie is a large toolbox for architecture and environment modeling in Blender, with more than fifty tools. Ivy generation, scattering, parametric objects, mesh editing helpers and grouping are all included.

Access runs through three pie menus: Main Pie on J for object tools, Edit Mode Pie on D for modeling tools, and GeoPack Pie on V for custom tools. Scattering supports painting, sourcing from the Asset Browser, effectors, camera culling and presets. Archviz and landscape artists can build and populate scenes without leaving the viewport.

Key features

  • 50+ architecture and environment tools
  • Three pie menus on J, D and V
  • Scattering with paint, effectors and culling
  • Ivy generation and parametric objects
