What Procutter Plus does

ProCutter Plus is aimed at furniture designers who model in Blender and then need to cut real material. With one click it produces the full set of cutouts for all parts of a project.

It accounts for how textures sit on each part and supports plywood, lumber, glass and metal. The cutouts can be passed straight to CutList Plus fx, which optimizes layouts for cutting. Woodworkers and cabinet makers can therefore move from a 3D design to a workshop plan with fewer manual steps.

Key features

  • One-click cutouts for all furniture parts
  • Takes texture placement into account
  • Plywood, lumber, glass and metal support
  • Direct handoff to CutList Plus fx
