What Flat UV Mapper does

Flat UV Mapper offers level-design style texture projection in Blender. Planar UV projections on selected faces can be applied, scaled, aligned and adjusted right in the 3D Viewport, without opening the UV Editor.

Two projection modes exist: World-Axis Aligned, in the style of Hammer or CSG tools, and Face-Normal Aligned. Tile size, offset and rotation are adjustable, with Flip U and Flip V options. Environment and blockout artists can texture surfaces quickly.

Key features

  • World-axis and face-normal projection modes
  • Tile size and offset controls
  • Rotation and Flip U/V
  • Works directly in the 3D Viewport
