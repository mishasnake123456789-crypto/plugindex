What Power Sequencer does

Power Sequencer brings editing helpers for content creators into Blender's Sequencer. Maintained by the community, it was once part of the bundled add-ons in Blender 4.1.

It is now supplied as-is under limited support. Version 2.0.4 was updated for a newer Sequencer API, no special permissions are needed, and it is distributed under GPL v3 or later. Video editors working inside Blender can speed up cutting and arranging strips. Editors who prefer to cut quickly with shortcuts can keep their video work in the same file as their 3D scenes.

Key features

  • Editing tools for content creators
  • Installs into the Sequencer
  • Version 2.0.4 for newer Sequencer API
  • No special permissions
