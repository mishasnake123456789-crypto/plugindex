What miauu's GridAxisPainter does

GridAxisPainter by miauu changes the appearance of the major axes of the 3ds Max viewport grid. Instead of the default look, each of the X, Y and Z axes can have a separate color, which makes orientation clearer at a glance.

It applies in any viewport and supports Autodesk 3ds Max and 3ds Max Design 2020 to 2026. Version 1.0 is currently listed, a demo video is available, and it is free. Modelers who often work in orthographic and perspective views simultaneously will find color-coded axes helpful for keeping their bearings.

Key features
