Blender add-on that lists object, material and texture stats and flags naming mismatches and broken texture paths.

What Quickstats does

Quickstats collects scene information in one Blender panel so you can audit a file at a glance. It covers objects, materials and textures, including polygon and triangle counts.

You can filter to meshes only or textured objects and sort by name, material, polygon count or type. The tool detects naming inconsistencies between objects and materials and validates texture paths to find broken links. Problem assets are highlighted, and one click selects them for fixing. It is reachable from both the editor menu and the right-click context menu.

Key features

  • Object, material and texture statistics in one view
  • Polygon and triangle counts
  • Filtering and sorting by several properties
  • Detects naming mismatches and broken texture links
  • One-click selection of problem assets
