What Luca Visual FX Film Lo-Fi Look does

Film Lo-Fi Look from Luca Visual FX generates aged, dreamy and slightly surreal imagery for DaVinci Resolve. It can place a customizable border around the picture to suggest an old print or photograph.

A generous set of parameters allows you to build a personal look, and an image well accepts your own textures, patterns or video clips to blend in. Besides Resolve, the effect runs in Final Cut Pro X, Motion, After Effects and Premiere Pro, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Editors making nostalgic montages, music videos or atmospheric shorts can dial in a distressed analog feel.

Key features

  • Vintage and surreal moody looks
  • Customizable frame border
  • Image well for your own textures or clips
