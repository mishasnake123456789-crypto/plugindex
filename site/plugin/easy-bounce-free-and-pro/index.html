What Easy Bounce Free & Pro does

Easy Bounce from mamoworld tools simplifies bouncing animation in After Effects. You keyframe the motion path you want in rough form, and Easy Bounce generates the bounces for you instead of animating each one by hand.

Both Free and Pro versions exist, and it supports After Effects from CS4 through the 2026 release. Licensing includes time-limited options and online activation. Animators making bouncing balls, logos or characters will save considerable effort.
