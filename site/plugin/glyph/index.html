What Glyph does

Glyph is a pie menu for Blender designed to speed sculpting. The wheel can be customized to match how you work, covering Blender's sculpt and paint tools.

It supports Blender 4.5 through 5.1 and works with Cycles and Eevee, is released under the GPL license, and ships with documentation and a demo video. Sculptors can keep brushes close to the cursor. Keeping brush choices in a radial layout means less mouse travel to panels, which makes longer sculpting sessions smoother.

Key features

  • Customizable pie menu
  • Covers sculpt and paint tools
  • Blender 4.5 through 5.1
  • Documentation and demo video
