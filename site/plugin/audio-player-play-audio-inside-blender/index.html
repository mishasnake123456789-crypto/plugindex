What Audio Player - Play Audio Inside Blender does

Audio Player is a Blender add-on from wans7 that brings a music player into the interface. You can listen to reference tracks or background music while working, without switching to another application.

A Playlist Panel opens one or several audio files, and an Open button adds more later. Standard media controls, volume adjustment, shuffle and repeat are included, and playlists are saved so they are still there after Blender is closed and reopened. Position adjustment is also available. It suits artists who like sound while they model, animate or render.

Key features

  • Playlist panel with multi-file opening
  • Standard media controls and volume
  • Shuffle and repeat playback
  • Playlists persist between sessions
