Blender add-on that manages texture layers and masks in a single node group for Eevee and Cycles.

What Ucupaint does

Ucupaint brings layer-based texturing to Blender. Images, vertex attributes and generated textures are stacked as layers and masks, with all nodes held in one node group that can be wired to any other nodes.

Channels can be added or removed freely, and modifiers such as a color ramp or math operation allow quick tweaks. UDIM tiles are detected automatically from UV islands, and images can be loaded from asset libraries. It works with Eevee and Cycles. Texture painters and material artists get a tidier, more Photoshop-like approach to building materials.

Key features

  • Layers and masks in one node group
  • Flexible channels with modifiers
  • Automatic UDIM detection
  • Works with Eevee and Cycles
