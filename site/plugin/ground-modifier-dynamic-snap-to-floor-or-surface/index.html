What Ground Modifier (Dynamic Snap To Floor Or Surface) does

Ground Modifier keeps geometry resting on the ground dynamically. It behaves like a normal modifier, so it can be added and stacked with other effects, and it works on any object transform and with animation.

A custom z-height lets the ground sit somewhere other than zero. An experimental surface option, slower to compute, snaps to uneven terrain. Handy for props, characters and scattered objects. Among its capabilities are dynamic snapping to the floor, works with animated objects and custom z-height.

Key features

  • Dynamic snapping to the floor
  • Works with animated objects
  • Custom z-height
  • Optional surface mode (experimental)
