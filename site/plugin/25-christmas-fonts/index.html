What 25 Christmas Fonts does

25 Christmas Fonts by Eldamar Studio is a font collection with hand-painted lettering for festive and wedding designs. The styles are made to pair with one another, including winter and wedding looks, so a project can use several coordinated typefaces.

It can be used in After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop and InDesign and is licensed for personal or commercial work. Typical jobs are Christmas cards, wedding invitations, branding, stationery, logos, frames and book covers, along with seasonal titles in video.
