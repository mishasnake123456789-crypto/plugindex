What BOOM Library Armageddon does

BOOM Library Armageddon gathers more than 3,100 sounds about disasters at extreme scale, including earthquakes, storms, avalanches and collapsing terrain. It works with After Effects, Premiere Pro and other audio and video software.

The tone is aggressive and dense, with distortion and saturation treated as part of the design. A Construction Kit supplies event-based building blocks that can be layered into evolving destruction scenes, and a designed section pushes into gigantic, beyond-real events. Distant, wide perspectives and closer, more physical ones are both covered, in 24-bit, 96 kHz WAV for Windows and Mac.

Key features

  • 3,100+ natural disaster sound effects
  • Dense, distorted and saturated design
  • Event-based Construction Kit for layering
  • Designed section of gigantic events
  • 24-bit, 96kHz WAV
