What Bake Simulation To Bone does

Bake Simulation To Bone converts cloth or physics simulations into bone animation that game engines can use. It rigs a simulated model in a few clicks, then bakes the motion onto bones.

The baked bone animation exports to any game engine, and the add-on aims to give more natural results than doing the conversion by hand. It works with cloth simulation and physics rigging. Documentation is included. It is meant for game developers who need dynamic cloth in real-time projects.

Key features

  • Quick rigging of simulated models
  • Bake simulation to bones
  • Export to any game engine
  • Cloth and physics support
