What AEJuice Pixel CRT Effect does

AEJuice Pixel CRT Effect gives modern visuals the glow and pixel grid of an old display. It works on video, photos, text and logos, and offers adjustable controllers together with vintage monitor images for the finishing touches.

The workflow is drag and drop with an intuitive interface, and any resolution is handled, adapting automatically to horizontal, square and vertical formats. The AEJuice Pack Manager offers preview, download, import and favourites. After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve are the main hosts, and transparent or black-background videos extend it to Sony Vegas, OBS and others.

Key features

  • Customisable CRT controllers and monitor images
  • Works on video, photos, text and logos
  • Any resolution, auto-adjusting to format
  • Pack Manager with preview and favourites
  • Video versions for other editors
