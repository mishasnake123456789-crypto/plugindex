What Save Cams - Saves / Loads Camera Settings And Batch Rendering does

Save Cams is a Blender plugin from Bookyakuno that records camera setups so you can reuse them. It captures position, rotation, camera type, lens, DoF, output resolution and frame range, and lets you load them onto any camera.

Batch rendering produces stills or animations from the add-on's list, the current selection, a collection, or every scene's active camera. Because angles are stored rather than keyframed, you avoid duplicating cameras. It is handy for product shots and architectural views with many angles.

Key features

  • Save and load full camera settings
  • Batch render from list, selection or collection
  • Includes resolution and frame range
  • No keyframes or extra cameras needed
