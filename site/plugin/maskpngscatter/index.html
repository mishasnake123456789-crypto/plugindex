What MaskPNGscatter does

MaskPNGscatter from Ratio fills a matte or mask area in After Effects with randomly placed copies of any PNG or layer. It can place hundreds of copies for particle-style looks, decorative overlays and general motion graphics.

Count, size, rotation, position offset and global scale are adjustable, and animating the random seed adds movement. Several instances can be stacked for layered results. It is lightweight and fast, needs no keyframes to get going, and gained 16bpc support in version 1.1.

Key features

  • Scatters a PNG or layer within a mask
  • Count, size, rotation and offset controls
  • Animatable random seed
  • Stackable instances
  • 16bpc support from v1.1
