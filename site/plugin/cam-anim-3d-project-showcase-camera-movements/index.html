What Cam Anim 3D - Project Showcase Camera Movements does

Cam Anim 3D supplies camera moves for showing models in Blender, so you pick a presentation style instead of animating from scratch.

Styles include Motion Control inspired by cinema robots and product filmmaking, a Viewport Experience that imitates exploring a model in Blender, Scrolling Transitions with social media style jump cuts, a Modern Orbit as a refined turntable, and Boomerang Loops. Dolly and other cinematic basics are covered too. Portfolio and product artists present with polish.
