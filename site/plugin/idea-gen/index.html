What Idea Gen does

Staring at an empty scene with no concept is common. Ace Of 3D's Idea Gen creates random combinations of ideas inside Blender, found in the 'N' tab of the 3D viewport, to start a modelling or sculpting exercise.

Select two categories and press Generate Ideas as often as you like. If you prefer surprise, an extra panel offers random category buttons, and an advanced panel brings in a third idea and category. It is aimed at artists of every level, from beginners to veterans hunting for challenge prompts.

Key features

  • Random idea pairing in the N tab
  • Pick two categories and generate
  • Random category buttons
  • Advanced panel with a third idea
  • Suits any experience level
