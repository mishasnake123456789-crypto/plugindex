What Music Search does

Music Search by Vakago Tools adds a search panel for free music and audio samples to After Effects and Premiere Pro. It saves editors from hunting across several websites for tracks to place under a video.

Many of the songs can be used in both commercial and non-commercial work, and most are released under Creative Commons licences. The relevant licence is displayed in the extension panel so you can check the terms before use. The tool requires After Effects 18.0 or Premiere Pro 15.0 and newer.

Key features

  • Searches free music and samples
  • Many tracks cleared for commercial use
  • Mostly Creative Commons licensed
  • Licence displayed in the panel
  • Requires AE 18.0+ or Premiere Pro 15.0+
