What Modular Suburban House Creator does

Modular Suburban House Creator builds residential buildings in Blender from reusable pieces. You assemble houses from pre-made walls, roofs, floors and structural elements, or extend the library with your own models.

A smart snapping system aligns components while building, and a visual browser offers categorized thumbnails with name search. Placement is precise, with movement in one-meter increments, ninety-degree rotation and X or Y mirroring, and parts can be duplicated along with their children. Game level designers and architectural visualization artists can block out neighborhoods quickly.

Key features

  • Snap-together modular components
  • Visual part browser with search
  • Expandable library for custom parts
  • 1-meter moves, 90 degree rotation and mirroring
  • Duplicate parts with children
