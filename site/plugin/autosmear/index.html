What Autosmear does

Autosmear from 3Dan creates smear effects for Blender animation in a couple of clicks. It is built around a geometry nodes group, with smear buttons added to the 3D viewport's N-panel.

Smears and duplicates are added with one or more objects selected, and their look is adjusted in the object's modifier. Version 3 was rebuilt with new nodes for Blender 4.5 as a new add-on, and cleanup options remove smears afterward. Animators get stylized motion cheaply.

Key features

  • Smears and duplicates from N-panel buttons
  • Appearance edited in the modifier
  • Version 3 rebuilt for Blender 4.5
  • Cleanup options
