A colour correction plugin with a full-screen heads-up display for grading HD footage and creating stylised looks.

What Yanobox Moods does

Yanobox Moods grades footage through an on-screen heads-up interface in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. It targets HD footage and works full screen.

Controls include desaturate, silver and contrast, with a combined silver and contrast bleach bypass look, plus exposure and gamma for white levels and colour casts. A three-way colour balance offers shadow, midtone and highlight wheels with punchy or classic modes, and Wash Shadow blends shadows with pure colours for vintage tones. It handles 8 to 16 bit depths, is GPU accelerated, and includes presets, a help card and a compare handle for before/after splits.

Key features

  • Full-screen heads-up display interface
  • Silver, contrast and bleach bypass controls
  • Three-way colour balance with punchy or classic modes
  • Wash Shadow for vintage looks
  • 8 to 16 bit support and GPU acceleration
