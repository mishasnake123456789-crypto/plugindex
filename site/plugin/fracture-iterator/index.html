Blender add-on for animated destruction that blends keyframes and physics, breaking objects recursively via Cell Fracture.

What Fracture-Iterator does

Fracture-Iterator helps animate destruction in Blender by mixing keyframes with physics simulation. It drives the Cell Fracture add-on included with Blender to break objects in a recursive, controllable fashion.

Dynamics on fragments are enabled at the current frame while earlier motion stays in place, and objects can be broken by loose parts. This lets artists stage staged, timed breakups in layers. It suits VFX and motion artists building collapses and shattering sequences.

Key features

  • Recursive breaking with Cell Fracture
  • Dynamics enabled at the current frame
  • Break by loose parts
  • Keyframes combined with physics
