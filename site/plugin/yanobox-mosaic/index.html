What Yanobox Mosaic does

Yanobox Mosaic turns clips into dynamic tiles through real-time texturing of pixels and adaptive tiling. It runs in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro, plus Final Cut Pro X.

Texturing tools constrain, balance and animate fragmentation. ASCII effects use any monospaced font and custom character set, recursive details build animated graphics on a grid from gradient ramps, noise or any luminance map, and wireframe overlays add borders, triangulations, crosses and grids. The After Effects camera is supported natively. Over a hundred presets are included, and you can import your own motifs. Motion designers get abundant graphic variety.

Key features

  • Dynamic texturing and tiling
  • ASCII effects with any monospaced font
  • Recursive grid details from luminance maps
  • Native After Effects camera support
  • 100+ presets and custom motifs
