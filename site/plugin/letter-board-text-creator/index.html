What Letter Board - Text Creator does

Letter Board - Text Creator builds letter boards in Blender without modeling each character by hand.

The board appears at the 3D cursor and can hold unlimited lines of text. Controls cover letter size, line spacing and letter spacing, with left, center or right alignment per line or for the whole board. Custom naming creates a matching collection that organizes letters, spacers and backgrounds. Good for signage, menus and decorative scenes.

Key features

  • Generates at the 3D cursor
  • Unlimited lines of text
  • Size, line spacing and letter spacing controls
  • Left, center or right alignment
  • Custom-named organizing collection
