What Trajectory Anim does

Trajectory Anim is a Blender add-on by trisnox that animates objects and bones from a line you draw, much like Follow Path constraints. You can animate position, rotation or both along the drawn path.

A second line drawn within the trajectory acts as a timing chart, giving control over timing and easing. Rotation can also be represented by a drawn line, and the trajectory can use an annotation. Motion designers get a direct, sketch-like way to choreograph movement without keyframing each point.

Key features

  • Animate objects or bones along a drawn line
  • Position and rotation options
  • Intersection line works as a timing chart
  • Control over timing and easing
  • Similar to Follow Path constraints
