What NodeFlow Importer does

NodeFlow Importer, by LitvyakovEugene, opens 3ds Max scenes that have been converted by NodeFlow, handling materials from V-Ray and Corona. It is listed under Import-Export and Material.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
