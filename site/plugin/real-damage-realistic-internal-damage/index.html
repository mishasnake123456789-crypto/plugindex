What Real Damage - Realistic Internal Damage does

Real Damage produces deep destruction in geometry and adds reinforcement, which suits broken concrete or walls in Blender. Procedural concrete and steel materials are generated automatically.

Settings cover depth, scale, roughness and mesh density, and the Internal Damage function creates deep breakage derived from the selected object and your parameters, down to near-complete breakage. Environment and VFX artists can build convincing wreckage quickly. Showing reinforcement inside the break makes damaged concrete read as real structure rather than a plain hole.

Key features

  • Damage depth, scale, roughness and density
  • Internal Damage generation
  • Procedural concrete and steel materials
  • Added reinforcement
