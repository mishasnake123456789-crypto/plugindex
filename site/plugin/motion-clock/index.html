What Motion Clock does

Motion Clock by PENCIL Park is a time tracker living in a panel within After Effects, Premiere Pro, Illustrator and Photoshop (versions without UXP). It records how long you spend on each project as you work.

Tracked time can be exported as a bar chart or as a one-click CSV report. Chameleon Mode matches the panel to the host's background, an Idle Time Alert with an adjustable delay can be switched on or off, the timer field is editable, and an archive stores past records. Freelancers and professionals who bill by the hour can see where the time went.

Key features

  • Real-time project hour tracking
  • Export as bar chart or CSV
  • Chameleon Mode matches host background
  • Idle Time Alert with adjustable delay
  • Archive of records
