What Digital Anarchy Light Wrap Fantastic does

Light Wrap Fantastic, from Digital Anarchy, is a compositing plugin that fakes backlighting on green or blue screen shots. Background colours bleed softly around the edge of the foreground subject, so the person or object looks as though it truly sits in the scene.

All adjustments live in one plugin, with several modes for different workflows and built-in masks that decide where the wrap appears or imitate light from a single direction. GPU rendering is optimised for Nvidia and AMD through CUDA and OpenCL. It pairs with keyers such as Keylight, UltraKey, Ultimatte and Primatte, and also finishes shots rotoscoped in Mocha Pro. Compositors can refine a key without extra node setups.

Key features

  • Wraps background light around the foreground edge
  • Reduces cut-out look on green and blue screen
  • Built-in masks for placement and directional light
  • CUDA and OpenCL GPU rendering
  • Works with Keylight, UltraKey, Ultimatte and Primatte
