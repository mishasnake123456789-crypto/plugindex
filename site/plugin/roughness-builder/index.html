Library of 500+ roughness textures for Blender, applied to a material's roughness channel with a click.

What Roughness Builder does

Roughness Builder ships with a large collection of roughness textures organized in categorized folders. You browse them with pagination and big thumbnails inside the 3D Viewport.

One click applies a texture, and auto-apply mode sends the selected one to the roughness channel immediately. Target the active material, the selected ones or every material on an object. Material artists wanting realistic surface variation can try many options quickly. Trying alternatives is fast, so surface finish decisions no longer require external texture libraries.

Key features

  • 500+ roughness textures
  • Categorized folders with thumbnail previews
  • One-click and auto-apply modes
  • Active, selected or all materials
