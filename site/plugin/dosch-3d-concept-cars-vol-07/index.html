What DOSCH 3D: Concept Cars Vol.07 does

Concept Cars Vol.07 adds 15 unbranded futuristic vehicles to the Dosch library. All are fully textured and delivered for Cinema 4D R15 through R24 along with many other file formats.

Scaling can be adapted, materials and textures changed, and colors altered, with your own images or labels applied. Elements may be switched on or off, faded and animated individually or as a whole. Because none carry a manufacturer identity, they work for architectural visualizations, simulations and renderings that need imaginative cars without brand concerns.

Key features

  • 15 non-branded concept cars
  • Cinema 4D R15-R24 and many formats
  • Editable scale, materials and colors
  • Own textures or labels
  • Switchable, transparent, animatable elements
