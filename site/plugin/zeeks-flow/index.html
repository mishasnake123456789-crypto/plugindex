What Zeeks Flow does

Zeeks Flow by Ezekiel-AA offers an interactive Bézier easing graph for adjusting the timing curves between F-Curve keyframes, making it easier to shape how motion accelerates and settles. It is listed under Animation.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
