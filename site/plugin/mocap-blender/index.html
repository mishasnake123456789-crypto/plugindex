What Mocap Blender does

Mocap Blender merges several motion capture files into a single new animation. Influence can be controlled at the bone level, and an inbuilt animation layer system keeps the work non-destructive.

Auto retargeting maps your armature to any mocap armature, automatic searching finds matching bones to cut setup time, and mirror naming helps apply capture data. Animators can mix walks, gestures and other clips into custom performances. Rather than overwriting data, the layers let you adjust and rebalance each source, which suits game and film animators building new performances from library clips.

Key features

  • Blend several mocap files together
  • Bone-level influence control
  • Auto retargeting and bone matching
  • Non-destructive animation layers
