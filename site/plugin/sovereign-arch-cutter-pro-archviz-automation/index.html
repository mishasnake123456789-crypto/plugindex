What Sovereign Arch-Cutter Pro (Archviz Automation) does

Sovereign Arch-Cutter Pro automates architectural cuts in Blender and prepares geometry for cleaner Boolean results.

Smart Wall Openings creates several openings at once with adjustable quantity, spacing and automatic height. Technical Door Cutouts use standardized dimensions, horizontal positioning and reliable deep cuts through complex walls. A U-Wall Generator builds custom U-shaped structures with adjustable wall thickness, base length and arm length. Archviz artists who model walls often can avoid repeating the same manual Boolean steps, which saves time on interiors, exteriors and floor plans with many openings.

Key features

  • Multiple wall openings at once
  • Standardized door cutouts
  • Reliable deep cuts through complex walls
  • U-Wall Generator
  • Geometry prepared for clean Booleans
