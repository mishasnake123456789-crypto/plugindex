What Time & Click Tracker - Break Time Reminder And Monitor Blender Activity In Real-Time does

Time & Click Tracker records your activity in Blender automatically. Tracking starts when you enable the add-on and measures active time to the second, displaying it in the N-panel as you work, even over long sessions and several Blender instances.

It also tallies mouse clicks and key presses, and notices idle periods, and break reminders encourage rest. Useful for monitoring productivity and ergonomics. The statistics can help freelancers estimate effort and anyone wanting healthier work habits notice when they have been working too long.

Key features

  • Second-accurate active time
  • Mouse click and key press counts
  • Idle detection
