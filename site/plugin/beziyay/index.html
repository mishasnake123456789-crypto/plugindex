What Beziyay does

Beziyay, from Shorts & Tales, visualizes the structure of a path in After Effects by building shape layers that show its bezier handles. It is aimed at creators who want to reveal how a design was constructed.

Vertices, tangents and handles each have styling controls, and it can also print readouts of vertex location, tangent angle and length. The output works well for behind-the-scenes case studies of typography, logos, data visualizations, icons and illustrations. It supports After Effects 2024, 2025 and 2026, and since version 1.1 it runs correctly in every interface language.

Key features

  • Builds shape layers showing bezier handles
  • Style controls for vertices, tangents and handles
  • Text readouts of position, angle and length
  • Works in After Effects 2024 to 2026
