Connects third-party transcription services to Premiere Pro, with translation and time-coded Word export.

What Transcript Converter does

Transcript Converter works with services such as ElevenLabs, Gladia, Deepgram, AssemblyAI and Sonix, feeding their output into Premiere's Text-Based Editing. Dialogue is transcribed and translated into over 100 languages in the panel.

One click exports a .docx table with timestamps and speaker names, and several JSON files can be processed at once. Info buttons show free quotas and rates, and OpenAI Whisper output works through local JSON conversion, as does Google Chirp. Helpful for editors in languages Premiere does not handle natively.

Key features

  • Integrates five transcription services
  • Transcribe and translate into 100+ languages
  • One-click Word export with timestamps and speakers
  • Batch JSON processing
  • Whisper and Google Chirp support
