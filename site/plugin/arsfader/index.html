What ARSfader does

ARS scripts's ARSfader is a script built for After Effects aimed at managing sound layers quickly. Normally volume and panning involve opening layer options or the effects panel, and this script puts the controls in one place.

Volume and panning can be adjusted on the fly, and the volume of all tracks can be changed together. Markers define fade-in and fade-out points, and buttons change volume and pan smoothly. It suits motion designers who need to balance music and effects within a composition without leaving their workflow.

Key features

  • Control audio tracks on the fly
  • Volume and panning without opening layer options
  • Change volume for all tracks at once
  • Marker-based fade in and fade out
  • Smooth volume and pan buttons
