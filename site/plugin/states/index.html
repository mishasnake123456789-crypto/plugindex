Stores property, animation and effect values as switchable states in After Effects.

What States does

States, by Hasan Atallah, captures values of properties, animations and effects as reusable presets for a layer or an entire composition. You can then flip between design variations without duplicating comps or saving countless project versions.

Unlike expression-based approaches, it avoids slowing down previews. Variations stay organized inside one project, which simplifies handling client revisions, and all states can be rendered at once. States can also be turned into a template for Mogrts, useful for motion designers delivering editable graphics.

Key features

  • Stores properties, animations and effects
  • Switch between composition states without duplicating
  • No performance hit from expressions
  • Render all states
  • Turn states into a Mogrt template
