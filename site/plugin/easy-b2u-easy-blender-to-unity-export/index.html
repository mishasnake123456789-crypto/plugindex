What Easy B2U - Easy Blender To Unity Export does

Easy B2U smooths the path from Blender to Unity. It exports through an FBX workflow with proper scale, URP material and shader support, batch export and LOD setup, and adds a Live Link feature.

It works with Blender 3.6 through 5.x. Indie developers, prop and environment artists, archviz creators and teams benefit from a consistent export every time instead of fixing scale and material issues in the engine. Notable points: correct scale in Unity, URP material support and batch export and LOD setup.

Key features
