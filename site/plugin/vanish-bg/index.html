What Vanish BG does

Vanish BG takes subjects out of their backgrounds right on the timeline. Rather than exporting frames for manual cutting in Photoshop, you process the clip inside the host app and get a lossless result in seconds.

It supports Premiere Pro and After Effects, versions 2020 through 2026, and a demo video is on the developer's page. Handy for quick composites and social content. It is a fast way to isolate people or objects for composites, overlays and social edits.

Key features
