What Blenquick Auto-Rigger does

Blenquick Auto-Rigger, or BAR, handles most steps of character rigging in Blender, including binding, marker detection, retargeting, rig-part duplication, generating the final rig and exporting. It supports humanoid, animal, vehicle and accessory rigs.

SNAPPY detects body markers automatically for humanoids in one click, and SNAPPY FACIAL does the same for faces. BINDY binds with a hybrid of voxelized and heat-map methods, and extra objects can be defined to improve facial binding. Animals have automatic body and facial marker setup with presets for birds, big cats and other species. Riggers and generalists wanting faster results will benefit.

Key features

  • One-click body and facial marker detection
  • Hybrid voxel and heat-map binding
  • Animal rig presets
  • Retargeting, rig duplication and export
  • Humanoid, animal, vehicle and accessory rigs
