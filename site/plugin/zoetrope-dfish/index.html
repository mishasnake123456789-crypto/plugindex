What Zoetrope DFISH does

Zoetrope DFISH straightens footage shot on fisheye lenses and action cameras. It removes barrel distortion and turns the curved image into a more natural wide-angle shot, and it works in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve.

Correction can be tuned separately for the inner and outer areas of the lens, and a horizon control helps when a camera was mounted off axis. A crop option hides stretched edge pixels on extreme lenses. Built-in presets cover common action cameras, and custom settings can be saved. Playback corrections render in realtime.

Key features

  • Separate inner and outer lens correction
  • Horizon control for off-axis mounting
  • Edge crop to hide stretching artifacts
  • Presets for popular action cameras and lenses
  • Realtime OpenGL-accelerated playback
