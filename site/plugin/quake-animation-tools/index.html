What Quake Animation Tools does

Quake Animation Tools from EdenWeby 3D combines four add-ons: Quake Motion Camera, Quick Easy Animate, Quake Handheld Camera and Sound React. Together they make a procedural kit for animating cameras, objects and lights.

Quake Motion Camera applies procedural movement in one tap, and camera tools include shake, a switcher and motion. More than 30 shake presets cover varied situations. Motion designers can speed up common animation tasks. Having these related tools bundled together keeps everything in one panel layout and reduces the effort of installing and learning them separately.

Key features

  • Four add-ons in one bundle
  • One-tap procedural camera motion
  • Camera shake, switcher and motion tools
  • 30+ camera shake presets
