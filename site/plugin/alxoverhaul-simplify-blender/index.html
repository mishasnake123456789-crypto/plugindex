What Alxoverhaul - Simplify Blender does

Alxoverhaul - Simplify Blender reduces menu hunting for modeling, weight painting and posing by gathering tools into custom panels and pie menus.

A Tab pie menu switches among Object, Edit, Sculpt, Pose and Weight Paint modes. The general panel groups armature pose position, isolation and overlay toggles. Tools include shape key transfer between models by nearest vertices, IK Symmetrize, a weight paint bucket tool, retopology aids that extract UV mapping and masks from seams, and left-click selection with a right-click tool. Keymaps are assignable.

Key features

  • Tab pie menu for mode switching
  • Shape key transfer regardless of topology
  • Rigging and weight paint helpers
  • Assignable keymaps and preference panel
