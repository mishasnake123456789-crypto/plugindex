Swaps the positions of two selected Blender objects, optionally keeping rotation or exchanging materials.

What Switcher Addon does

Switcher Addon exchanges the places of two selected objects with one button. It is a quick way to test which prop or part fits better in a spot.

A Keep Rotation option lets each object retain its own orientation or take on the other's, and material switching can be enabled, disabled or limited to swapping just the two materials. Material assign and delete tools are included as well. Layout artists rearranging scenes will save repetitive moving.

Key features

  • Swap positions of two objects
  • Keep Rotation option
  • Optional material switching
  • Assign and delete material tools
