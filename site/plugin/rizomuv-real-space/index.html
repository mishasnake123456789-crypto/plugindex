What RizomUV Real Space does

RizomUV Real Space is Rizom Lab's UV unwrapping software, aimed at professionals who produce physical, real-world products. It flattens models into non-overlapping maps while keeping real measurements, so layout scale and proportions match the original object.

Meshes with over a million polygons, including non-quad geometry, unwrap in seconds. Stretch analysis helps verify the quality of a layout. It is listed for Cinema 4D artists who need accurate, dimension-aware UVs, such as those working on product design, manufacturing visualization or print-oriented 3D assets.

Key features

  • Flattens million-polygon models in seconds
  • Preserves real-world dimensions
  • Handles complex meshes and non-quads
  • Stretch analysis of unwrapped maps
