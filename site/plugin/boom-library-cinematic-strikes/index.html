What BOOM Library Cinematic Strikes does

Cinematic Strikes is a BOOM Library collection of percussive sound effects for film and trailer work. As plain WAV files it can be used with Premiere Pro or any other editor.

It covers hits, flams, rolls, swells, and both single and ensemble drum sounds. The Construction Kit was recorded with three microphone positions and various drums and beaters, with control over attack and release. The Designed edition offers layered, processed sounds such as booms, whoosh hits, cracks and punches. A discounted bundle combines both editions. Files are 24-bit, 96 kHz for Windows and Mac.

Key features

  • Hits, flams, rolls, swells, singles and ensembles
  • Construction Kit with three mic positions
  • Designed edition with layered booms and cracks
  • Bundle combining both editions
  • 24-bit / 96 kHz WAV
