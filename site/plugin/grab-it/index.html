Blender add-on that lets animators attach objects to characters or other objects and release them later in three clicks.

What Grab-It does

Grab-It handles the common animation need of picking something up and putting it down. An object or bone is attached to another object at one point on the timeline and released at a later one, all in three clicks.

Bone Detection takes the bone you selected last when grabbing in object or pose mode, and markers show where grabs and drops occur. Character animators handling props such as cups, weapons or tools avoid manual constraint juggling.

Key features

  • Grab, Parent and Drop at chosen frames
  • Bone detection from last selected bone
  • Timeline markers for grab and drop
  • Three-click workflow
