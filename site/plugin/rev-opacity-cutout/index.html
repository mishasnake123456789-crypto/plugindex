What Rev Opacity Cutout does

REV Opacity Cutout generates geometry that follows the shape of a texture mask, with Unreal Engine 5 workflows in mind. It is useful for foliage, leaves and decals where overdraw from transparent cards is wasteful.

Planar mode makes quick cutouts for flat cards, while 3D modes preserve surface flow on curved or complex meshes. Quality presets and advanced contour cleanup and triangulation controls are provided, with Nanite-friendly output. In practice that means cutout geometry from texture masks and planar and 3D modes.

Key features

  • Cutout geometry from texture masks
  • Planar and 3D modes
  • Quality presets with contour cleanup
  • Nanite-friendly output
