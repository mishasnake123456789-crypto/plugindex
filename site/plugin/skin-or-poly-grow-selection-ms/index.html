What Skin_Or_Poly_Grow_Selection.ms does

Paul Hormis wrote Skin_Or_Poly_Grow_Selection.ms and published it on ScriptSpot. It expands the current selection when you are working in a Skin or Poly context, which saves repeated manual selection steps while weighting or editing.

It arrives as one .ms file, is listed at version 1 and works with 3ds Max 8, 7 and 6. A demo video is available, and the developer has a page at hyperent.com. It is relevant mostly to users of those older 3ds Max versions who want a quick grow operation in their toolset.
