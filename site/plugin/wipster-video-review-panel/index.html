What Wipster Video Review Panel does

The Wipster Video Review Panel puts review and approval inside After Effects and Premiere Pro. You send an edit to your team from the panel, and the encoding, uploading, sharing and feedback collation happen in the background.

When reviewers comment, their notes arrive as markers directly on your timeline, and team members can collaborate in real time. You can also copy yourself on review emails to confirm they were delivered. A Wipster account is required and a free trial is available. Editors who want fewer exports and email chains will find it streamlines approvals.
