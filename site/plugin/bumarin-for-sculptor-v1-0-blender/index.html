What Bumarin For Sculptor V1.0 - Blender does

Bumarin For Sculptor is a Blender environment by bumarin where 3D mesh is drawn with the Grease Pencil. Three pencils generate clay-like 3D pieces, each with its own character, and you combine them into a unified sculpture.

A block-in pencil assembles a sculpture gradually by piling up clay pieces, and a fiber generator converts a plain tube into a muscle bundle for anatomical work. It is a fit for sculpting, anatomy, and organic or fractal forms for artists who prefer drawing gestures to pushing vertices.

Key features

  • Draw 3D mesh with Grease Pencil
  • Three pencils generate clay pieces
  • Block-in pencil for stacking shapes
  • Fiber generator makes muscle bundles
  • For anatomy, organic and fractal modeling
