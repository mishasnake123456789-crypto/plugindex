What Turbo Charged Custom Vehicle Generator does

This add-on builds a fully customizable vehicle rig. Set wheel and body dimensions, suspension, horsepower, torque, brakes and steering sensitivity, and choose front, rear or all-wheel drive.

The finished vehicle can be driven with the keyboard in real time and the run recorded as animation. It is helpful for car chases, previs and game-style simulations without hand-rigging a vehicle. Day to day, that covers adjustable vehicle dimensions and suspension, horsepower, torque, brakes and steering and FWD, RWD and AWD.

Key features

  • Adjustable vehicle dimensions and suspension
  • Horsepower, torque, brakes and steering
  • FWD, RWD and AWD
  • Keyboard driving and recording
