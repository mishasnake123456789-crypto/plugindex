Blender add-on that copies Movie Clip lens distortion parameters onto a render camera.

What Sync LD does

Sync LD, by K_Naoki, passes the lens distortion parameters from a Movie Clip over to your render camera so that tracked footage and rendered elements agree. It is listed under Camera.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Syncs Movie Clip distortion
  • Applies parameters to the render camera
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later
  • Requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer
