What Plant Library does

Plant Library is a small Blender add-on from arsha 3D plant designer containing ten plant models. Thumbnails appear in a Plants panel, so you can browse visually and pick the one you want.

A click places the asset at the 3D cursor and leaves it selected. It works with objects or collections, which makes the library expandable, and the models are compatible with Cycles and Eevee. Artists decorating interiors or scenes can add greenery quickly.

Key features

  • Ten ready-to-use plant models
  • Thumbnail previews in the Plants panel
  • One-click placement at the 3D cursor
  • Expandable object or collection library
