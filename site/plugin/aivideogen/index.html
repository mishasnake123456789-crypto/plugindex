After Effects panel for generating text-to-video and image-to-video clips from Replicate models directly into your comp.

What aiVideoGen does

aiVideoGen by Clayton West connects After Effects to video models hosted on Replicate, including Kling, Seedance, Veo-3 Fast, MiniMax hailuo-02 and PixVerse. Both text-to-video and image-to-video generation run from one panel.

Finished clips download, save and drop into the active composition scaled and centered. A one-click stage snapshot can serve as an image reference, and some models accept first-frame and last-frame input from the timeline. Per-model options and a cost estimator help budgeting, while alerts flag blocked content and low credits.

Key features

  • Text-to-video and image-to-video from one panel
  • Clips auto-imported to the active comp
  • Stage snapshot as image reference
  • First and last frame input for select models
  • Per-model controls and cost estimator
