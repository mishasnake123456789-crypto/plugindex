What True Terrain 5 does

True Terrain 5 from True-VFX unifies the pieces needed to build natural environments in Blender. Terrain, water, scatter and materials work together from one sidebar panel.

Terrain can be created in a click from optimized presets, of which there are more than 150 covering shapes, materials, scatter setups and water types, plus over 220 assets such as trees, rocks, grass and flowers. A heightmap layer includes 80 or more heightmaps and accepts your own, and a noise layer shapes landscapes procedurally. Landscape artists and game environment designers build vistas fast.

Key features

  • One-click terrain from presets
  • 150+ presets and 220+ assets
  • Heightmap layer with 80+ maps
  • Procedural terrain noise layer
