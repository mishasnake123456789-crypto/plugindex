What Zbundle - Zcloth & Zsmoke does

ZBundle pairs two add-ons for simulation work. zCloth supplies an expanding collection of ready cloth setups to drag in and adjust, plus 2K, 4K and 8K fabric surfacing textures made for Eevee and Cycles.

zSmoke generates real-time smoke without relying on Blender's volume system. Together they help artists get cloth and smoke effects looking good quickly, without long simulation setups or heavy volumetric rendering. The two tools are designed to complement one another when a scene needs both fabric and atmosphere.

Key features

  • Pre-built cloth setups
  • Cloth textures in 2K, 4K and 8K
  • Designed for Eevee and Cycles
  • Real-time smoke without the volume system
