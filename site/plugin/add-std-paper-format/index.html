Blender add-on that creates planes sized to standard paper formats such as A4, US Letter, newspaper and billboard.

What Add Std Paper-Format does

Add Std Paper-Format creates plane objects with the exact dimensions of standard paper in Blender. Formats include European A, B and C series, US, Japanese and Chinese sizes, billboards, newspapers and books.

Custom formats can be defined with sliders or Blender's length units, and each object is named after its format. It supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer and needs no special permissions.

Key features

  • Planes in standard paper sizes
  • European, US, Japanese and Chinese series
  • Billboard, newspaper and book formats
  • Custom sizes via sliders or units
  • Objects named by format
