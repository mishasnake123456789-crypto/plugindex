What Blender Pro Hair Suite Addon does

Blender Pro Hair Suite is a hair and curve toolkit that converts hair cards into clean hair curves in one click. It copes with overlapping UVs, broken topology and unconventional layouts.

Auto root alignment uses custom vector targeting to correct root-to-tip orientation. Character artists can recover grooming from card-based hair assets and edit them as curves. In practice that means one-click hair card to hair curve conversion and handles overlapping UVs and broken topology.

Key features

  • One-click hair card to hair curve conversion
  • Handles overlapping UVs and broken topology
  • Auto root alignment with vector targeting
