What Fractal Noise Browser does

Fractal Noise Browser by synthetick gives After Effects users a visual library of more than 250 noise presets, sorted into nine categories. It supports both Fractal Noise, which is GPU-accelerated and loopable, and Turbulent Noise, according to your preference.

Thumbnails and animated previews make exploring easy, and a preset applies with a double-click, or a single click plus Apply. Randomize Seed produces instant variations with seamless looping. The panel is resizable, dockable and scrollable, and a user section stores your own presets.

Key features

  • 250+ presets in 9 categories
  • Works with Fractal Noise and Turbulent Noise
  • Thumbnails and animated previews
  • Randomize Seed with seamless looping
  • User Presets section
