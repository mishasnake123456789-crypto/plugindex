What Jiggle Physics does

Jiggle Physics gives rigs secondary motion in Blender. It is a fork of shteeve3d's Wiggle 2 add-on and uses a verlet solver that can handle anything from loose ropes to stiff rods.

Parameters run from zero to one, and the solver resists exploding. It performs reasonably in the viewport so you can tweak while an animation plays. Bone squash and stretch are supported, with collision against scaled empties and limited mesh collision. Overlays show the detected rest pose and simulation. Animators adding tails, ears or hair get natural follow-through.

Key features

  • Verlet jiggle solver with 0-1 parameters
  • Tweak while animation plays
  • Bone squash and stretch
  • Empty and limited mesh collision
