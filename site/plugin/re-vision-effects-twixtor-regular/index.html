What RE:Vision Effects Twixtor Regular does

Twixtor Regular generates in-between frames so footage can be slowed, sped up or converted between frame rates. It is available for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro, plus other supported hosts.

Speed is controlled with one parameter, and frame-by-frame control can be keyframed at any time. You can add or remove motion blur, mark cuts so interpolation doesn't smear across them, and process interlaced material sensibly. After Effects users can remap both audio and video through the standard time remapping tools. It is GPU accelerated, with universal Mac downloads covering Apple ARM.

Key features

  • Speed changes from one parameter
  • Frame rate conversion
  • Add or remove motion blur
  • Cut marking and interlaced footage handling
  • GPU accelerated; Apple ARM supported
