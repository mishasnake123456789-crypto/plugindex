What Bexar Studio - Professional Color Grading does

Bexar Studio brings a Lightroom-like grading workflow into Blender. Scene Mode grades Blender renders, and Photo Mode edits imported images, both without touching the compositor node tree.

Sliders cover exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, color balance, mood and atmosphere, so no nodes are needed. Cinematic preset looks are included, and results appear live in the viewport. It suits artists who want fast, photographer-friendly color control over their renders. Among its capabilities are scene and Photo editing modes, exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows controls and cinematic preset looks.

Key features

  • Scene and Photo editing modes
  • Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows controls
  • Cinematic preset looks
  • Real-time viewport grading
