What iZotope VEA does

VEA takes a voice recording and makes it sound more professional using technology drawn from iZotope's RX, Ozone and Nectar products. It works in DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro with only a few controls.

A shape control adapts the tone to the speaker, and Audiolens can capture a reference from any source so the voice can be compared to it and moved closer. Boost adds presence and power through compression and limiting, while Clean reduces noise without harming the voice. Vloggers, podcasters, interviewers and editors fixing uneven dialogue can improve audio quickly without learning a complex signal chain.

Key features

  • Shape control tailored to the voice
  • Audiolens reference matching
  • Boost for presence and level consistency
  • Clean background noise reduction
