What Extreme Pbr Nexus does

Extreme PBR Nexus is a material browser and blending system for Blender, compatible with Cycles and Eevee. More than 1800 materials are organized into libraries and categories, searchable by tag.

Any two materials can be painted between to create worn or layered surfaces, or blended along the Z axis from top or bottom, and a Shader Overlay mixes all scene materials together. FX layers add effects such as worn edges. Animated water and displacement based on native modifiers are included. Look-development artists and archviz users can dress scenes quickly.

Key features

  • 1800+ materials with tags and search
  • Texture painting between materials
  • Z-axis blending and Shader Overlay
  • FX layers for worn edges
  • Animated water and displacement
