What Video Player - Play Videos Inside Blender does

Video Player lets you watch tutorials or other videos without leaving Blender. Files open from a Playlist Panel, and several can be loaded at once.

Videos appear in a new resizable window, and scrolling the middle mouse wheel also resizes it. Built-in volume controls are provided. It is handy when learning from reference clips or tutorials alongside your work on a single screen. Keeping reference or tutorial footage in a separate window avoids switching between applications mid-task.

Key features

  • Playlist Panel with multi-file loading
  • Opens in a resizable window
  • Mouse wheel resizing
  • Built-in volume controls
