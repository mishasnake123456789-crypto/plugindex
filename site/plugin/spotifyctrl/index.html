What SpotifyCtrl does

SpotifyCtrl puts Spotify's playback controls inside After Effects, so you can skip tracks or pause music without switching windows during a long session. It only works with Spotify Premium accounts.

The first release is 1.0.0 and runs on After Effects 2023 through 2026. Animators and editors who like to work with background music will appreciate staying focused on the project. A demo video is available. That means fewer trips between windows during long animation or editing sessions, and a demo video is available to preview the panel.
