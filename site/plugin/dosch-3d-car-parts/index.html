What DOSCH 3D: Car Parts does

DOSCH 3D: Car Parts is a large library of 220 detailed, textured automotive components for Cinema 4D. It includes batteries, brake pads and disks, exhaust systems, jacks, springs, windshield wipers and many more.

Every part can be scaled, recolored and retextured, with room for your own labels. Elements can be switched on or off, faded through transparency and animated separately or together. Technical illustrators, workshop and parts-catalog designers, and advertisers can assemble exploded diagrams and product shots of components with little modeling effort.

Key features

  • 220 detailed textured car part models
  • Batteries, brake discs, exhausts, springs, wipers
  • Adjustable scale, materials and colors
  • Switchable elements with transparency
  • Animatable parts
