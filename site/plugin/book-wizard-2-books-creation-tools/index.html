What Book Wizard 2: Books Creation Tools does

Book Wizard 2 builds libraries in Blender, from a small stack to rows of full shelves. It has two scripts: a Books Generator and a Covers Generator, covering placement and appearance separately.

The Books Generator duplicates base book models across shelves with random dimensions, positions and small realistic deformations. The Covers Generator swaps textures on the supplied Cycles material to produce many cover combinations. More than twenty settings guide book creation. Interior designers, set dressers and architectural visualizers who need believable bookshelves can avoid modeling each volume.

Key features

  • Books Generator and Covers Generator scripts
  • Random dimensions, positions and deformations
  • Cover texture variations on a Cycles material
  • 20+ book creation settings
