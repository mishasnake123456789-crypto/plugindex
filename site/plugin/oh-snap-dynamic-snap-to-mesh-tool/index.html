What Oh Snap! Dynamic Snap To Mesh Tool does

Oh Snap! places meshes onto surfaces by snapping to face, edge or vertex. Pick up to ten meshes and the add-on chooses them at random, adding either linked instances or full copies, or restricting placement to a particular target mesh.

Random local rotation, scale and depth add variety. Draw mode snaps meshes as you drag the mouse across a surface, and Array mode places up to 30 snap meshes at once. It is well suited for dressing scenes with rocks, plants or clutter.

Key features

  • Up to 10 meshes chosen at random
  • Snap to face, edge or vertex
  • Random rotation, scale and depth
  • Draw and Array modes
