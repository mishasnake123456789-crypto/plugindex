What Pro Camera Tools For Blender - Realistic Cinematic Camera System does

Pro Camera Tools gathers cinematography features into a single Blender sidebar panel. You can create depth-of-field-ready cameras aligned to your view and choose sensor presets for Full Frame, APS-C, Micro Four Thirds and Super 35.

Exposure is controlled through aperture, ISO and EV compensation, and orbit, dolly and crane rigs handle movement. Render presets cover 4K, 2K, square, vertical and CinemaScope formats, handheld shake comes from procedural noise, and multiple cameras are supported. Filmmakers and archviz artists wanting photographic behavior from their cameras will find these controls straightforward.

Key features

  • DOF-ready cameras with sensor presets
  • Aperture, ISO and EV exposure control
  • Orbit, dolly and crane rigs
  • Render presets for 4K to CinemaScope
  • Procedural handheld shake
