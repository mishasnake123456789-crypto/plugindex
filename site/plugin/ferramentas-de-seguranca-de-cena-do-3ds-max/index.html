What Ferramentas de Segurança de Cena do 3ds Max does

Autodesk offers this scene security tool, listed here with a Portuguese title, as its recommended way to protect 3ds Max from known malicious scripts. It looks for CRP\ADSL, ALC, ALC2, PhysXPluginMfx, MSCPROP and variants in startup scripts and scene files, however they load.

It prevents 3ds Max from saving scenes damaged by those scripts, asks for confirmation before removing items, and runs in silent and other non-interactive scenarios such as command-line rendering and batch scripts. Actions are written to Max.log and the MAXScript listener. A Notify me about updates option, on by default, reports new versions on the Autodesk App Store.

Key features

  • Detects and removes known malicious scripts
  • Covers startup scripts and scene files
  • Confirmation prompt before removal
  • Works in silent and batch scenarios
  • Optional update notifications, on by default
