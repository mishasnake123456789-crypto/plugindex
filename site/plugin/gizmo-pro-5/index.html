What Gizmo Pro 5 does

Gizmo Pro 5 is a Blender addon from Max Derksen that supplies gizmos to reduce the number of clicks needed while editing. Supported modes are Object, Mesh, Curve, Surface, Armature and Pose.

Three 3D manipulators are provided: GizmoPRO, which is fully customizable, Luna, a classic design with moderate customization, and Malibu, a simplified one with essentials. Extra tools are Searcher, Camera controls, Scale, a 3D Cursor gizmo, Shear, Mirror and Placement, and modifier keys such as Shift and Ctrl assign extra operators to the move cone.

Key features

  • Three manipulators: GizmoPRO, Luna and Malibu
  • Covers object, mesh, curve, surface, armature and pose editing
  • Placement, Mirror, Shear and Scale tools
  • 3D Cursor and Camera gizmos
  • Modifier keys assign extra operators
