What CursorKit does

CursorKit, by Sebastian Moreno, helps show interface interactions in After Effects. It generates animated cursors so product and app demos look like real use without hand-animating every move.

Several cursor types are available, along with click and drag actions and an automatic typewriter effect. It works with After Effects from CC 2015 up to 2022. UI and UX motion designers building prototype videos or explainer clips can quickly add convincing pointer behaviour.

Key features

  • Multiple cursor types
  • Click and drag actions
  • Auto typewriter effect
  • For UI video prototypes
  • After Effects CC 2015 through 2022
