What BOOM Library Magic does

BOOM Library Magic is a sound effects bundle for spells and supernatural events, supplied as WAV files for use in Premiere Pro. It joins the Construction Kit and Designed editions into one package of 573 files, 3,176 sounds and 12.2 GB.

The Construction Kit has 442 files and 2,652 raw sounds for those who like to build effects themselves, and the Designed edition has 131 files and 524 finished sounds that are mixed and mastered. Spell types include fire, water, ice, acid, electricity and healing. Files are 24-bit, 96 kHz, with PDF and XLS content lists for browsing.

  • Construction Kit: 442 raw-source files
