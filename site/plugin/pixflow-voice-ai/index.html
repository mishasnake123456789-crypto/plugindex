What Pixflow Voice AI does

Pixflow Voice AI generates spoken narration from written text directly inside After Effects and Premiere Pro, and also works in Audition 22.0 and later. You write a script or a prompt, select a language and voice, and the plugin produces a natural-sounding voiceover.

There are 22 voice actors with different styles and tones, support for 29 languages, and control over the emotion in the delivery. All steps are handled within the editing software, and free tokens are included so you can try the voice generation.

Key features

  • 29 languages
  • 22 voice actors with varied styles
  • Voice emotion control
  • Script to voiceover inside the editor
  • Free tokens included
