What Quick Studio does

Quick Studio is a Blender add-on from Render Blue for preparing backgrounds, lighting and world settings in a single panel, with editable assets bundled in. It is organized into three sections: background, lighting and world.

There are 16 editable backgrounds with their own parameters and 16 materials built on 4K ambientCG.com textures (CC0). New spot, point and area lights automatically aim at an object and stay visible in the viewport and render. A panel lists all lights for direct selection and adjustment. It speeds up product shots and quick animation setups.

Key features

  • Background, lighting and world in one panel
  • 16 editable backgrounds
  • 16 materials with 4K CC0 textures
  • Auto-targeting spot, point and area lights
  • Panel listing all lights
