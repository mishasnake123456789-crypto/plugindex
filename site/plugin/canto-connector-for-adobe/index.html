What Canto Connector for Adobe does

Canto Connector for Adobe links Canto's digital asset management to After Effects and Premiere Pro, so teams can reach their central image library while they work. You can search for assets and preview them before use.

Selected images arrive as new layers or new files, with low or high-resolution previews usable in layouts. When work is finished, it can be returned to Canto either as a new version of a master or as a new asset. The connector also supports Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign, making it suitable for teams that already run Canto as their DAM.

Key features

  • Search and preview Canto library files
  • Pull images in as layers or new files
  • Low-res or hi-res previews in layouts
  • Send back as a new master version or new asset
  • Also works in Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign
