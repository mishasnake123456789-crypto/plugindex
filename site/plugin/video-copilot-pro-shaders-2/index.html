What Video Copilot Pro Shaders 2 does

Video Copilot Pro Shaders 2 provides a large set of surface materials for 3D work. More than 300 physical shaders mimic real material properties and are organized in 12 categories, including metal, plastic, stone, plaster, fabric and concrete.

All textures are fully tiled. Files are delivered in universal formats for Cinema 4D, 3ds Max, Maya and Element 3D, with a convenient installer for Cinema 4D users. The pack is built exclusively for Element 3D v2. Motion designers wanting realistic starting materials for titles and objects can use it without authoring textures.

  • 12 material categories
