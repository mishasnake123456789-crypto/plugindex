Blender add-on that makes display-ready paintings, photos and posters from images while keeping their proportions.

What Artgen does

ACP's Artgen helps fill Blender scenes with wall art. It builds correctly sized canvases from images, handy when you need many, and respects each source's original dimensions and ratio.

A tab in the 3D view's right-hand N-panel holds the controls. Generate creates the artwork and opens a configuration panel at the viewport's lower left. Interior designers and archviz artists decorating walls can place framed pieces quickly. Keeping proportions intact means photographs and posters never look stretched when hung in a scene.

Key features

  • Canvases from images
  • Preserves original dimensions and ratios
  • N-panel tab
  • Generate button with config panel
  • Handy for creating many artworks
