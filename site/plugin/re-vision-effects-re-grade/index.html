What RE:Vision Effects RE:Grade does

RE:Vision Effects RE:Grade is a color correction plugin for After Effects and Premiere Pro that rescues difficult footage. Automatic Contrast Enhancement targets uneven lighting, like shooting against a window, while multi-resolution processing balances global contrast, local contrast and sharpness without halos or clamping.

It works with Hald Clut files, including saving 3D LUTs and inverting them for consistency across applications. Exposure Fusion tone mapping gives an HDR-like look, and log curves can be adjusted. A mini color corrector with CDL controls, HLS limiters and color fringe reduction complete the toolset.

Key features

  • Automatic Contrast Enhancement for uneven light
  • Multi-resolution contrast without halos
  • Hald Clut support with 3D LUT saving and inversion
  • Exposure Fusion tone mapping and log handling
  • Mini corrector with CDL and fringe reduction
