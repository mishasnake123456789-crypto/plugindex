3ds Max script that cleans up the topology of 3D objects, with undo, faster processing and material ID preservation.

What Mesh Cleaner does

Mesh Cleaner tidies the topology of objects in 3ds Max and works on any 3D object, though triangulated curved shapes give weaker results. It is meant for models that arrive messy, such as imported or converted meshes.

Version 1.5 added an undo option and quicker processing. A much faster Explode function takes over from the earlier Detach Faces step. A single spinner sets vertex cleaning and normal angles for improved topology, and you may choose to keep face material IDs. Modelers can use it to prepare meshes before further editing, texturing or optimization.

Key features

  • Cleans topology on any 3D object
  • Undo option
  • Explode function replacing Detach Faces, much faster
  • Single spinner for vertex cleaning and normal angles
  • Option to reserve face material IDs
