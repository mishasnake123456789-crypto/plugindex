What AE Shapes Blender does

AE Shapes Blender by Zohaib Malik creates intermediate shapes between paths in After Effects. Two methods are provided: Blend Directly and Blend Along Spine, so you can morph in place or follow a curve.

Advanced properties include Stretch, Scale, Offset, Loop, Fill, Stroke, Opacity and Twist. The spine can be replaced, the blend frozen or reversed, and path tools match vertices, shift them, reverse paths and open or close them. Layers are organized into Calc, Spine, Blend and Master. Blends can be exported to SVG via Bodymovin's SMIL format.

Key features

  • Blend Directly and Blend Along Spine methods
  • Stretch, Scale, Offset, Loop, Twist and more
  • Spine replacement, freeze and reverse order
  • Match Vertices, Shift Vertex, Reverse and Open/Close Paths
  • SVG export via Bodymovin SMIL
