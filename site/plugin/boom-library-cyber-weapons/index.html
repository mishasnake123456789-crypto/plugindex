What BOOM Library Cyber Weapons does

BOOM Library Cyber Weapons covers futuristic and sci-fi weaponry, from sidearm-grade lasers up to huge destructive beams. It is delivered as WAV files for After Effects, Premiere Pro and other audio and video tools.

The Construction Kit holds 4,032 sounds in 672 files, including Foley, mechanics, synthesized beams, impacts, servos, shots, whooshes, tails and transients. The Designed collection offers 576 finished weapon sounds in small, medium and large versions. A bundle combines both at 9.2 GB. Samples are meant for layering and crossfading, in 24-bit, 96 kHz with PDF and XLS content lists.
