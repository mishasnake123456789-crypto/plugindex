What Logo Tracer does

Logo Tracer turns raster images into Mesh or Curve objects in Blender without SVG intermediates. Choose an image, check the preview, adjust and apply.

Output is dynamic, so results update as you adjust sample quality, threshold, smoothness and more, with no exporting or importing. Built-in optimization controls mesh complexity. Motion designers and 3D logo artists can quickly bring flat artwork into 3D. Skipping the export and import steps means you can fine tune the trace until it matches the source image closely.

Key features

  • Image to Mesh or Curve
  • No SVG required
  • Live results while tweaking
  • Sample quality, threshold and smoothness
  • Built-in optimization
