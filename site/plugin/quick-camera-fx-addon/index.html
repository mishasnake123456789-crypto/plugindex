What Quick Camera Fx Addon does

Quick Camera Fx Addon assembles a set of camera animation tools in Blender. Included are shake, look, zoom, tracking, camera switching, path following and 360 rendering for building shots quickly.

Camera Shake has adjustable amount, speed, angle and intensity. Camera Look and Camera Zoomer let you zoom, rotate and move with speed, direction and angle under your control, and Camera Track follows an object or character. Useful for animators setting up cinematic moves.

Key features

  • Adjustable camera shake
  • Look and zoom animators
  • Camera track follows objects
  • Path following, switching and 360 rendering
