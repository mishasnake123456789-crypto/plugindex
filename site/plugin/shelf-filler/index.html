Blender add-on that fills bookshelves with books and decor in one click, adapting to each compartment.

What Shelf Filler does

Shelf Filler dresses bookshelves in Blender automatically. It detects each compartment separately and fills it with books standing, leaning, tilted or piled flat, so a shelf looks lived in rather than stamped from a pattern.

Decorative objects get scattered between the books with irregular spacing. You can use your own book and decor models in any proportion and they are fitted and oriented to the space. A seed controls the randomness, so you can try variations or lock one in, and sliders in the sidebar adjust scale, gaps, density and lean. Archviz interiors, libraries and studies are obvious uses.

Key features

  • Detects and fills each shelf compartment individually
  • Standing, leaning, tilted and stacked book arrangements
  • Scatters decorative objects between books
  • Works with your own book and decor models
  • Seed-based randomization with density and lean sliders
