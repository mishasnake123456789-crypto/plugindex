What ARKit Blendshape Helper does

ARKit Blendshape Helper, by SummonAngelus, prepares a face mesh for Apple ARKit style facial animation. It adds the full set of 52 ARKit blendshapes together with 18 unmirrored extra shapekeys, which are intended for sculpting with symmetry before being divided into right and left halves.

Extra tools handle linking drivers, selecting meshes, mirroring and organizing shapekeys, trimming the busywork of rigging a character for face capture or lip sync workflows.

Key features

  • Adds the 52 ARKit blendshapes
  • 18 unmirrored extra shapekeys
  • Split shapes into left and right parts
  • Driver linking
  • Mirroring and shapekey organization
