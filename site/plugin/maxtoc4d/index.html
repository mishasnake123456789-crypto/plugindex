What MaxToC4D does

MaxToC4D by 3DtoAll transfers scenes from 3ds Max to Cinema 4D without manual exporting and rebuilding. Geometry and materials come across for several renderers, including V-Ray, Corona, Octane and Redshift.

Extra scene details such as output resolution and the environment's color, image and type are also carried over. It works with 3ds Max 2015 through 2026 and Cinema 4D R17 through 2026, and is run from a toolbar button or an integrated menu in Cinema 4D. It is available on Windows only. Studios moving projects between the two programs save considerable time.

Key features

  • Two-click scene transfer from 3ds Max
  • Supports V-Ray, Corona, Octane and Redshift
  • Carries resolution and environment settings
  • 3ds Max 2015-2026 and Cinema 4D R17-2026
  • Windows only
