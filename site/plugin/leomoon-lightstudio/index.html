What Leomoon Lightstudio does

LeoMoon LightStudio is a free, open-source way to light objects in Blender. Lights are added around a subject and grouped into profiles that can be switched with one click, which is useful for product renders where individual lights matter more than a fixed HDR.

Fifteen HDR light textures ship with it, and every light may choose its own. Options are available per light through the Light Node and can be animated. A 2D control panel maps to 3D positions, where a double-click toggles a light and a right-click solos it. It supports Cycles and EEVEE, exports lights as EXR, and imports or exports profiles.

Key features

  • Switchable light profiles
  • 15 HDR light textures
  • 2D control panel with 3D positioning
  • Cycles and EEVEE switch with EXR export
  • Per-light options that can be animated
