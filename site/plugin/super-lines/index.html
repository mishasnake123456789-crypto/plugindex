What Super Lines does

Super Lines, from Motion Design School, generates animated lines for After Effects. Everything is made from shape layers, so the results stay lightweight and editable, and shape modifiers are supported.

It offers two looks, cartoon and abstract, plus extra types: regular, inverted and both. Unlimited variations can be created, making it useful for transitions, lettering and speed lines that trail moving objects, with no keyframing of every line by hand.

Key features

  • Cartoon and abstract styles
  • Regular, invert and both types
  • Unlimited variations
  • Built entirely from shape layers
  • Shape modifier support
