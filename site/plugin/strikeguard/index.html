What StrikeGuard does

StrikeGuard checks your content for copyrighted material before it is published. The goal is to catch problem clips early, so creators avoid copyright strikes on their channels and the loss of monetization that can follow.

A scan finishes in seconds, which makes it practical to run on every project. Version 2.1.0 introduced a refreshed interface. Listings show support for Premiere Pro versions from 13.0 through 25.0, and the tool is free. Channel owners and editors delivering to video platforms are the people most likely to benefit.
