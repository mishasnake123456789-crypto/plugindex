What Mobilo Regular Typeface does

Mobilo Regular is the companion face to Mobilo Bold Animated, from the same Animography project that explores balance between randomness and a systematic approach in type design and animation.

It lets titles pair a calmer weight with the animated bold one, and it works with After Effects 2020 through 2024. Buying four or more Animography animated typefaces earns a 25 percent discount, and a demo video is posted on the developer's page.

Key features

  • Companion to Mobilo Bold Animated
  • Part of a randomness-versus-system type project
  • Supports After Effects 2020 to 2024
