What Product Rendering Tools, A Blender Addon That Makes Product Rendering Easy And Fast does

Product Rendering Tools from Gachoki Studios speeds up product visualization. Assets can be appended or linked from other blend files, or brought in from OBJ and FBX.

Images can be projected onto meshes as decals or labels, any mesh can become a shadow catcher for compositing, and images or animations are rendered from the add-on. A compact workflow for mockups and packshots. Bringing these steps together shortens the route from imported model to finished image for online shops and marketing.

Key features

  • Append, link or import assets
  • Project images as decals or labels
  • Shadow catcher conversion
  • Render images or animations
