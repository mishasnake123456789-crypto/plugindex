Blender add-on that sets up studio lighting and cameras for a model and exports to major 3D marketplace specs.

What Product Autouploader does

Product Autouploader prepares 3D models for sale. It builds a studio scene around the model and then exports in the formats and specifications that marketplaces ask for.

One-click Product Scene Setup creates eight camera angles, a Key, Fill and Rim lighting system, a shadow catcher ground plane and tidy scene organization. Lighting is tuned with Base Strength, Key, Fill and Rim sliders. Cameras include Main Studio, Turntable, Wireframe, Hero Angle, Detail Macro, Low Dramatic, High Beauty and Corner Dynamic, plus preset exports for six marketplaces.

Key features

  • One-click product scene setup
  • Eight camera angles
  • Key, Fill and Rim lighting with sliders
  • Shadow catcher ground plane
  • Preconfigured marketplace export settings
