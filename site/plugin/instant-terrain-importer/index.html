What Instant Terrain Importer does

Instant Terrain Importer speeds up landscape setup. One click brings in a terrain and sets displacement and subdivision automatically, with shading and texturing handled for you.

Twenty terrains in four types are included, along with a procedural PBR shader system offering snow, rock, desert and volcanic looks. Displacement is non-destructive, so you can adjust it afterward. Environment artists and background builders can place convincing ground quickly. Because the heavy setup is automated, landscape work can move straight to composition and lighting instead of wrestling with displacement settings.

Key features

  • One-click import with auto displacement
  • 20 included terrains in four types
  • Procedural PBR shaders: snow, rock, desert, volcano
  • Non-destructive displacement
  • Automatic subdivision and shading
