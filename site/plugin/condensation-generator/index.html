What Condensation Generator does

Condensation Generator adds realistic beads of water to objects in Blender with one click. It is particle based, aimed at product shots such as cold drinks, and can create over 100,000 droplets that do not intersect.

Droplet distribution is physically based with approachable controls, and vertex groups steer size and density so wet areas can be placed deliberately. It can also generate air bubbles inside glass, and an advanced interface is available for deeper tweaking.

Key features

  • Thousands of non-overlapping droplets in one click
  • Over 100,000 droplets possible
  • Vertex groups control size and density
  • Air bubbles inside glass
  • Advanced controls panel
