What CelWizard Frame by Frame Library does

CelWizard Frame by Frame Library is a pack of ready-made hand-animated elements for After Effects. It brings a cel-style look to motion graphics without requiring each animation to be drawn manually.

The collection holds over 320 editable pieces, including 51 looping elements, 52 morphing elements, 60 hand gestures, 121 isometric solids and 38 preview elements. It is compatible with After Effects 2022 through 2025. Motion designers can use it to add tactile, frame-by-frame personality to explainers, social posts and title sequences.
