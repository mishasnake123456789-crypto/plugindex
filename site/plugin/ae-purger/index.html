What AE Purger does

AE Purger is a small utility for After Effects 2025 and 2024 that frees up memory and disk cache without leaving the application. It was made by Renico van Wyk and is free.

One function clears the After Effects cache, and another purges RAM. Because clearing the cache removes previously rendered files, the tool shows an alert before it proceeds so nothing is lost by accident. Artists dealing with sluggish previews or memory pressure get a quick maintenance step.

Key features

  • Clears the After Effects cache
  • Purges RAM inside the app
  • Warns that rendered files will be deleted
  • Works with After Effects 2025 and 2024
