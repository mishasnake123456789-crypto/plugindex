What Dosch 3D: Space does

Space travel is the theme of this Dosch Design set of 20 completely textured models, supplied for Cinema 4D version 8.5 and up. Each one can be scaled, retextured, recolored and animated to fit a scene.

Materials, textures and colors are open to change, and you can apply your own images or labels. Individual elements can be turned on or off, or set to any transparency, and animated separately or as a whole. Educational animations, science-fiction illustration and advertising can take advantage of the models without custom modeling.

Key features

  • 20 fully textured space-travel models
  • Cinema 4D 8.5 and above
  • Editable scale, materials and colors
  • Elements switchable with transparency
  • Animatable individually or as a whole
