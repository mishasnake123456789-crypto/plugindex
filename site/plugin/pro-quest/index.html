What Pro-Quest does

Pro-Quest is a productivity suite for Blender that logs how long you actively work on each project, with smart idle detection. It can show your habits across sessions.

Quest Mode is an optional gamification layer where actions earn experience points, levels and tiered achievements, and daily, weekly and monthly quests are generated randomly. It adds motivation for freelancers and learners. It suits people who like to see how their time is spent and who benefit from a bit of game-like encouragement.

Key features

  • Automatic work time logging
  • Smart idle detection
  • Experience points and levels
  • Random daily, weekly and monthly quests
