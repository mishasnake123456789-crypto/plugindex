What AI Motion Blur does

AI Motion Blur from Blace Plugins generates blur from the movement it detects in your footage, rather than from a fixed shutter setting. It runs inside After Effects and Premiere Pro and is powered by blace.ai.

The 2025 model includes a speed versus quality selector, a Stop Processing button that cancels long calculations immediately, and a CUDA Memory Sharing option to speed up renders above 4K on CUDA hardware. It helps when footage was shot with a fast shutter or a clip lacks blur and looks choppy. Editors and VFX artists can smooth motion without re-shooting.

Key features

  • Blur derived from movement in the footage
  • Powered by blace.ai
  • Speed versus quality selector for the 2025 model
  • Stop Processing button
  • CUDA Memory Sharing for resolutions above 4K
