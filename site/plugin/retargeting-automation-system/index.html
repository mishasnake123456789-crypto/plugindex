What Retargeting Automation System does

Retargeting Automation System streamlines the remapping of animation with Auto-Rig Pro. It identifies rig types automatically and builds presets on the fly, so you spend less time configuring bone maps.

Single and batch processing are supported, with Manual, Auto (First) and Auto (Each) batch modes. Presets form as you choose FK or IK for arms and legs. A Limb Locker system addresses slipping or dancing feet in FK animation, and one-click tools handle rest poses and bone exclusion. Support periods are included at no extra cost.

Key features

  • Automatic rig type identification
  • Presets built on the fly via FK or IK choice
  • Single and batch retargeting modes
  • Limb Locker for foot slipping
  • One-click rest pose and bone exclusion
