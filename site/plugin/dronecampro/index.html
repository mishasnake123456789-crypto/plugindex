Blender add-on for flying a drone camera or driving vehicles by keyboard while recording the movement as animation.

What Dronecampro does

Dronecampro lets you control a built-in drone camera and other vehicles with the keyboard and record the motion straight into Blender animation.

Vehicle modes include cars, airplanes, helicopters, drones, rockets, ships and boats, each with tunable settings. Keys handle movement, rotation on X, Y and Z, and rotation resets, with numpad zoom and camera view and Shift and Ctrl to change speed. Recording can be started and stopped, which makes quick camera fly-throughs.

Key features

  • Built-in drone camera
  • Seven vehicle modes
  • Per-mode fine-tuning
  • Keyboard control of movement and rotation
  • Records animation directly
