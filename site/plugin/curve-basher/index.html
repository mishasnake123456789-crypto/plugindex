Blender add-on for generating curves and kitbashing along them, with wire generation and sci-fi cable presets.

What Curve Basher does

Curve Basher creates curves in Blender and lets you attach kitbash parts along their length. It includes curve generators, a Wire Generator, a preset browser and sci-fi cable and chain presets.

The Wire Generator produces large numbers of cables, offers a grid or array layout, and can sag them like hanging wires. Curvecast creates curves with auto-oriented handles by clicking on mesh surfaces, and bezier curves can be drawn on surfaces. The Curvebash Browser adds kitbash to curves and mesh edges by scrolling presets. Hard surface and environment artists dress scenes with cables and chains easily.

Key features

  • Wire Generator with grid mode and sag
  • Curvecast on mesh surfaces
  • Curvebash Browser presets
  • Sci-fi cable and chain presets
