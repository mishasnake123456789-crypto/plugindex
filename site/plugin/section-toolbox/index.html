What Section Toolbox does

Section Toolbox gives Blender parametric section boxes and planar sections. A one-click box wraps the current selection using its evaluated bounds, modifiers included.

Per-axis anchors pin faces while resizing, with live Width, Depth, Height, Move and Rotate controls. Outputs are versioned and exports to SVG and DXF are clean, with cut and projection lines that account for occlusion. Architects and product designers get drawings from 3D. Parametric controls let you revise the cut position later, and versioned results keep earlier drawings for comparison.

Key features

  • One-click section box from selection
  • Per-axis anchors
  • Versioned outputs
  • SVG and DXF 2D export
  • Occlusion-aware lines
