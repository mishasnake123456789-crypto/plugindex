Blender add-on that removes visible tiling repetition from image textures with one click using layered procedural blending.

What Anti-Tile does

Anti-Tile hides the obvious repeating pattern you see when a texture is tiled across a large surface. It deploys in one click on any image texture and can be removed just as easily with no leftover complexity.

Triple layer procedural blending provides individual control over each layer, and per-object randomisation varies mapping even among objects sharing a material. A same-object, same-mapping policy keeps normal maps lined up with diffuse maps. Environment and material artists working on floors, terrain and walls will appreciate more natural-looking surfaces.

Key features

  • Triple layer procedural blending
  • Per-object mapping randomisation
  • Normal maps stay aligned with diffuse
  • One-click deployment and removal
