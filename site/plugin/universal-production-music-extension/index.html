What Universal Production Music Extension does

The Universal Production Music Extension connects Premiere Pro to a library of production music. Search and filter the catalogue, explore themed playlists or your own, and import without leaving the project.

You can download tracks, Alt versions or stems in your chosen audio format, adjust volume from within the extension, and generate cue sheets directly. It suits editors who license music for film, TV and online video. Keeping search, import and paperwork in one panel shortens the usual hunt through separate library websites.

Key features

  • Search and filter the music catalogue
  • Themed and personal playlists
  • Tracks, Alt versions and stems
  • Choice of audio format
  • Cue sheet generation
