3ds Max modifier that fills mesh holes with triangles blended into surrounding geometry, with adjustable density and continuity.

What Tri Fill Pro does

Tri Fill Pro closes holes in 3ds Max meshes with triangles that blend into the surrounding surface. It takes polygon density into account, shifting smoothly between low-poly and high-poly regions, and the density of the fill can be adjusted.

Three continuity settings, Curvature, Tangent and Flat, set how the fill meets the mesh. It can repair broken geometry, and also support a fast workflow where you rough out a shape with triangles and later remesh into quads or retopologize with another tool. The modifier is by Marius Silaghi, and is useful for scans and sculpts that have gaps.

Key features

  • Fills holes with triangles blended into the mesh
  • Polygon density aware transitions
  • Adjustable triangle density
  • Curvature, Tangent and Flat continuity
  • Useful for repair and rough triangle modeling
