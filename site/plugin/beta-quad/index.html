Blender retopology tool that converts large n-gons into small quads that follow the border direction and original face shape.

What Beta Quad does

Beta Quad works on selected faces to retopologize them in Blender. It turns one large n-gon into many small quads with an original algorithm that respects the direction of the border.

It can operate from a single n-gon up to parts of a mesh or all faces, and the quads follow the shape of the original face. Retopology and hard-surface modelers cleaning up booleans and n-gons can get orderly quad flow quickly.

Key features

  • Converts n-gons to small quads
  • Original algorithm respecting border direction
  • Follows original face shape
  • Works on one face or the whole mesh
