What AEJuice Animated Markers does

Animated Markers gives video editors 300 hand-animated marker graphics to place over footage, such as highlights, scribbles and callouts. Each is delivered as a frame-by-frame MOV file with a transparent background at 4000 by 4000 pixels.

They can be recoloured and scaled individually, which helps them fit different projects, and are popular in music videos. Works with After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and other editors. The Pack Manager adds fast preview, drag-and-drop import and favorites.

Key features

  • 300 frame-by-frame animated markers
  • MOV with transparent background
  • 4000x4000 resolution
  • Colour and scale adjustment per marker
  • Pack Manager with favorites
