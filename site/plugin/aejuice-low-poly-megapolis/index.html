What AEJuice Low Poly Megapolis does

AEJuice Low Poly Megapolis is a ready-made set for assembling stylized cities. It has more than 100 objects, among them 57 buildings, 14 city cars, airport elements, road elements and landscape, adding up to roughly 3.5 million polygons.

No plug-ins are needed. Scenes were created in Cinema 4D and are render-ready with Physical Sky, and files are supplied as .c4d, .obj, .fbx and .3ds so other 3D editors can use them. Motion designers and 3D artists can build aerial shots, maps or explainer backdrops without modeling every block.

Key features

  • 100+ low poly objects
  • 57 buildings, 14 cars, airport and road elements
  • About 3.5 million polygons in total
  • Formats: .c4d, .obj, .fbx, .3ds
  • No plug-ins required
