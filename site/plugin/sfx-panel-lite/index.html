What SFX Panel Lite does

SFX Panel Lite is the free edition of SFX Panel Pro. You register the sound effects you use most, organize them in the panel, and place any of them at the playhead with a single click.

Placement can also be applied to several selected clips in one undo step. Each effect remembers its own volume in decibels and frame offset, imported sounds are trimmed to remove leading silence, and a preview plays in the panel. Favorites, usage-based sorting, drag-and-drop ordering and filtering keep large lists manageable. The interface is available in English and Japanese, chosen automatically.

Key features

  • One-click placement at the playhead
  • Batch placement on selected clips, one undo
  • Per-effect volume and frame offset
  • Trim on import removes leading silence
  • Favorites, sorting and filtering
