3ds Max script by MastaMan that inserts Great Catalog 3D models into the scene with a single Download button.

What GC Merger does

GC Merger connects 3ds Max to the Great Catalog site so models can be placed directly in a scene. Pressing Download in the script makes the model appear within seconds, with no manual downloading or unzipping.

Catalog browsing, a Favorites list and a view of your collection are all available inside the 3ds Max window. Models arrive with correct names for the model, materials and textures, and include all the textures and assets they need. The author stresses that it adds no useless layers, helpers or lights, and that scenes do not slow down after insertion. Visualization artists building interiors will save time.

Key features

  • Insert catalog models with one Download button
  • Browse and keep Favorites inside 3ds Max
  • Models, materials and textures with correct names
  • All required textures and assets included
  • No extra layers, helpers or lights added
