What GTR2 / rFactor GMT Importer does

GTR2 / rFactor GMT Importer, by haunetal1990, imports GMT model files from GTR2 and the first rFactor, with support for bringing in several files at once. It is listed under Import-Export.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
