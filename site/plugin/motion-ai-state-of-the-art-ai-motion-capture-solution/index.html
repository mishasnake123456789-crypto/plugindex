What Motion Ai: State-Of-The-Art Ai Motion Capture Solution does

Lianheng Tech's Motion AI adds AI-based motion capture to Blender, organized around three key workflows meant to make capture easier for animators.

Its capture workflow includes pose capture for raw human armatures and faces. It is distributed as a Blender extension, with different capture algorithm tiers across editions and professional team support on one edition. Documentation is included. Animators wanting AI-driven capture inside Blender can use it. Different editions suit hobbyists and teams, so you can start small and move up if needed.
