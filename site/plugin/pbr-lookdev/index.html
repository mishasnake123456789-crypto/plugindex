Builds stacked PBR materials in Blender with masks, overlays and UDIM handling, aimed at Substance Painter users.

What PBR Lookdev does

PBR Lookdev by BHiMAX stacks multiple shader layers into one material with blend and mask controls. It targets artists who paint textures in Substance Painter and complete the lookdev, rendering and presentation stages in Blender.

Layers can use any of five shader types: Principled BSDF, Glass, Emission, Diffuse or Subsurface Scattering. Masks, overlays and UDIM textures are handled as well, giving a controlled way to build complex surfaces without wiring large node trees manually.

Key features

  • Layered PBR shader system
  • Blend and mask controls
  • Principled, Glass, Emission, Diffuse and SSS layers
  • Overlays and UDIM support
