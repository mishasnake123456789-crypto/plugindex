What Curveslice Pro does

CurveSlice Pro offers the interactive curve cutting known from ZBrush inside Blender. Draw a line across a mesh, see what will be removed, and cut with a single keystroke, without boolean setups or manually created curves.

There are three cut modes. Trim discards one side, Slice divides the mesh into two objects, and Intersect retains just one side's geometry. A red overlay previews exactly what will go before you commit, and points are placed interactively by clicking. Sculptors and modelers gain a fast way to carve shapes.

Key features

  • Draw a cut line across any mesh
  • Trim, Slice and Intersect modes
  • Live red-overlay preview
  • Cut with a single keystroke
  • No boolean or curve setup required
