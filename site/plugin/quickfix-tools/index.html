Fixes and optimizes Blender scenes with mesh simplification, shader cleanup, a scene analyzer and one-click FBX or OBJ export.

What Quickfix Tools does

Quickfix Tools brings several repair and delivery steps together. A Smart Mesh Simplifier offers Decimate, Voxel Remesh and Quadriflow modes and detects high-poly meshes automatically.

Other tools cover shader cleanup, scene analysis and render optimization, and one-click export writes FBX or OBJ to an automatically safe folder with optional ZIP and no overwrite errors. It is meant for artists preparing scenes for sharing or engines. Bundling these steps helps when a scene must be slimmed down and handed over without opening several separate tools.

Key features

  • Smart Mesh Simplifier: Decimate, Voxel Remesh, Quadriflow
  • Shader cleanup
  • Scene analyzer
  • Render optimization
  • One-click FBX/OBJ export with auto ZIP
