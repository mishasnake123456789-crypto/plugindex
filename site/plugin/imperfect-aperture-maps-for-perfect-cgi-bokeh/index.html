What Imperfect Aperture Maps For Perfect Cgi Bokeh does

This pack gives Blender cameras more photographic bokeh through 95 high-resolution aperture maps modeled on real lenses. Some include dirt and chromatic aberration, and shapes cover real-world apertures, Lens Baby and Neptune art lenses, and invented ones.

A Blender camera rig is included: swap the map in the image texture node, adjust the Bokeh Camera aperture and set the Focal Point. The maps also suit Cycles, V-Ray, Corona, Octane, Arnold, Frischluft Lenscare and DOF Pro. Documentation is included.

Key features

  • 95 high-resolution aperture maps
  • Dirt and chromatic aberration variants
  • Ready camera rig for custom bokeh
  • Works with many render engines
