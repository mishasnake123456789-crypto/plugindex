What TextFlex does

TextFlex, by Lotfi Jilal, is a text animation tool for After Effects. Instead of building animators and keyframes manually, you choose whether text is split into characters, words or lines, and the animation is generated for you.

There are three modes: In, In & Out, and Out only. A text blur property is available, a checkbox enables per-character 3D from the panel, and presets can be saved and deleted. One Animate button handles both applying and resetting. It speeds up title and caption work where many text layers need similar movement.

Key features

  • Animate by characters, words or lines
  • In, In & Out and Out modes
  • Text blur property
  • Per-character 3D checkbox
  • Preset support
