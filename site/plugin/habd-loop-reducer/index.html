What HABD Loop Reducer does

HABD Loop Reducer by Habdorn changes how many segments make up tubes and profile shapes, helping reduce or reshape the loops along them. It is listed under Mesh.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.
