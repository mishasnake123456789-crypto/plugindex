What Cappuccino - Animated Typeface does

Cappuccino is an animated display typeface for After Effects from Animography. Its geometric animations feel almost liquid, yet foamy, giving titles a fun, bold and energetic character.

It works with After Effects 2020 through 2026, includes pseudo controls and the Animography Controller, and is optimized for Font Manager. Version 1.5 adds new characters, updated kerning and metrics, bug fixes and optimizations. It is a good choice for lively intros, packaging graphics and social video headlines.

Key features

  • Animated display typeface with liquid-like geometry
  • Pseudo controls
  • Includes the Animography Controller
  • Optimized for Font Manager
  • Version 1.5 adds characters and kerning updates
