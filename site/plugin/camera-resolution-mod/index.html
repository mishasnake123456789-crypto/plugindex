What Camera Resolution Mod does

Camera Resolution Mod, made by BodyulCG, lets you store a separate render resolution on each camera in a 3ds Max scene. When you switch cameras, the matching resolution is applied instead of being reset by hand.

Presets can be renamed and updated, and a Move Time Slider option is available. The modifier works with 3ds Max versions from 2010 through 2024. It is handy when one scene contains cameras for different outputs such as wide stills, vertical crops and animation shots, saving repeated changes in the Render Setup dialog.

Key features

  • Per-camera render resolution
  • Renameable, updatable presets
  • Move Time Slider option
  • Works with 3ds Max 2010 through 2024
