What Pixel Lab Octane Lighting Essentials does

Pixel Lab Octane Lighting Essentials is a set of 20 lighting studios made for Octane in Cinema 4D. Each comes with a full Xpresso rig so lights and colors can be customized.

Scenes cover product visualization, dramatic and cinematic looks, portraits, cars and jewelry. Volumetric options such as the foggy Ray of God scene and a moody fog setup add atmosphere. Artists wanting reliable studio lighting for client renders can load one of these setups and fine-tune it, rather than starting with an empty scene.

Key features

  • 20 customizable Octane lighting studios
  • Xpresso rig in every studio
  • Product, cinematic, portrait, car and jewelry scenes
  • Volumetric fog and Ray of God options
