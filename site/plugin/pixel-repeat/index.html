What Pixel Repeat does

Pixel Repeat, created by Max van Leeuwen, is an After Effects plugin that repeats pixels to produce glitch art, streaks and other unusual visuals. The repeated pixels stretch across the frame in a stylized way.

A Visualization mode, which now supports transparency, helps preview the effect. Controls include Threshold, Override and Include Interior options. It is compatible with After Effects 2024 and 2025. Artists looking for a fast route to distorted, smeared looks can apply it to footage or graphics.

Key features

  • Loops pixels for glitch and streak looks
  • Visualization mode with transparency
  • Threshold control
  • Override and Include Interior options
  • Works with After Effects 2024 and 2025
