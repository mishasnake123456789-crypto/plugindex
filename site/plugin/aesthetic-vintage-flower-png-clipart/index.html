What Aesthetic Vintage Flower PNG Clipart does

Aesthetic Vintage Flower PNG Clipart is a collection from Graphic Spirit that supplies ready-made floral artwork for After Effects and Premiere Pro projects, as well as other Adobe apps. It saves designers from drawing or cutting out botanical elements themselves.

The pack includes 80 isolated vintage flowers in PNG, ranging from 1700 to 3900 pixels, 14 seamless PNG patterns at 4000 by 4000 pixels, and 10 seamless vintage JPG backgrounds at the same size. It suits posters, graphic designs and printable t-shirts, and is listed as compatible with After Effects 15.0 and Premiere Pro 12.0 or newer.

Key features
