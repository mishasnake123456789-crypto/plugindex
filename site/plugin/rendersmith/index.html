What RenderSmith does

RenderSmith gathers the chores around rendering in After Effects into a single panel. Large projects tend to pile up proxy files, pre-comps and backup copies, and this tool keeps them under control from one spot.

A guard feature spots compositions containing plugins that cannot render normally, such as Element 3D, and flips them to the internal renderer. Extra utilities include Deep Clean, Comp Doctor, Ghost Mode and Safe Mode. Project saves during a render retry through brief hiccups rather than stopping the job. It supports After Effects from 2022 through 2026, and suits artists with heavy, multi-stage comps.

Key features

  • Single panel for proxies, pre-renders and finals
  • Automatic backups
  • Switches incompatible comps to the internal renderer
  • Comp Doctor and Deep Clean utilities
  • Supports After Effects 2022 to 2026
