3D transition plugin that builds particle and lighting animations out of your own clips.

What FxFactory Block Party does

Block Party creates transitions between clips by rebuilding them as animated 3D geometry mixed with particles and lighting. It is available for After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and uses the footage itself as the source material.

Fifty presets are included as starting points, and the particle system is highly customizable through lighting and camera effects. No keyframes are required, since changes made to parameters are applied by the plugin directly. Camera settings include motion blur, depth of field blur and field of view. Metal acceleration allows real-time setup and rendering.

Key features

  • 3D particle and lighting transitions
  • Geometry built from your source clips
  • 50 built-in presets
  • No keyframes needed
  • Camera motion blur, depth of field, Metal acceleration
