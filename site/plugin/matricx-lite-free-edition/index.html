What Matricx Lite - Free Edition does

MatricX Lite is the free version of a procedural material tool for Blender. Hard-surface materials are applied straight from the N-Panel, so you can finish a surface without unwrapping the model.

You browse presets, adjust live settings and apply instantly, and selecting an object loads its material controls into the panel automatically. The textures are packed inside the add-on, so there are no external files to manage. It is a quick way to give mechanical and sci-fi models convincing surfaces.

Key features

  • Procedural hard-surface materials from the N-Panel
  • Preset browsing with instant apply
  • Controls load automatically on selection
  • Textures packed in the add-on
  • No UV unwrapping required
