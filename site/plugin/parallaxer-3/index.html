What Parallaxer 3 does

Parallaxer 3 by Cream-FX builds 2.5D parallax moves from layered artwork in After Effects. A single action converts the file into a 3D composition and applies dynamic layer scaling, so depth looks right as the camera moves.

This removes the manual job of enabling 3D, spacing layers and compensating for scale. It works with After Effects from CC up to the 2026 release. Designers and editors who want to animate illustrations, photos cut into layers or flat scenes with a sense of depth can reach a convincing result fast.

Key features

  • One-click 3D parallax animation
  • Converts layered files into 3D compositions
  • Dynamic layer scaling
  • After Effects CC through 2026
