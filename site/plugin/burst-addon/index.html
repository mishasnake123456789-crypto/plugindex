What Burst Addon does

Burst Addon, from EdenWeby 3D, generates burst effects in Blender that can be used as splashes, ground explosions, snow bursts or flying paper. It ships with 12 presets and all settings can be customized.

Place a burst anywhere in the scene, add smoke by hand, and custom debris can be made from stones, snow, paper, water drops or any other object you supply. Documentation is included, making it a quick way to add impact moments to shots.

Key features

  • Twelve included presets
  • Fully customizable settings
  • Placeable anywhere in the scene
  • Custom debris from your own objects
  • Manual smoke option
