What Section Pro - Floor Plans, Elevations & Technical Drawings does

Ruben Messerschmidt's Section Pro lets architects and designers pull plans, elevations and sections out of 3D models in Blender. Section planes at any angle cut through objects, collections, instances and geometry nodes output.

Section fills can carry colors per object, collection or material. With a click it makes a 2D drawing as an edge mesh with the cut outline, projected silhouettes and hidden lines on another layer. Export goes to DXF and SVG for CAD and layout programs.

Key features

  • Section cuts at any angle with multiple planes
  • Fill colors by object, collection or material
  • One-click 2D technical drawings
  • Hidden edges on a separate layer
  • DXF and SVG export
