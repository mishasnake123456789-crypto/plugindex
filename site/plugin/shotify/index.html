What Shotify does

Shotify supports shot management for editorial and VFX handoffs. It creates shot placeholders and labels, renumbers clips, and exports thumbnails or movie references.

Numbering is configurable through scene number, first shot, increment, shot padding and shot count fields, with cut-in frame number and frame rate settings. It works with Premiere Pro 2023 through 2026. It is aimed at teams handing shot lists and references from editorial to visual effects vendors.

Key features

  • Shot placeholders and labels
  • Renumber clips
  • Export thumbnails or movie references
  • Scene, increment and padding numbering fields
  • Premiere Pro 2023 to 2026
