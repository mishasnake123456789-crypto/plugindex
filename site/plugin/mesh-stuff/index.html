A Blender edit-mode add-on with Distribute, Rotate, Plane to Plane and Mirror tools plus guide lines.

What Mesh Stuff does

Mesh Stuff gathers four modeling helpers in the side panel of Blender's edit mode. They simplify common array, alignment and symmetry tasks, and guide lines support precise placement.

Distribute works like an edit-mode array, spacing two selected mesh objects evenly along a straight line, with the count adjustable afterwards. Rotate builds a circular array around a face or edge chosen as the center. Plane to Plane orients meshes by matching selected faces, carrying linked geometry along with adjustable orientation, while Mirror reflects a mesh across a plane. Hard-surface modelers will find them handy for kitbashing.

Key features

  • Distribute meshes evenly on a line
  • Circular array around a face or edge
  • Plane to Plane face orientation
  • Mirror across a plane
  • Guide lines
