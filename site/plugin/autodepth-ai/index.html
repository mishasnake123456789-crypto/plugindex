Blender add-on that makes depth maps from images using AI services and turns them into displaced 3D models.

What Autodepth Ai does

AutoDepth AI generates depth height maps from pictures in Blender by calling online AI services. It then applies displacement or builds 3D models from the maps, and can also make images from text prompts.

Maps arrive in roughly five seconds at higher resolution. A one-click local installation sets up Stable Diffusion and ControlNet. Concept artists and illustrators can turn flat artwork into relief models and 3D backgrounds quickly. Depth maps can be generated from artwork or photos, making it a quick way to prototype relief models and parallax backgrounds.

Key features

  • AI depth map generation from images
  • Text-to-image inside the add-on
  • Displacement and 3D model generation
  • Local Stable Diffusion and ControlNet install
