What Smart Decal - One-Click Decal Projection & Surface Placement does

Smart Decal places decals on any object by detecting the surface under your mouse and aligning to it automatically. This avoids manual rotation and positioning for labels, scratches and markings.

Load a decal library, choose from a preview list, or import decal maps from your own files with the Import Decal button. Materials are assigned automatically on import, snapping is supported, and the workflow is non-destructive. More than 100 ready-made decals are included, generated with AI. Hard surface artists are the main audience.

Key features

  • Raycast placement aligned to the surface
  • Preview list from a decal library
  • Import your own decal maps
  • Automatic material assignment
  • Over 100 decals included
