What ShapeShifter does

ShapeShifter is a Blender add-on that works procedurally: built-in textures drive how the mesh is generated and deformed, live. It is aimed at artists who want complex organic forms without sculpting or modeling every detail.

Because the shapes respond to texture settings, you can explore many variations quickly and arrive at unique results. It runs with Cycles and Eevee alike and supports Blender 3.0 to 5.0. It is released under the GPL license, and documentation is included to help new users get started.

Key features

  • Real-time procedural mesh generation and deformation
  • Built-in procedural textures drive the shapes
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
  • Works with Blender 3.0 through 5.0
  • GPL licensed, documentation included
