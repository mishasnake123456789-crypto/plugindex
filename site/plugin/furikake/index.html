A lightweight particle generator for After Effects with multi-core rendering and Multi-Frame Rendering support.

What Furikake does

Furikake generates particles inside After Effects with an engine optimized for multiple CPU cores, so very large particle counts remain practical. It is meant for motion graphics, VFX and game-style effects.

Emitter shapes include points, boxes, spheres and grids, and direction and speed are adjustable. Particles may be basic spheres, static or animated 2D textures, or 3D textures, and size, opacity and colour can change through each particle's life. Gravity, wind, bounce and turbulence add physical behaviour, while a Child Particle System creates secondary particles. After Effects lights drive shading and shadows, with depth of field and 32-bit colour.

Key features

  • Many emitter shapes with direction and speed control
  • Sphere, 2D, animated and 3D texture particles
  • Gravity, wind, bounce and turbulence
  • Child Particle System for secondary particles
  • Multi-core engine with MFR and AE light support
