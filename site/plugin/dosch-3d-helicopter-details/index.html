What Dosch 3D: Helicopter Details does

Dosch 3D: Helicopter Details gives Cinema 4D users a fully textured helicopter delivered with all the important details of a real machine. It is made to be reshaped to suit a project: the scale can be changed, and materials, textures and colors adapted, including your own textures or labels.

Individual elements can be hidden or faded by transparency, and they can be animated separately or the whole helicopter can be. Visualization artists, ad designers and animators can use it for rescue scenes, transport illustrations or technical presentations.

Key features

  • Fully textured helicopter with key details
  • Adjustable scale
  • Editable materials, colors and labels
  • Elements hidden or transparent
  • Animate parts or the whole model
