What Time Bend does

Time Bend from Satori is a more flexible alternative to After Effects' Time Displacement effect, available in After Effects and Premiere Pro. It uses a map to decide which frame each pixel shows, producing time-warping looks and offers more control over the frame sequence.

Channel modes include Luma, RGB, or one of red, green, blue or alpha. Five blend modes combine source and destination, and curve shapes cover linear ramp, triangle and three sine phases with phase, floor and a graph. Destination Blend Size and Offset govern transitions, loops are supported, time mapping can be animated via curve phase, and a sample projects pack is included.

Key features

  • Time Displacement alternative
  • Luma, RGB and single channel modes
  • Five blend modes
  • Curve shapes with phase, floor and graph
  • Loop support and sample projects pack
