Disconnects Blender nodes by shaking them, a Houdini-inspired gesture, or by pressing E.

What Shake Cut does

Shake Cut brings a fast node disconnect gesture to the Blender Node Editor. Grab a selected node and shake it to detach or dissolve its connections.

Several selected nodes can be disconnected as a group while keeping the links between them, and E gives a manual disconnect. Artists who rearrange big node graphs often will save clicks. It is a small tool, but one that speeds up reorganizing large shader or geometry node trees.

Key features

  • Shake to detach a node
  • Multi-node group disconnect
  • Preserves links within the group
  • E key for manual disconnect
