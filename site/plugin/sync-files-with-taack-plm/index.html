What Sync Files With Taack PLM does

Sync Files With Taack PLM, by Adrien-Guichard, connects Blender to Taack PLM so files can be uploaded and versioned from within the program. It is categorised under Workflow.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.
