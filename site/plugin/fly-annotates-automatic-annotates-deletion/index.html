Automatically removes Blender annotation strokes with delete, fade, expand and disintegration animations.

What Fly Annotates - Automatic Annotates Deletion does

Fly Annotates deals with annotations that would otherwise have to be erased by hand. Choose a method in the N panel and it keeps handling everything you draw until you stop it.

Instant Delete removes strokes the moment you finish, Fade Away uses a delay value, Expand Fade grows and dissolves them with an expanding factor, and Stroke Disintegration and Double Sided Disintegration break them apart. Handy for live demos, tutorials and sketching over a viewport during presentations.

Key features

  • Instant Delete method
  • Fade Away with delay value
  • Expand Fade with expanding factor
  • Stroke and Double Sided Disintegration
