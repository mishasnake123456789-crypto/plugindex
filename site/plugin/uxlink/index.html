What UXLink does

UXLink bridges Figma and After Effects. Designs move from Figma to After Effects in two clicks, and layouts can return from After Effects to Figma.

Group masks and Figma motion are supported, along with components, component instances and multi-style text. Effects such as drop shadow and layer blur transfer too. UI designers animating product screens avoid rebuilding artwork by hand, keeping layouts consistent between design and motion work.

Key features

  • Figma to After Effects in two clicks
  • After Effects to Figma transfer
  • Group masks and Figma motion
  • Components and multi-style text
  • Drop Shadow and Layer Blur
