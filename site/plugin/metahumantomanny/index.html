Blender add-on that tidies MetaHuman meshes and rigs for binding to the Unreal Engine 5 Manny skeleton.

What MetahumanToManny does

MetahumanToManny prepares MetaHuman characters for use with the UE5 Manny skeleton. It cleans up vertex groups, seams and weights, sets up LOD hierarchy, and generates IK bones.

Convert Skeleton To Manny handles face bone weight cleanup and seam fixes on a mesh and its LODs, then removes bones outside the keep list. Generate IK Bones adds Manny's non-deforming IK and auxiliary bones. Further tools fix twist bone names, finger bulges and toes, delete unused groups, merge duplicate seam vertices, parent LODs to a LodGroup empty and bind to Manny.

Key features

  • Converts skeleton to Manny
  • Generates UE5 IK and auxiliary bones
  • Vertex group and face weight cleanup
  • Seam fixing for clean rebinding
  • LOD hierarchy setup for Unreal
