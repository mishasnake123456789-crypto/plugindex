What FTW3DForge Image Projector does

FTW3DForge Image Projector places images onto 3D surfaces in Blender. It loads an image as a plane sized from its DPI, projects it onto a target mesh by ray casting and creates the UV mapping for you.

A live preview updates as the plane moves. When happy, bake to texture at the resolution you choose and export PNG, JPG, TIFF or EXR. It copes with subdivision modifiers and keeps UV corners intact, follows geometry nodes output on the target, and respects metric and imperial unit settings.

Key features

  • Image plane import sized by DPI
  • Ray-cast projection with automatic UVs
  • Live preview while moving the plane
  • Bake to PNG, JPG, TIFF or EXR
  • Works with subdivision and geometry nodes
