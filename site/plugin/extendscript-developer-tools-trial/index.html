What ExtendScript Developer Tools Trial does

This is the trial edition of ExtendScript Developer Tools by Vakago Tools, aimed at people who write scripts for Adobe software. It lets developers try the feature set before they buy the full version, though with reduced functionality.

The trial expires after 15 days and appears as its own entry under Window > Extensions, labelled TRIAL. It requires After Effects 18.0 or Premiere Pro 15.0 or newer, and is also compatible with Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign and Audition.
