What Retime: An Animation Scale That Doesn't Suck does

Retime is a Blender plugin that changes animation timing in frames instead of with a scale percentage. When you want a move to take longer or shorter, it shifts everything that comes after it, so the rest of the animation stays tight and your markers still line up.

Editing works while the animation is playing and does not depend on where the playhead sits. Because timing is expressed in whole frames, adjustments are predictable, and customization options let you tune how it behaves. Animators who constantly tweak pacing will appreciate not having to rebuild their timeline afterwards.

Key features

  • Frame-based retiming instead of scale percentage
  • Shifts following items to keep animation tight
  • Works during playback
  • Keeps markers useful
