What Creation Effects: Pulse does

Creation Effects: Pulse is a template for building animations driven by sound. Its 30 visualiser presets span looks like a flamethrower, inkblot, kaleidoscope, pinwheel, swarming shapes, dot-and-line networks and 3D particles.

Fifteen use the Audio Spectrum effect to follow frequencies, and fifteen respond to loudness through motion, scale, colour and speed. Five audio-driven footage effects: 3D Push, Crystal Dream, Glitch and Time Stutter. Each preset has simple controls, and a copy-and-paste expression can link any property to your audio. HD and UHD comps are included, and it works in any language version. Ideal for lyric videos, VJ loops and music reels.

Key features

  • 30 audio visualiser presets
  • 15 spectrum-driven and 15 loudness-driven
  • 5 audio-driven footage effects
  • Copy-and-paste expression for any property
  • HD and UHD compositions
