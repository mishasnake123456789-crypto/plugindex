What Render Preset does

Render Preset, by tonton_, lets you store render configurations and apply them again whenever you need them, which is handy when you switch between draft and final settings. It is listed under Render and Pipeline.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
