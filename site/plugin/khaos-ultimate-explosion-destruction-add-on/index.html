What Khaos: Ultimate Explosion/Destruction Add-On does

Khaos delivers explosion and destruction effects in Blender, supplied in two flavors. KHAOS Detonator builds procedural mesh explosions, while KHAOS Legacy offers smoke, debris particles and rigid body fracture tools.

Detonator relies on mesh emitters you can shape, with visual aids that speed up building and tuning a blast. Legacy adds fire, smoke and dirt particle systems in a few clicks and has tools to fracture, constrain and destroy objects. A 3D asset database is included too. VFX artists creating blasts, collapses and debris can work faster.

Key features

  • KHAOS Detonator procedural explosions
  • Customizable mesh emitters
  • Fire, smoke and dirt particle systems
  • Rigid body fracture and destruction tools
