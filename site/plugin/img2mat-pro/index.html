What img2mat_pro does

Img2Mat_Pro extracts color palettes from any image and produces Blender materials, swatches, labels, palettes and assets in a single click.

Pantone/PMS matching works through your own licensed Adobe Color Book files, notable-color sampling picks key hues, and annotated callout boards support fabrication. Product designers and visualizers who need accurate color references can work faster. The annotated boards help communicate material choices to manufacturers or clients, which is useful for design visualization and product development.

Key features

  • Palette extraction from any image
  • One-click materials and swatches
  • PMS matching via your own .acb files
  • Annotated callout boards
