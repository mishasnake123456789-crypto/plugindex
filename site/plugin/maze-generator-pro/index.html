What Maze Generator Pro does

Maze Generator Pro creates three-dimensional mazes inside Blender and aims at playable environments rather than simple wall layouts. Several generation algorithms and themes cover dungeons, sci-fi corridors and garden mazes.

Smart rooms, enemy boss spawn points, dynamic lighting and rigid body physics can be included, and presets produce results quickly. Game developers, level designers and 3D artists can use it to prototype layouts or build production-ready maze scenes. It works with Blender 4.2 LTS and newer and needs no special permissions.

Key features

  • Multiple maze algorithms
  • Dungeon, sci-fi and garden themes
  • Smart rooms and boss spawn points
  • Dynamic lighting and rigid body physics
  • Quick presets
