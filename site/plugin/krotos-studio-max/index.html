What Krotos Studio Max does

Krotos Studio Max is a sound design tool that works as a standalone app and plugin; you can drag results into Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and other hosts. You perform sounds and music in real time, then place them on the timeline.

It comes with the full Krotos Sound Library of over 36,000 effects plus Max-exclusive expansions, hundreds of presets in the main categories, and hundreds of automatable parameters. Your own sounds can be added and custom presets saved and recalled. Output is multi-channel with royalty-free effects and music, and new sounds arrive monthly. Useful in film and TV post-production, game audio and content creation.

Key features

  • 36,000+ sound effects plus Max-only expansions
  • Hundreds of presets
  • Perform sounds and drag them to the timeline
  • Add your own sounds and save custom presets
  • Multi-channel output with monthly library updates
