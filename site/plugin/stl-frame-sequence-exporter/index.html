What STL Frame Sequence Exporter does

STL Frame Sequence Exporter, by James-Hogle, writes an animated mesh out as a sequence of STL files, one per frame. It is tagged under Import-Export and Animation.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
