Blender add-on for passing props between a character's hands without editing constraints or keys.

What PropHandoff does

PropHandoff, by augustin-gourves, handles the moment a character hands an object from one hand to another, without you having to touch constraints or keyframes yourself. It is tagged under Animation and Rigging.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Move props between hands
  • No manual constraints or keys
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later
  • Requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer
