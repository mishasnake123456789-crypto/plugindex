What Zoetrope Z COLR does

Zoetrope Z COLR is a set of four generators that create subtle animated color backgrounds. They suit title cards, slides, compositing elements and lighting looks, and run through FxFactory in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve.

Z Breath pulses a vignette-like gradient, Z Bars meanders five chosen colors as vertical bars, Z CMYK applies an oscillating CMYK filter over gradients, and Z Fields blends a soft five-color field. Presets include well-known palettes, you can save your own, and the color picker works with resources such as Adobe Kuler.

Key features

  • Z Breath pulsing two-color gradient
  • Z Bars with five-color palettes
  • Z CMYK oscillating filter over gradients
  • Z Fields soft five-color blends
  • Preset system and integrated color picker
