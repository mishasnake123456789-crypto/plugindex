What AEJuice Cosmic Animated Alphabet does

Cosmic Animated Alphabet is a set of animated characters for titles and kinetic type. It contains a full 26-letter alphabet plus ten numbers and ten symbols, all pre-animated and each animated individually and made entirely from After Effects layers.

Colours and animation can be selected and customized per element. It works through the AEJuice Pack Manager plugin in After Effects and Premiere Pro, with fast preview, drag-and-drop import and favorites, and with DaVinci Resolve as well. Typical uses include intros, title sequences, tutorials and information videos where words are spelled out letter by letter.

Key features

  • Full alphabet plus 10 numbers and 10 symbols
  • Each character animated individually
  • Made from After Effects layers
  • Customizable colours and animation
  • Pack Manager preview and favorites
