Blender add-on for reframing equirectangular 360 degree photos with a camera, multiple projections and LUT export.

What 360 PhotoLab does

360 PhotoLab is an open-source Blender add-on that reframes equirectangular panoramas using a camera. Loading a source image switches to Rendered mode and Camera View automatically.

Three projection modes are available (RET, CIL and CLV), with a comparison preview, and framing controls for yaw, pitch or vertical shift, roll and horizontal field of view come with fine-tune buttons. A .cube LUT can be applied for correction and export goes to PNG, TIFF or JPEG. Also included are resolution presets, print-size estimates and careful seam handling at plus or minus 180 degrees. Good for drone panoramas, virtual tours and landscapes.

Key features

  • Camera-based reframing of 360 photos
  • RET, CIL and CLV projection modes
  • Yaw, pitch, roll and field of view controls
  • Optional .cube LUT and multi-format export
  • Seam handling at the 180 degree edge
