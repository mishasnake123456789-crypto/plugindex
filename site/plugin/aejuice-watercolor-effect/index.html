What AEJuice Watercolor Effect does

AEJuice Watercolor Effect converts photos into watercolor-style artwork and animates the result in After Effects. The workflow combines Photoshop and After Effects and offers four animation categories.

Reveal is a ten-second animation that appears to paint the design as you watch. Freeze-frame grabs one frame and makes a watercolor design with text, useful for titles and call outs. Infinite Slideshow builds an unlimited slideshow from your designs or blank slides, and Logo Sting produces a logo sting in minutes.

Key features

  • Reveal animation of about ten seconds
  • Freeze-frame watercolor with text
  • Infinite Slideshow with unlimited slides
  • Logo Sting builder
  • Photoshop and After Effects workflow
