What Pixel Galactic does

Pixel Galactic is a GPU-accelerated plugin for After Effects and Premiere Pro from Pixel Sorter Studio. It reacts to motion in a clip and converts it into soft trails, stuttering echoes and fluid pixel warping.

It works in both host apps and supports Adobe releases from 2020 through 2026, including Media Encoder for exports. Motion designers and video artists can use it for music visuals, transitions and stylized shots where they want footage to smear and ripple instead of staying clean.

Key features

  • GPU-accelerated processing
  • Dreamy motion trails
  • Glitchy echo effects
  • Flowing pixel distortions
  • Supports Adobe 2020 through 2026 and Media Encoder
