What Quick Asset Manager does

Quick Asset Manager, by Clonephaze, manages the asset files stored on your machine from within Blender, covering creation, editing, organizing and deletion. It is listed under User Interface and System.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
