What Camera Flow Pro does

Camera Flow Pro is a camera rig for Blender built on a slow parent, damped motion approach. Even rough or jerky input is smoothed, producing the fluid movement of a gimbal or a skilled operator.

A real-time preview matches the baked outcome, with a Cache Cam toggle, and the workflow is non-destructive because baking makes a copy of the camera with automatic naming. One button handles tracking, aiming and following, with adjustable speed and smoothness that can be toggled and keyframed. Filmmakers, motion designers and archviz artists who want cinematic camera moves will find it useful.

Key features

  • Slow parent damped motion for smooth moves
  • Real-time preview that matches the bake
  • Non-destructive bake to a camera copy
  • One-button tracking, aiming and following
  • Adjustable speed and smoothness
