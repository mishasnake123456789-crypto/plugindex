What Picturedesk for Adobe Creative Cloud does

Picturedesk for Adobe Creative Cloud connects the IDS PictureDesk archive to Premiere Pro. Millions of licensable images from more than a hundred photo suppliers can be browsed, covering celebrity, news, editorial and creative material.

AI-driven Visual Search finds matches, including from an image you already have. Lightboxes hold collections, either your own or shared by colleagues, files open directly in Adobe apps, edits can be uploaded and linked back to originals, and video clips can be trimmed before download. Good for editorial and publishing teams.

Key features

  • Search millions of licensable images
  • AI-driven Visual Search
  • Lightboxes for collections
  • Open files directly in Adobe apps
  • Trim video clips before download
