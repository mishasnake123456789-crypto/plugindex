What Hugly does

Hugly works in two directions on expression code. In one direction it scrambles expressions into compact, unreadable text, a simple way to protect rigs and templates you distribute. In the other, it reformats expressions into a tidy, readable form.

That makes it useful for template sellers guarding their work as well as for artists who want to pass clean code to teammates. It is a YB Motion release, version 1.0, compatible with After Effects CC 2019 through 2026, and a demo video is available.

Key features

  • Uglifies expressions into unreadable code
  • Cleans expressions into readable form
  • Protect your work or share it clearly
  • Supports After Effects CC 2019 to 2026
  • Demo video available
