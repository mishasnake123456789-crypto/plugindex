Adds adjustable noise-based shake, wiggle or wobble to objects and cameras in Blender with one click.

What Shakemaker does

ShakeMaker applies camera shake and object jitter through noise modifiers, controlled from the N-panel in the 3D viewport. One click is enough to make an object or camera shake, wiggle, oscillate, vibrate or wobble.

Scale, strength, depth and phase are adjustable, and a random-settings option gives varied results quickly. The Advanced Shake Control panel opens up separate X, Y and Z axis settings, and the shake can be limited to a frame range. Turning off random phase yields clean oscillation. Presets can be saved, and common camera options sit in their own panel.

Key features

  • Noise modifiers with scale, strength, depth, phase
  • Per-axis X, Y and Z control
  • Limit shake to a frame range
  • Built-in and custom presets
  • Dedicated camera settings panel
