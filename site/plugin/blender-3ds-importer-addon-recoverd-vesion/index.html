What Blender 3Ds Importer Addon Recoverd Vesion does

This add-on restores support for the older .3ds format in Blender. It reads files without Autodesk 3ds Max installed, helping those who have old libraries of models and want them in Blender with one click.

It converts quads to triangles without otherwise changing the topology, and handles 3ds files from versions between 2008 and 2020. The newer format can also be loaded, but errors such as exploded drawing and missing textures from DOS mode may appear. It works with Blender 2.9 and later. Archivists and artists updating legacy assets will find it useful.

Key features

  • Single-click .3ds import
  • No Autodesk 3ds Max required
  • Quads converted to triangles
  • Supports files from 2008 to 2020
  • Blender 2.9 and later
