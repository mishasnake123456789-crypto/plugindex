What Creation Effects: 3D Flag does

Creation Effects: 3D Flag is a template for making flags blow in the wind inside After Effects. Any image, text or video can become the fabric, complete with 3D ripples, and it hangs from a 3D flagpole topped by a metal sphere with shaking ropes.

Three flag resolutions are provided up to 4000x2000, along with nine transparent 2D flagpole PNGs, seven fabric textures or your own, five wind sound effects and 25 country flags. It needs After Effects 17.0 (2020) or later on PC or Mac, uses no plugins and works in any language. Artists making patriotic spots, sports intros or news graphics can produce flags quickly.

Key features

  • Three flag resolutions up to 4000x2000
  • 3D flagpole with metal sphere and moving ropes
  • Seven fabric textures or custom image
  • Five wind sound effects
  • 25 country flags included
