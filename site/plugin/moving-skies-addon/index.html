What Moving Skies Addon does

Moving Skies Addon creates skies that actually move in Blender. Clouds are animated, adding time and life to cinematic shots, game environments and architectural visualizations.

Seven presets cover different sky types and cloud compositions, and you can build your own with the customizable settings. Lighting is adjustable, and HDR images are supported for illumination. Documentation is included, from EdenWeby 3D.

Key features
