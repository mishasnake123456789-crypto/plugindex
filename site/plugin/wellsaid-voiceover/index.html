What WellSaid Voiceover does

WellSaid Voiceover connects Premiere Pro to WellSaid Studio, so AI-generated narration appears directly in the edit. Clips are synced automatically, and sections and takes from Studio can be sent over.

The service offers hundreds of voice styles, quick retakes, emphasis control, and cues and pronunciation tools, making it possible to revise narration without a recording session. It is meant for editors who already use WellSaid Studio. It suits editors who produce narration for explainers, training and marketing videos and want to avoid recording sessions.

Key features

  • Direct access to WellSaid Studio clips
  • Automatic clip syncing
  • Real-time AI voiceover generation
  • Retakes, emphasis and pronunciation tools
  • Hundreds of voice styles
