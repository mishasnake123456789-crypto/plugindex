What Noise Industries Photo Montage does

Photo Montage from Noise Industries automates slideshow production in After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve. Select your photos and the plug-in assembles the montage with no keyframes, using over a hundred presets.

AI-driven pan and zoom finds prominent regions in each picture for natural movement, and a different transition can be assigned to each photo. You set a duration per photo and the effect handles the rest. Wide Gamut HDR support, Metal GPU acceleration, motion blur and formats including JPEG, PNG, PSD, OpenEXR and HEIC are supported. Great for event recaps and family videos.
