What Better Fbx Importer & Exporter does

Better FBX Importer & Exporter relies on Autodesk's official FBX SDK to move assets between Blender and other software. It supports every FBX version and imports FBX, DAE, OBJ, DXF and 3DS, with export to FBX, DAE, OBJ and DXF.

Mesh LODs can be exported for Unreal Engine and Unity, PBR materials are imported from 3ds Max and Maya, and PBR export targets Unity, Unreal and Godot. N-gons, smoothing groups and bone orientation are taken care of automatically, and both Cycles and Eevee are supported, with drag-and-drop and collection export. Pipeline TDs and game artists benefit.

Key features

  • Imports FBX, DAE, OBJ, DXF and 3DS
  • Exports FBX, DAE, OBJ and DXF
  • LOD export for Unreal and Unity
  • PBR material transfer with Max and Maya
  • Automatic bone orientation
