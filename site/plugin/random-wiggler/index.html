What Random Wiggler does

Random Wiggler brings the feel of After Effects' random() and wiggle() into Premiere Pro. Leyero's plugin offers camera movement, smooth wiggle and flicker looks.

Frequency and amplitude are adjustable, along with an octaves multiplier, and since version 1.1.0 you can animate amplitude and frequency over time. Editors can add handheld motion or flicker without leaving the timeline. It is practical for handheld feels, shaky titles and flickering lights where an editor wants randomness that stays adjustable.

Key features

  • Camera movement effect
  • Smooth wiggle
  • Flickering
  • Frequency, amplitude and octaves controls
  • Animatable amplitude and frequency
