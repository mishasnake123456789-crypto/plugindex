What VRML2 Exporter does

VRML2 Exporter, by thebreat, writes mesh objects out in the VRML2 format, keeping their colors and textures, which suits older viewers and pipelines that still read VRML. It is listed under Import-Export.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features
