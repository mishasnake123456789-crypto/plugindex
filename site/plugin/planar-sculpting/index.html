What Planar Sculpting does

Planar Sculpting by clo1to5 brings back a sculpting behavior that was dropped in Blender 4.5: the Scrape/Fill brush combined with Original Normal for planar results. It is aimed at surface scraping tasks such as carving rocks.

It works with Blender's regular brush size and strength shortcuts, F and Shift+F, and holding Alt swaps between its modes. Sculptors who preferred the earlier workflow can keep using it in newer versions.

Key features

  • Replicates old Scrape/Fill with Original Normal
  • Uses default brush size and strength shortcuts
  • Alt key swaps behavior
  • Suited to rock and surface scraping
