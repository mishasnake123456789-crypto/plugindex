What Arnold does

Arnold is used in film and animation. It ships with 3ds Max and is available for Cinema 4D as the C4DtoA plugin. It handles hair, volumes and complex geometry well.

FAQ

Arnold is sold as a subscription. Current plans and trial details are on the Autodesk website.

Arnold works with 3ds Max and Cinema 4D.

Who develops Arnold?
