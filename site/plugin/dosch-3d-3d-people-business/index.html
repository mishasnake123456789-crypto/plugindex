What DOSCH 3D: 3D-People - Business does

DOSCH 3D: 3D-People - Business offers 120 fully textured human models dressed as business people, posed in many ways. The figures load readily into Autodesk 3ds Max and depict office workers realistically.

They are intended to populate scenes like lobbies, meeting rooms, conference spaces and city streets. Architectural visualizers and presentation designers can use them to give a sense of scale and activity without hiring a character artist or photographing cutouts.
