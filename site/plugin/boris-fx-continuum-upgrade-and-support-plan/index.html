What Boris FX Continuum - Upgrade and Support Plan does

This plan is for existing Boris FX Continuum owners who want to keep receiving new versions and technical help. It covers releases such as Continuum 2022 and 2022.5 for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro.

Those releases brought Particle Illusion fluid dynamics with new presets and Library Scenes, Multi-Frame Rendering on over 350 effects, native Apple M1 support and more GPU-accelerated effects. Colour work gained film stock grades and digital gobo presets, blur gained Smear Blur and a new Prism filter, and Mocha tracking reached more than 100 effects. 2022.5 added GPU transitions and Particle Illusion 3D models via Cinema 4D import.
