What Pixel Lab Aura II: Professional 3D Environments does

Pixel Lab Aura II is a pack of 10 ready-made 3D environments built for Octane Render in Cinema 4D. They serve as backplates for animations or product shots and include a catwalk show, forest, sci-fi tunnel, hangar and broadcast studio.

Each studio has an Xpresso rig with sliders to change lights, temperature, color, glows and other properties. Lighting and texturing are already dialed in, delivered at high resolution. Motion designers and product visualizers get polished sets without modeling and lighting them from scratch.
