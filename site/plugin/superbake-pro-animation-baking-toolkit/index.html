Blender toolkit that bakes parenting, constraints and drivers down to plain keyframes via a GLTF round trip.

What Superbake Pro - Animation Baking Toolkit does

Animation that relies on constraints, drivers or parent chains often falls apart outside Blender. Superbake Pro converts it all to ordinary keyframes in a single operation, using a GLTF-based bake and export path.

Parent-child structures survive, every constraint type and driver-controlled motion is baked, and camera, light and empty animation is kept even when those are parented. Pick selected objects, visible objects or the whole scene; it switches to the scene automatically when nothing is selected. Handy for moving animation to game engines and other software.

Key features

  • Bakes constraints and drivers into keyframes
  • Keeps parent-child hierarchies
  • Preserves camera, light and empty animation
  • Selected, visible or full-scene modes
  • GLTF-based bake and export
