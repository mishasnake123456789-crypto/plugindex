What Tissue does

Tissue offers computational design tools for Blender meshes. Created by Alessandro Zomparelli, it was among the add-ons bundled with Blender 4.1 and is now supplied as it stands with limited support.

The download is about 111 KB, needs no special permissions and is released under GPL v2 or later. It supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. Designers, architects and generative artists exploring patterns, tessellations and algorithmic forms can use it as a base for parametric work.
