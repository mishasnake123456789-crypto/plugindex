Blender add-on that automatically reveals the selected object in the Outliner when clicked in the 3D Viewport.

What Auto-Highlight In Outliner does

Auto-Highlight In Outliner removes a tedious step in Blender: normally you must press a button to locate a selected object in the Outliner. With this add-on, clicking in the 3D Viewport expands the Outliner to the object automatically.

A toggle collapses all other collections, multiple Outliners and separate windows are supported, and it works with left-click and right-click selection. An option shows the real object rather than its grayed-out parented duplicate. Artists with large scenes can keep track of their hierarchy at a glance.

Key features

  • Auto-expands Outliner on selection
  • Collapse Other Collection toggle
  • Multiple Outliners and windows supported
  • Left and right click select
