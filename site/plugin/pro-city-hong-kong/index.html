What Pro City - Hong Kong does

Pro City - Hong Kong generates procedural cityscapes based on Hong Kong's dense architecture. It relies on geometry nodes to produce skyscrapers and urban buildings.

Results render in Cycles and Eevee. It is Blender Extension compatible, GPL-licensed and includes documentation. It suits skylines, backgrounds and night city scenes. Day to day, that covers geometry Nodes city generation, hong Kong-style skyscrapers and cycles and Eevee rendering. Also on board: extension compatible.
