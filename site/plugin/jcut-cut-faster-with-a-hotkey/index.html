What JCut - Cut faster with a hotkey does

JCut is a Blender add-on from ArtVit aimed at making mesh cuts quicker. The idea is to trigger the cut with a hotkey rather than moving through menus, so it fits modelers who cut a lot. It is tagged under Mesh and Modeling.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
