Blender add-on for real-time fracture generation with editable cuts and adjustable resolution, exporting to RBDLab.

What Fracturelab does

FractureLab creates fractures in Blender with accuracy and instant feedback. You can design exact cut patterns or generate many detailed fractures, then edit, adjust or remove cuts whenever you wish.

Fracture resolution can be raised or lowered, new cuts can be generated automatically from initial fractures, and a damage module handles surfaces like stone walls and cobblestone paths. Results export directly to RBDLab for simulation. It comes from BeFX Studios and suits destruction and fragmentation effects.

Key features

  • Real-time fracture generation
  • Exact cut patterns, editable at any time
  • Adjustable fracture resolution
  • Damage module for stone and cobblestone
  • Direct export to RBDLab
