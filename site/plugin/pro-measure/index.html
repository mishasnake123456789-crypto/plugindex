Blender add-on for measuring dimensions, distances, angles and areas in the viewport.

What Pro Measure does

Pro Measure gives real-time dimensional feedback inside the Blender viewport, helpful for technical modeling, architectural design and quality control.

Five measurement types cover object dimensions, bounds, distance, multi-point and area, with color-coded axis indicators. Multi-point measurements persist across save and reload, snapping works on vertices, edge midpoints and face centers, and units run from micrometers to kilometers in metric and imperial. Measurements can be pinned, organized and renamed, and line, color and text styling is customizable.

Key features

  • Five measurement types
  • Vertex, edge and face snapping
  • Metric and imperial units
  • Persistent, customizable on-screen display
