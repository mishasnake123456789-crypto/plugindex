Provides adjustable low-cost preview rendering in Blender without changing final render settings.

What Preview Master does

Preview Master lets you check progress on a scene with a render that is quick but still informative. A dedicated panel adjusts resolution, sample count and render quality.

Those preview settings are separate from the final ones, so switching between draft and finished output never disturbs your delivery configuration. You choose the balance between speed and quality. Lighting and lookdev artists who test often will avoid retyping values each time.

Key features

  • Adjustable preview resolution, samples and quality
  • Separate from final render settings
  • Speed versus quality balance
  • Dedicated panel
