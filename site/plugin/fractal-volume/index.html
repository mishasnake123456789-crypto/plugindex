A GPU effect generating true 3D fractal noise in After Effects that reacts to scene cameras and lights.

What Fractal Volume does

Fractal Volume produces three-dimensional fractal noise that actually exists in space, rather than a flat texture. Because it responds to cameras and lights in your scene, you can fly through clouds, nebulae or abstract formations and have them shaded correctly.

You can pick from five fractal types and four noise types, crop the volume along each axis, adjust Map Depth and tweak color, brightness and contrast. Noise density and render quality can be adjusted to balance speed and detail on the GPU. It is useful for atmospheric VFX and backgrounds.

Key features

  • True 3D fractal noise in space
  • Reacts to cameras and lights
  • 5 fractal types and 4 noise types
  • Crop control on X, Y and Z axes
  • Adjustable density and render quality
