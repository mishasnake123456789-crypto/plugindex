What The Whimsy Wonder Walker - Cycles does

The Whimsy Wonder Walker - Cycles is a 3ds Max tool that blends scripting with rigging to produce walk cycles of many styles. It is meant for character animators who want a ready starting point for walking motion instead of keying every step from scratch.

Everything comes packaged as a zip that installs into the 3dsmax\scenes directory. The supplied scenes were made with Max 2009 using centimetre units, and the open procedure accounts for differences in SystemUnitSize so scenes load correctly on other setups. It is useful for blocking out cycles quickly and for studying how different walking types are constructed.

Key features

  • Generates walk cycles of many types
  • Combines MaxScript with a rigging setup
  • Zip package installs to the 3dsmax\scenes folder
  • Scenes built in Max 2009 with centimetre units
  • Open procedure handles SystemUnitSize differences
