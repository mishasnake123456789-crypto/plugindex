What Audio Trigger Lite does

Audio Trigger Lite animates properties in time with music tempo. You set a BPM, and the add-on builds a beat grid that can be used to drive values such as scale, light strength or any other animatable property.

Motion designers making music visualizers or rhythm-driven animation can skip hand-keying every pulse. Because it works with a regular beat grid, results stay tightly in sync with the track tempo, and the lite version covers the essentials of that idea.

Key features

  • BPM-based beat grid
  • Drives any property
  • Syncs animation to music tempo
