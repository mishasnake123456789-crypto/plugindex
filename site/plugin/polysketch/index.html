What PolySketch does

PolySketch, by anup-gupte, brings CAD drafting habits to Blender with 2D drafting tools, face cutting and exact mitered polyline offsets. It is listed under Modeling and Mesh.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • CAD 2D drafting
  • Face cutting
  • Exact miter polyline offset
