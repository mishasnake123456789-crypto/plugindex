Extracts distinct frames from a video in Blender by comparing pixels, and converts between images and video.

What Different Sequential Images Catcher does

Different Sequential Images Catcher grabs frames from a video that actually differ from each other. It compares each frame's pixels to the previous one, and when the difference condition is met, even a single changed pixel, the frame is exported.

It keeps comparing through the video, and it can also convert existing images or frames into a video and back again. This helps when pulling unique stills from footage or rebuilding a clip from a series of images.

Key features

  • Pixel comparison against the previous frame
  • Exports only frames that change
  • Works with videos that have frames on disk
  • Converts images or frames to video
  • Converts video back to images
