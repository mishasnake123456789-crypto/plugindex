What YY_MagicKey does

YY_MagicKey by Andrew Yang is a compact keyer for After Effects that removes a background color without a maze of settings. You pick the Background Color to key out, then use Crush Transparent and Crush Opaque to tighten the matte.

Despill Amount removes cast color from the background, while Despill Size sets how far from the edge the despill reaches. Output views for Source, Alpha Matte, Despill Matte and Final Output help judge the result. It suits quick green screen jobs.

Key features

  • Background Color key control
  • Crush Transparent and Crush Opaque
  • Despill Amount and Despill Size
  • Source, Alpha Matte, Despill Matte and Final views
