A Blender add-on for assigning bone parents from a list and binding meshes, curves or lattices to bone groups.

What All Bone Parents does

All Bone Parents saves rigging time by letting you assign parents to bones from a list rather than selecting each bone individually. It also lists bone groups, which can be used to bind meshes, curves or lattices to specific groups.

The groups appear as targets for the hook and armature modifiers, a GPU-drawn display of the bone target shows in the viewport when an item list is active, and bones may be listed from multiple armature objects. Documentation is included. Riggers building complex skeletons or hook setups will find it removes a lot of repeated clicking.

Key features

  • Assign parents from a list
  • Bone group list for binding meshes, curves and lattices
  • Targets for hook and armature modifiers
  • GPU draw of the bone target
  • Supports multiple armature objects
