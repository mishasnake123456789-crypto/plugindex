What AEJuice Newspaper Constructor does

AEJuice Newspaper Constructor assembles newspaper-style stories in After Effects and Premiere Pro from ready-made scenes. Text, colours, photos and backgrounds can be customised, with any language and font supported.

Scene types cover breaking news, headlines, front pages, headlines with a photo and social networks. Textures were made in 4K from photos of real newspapers, camera moves come from Cinema 4D and After Effects, and your own videos can appear as moving images. It is built entirely in After Effects and resizes to any resolution. The Pack Manager provides preview, drag-and-drop import and favourites, and fast downloads.

Key features

  • Breaking news, headline and front page scenes
  • 4K textures from real newspapers
  • Customisable text, colours, photos and backgrounds
  • Insert your own videos
  • Auto-resize to any resolution
