What Instant Compositor does

Instant Compositor builds a full compositing setup in a single click. It offers 24 presets designed by a professional compositor to mimic various film and art styles, and works with Cycles and Eevee renders, output from other software, and real footage or images.

Instant Composite! creates the chosen setup, while Load Preset and Load Tool nodes bring a preset or tool into an existing tree. Tools such as anamorphic effects are included. It gets a graded look quickly.

Key features

  • 24 premade compositing setups
  • One-click Instant Composite
  • Load Preset and Load Tool nodes
  • Works with renders and footage
