Automatically mixes all audio layers in an After Effects comp, including nested compositions.

What Auto Audio Mixer does

Auto Audio Mixer by KiwiVFX balances the audio layers of the active After Effects composition automatically. It respects layer and comp in and out points and walks through nested comps recursively, so complex projects can be mixed in one pass.

Two mixing methods and two duration modes, composition or work area, are available. Nine built-in audio effects are included, among them high and low pass, reverb, bass and treble, flanger and chorus. Leftover audio can be stretched to blend into the next layer or fill the duration, interfering audio can be disabled, a layer can be bypassed by tag, and a minimum volume can be set. Hotkeys use Easy Ease, Bezier or linear keyframe interpolation.

Key features

  • Mixes audio layers with in and out point handling
  • Recursive processing of nested comps
  • Nine bundled audio effects
  • Bypass by tag and minimum volume settings
  • Hotkeys with selectable keyframe interpolation
