What DOSCH 3D - Environments - Clean Room - FBX does

DOSCH 3D - Environments - Clean Room - FBX is an asset pack for 3ds Max made up of five detailed clean room models and scenes. All of them arrive completely textured and in FBX format, so they can be opened and used straight away.

The release is version 1.0.0, the first one in the store. Visualization artists, technical illustrators and designers who need sterile laboratory, medical or manufacturing settings can drop them into scenes instead of modeling the rooms from scratch.
