Qshot 2

FreeBlender

Blender add-on for multi-camera animation, with each camera acting as one shot, stacked into a timeline and rendered together.

What Qshot 2 does

Qshot 2 organizes multi-camera animation in Blender around the idea that each camera covers one shot travelling from point A to point B. Shots are stacked along a timeline and rendered as one continuous animation.

In and out keyframes can be set for each camera without selecting it, pressing I and choosing a key type. Spline-based and linear interpolation are offered, covering both eased and constant movement. Filmmakers, previs artists and motion designers can plan sequences of camera moves efficiently.

Key features

  • One camera per shot
  • Quick in and out keyframes without selecting the camera
  • Spline and linear interpolation
  • Shots stacked on a timeline
