What Phosphor: Crt Factory does

Phosphor: CRT Factory gives scenes and renders the look of an old display. It simulates curved glass, phosphor dot structure, scanlines, glow diffusion, film grain and signal glitches.

Because it uses the viewport compositor in Blender 5.0, the live viewport matches the final render. The CRT signal chain includes color response and display behavior, screen curvature with adjustable barrel distortion, and a procedural scanline system with controls for density, shape, distortion, detail and scale. It is handy for retro screens and VHS-style looks.

Key features

  • Live viewport compositing matching final render
  • Scanlines, glow diffusion and color response
  • Screen bulge with barrel distortion
  • Procedural scanline controls
  • Film grain and signal distortion
