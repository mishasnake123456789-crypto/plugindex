What 3D Extruder does

3D Extruder is a plugin from Ben Rollason that creates high-quality 3D extrusions in After Effects. It is notable for broad compatibility, running on versions from CS6 through 2020, with the changelog noting an update for After Effects 2021.

A demo video is available on YouTube, and the plugin is free. It is a good option for artists who work on older installs but still want dimensional text and graphics.
