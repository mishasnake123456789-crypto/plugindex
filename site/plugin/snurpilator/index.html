What Snurpilator does

SNURPilator lets you draw directly in the Blender viewport as if sketching, replacing tedious curve setup. It is aimed at rapid concepting, sculpt blockouts, hair, ribbons, ropes and 3D graffiti.

Strokes are single gestures with round, square or flat shapes, adjustable thickness, taper and pen pressure. Smoothing and axis mirroring give instant variation, and the workflow stays non-destructive. When ready, convert curves to mesh, combine strokes and voxel remesh them into solid forms.

Key features

  • Draw curves directly in the viewport
  • Stroke shape, thickness, taper and pressure
  • Smoothing and axis mirroring
  • Convert to mesh and voxel remesh
