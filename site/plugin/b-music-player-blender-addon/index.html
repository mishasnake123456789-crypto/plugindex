What B - Music Player (Blender Addon) does

B - Music Player lets you listen to your own tracks without leaving Blender. A small popup player appears over the interface, so there is no need to switch to another application while you model or animate.

It handles common audio formats such as mp3, wav, FLAC, M4A and aac, and you can assign your own shortcut key to bring the player up. The add-on also ships with a custom icon set and developer documentation. It suits Blender users who like background music during long sessions.

Key features

  • Popup music player inside Blender
  • Plays mp3, wav, FLAC, M4A and aac
  • Assignable shortcut key
  • Custom icon set
