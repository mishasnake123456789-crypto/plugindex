What Auto Sort Collections does

Auto Sort Collections is a Blender add-on by Shuimeng that organizes objects into collections according to their type, which saves manual outliner cleanup after imports or kitbashing.

Version 1.0.3 added the ability to exclude particular objects and collections from sorting, and it improved sorting of non-selectable objects. The add-on needs no special permissions, supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer, and is released under the GNU GPL v3 or later.

Key features

  • Sorts objects into collections by type
  • Exclude objects and collections (1.0.3)
  • Handles non-selectable objects
  • Requires no special permissions
  • Supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
