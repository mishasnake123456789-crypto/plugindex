What DOSCH 3D: Car Details V3 - Hydrogen Fuel Cell does

Based on a brand-neutral sedan, this Dosch model is built around a hydrogen fuel cell drive. It is delivered for Cinema 4D R15 to R24 together with many other formats and is fully textured.

Adapt the scale, change materials, textures and colors, and apply your own textures or labels as needed. Individual elements can be turned on or off, set between fully visible and invisible, and animated either separately or as one model. Engineers, trainers and motion designers can use it to visualize hydrogen mobility without modeling the vehicle themselves.
