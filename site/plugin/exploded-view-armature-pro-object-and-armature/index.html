What Exploded View Armature Pro - Object & Armature does

Exploded View Armature Pro creates exploded views of armatures in Blender, helpful for showcasing, explaining or debugging complex rigs. Animators, riggers, educators and technical artists can use it.

Bones are separated along X, Y, Z or all axes, from the armature center or a bone's parent, affecting all bones or only those selected in Pose Mode. Global sliders fine-tune each axis, and a smart reset restores the original pose in one click. Controls are in the sidebar, for Blender 4.x and above.

Key features

  • Separates bones along any axis
  • Armature center or parent origin
  • Selected or all bones in Pose Mode
  • Smart one-click reset
