What Photometric Light Plus does

Photometric Light Plus, by MarcoCaturano3dArtist, supports IES and EULUMDAT photometric lights in Blender and shows a halo in the viewport that reflects the distribution. It is listed under Lighting and Import-Export.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.5.0 or newer.

Key features

  • IES light support
  • EULUMDAT light support
  • Viewport photometric halo
