What Natural HD Grass Backgrounds does

Natural HD Grass Backgrounds from Graphic Spirit is a collection of grass images that can be used in After Effects and Premiere Pro. It is organized in four groups, each with 22 pictures: natural, dark, red tone and blue tone.

The variety allows a lawn or meadow texture to be tinted to suit a scene's mood. The publisher points to uses such as web backgrounds, headers, printing, packaging design, posters and outdoor advertising, so it suits designers who need quick green backdrops.
