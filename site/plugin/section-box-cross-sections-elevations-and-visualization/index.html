What Section Box - Cross Sections, Elevations, And Visualization does

Section Box brings architectural-style sections to Blender. Section boxes can be placed around selected objects, collections or anywhere else, producing cross sections, elevations and views.

Cross sections give clean visual output for precise cuts, elevations can be generated alongside a section or by themselves, and DXF export sends the result to CAD workflows. Architects, interior designers and product designers can produce drawings from their 3D models. Producing sections directly from the model keeps drawings consistent with design changes rather than requiring separate redrawing.

Key features

  • Section boxes around objects or collections
  • Clean cross section output
  • Elevation views
  • DXF export
