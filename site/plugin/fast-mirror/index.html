What Fast Mirror does

Fast Mirror is a stand-alone symmetry tool for Blender modeled after the Auto Mirror operator from Random Flow and Creative Flow. It splits the mesh and sets up a mirror modifier based on your choices, guided by a graphical interface that shows the points needed for the operation.

The side holding the real geometry can be relocated at any time, and several objects can be mirrored in one pass. Releasing it separately keeps an add-on collection simple. Modelers who build one half of a character or product and want symmetry immediately will find it convenient.

Key features

  • Splits mesh and applies mirror modifier
  • Graphical UI shows required points
  • Relocate the real geometry side anytime
  • Multi-object mirroring
  • Stand-alone add-on
