Camera toolkit for Blender with cinematic moves, operator-style walking, focus pulls, shake and shot management.

What Cinecraft does

Cinecraft brings several camera disciplines into one Blender add-on, compatible with versions 3.6 to 5.1. Moves are baked to keyframes so they remain editable.

Path Walker simulates a camera operator's walk with footsteps, breathing and sway. Camera Moves include Orbit, Crane, Dolly Zoom, Push In and Truck, with two stackable on one camera. Focus pulling, shake and shot management complete the set. Animators and filmmakers can build convincing camera work without hand-keying every curve.

Key features

  • Path Walker with footsteps, breathing and sway
  • Orbit, Crane, Dolly Zoom, Push In and Truck
  • Focus pulling and camera shake
  • Shot management, moves baked to keyframes
