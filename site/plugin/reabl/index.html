What Reabl does

ReaBl connects Blender with REAPER under shared timeline logic. Transport and timeline are synchronized, supporting scoring, Foley, editorial and timeline-accurate audio work on one machine or across several.

A Setup Assistant installs the OSC library and the REAPER OSC configuration. Sound designers can work against Blender's picture with their usual DAW. It is aimed at composers and sound designers who want picture and audio to stay locked together. It targets projects where picture and sound are produced on separate systems but must remain in step.

Key features

  • Transport and timeline sync
  • Setup Assistant installs OSC library
  • REAPER OSC configuration included
  • Works across multi-machine setups
