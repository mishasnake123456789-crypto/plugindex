What Vertex Animation Toolkit (VAT + VAB) does

Vertex Animation Toolkit, by Flement, bakes animated meshes to Vertex Animation Textures or Vertex Animation Buffers (VAT and VAB), a technique used to play complex motion cheaply in real-time engines. It is categorised under Workflow.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Bake to Vertex Animation Textures
  • Bake to Vertex Animation Buffers
