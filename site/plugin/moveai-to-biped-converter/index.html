What MoveAI To Biped Converter does

MoveAI To Biped Converter brings animation captured with Move AI into 3ds Max by turning it into Biped motion. Move AI records movement without suits or markers, and this script handles the step of getting that data onto a Biped rig.

It runs as a MaxScript tool and works with 3ds Max versions 2019 through 2024. The listed release is version 1, last updated March 3, 2024, and it is free. Animators who use markerless capture and then need to edit or retarget it with Biped tools in Max can avoid building the conversion setup manually.
