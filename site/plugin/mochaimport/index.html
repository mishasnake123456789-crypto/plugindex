What MochaImport+ does

MochaImport+ bridges mocha and After Effects, a gap that the built-in transfer leaves wide open. It takes planar-track results and turns them into usable comp content with a few clicks.

You can build a stabilized precomp, shuttle masks, effect points and layers in both directions, and apply perspective distortion through a variety of corner-pin style effects, including native ones like Corner Pin and CC Power Pin and several third-party options from Red Giant, Boris FX and Sapphire. Tracked masks can be keyframed, and KBar and scripting hooks are included. Compositors who track in mocha will save a lot of manual setup.

Key features

  • Builds stabilized precomps from mocha data
  • Two-way transfer of masks, points and layers
  • Works with many corner-pin effects
  • Keyframes tracked masks
  • KBar and scripting support
