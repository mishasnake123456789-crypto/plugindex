What Bonechainrenamer does

BoneChainRenamer saves rigging time by renaming a whole chain of bones together. You pick a root name and every bone in the chain is renamed to match, instead of editing each name by hand.

It works with Blender 2.79 and 2.8 and is released under the GPL license. Version 0.1.1 fixed a bug with subdivided bones. Documentation is available from the developer. It is useful for animators and riggers who keep armatures consistently named for mirroring and retargeting.

Key features

  • Rename whole bone chains at once
  • Based on a chosen root name
  • Works with Blender 2.79 and 2.8
  • Fix for subdivided bones
