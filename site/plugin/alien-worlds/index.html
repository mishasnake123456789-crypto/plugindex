What Alien Worlds does

Alien Worlds, by FM3D, generates celestial bodies in Blender entirely procedurally. Planets, stars and black holes can be created, and details such as craters and oceans are adjustable.

It is optimized for Cycles rendering and controlled from the N panel. Documentation and a demo video that walks through all the settings are included, making it a good fit for space-themed scenes.

Key features

  • Procedural planets, stars and black holes
  • Crater and ocean controls
  • Optimized for Cycles
  • N panel interface
  • Documentation and demo video
