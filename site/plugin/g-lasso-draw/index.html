What G-Lasso Draw does

G-Lasso Draw, by Bookyakuno, turns Grease Pencil lassos into mesh polygons so rough models and ideas appear quickly. You sketch a shape, and the add-on generates the polygon from it.

Grease Pencil input includes camera shake compensation and square or circle shapes. The meshes are shaped by modifiers, so they can be adjusted non-destructively after creation. It is aimed at concept modeling and brainstorming, where speed matters more than precision.

Key features

  • Lasso strokes with Grease Pencil create polygons
  • Camera shake compensation
  • Square and circle shape options
  • Non-destructive shaping through modifiers
