Blender add-on offering over 30 customizable compositing presets for color, optics, passes and geometry.

What Quick Compo does

Quick Compo sets up compositing effects on renders in Blender through presets. More than thirty adjustable filters are sorted under the headings Passes, Optic, Color, Luminosity, Geometry and Volumetric, and you can preview them on your render before settling.

Custom settings can be saved as new presets and exported or imported for sharing. An experimental mode lets advanced users modify the node tree and insert nodes of their own. It works with Cycles and EEVEE and is compatible with the Realtime Compositor.

Key features

  • 30+ compositing presets
  • Categories from Color to Volumetric
  • Save, export and import custom presets
  • Experimental node tree editing mode
  • Works with Realtime Compositor
