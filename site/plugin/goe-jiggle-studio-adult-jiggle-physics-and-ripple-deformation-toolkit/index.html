What Goe Jiggle Studio Adult - Jiggle Physics & Ripple Deformation Toolkit does

GOE Jiggle Studio Adult adds secondary motion to character meshes in Blender, aimed at adult and NSFW animation as well as creatures, tails and tentacles. Pick a proxy preset, pick a cloth preset and click Bind Jiggle.

It includes nine procedural proxy presets, six cloth motion presets and a Ripple Maker for localized impact and contact deformation. Surface Deform setup, collision tools, cloth bake tools and a Health Check are provided, along with a demo character and the full Python source.

Key features

  • One-click jiggle binding
  • Nine proxy presets and six cloth presets
  • Ripple Maker for impact deformation
  • Collision, bake tools and Health Check
