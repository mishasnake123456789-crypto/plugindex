What Ezdecal - Fast Decal Placement & Baking does

EzDecal is a Blender add-on for placing and baking decals. It includes more than fifteen ready-made decals and accepts custom textures, projecting images onto the object surface with shrinkwrap so they stick to the target mesh.

Aging effects fade decals into the surface, and baked decals work with cloth, soft body and rigid body simulations. It suits artists adding labels, damage marks and surface details. Highlights cover 15+ built-in decals plus custom textures and automatic surface projection with shrinkwrap.

Key features

  • 15+ built-in decals plus custom textures
  • Automatic surface projection with shrinkwrap
  • Aging effects
  • Baked decals work with simulations
