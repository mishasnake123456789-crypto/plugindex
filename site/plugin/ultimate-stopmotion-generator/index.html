What Ultimate Stopmotion Generator does

This Blender add-on gives smooth animation the choppy charm of stop motion. From a side panel you pick the frame range to process, and a frame step sets the interval that creates the stop-motion jump.

Interpolation can be switched between Stop Motion (Constant), Linear and Smooth. Time Jitter adds small timing variations for a more organic rhythm, and Position Noise introduces random spatial vibrations that mimic handled models. It works with both Eevee and Cycles, and is useful for stylized shorts and motion graphics.

Key features

  • Start and end frame range
  • Adjustable frame step
  • Constant, Linear or Smooth interpolation
  • Time Jitter and Position Noise
