Blender add-on with gesture-based pie menus for common tasks, plus copy, keyframing and camera operators.

What 3D Viewport Pie Menus does

3D Viewport Pie Menus adds circular, gesture-driven menus to Blender to reduce trips through the interface. Some menus include new operators, not just shortcuts.

Copy Pie, on Ctrl+C, moves modifiers, constraints and custom properties from one object or bone to another, bringing drivers, geometry node inputs, physics and baking settings along. Keyframing pies in animation editors set keyframe type, handle type, interpolation mode and extrapolation, and camera controls are included. Animators and modelers who prefer flicking a mouse over hunting through menus will work faster.

Key features

  • Gesture-based pie menus
  • Copy Pie for modifiers, constraints and properties
  • Keyframing pies for type, handles, interpolation
  • Camera control operators
