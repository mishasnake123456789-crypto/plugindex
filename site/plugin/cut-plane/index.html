What Cut Plane does

Cut Plane, by Firg, places a plane you can move and orient, then cuts the mesh along it, with a live preview before you commit. It is listed under 3D View and Mesh.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Cut with a positionable plane
  • Live preview
