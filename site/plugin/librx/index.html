What Librx does

LibrX is a VFX library for Blender that offers ready-made effects so you do not have to run each simulation yourself. The collection holds pre-simulated cloth, particles, smoke, fire, explosions, curtains and lights.

It works with Cycles and Eevee on Blender 4.0 through 5.1 and installs as an add-on, listed as a compatible extension. Documentation is included. Handy for adding effects to shots quickly. Having these effects pre-calculated means you can drop them into a scene and focus on lighting and composition instead of waiting on simulation bakes.

Key features

  • Pre-simulated cloth, smoke, fire and explosions
  • Curtains, particles and lights included
  • Cycles and Eevee support
  • Blender 4.0 through 5.1
