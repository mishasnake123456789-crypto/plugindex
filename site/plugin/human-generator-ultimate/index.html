What Human Generator Ultimate does

Human Generator Ultimate lets Blender artists create realistic human characters inside the program. The developer positions it as simpler to use than previous versions, with a full set of ready-made elements.

Characters come with a rig, hairstyles, clothing and expressions, and a pose library is included. They render with both Cycles and Eevee, and documentation accompanies the add-on. Animators, illustrators and archviz artists needing people for scenes can produce believable figures quickly rather than modeling each one from scratch.

Key features

  • Photorealistic human generator
  • Hairstyles, clothing and expressions
  • Rigged characters
  • Pose library
  • Cycles and Eevee rendering
