What Contrast Pro does

Marius Silaghi's Contrast Pro is a 3ds Max modifier that brings out the visible detail of 3D models. It can raise contrast across an entire model or emphasize only specific areas, making fine forms stand out more clearly.

With Uniform enabled, areas of big and small polygons receive equal contrast, so mesh density does not affect the outcome. It works across a wide range of 64-bit 3ds Max versions, files and licenses are accessible from the developer's My Downloads page, and a demo video is available. Sculptors and scan cleanup artists can use it to bring out detail.

Key features

  • Enhances detail on 3D models
  • Contrast on the whole model or selected areas
  • Uniform option for varying polygon sizes
  • Works across many 64-bit 3ds Max versions
  • Demo video available
