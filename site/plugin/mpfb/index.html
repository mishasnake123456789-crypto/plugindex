Blender human character generator that builds, rigs and textures humanoid meshes with a clothes and asset library.

What MPFB does

MPFB creates and edits human characters inside Blender. With one click it makes a humanoid mesh, which can then be adjusted through parametric modeling of body features.

It offers automatic rigging with several rig choices, including Rigify, in both IK and FK modes. Procedural skin and eye materials are provided, and an asset library handles clothes, body parts and materials, fully compatible with MakeHuman assets. Animators, game developers and illustrators who need custom characters quickly can start from a believable base.

Key features

  • One-click humanoid mesh
  • Parametric body modeling
  • Several rigs including Rigify, IK and FK
  • Procedural skin and eye materials
  • MakeHuman asset compatibility
