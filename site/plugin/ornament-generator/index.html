What Ornament Generator does

Ornament Generator is a Blender add-on from PeakDreams Studio that creates ornamental detail along edges you have selected. You pick a design from a library of up to 426 ornaments in 15 categories, each with a preview, press the Generate Ornament button, and then tune the result.

The ornaments are hand-made, seamless and designed to follow your surface. They come UV unwrapped, with a decimate option, an extra scale setting and a vertical offset. A refresh option lets you add your own ornaments to the library. It suits architectural and decorative modeling.

Key features

  • Library of up to 426 ornaments in 15 categories
  • Generates from a selection of edges
  • Seamless hand-made designs that follow the surface
  • UV unwrapped with decimate option
  • Scale and Z offset controls, plus custom additions
