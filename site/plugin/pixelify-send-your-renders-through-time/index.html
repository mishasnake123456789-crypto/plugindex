What Pixelify - Send Your Renders Through Time does

Pixelify is a Blender add-on from FANNEC that converts finished renders and texture images into pixel art. It is aimed at artists who want a retro or game-console look without redrawing anything by hand.

A single click runs the conversion, and several textures can be processed together with shared settings. You choose a dithering method such as Bayer, Floyd-Steinberg or Median-Cut, pick a sampling mode from nearest neighbor to Lanczos, and use either the built-in palette or one you define. Brightness, contrast, saturation, gamma and hue can be tuned, presets store your looks, and the originals can always be restored.

Key features

  • One-click pixel art conversion with batch processing
  • Bayer, Floyd-Steinberg and Median-Cut dithering
  • Built-in and custom color palettes
  • Presets and non-destructive reverting to originals
