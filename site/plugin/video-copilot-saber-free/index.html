What Video Copilot Saber (Free) does

Saber is a free Video Copilot effect that creates energy and light beams in After Effects. It's known for glows that fall off realistically, along with advanced controls for the core of the beam.

Distortion is built in, twenty-five presets provide quick starting points, and text or mask outlines can be turned into dynamic glowing lines. Effects can be stacked for more complex results, which suits sci-fi, music visuals and title design.

Key features

  • High-quality energy and light beams
  • Realistic glow falloff
  • Advanced core settings and built-in distortion
  • 25 presets
  • Glowing text and mask outlines, stackable
