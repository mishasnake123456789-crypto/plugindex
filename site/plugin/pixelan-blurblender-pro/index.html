What Pixelan BlurBlender Pro does

Pixelan BlurBlender Pro delivers over 650 GPU-accelerated blur presets for After Effects and Premiere Pro, which you can shape, blend and mask into looks that built-in tools cannot produce. You also control where in the scene the blur appears.

It blurs titles, lower thirds and other alpha graphics in ways optimized for video. Blur direction and length can be keyframed for wind, flares and light rays, and spot, edge and tilt-shift variants are included. A large Effects Browser helps compare, mark and save presets per project or client, with randomization available.

Key features

  • 650+ GPU-accelerated blur presets
  • Optimized for titles and alpha graphics
  • Keyframable direction and length for wind and light rays
  • Spot, edge and tilt-shift blurs
  • Effects Browser with saved presets
