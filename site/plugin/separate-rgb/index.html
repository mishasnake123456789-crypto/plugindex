What Separate RGB does

Separate RGB, by Rowbyte, treats the three colour channels of an image as independent layers. You can move, rotate, scale, blur, blend, fade or distort red, green and blue separately, which opens up glitch, split-tone and fringing looks.

Lens Distortion can be applied per channel, giving a quick chromatic aberration, and the boundaries grow automatically so shifted channels aren't clipped. The plugin works in both After Effects and Premiere Pro.

Key features

  • Independent control of R, G and B
  • Move, rotate, scale, blur, blend, fade, distort
  • Per-channel lens distortion
  • Boundaries grow automatically
  • After Effects and Premiere Pro
