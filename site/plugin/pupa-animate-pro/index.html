What Pupa Animate Pro does

Pupa Animate Pro is a Blender toolset from Luwizart for rigging characters and applying, customizing and retargeting their animations. It combines AI auto-rigging, an approachable retargeter, an animation library and animation layers.

AI rigging handles custom rigs as well as Auto Rig, Human Gen and Rigify, and several binding methods attach animations to rigs. The retargeter stores presets and loads animations automatically. You can bring in custom animations from any source rig, and animation layers with live adjustment let you tailor the motion afterward.

Key features

  • AI auto-rigging for several rig types
  • Retargeter with saved presets
  • Animation library and custom imports
  • Multiple binding methods
  • Animation layers with live adjustment
