3ds Max script that creates a camera on a drawn spline, path-constrains it and keyframes eased motion along the path.

What EasyCameraAlongPath does

EasyCameraAlongPath automates the setup for a camera flythrough in 3ds Max. You draw a spline, and the script creates a new camera, links it and path-constrains it to that curve in one pass.

The camera is keyed to travel the spline from 0 to 100 percent, with eased tangents at the start and end of the motion. It works with 3ds Max version 9 and later and comes as a lightweight .ms script file. Animators and visualization artists who often build walkthrough shots can skip the repeated constraint setup.

Key features

  • Creates a camera path-constrained to a drawn spline
  • Keyframes motion from 0 to 100 percent
  • Ease in and ease out tangents
  • Linking and constraint setup in one pass
  • Light .ms script for 3ds Max 9 and later
