What Blaze Add-On does

Blaze Add-on from R Animation Studios adds a cinematic fire or smoke shader to Blender in a single click. It includes more than 35 color presets for fire and smoke.

All controls live on a single node, and the procedural fire shaders need neither Mantaflow nor VDB simulation. Universal presets include Fire and Fire plus Smoke looks. Motion designers and VFX artists can get quick, light-weight flames without heavy simulation.

Key features

  • One-click fire or smoke shader
  • 35+ color presets
  • All controls on one node
  • No Mantaflow or VDB needed
