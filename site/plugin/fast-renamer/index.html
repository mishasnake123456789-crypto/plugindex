What Fast Renamer does

Fast Renamer speeds up layer naming and covers After Effects, Photoshop and Illustrator under a single license. The interface comes in English, Spanish, French, Italian and Chinese.

You can add prefixes and suffixes, control the numbering iteration, and keep the existing name if desired. Preset lists such as Simple Body, Body, Hands, Character Animator and Draw are included, and an interface lets you make your own lists. Handy for rigged characters and big layered files.

Key features

  • Works in After Effects, Photoshop and Illustrator
  • One license for all three apps
  • Five interface languages
  • Prefix, suffix and iteration control
  • Preset and custom naming lists
