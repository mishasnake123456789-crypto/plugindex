What Cam-Fx does

Cam-FX by Bproduction adds custom bokeh and lens imperfections to Cycles renders in Blender. It ships with 120 bokeh textures to choose from.

Custom aperture maps allow bokeh shapes such as hearts, musical notes or snowflakes, and the Lens Dirt function adds dust around light sources as on a grubby lens. It runs on every Blender version and is bundled with a video tutorial, a PDF manual and a pair of test scenes. Advanced users seeking photographic, stylized depth-of-field can customize highlights.

Key features

  • 120 bokeh textures
  • Custom aperture shapes
  • Lens Dirt around light sources
  • Video tutorial, PDF manual and sample scenes
