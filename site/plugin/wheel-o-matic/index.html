What Wheel-O-Matic does

Wheel-O-Matic automates the rotation of wheels in Blender, using drivers instead of baking or simulation.

It handles single wheels, wheels on vehicles, wheels driven by bones and wheels following curves and meshes. The result is additive and non-destructive. Animators of cars, carts and machinery avoid keyframing each rotation. Rotation follows the movement of the wheel object, so changing the path or speed of a vehicle automatically updates how its wheels turn without any extra keyframing or cleanup.

Key features

  • Drivers instead of baking
  • Any direction of rotation
  • Works with objects, curves, meshes and bones
  • Additive and non-destructive
