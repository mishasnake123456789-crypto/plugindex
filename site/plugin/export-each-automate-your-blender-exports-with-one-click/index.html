What Export Each - Automate Your Blender Exports With One Click does

Export Each saves repetitive exporting in Blender. Choose an export path, pick Collections or Objects mode, select a format and press export, and every item becomes its own file, which is ideal for preparing asset libraries and game-ready models.

Formats come from a dropdown and use Blender's built-in exporters, including FBX. Filenames are handled safely so no manual renaming or texture management is required. The tool is in the N panel of the 3D Viewport and includes documentation. Game developers and 3D artists delivering many separate assets will save many exporting steps.

Key features

  • Batch exports objects or collections as separate files
  • Collections and Objects modes
  • Format dropdown including FBX
  • Safe filename handling
  • N panel access
