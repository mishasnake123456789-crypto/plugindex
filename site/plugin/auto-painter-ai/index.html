What Auto Painter Ai does

Auto Painter AI adds AI texture generation to Blender, relying on Stable Diffusion WebUI together with ControlNet. Depth and normal maps from your model, plus a text prompt, guide the output.

Generate Multiview Maps bakes the maps from a selected model, and Generate Guided AI Image sends them with the prompt to the AI server. Connection types support different setups. Concept and texture artists can explore surface ideas fitted to a model.

Key features

  • Stable Diffusion WebUI and ControlNet
  • Bakes multiview depth and normal maps
  • Guided AI image generation from prompts
  • Configurable connection types
