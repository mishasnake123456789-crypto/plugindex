Blender add-on that unfolds a mesh into a printable paper craft net as PDF or SVG.

What Export Paper Model does

Export Paper Model flattens a Blender mesh into a net you can print and fold into a paper model. Output is a PDF or SVG file, with the layout shaped by seam marks.

Textured objects can be baked to the net, including painted or procedural textures, optionally with lighting or ambient occlusion. Dimensions stay exact, with a scale factor such as 1:72, and the add-on copes with edges shared by several faces, flipped normals and surfaces with holes. Allowances for thick stock like cardboard are built in, as are options for colors and line styles. Hobbyists and educators can make craft models.

Key features

  • Printable net as PDF or SVG
  • Seam-based layout
  • Baked textures with optional lighting
  • Scale factor such as 1:72
  • Thick paper compensation
