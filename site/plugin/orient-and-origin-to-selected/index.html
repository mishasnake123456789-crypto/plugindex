What Orient And Origin To Selected does

Orient And Origin To Selected, by Orange Turbine, lets you reset an object's pivot and local axes from inside Edit Mode. It takes its cue from whatever geometry you have selected.

Orient to Selected appears in the orientation dropdown under Local Space, and Origin to Selected is under Mesh > Snap. It requires no special permissions and is written entirely in Python. Modelers fixing pivots on imported or kitbashed parts avoid detours into object mode.

Key features

  • Orients local axes to the selection
  • Sets origin from selection in Edit Mode
  • Integrated in dropdown and Snap menu
  • Pure Python, no special permissions
