What Liquid Glass does

Liquid Glass is a plugin from grape design that makes a glassy, refractive shape resembling current interface design. One click creates it.

Size, corner radius, fill and effects can be adjusted, and blur and distortion are applied to transparent areas so the content behind bends realistically. Everything is animatable from the Effect Controls, and the interface is kept simple and minimal. UI and motion designers recreating frosted, lens-like panels and buttons can build them quickly.

Key features

  • One-click liquid-glass shape
  • Size, corner radius, fill and effects can be adjusted
  • Blur and distortion on transparent areas
  • Animatable from Effect Controls
  • Minimal UI
