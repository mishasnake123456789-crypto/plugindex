What Content Cloud Plugin for Box does

Content Cloud Plugin for Box connects your Box.com storage to After Effects and Premiere Pro through a panel inside the app. Editors and motion designers who keep media on Box can browse files without leaving their project.

Assets can be downloaded, uploaded, dragged in, previewed (including PSD, audio and video formats) and linked. Several accounts can be added and switched quickly. The developer says it includes no analytics or tracking code, never collects usage data and complies with the EU GDPR. A one-month free period precedes subscription billing.

Key features

  • Browse and preview Box assets, including PSD, audio and video
  • Download, upload and drag-and-drop
  • Asset linking
  • Quick multi-account switching
  • No analytics or tracking code
