What World Apply does

World Apply is a Blender add-on by Baraa-Khaled-Youssef that solves a common annoyance: HDRI strength and rotation set up for the viewport do not carry over to the final render. One button syncs the viewport world settings with the scene world.

The add-on is tiny, about 2.8 KB, and is released under the GNU GPL v3 or later. It asks for read access to HDRI files in Blender's directories and supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. It is useful for anyone lighting a scene with an HDRI who wants the render to look like what they tuned.

Key features

  • One button syncs viewport and scene world
  • Applies HDRI strength and rotation to renders
  • Very small download
  • Needs read access to HDRI files
  • Supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
