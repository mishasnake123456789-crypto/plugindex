What Nx CanvasFlow does

Nx CanvasFlow reinterprets video as moving impressionist paintings inside DaVinci Resolve. It is designed for motion, so brushstrokes behave sensibly from frame to frame, and it can be used on the Edit, Fusion and Color pages.

An adaptive artistic blur sets how soft and abstract the strokes appear through an adjustable neighborhood radius. Embossed brush texture imitates pressure with randomized angles, and edge scatter breaks up outlines for a hand-painted feel. Filmmakers, music video creators and motion designers can turn ordinary shots into expressive artwork.

Key features

  • Impressionist video stylization
  • Adaptive artistic blur with radius control
  • Embossed brush texture
  • Edge scatter
  • Works on Edit, Fusion and Color pages
