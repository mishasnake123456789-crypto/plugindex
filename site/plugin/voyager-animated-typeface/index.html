What Voyager - Animated Typeface does

Voyager is an animated sans serif from Animography, shaped by the clean Swiss tradition of typography but updated for current motion work. Letterforms come with animation built in, so titles can be set without hand-keyframing each character.

It suits corporate explainers, clean title cards and minimalist lower thirds. Compatibility covers After Effects 2020 through 2024. Animography offers a 25 percent discount when four or more of its typefaces are ordered, and a demo video is available.
