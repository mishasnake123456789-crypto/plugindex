3ds Max script that converts scenes between render engines to V-Ray or Corona and exports PBR/glTF materials.

What V-RayMax Converter PRO does

V-RayMax Converter PRO moves materials, maps, lights, cameras, modifiers, helpers and GI environment from other render engines into V-Ray, and from V-Ray into Corona Renderer. It also converts proxies and render elements, sky and cloud systems, and Physical cameras.

V-Ray and Corona materials can be turned into Standard, Physical, PBR/OpenPBR and glTF versions for .glb export to Blender and other viewers. Batch processing handles multiple .max scenes and .mat libraries, with a MaxScript API for use without the interface, and an ACEScg workflow is configured automatically. Handy for studios migrating archives.

Key features

  • Converts other engines to V-Ray and V-Ray to Corona
  • Proxies, render elements, sky and cameras
  • Exports Standard, Physical, PBR/OpenPBR and glTF materials
  • Batch processing of scenes and libraries
  • Automatic ACEScg setup in V-Ray
