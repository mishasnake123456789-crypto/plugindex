What Sculpt-Paint Wheel does

Sculpt-Paint Wheel offers radial menus for picking brushes and tools in Blender's sculpting and painting modes. Three wheels are provided: Sculpt, Paint and Weight.

Holding Space in Sculpt mode opens the Sculpt Wheel, prefilled with default brushes. The Paint Wheel covers Texture Paint, Vertex Paint and Grease Pencil Draw and Vertex modes, and the Weight Wheel covers weight paint. Sculptors and painters can change brushes without pausing to scroll lists.

Key features

  • Sculpt, Paint and Weight wheels
  • Space-hold invokes the Sculpt Wheel
  • Default brushes prefilled
  • Covers texture, vertex and weight modes
