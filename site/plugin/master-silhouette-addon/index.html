What Master Silhouette Addon does

Master Silhouette Addon helps you judge shape readability and composition. One click or Alt+X toggles between normal and silhouette modes.

Silhouette, object and background colors can be set, the view rotation can be locked to avoid accidental movement, and collections can be filtered. Useful for modelers and animators checking poses and forms. Under the hood you get alt+X silhouette toggle, adjustable silhouette, object and background colors, view rotation lock and collection filtering.

Key features

  • Alt+X silhouette toggle
  • Adjustable silhouette, object and background colors
  • View rotation lock
  • Collection filtering
