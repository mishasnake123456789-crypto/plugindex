What Boris FX Suite does

Boris FX Suite is a bundle of the company's products: Continuum, Sapphire, Mocha Pro, Silhouette, SynthEyes, Optics and a Pro Audio division. It targets After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and other hosts, along with standalone use.

A single manager installs and licenses the whole suite or just the pieces you pick, activates and deactivates licenses, handles version updates, and links to training and support. Together the bundle offers over 270 effects, more than 60 transitions and 3,000-plus presets. Host support also extends to Media Composer, Final Cut Pro and Photoshop, on macOS, Windows 10 and Linux, though Continuum excludes Linux.
