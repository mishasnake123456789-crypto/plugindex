What Blaze Animate - Click & Drag Animation does

Blaze Animate brings click-and-drag posing to Blender. Instead of searching bone hierarchies, you select and move rig controllers right in the viewport.

Smart selection helps in crowded rigs: Shift+Click adds to the selection and double-click to select a hierarchy. Visual feedback shows what is selected. It speeds up posing and animation of characters. Direct viewport interaction helps animators stay focused on posing instead of navigating outliner lists and bone names.

Key features

  • Click and drag rig controllers
  • Smart selection in dense areas
  • Double-click to select hierarchy
  • Visual feedback
