What Lazy Gizmo does

Lazy Gizmo is a rotation gizmo from Bookyakuno built for posing. Rather than tracking a fixed arc, it rotates continuously according to how far you drag the mouse, which feels more natural than the standard gizmo.

XYZ rotation appears as three colored lines, trackball rotation works from the center, and a white outer circle rotates the view. Shift gives smooth rotation, Ctrl snaps to 5-degree steps, and Ctrl plus Shift snaps to 45 degrees. It works in Object and Pose Mode for animators and character riggers.

Key features

  • Continuous rotation from mouse drag
  • XYZ lines, trackball and view rotation
  • Shift for smooth, Ctrl for 5-degree steps
  • Object Mode and Pose Mode
