Separates Premiere Pro clips into AI stems such as vocals and drums, or dialogue, effects and music.

What Moises does

Moises brings stem separation into the timeline. Music splits into vocals, drums, guitars, bass, piano, keys and strings, and each stem returns to the project bin as a WAV clip. Cinematic mode splits video soundtracks into dialogue, sound effects and music.

It detects the selected timeline audio or lets you choose a file from disk, and pairs the device by QR code or moises.ai/pair. Useful for podcast cleanup, dubbing, score replacement or rebalancing a noisy interview.

Key features

  • Stems: vocals, drums, guitars, bass, piano, keys, strings
  • Cinematic mode for dialogue, effects and music
  • Detects timeline selection or picks from disk
  • Stems imported as WAV clips
  • QR code device pairing
