Blender add-on with social media and cinematic render presets for resolution, ratio and frame rate.

What Frame Kit does

Frame Kit sets render dimensions and frame rate through presets, which speeds up preparing deliverables for different platforms.

Presets cover TikTok, Instagram, Reels and YouTube, plus cinematic aspect ratios such as 1.85:1, 2.35:1 and 2.39:1. An orientation toggle flips between horizontal and vertical, a multiplier scales resolution absolutely, and standard sizes run from 720p to 8K. Frame rates range from 23.98 to 120 fps with custom fields available.

Key features

  • Social media presets for TikTok, Reels, YouTube
  • Cinematic aspect ratios
  • Orientation toggle and resolution multiplier
  • Frame rate presets from 23.98 to 120
