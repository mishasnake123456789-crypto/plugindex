What Imperfekt | Procedural Surface Imperfections does

Imperfekt adds realistic wear to Blender materials through a panel in the 3D view. Artists in product and architectural visualization, game assets, character art and VFX can apply smudges and weathering in one click.

The library has more than 65 fully procedural imperfections, organized in categories with search and favorites, and the original material is preserved. A real-time fingerprint generator offers 1K to 8K output, and each effect has node-based controls for customization.

Key features

  • 65+ procedural imperfections
  • One-click application to any material
  • Real-time fingerprint generator up to 8K
  • Non-destructive, searchable library with favorites
