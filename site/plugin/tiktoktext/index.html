What TikTokText does

TikTokText from .nook helps creators make text that looks native to short-form video platforms. It supports TikTok, Reels, Shorts and CapCut inspired styles within After Effects.

It offers smooth stroked text, line-wrapping text boxes with justification and transparency options, word accent animations, neon glow effects and a faux 3D drop depth. Accent styles can use colour or box highlights, giving fast ways to emphasize words. It's aimed at creators who want caption styling that matches current short-form trends without building each look from scratch.

Key features

  • Smooth outlined text
  • Wrapping text boxes with justification
  • Word accent animations
  • Neon glow and faux 3D drop depth
  • Colour and box accent styles
