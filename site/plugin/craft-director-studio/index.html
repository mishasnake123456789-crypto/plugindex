What Craft Director Studio does

Craft Director Studio is an animation plug-in from Craft Animations. Hook it up to a model and you can steer cars or aircraft through a 3D environment, adjusting variables such as suspension, instead of keyframing every movement.

A wide choice of camera effects can be mixed. Cameras can be humanized with subtle shakes, SphereCam gives dramatic angles, and a Spline Speed Controller offers precise pacing. The listing notes support for 2026 Windows versions of Autodesk 3ds Max and Maya. Animation teams working on vehicle and flight sequences gain a more direct way to create motion.

Key features

  • Drive vehicles and aircraft through 3D scenes
  • Adjustable variables such as suspension
  • Humanized camera shakes and SphereCam
  • Spline Speed Controller
  • Supports 2026 Windows versions of 3ds Max and Maya
