What Dither Fx does

Dither FX is a compact compositor add-on for retro-style render looks. It creates stylized dithering and posterization effects and works with both Cycles and Eevee.

It installs as a Blender extension, supports Blender 3.6 and later, and includes documentation. Useful for pixel-art-inspired visuals and lo-fi game aesthetics. In practice that means stylized dither looks, posterized render looks, cycles and Eevee compatible and installs as a Blender extension. The result can give renders a distinctive, low-fidelity character.

Key features

  • Stylized dither looks
  • Posterized render looks
  • Cycles and Eevee compatible
  • Installs as a Blender extension
