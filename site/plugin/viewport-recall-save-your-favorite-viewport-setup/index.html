What Viewport Recall - Save Your Favorite Viewport Setup does

Viewport Recall stores combinations of 3D Viewport settings so you can switch between working setups instantly. Visibility, gizmos, overlays and shading are all captured and can be restored in one click.

Selective loading lets you decide per parameter whether a setting is applied or ignored when a preset is recalled. Presets can be reached through the N sidebar panel, the View menu, a pie menu with Ctrl+Alt+Z by default, or your own keyboard shortcut. It is helpful for artists who alternate between modeling, sculpting and review views and dislike toggling options by hand.

Key features

  • Saves visibility, gizmos, overlays and shading
  • Per-parameter selective loading
  • Sidebar, menu, pie menu and shortcut access
  • One-click recall of any preset
