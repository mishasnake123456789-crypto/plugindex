What ap_batch_relinker does

ap_batch_relinker by Aurelien Poirier reconnects offline footage in After Effects by scanning a new source root folder, ignoring special characters in names. A single pass filters by offline-media extensions and normalizes filenames.

Its AEPX method patches paths in the project XML and keeps separate PSD, AI and PSB layers intact. An optional pre-pass renames files and folders on disk to strip accents, with a preview dialog. A paginated multi-match resolver lets you choose the right candidate per file, and Force Relink re-runs matching even for items reported as online.

Key features

  • Relinks from a new root folder ignoring special characters
  • AEPX method preserving PSD, AI and PSB layers
  • Optional on-disk accent-stripping renames with preview
  • Multi-match resolver with per-file apply
  • Force Relink for items reported online
