Blender add-on that creates 3D models out of 2D projections drawn on curves and mesh planes, controlled in real time.

What Projection Modeling does

Projection Modeling creates 3D shapes from 2D projections in Blender. Projections are drawn from curves and mesh planes and drive the resulting 3D form live.

a 2D fill mode and a Pure Curve mode are provided, and Pure Curve works together with the Curve Work add-on. Concept modelers and designers who think in front, side and top silhouettes can produce volumes more intuitively. Because the shape is controlled by its projections, edits to a profile immediately update the model.

Key features

  • 3D models from 2D projections
  • Real-time shape control
  • 2D fill and Pure Curve modes
  • Works with Curve Work add-on
