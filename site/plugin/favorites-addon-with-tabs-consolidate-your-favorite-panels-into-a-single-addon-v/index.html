What Favorites Addon With Tabs, Consolidate Your Favorite Panels Into A Single Addon View For Quick Access does

Favorites Addon With Tabs adds a dedicated tab to the 3D View where you collect panels from different installed add-ons. It spares you from scrolling through a long sidebar.

Panels can be reordered with up and down buttons, split into separate tabs, color-coded in their headers and expanded or collapsed individually or all at once. A dialog helps you choose what to include. Your selection and organization are kept between sessions, so the layout is there when you reopen Blender.

Key features

  • Collects panels from various add-ons
  • Reorder with up and down buttons
  • Separate favorited panels into tabs
  • Color-coded panel headers
  • Layout saved between sessions
