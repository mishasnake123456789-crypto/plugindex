What Subtitle Pro does

Subtitle Pro helps editors build subtitles in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Text can be typed directly or brought in from SRT files, and a YouTube importer fetches captions from a copied video link.

It can translate sentences, check for overlapping times and search within the table view, which autosaves and offers a UI for shifting rows. Common subtitle formats are supported along with UTF-8, UTF-8 with BOM, ANSI and UTF-16 encodings. After Effects users also get animation templates, such as Classic, Fading, Abstract, Lower Thirds and Advanced Timeline, with preview, plugin sync and marker control.

Key features

  • SRT import and direct text entry
  • YouTube caption importer
  • Translation and overlap checking
  • Table view with autosave and row shifting
  • Animated templates in After Effects
