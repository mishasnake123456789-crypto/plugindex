What Blender Addon | Auto Krita To Blender does

Auto Krita To Blender, by VFX Grace, links Blender with the painting application Krita. It lets you view the final composition of a Krita project in Blender at fast reading speed, including very large 4K or 8K canvases.

One Blender project can interact with several Krita projects at once. PNG and JPG export are supported, hotkeys are customizable with default shortcuts for exporting JPG and PNG, and documentation is included. Artists who paint textures or concept art in Krita and use it as a reference or texture source in Blender will avoid repeated manual exports.

Key features

  • Shows Krita output in Blender quickly
  • Works with 4K and 8K projects
  • One Blender file with multiple Krita projects
  • PNG and JPG export
  • Customizable hotkeys
