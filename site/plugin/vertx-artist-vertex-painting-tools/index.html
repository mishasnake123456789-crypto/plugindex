What Vertx Artist - Vertex Painting Tools does

VertX Artist provides a dedicated workflow for vertex colors in Blender. You can split RGBA into separate channels, such as R, G, B, A or combinations, and paint on a specific channel directly.

More control comes from HSV edits, color selection, non-destructive transforms, alpha gradients, palettes and an improved eyedropper. Game artists who rely on vertex data for masks, tinting and baked effects can paint faster and with finer precision.

Key features

  • Separate RGBA channel painting
  • HSV adjustments
  • Non-destructive transformations
  • Alpha gradients and palettes
  • Enhanced eyedropper
