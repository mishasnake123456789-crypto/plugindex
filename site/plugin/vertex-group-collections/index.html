What Vertex Group Collections does

Vertex Group Collections adds structure to what Blender shows as one long list. Groups can be renamed and sorted into folders and subfolders.

You decide which vertex groups belong to which folder, and the system was stress tested with up to 1000 groups on a single model. Documentation is included. It helps riggers and character artists working with dense, cluttered group lists. Among its capabilities are folders and subfolders for groups, direct renaming and assign groups to folders.

Key features

  • Folders and subfolders for groups
  • Direct renaming
  • Assign groups to folders
  • Tested up to 1000 groups
