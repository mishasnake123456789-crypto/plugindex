What Kingluma Fresh Face does

Fresh Face from Kingluma applies light cosmetic touch-ups to people on camera. It suits interviews, talking heads and other close or medium shots where faces fill the frame, and works in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro, as well as Final Cut Pro X and Motion.

Skin smoothing de-emphasizes one level of texture so it looks natural and keeps detail, and separate eye and mouth level controls draw attention to those features. Masking is driven by machine-learning face tracking, so adjustments follow the subject. It runs on both Apple Silicon and Intel processors.

Key features

  • Natural skin smoothing that keeps detail
  • Eye and mouth level adjustments
  • Masking from machine-learning face tracking
  • Works in Final Cut Pro X, Motion and Adobe hosts
  • Apple Silicon and Intel
