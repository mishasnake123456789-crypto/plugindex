What Alpha Cut does

Alpha Cut removes backgrounds and generates masks without leaving Blender's Image Editor.

One click produces a transparent-background image, and automatic rotoscoping creates masks for tracking and masking workflows, reducing frame-by-frame manual work. It gives clean alpha and image masks with a fast preview and progress display. It runs entirely inside Blender, with no external software or web services. It suits compositors, designers and animators who need quick cutouts from still images or footage frames, and it keeps the whole process on the local machine.

Key features

  • One-click background removal
  • Automatic rotoscoping
  • Clean alpha and image masks
  • Fast mask preview with progress
  • No external software or web services
