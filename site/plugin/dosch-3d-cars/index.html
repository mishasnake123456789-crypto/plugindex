What DOSCH 3D: Cars does

Dosch 3D: Cars is a general-purpose vehicle pack of 16 detailed, fully textured models. It runs in Cinema 4D and other applications and is aimed at architectural visualizations, simulations and renderings.

Scaling, materials, colors and lighting can all be adapted. Textures may be replaced with your own, labels added, and elements switched on or off or set to varying transparency. Whole cars or individual parts can be animated. It gives visualization artists a ready set of traffic and parking cars without custom modeling.

Key features

  • 16 detailed textured cars
  • Cinema 4D and other applications
  • Adjustable scale, materials and colors
  • Own textures and labels
  • Switchable, transparent, animatable elements
