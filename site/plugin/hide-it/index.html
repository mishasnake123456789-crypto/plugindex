What Hide It does

Hide It, from KVD, manages hidden items from a dedicated panel. Hidden objects are listed, so you can bring them back individually or all at once.

A Hide selected button removes objects from the viewport, and a checkbox also keeps them out of the render. Unhide all reveals everything, including items hidden in the outliner, Unhide last restores in reverse order, and a steps selector sets how many return at once. Vertices, edges and faces can be hidden too, and since v1.1 mesh objects can be hidden by material.

Key features

  • Panel listing currently hidden objects
  • Hide in render option
  • Unhide all, last or by steps
  • Hide vertices, edges, faces or by material
