What Linkem - Your Ultimate Blender Add-On For Streamlined Workflow does

Linkem from EasyLight makes it easier to reuse content stored in separate .blend files. Instead of hunting through the Append dialog, you browse previews in dedicated panels inside Blender.

Material and object panels let you preview and assign or spawn items, collections can be linked and managed, and animations can be previewed and applied. A brush list panel handles brushes from different files, and entire scenes can be imported. Documentation is included, making it useful for building asset libraries.

Key features

  • Material preview and assignment from other files
  • Object preview and spawning
  • Link and manage external collections
  • Animation and brush panels
  • Import entire scenes
