Blender add-on that assigns viewport display colors to selected meshes in one click without touching materials.

What Colorize Objects does

Colorize Objects gives each selected mesh a custom viewport color in Blender with a single click. It only affects display colors, so materials and shaders remain unchanged and nothing needs undoing later.

Smart Color Management randomizes or cycles through your palette without repeating colors until necessary. The palette is fully customizable, with as many colors as you want to add, remove or tweak. It helps tell similar objects apart while blocking out geometry, organizing a busy scene or reviewing a model with others.

Key features

  • One-click viewport color assignment
  • Leaves materials and shaders untouched
  • Random or cycling color selection
  • No repeats until needed
  • Customizable palette
