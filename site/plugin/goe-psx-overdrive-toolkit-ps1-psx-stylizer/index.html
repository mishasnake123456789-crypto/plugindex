What Goe Psx Overdrive Toolkit Ps1 / Psx Stylizer does

GoE PSX Overdrive Toolkit pushes Blender scenes toward the look of mid-nineties console graphics. It ships ten style presets, twelve atmosphere presets and ten sky HDRs, plus tools for vertex wobble, dithering and old-school render settings.

Style presets such as PS1 Horror, Silent Hill Fog, VHS Dirty and CRT Monitor set geometry reduction, flat shading and overall intensity, and an authenticity slider scales the degradation. A Geometry Nodes wobble offers amplitude, frequency, hold frames and axis modes. Texture dithering has three methods, and render effects include low resolution, five-bit color and cheap shadows. Duplicate-first and restore tools keep the process safe.

Key features

  • Ten PSX style presets
  • Authenticity slider
  • Geometry Nodes vertex wobble
  • Three texture dithering methods
  • Backup and restore for materials
