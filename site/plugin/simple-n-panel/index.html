What Simple N-Panel does

Simple N-Panel is a Blender utility from Chipp Walters that collects the N-Panel tabs of your add-ons into a single popup menu. A heart-shaped button on the 3D View header opens it, or use Shift plus the accent grave key at the cursor, and the shortcut can be changed in preferences.

Shift-clicking the heart shows or hides the N-panel. A refresh option finds tabs from installed add-ons and extensions automatically, and from the preferences you can reorder panels and hide rarely used ones. Settings can be exported and imported, which keeps a crowded sidebar manageable.

Key features

  • Popup menu for all N-Panel add-on tabs
  • Heart button on the viewport header
  • Shift+accent grave hotkey, changeable
  • Automatic refresh finds new tabs
  • Reorder, hide and export panel settings
