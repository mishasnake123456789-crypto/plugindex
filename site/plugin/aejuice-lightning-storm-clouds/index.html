What AEJuice Lightning Storm Clouds does

Lightning Storm Clouds provides ready-made lightning and sky elements for landscapes, night scenes, movies, music videos and TV. All 68 clips are rendered in 4K and come as videos with transparent or black backgrounds, so they work in After Effects, Cinema 4D, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and other editors.

The set includes lightning with and without glow, in normal and slow motion, ten looping storm clouds with alpha, five real clouds, and three globe lightning loops. Files are high bitrate H264.

Key features

  • 68 prerendered 4K elements
  • Lightning with and without glow, slow-motion versions
  • Looping storm clouds and real clouds
  • Alpha channel for transparent compositing
  • High bitrate H264 files
