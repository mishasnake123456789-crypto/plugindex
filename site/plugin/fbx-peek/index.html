Previews FBX files in Blender without importing them, with raycast placement, PBR material linking and engine export.

What Fbx Peek does

FBX Peek lets you browse FBX assets from inside Blender without cluttering your open project. Models are rendered in a background scene, so nothing is imported until you choose.

Thumbnails use a matcap and cavity shader and render asynchronously with an animated progress bar. Raycast placement drops a model where the mouse points, PBR materials can be linked automatically, metadata can be inspected, and a game-engine exporter is included. It suits artists sorting through large FBX libraries.

Key features

  • Background FBX previews without importing
  • Matcap and cavity thumbnails, rendered asynchronously
  • Raycast placement at the mouse position
  • PBR material auto-linking
  • Metadata inspection and game-engine exporter
