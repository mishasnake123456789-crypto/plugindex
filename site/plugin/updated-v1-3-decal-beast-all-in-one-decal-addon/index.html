What Updated V1.3 - Decal Beast - All In One Decal Addon does

Decal Beast adds graffiti and decals to 3D models in Blender. You drag a decal onto a mesh, even one with several materials, or apply the same decal to many objects at the same time.

It ships with over 250 hand-drawn scribbles and more than 25 graffiti fonts, and lets you type custom words with control over word, line and letter spacing. Decals sit on layers that you reorder non-destructively, similar to Photoshop. Color, roughness and aging effects can be tuned, and everything can be baked into textures with one click.

Key features

  • Drag-and-drop decals on any mesh
  • 250+ scribbles and 25+ graffiti fonts
  • Custom text with adjustable spacing
  • Layer-based non-destructive editing
  • Color, roughness, aging and one-click bake
