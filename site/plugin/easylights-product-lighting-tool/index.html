Blender N-panel tool for quickly rigging product shots with IES, HDRI, rim, spherical and volumetric lights.

What Easylights- Product Lighting Tool does

Lighting a product by hand means placing lamps, tweaking values and repeating. Easylights moves that routine into Blender's N panel, under an EasyLight tab that works while you are in Object Mode: pick the model, run an operator, then refine the numbers.

IES and HDRI rigs go in with a single click, and three HDRIs ship with it. Other panels scatter the chosen lights within a rectangle using a random seed, wrap an object in volumetric fog with density and cube-size settings, and draw rim lights from the direction you sketch with annotations. Spherical layouts for area and point lights are included too.

Key features

  • One-click IES and HDRI lighting setups
  • Three bundled HDRIs
  • Randomize light position and color with a seed
  • Volumetrics panel with density and size controls
  • Annotation-driven rim lights
