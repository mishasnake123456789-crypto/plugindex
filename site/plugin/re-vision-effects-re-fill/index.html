Repairs holes and bad pixels in After Effects and Premiere Pro images by filling regions defined by your matte.

What RE:Vision Effects RE:Fill does

RE:Vision Effects RE:Fill mends damaged areas in images for After Effects and Premiere Pro. You provide a matte marking which pixels need fixing, and the plugin fills them using nearby good pixels, mirrored pixels around the hole, or cloned content from a good region.

The clone source can come from another point in time or another sequence. It fixes distortion gaps, gaps between lined-up objects and scratch lines, and extended alpha operators feather, grow, shrink and choke. Large holes can also become gradients, washes or kaleidoscopic mirror effects.

Key features

  • Fills holes via nearest pixels, mirroring or cloning
  • Clone from another time or sequence
  • Repairs scratches and warp gaps
  • Extended alpha operators
  • Gradient and kaleidoscope fills for large holes
