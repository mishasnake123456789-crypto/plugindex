Adds customizable procedural screws to Blender, with 33 heads, two thread profiles and cutter versions.

What Quick Screws does

Quick Screws creates high-quality procedural screws meant for hard surface work and mechanical mockups. There are four types: full screws with head and body, head-only screws, and two cutter types with objects that cut into other geometry.

It offers 33 head styles, a pair of materials and two thread profiles, all adjustable. A time saver for detailed mechanical models. Procedural parts stay editable, so changing size, head style or thread afterwards is quick compared with remodeling hardware.

Key features

  • Four screw types including cutters
  • 33 heads and two thread profiles
  • Two materials
  • Fully customizable
