What Stretch does

Stretch by irrealix is an After Effects plug-in that drags an image's pixels along curves you define with two mask shapes. The result is the streaked, elongated look common in abstract title cards and transitions.

It works in two modes, stretching either along or across the masks, and a Stretch Ramp gives precise control over how far pixels travel. Noise and ramp distortion can be animated through Noise Offset, a Strips Random Factor adds detail, and an Opacity Ramp fades the streaks. A single click sets up a seamless loop, and a wireframe view with an adjustable count of base mesh points shows the geometry. Motion designers get a fast route to flowing pixel trails.

Key features

  • Stretch Along or Across two mask shapes
  • Stretch Ramp for exact pixel travel
  • Noise and Ramp distortion with animated offset
  • One-click seamless loop
  • Mesh wireframe preview with adjustable points
