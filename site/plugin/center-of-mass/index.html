Calculates a rigged character's center of mass in Blender, projects it to the floor and shows balance.

What Center Of Mass does

Center Of Mass computes the center of mass for any rigged character or object and projects it onto the floor plane, so you can see how balance relates to the points of contact.

Masses can be assigned to bones or objects, following realistic human body mass distributions, and multiple centers of mass can exist in one scene. It helps animators make poses and movements that feel physically grounded. Highlights include automatic center of mass calculation, floor projection and balance display and realistic mass distribution for bones.

Key features

  • Automatic center of mass calculation
  • Floor projection and balance display
  • Realistic mass distribution for bones
  • Multiple centers per scene
