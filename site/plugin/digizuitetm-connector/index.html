What Digizuite™ Connector does

The Digizuite Connector brings the Digizuite digital asset management platform into After Effects and Premiere Pro. Editors can look for images, graphics, video and other rich media and insert them into a project without switching to a browser.

Changes made to files inside the Adobe applications are saved back to the DAM automatically, and check-in and check-out prevents anyone overwriting someone else's work. You can filter and browse folders, preview an asset next to its metadata, and sign in with single sign-on or DAM credentials. Local files can be uploaded and shared with colleagues, with user rights protecting them.

Key features

  • Search and insert DAM assets in Adobe apps
  • Edits saved back to the DAM automatically
  • Check-in and check-out to avoid overwrites
  • Preview assets with their metadata
  • Single sign-on or DAM credentials
