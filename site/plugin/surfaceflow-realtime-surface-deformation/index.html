What Surfaceflow - Realtime Surface Deformation does

Surfaceflow conforms one mesh to another in Blender instantly. Choose a target mesh and the modifier wraps onto it, with no binding, unwrapping or baking involved.

Results update in real time while you change settings, even on complex meshes. It performs true wrapping, not just projection, with controls for offset, masking, limits, quality and more. As a native modifier it adds no extra panels. Modelers fitting armor, clothing, decals or panels to curved bodies will iterate quickly.

Key features

  • Real-time surface wrapping
  • No binding, unwrapping or baking
  • True wrapping beyond projection
  • Offset, masking and limit controls
