What Pastiche2 does

Pastiche2 by Motion Boutique turns any shape, text or footage into a distributed collage built from native After Effects layers with a single click. It can then morph the arrangement across those generated layers.

Spatial Interpolation defines the trajectories during displacement, with Linear, Inner/Outer Half Circle, ZigZag, custom and other styles. It runs on Apple M1 (Silicon) architecture, and shows a warning when a layer's position has the Separate Dimensions switch on.

Key features

  • One-click collage from shapes, text or footage
  • Morphing animation across generated layers
  • Spatial Interpolation trajectory styles
  • Apple Silicon support
  • Separate Dimensions warning
