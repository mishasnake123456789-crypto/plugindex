What miauu's Tab Panel does

Tab Panel by miauu tidies up the 3ds Max interface by turning toolbars into tabs. Existing toolbars can be converted, and new tabs can be created and filled with action buttons of your choice.

Tabs can be renamed, hidden and shown, and rearranged to suit your workflow, and any tab can be converted back to a toolbar if you change your mind. Users with many custom script toolbars cluttering the screen can reclaim space and find tools faster, without losing anything.

Key features

  • Converts existing toolbars into tabs
  • Creates new tabs with action buttons
  • Rename, hide, show and rearrange tabs
  • Converts tabs back to toolbars
