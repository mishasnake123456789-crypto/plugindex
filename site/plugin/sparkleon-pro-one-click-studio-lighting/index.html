What Sparkleon Pro - One-Click Studio Lighting does

Sparkleon Pro creates a full studio system inside Blender from a single selection. Pick an object and a look, and it generates the lights, environment, camera framing and render settings as editable Blender objects.

The library holds 55 lighting presets covering clean product shots through dramatic and stylized looks, plus 50 studio environments built from real geometry across nine stage shapes such as cyclorama, dome, V-wall and L-corner. Smart Setup styles combine the pieces further. Product and visualization artists get a repeatable lighting setup without placing every light by hand.

Key features

  • 55 lighting presets from product to cinematic
  • 50 studio environments in nine stage shapes
  • Smart Setup styles
  • Lights and camera stay editable objects
  • Render settings generated automatically
