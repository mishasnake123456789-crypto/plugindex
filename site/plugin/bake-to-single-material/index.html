What Bake To Single Material does

Bake To Single Material, by Adam-Bernard, combines the multiple materials on an object into a single baked material, which simplifies models for export and real-time use. It is listed under Material and Import-Export.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.1.0 or newer.
