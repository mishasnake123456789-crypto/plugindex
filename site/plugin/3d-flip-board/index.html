What 3D Flip Board does

3D Flip Board from Froggy Team produces a fully animated split-flap board in After Effects with a single click. It works for text displays or as a transition between images.

Animation can run from keyframes or be driven by markers, and pseudo effect controls give access to adjustments. Four auto flip modes are available: Bi-direction, Forward, Backward and One Flip. The download includes a sample project to learn from. It gives designers a classic airport-board look without building the geometry.

Key features

  • Animated 3D flip board in one click
