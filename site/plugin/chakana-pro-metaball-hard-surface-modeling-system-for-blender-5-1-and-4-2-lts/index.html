What Chakana Pro | Metaball Hard Surface Modeling System For Blender 5.1 & 4.2 Lts does

Chakana Pro is a Blender plugin that uses metaballs for quick prototyping of hard surface designs. You build forms from volumetric shapes, then convert the result into clean, automatically remeshed geometry ready for detailing.

Five primitives are provided: Ball, Capsule, Plane, Ellipsoid and Cube, plus a global Resolution slider and a pie menu in the viewport. A negative tool switches a metaball to subtraction so it cuts recesses, holes and concave areas. It is aimed at concept modelers who want to iterate quickly.

Key features

  • Ball, Capsule, Plane, Ellipsoid and Cube primitives
  • Negative tool for carving recesses and holes
  • Global resolution slider and pie menu
  • Converts to remeshed geometry
