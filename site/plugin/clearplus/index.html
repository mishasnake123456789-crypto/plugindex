What ClearPlus does

ClearPlus, from vimager, is made for footage that looks washed out or murky. It improves hazy, underwater and low-light clips in After Effects and Premiere Pro, with dedicated modes for each type of problem.

An advanced dehaze mode is designed to limit halo effects. Contrast and histogram modes aim to avoid flicker, and a Details slider gives further control over sharpness. Multi-Frame Rendering is supported, and the plugin runs natively on Apple M1 silicon.

Key features

  • Dehaze, underwater and low-light improvement
  • Dehaze mode that reduces halos
  • Contrast and histogram modes against flicker
  • Details slider
  • Multi-Frame Rendering and Apple M1 support
