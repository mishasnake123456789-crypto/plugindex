A glow and light-beam tool for After Effects and Premiere Pro with masking, blur direction and colour controls.

What AEJuice Glowy Effect does

AEJuice Glowy Effect collects beam angle, light intensity, colour and other glow settings in one location. Masks isolate the glow to chosen objects in the frame, so you can brighten a single element rather than everything.

Blur direction can be customised, Lighten blend mode is used with masks and Screen for the whole frame, and a Glow Effect Fast option shortens rendering. It runs in After Effects CC 2015 or newer and Premiere Pro CC 2021 or newer. Editors and motion designers looking to add atmosphere to objects or full shots will find it quick to apply.

Key features

  • Mask support to isolate the glow
  • Beam angle, intensity and colour controls
  • Customisable blur direction
  • Lighten and Screen blend modes
  • Fast option for quicker renders
