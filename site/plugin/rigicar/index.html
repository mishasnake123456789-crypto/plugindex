What Rigicar does

Rigicar is a Blender add-on by Picto Filmo for realistic vehicle animation that needs no rig or bones. Enable it on the car body, animate its location along a path, and the wheels spin, steer and bank automatically based on speed.

Dynamic dampers can be switched on with a click, and front and rear axles can have separate wheel sizes. Anchors in the setup panel tie other objects to the body, wheels, brakes or steering, and brake controllers are in the advanced animation panel. A RigiCar setup can be appended into a new project.

Key features

  • No rig or bones needed
  • Automatic wheel spin, steering and banking
  • Real-time dynamic dampers
  • Separate front and rear wheel sizes
  • Brake controllers and helper anchors
