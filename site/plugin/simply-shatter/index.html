Blender add-on for controllable shattering of glass and objects, generating fractures driven by physics simulation.

What Simply Shatter does

Simply Shatter, from TAngraFX and Simply, creates fracture patterns and lets physics drive the breakup of glass, objects and other materials. Low poly and high poly fracture methods are available, each with tunable resolution and threshold.

Auto Keyframe and Fix Normals are supported, along with transform controls that include randomization. The result is a convenient route to smashing effects for VFX shots, product reveals and motion graphics.

Key features

  • Low poly and high poly fracture methods
  • Adjustable resolution and threshold
  • Physics-driven simulation
  • Auto Keyframe and Fix Normals
  • Transform controls with randomization
