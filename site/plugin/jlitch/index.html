What Jlitch does

Jlitch from Pixel Sorter Studio generates glitches by imitating the way video compression breaks and by feeding frames back into themselves. The results range from faint digital decay to wildly psychedelic output.

It runs in both After Effects and Premiere Pro, and host versions 2021 through 2026 are listed as compatible. Version 1.1.1 refreshed the underlying framework, and there is a demo video on YouTube. Glitch artists and editors after abstract visuals or a damaged-signal feel can dial the amount of corruption to taste.

Key features

  • Glitches from compression errors and accumulation feedback
  • Subtle decay to psychedelic chaos
  • After Effects and Premiere Pro
  • Compatible with 2021 to 2026 host versions
