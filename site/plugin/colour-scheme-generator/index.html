What Colour Scheme Generator does

Colour Scheme Generator is an After Effects plugin from NT Productions. It builds an adjustable palette of matching colors pulled from your image, giving you a quick reference or a starting point for graphics and grading choices.

This free release is version 1.0, published on December 21, 2021, and runs on After Effects releases from CC through 2022. A demo video is available online. It is aimed at artists who want swatches that harmonize with their footage.

Key features

  • Generates a palette of colors matching an image
  • Adjustable palette
  • Works with After Effects CC through 2022
  • Version 1.0 initial release
  • Demo video available
