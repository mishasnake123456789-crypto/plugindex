What Restart: A Simple Functionality does

Giambattista Von Nettesheim's Restart: A Simple Functionality gives Blender a one-click restart so you reset the session without closing and reopening it yourself.

It lives in the Blender interface and installs the usual way through Preferences > Add-ons. It helps when troubleshooting, testing add-ons or clearing memory. Developers and tinkerers who relaunch often will appreciate the shortcut. Because it needs no configuration, it can stay installed permanently and be ignored until a reset is useful.
