What AEJuice Human Anatomy does

AEJuice Human Anatomy is a content pack for After Effects and Element 3D that helps produce medical presentations. It supplies over 380 ready-made scenes of the body, brain, heart, liver, intestines, lungs, stomach, kidneys, skull and more, each with 32 shots.

Extras include 40-plus OBJ 3D models, 27 Element 3D presets, 41 animation comps, five text scenes, 36 animated icons and nine infographic elements. Twelve real MRI videos and 35 X-ray images are included. Expression controls animate skin, muscle, skeleton and inner layers. A pre-rendered option suits other video editors.
