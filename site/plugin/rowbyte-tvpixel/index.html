What Rowbyte TVPixel does

TVPixel by Rowbyte makes video look as if it's displayed on a screen seen close up. Footage is divided into red, green and blue pixels in the pattern of a television or LCD panel.

The gap between pixels can be adjusted, which controls how pronounced the grid appears. After Effects gets 32-bit floating point support, and the effect works in Premiere Pro too, on both Mac and Windows. It's useful for retro displays, monitor insert shots and glitch aesthetics.

Key features

  • TV or LCD sub-pixel pattern
  • Adjustable pixel gap
  • 32-bit float in After Effects
  • After Effects and Premiere Pro
  • Mac and Windows
