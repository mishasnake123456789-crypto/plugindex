What Env Lights Design does

Env Lights Design lets lighting follow your shots. Once a Light Set is made and tied to a camera, its atmosphere takes effect every time that camera becomes active.

A Light Set treats sky, sun, clouds, fog and tone as a single look, and the sky can be built or an HDRI connected. It benefits animators and filmmakers who change mood across camera cuts. This is helpful in animations with several shots, where each camera cut needs a distinct sky or weather without manual swaps.

Key features

  • Light Sets combine sky, sun, clouds, fog and tone
  • Assign a Light Set to each camera
  • Build a sky or connect an HDRI
  • Environment follows the shot
