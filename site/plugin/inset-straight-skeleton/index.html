What Inset Straight Skeleton does

Inset Straight Skeleton produces an inset within a selection of mesh faces by applying a straight skeleton algorithm. It is an operator in the 3D View, and Blender 4.1 used to bundle it.

It supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer and is distributed under GPL v2 or later. Modelers creating panels, floor plans and roofs who need uniform inset borders that handle irregular outlines benefit from the algorithm. Unlike a simple scaled inset, a straight skeleton follows the outline so corners and concave shapes stay consistent.
