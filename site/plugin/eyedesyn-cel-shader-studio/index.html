What eyedesyn Cel Shader Studio does

eyedesyn Cel Shader Studio is a library of over 900 procedural cel-style materials for Cinema 4D. It works with any version of Cinema 4D and Cineware, including C4D Lite, and does not need Sketch & Toon.

Styles range from watercolor, smudges, brush strokes, grain, diffuse, shiny diffuse and halftone to wood, paint, stone and grunge, along with fully tileable 2D wood, stone and pattern cel textures. Components such as specular, shadow and outline layers can be mixed and matched. Designers wanting a hand-drawn or illustrated look in 3D can assemble it quickly.

Key features

  • 900+ procedural cel shader materials
  • Watercolor, brush stroke, grain and halftone styles
  • Wood, paint, stone and grunge variants
  • Tileable 2D textures
  • No Sketch & Toon required
