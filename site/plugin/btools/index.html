What Btools does

Btools combines everyday Blender utilities in a pie menu opened with Ctrl+W. It covers object selection, renaming, materials, snapping, alignment and flipping.

Objects Checker Deselect applies an alternating selection pattern across objects. Super Rename renames objects, meshes, materials and collections together, with an optional suffix. Add Materials applies a material to many selected objects at once, with a viewport display color that defaults to random. Further tools handle snapping, alignment and flips. General Blender users and modelers who like pie-menu access to small helpers will find it convenient.

Key features

  • Pie menu opened with Ctrl+W
  • Checker Deselect for objects
  • Super Rename for objects, meshes, materials and collections
  • Add Materials to many objects at once
  • Snapping, alignment and flipping tools
