What Autofocus Pro does

Autofocus Pro adds one-click focusing to the Blender camera, giving depth of field that behaves like a real camera. You can focus anywhere in the frame without creating shrinkwrap modifiers for each scene, and pull focus smoothly.

Ready-made focus regions target the middle of the frame, its left third, or a subject crossing the frame, and custom regions are drawn as a bounding box. Focus pulls can imitate a real lens or work quickly for fast action, and focus is keyframed on every frame automatically. Animators and product renderers save time on rack-focus shots.

Key features

  • Single-click autofocus on the camera
  • Center, left-third and moving-subject regions
  • Custom regions drawn as a bounding box
  • Smooth focus pulls or quick focusing
  • Automatic keyframing on every frame
