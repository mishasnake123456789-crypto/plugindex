What Camera Viewer does

Camera Viewer is a Blender add-on by KENKEN that displays the camera view as a preview within the 3D viewport, so you can watch the shot while working elsewhere in the scene. The camera can be locked, and the preview can be placed and transformed as you wish.

Overlay settings are independent from the main viewport and adjustable with UI buttons, and color management is supported. A navigation mode uses W, A, S, D for movement, E and Q for height, Shift to pan and the scroll wheel to change focal length. Display can be Solid, Material or Rendered, with Alt+F1 to toggle and F1 to modify.

Key features

  • Camera preview inside the 3D viewport
  • Lock camera and adjustable preview position
  • Independent overlays and color management
  • WASD navigation with focal length via scroll
  • Solid, Material and Rendered display modes
