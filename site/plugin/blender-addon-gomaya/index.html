What Blender Addon Gomaya does

Blender Addon Gomaya streamlines passing models between Blender and Maya. It removes the repeated export and import cycle that artists otherwise go through when switching applications.

Model data and original pivot coordinates are preserved during the exchange. It supports Blender 4 and up, including 5.0, and Maya 2022 through 2026. Documentation is provided and the add-on is released under the GPL.

Key features

  • Direct data transfer between Blender and Maya
  • Preserves pivots and model data
  • Supports Blender 4+ and Maya 2022-2026
  • Removes manual export and import steps
