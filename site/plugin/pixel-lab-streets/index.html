What Pixel Lab Streets does

Pixel Lab Streets pairs 4K road textures with street props for building urban scenes in Cinema 4D. The textures come in almost 100 variations, UVW mapped and PBR based, split into dry painted, dry simple, wet simple and wet painted sets that can be mixed and matched.

Nearly 100 low-polygon kitbash models add signs, lampposts, guard rails, trash cans, bridges, sidewalks and subway entrances with detailed textures. Versions are prepared for Octane and Redshift as .lib4d files, with OBJ and FBX files and full PBR maps for other renderers. Environment artists can assemble roads quickly.

Key features

  • Nearly 100 4K PBR road textures
  • Dry, wet, painted and simple road variants
  • Nearly 100 low-poly street kitbash models
  • Octane and Redshift .lib4d libraries
  • OBJ, FBX and PBR maps for other renderers
