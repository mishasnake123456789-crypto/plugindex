An iZotope effects plugin for Premiere Pro CC 2025 combining saturation, lo-fi, modulation, reverb and delay behind per-effect EQ.

What iZotope FXEQ does

iZotope FXEQ gives each of its five creative effects its own equalizer. Saturation, lo-fi, modulation, reverb and delay can be shaped by a 6-band EQ with four filter shapes and adjustable Q.

Saturate has 8 modes, Lo-Fi has 4, modulation covers chorus, flanger, phaser and doubler, and there are 3 reverbs and 4 delay types. A zero-latency limiter prevents clipping, and 50-plus presets plus a random picker speed up exploring. FX Only mode drops the dry signal for use on sends.

Key features

  • 6-band EQ for each effect
  • Saturate and Lo-Fi modules
  • Chorus, flanger, phaser and doubler
  • 3 reverbs and 4 delay types
  • 50+ presets and FX Only mode
