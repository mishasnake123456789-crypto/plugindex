What Dither Generator does

Dither Generator brings stylized dithering looks to Blender renders in Eevee and Cycles without complex node setups.

Styles include Bayer, Halftone, ASCII, Hex Halftone and Noise, with scale, warp and line distortion controls. Retro color spaces cover RGB, CIELAB, YIQ (NTSC), monochrome and palettes. Scanlines and masking target edges, shadows or highlights, and a post stack adds glow, blur and chromatic aberration. Results can be adjusted live, which is handy for stylized games, posters and retro-themed videos.

Key features

  • Bayer, Halftone, ASCII, Hex Halftone and Noise
  • Pattern scale, warp and line distortion
  • Retro color spaces and palettes
  • Scanlines and targeted masking
  • Post stack with glow, blur and aberration
