What Magic Vertex Painter does

Magic Vertex Painter combines vertex painting with procedural scattering, letting you brush assets such as grass, pebbles and flowers onto a model with real-time feedback.

Brushes have Draw, Eraser and Blur modes, and one object can hold up to 10 asset channels, each with density, seed, scale and rotation controls and a vertical growth slider. Ground masks accept custom images, a procedural ground shader uses Noise, Voronoi, Wave and Brick textures, and each channel gets its own procedural wind animation.

Key features

  • Brush-based asset painting
  • Up to 10 independent asset channels
  • Masks and procedural ground shader
  • Per-channel wind animation
