A Geometry Nodes Blender add-on that generates procedural flames on meshes or drawn curves, with smoke, sparks and burn marks.

What Flame Creator does

Flame Creator builds flames in Blender procedurally through Geometry Nodes. Flames can be attached to any mesh object, or you can draw curves to place them freely, with control over size, height, smoke, sparks and colors.

Size and height are adjustable with vertex group masking, a curve drawing tool places flames directly, and particle sparks add detail. Smoke can be volumetric, 2D or both, and burn marks darken the surface. It renders in EEVEE and Cycles, though EEVEE Next in 4.2 and later may need tweaks to match color and brightness. VFX artists will find it flexible for stylized and realistic fire.

Key features

  • Geometry Nodes flame generation
  • Size and height with vertex group masking
  • Curve drawing tool
  • Particle sparks, smoke and burn marks
  • EEVEE and Cycles rendering
