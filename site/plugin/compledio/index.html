An AI panel for Premiere Pro that generates B-roll from timeline audio and places it on the V2 track.

What Compledio does

Compledio B-Roll listens to your timeline's audio and context, then generates B-roll clips that fit the narrative. It runs natively as a dockable panel in Premiere Pro.

Clips are inserted on the V2 track synced to the audio, which requires Premiere Pro 25.0 or later, and generated assets are organized into bins automatically. Up to three style reference images can guide the look, and frames can be captured from the timeline to use as references.

Key features

  • Contextual AI B-roll generation
  • Placement on V2 synced to audio
  • Dockable native panel
  • Automatic bin organization
  • Up to three style reference images
