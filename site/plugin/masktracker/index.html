What MaskTracker+ does

MaskTracker+ from mamoworld tools builds on the Mask Tracker built into After Effects, adding planar tracking capability. You load tracking data from a mask and then turn it into something useful.

Available functions include a move, a corner pin and a stabilized precomp. It handles masks tracked by the built-in tracker, data pasted from mocha, manually keyframed masks and information from other sources. This helps with screen replacements, patching and stabilizing shots without leaving After Effects. It is compatible with releases from CC up to 2026.

Key features

  • Loads tracking data from a selected mask
  • Move, corner pin and stabilized precomp outputs
  • Accepts data pasted from mocha
  • Works with manually keyframed masks
  • Compatible from CC through 2026
