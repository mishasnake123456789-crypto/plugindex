What Ae Track 2 Blend does

Ae Track 2 Blend, by Watermelon, carries a camera solved in Adobe After Effects into Blender. It rebuilds the tracked scene so you can continue working with the same camera motion.

It imports the camera with its frame rate and timing, sets focal length and sensor fit, puts the source video in the camera background, brings in solid planes and adjusts the scene position. Documentation is included.

Key features

  • Imports camera, FPS and timing
  • Sets focal length and sensor fit
  • Adds source video as camera background
  • Imports solid planes
  • Documentation included
