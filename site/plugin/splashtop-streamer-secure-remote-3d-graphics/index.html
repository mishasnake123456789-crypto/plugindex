What Splashtop Streamer -- Secure Remote 3D Graphics does

Splashtop Streamer lets 3ds Max artists work on a high-end workstation from elsewhere. The desktop streams to Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, Android and Chromebook devices at up to 4K and 40 fps with low latency, and Retina 5K iMac Pro streaming is supported.

Hardware-accelerated encoding and decoding uses Intel, NVIDIA and AMD, multiple monitors can be streamed, and remote stylus and HID devices such as Wacom tablets work. Security includes TLS 1.2, AES 256-bit encryption, device authentication and two-factor authentication. Enterprise options include single sign-on, scheduled access and an on-premises version.
