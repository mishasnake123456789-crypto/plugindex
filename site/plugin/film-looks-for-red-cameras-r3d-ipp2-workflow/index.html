What Film Looks for Red Cameras R3d Ipp2 Workflow does

Film Looks for Red Cameras is a small set of four LUTs by Thiago Vibesp, built for footage graded in the R3D IPP2 workflow. They come as prfpset files for loading into Premiere Pro.

They are described as suited to RED models including Komodo, Helium, Monstro and Gemini. After installation the files usually appear in the Downloads folder on Mac or Windows, and the developer offers support by message. Colorists and filmmakers who want a quick cinematic starting point for RED material can apply them directly to clips.

Key features

  • Four LUTs in prfpset format
  • Made for the R3D IPP2 workflow
  • Fits Komodo, Helium, Monstro, Gemini and other RED models
  • For use in Adobe Premiere Pro
