What DOSCH 3D: Cars 2010 - Europe does

European makes are the focus of Dosch 3D: Cars 2010 - Europe, a Cinema 4D pack of 18 fully textured vehicles. Marques include Alfa Romeo, Audi, Jaguar, Mercedes and Porsche.

The models can be scaled to suit a scene, and materials, textures and colors are open to change, with your own textures and labels supported. Individual elements may be turned off or made transparent, and animation is possible for parts or whole cars. They are intended for architectural visualizations, simulations and renderings that need credible vehicles on streets or driveways.

Key features

  • Adjustable scale
  • Editable materials, colors and labels
  • Switchable, transparent, animatable elements
