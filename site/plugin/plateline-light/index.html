What Plateline Light does

Plateline Light, by Hannes-Werner-3, sets up cameras in bulk based on plates and image sequences, so each shot can be matched quickly. It is tagged under Camera, Animation and Import-Export.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.
