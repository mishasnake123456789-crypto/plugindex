What Dosch 3D: Interior Details does

Dosch 3D: Interior Details is a large collection of 160 textured models for furnishing visualizations of homes and offices in Cinema 4D. The range covers interior objects that make rooms look lived in.

Each item can be scaled, retextured and edited freely, with colors changed and personal textures or labels applied. Elements can be switched off or made partially transparent, and they can be animated. Architects, interior designers and visualization artists will find the volume useful for populating many scenes without hunting for individual props.

Key features

  • 160 detailed textured interior models
  • Residential and office objects
  • Adjustable scale, materials and colors
  • Elements switchable with transparency
  • Animatable models
