A Premiere Pro extension bringing AI generation tools into the timeline for B-roll, transitions, relighting and style.

What Chat Video Pro does

Chat Video Pro puts more than ten AI workflows inside Premiere Pro so editors can generate material without leaving the timeline. It makes B-roll stills with camera, lens and aperture controls, and has a library of camera moves that can be applied to any still.

Two-clip transitions can be auto-filled from templates or your prompt, scenes can be relit by moving key, fill and rim, and a mood and style can be transferred with one prompt. Output arrives frame-accurate and timeline-aware.

Key features

  • Generated stills with camera and lens controls
  • Preset camera moves for any still
  • AI-filled transitions between two clips
  • Directional relight
  • Mood and style transfer
