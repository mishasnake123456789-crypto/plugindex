What Leximo does

Leximo is a Premiere Pro plugin for localizing voice content. It translates what people say in your project and generates new speech in other languages, using voice synthesis to keep the qualities and tone of the original voice so nothing needs re-recording.

The tools sit within the editing workflow, from the timeline through to final export. Videos, courses, documentaries and social reels can all be turned into multilingual versions without a separate dubbing session. Creators and small production teams reaching international audiences are the likely users.

Key features

  • Translates spoken content into multiple languages
  • Voice synthesis keeps voice character and tone
  • No re-recording of the original delivery
  • Generates translated voice audio automatically
  • Works from timeline to final export
