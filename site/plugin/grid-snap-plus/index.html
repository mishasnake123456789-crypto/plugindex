What Grid Snap Plus does

Grid Snap Plus improves Blender's grid snapping by allowing different snap increments on the X, Y and Z axes, which fits tile-based and voxel-style projects.

Increments remain constant at any zoom level in both perspective and orthographic views. Presets such as 0.5 × 0.5 × 0.5 or 2 × 2 × 2 speed up setup, and snapping can be relative to a selected object or geometry, an object's origin or the world origin. It gives modular level designers precise placement.

Key features

  • Independent X, Y, Z snap intervals
  • Constant increments at any zoom
  • Quick preset values
  • Relative to selection, origin or world
