What Procedural Light Studio Addon does

Procedural Light Studio sets up photography-style lighting in Blender through geometry nodes. It offers five light types: left, right, front, rim and top panel, which together make a classic studio arrangement for product and character shots.

Left and right lights simulate side sources, with adjustable angles and intensities for shadows and highlights. The rim light creates a halo around an object's edges, and top emission panels add depth from above. An optional volumetric fog panel adds atmosphere. Product visualizers and portfolio artists will get a controllable studio quickly.

Key features

  • Five geometry-node light types
  • Adjustable side lights
  • Rim light with adjustable intensity
  • Top emission panels
  • Optional volumetric fog panel
