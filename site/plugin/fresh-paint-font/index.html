What Fresh Paint Font does

Fresh Paint Font by Highground is a graffiti-style tagging typeface for After Effects and Premiere Pro. Its letters are thick, bold and legible, drawn in a rough paintbrush manner with detailed streaks that suggest quick, organic motion.

The set holds two alphabets and 174 characters covering letters, numbers, symbols and punctuation. Extras include streaks, splats and underlines for decoration. It works with After Effects 15.0 and later and Premiere Pro 12.0 and later, suited to street-style titles and posters.

Key features

  • Graffiti handstyle in rough paintbrush style
  • Two alphabets of bold, legible letters
  • 174 characters
  • Streaks, splats and underlines
  • After Effects 15.0+ and Premiere Pro 12.0+
