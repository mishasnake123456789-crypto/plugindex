What Citybuilder 3D does

CityBuilder 3D lets Blender artists assemble city scenes from more than 115 modeled and textured buildings. Designed by artists working in Hollywood, it adds each asset to a scene with a button click.

There are eight themed kits of fifteen assets each, styled as Industrial, Metropolitan, Soviet, Hong Kong, Derelict Future, Cyberpunk Clean Future, Medieval and Warzone, with an extra procedural asset that varies randomly. Materials are tuned for Cycles. Filmmakers and game designers needing quick skylines or backdrops can populate large scenes fast.

Key features

  • 115+ building assets
  • Eight themed kits
  • Bonus procedural asset with variation
  • Materials optimized for Cycles
