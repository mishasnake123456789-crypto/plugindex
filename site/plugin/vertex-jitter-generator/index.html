What Vertex Jitter Generator does

Vertex Jitter Generator recreates the wobbling vertices seen in early 3D games. One click builds a Displacement modifier, a noise texture and an Empty controller, with the drivers already wired up.

Amplitude, Noise Scale and Speed are adjusted in real time from the Vertex Jitter N-panel. The animation is driven by math-based drivers that follow the timeline, and the setup can be removed again. The effect is non-destructive, making it appealing for retro-styled shorts and games.

Key features

  • One-click modifier, texture and controller setup
  • Amplitude, Noise Scale and Speed controls
  • Driver-based, timeline-reactive animation
  • Removable and non-destructive
