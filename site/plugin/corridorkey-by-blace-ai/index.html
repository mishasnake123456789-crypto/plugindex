What CorridorKey by blace.ai does

This is Blace's take on CorridorKey, a green screen keyer created in cooperation with Corridor Digital. It relies on specially trained AI models to deliver keys ready to composite, available in After Effects and Premiere Pro.

The models repair transparent pixels, and several of them ship with the plugin. It can be paired with After Effects' own Advanced Spill Suppressor to remove colour bleed. The code is written entirely in C++ for stability, with reported render times two to four times shorter. Speed is best on NVIDIA and Windows through CUDA, but AMD GPUs are also supported.

Key features

  • High-quality AI green screen keys
  • Models repair transparent pixels
  • Several custom-trained models included
  • Pairs with Advanced Spill Suppressor
  • C++ build; CUDA and AMD GPU acceleration
