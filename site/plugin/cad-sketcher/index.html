What CAD Sketcher does

CAD Sketcher, by hlorus, brings constraint-based sketching to Blender: you draw parametric geometry and define dimensions and relationships, as in a CAD program. It is listed under 3D View, Modeling, Mesh and Object.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 5.0.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Parametric geometry sketching
