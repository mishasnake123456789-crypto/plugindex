What Realblackhole 2.0 - Procedural Realistic Generator Addon does

Realblackhole 2.0 builds a realistic black hole scene in Blender's Cycles engine. Rather than assembling shaders manually, the add-on generates the black hole, an accretion disk and a space background procedurally, using custom nodes.

Each element can be adjusted and animated individually, and the custom nodes help manage textures across the composition so changes remain manageable. A guide and a presentation of the add-on are included. Space and sci-fi visual artists who need a believable cosmic centerpiece for stills or animations are the people who will gain most.

Key features

  • Procedural black hole, accretion disk and background
  • Custom nodes manage textures across the composition
  • Each element adjustable and animatable
  • Built for Cycles
