What QuickOpen does

QuickOpen speeds up the routine of opening, managing and saving projects in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Workflows are customisable so they suit the way you organise your files.

You can jump straight to recent files or navigate folders to find a project, and saving via Save As takes you to your chosen location, where file names can be typed in. Existing versions are checked before saving to avoid clutter or overwriting. Artists and editors who juggle many versioned project files will cut down on browsing.

Key features

  • Open recent files or browse folders
  • Save As with navigation to the chosen location
  • Manual filename entry
  • Existing version check before saving
  • Works in After Effects and Premiere Pro
