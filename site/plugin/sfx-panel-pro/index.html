What SFX Panel Pro does

SFX Panel Pro is a Premiere Pro panel where you register your most-used sound effects and drop them onto the timeline instantly. Each effect keeps its own volume, frame offset and trim settings, which are applied automatically.

Placement happens at the playhead, at the start of every selected clip, or at every clip on a chosen video track, all in one undo step. When the target track is occupied, you can choose to be warned, use an empty track below, or overwrite based on real clip duration. Trim on import removes silence from the head, tail or both. Editors who cut with many repeated effects work faster.

Key features

  • One-click placement with volume and offset applied
  • Batch placement on selected clips or a video track
  • Occupancy handling: warn, empty track or overwrite
  • Trim import from head, tail or both
  • Single undo for batch operations
