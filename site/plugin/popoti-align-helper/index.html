Blender add-on for aligning and distributing objects by position, rotation and scale from the viewing perspective.

What POPOTI Align Helper does

POPOTI Align Helper brings alignment tools like those in Photoshop or PowerPoint to Blender. Objects are aligned along XYZ coordinates as seen from your current view, covering position, rotation and scale.

Two distribution modes space objects uniformly by origin or spacing, or fix the first and last objects and spread the middle ones. Ground align snaps objects to the ground individually or together, and a fall function projects objects down onto others without physics. Layout artists arranging props and furniture speed up scene dressing.

Key features

  • Perspective-based alignment
  • Two distribution modes
  • Ground align
  • Fall function using Z-ray projection
