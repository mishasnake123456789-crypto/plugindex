What Softwrap - Dynamics For Retopology does

Softwrap lets you recycle the topology of existing models for retopology in Blender. A purpose-built softbody simulation drapes the mesh over a sculpt or scan, much as a shrinkwrap modifier would.

Inside, outside and surface snapping modes are provided, along with a gravity force and a Seam Force like that of Blender's cloth simulator. Quad Smooth applies forces to restore quad shapes, while the subtler Topologic Smooth avoids shrinking the mesh, and stiffness can be controlled. Character artists can adapt a base mesh onto new scans quickly.

Key features

  • Softbody simulation snaps to sculpts
  • Inside, outside and surface modes
  • Gravity and Seam Force
  • Quad Smooth and Topologic Smooth
