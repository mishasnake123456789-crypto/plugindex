What Aejuice Flash FX Elements does

Animated elemental effects drawn frame by frame make up AEJuice Flash FX Elements. It covers fire, earth, water, air and lightning, and is meant for editors who want stylised magic or action accents without building them from scratch.

Over 160 clips are delivered as .mov files with an alpha channel. They work in After Effects CC 2015 and later and Premiere Pro CC 2021 and later, with DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, Filmora, Final Cut, Nuke and others handling the transparent or black-background videos. Older After Effects releases can open project versions supplied as is. Windows 10+ and macOS 10.14+ are supported, including M1 and M2.
