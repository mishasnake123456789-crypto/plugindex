Breaks After Effects layers into Voronoi or patterned pieces as masks or shape layers.

What Voronoi Shatter does

Voronoi Shatter is an extension that fractures a layer into pieces. It writes Voronoi masks onto selected layers or creates Voronoi shape layers, depending on your need.

The pattern may be Voronoi with a chosen piece count, or square, triangle, horizontal-line or vertical-line layouts. A brightness map can arrange pieces by pixel luminance, size is adjustable with optional randomness, and Shape Clip uses a layer's own masks as the outer edge. Good for shatter reveals and cellular graphics.

Key features

  • Voronoi masks and shape layers
  • Selectable patterns: Voronoi, square, triangle, lines
  • Brightness map arrangement
  • Adjustable size with randomness
  • Shape Clip for outer edges
