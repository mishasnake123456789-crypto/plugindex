What ZG Social Video Kit does

ZG Social Video Kit, by ZeroGeometry, collects tools for making short-form video in Blender: reframing to vertical, syncing animation to beats, building seamless loops and rendering wedges. It is tagged under Animation, Render and Camera.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
