Blender city generator add-on that builds roads, towers, parks, cars and environments.

What Icity does

iCity assembles urban scenes in Blender from roads, towers, parks, vehicles and their surroundings. It fits car simulations, real-time animated cityscapes and architectural design work.

Documentation and a demo video are provided, and it supports Blender 4.5 through 5.0. Archviz artists, game developers and animators needing city backdrops can generate large urban layouts instead of modeling each block. Having streets, buildings and traffic produced together keeps the scale and style of a scene consistent from one block to the next.

Key features

  • Builds streets, towers, parks and vehicles
  • For simulations, animation and architecture
  • Documentation and demo video
  • Blender 4.5 through 5.0
