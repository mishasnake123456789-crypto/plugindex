What Parametric Structures Pro does

Parametric Structures Pro generates architectural forms in Blender that can be adjusted in real time. It covers tensile membranes, domes and hyperbolic paraboloids, and converts curves into 3D shapes with mesh fill and presets.

Smart paneling offers diamond, waffle, honeycomb, plate and custom tessellations with control over thickness, density, offset and edge shape. A physics simulation and material controls are included. Architects and designers can explore lightweight structure concepts and iterate on them without remodeling.

Key features

  • Tensile membranes, domes and hyperbolic paraboloids
  • Curve-to-3D conversion with presets
  • Diamond, waffle, honeycomb and plate paneling
  • Control of thickness, density and offset
  • Physics simulation and material options
