What Chatty does

Chatty from lifehAEcks creates animated chat conversations directly within After Effects. Messages can include text, emojis, media, audio and avatars, and the look is adjustable through customizable styles.

The results suit social videos made for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, as well as explainers and UI storytelling. It can also produce fake phone screens, chat overlays and green screen inserts for film, TV and advertising, and it helps designers prototype chat-based visuals quickly before committing to a final design.

Key features

  • Animated chat conversations inside After Effects
  • Text, emoji, media, audio and avatar support
  • Customizable chat styles
  • Phone-screen and overlay inserts
  • Quick prototyping of chat visuals
