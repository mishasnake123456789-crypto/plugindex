What BOOM Library SFX Urban Explosions does

BOOM Library SFX Urban Explosions was recorded in a real urban setting, in association with Battlefield V, so the blasts carry authentic city reverbs and tails. One plain-field explosion is included for non-urban use. It works with After Effects and Premiere Pro.

The Construction Kit uses up to 42 microphones, giving each blast at least 24 channels and a 4-channel ambisonic file, plus sweeteners for impact, punch, tail and rumble. The Designed edition sorts ready sounds into Indoor, Neutral and Urban and by size, distance, speed and character. Both come in stereo and 5.1, 24-bit, 96 kHz; the bundle has 2,417 sounds.

Key features

  • Real urban environment recordings
  • Up to 42 microphones, 24+ channels per blast
  • Sweeteners for impact, punch, tail and rumble
  • Designed edition by category, size and character
  • Stereo and 5.1 WAV, 24-bit/96kHz
