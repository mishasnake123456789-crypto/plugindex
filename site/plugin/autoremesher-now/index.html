Automatic remeshing add-on for Blender that produces consistent quad topology with several strategies.

What Autoremesher Now does

Autoremesher Now rebuilds meshes automatically, giving consistent quad topology that is ready for rigging and texturing. Several remeshing strategies are available and results appear instantly on processed meshes.

Beyond remeshing it handles tris-to-quads conversion, retopology, topology cleanup, mesh repair, decimation and LOD optimization. It is available as a Blender extension, GPL-licensed and documented. It suits artists and studios turning scans or sculpts into usable meshes. Day to day, that covers multiple remeshing strategies, consistent quad topology and tris to quads and cleanup.

Key features

  • Multiple remeshing strategies
  • Consistent quad topology
  • Tris to quads and cleanup
  • Repair, decimation and LOD optimization
