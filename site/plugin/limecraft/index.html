What Limecraft does

The Limecraft panel connects a Premiere Pro timeline to the Limecraft cloud platform. One click sends audio for AI transcription and subtitling, and secure collaboration and media asset management features are available without leaving the editor.

Since the 2025.6 release editors can exchange sequences with one another. Multi-channel audio and high-quality proxies are handled for post-production, and the cloud services support multi-factor authentication. Workflows for subtitling, delivery, localisation and custom media operations can be adapted to a company's needs.

Key features

  • One-click AI transcription and subtitling
  • Sequence exchange between editors
  • Multi-channel audio and quality proxies
  • Multi-factor authentication
  • Adaptable subtitling and delivery workflows
