What Prompt Edit does

Prompt Edit supplies video editing templates and assets inside Premiere Pro, and adds AI tools for creating new ones. AI video images and clips can be dragged onto the timeline, and screenshots from the timeline serve as reference images.

Cuts can be added directly in the timeline, and an AI Video Transitions page arrived in 1.9.3. It supports Premiere Pro 9.0 and later. This gives editors starting points and fresh material in one place, without leaving the project.

Key features

  • Templates and assets for Premiere Pro
  • AI tools for generating assets
  • AI Video Transitions page (1.9.3)
  • Timeline screenshots as references
  • Drag and drop AI media to the timeline
