What Plating Generator And Greebles does

Plating Generator and Greebles by Mark Kingsnorth gives hard surface models surface detail in Blender. It can cut panel lines into faces or scatter smaller objects across flat or curved meshes, using a random seed.

Customizable plating patterns can be saved, shared and reused as presets, with some bundled. You may build your own greeble libraries or use the default one, with overlapping or non-overlapping placement. Concept artists and sci-fi modelers can add convincing complexity to spaceships and machines quickly.

Key features

  • Panel line plating patterns
  • Greeble scattering with random seed
  • Shareable preset configurations
  • Custom greeble libraries
