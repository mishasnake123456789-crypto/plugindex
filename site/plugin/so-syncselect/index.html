What So-Syncselect does

SO-SyncSelect is a Blender add-on that synchronizes selection between the Outliner and the 3D viewport. What you pick in one place is reflected in the other, reducing hunting around in busy scenes.

Viewport synchronization was promoted from experimental status in version 1.1. It comes as a Blender extension covering 3.6 to 4.5, with documentation, an MIT license and free updates. It benefits anyone who works heavily in the Outliner. Nothing else is changed in your scene.

Key features

  • Syncs Outliner and viewport selection
  • Viewport sync no longer experimental since 1.1
  • Works with Blender 3.6 through 4.5
  • MIT license with documentation
  • Free updates
