What Dynamic Flow - Real Time Water Simulation does

Dynamic Flow is a Geometry Nodes add-on that produces flowing water without baking, caching or traditional fluid simulation. It works in both Eevee and Cycles.

Water direction follows the terrain with natural noise, shoreline foam appears automatically, and collisions can use collections or objects. Because it is lightweight and real time, environment artists can build rivers and shorelines and see them immediately. In practice that means real-time flowing water, no baking and terrain-aware flow direction.

Key features

  • Real-time flowing water, no baking
  • Terrain-aware flow direction
  • Automatic shoreline foam
  • Collision support with objects or collections
