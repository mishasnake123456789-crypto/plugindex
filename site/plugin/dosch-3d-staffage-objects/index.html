Fully textured architectural staffage models for Cinema 4D visualizations, with adjustable materials, lighting and animation.

What Dosch 3D: Staffage Objects does

Staffage objects are the small details that bring architectural renderings to life, and Dosch 3D: Staffage Objects supplies a collection of them as detailed, fully textured Cinema 4D models.

Scaling, materials, colors, transparency, animation and lighting are all adjustable, and you can supply your own textures or labels. Individual elements can be hidden or faded from fully visible to invisible. Architects and visualization artists can use the models to fill out street, garden and building scenes quickly instead of modeling each prop.

Key features

  • Textured architectural detail models
  • Adjustable scale, materials and colors
  • Own textures and labels supported
  • Elements switchable with transparency
  • Animation and lighting adaptable
