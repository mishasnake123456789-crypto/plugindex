Keyframe-free After Effects plugin that creates loopable blood-drip effects on text or objects.

What Bleed does

Bleed from MotionCodes adds a bleeding look to type and objects in After Effects. It needs no keyframes to run and loops the animation at any duration, which is handy for horror titles and thumbnails.

Density and pressure of the dripping are adjustable, along with coloring and further settings that shape the result. Controllable sound effects react to the speed of the drips. Motion designers can get a convincing look without hand-animating each drop.

Key features

  • Bleeding effect on text or images
  • No keyframes required
  • Loops at any duration
  • Density, pressure and color controls
  • Sound effects that follow drip speed
