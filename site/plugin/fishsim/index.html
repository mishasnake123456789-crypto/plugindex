Blender add-on that simulates a fish swimming toward a target, with three pre-rigged fish included.

What FishSim does

FishSim animates the swimming motion of a fish chasing a target in Blender. It ships with three pre-rigged models: Shark, Goldfish and Archer Fish.

From a dedicated FishSim tab you add a fish, pick the rig and press Simulate. It supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. Animators and environment artists can produce believable aquatic movement without keyframing each wave of the body. The rigs and simulation make it practical to add schools or single hunters to underwater shots without hand animation.

Key features

  • Simulates fish chasing a target
  • Shark, Goldfish and Archer Fish rigs
  • Simple tab workflow
  • Supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
