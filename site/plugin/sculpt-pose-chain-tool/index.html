What Sculpt Pose Chain Tool does

Sculpt Pose Chain Tool, by the_grass_trainer, lets you drop forward-kinematics chain points while in Sculpt Mode and use them to pose the mesh directly. It is listed under Sculpt.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.3.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Place FK chain points in Sculpt Mode
  • Pose the mesh from the chain
