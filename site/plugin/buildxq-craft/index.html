Blender add-on that assembles parametric buildings from your own wall, floor and cornice models, with eyedropper swapping.

What Buildxq Craft does

BuildXQ Craft constructs buildings in Blender out of models you supply, without node trees or hand modeling. Everything is parametric, so you can stretch, swap, stack and restyle a facade live.

To replace a wall piece, double-click one wall and then click another with the eyedropper. Up to six stackable levels, from ground to parapet, can be set per side, and bays or extensions push part of a facade out as a wing. A material helper covers colour and weathering such as rain, wear and grime. Personal, Colonial and Corporate styles come with examples, and the geometry stays editable and exportable.

Key features

  • Eyedropper swap of wall models, one click per wall
  • Six stackable levels configurable per side
  • Bays and extensions that push facades outward
  • Material helper with procedural weathering
  • Three ready styles with example models and a demo building
