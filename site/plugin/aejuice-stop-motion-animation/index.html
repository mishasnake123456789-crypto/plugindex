What AEJuice Stop Motion Animation does

AEJuice Stop Motion Animation is a roughly one-hour introductory course rather than a plugin. It is aimed at beginners who want to learn how to design and animate a character in stop-motion style using Photoshop, After Effects and the free Stop Motion Studio app.

Lessons follow a full production path: concept art, storyboard, animatic, shooting the animation in a free app, cell animation in Photoshop, and compositing in After Effects. Tips for improving your professional workflow round it out, giving newcomers a complete overview of how a short project moves from idea to finished piece.

Key features

  • Concept art, storyboard and animatic basics
  • Stop motion shooting with a free app
  • Cell animation in Photoshop
  • Compositing in After Effects
  • Workflow tips for professionals
