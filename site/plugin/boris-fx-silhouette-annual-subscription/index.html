What Boris FX Silhouette - Annual Subscription does

Silhouette is Boris FX's toolset for cleaning up and compositing shots. It installs as a plugin for After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and can also be launched as its own application. This edition is sold as an annual subscription.

Artists draw roto shapes, paint out unwanted elements, pull green-screen keys, track motion and warp or morph images, all inside a node graph of more than 400 effects. Machine-learning helpers assist with mattes and tracking, covering faces, depth, heads, objects and points. Compositors and finishing artists who handle difficult plates will find the most use here.

Key features

  • Roto and paint with auto paint
  • Node graph with 400+ effects
  • ML-assisted mattes and tracking
  • Keying, warping and morphing
  • Plugin or standalone use
