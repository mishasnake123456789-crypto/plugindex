What Power Robot AE Work Manager does

Power Robot AE Work Manager is a timesheet that lives in the After Effects interface. Pick an activity with one click and the panel logs time in the background, so you can see how a project's hours were spent.

Data is saved at every switch and recovered if the app closes unexpectedly. A session summary shows time and cost with a bar chart, hourly rates and totals. Reports export as HTML, either an internal version with rates or a client one with time only, or as timestamped CSV. Multiple projects and a currency symbol are supported.

Key features

  • Per-activity time tracking
  • Automatic session recovery
  • Session summary with hourly rates and bar chart
  • Internal and client HTML reports plus CSV export
  • Tracks multiple projects
