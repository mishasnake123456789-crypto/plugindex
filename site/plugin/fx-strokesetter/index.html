What FX StrokeSetter does

FX StrokeSetter by Fremox gives After Effects shape layers quick stroke control. Buttons set caps to Butt, Round or Projecting, and corners to Miter, Round or Bevel join, applying the change to every selected shape layer simultaneously.

Alignment can be changed in bulk as well, and strokes can be given Taper, Wave or Dashes. Control is by expressions, either locally with effects on each layer or globally via a master null, using simple shortcuts. Designers fixing the look of line art across many layers avoid opening dropdowns for each one.

Key features

  • Butt, Round and Projecting caps
  • Miter, Round and Bevel joins
  • Change several shape layers at once
  • Taper, Wave and Dashes for strokes
  • Local or global control via a master null
