What RootMaker does

RootMaker prepares character animations for game engines that expect a root bone. It can create a new root bone and bake its motion into your current animations, or bake a root bone you already have into the actions.

You choose the target armature, the name of the root bone and its length. A bake postfix option avoids overwriting the original action. Riggers and game animators exporting to engines can skip manual root setup.

Key features

  • Creates and bakes a root bone
  • Bakes an existing root bone into actions
  • Configurable armature, bone name and length
  • Bake postfix to keep originals
