What Shot Sculpt Pro does

Shot Sculpt Pro supports shot sculpting, sometimes called tech anim, in Blender. You sculpt and edit corrective shapes on referenced characters in a dedicated SSP Workspace, working on the final animated result rather than on rest poses.

Cloth or tissue simulations can be baked to per-frame shapekeys for easier editing, and several options bake meshes down to shapekeys for full control over cached meshes. The SSP Mesh Wrangler modifier, a geometry nodes setup, transfers deformation from one mesh to another, and special modifier setups provide further control. Technical animators in film or game cinematics will find it relevant.

Key features

  • Sculpt and edit on referenced characters
  • Bake cloth or tissue sims to per-frame shapekeys
  • SSP Mesh Wrangler geometry nodes modifier
  • Dedicated SSP Workspace
