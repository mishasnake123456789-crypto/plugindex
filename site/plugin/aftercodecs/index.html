What AfterCodecs does

AfterCodecs is an export plugin that gives Adobe applications modern codecs without QuickTime. On Windows and macOS it offers ProRes, H264, H265 and all four HAP variants from Vidvox, available in After Effects, Premiere Pro and Media Encoder.

MultiRender exports thousands of videos in batch. Premiere Pro and Media Encoder gain extras like exporting each audio track separately, one-click lower-resolution buttons and custom frame rate entry. A one-parameter YouTube profile follows upload recommendations, and advanced options include file size targeting, 10-bit, full range, 4:2:2 and 4:4:4 chroma, and tuning profiles. After Effects exports straight from the render queue.

Key features

  • ProRes, H264, H265 and HAP without QuickTime
  • MultiRender batch export
  • Separate audio track export in Premiere Pro
  • YouTube upload profile
  • FileSize targeting, 10 bpc and 4:4:4 options
