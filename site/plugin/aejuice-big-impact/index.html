What AEJuice Big Impact does

Big Impact is a pack of ready-made destruction footage that can be dropped into movies, CGI, music videos and TV. It includes shockwaves, big bangs, atomic explosions, asteroids and meteors, collapsing buildings, airwaves and dust impacts, all rendered in 4K at 24 frames per second.

Files come in two AVI formats with an alpha channel plus an H264 version with separate alpha, and luma mattes are supported. Selected assets are divided into front (A) and back (B) parts so an object like a meteorite can sit between them. Works with After Effects, Cinema 4D, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and other editors.

Key features

  • 200+ pre-rendered destruction assets
  • 4K at 24 fps
  • AVI with alpha, plus H264 with separate alpha
  • Luma matte support
  • A/B split layers for placing objects between
