What Auto Eye does

Auto Eye is a procedurally built eye for Blender that stays sharp at any resolution, even in extreme close-ups. It works in Cycles and Eevee and covers realistic, cartoon and animal looks.

Customization comes from eight shape keys and material parameters, including iris collarette, crypts, fake shadow and cornea mask hardness, in Full and Lite node versions. Cycles provides a real-time preview. Character artists can drop in convincing eyes without painting textures.

Key features

  • Fully procedural, resolution independent
  • Realistic to cartoon and animal styles
  • Eight shape keys plus material parameters
  • Full and Lite node versions
