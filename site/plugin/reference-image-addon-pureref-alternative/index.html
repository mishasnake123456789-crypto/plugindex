What Reference Image Addon (Pureref Alternative) does

The Reference Image Addon by Redmoon Studio keeps reference images inside the Blender workspace.

A dedicated sidebar panel in the 3D view loads and shows several images. Holding 5 turns the Outliner into an image viewer, and 4 and 6 cycle through images. Tap or hold hotkeys for toggled or temporary viewing, and the Outliner returns to its original state. New images can be added from your computer. It aims to replace a separate reference-board app for artists who prefer one window.

Key features

  • Sidebar panel for multiple references
  • Hold 5 for Outliner reference viewer
  • 4 and 6 hotkeys cycle images
  • Temporary or toggled view modes
  • Add images from your computer
