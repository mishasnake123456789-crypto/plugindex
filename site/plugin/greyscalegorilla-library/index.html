What Greyscalegorilla Library does

Greyscalegorilla Library delivers a large collection of materials, models, HDRIs and textures to Cinema 4D by way of the Cargo platform. A click sends the chosen asset into your scene, under an individual commercial license.

The library holds over 3,500 scanned and procedural materials, 1,000 models, 650 textures and 600 HDRIs, and every new release is included. Assets arrive as clean hierarchies and instances built on USD and MaterialX, and support extends across Cinema 4D 2023 to 2026 with Redshift, Octane, V-Ray and Arnold. Artists wanting dependable lookdev content will find it helpful.
