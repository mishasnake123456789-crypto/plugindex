Blender add-on that places and animates the Sun using geographic location, date and time.

What Sun Position does

Sun Position aims the Sun in Blender according to a real location and moment in time. Its calculations are based on the NOAA online calculator and Jean Meeus's Astronomical Algorithms, which makes daylight studies accurate.

Sun Object Mode animates a sun lamp, sky texture and visualization collection from date, time and place, while Environment Mode synchronizes a sun light with an environment texture so they rotate together. Controls are in World Properties. Architects, product visualizers and anyone needing time-of-day lighting can use it.

Key features

  • Location, date and time driven sun
  • Based on NOAA and Meeus algorithms
  • Sun Object and Environment modes
  • Controls in World Properties
