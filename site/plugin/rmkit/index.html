What rmKit does

rmKit, by Timothee-Yeramian, is a Blender modeling toolbox combining mesh operators, precision tools and material helpers. Its operators can connect edges, make tubes, extrude along edges, thicken meshes and manage vertex normals using selection sets.

Workflow helpers cover fast material assignment, radial alignment and workplane setup, while the precision side adds a dimension display, a linear falloff tool, move-to-furthest and a knife script. Modelers get a wide toolbox of small time savers.

Key features

  • Connect edges, create tubes, extrude along edges
  • Thicken meshes and manage vertex normals
  • Quick material assignment and workplane setup
  • Dimensions visualizer and linear falloff
  • Radial alignment and knife tools
