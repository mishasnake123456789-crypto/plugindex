What Blender Add-On : Johnson Solids does

The Johnson Solids add-on from Arkiweez brings the complete set of 92 convex polyhedra with regular faces into Blender, so you can place them without constructing each one manually.

It renders in Cycles and Eevee, supports Blender 4.0 through 5.0, includes documentation and uses the GPL license. Teachers, mathematicians, designers and geometry enthusiasts building polyhedron-based art or demonstrations get a ready library of shapes. Having every solid pre-built also saves the time of working out their coordinates or constructing them from primitives.
