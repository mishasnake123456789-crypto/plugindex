Blender add-on to align objects to an active object's bounds, distribute them evenly and move origins.

What SCO ButtObjects does

SCO ButtObjects collects alignment helpers for Blender scenes. Selected objects can be lined up against the bounds of the active object, the 3D cursor or the active object's origin, along X, Y or Z, at the minimum, center or maximum, and placed inside or outside.

When aligning to origin it can also copy the active object's scale and rotation. A distribute function evenly spaces three or more objects, and an Edit Mode tool moves an origin to the center of the selected geometry. Panels sit in the Item tab of the 3D Viewport sidebar.

Key features

  • Align to active bounds, 3D cursor or origin
  • Min, center or max along X, Y or Z
  • Inside or outside bounds placement
  • Even distribution of three or more objects
  • Move origin to center of selected geometry
