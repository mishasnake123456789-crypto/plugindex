What Sadcursor does

Sadcursor is a Blender plugin for moving the 3D cursor quickly. Normally this takes several key presses and menu steps; here, a left click snaps it onto a vertex, edge or face.

You can change just the position, or also set orientation based on the selected geometry. Additional options let you adjust other related positions. Modelers who rely on the cursor for placing objects and pivots will save time. Because the cursor is central to adding objects and setting pivots, placing it accurately in one click speeds up many small modeling steps across a session.

Key features

  • Snap cursor to vertex, edge or face
  • Position-only or position plus orientation
  • Orientation from selected geometry
  • Left-click operation
