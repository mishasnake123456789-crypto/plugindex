Blender toolkit for renaming, reordering and remapping armature bones, with synced updates to actions and drivers.

What Bone Engraver does

Bone Engraver helps riggers who need to rename or reorganize armature bones without breaking animation. It includes rename mapping, a reorder interface and a matrix mapper.

Bone Renamer builds a mapping and applies it to one armature or across many actions and rigs. Auto Sync mirrors renames to Actions, Drivers and NLA tracks in real time, keeps a change history, and works from the properties panel or F2. A Bone Matrix Mapper captures and restores transformation matrices, and an N-Panel handles bone order.

Key features

  • Bone Renamer with mapping
  • Auto Sync across Actions, Drivers and NLA
  • Change history and F2 shortcut
  • N-Panel bone reorder
  • Bone Matrix Mapper
