What Mimir for US region does

This is the US region version of Mimir, a connector between Adobe editing apps and a media asset management system. It gives After Effects and Premiere Pro users access to their whole media collection, from deep archive to material still arriving, with search and import available in proxy or in full-res form.

Captions made by transcription services can be brought into Premiere Pro automatically, and AI-driven metadata tagging helps find content. If you edit with proxies, high-resolution versions can be rendered centrally. The system covers MAM, PAM and DAM needs.

Key features

  • Search and import in proxy or full resolution
  • Archive through live media access
  • Automatic caption import via transcription
  • AI metadata tagging
  • Central high-res render of proxy edits
