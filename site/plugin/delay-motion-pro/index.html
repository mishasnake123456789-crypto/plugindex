What Delay Motion Pro does

Delay Motion Pro, from TOKI Projects, creates trailing, delayed transform motion that holds up when layers are parented. Delay setups often break once hierarchies and anchor points come into play, and this tool is built to avoid that.

Delays are chained automatically, setup is quick, and adjustments are simple. Position follow works with parent and child hierarchies, anchor point handling reduces offsets, and stronger exception handling keeps expressions stable. It is compatible with After Effects 2022 through 2026 and suits follow-through animation on groups of layers.

Key features

  • Stable transform delays with parenting
  • Automatic delay chaining
  • Position follow for parent/child hierarchies
  • Reduced anchor point offsets
  • Compatible with After Effects 2022 to 2026
