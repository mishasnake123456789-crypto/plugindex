What K3D UV Islands Keeper does

K3D UV Islands Keeper stores UV island selections and their transformations in several slots. When you edit your mesh, it helps maintain the layout and prevents accidental distortions of islands already placed.

Everything sits in the N-panel, close at hand. Texture artists who revise geometry after unwrapping can keep their carefully arranged UVs intact.

Key features

  • Multi-slot memory for UV selections and transforms
  • Maintains layout through topology changes
  • Prevents accidental distortion
  • N-panel access
