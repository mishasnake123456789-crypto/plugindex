What DOSCH 3D: Car Details V3 - Electric does

This entry in Dosch Design's third-generation car series represents an electric sedan with no brand marking. It includes the essential parts and systems of a car with a battery and electric drive, fully textured for Cinema 4D.

Scaling is adjustable, materials, textures and colors can be changed freely, and your own labels may be added. Each element can be switched on or off, faded from fully visible to invisible and animated on its own or with the rest. It suits technical animations and explainers about electric mobility.

Key features

  • Editable scale, materials and colors
  • Own textures and labels supported
  • Transparency and animation of elements
