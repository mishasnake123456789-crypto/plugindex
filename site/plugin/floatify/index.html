A Blender add-on that makes a selected object float on an ocean surface.

What Floatify does

Floatify makes objects bob on water in Blender. Select the object, click Realise, and it is prepared to float; then keyframing the ocean produces the floating motion without manual animation.

The controls are in the N panel after installation. Documentation is included and it is compatible with Blender 3.0 through 3.5. Artists animating boats, buoys, debris or toys on water can get convincing motion quickly without setting up a physics simulation.

Key features

  • Makes selected objects float on water
  • One-click Realise action
  • N panel access
  • Keyframe the ocean to animate
  • Blender 3.0 to 3.5
