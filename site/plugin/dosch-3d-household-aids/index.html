What Dosch 3D: Household Aids does

Dosch 3D: Household Aids packs 35 everyday home objects into one library for Cinema 4D and other applications. Each is fully textured and detailed enough for close-up visualizations.

Every model can be scaled, recolored and re-textured, with room for your own textures and labels. Elements can be turned on or off, given partial transparency, and animated individually or as a whole object. Interior visualizers, advertising designers and illustrators can furnish scenes quickly without modeling common domestic items.

Key features

  • 35 detailed textured household models
  • Adjustable scaling
  • Editable materials, textures and colors
  • Switchable elements with transparency
  • Individual or whole-object animation
