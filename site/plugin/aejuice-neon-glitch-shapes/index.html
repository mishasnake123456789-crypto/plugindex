What AEJuice Neon Glitch Shapes does

AEJuice Neon Glitch Shapes bundles 72 animated neon forms with a glitchy edge. Each is a pre-rendered 3840x2160 ProRes video with an alpha channel, so it can be dropped on a timeline and combined with blending modes.

After Effects projects and Premiere Pro motion graphics templates come too, both at 30 fps. The Pack Manager supplies quick previews, drop-in import and favourites, and downloads are speedy. Creators working on music videos, social content and advertising can add punchy overlays without building shapes from scratch.
