Adds a hotkey-summoned notepad to Blender with notes, to-do lists and drawings, drawn through a custom GPU interface.

What Blender Notepad 2 does

Blender Notepad 2 keeps notes inside Blender so you do not have to switch to another app. Its interface is drawn on the GPU, which gives smooth scrolling and comfortable multiline editing.

A hotkey opens notes, to-dos or drawings in most Blender areas; items can be rearranged and kept either globally or with a scene. Tags work as pages or filters. Artists tracking shot feedback, reminders or sketches next to their work will find it handy.

Key features

  • GPU-rendered multiline editing
  • Notes, to-dos and drawings via hotkey
  • Reorder notes and lists
  • Store globally or per scene
  • Tags as pages or filters
