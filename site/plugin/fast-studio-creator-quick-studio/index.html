What Fast Studio Creator (Quick Studio) does

Fast Studio Creator, also called Quick Studio, creates a ready-to-render studio environment in Blender with one click. It adds a lighting rig, a bank of light sources, gobo projection, dust, fog and an HDRI world.

It is handy for showing 3D content or building product visualizations without setting up a stage by hand. Version 3.2 added 30 custom large-studio lighting presets and a meeting gallery, and presets are reached through the Asset browser. Documentation is included.

Key features

  • One-click studio with lights, HDRI and fog
  • Light array similar to large pavilions
  • Gobo light and dust effects
  • 30 studio lighting presets
  • Presets available in the Asset browser
