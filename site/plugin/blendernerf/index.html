What Blendernerf does

BlenderNeRF generates datasets for neural radiance fields and Gaussian Splatting from Blender scenes. A single click exports renders along with their camera parameters, while you keep full control over the scene and camera.

Documentation is included, and a fully free, open-source version is available on GitHub. It is aimed at researchers and developers who need synthetic training data. Day to day, that covers single-click render and camera export, neRF datasets and gaussian Splatting datasets.

Key features

  • Single-click render and camera export
  • NeRF datasets
  • Gaussian Splatting datasets
  • Full scene and camera control
