What WQ Pro does

WQ Pro, by Terrible Junk Show, listens for double-clicks on layers and runs a script in response. Version 1.1 added support for multiple layers.

It works with After Effects 2022 through 2024 and has a demo video. The developer has discontinued it, so it may be of interest mainly to those who already use it. Since it is discontinued, it is mainly relevant to existing users who want a shortcut for launching scripts from layers.

Key features

  • Detects double-clicks on layers
  • Triggers a script
  • Multi-layer support from 1.1
  • Supports After Effects 2022 to 2024
  • Discontinued
