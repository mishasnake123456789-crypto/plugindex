Detects beats in music and turns them into markers in Premiere Pro so cuts can follow the rhythm.

What BeatEdit 2 does

BeatEdit 2 analyzes a music track and places markers at the beats, helping editors cut picture in time with sound. It can aid manual editing or drive an automatic edit through Premiere's Automate to Sequence.

Quantization improves detection, and you can choose beats only or other rhythmic peaks for more variety. Markers can be batch created, moved and deleted within the work area. A beat info panel shows BPM and selection counts, and music can be loaded from the sequence.

Key features

  • Beat detection with quantization
  • Sequence or clip markers on the beat
  • Works with Automate to Sequence
  • Batch create, move and delete markers
  • Beat info panel with BPM
