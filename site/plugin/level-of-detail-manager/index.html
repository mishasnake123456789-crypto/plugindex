Blender add-on that substitutes simpler meshes, modifiers, constraints and materials as objects move away from the camera.

What Level Of Detail Manager does

Level Of Detail Manager adds distance-based LOD for the Internal and Cycles renderers in Blender. Objects can be replaced by simpler versions as they move away from the camera, cutting viewport load and render time.

Each object can use LOD in the viewport, during render or both. LOD Groups assign high-polygon meshes to close-ups, with lighter versions for distant ones, and modifiers, constraints and materials can be switched too. Scene builders with dense environments can keep files manageable.

Key features

  • Distance-based swaps of mesh, modifiers, constraints and materials
  • Per-object viewport and render control
  • LOD Groups
  • Works with Internal and Cycles renderers
