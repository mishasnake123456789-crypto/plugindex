Saves and recalls viewport and camera positions in Blender with hotkeys, thumbnails and multiple sets.

What Bookmark Jumper does

Bookmark Jumper lets you store views and return to them instantly. Version 2.0 introduces multiple bookmark sets, up to five with 45 positions in total, managed in a tabbed interface.

Each bookmark gets an automatic 960x540 screenshot thumbnail with an expandable preview, saved as a PNG next to the blend file. Camera bookmarks capture focal length and related data so a camera can be reconstructed from saved data. Useful for inspecting large scenes or comparing angles repeatedly.

Key features

  • Up to five sets and 45 positions
  • Automatic 960x540 thumbnails
  • Camera bookmarks capture focal length
  • Camera reconstruction from bookmarks
  • Hotkey switching
