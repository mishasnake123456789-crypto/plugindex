What Dosch 3D: Racing Cars does

Dosch 3D: Racing Cars brings over 20 detailed models of race cars, circuits and extra details to Cinema 4D. The files are supplied for version 8.5 and above as well as R15 through R24, and they are fully textured.

Scaling, materials, textures and colors can be adapted, with room for your own textures and sponsor labels. Individual elements may be switched on or off or made transparent, and animation works on single parts or the entire model. Motorsport visuals, advertising and technical illustrations are the obvious uses.

Key features

  • 20+ racing cars, tracks and details
  • C4D 8.5+ and R15 to R24 files
  • Custom textures and labels
  • Switchable, transparent elements
  • Part or whole-model animation
