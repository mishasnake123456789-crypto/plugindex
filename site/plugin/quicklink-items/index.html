What QuickLink Items does

QuickLink Items by NT Productions finds and replaces missing items in an After Effects project. It can scan an entire drive or specific folders and then reports which assets were found and which remain missing.

Image sequences are handled by using the source file name so renamed items replace correctly, and camera raw plus .mkv and .ts files are supported. It works with nearly every language and script and with most After Effects versions on Mac and Windows. It suits anyone opening projects with offline media.

Key features

  • Scans whole drives or chosen folders
  • Clear found and missing report
  • Image sequence handling by source name
  • Camera raw, .mkv and .ts support
  • Mac and Windows, most After Effects versions
