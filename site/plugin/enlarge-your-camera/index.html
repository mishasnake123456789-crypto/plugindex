What Enlarge Your Camera does

Enlarge Your Camera protects your composition when you widen or reshape a render in Blender. Instead of reframing by hand after changing the aspect ratio, it adjusts the camera for you.

Enter a pixel value and press Add margins, or drag the resolution interactively to preview the new framing. The focal-length method keeps perspective and camera position, while the camera-move method uses a chosen object or the depth of field as the point of interest. Documentation is included.

Key features

  • Preserves framing across aspect ratio changes
  • Add margins by pixel value
  • Interactive resolution dragging
  • Focal-length or camera-move methods
