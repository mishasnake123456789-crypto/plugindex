What Paintbridge Ps does

PaintBridge PS connects Blender and Photoshop so you can paint with Photoshop's brush engine and see it projected on your model.

Data moves over a WebSocket directly between memories, with no temp files, at 512px up to 8K. Nine passes including Normals, Depth, AO and Color ID reach Photoshop as layers, camera control comes from Photoshop, and projection is ROI-based. A texture slot browser sets targets and masks, color conversion is automatic, and capture history stores up to 30 entries.

Key features

  • WebSocket transfer without temp files
  • Nine capture passes as Photoshop layers
  • ROI-based paint projection
  • Capture history up to 30 entries
