What Formula to Nodes does

Formula to Nodes, by Prince_Eagle, lets you type formulas in a VEX-like style, or describe the effect you want, and get Blender node groups back instead of wiring everything by hand. It is listed under Geometry Nodes and Node.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • VEX-style formulas converted to nodes
  • Describe an effect to get a node group
  • Listed under Geometry Nodes and Node
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later
  • Requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer
