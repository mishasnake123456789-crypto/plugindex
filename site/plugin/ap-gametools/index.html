Blender utility add-on for optimizing and exporting static models to Unity, Unreal, Godot and Sketchfab.

What Ap Gametools does

AP GameTools bundles the chores of preparing static models for game engines in Blender. Exporting can target a game engine (optionally centered), the selection as FBX, or the full scene as FBX, plus per-engine pipelines for Unity, Unreal and Godot that bring in materials and textures and prepare shaders.

Decimation tools remove doubles, planar decimate and strip modifiers. Material tools separate, join and rename by material, UV tools create lightmap UVs and pack, and texture resizing makes 4k to 256 copies converted to JPEG. Organizing helpers clear parents and delete empties.

Key features

  • Export pipelines for Unity, Unreal and Godot
  • FBX export of selection or scene
  • Decimation and remove doubles tools
  • Material and UV utilities including lightmaps
  • Texture resizing from 4k to 256
