What Turnaround Camera does

Turnaround Camera creates a camera rotation around whatever object is selected in Blender, useful for showing a model from all sides. It used to be among the add-ons bundled with Blender 4.1 and is now offered as a standalone extension with limited support.

It is found in the 3D Viewport sidebar under the View tab, needs no special permissions, and works with Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. It is released under the GNU General Public License version 2 or later and was originally written by Antonio Vazquez. Artists making product spins and asset showcases can set it up quickly.

Key features

  • Camera rotation around a selected object
  • Sidebar View tab access
  • Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
  • No special permissions
