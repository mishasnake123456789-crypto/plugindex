What LipsyncTool does

From Mikhail Ivanchuk comes LipsyncTool, a script for After Effects aimed at animating character speech. Instead of stepped holds, it produces smooth, interpolated mouth movement from keyframe presets that are triggered by timeline markers.

You build a preset layer containing your visemes, such as A, E, O and M, and tag each with markers. Applying that layer to your main artwork layer then places the keyframe sets wherever your markers fall, including range markers and multi-frame animations. No expressions are involved, which keeps comps light and editable. It supports After Effects from CC 2018 through 2025 and suits character animators.

Key features

  • Preset layer holds all visemes with markers
  • Marker-triggered keyframe sets, including ranges
  • Smooth interpolation with no holds
  • No expressions required
