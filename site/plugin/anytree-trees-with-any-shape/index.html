What Anytree - Trees With Any Shape does

Anytree is a Blender add-on for producing 3D trees whose silhouette follows a shape you supply. Instead of tweaking a generic generator until it looks right, you model the outline or sketch it with curves and let the tool build the trunk, branches and foliage around it.

A broad set of parameters lets you adjust form and appearance, while a toggle adds leaves and twigs chosen from more than forty hand-modelled real species. Bark materials come included, UVs are produced automatically with one click, and the mesh keeps controllable, optimized quads, which suits environment artists who need many believable trees.

Key features

  • Builds trees from modeled shapes or drawn curves
  • Leaves and twigs from 40+ hand-modelled species
  • Bundled bark materials
  • One-click automatic UVs
  • Controllable quad topology
