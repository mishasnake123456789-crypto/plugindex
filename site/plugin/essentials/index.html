What Essentials does

Essentials, by Jackson Zheng, gathers small improvements that make everyday Blender use smoother. Instead of installing many single-purpose add-ons, you get a collection of workflow features that the base application does not offer.

It is distributed as a GPL Blender extension and works with Blender 4.3 through 5.2. Documentation comes with it, along with product updates and a twelve month support period from the developer. It is aimed at general Blender users and modelers who want their daily tools to feel more convenient.

Key features
