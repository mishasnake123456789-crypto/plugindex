Raytraced shadow and light effect for After Effects, driven by a layer's alpha, with GI, AO and SSS looks.

What Plugin Everything Shadow Studio 3 does

Shadow Studio 3 casts shadows and light from the alpha channel of any layer in After Effects. Raytracing lets it imitate global illumination, ambient occlusion, subsurface scattering and glossy reflections, giving flat elements a convincing sense of depth.

Directional and point lights create volume, and extrusion and bevel are built in. An RGBA pipeline sends coloured light from the layer, a gradient editor animates and loops gradients, and inner shadow is a checkbox that works in every mode. Over 36 presets and project files are included, such as a 360-degree directional point rig, and all 37 blending modes support alpha biasing and matte gamma. Styles run from realistic to retro or cartoon.

Key features

  • Raytraced shadows from the alpha channel
  • GI, AO, subsurface and glossy reflection looks
  • Directional and point lights, extrusion and bevel
  • RGBA pipeline and gradient editor
  • 36+ presets, 37 blending modes
