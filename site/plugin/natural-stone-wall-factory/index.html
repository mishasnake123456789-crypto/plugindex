What Natural Stone Wall Factory does

Natural Stone Wall Factory generates stonework in Blender without the manual labor of pulling vertices to fit stones together. Floors, walls and columns are built procedurally, with bricks that interlock perfectly, even in three dimensions.

It comes with documentation, is released under the MIT license and is listed as compatible with Blender 4.5. Johann Anton Myburgh's tool suits environment builders who need believable masonry.

Key features

  • Procedural stone floors, walls and columns
  • Bricks interlock in three dimensions
  • Replaces manual vertex pulling
  • Documentation included
  • MIT license
