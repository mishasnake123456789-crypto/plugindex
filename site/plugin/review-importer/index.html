What Review Importer does

Review Importer is a script by Konstantin Reinhart that brings client feedback directly into After Effects. Comments exported from kollaborate.tv, Vimeo, frame.io or screenlight.tv are read from a CSV file.

The script creates a text layer in which each review becomes a marker, and you choose the marker color. Toggling the layer's visibility shows the comments as a text overlay. This lets editors and animators work through revision notes at the exact points on the timeline where they apply. It is compatible with After Effects from CC 2018 through 2024.

Key features

  • Reads CSV exports from review sites
  • Supports kollaborate.tv, Vimeo, frame.io and screenlight.tv
  • Comments become colored markers
  • Text overlay by toggling layer visibility
