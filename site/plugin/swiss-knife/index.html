What Swiss Knife does

Swiss Knife by Motion Design School bundles 20 tools for After Effects, arranged in four groups: parallaxer, interactor, placeholder and support. It is meant for artists doing character animation, editing, templates and UI/UX animation.

Parallax 2D fakes depth with flat layers, while Parallax 3D spreads layers between near and far distances. Dependence ties one property to another, Animarker links animation to markers, Offset adds time delay, and Revolver connects rotation to a main object. Placeholder, Extruder, Stack and Switcher help manage footage stacks and precomposing.

Key features

  • Parallax 2D and Parallax 3D
  • Dependence and Interactor for linked animation
  • Animarker and Offset for marker and delay control
  • Placeholder, Extruder and Stack tools
  • Switcher precomposes and enables Time Remap
