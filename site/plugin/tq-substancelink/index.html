What Tq Substancelink does

TQ SubstanceLink connects Blender to Substance Painter and creates an organized project structure in which assets and materials remain linked for good. Imports and exports are automated.

Folders are created automatically, locally or in the cloud, textures stay in sync, and non-destructive editing between the two programs is supported. It keeps texturing pipelines tidy. Keeping both programs linked cuts down on file management mistakes, which is valuable on larger projects with many texture sets.

Key features

  • Automatic structured project folders
  • Permanent asset and material links
  • Texture sync
  • Non-destructive editing
