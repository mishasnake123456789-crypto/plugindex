What Batch Asset Placer does

Batch Asset Placer, by Andrey-Pestryakov, takes the assets you select in the Asset Browser and adds them at the position of the 3D cursor. It is listed under Import-Export.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.5.0 or newer.

Key features
