Blender add-on that flattens model parts and nests them on sheets for cutting.

What Disassembler And Nesting does

Designed for people who build furniture or cabinets in Blender and then have to make them for real, this add-on turns a finished 3D model into a cut-ready layout. Parts are duplicated and flattened onto the XY plane, sorted by width and length.

Parts of equal dimensions get one color, and every row has a common thickness. The nesting step arranges the panels on a sheet with adjustable sheet dimensions and saw kerf. Volume, mass and surface area can be calculated, sizes can be written into each plank, and results export as CSV for spreadsheets or DXF for CAD, with a scale setting. Documentation is included.

Key features

  • Flattens and sorts parts on the XY plane
  • Color coding by matching size
  • Sheet nesting with saw thickness
  • Volume, mass and area calculations
  • CSV and DXF export
