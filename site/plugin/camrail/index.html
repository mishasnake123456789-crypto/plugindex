Blender add-on for animating a camera along a curve with tracking, easing and shakes.

What Camrail does

Camrail moves any camera along a curve without constraints or baked animation. It is made for cutscenes, product shots and previews.

Works with Bezier, NURBS and Poly curves, including predefined rails and custom ones. Target tracking has a height offset, pitch, yaw and roll adjust with live preview, and keyframes are set and cleared from the 3D View sidebar. Camera easing and shakes are included, with a one-click hand shake effect.

Key features

  • Camera along any curve type
  • Target tracking with height offset
  • Pitch, yaw and roll with preview
  • Easing and hand shake effect
