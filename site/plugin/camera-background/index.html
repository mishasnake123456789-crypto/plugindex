Camera Background

Developer: Basem ALi Deeb

FreeBlender
Download Free

Adds background images to Blender renders through a compositor node setup with scaling and shadow catcher support.

What Camera Background does

Camera Background places a picture behind your render using the compositor. Four preset scales of 100, 75, 50 and 25 percent are offered, along with custom scaling from 10 to 200 percent.

Scaling keeps the entire background without cropping and maintains aspect ratio. Lower scales preview faster, and opacity control, shadow catcher support and K-Cycles Post FX compatibility are provided. Product and VFX artists integrating objects with photographs get quick results.

Key features

  • Four preset scales and custom 10-200%
  • No-crop scaling with aspect ratio kept
  • Faster preview at lower scales
  • Shadow catcher and opacity control
