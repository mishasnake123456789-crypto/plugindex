What AEJuice Text Effects does

AEJuice Text Effects supplies ready-made title animations for After Effects and Premiere Pro. It contains 14 styles with more than 200 text compositions, plus a bonus of over 100 animated elements to dress up a title.

The compositions support any language and any font. Most animations install their fonts automatically, or you can use your own typefaces. It supports CC 2015 through CC 2024 and newer, on Windows 10 or later and macOS 10.14 or later, including M1 and M2 machines. Editors can build polished titles without keyframing each letter.

Key features

  • 14 text styles with 200+ compositions
  • 100+ bonus animated elements
  • Any language and any font
  • Automatic font installation for most animations
  • Windows and macOS, including M1 and M2
