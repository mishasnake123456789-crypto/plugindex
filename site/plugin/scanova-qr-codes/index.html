What Scanova QR Codes does

Scanova QR Codes lets editors put QR codes on screen without leaving Premiere Pro. Create them in the app, or link a Scanova account to import dynamic ones whose destination can change.

Styles are customizable for clear overlays, and codes can be dragged onto the canvas or timeline. Dynamic destination URLs can be updated at any time, even after rendering, without re-rendering or re-uploading, and a connected account tracks scan location, device and date.

Key features

  • Add QR codes inside Premiere Pro
  • Import dynamic codes from Scanova
  • Customizable overlay styles
  • Update destinations without re-rendering
  • Scan tracking via Scanova account
