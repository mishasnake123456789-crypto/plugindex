What Driver Espresso Lite does

Driver Espresso Lite, by OEBS_Studios, supplies a small collection of ready-made recipes using Blender's built-in drivers, so common setups do not need to be worked out from scratch. It is listed under Animation.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
