What Auto-Scale FPS does

Auto-Scale FPS, by William-Leong, keeps animation timing intact when you change the frame rate of a Blender scene by scaling the timeline and Actions accordingly. It is listed under Animation.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Scales Actions to match
