What Energy Fx Addon does

Energy Fx Addon, from EdenWeby 3D, delivers ready-made energy visuals. Choose a preset, add it to the scene, tweak the settings and place it anywhere.

There are 13 presets, among them Electrics, Glowing Lines, Shockwave Burst and Saber rods, with more features said to be on the way. Motion designers and VFX artists can add sci-fi power effects quickly without building them from scratch.
