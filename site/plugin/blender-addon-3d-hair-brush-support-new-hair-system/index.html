What Blender Addon | 3D Hair Brush - Support New Hair System does

The free viewer for 3D Hair Brush, from VFX Grace, loads four human hair presets in Blender with one click. It stands in for the full 3D Hair Brush grooming tool, which works with Blender's new hair system.

The full tool offers brushes for the old particle system and the new hair system, and non-destructive, hierarchical children modifiers such as Length, Noise and Clump that can be reversed. A preset library is included. Character artists can preview groom styles before choosing to grow their own.
