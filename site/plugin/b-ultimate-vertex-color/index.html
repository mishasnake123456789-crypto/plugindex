Edits RGBA vertex data in Blender with channel previews, per-channel editing, gradients, blend modes and AO baking.

What B- Ultimate Vertex Color does

B- Ultimate Vertex Color helps author RGBA vertex data, which is common in game and stylized pipelines. Channels can be viewed on their own, so R, G, B or A shows as a grayscale preview without changing data.

You can lock channels and edit only those you need, paint with opacity, blend modes and hue, saturation and brightness adjustments, apply gradients, and bake ambient occlusion or image data into vertex attributes. Channel Sync links the previewed channel to the active one. Technical artists packing masks into vertices will find it useful.

Key features

  • Non-destructive R, G, B, A grayscale preview
  • Per-channel editing with locks
  • Painting with opacity and blend modes
  • Gradient tools
  • Bake ambient occlusion or images into vertex attributes
