Blender add-on that imports images as planes parented to the camera and sized to its field of view.

What Camera Plane does

Camera Plane, from Les Fées Spéciales, imports pictures into Blender and parents them to a chosen camera. Each plane adjusts automatically to the camera's field of view or focal length, so it keeps filling the frame as lens settings change.

Distance and width come from the image object or camera properties. Several images can be imported at once and spaced evenly in depth, and a filterable list can be sorted alphabetically or by distance, with drag-to-set distance across planes. Compositors and matte painters building layered parallax backdrops get quick setups.

Key features

  • Images parented to the camera
  • Planes follow FOV or focal length
  • Multiple images spaced evenly in depth
  • Filterable list sorted by name or distance
