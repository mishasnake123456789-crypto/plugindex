Sends an email or text message when a Blender render finishes, with timing details and a custom note.

What Render Notifications // Email And Text Alerts does

Render Notifications tells you when a long render is done, so you can step away from the computer. It can send an email or an SMS, depending on how you configure it.

Messages can include the project name, start and end times and completion status, plus an optional custom tag or note. Most carriers are supported, and you can add your own through an email gateway. Anyone who renders overnight or on a remote machine will appreciate the alert instead of checking by hand.

Key features

  • Email or SMS when a render completes
  • Project name, times and status in the message
  • Optional custom tag or note
  • Support for most carriers
