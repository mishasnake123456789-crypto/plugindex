What Dosch 3D: Airplanes does

Dosch 3D: Airplanes gathers ten fully textured aircraft in one collection, including an Airbus 380, a Boeing 747, a Cessna and a Bell 222 helicopter. They are intended for visualizations, animations and technical illustrations in Cinema 4D.

Every model can be rescaled and recolored, textures and labels can be replaced with your own, and individual elements can be hidden or given any transparency. Parts or entire aircraft can be animated. Having several aircraft types in one pack helps artists populate airport scenes or create aviation graphics.

Key features

  • Ten fully textured aircraft models
  • Includes A380, 747, Cessna and Bell 222
  • Replaceable textures and labels
  • Elements hidden or made transparent
  • Animatable parts and whole models
