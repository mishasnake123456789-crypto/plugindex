Blender OSL camera that simulates physical lenses with custom bokeh and depth of field.

What Deepfocus does

Deepfocus recreates a real camera lens in Blender through an OSL camera, giving cinematic depth of field and customizable bokeh. It is GPU-friendly and designed for Cycles.

It has a built-in F-stop, focus peaking, and imperfections like bokeh bias, field curvature, chromatic aberration and lens breathing. Custom bokeh and lens dirt come from image textures, and presets cover Cinema Prime, Anamorphic, Vintage Petzval, Tiltshift and Newtonian Telescope. An advanced distortion mode and diffraction spike are included.

Key features

  • OSL-based optical lens simulation
  • Focus peaking and lens imperfections
  • Custom bokeh and lens dirt textures
  • Presets for cinema, anamorphic and vintage lenses
