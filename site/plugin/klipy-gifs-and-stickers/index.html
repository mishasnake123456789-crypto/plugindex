What KLIPY: GIFs and Stickers does

KLIPY adds a searchable collection of GIFs, stickers and memes directly to Premiere Pro. Rather than hunting through websites and downloading files, you search for a reaction or emotion in the panel and see matching results.

Clicking an item inserts it into your sequence after instantly converting it into an optimized MP4. The panel is a lightweight native one built inside the application. It is aimed at editors producing quick digital content, fan edits and other projects where reaction clips and stickers are a regular part of the cut.

Key features

  • Browse millions of GIFs and stickers in-app
  • Search by reaction, emotion or meme
  • Click to insert into the sequence
  • Automatic conversion to optimized MP4
  • Lightweight native panel
