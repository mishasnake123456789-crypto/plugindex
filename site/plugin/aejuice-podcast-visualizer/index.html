What AEJuice Podcast Visualizer does

AEJuice Podcast Visualizer converts audio episodes into shareable videos for YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. Sixteen designs are included, with colours, texts and photos customisable.

Drop in audio and the animation adjusts itself, generating audio waves that transition into personalised content. Three sizes (vertical, horizontal and square) are produced automatically. In After Effects, a CSV file can drive batch processing to create over 1,000 videos in one click. The Pack Manager adds preview, drag-and-drop import and favourites. Podcasters who publish many episodes will save considerable effort.

Key features

  • 16 included designs
  • Automatic audio waves from podcast audio
  • Customisable colours, texts and photos
  • Batch processing via CSV in After Effects
  • Vertical, horizontal and square outputs
