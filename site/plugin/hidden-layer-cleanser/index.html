What Hidden Layer Cleanser does

Hidden Layer Cleanser by Dino Qiu tidies an After Effects project by deleting layers that are unused or hidden. It is meant for preparing an organized composition for archiving or handing over to another person.

It recognizes hidden layers that effects still depend on, such as displacement maps, and keeps them out of the selection or deletion to prevent accidental breakage. It works with After Effects from CS6 through 2026, and a demo video is available.

Key features

  • Removes unused or hidden layers
  • Prepares projects for archive or handover
  • Protects hidden layers referenced by effects
