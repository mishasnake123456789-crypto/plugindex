What Dirty Letterbox : Film Matt Aspect Ratios does

Dirty Letterbox: Film Matt Aspect Ratios is a pack for After Effects and Premiere Pro that adds a worn film frame to footage. It provides dirty letterbox mattes for 4x3, 2.35x1 and 16x9 aspect ratios, with four matte styles set to Multiply covering Super 8, 16mm, Anamorphic and Kodak.

Four perforation films for Super 8 and 16mm come as modular 8mm and 16mm elements in Screen mode, and two high-resolution film dust elements are included. Files are 8K quality and apply by drag and drop. Editors can quickly give a clip an analog, projected feel.

Key features

  • Dirty letterbox mattes in 4x3, 2.35x1 and 16x9
  • Super 8, 16mm, Anamorphic and Kodak film mattes
  • Modular 8mm and 16mm perforation elements
  • Two film dust elements
  • 8K files, drag-and-drop
