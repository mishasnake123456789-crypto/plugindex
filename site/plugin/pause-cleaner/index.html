What Pause Cleaner does

Pause Cleaner speeds up the editing of spoken material in After Effects. It analyzes the audio, finds silent stretches, and produces a cleaned version of your composition without them, so you aren't slicing every pause by hand.

The result keeps the flow of a talking-head or narration sequence while quickening the pace. Editors producing tutorials, interviews or social clips can use it as a first pass. Release 1.0.0 supports After Effects 2022 through 2026.

Key features

  • Automatic silence detection
  • Builds a cleaned copy of the composition
  • No manual slicing of gaps
  • Keeps spoken sequences flowing
  • Supports After Effects 2022 to 2026
