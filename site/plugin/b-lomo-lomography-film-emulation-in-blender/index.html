What B-Lomo: Lomography Film Emulation In Blender does

B-Lomo is a custom camera for Blender that makes renders look like film photographs, in the style of the Lomography community. The look is created directly in the 3D viewport, with no post-processing or compositor setup needed.

Six Lomo film recipes are inspired by real film stocks, and two lens profiles are provided: a built-in fisheye and a 35mm. Lens characteristics such as vignetting, aberration and film grain are built in, and three scalable resolution presets use the film 3:2 aspect ratio. Recipes can be changed easily. Artists wanting a nostalgic photographic finish will find it quick.

Key features

  • Six Lomo film recipes
  • Fisheye and 35mm lens profiles
  • Vignetting, lens aberration and film grain
  • Three 3:2 resolution presets
  • No compositor needed
