What Dynamic Brush Menus does

Dynamic Brush Menus gives fast access to brushes and tools in Blender's Sculpt and Paint modes. It opens with the spacebar and was bundled with Blender 4.1 originally.

The add-on is about 15 KB, needs no special permissions and is released under GPL v2 or later for Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. Sculptors and texture painters who change brushes constantly can do so without moving to the toolbar. Because the menu opens under the cursor, switching brushes interrupts a stroke far less than reaching for the toolbar.

Key features

  • Spacebar menu for brushes and tools
  • Sculpt and Paint modes
  • Lightweight download
  • For Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
