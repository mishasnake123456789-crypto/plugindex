A native After Effects plugin for optical effects, supplied with 30 HD presets and sample footage.

What Diopter does

Diopter, from The Waenderer, creates lens-style optical effects in video within After Effects. A presets gallery offers 30 HD presets to start from, and the package includes 17 video and 19 still sample clips.

It is native on Apple Silicon through a universal binary, supports Multi-Frame Rendering in After Effects 22 and newer, and handles 16 and 32 bits per channel. It works with After Effects CS6 and later on Mac and Windows, and a user guide is included. Artists after realistic glows, flares and lens artefacts can browse and adapt presets.

Key features

  • 30 HD presets with gallery
  • 17 video and 19 still sample footages
  • Apple Silicon universal binary
  • Multi-Frame Rendering and 16/32 bpc
  • After Effects CS6 and up, Mac and Windows
