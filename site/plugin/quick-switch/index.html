What Quick Switch does

Quick Switch is a Blender add-on by Silvie3D that switches an editor area to another editor with one click. It is designed for laptops and single-monitor setups, where fewer open areas means less clutter.

Buttons appear on their own at the far right of every editor header. Grouped Editor Switching lets you define sets of editors and move between them like browser tabs, with Up and Down controls to reorder them. A toggle moves the buttons to the header's left or right side, a separator toggle is available, and a custom key binding avoids clashing with built-in shortcuts.

Key features

  • Header buttons to switch editors
  • Grouped editor switching like browser tabs
  • Reorder editors within a group
  • Left or right placement and separator toggle
  • Custom key binding
