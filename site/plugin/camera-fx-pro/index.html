What Camera Fx Pro does

Camera FX Pro delivers film-style camera behaviors without manual rigging or keyframing. A Speed Ramp accelerates or slows the camera within a time window, and Camera Shake has adjustable intensity, position and rotation.

Dolly Zoom moves the camera while zooming the lens the opposite way, targeting a subject automatically. A Camera Tracker follows objects, and rack focus shifts attention between subjects. Filmmakers and motion designers get polished moves quickly. Each effect is controlled by simple parameters, so adjusting a shot after review is straightforward.

Key features

  • Speed Ramp
  • Camera Shake with intensity controls
  • Dolly Zoom with automatic subject targeting
  • Object tracking and rack focus
