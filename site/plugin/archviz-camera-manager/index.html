What Archviz Camera Manager does

Archviz Camera Manager gathers every camera of a scene into a single, orderly panel. A new camera can be added with one click and starts from the current viewport position and orientation.

Cameras are placed automatically in a dedicated collection, and the list gives per-camera rename, select, lock and view controls. A settings panel offers perspective or orthographic mode, among other options, plus preview, render and screenshot tools. Architectural visualizers delivering many views of one interior will appreciate the speed.

Key features

  • One-click camera from the current viewport
  • Auto-grouping into a Camera collection
  • Per-camera rename, select, lock and view
  • Preview, render and screenshot tools
