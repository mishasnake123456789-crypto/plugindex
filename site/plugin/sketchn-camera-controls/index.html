What Sketchn Camera Controls does

Sahin Ersoz's Sketchn Camera Controls offers fast ways to put cameras against geometry. One click aligns the active camera to a chosen vertex, edge or face, and the 3D Cursor can jump to a vertex, an edge center or a face center.

You can also tilt the camera by preset values or any rotation you like. Alt+Right Mouse click opens a Camera Controls pie menu, while numpad shortcuts handle vertex, edge and face alignment. It ships as part of Sketch N' Carve. Product and architectural visualizers can frame shots faster.

Key features

  • Align camera to vertex, edge or face
  • Snap 3D Cursor to element centers
  • Tilt at preset or custom angles
  • Pie menu on Alt+Right Mouse
  • Numpad alignment shortcuts
