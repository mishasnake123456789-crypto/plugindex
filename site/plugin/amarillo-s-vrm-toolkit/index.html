What Amarillo's VRM Toolkit does

Amarillo's VRM Toolkit, by AmarilloArts, exports characters as VRM 0.x avatars as well as .amaCharacter files. It is tagged under Import-Export and Animation.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
