What Sfx Studio does

Sfx Studio lets editors find sound effects without opening a browser. It searches over half a million Creative Commons sounds from Freesound.org, which can be previewed in a built-in player and imported or dropped onto the timeline.

Custom effects can also be generated with AI. Licensing differs: CC0 sounds are public domain, CC-BY requires crediting the author, and CC-BY-NC requires credit and bars commercial use. It is aimed at editors who need quick sound effects and want to respect license terms when publishing.

Key features

  • Search 500,000+ Freesound sounds
  • AI sound effect generation
  • Built-in preview player
  • Import or drop onto the timeline
  • Shows CC0, CC-BY and CC-BY-NC licensing
