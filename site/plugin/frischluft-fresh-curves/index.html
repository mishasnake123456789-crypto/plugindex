What Frischluft Fresh Curves does

Fresh Curves from Frischluft offers a more capable curves tool for After Effects and Premiere Pro than the native effect. It brings extra control and accuracy to grading, working in brightness, saturation and hue channels as well as the usual RGBA.

Relative Curves adjust one attribute according to another, with pre-blur and post-blur curves, and curves are drawn as Bezier cubic splines with tangent handles and interpolation modes similar to the graph editor. Invert Curves lets you work in your own colour space by applying a custom gamma, grading, and then reversing it. Histograms, a large resizable interface with zoom for out-of-range float values, and direct value editing complete the toolset.

Key features

  • Brightness, saturation and hue channels beyond RGBA
  • Relative Curves with pre- and post-blur
  • Bezier splines with tangent handles
  • Invert Curves for custom colour spaces
  • Integrated histograms and zoomable interface
