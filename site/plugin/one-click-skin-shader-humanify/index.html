What One Click Skin Shader - Humanify does

One Click Skin Shader - Humanify from Blendify applies photorealistic skin shading to human models in Blender with one click. It is designed for artists at any experience level who want convincing results without building a skin shader from scratch.

It supports Blender versions 4.0 through 5.2, is released under the GPL license and includes documentation. Character artists, portrait renderers and visualization teams can quickly get a believable base skin look and refine it as needed.
