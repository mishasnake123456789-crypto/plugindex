What Bonera - Manual Rigging Toolkit does

Bonera is a Blender addon from TinkerBoi that speeds up the repetitive parts of rigging by hand. Instead of a full rigging system, it is a collection of individual utilities that you use where needed.

Generate Stretch Chain makes stretchy bone chains quickly, Generate Eyelid builds an eyelid setup at once, and Generate Twist Bone gives the forearm a fast twist setup that avoids the candy wrap effect. Add / Apply Bone Shape offers premade widgets with thumbnails and supports your own, and Bone Chain from Object Hierarchy helps with hard surface models.

Key features

  • Generate Stretch Chain
  • One-click eyelid setup
  • Twist bone setup for forearms
  • Bone shape widgets with thumbnails
  • Bone chain from object hierarchy
