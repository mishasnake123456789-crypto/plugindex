Blender add-on for rendering 360, 180 and dome panoramas with EEVEE by stitching a six-camera cubemap.

What Eevee 360 does

Eevee 360 brings panoramic and dome output to Blender's EEVEE engine. It renders a cubemap from six cameras and stitches the result into equirectangular 360, 180 degree, skydome or custom dome formats.

A single click takes care of camera placement, resolution scaling and the stitching pass, and you can render either stills or animation. Output goes up to 16K. The stitching step uses a lightweight node tree for faster final composition, and an experimental tilted dome preset offers square output with an upward camera tilt. It has been tested from Blender 2.93 through 5.1.

Key features

  • 360 equirectangular, 180, skydome and custom dome modes
  • Six-camera cubemap stitched automatically
  • Resolutions up to 16K
  • Stills and animation sequences
  • Experimental tilted dome preset
