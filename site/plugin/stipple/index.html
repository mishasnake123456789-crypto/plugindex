What Stipple does

Stipple from Rowbyte renders footage as a field of dots, with the pattern determined by the image beneath. Dots are placed through a physics simulation, so they settle and move coherently from frame to frame instead of flickering.

External layers can supply colour maps, sprites can be randomised, and processing runs through a GPU pipeline. It suits artists making illustrative, engraved or pointillist treatments of video and titles inside After Effects.

Key features

  • Temporally coherent stippling driven by footage
  • Patterns built by a physics simulation
  • External layers as colour maps
  • Randomize sprites option
  • GPU processing pipeline
