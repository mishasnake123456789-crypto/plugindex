What Rase Grimm Font Family does

Rase Grimm Font Family by Highground is a display typeface that mixes blackletter forms with graffiti attitude, made for After Effects and Premiere Pro titles. It comes in three variants: Grimm Regular, Grimm Curves, and the all-caps Grimm Fat.

The regular and curved styles have traditional capitals and lowercase letters. All three include sets of initial, terminal and isolated forms as contextual alternates, so the start and end of a word or phrase can be embellished automatically with special characters. Stylistic alternates, standard and discretionary ligatures, Latin supplement characters for multilingual text, and a full range of punctuation and symbols are included.

Key features

  • Three variants: Regular, Curves and all-caps Fat
  • Contextual initial, terminal and isolated letter forms
  • Automatic word-edge embellishments
  • Stylistic alternates and ligatures
  • Latin supplement characters for multilingual text
