What Sculpt Stroke Replay does

Sculpt Stroke Replay lets you redo your latest brush stroke in Blender and decide how strong it should be. A live preview shows the effect before you accept it.

Strength can be positive or negative, from partial reduction to amplification, and the controls sit in the Sculpt Mode sidebar with an optional shortcut. Sculptors who want to refine or soften a stroke instead of undoing and redoing it will find it quick.

Key features

  • Replays the last sculpt stroke
  • Strength preview updates live until you confirm
  • Positive and negative strength
  • Sculpt Mode sidebar panel
