What BOOM Library Magic - Arcane Forces does

BOOM Library Magic: Arcane Forces delivers supernatural sound effects for After Effects, Premiere Pro and other tools that read WAV files. Elements such as fire, ice, electricity, energy, projectiles, summons, teleports, whooshes and wind are pushed to extremes.

The Designed edition has 496 ready-made sounds with variations in size, length and character, such as arcane, divine and cursed. The Construction Kit offers 3,240 raw sources for building your own, and the Bundle merges both for 3,700 sounds at 12.8 GB. All files are 24-bit, 96 kHz, with PDF and XLS content details.

Key features

  • Designed edition with 496 sounds
  • Construction Kit with 3,240 sources
  • Bundle of 3,700 sounds, 12.8GB
  • Arcane, divine and cursed variations
  • 24-bit, 96kHz WAV
