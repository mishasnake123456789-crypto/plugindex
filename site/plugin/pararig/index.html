Blender rigging add-on that auto-generates draggable slider rigs and the driver bindings behind them.

What ParaRig does

ParaRig by Zosuya builds slider-style control rigs and wires up the drivers for you, so adjustable parameters become draggable controls without hand-built driver setups. It is listed under Rigging.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
