Blender add-on for creating and managing vertex groups, face maps and its own Zen Sets from object, collection and mesh element selections.

What Zen Sets does

Zen Sets handles groups in Blender: built-in Vertex Groups, Face Maps and custom Zen Sets Groups made from selections of objects, collections or mesh elements. Selections can be saved and restored whenever needed.

Multi-object support works across all group types, and a display system visualizes group contents in the viewport. Modelers and riggers who juggle many selections will find it a convenient way to store and revisit them. Stored sets save time on complex models where you repeatedly need the same group of faces, vertices or objects.

Key features

  • Vertex Groups, Face Maps and Zen Sets Groups
  • Save and restore selections
  • Multi-object support
  • Viewport display of groups
