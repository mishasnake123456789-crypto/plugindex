Applies keyframed motion presets such as slide, bounce, spin and pulse to selected Blender objects, plus auto animate modes.

What Click Motion does

Click Motion lets you animate objects without setting keys by hand. Select objects, choose a preset, and keyframed motion with easing and timing is created.

Presets are grouped into movement, rotation and scale/FX, with looks like Slide, Bounce, Float, Shake, Spin, Sway, Wiggle, Pulse, Pop, Blink and Strobe. Auto Animate offers Balanced, Chaos and Cinematic modes, and a speed slider plus delay control timing. Motion designers can block out titles or logos quickly.

Key features

  • Motion, rotation and scale/FX presets
  • Auto Animate: Balanced, Chaos, Cinematic
  • Speed slider for timing
  • Delay control
  • Creates real keyframes with easing
