What Cinemacolour does

FishCakes' Cinemacolour supplies creative color looks for Blender, made to work with Filmic Blender. Version 2.0 holds 100 premium looks that give renders a cinematic or stylistic feel.

A look browser in the UI lets you step through them, and a free test scene is provided. It is meant for Cycles and includes documentation. Artists who want fast color grading options without building LUTs themselves can compare styles in seconds.

Key features

  • 100 premium looks in version 2.0
