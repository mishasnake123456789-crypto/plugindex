Manages language or content versions inside one After Effects project by tagging layers.

What Versioner does

Versioner removes the need to duplicate projects or comps when you deliver multiple language or content variations. Layers are tagged to specific versions, and one click switches between them.

You can create, duplicate and delete versions, convert to a chosen one, unlink layers, and change a layer's name or color. The plugin tracks versions through layer IDs and timeline markers, cleans up its own markers and unused IDs, and never deletes your artwork. It suits motion designers producing localized deliverables.

Key features

  • Tag layers to versions, switch with one click
  • No duplicated projects or comps
  • Create, duplicate and delete versions
  • Convert to a selected version or unlink layers
  • Tracks versions via layer IDs and markers
