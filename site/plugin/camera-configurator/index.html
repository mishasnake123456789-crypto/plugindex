What Camera Configurator does

Camera Configurator is a camera management add-on for Blender 5.x that treats each camera as a shot. You can create as many shots as you need, and every shot keeps its own camera, rig, frame range, focus and F-stop values, target tracking and render options.

Seven rig styles come built in, namely Dolly, Orbit, Crane, Handheld, FPS, Drone and Static, each with automatic animation. Render profiles, batch rendering and reusable shot libraries streamline production. It suits filmmakers and animators handling many angles.

Key features

  • Unlimited shots with individual camera setups
  • Seven rigs: Dolly, Orbit, Crane, Handheld, FPS, Drone, Static
  • Per-shot DOF, F-stop and focus object
  • Render profiles and batch rendering
  • Reusable shot libraries
