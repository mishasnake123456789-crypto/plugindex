A Blender add-on for painting displacement onto meshes using layers, with modifiers handled behind the scenes.

What Displace Painter does

Displace Painter lets you sculpt surface relief in Blender by painting displacement through a layer-based stack. You can paint mountains, valleys, rivers, metal plating, exposed wires or procedural damage while a simplified system looks after the modifiers.

Layers can be reordered and tracked, an influence control decides whether a layer affects another's displacement, and the detail system allows as many separate details as the mesh requires. Materials are driven through attribute names, covering color, roughness, texture and shader. Terrain and environment artists will find it more organized than brush-only sculpting.

Key features

  • Layer-based displacement painting
  • Influence control between layers
  • Unlimited separate details
  • Material control via attribute names
