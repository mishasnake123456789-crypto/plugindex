What Datamosh 2 does

Datamosh 2 from Plugin Play recreates the smeared, melting look of corrupted video compression inside After Effects. Instead of breaking files by hand, you control the glitch with parameters and timeline markers.

You can remove frames, hijack motion so one clip drags another, and swap motion from different footage. Mosh Maps shape where the effect lands, and over 60 moshing algorithms, among them Multiply, Average, Mirror, Sweep and sine and cosine variants, give a wide palette. This version is claimed to be 16 times more precise and adds six parameters. Music video editors and glitch artists get repeatable results without hacking codecs.
