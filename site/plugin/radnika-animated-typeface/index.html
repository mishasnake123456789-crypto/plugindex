What Radnika - Animated Typeface does

Radnika is an animated sans-serif from Animography meant for many uses. Its design bridges the expressive, strong faces of the 1800s with the more reserved typefaces of the 1900s.

It is built for After Effects 2020 through 2024, with animation included in the letterforms, and a demo video is on the developer's page. It can serve headlines, captions and brand sequences alike. Its flexible character makes it a safe pick for projects that need one animated font to cover many moods.

Key features
