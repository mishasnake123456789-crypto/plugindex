What Lim - Unofficial Animation Toolkit For Human Generator & Blender does

LIM is an unofficial animation toolkit for characters made with Human Generator in Blender. It provides over 360 ready clips: 166 body animations in nine categories and 196 facial animations in ten categories covering eyes, head, neck and shape key motion.

Facial animations loop fully, and more than half of the body categories support seamless loops. A built-in Animation Browser works globally rather than being tied to a file. Workflow tools for Rigify rigs complement the library. Animators wanting to populate characters with idle, gesture and expression motion quickly will save a lot of work.

Key features

  • 166 body and 196 facial animations
  • Loopable facial clips and many looping body clips
  • Global Animation Browser
  • Workflow tools for Human Generator Rigify rigs
