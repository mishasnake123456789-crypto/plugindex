What BOOM Library Cinematic Impacts does

BOOM Library Cinematic Impacts is a sound toolkit for After Effects, Premiere Pro and other software, built around the structure of an impact. Four elements, lead-in, impact, sustain and tail, build tension, deliver power, carry depth and leave a lasting finish.

The Designed pack contains effects that can be dragged in and used immediately. The Construction Kit takes a modular approach, letting you mix and match the four components yourself. Files are 24-bit, 96 kHz WAV for Windows and Mac. Sound designers, editors and content creators working on trailers, commercials, promos, games, movies and TV can use it to punctuate titles and highlights.

Key features

  • Lead-in, impact, sustain and tail elements
  • Drag-and-drop Designed pack
  • Modular Construction Kit
  • Impacts that stay clear of the mix
  • 24-bit, 96kHz WAV
