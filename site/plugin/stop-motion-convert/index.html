What Stop Motion Convert does

Stop Motion Convert, by Kamil Pekala, takes a long continuous recording and turns it into a stop motion-style animation. The conversion happens within Premiere Pro as an extension.

It supports Premiere Pro CC 2019 through 2025, includes a fly-out menu with related issues fixed in version 1.0.5, and has a demo video on YouTube. It saves manual frame selection and is aimed at those making stop motion style videos from continuous footage.

Key features

  • Converts long recordings into stop motion
  • Extension inside Premiere Pro
  • Premiere Pro CC 2019 to 2025
