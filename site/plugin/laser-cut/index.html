Cuts Blender meshes along a drawn line with a laser preview, parallel cuts and mirror-axis cutting.

What Laser-Cut does

Laser-Cut turns a line you draw into a cut through the mesh, shown first as an emissive laser preview. It provides clean bisects as well as parallel cuts and mirror-axis cutting.

Parallel cuts range from 1 to 64 with spacing control, and mirror cutting works on the X, Y and Z axes. It works with Cycles and Eevee and includes documentation. Modelers can slice objects precisely with a visual guide.

Key features

  • Real-time emissive laser preview
  • 1 to 64 parallel cuts with spacing
  • Mirror-axis cutting on X, Y and Z
  • Clean bisects
