What Boom Library - Fields & Spaces: Outdoor Impulse Responses - Stereo does

Fields & Spaces: Outdoor Impulse Responses (Stereo) from BOOM Library is a set of 123 reverb captures taken outdoors, ranging from natural landscapes to urban surroundings. It is meant for After Effects and Premiere Pro workflows where a third-party convolution reverb plugin is available to load the files.

The stereo version was mixed down from the ambisonic edition and keeps dense, rich tails. Most locations were recorded at several distances, and files are 24-bit WAV at 48 to 96 kHz for Windows and Mac. The library totals 1.2 GB and helps sound designers place effects or dialogue convincingly outdoors.

Key features

  • 123 outdoor impulse responses
  • Natural and urban locations
  • Multiple recording distances for most spots
  • 24-bit WAV, 48-96kHz
  • Requires a third-party convolution reverb
