Camera preset and animation manager for Blender with quick rigs, DOF targets and cinematic toggles.

What Ez Cam does

Ez Cam saves, recalls, renames, updates and deletes camera presets that hold transform, focal length and other settings, including depth of field, motion blur and transparency.

EZ Quick Camera Rig builds an animated camera rig with a single click, and a quick tool creates a DOF target. Cinematic effects such as motion blur and film transparency can be toggled easily. It suits artists who reuse a handful of camera setups across scenes.

Key features

  • Camera presets with transform and focal length
  • DOF, motion blur and transparency saved
  • One-click animated camera rig
  • Quick DOF target creation
