What Camera Shake "Blender Kit" does

Camera Shake Blender Kit from 3DVision gives scenes a dynamic feel. Four effects are included: Soft, Earthquake, Run and Beast attack, suitable for architecture, industrial prototypes and characters.

Circular, traveling movement across X and Y and straight-line movement along Z can be used independently or together, with options managed through the properties panel. Simple to add energy to a camera. The effects can be combined or layered to simulate impacts, running footage or creature attacks, adding drama to otherwise static shots.

Key features

  • Soft, Earthquake, Run and Beast attack shakes
  • Circular XY and linear Z motion
  • Use independently or combined
  • Properties panel controls
