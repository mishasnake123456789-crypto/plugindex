What SF Caddy does

SF Caddy from Script-Fighter is a quick asset replacement tool for After Effects, developed with character animation and lip-sync in mind. It swaps items inside a replacement precomp and can drive lip-sync from markers.

Items inside the replacement precomp can be animated, there is an edit mode and an option to reverse the animation, and the panel docks within After Effects on both Mac and Windows. It also works for anything else that needs repeated replacement, such as swapping expressions, mouths or props.

Key features

  • Quick asset replacement
  • Lip-sync driven by markers
  • Animate items inside the replacement precomp
  • Edit mode and reverse animation
  • Dockable panel on Mac and Windows
