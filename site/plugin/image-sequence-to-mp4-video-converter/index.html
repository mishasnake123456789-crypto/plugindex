What Image Sequence to MP4 Video Converter does

Image Sequence to MP4 Video Converter turns rendered frames into finished video without leaving Blender. One click encodes a folder of images with Blender's own FFmpeg encoder.

Resolution presets such as 1080p, 4K and vertical, or custom sizes, are offered with fill, fit and stretch scaling methods. Codec control includes H.264, AV1 and DNxHD with quality presets, color management works with AgX, Filmic and Standard, and the scene frame rate is used automatically. Sequences in BMP, TGA, TIF, EXR, JPG or PNG work.

Key features

  • One-click image sequence to MP4
  • Resolution presets and fit methods
  • H.264, AV1 and DNxHD codecs
  • Color management integration
  • Accepts BMP, TGA, TIF, EXR, JPG and PNG frames
