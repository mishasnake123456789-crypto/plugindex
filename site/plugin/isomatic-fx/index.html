What Isomatic FX does

Isomatic FX is a Fremox plugin for isometric artwork in After Effects. It supports two approaches: a faked 2D version that transforms flat layers, and true 3D when you need real depth.

In the faked mode, Left, Right and Top buttons skew a layer onto the matching isometric plane, three movement sliders keep it constrained to an axis, and a position expression is applied to the layer. Clicking a faked isometry button a second time turns it off while keeping the sliders and expression. Floating licenses are supported, and it works from After Effects CS6 through 2026.

Key features

  • Faked 2D or true 3D isometric modes
  • Left, Right and Top isometry buttons
  • Three movement axis sliders
  • Toggle off while keeping sliders and expression
  • Floating license support
