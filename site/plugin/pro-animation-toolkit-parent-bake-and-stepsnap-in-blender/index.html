What Pro Animation Toolkit - Parent, Bake, And Stepsnap In Blender does

Pro Animation Toolkit covers parenting, baking and snapping. Smart parenting modes, Child-Of and LocRot, snap automatically for both objects and bones.

Influence control lets you animate, enable, disable, insert or delete parent influence keys. Constraint baking and StepSnap transform tools complete the set. Useful for handing props between hands or switching parent spaces. Together the tools cover common situations such as switching a prop between hands or baking a constraint into keys.

Key features
