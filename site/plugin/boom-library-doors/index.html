What BOOM Library Doors does

BOOM Library Doors is a royalty-free collection of more than 2,100 sounds in over 350 WAV files, about 3.5 GB in all. It works in After Effects, Premiere Pro and other WAV-compatible software.

Coverage includes wooden and metal room doors, security doors, gates, sliding and electric doors and lifts, plus furnishings such as cupboards, drawers, refrigerators and lockers. Door-related items like keys, chains, bolts, punch cards and knobs are included. Ninety doors and furnishings were captured close up with MS or XY microphones, both dry and with real reverb. Format is 96 kHz, 24-bit.

Key features

  • 2,100+ sounds across 350+ WAV files
  • Wooden, metal, sliding, electric and lift doors
  • Furnishings, keys, chains and bolts
  • Close-miked dry and reverb versions
  • 96kHz/24-bit WAV with PDF and XLS info
