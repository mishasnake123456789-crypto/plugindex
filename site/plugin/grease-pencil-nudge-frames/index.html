Blender add-on that shortens or extends grease pencil frame exposure with D and F keys in the dopesheet.

What Grease Pencil Nudge Frames does

Grease Pencil Nudge Frames makes hand-drawn timing tweaks quicker in Blender. Hover over the Grease Pencil Dopesheet and tap D to cut a frame's exposure by one frame or F to add one.

Holding a key applies bigger adjustments. The change applies across every unlocked layer of all selected grease pencil objects. Shortening is blocked when a keyframe sits just one frame from the next, preventing overwrites. Keys can be remapped in Preferences by searching for nudge, and Blender 4.3.1 or newer is needed.

Key features

  • D shortens and F extends the current frame
  • Applies across unlocked layers of selected objects
  • Protects against accidental overwrites
  • Remappable shortcuts
  • Blender 4.3.1 and newer
