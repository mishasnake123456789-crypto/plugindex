What BOOM Library Creature Foley does

Creature Foley from BOOM Library supplies movement sounds for monsters and animals, delivered as WAV files that work in Premiere Pro. The full bundle holds more than 19,000 sounds across 1,689 files and 16.15 GB.

Categories include footsteps, claws, beaks, bites, insects, body drops and falls, scratches, swipe attacks, motion and wings. Surface interactions were captured on materials such as concrete, dirt, grass, gravel, sand, snow, water, mud and wood, with up to 20 variations per file. Files are 24-bit, 96 kHz for Windows and Mac, and sound editors can pick the edition that suits the job.
