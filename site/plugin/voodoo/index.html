What voodoo does

voodoo is a PENCIL Park tool that makes puppet pin animation easier in After Effects. It centers on pin tracking, letting pins follow motion, and on controllers that let you puppeteer them.

The controller size can be changed, and the interface offers multilanguage support, including Japanese. It is compatible with After Effects 2020 through 2024, and a demo video is available. Animators who deform characters and artwork with pins can use it to rig and move them with less manual keyframing.

Key features

  • Pin tracking for puppet pins
  • Controllers for puppeteering
  • Adjustable controller size
  • Multilanguage support including Japanese
  • Compatible with After Effects 2020 to 2024
