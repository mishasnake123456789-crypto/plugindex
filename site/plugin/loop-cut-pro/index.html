What Loop Cut Pro does

Loop Cut Pro is a Blender add-on from Nodes Interactive for adding loop cuts with one click right under the cursor, and you can keep editing the mesh continuously. The default hotkey is Alt+R.

Modes can be combined: Preserve Shape on Alt, Snap to Center on Ctrl, and Even spacing on Shift with Flip Even on F. It benefits modelers doing retopology, hard surface work and edge-loop heavy tasks where loop cuts are added constantly.

Key features

  • One-click loop cuts under the cursor
  • Continuous mesh editing
  • Preserve Shape and Snap to Center modes
  • Even mode with Flip Even
  • Default hotkey Alt+R
