What Flaxflow - Motion Tracking & Geometry Toolkit does

FlaxFlow extends Blender's tracking panel and 3D viewport with tools for match-moving. An optical flow tracker aims to run faster and more reliably than the native one, while an improved camera solver lowers solve errors and color-codes trackers by their error.

One-click geometry tools let you click footage to get a 3D position, connect points into geometry and reproject textures. A filtering system sorts, deletes and refines tracks, including an outlier finder. Other additions are a sparse point cloud generator, per-frame coverage display, ST map generation and chessboard calibration.

Key features

  • Optical flow tracker
  • Enhanced camera solver with error color-coding
  • One-click filtering and outlier finder
  • Geometry built from tracked footage points
  • ST map generation and chessboard calibration
