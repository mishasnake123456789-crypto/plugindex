A Blender add-on that removes visible texture tiling and blends two materials with height-aware transitions.

What V-Tile Pro does

V-Tile Pro offers two non-destructive tools for materials in Blender. Anti-Tile mode removes the visible repetition from a textured material in one click, which is a common problem on large floors, walls and terrain.

Eighteen presets cover fabric, wood, stone, metal, architecture and organic surfaces, and there is auto-detect. The Material Mixer blends two materials with height-aware transitions, so peaks and valleys take different materials, controlled by live sliders for amount and scale. Everything updates in the viewport. Environment and archviz artists polishing surfaces will find it quick.

Key features

  • Anti-Tile mode in one click
  • 18 presets plus auto-detect
  • Material Mixer with height-aware blending
  • Live sliders for blend amount and scale
  • Non-destructive
