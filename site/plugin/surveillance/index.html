Blender add-on that shows clickable camera thumbnails in the 3D viewport for quickly switching scene cameras.

What Surveillance does

Surveillance gives you a visual way to choose among the cameras in a Blender scene. Each camera appears as a preview thumbnail you can click to make active.

Two display modes, Filmstrip and Tiled, are reached from the View > Cameras menu. The two operators are meant to cut the time spent working out which camera is which. It is compatible with the Cycles and Eevee render engines and ships with documentation, making it handy for scenes with many viewpoints.

Key features

  • Clickable preview thumbnail for every camera
  • Filmstrip and Tiled display modes
  • Found under View > Cameras
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
  • Documentation included
