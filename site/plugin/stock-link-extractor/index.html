What Stock Link Extractor does

Stock Link Extractor scans a project and lists the web links behind your stock media, which makes it simpler to gather everything that needs licensing before delivery. It works with After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve projects.

It can pull video, audio and image links, recognizing providers such as Getty Images, Adobe Stock, iStock, Shutterstock, Unsplash and Pexels, and others including Filmsupply and Dissolve. Switches for videos and for images let you limit the search to the media type you need.

Key features

  • One-click URL extraction from projects
  • Video, audio and image links
  • Recognizes Getty, Adobe Stock, iStock, Shutterstock and more
  • Filmsupply and Dissolve support
  • Toggles for videos and images
