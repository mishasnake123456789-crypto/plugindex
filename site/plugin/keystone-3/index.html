What Keystone 3 does

Keystone 3 by Romain Cousin collects layer-handling utilities for animators in After Effects. It aligns, staggers, orders and stretches layers and applies colour labels.

Stagger comes with Bezier controllers, three calculation methods and a cumulative mode, and there are Keyframe Values and Layer Order sections. The Anchor section repositions anchor points or creates nulls with a Ctrl or Cmd click, targeting layers, groups, the comp, custom coordinates or internal anchors of shape and text layers; those nulls sit above the selected layers and are trimmed to match their children. Section shortcuts speed navigation. Useful for layered motion graphics.

Key features

  • Align, stagger, order and stretch layers
  • Stagger with Bezier controllers and cumulative mode
  • Keyframe Values and Layer Order sections
  • Anchor repositioning and null creation
  • Section shortcuts
