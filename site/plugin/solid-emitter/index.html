What Solid Emitter does

Solid Emitter from Dobro makes any mesh launch other objects as rigid bodies in Blender.

You choose how many objects are fired, from small batches to avalanche mode, and aim the stream with an empty, precisely or randomly. Expulsion velocity ranges from gentle nudges to strong launches. Collision settings control whether emitted objects bounce, slide or splat. It suits debris and falling-object effects. Because emitted objects are real rigid bodies, they interact with the rest of the scene rather than passing through it.

Key features

  • Control the number of launched objects
  • Aim with an empty
  • Adjustable expulsion velocity
  • Bounce, slide or splat collision types
  • Avalanche mode
