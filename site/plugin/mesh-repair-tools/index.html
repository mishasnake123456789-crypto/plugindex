What Mesh Repair Tools does

Mesh Repair Tools by SineWave brings several mesh-fixing functions together in a single Blender panel. They are triggered with simple clicks and are mainly meant for preparing models for 3D printing.

The order of fixes can be adjusted to suit your needs, and redundant functions have been left out to keep the interface lean. The add-on is about 13.5 KB, needs no special permissions and works with Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. Makers and printing hobbyists get a quick route to watertight meshes.

Key features

  • Multiple fixing tools in one toolbox
  • Adjustable fixing sequence
  • Designed for 3D printing cleanup
  • Lightweight download
