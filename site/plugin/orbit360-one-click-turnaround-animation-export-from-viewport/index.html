What Orbit360 - One Click Turnaround Animation Export From Viewport does

Orbit360 creates turntable animations straight from the Blender viewport. It is meant for portfolios, product visualization, client previews and social posts.

Export as a PNG sequence, GIF or H.264 MP4, with a fallback chain through imageio, FFmpeg and the Sequencer. Resolution presets include 1080p, 720p, 4K and square formats, and all viewport shading modes work, with wireframe color control. Grid, axes and floor can be hidden, and output lands in a timestamped folder.

Key features

  • PNG sequence, GIF and MP4 export
  • Resolution presets including 4K and square
  • All viewport shading modes with wireframe options
  • Clean output with auto-hidden grid and axes
