What AEJuice Light Leaks Volume 3 does

AEJuice Light Leaks Volume 3 provides another 45 light leak clips to layer over footage. They suit editors who have exhausted earlier packs and need fresh looks for transitions and mood.

Import is by drag and drop, and the pack is organised through the AEJuice Pack Manager with fast preview and favourites. It runs with After Effects CC 2015, Premiere Pro CC 2021 and Final Cut Pro 10.4.9 or newer, while DaVinci Resolve, Sony Vegas, Avid and OBS can use the transparent or black-background videos.
