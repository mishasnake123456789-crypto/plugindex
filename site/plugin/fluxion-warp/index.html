What Fluxion Warp does

Fluxion Warp is a grape design plugin that creates a vector field from a mask and pushes the image along the resulting flow. It gives flowing, smeared motion that follows a path you draw.

The width and strength of the field are adjustable, and turbulence adds natural variation with a speed setting and an animatable noise phase. Noise map scale, flow direction reversal, endpoint behavior and width falloff are also controllable. The controls are simple and intuitive, making it suitable for liquid-like distortions and wind-swept looks.

Key features

  • Vector field generated from a mask
  • Field width and strength controls
  • Turbulence with animatable noise phase
  • Reverse flow direction
  • Endpoint behavior and width falloff
