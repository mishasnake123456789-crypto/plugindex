What REDitors does

REDitors is a plugin for Premiere Pro and After Effects that adds AI-assisted editing tools inside the app. It targets common time-consuming steps like trimming dead air and cutting footage down.

Its toolset covers a silence remover with auto cut, captions, transcription, voice to text, motion graphics and titles, all in one plugin. Editors who produce speech-heavy content such as talking-head videos can use the speech-to-text functions to generate captions and transcripts, then rely on automated cutting to speed up the rough edit.

Key features

  • Silence remover and auto cut
  • Captions and transcription
  • Voice to text
  • Motion graphics and titles
  • AI-assisted editing inside the host app
