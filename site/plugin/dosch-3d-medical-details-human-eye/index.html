What Dosch 3D: Medical Details - Human Eye does

Medical Details - Human Eye is an anatomical model from Dosch Design for Cinema 4D, made for close-ups and animations. It includes the optic nerve, blood vessels, muscles, retina, lacrimal gland and other structures, all fully textured.

Scale, materials and textures can be changed, every color can be edited, and your own textures and labels can be applied. Parts can be hidden or made see-through, which helps expose inner layers, and elements or the whole eye can be animated. Medical illustrators, educators and science-video makers can use it for explanatory visuals.

Key features

  • Detailed textured human eye anatomy
  • Optic nerve, retina, muscles, lacrimal gland
  • Editable materials, colors and labels
  • Hide or fade parts to reveal inner layers
  • Animation of elements or whole model
