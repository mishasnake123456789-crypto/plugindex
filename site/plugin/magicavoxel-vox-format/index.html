What MagicaVoxel VOX format does

MagicaVoxel VOX format brings .vox files from MagicaVoxel into Blender. It offers multiple meshing styles, color handling and material options.

Meshing types include Models, simple cubes or greedy meshing styles. Material options range from Ignore and Vertex Color to Material Per Color and textured models with UV unwrap. Joined models, a changeable voxel size and configurable origins are supported. Voxel artists can reuse their work in Blender. Choosing between greedy and simple cube meshing lets you favor lower polygon counts or exact voxel blocks.

Key features

  • Models, simple cubes or greedy meshing styles
  • Several material modes including UV textured
  • Join models together
  • Adjustable voxel size and origin
