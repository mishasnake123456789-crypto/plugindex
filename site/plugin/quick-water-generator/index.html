What Quick Water Generator does

Quick Water Generator adds water to Blender scenes through a few simple controls in the 3D Viewport side panel. One click produces surfaces for rivers, lakes and oceans, with adjustable waves, reflections, transparency and color.

Terrain support lets you add landscapes and ground surfaces, and built-in assets such as rocks, plants and other decorations help finish a scene. The tab opens with the N key. It works with Blender 3.0 through 5.0 and is distributed under the GPL. Environment and landscape artists wanting a ready water setup without a simulation will find it straightforward.

Key features

  • One-click rivers, lakes and oceans
  • Wave, reflection, transparency and color controls
  • Terrain support
  • Built-in rocks, plants and decor
  • Blender 3.0 to 5.0
