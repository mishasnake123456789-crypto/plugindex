What Group It! Add-On does

Group It! gives Blender a simple grouping system. Select two or more objects, click Group, and they are wrapped in a Grouped Cube that acts as a parent; Ungroup reverses it, removing the parent and the bounding cube.

Groups can be duplicated with their contents using a button or Ctrl+Shift+D, deleted along with their objects, locked, renamed and color-coded. Real-time Auto Resize updates the cube as objects inside move. Modelers and layout artists who want to move clusters of objects as a unit without complicated hierarchies will find it handy.

Key features

  • Group two or more objects in two clicks
  • Ungroup removes parent and bounding cube
  • Duplicate Group with Ctrl+Shift+D
  • Delete, lock, rename and color-code groups
  • Real-time Auto Resize
