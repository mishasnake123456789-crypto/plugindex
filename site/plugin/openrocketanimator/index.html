What Openrocketanimator does

OpenRocketAnimator, or ORA, imports 3D rocket models exported from OpenRocket and animates them using CSV simulation data, aiming at physically accurate flight visualizations.

It loads .obj models, with a Fix Scale option, reads OpenRocket .csv files and detects the position, time and rotation columns automatically, then animates the rocket. Hobbyists, students and rocketry teams can produce clear flight visuals. Under the hood you get imports OpenRocket .obj models and fix Scale option.

Key features

  • Imports OpenRocket .obj models
  • Fix Scale option
  • Detects position, time and rotation columns
  • Animates from simulation CSV
