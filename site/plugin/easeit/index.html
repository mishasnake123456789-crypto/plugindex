What Easeit does

Easeit, by AndyCuccaro, adds easing presets to keyframes in Blender. Choose two or more keyframes whose values differ, then click a preset.

It works in both the timeline or dope sheet and the graph editor, putting an Easeit tab into the editor sidebar. Written in Python, released under GPL-3.0 or later and requiring no special permissions, it supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. Animators get quick, consistent easing without hand-editing handles.

Key features

  • Applies easing presets to selected keyframes
  • Works in dope sheet and graph editor
  • Easeit tab in the editor sidebar
  • Several easing presets
  • Supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
