What Easy Section does

Easy Section creates and manages architectural sections in Blender and exports them for CAD.

A Boolean engine adds, updates and removes sections non-destructively, managing modifiers and cleanup automatically. Smart Sorting cuts walls and slabs but keeps furniture and plants intact. Fast Mode previews sections before real-time Booleans, with a one-click flip, and DXF export offers layered output with functional hatches. Architects can generate plans and sections from a 3D model and hand them to CAD without redrawing.

Key features

  • Boolean-based section engine
  • Non-destructive modifier management
  • Smart Sorting keeps furniture and plants
  • Fast Mode preview and flip
  • Layered DXF export with hatches
