What Wrapper Addon does

Wrapper Addon produces physics-driven models in Blender through a short generate-and-bake process. You press Generate Wrap, watch the simulation reach a look you like, and then bake to finalize the object.

It works with both Cycles and Eevee, is compatible with Blender 3.6 through 4.4, and is released under the GPL license. The developer, Blender Procedural, includes documentation to explain the settings.

Key features

  • One-click Generate Wrap
  • Bake step to finalize the result
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
  • Blender 3.6 through 4.4
  • Documentation included
