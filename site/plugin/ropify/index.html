Blender add-on that creates ropes, cables, chains, belts and straps procedurally from curves drawn in the viewport.

What Ropify does

Ropify builds rope-like objects in Blender by drawing in the viewport, with a non-destructive result.

Free, Catenary and Spiral modes shape the path, and profiles give rope, string, chain or belt looks. Surface snapping uses normals, chains can use your own meshes, and end caps suit hooks and connectors. Procedural noise adds natural variation, and everything stays editable in real time. It works well for props, rigging details and environment dressing.

Key features

  • Ropes, cables, chains, belts and straps
  • Free, Catenary and Spiral drawing modes
  • Surface snapping using normals
  • Custom meshes for chains
  • Procedural noise and end caps
