Blender add-on that creates a camera from the current viewport angle and keyframes its transform and lens settings.

What Camera2View does

Camera2View, by zets, turns whatever you are looking at in the Blender viewport into a camera with one click. The new camera matches your exact position and angle, then the view switches to it and locks.

A sidebar tab keyframes position and rotation for basic moves, and individual settings such as lens shift, clipping range, lens unit, ortho scale and focal length can be keyed separately. Buttons step between multiple Camera2View cameras and jump between keyframes. It suits blocking camera moves for previs, cutscenes and flythroughs.

Key features

  • Camera from current viewport view
  • Switches to camera view automatically
  • Keyframes position and rotation
  • Keys focal length, ortho scale, clipping and lens shift
  • Next and previous camera and keyframe buttons
