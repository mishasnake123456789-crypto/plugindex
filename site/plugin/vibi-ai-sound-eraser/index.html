Separates a clip's audio into per-speaker stems and background, then returns a cleaned mix to the Premiere Pro timeline.

What VIBI: AI Sound Eraser does

VIBI: AI Sound Eraser is a UXP panel that splits dialogue recordings into individual speakers plus a background track. Audio can come from a local file, the Project panel or the Timeline, and a waveform preview confirms you picked the right source.

After separation, a mixer lets you mute stems or adjust each one in decibels, while a timecoded transcript shows who spoke when. Speakers can be renamed or added, and lines reassigned, before the audio is regenerated. The final WAV mix goes back to the Project or Timeline. Separation costs roughly one credit per minute of audio.

Key features

  • One-click split into speaker stems and background
  • Per-stem on/off and dB level controls
  • Timecoded transcript by speaker
  • Reassign lines, then regenerate the audio
  • Export the mix to Project or Timeline
