What Frameforge 1.0 - One-Click Cycles Render Setup does

FrameForge 1.0 sets up a presentation scene around a selected model in Blender. It auto-frames the asset and adds an editable camera, lighting, background and shadow arrangement.

Studio and creative lighting presets are ready to use, camera angles can be saved and loaded, and depth of field and output settings are included.
