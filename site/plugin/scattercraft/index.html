Blender add-on that scatters objects in volumes, over surfaces or along splines, each as its own named layer.

What Scattercraft does

ScatterCraft distributes objects across a Blender scene in three modes: volume, surface and spline.

Volume mode fills a cube, sphere or custom mesh. Surface mode covers any mesh, aligning to normals and keeping objects upright on slopes. Spline mode runs objects along a curve and updates live as you drag control points. Every scatter becomes a named layer you can lock or clear, and a live scatter can be frozen once happy.

Key features

  • Volume, surface and spline scattering
  • Normal alignment and upright rotation on slopes
  • Live updating along curves
  • Named layers to lock or clear
  • Freeze a live scatter
