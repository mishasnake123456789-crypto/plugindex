What FX Launcher Pro does

FX Launcher Pro works like a command palette for effects. Press Alt+Space, start typing the name of a video effect, transition or audio effect, and press Enter to apply it, which skips scrolling through the Effects panel.

Favorites provide quick access, shortcuts can be customized, and settings are reachable from the tray icon on Windows or menu bar on Mac, with Apple Silicon and Intel support and auto-start. A Spanish search version exists. It works with Premiere Pro 2025 and 2026.

Key features

  • Alt+Space search to apply effects
  • Effects, transitions and audio effects
  • Favorites and custom shortcuts
  • Tray or menu bar settings
  • Mac Apple Silicon and Intel support
