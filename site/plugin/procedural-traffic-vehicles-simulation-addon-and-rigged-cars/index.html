What Procedural Traffic - Vehicles Simulation Addon & Rigged Cars does

Procedural Traffic builds a traffic system in Blender using geometry simulation nodes. The setup is customizable and automated.

It ships with rigged high-poly cars, people as scene assets and procedural materials. City scene builders and motion designers can put moving vehicles into streets without animating each car by hand. Because the system is procedural and automated, traffic density and behavior can be adjusted for different shots, from sparse streets to busy intersections, with little rework.

Key features

  • Traffic system on geometry simulation nodes
  • Rigged high-poly cars
  • People included as assets
  • Procedural materials
