What Boris FX Sapphire Annual Subscription does

Sapphire is Boris FX's collection of stylized effects for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. It focuses on wipe transitions, lens flares, glares and defocus looks, along with a builder for assembling custom effects and transitions.

The 2026.5 update adds new wipes such as WipeWedge, WipeWeave, WipeTiles and WipeStar, plus an Advanced Defocus that imitates how a physical lens softens an image. The builder gains tools, and over a hundred new presets arrive.
