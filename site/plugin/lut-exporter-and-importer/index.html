What LUT Exporter and Importer does

LUT Exporter and Importer works with the video sequencer's color grading. It turns an adjustment layer into a .cube LUT file, and can read a .cube file back into a layer.

Editors can share a grade between Blender and other software, or reuse a look across projects. Colorists who want portable looks benefit from this two-way conversion.

Key features

  • Adjustment layer to .cube
  • Imports .cube LUTs
  • Shares grades across tools
