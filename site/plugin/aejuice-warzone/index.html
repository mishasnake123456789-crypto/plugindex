What AEJuice Warzone does

AEJuice Warzone is a collection of action-movie effects for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. It covers ballistics, blasts, fire and smoke, planetary impacts and pyrotechnic extras, all in 4K with alpha.

Clips can also be used as transparent or black-background videos in many other editors beyond the main hosts. Dropping a muzzle flash, blast or impact onto a shot is much quicker than simulating one. Editors and motion designers working on trailers, game footage, short films or war scenes get ready-made elements.

Key features

  • Ballistics and explosion assets
  • Fire, smoke and planet impact assets
  • Pyrotechnic extras
  • 4K with alpha channels
  • Transparent or black-background video for other editors
