Shader that renders images and video as ten display types like CRT, LCD, OLED and E-Ink in Cycles and EEVEE.

What Monitorshaderfx does

MonitorShaderFX is a single Blender node group that makes any image, video or texture look like it is shown on a physical display. Ten modes are chosen on one dial: CRT, LCD, OLED, Phosphor, Retro, E-Ink, Matrix, LED, Plasma and Flip-Dot.

Pixel Gap, edge Softness and sub-pixel Grid shape the screen character, with adjustments for scanlines, glow and vignette. Works in Cycles and EEVEE for screens in sci-fi and tech scenes.

Key features

  • Ten display modes on one dial
  • Pixel Gap, Softness and sub-pixel grid
  • Scanline, glow and vignette adjustments
  • Cycles and EEVEE support
