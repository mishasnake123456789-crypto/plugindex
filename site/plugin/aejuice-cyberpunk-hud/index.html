What AEJuice Cyberpunk HUD does

AEJuice Cyberpunk HUD is a template pack inspired by neon, futuristic interfaces. It targets After Effects, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and was built entirely in After Effects with compositions, layers and effects left open for inspection.

The pack includes 15 animated Instagram stories, 20 backgrounds in horizontal and vertical versions, 30 text animations and 50 assets such as glitches, elements and distortion maps. Text, fonts, colours, images and videos can be swapped, and the Pack Manager offers fast preview, drag and drop import, favourites and automatic resizing for vertical, square and horizontal frames up to 4K. Social video makers can produce on-trend graphics quickly.

Key features

  • 15 animated Instagram stories
  • 20 backgrounds in horizontal and vertical

  • Fully editable After Effects compositions
  • Auto-resize up to 4K through Pack Manager
