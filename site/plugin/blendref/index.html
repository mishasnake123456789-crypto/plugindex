Blender add-on for viewing image and video references inside the viewport, similar to PureRef.

What Blendref does

BlendRef keeps references in Blender itself rather than in a separate app. The image part behaves much like PureRef or BeeRef, and video references are supported too, which is useful while sculpting, animating or modeling.

Import a folder or single images and arrange them in columns, flip, rotate and scale them, and add text to sort groups. Videos get an A-to-B loop and slow motion. References live in collections you can append into other blend files, and image sequences, gif and webm files are accepted with media info shown.

Key features

  • Image and video references in the viewport
  • Loop sections of a video, with slow motion
  • Flip, rotate, scale and arrange in columns
  • References stored in appendable collections
  • Image sequences, gif and webm support
