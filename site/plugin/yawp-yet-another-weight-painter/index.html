What Yawp - Yet Another Weight Painter does

YAWP is a weight painting helper for Blender riggers. A single sidebar panel shows vertex group data and lets you work with weights without switching tools.

One click reveals the immediate parent-child relationships of a vertex group. Auto-selection picks a group from a vertex selection by highest average weight, and an influence overlay temporarily highlights vertices affected by groups that co-affect the active one. A Mean Weight slider preserves relative differences while moving overall weight.

Key features

  • Vertex group relationship lookup
  • Auto-selection by highest average weight
  • Influence overlay for co-affecting groups
  • Mean Weight slider
  • Everything in one sidebar panel
