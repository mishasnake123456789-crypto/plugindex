What Sculpt To Curve does

Sculpt To Curve is a Blender add-on by cardonadigital for sculptors and modelers. It lays pre-built, retopologized meshes along splines you can edit, so you can shape horns, tentacles, scales and chains in a few clicks.

The library holds 30 horns, 15 scales, 10 chains and 5 tentacles, each retopologized and UV unwrapped. You shape results by moving control points, and an options panel governs repetition, scaling and spline-following. Named presets persist between sessions, and an Autofix button fills the spline with repeating pieces and narrows the ends.

Key features

  • 30 horns, 15 scales, 10 chains, 5 tentacles
  • Retopologized and UV unwrapped meshes
  • Shaped by moving curve control points
  • Repeat, scale and follow options
  • Saveable presets and Autofix button
