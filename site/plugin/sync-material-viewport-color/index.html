What Sync Material Viewport Color does

Sync Material Viewport Color, by Qiu-Feng, sets the diffuse color, metallic and roughness used for viewport display so the solid view matches your material. It is listed under Object and Material.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
