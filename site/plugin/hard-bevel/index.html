What Hard Bevel does

Hard Bevel bevels selected faces and edges in Blender without overlapping geometry. Where the stock Bevel leaves overlaps on sharp faces, this tool avoids them.

It also handles ngon shapes, including game-style forms such as flames and crystals. Hard-surface and stylized modelers get clean bevels on awkward shapes. Avoiding overlaps means the result stays clean for subdivision, baking and game use, where overlapping faces cause shading errors and extra cleanup.

Key features

  • No overlapping geometry at sharp faces
  • Handles ngon shapes
  • Faces and edges bevel
  • Suits stylized forms
