What Pro Aligner - Align Any Object With A Click does

Pro Aligner, from CG GALAXY, makes object alignment quick in Blender, including for objects whose rotation was already applied and would normally be hard to correct. Alignment works along a single axis or several, via a simple menu.

Related actions are collected there too: invert rotation around chosen axes, apply origin to move the origin to an object's center, apply rotation to zero it on every axis, and move to center to place the object at the middle of 3D space.

Key features

  • Align along one or several axes
  • Works after rotation has been applied
  • Invert rotation around chosen axes
  • Apply Origin and Apply Rotation
  • Move to Center
