Blender add-on that connects Grease Pencil layers to armature bones automatically by matching names.

What Pencilrig does

BlenderGem's PencilRig ties 2D drawings to a skeleton. Give a Grease Pencil layer and a bone the same name, and the add-on binds them from a dedicated panel in one click, which can also be undone just as easily.

The panel picks the Grease Pencil and Armature objects and manages layers, with batch selection and search for locating layers. Naming rules tolerate certain differences. 2D artists rigging hand-drawn characters in Blender skip tedious manual parenting.

Key features

  • Auto-link layers to bones by name
  • Dedicated management panel
  • Batch selection and search
  • One-click link and unlink
  • Flexible naming convention support
