Aligns objects and creates equal gaps along X, Y or Z from a single N-panel in Blender.

What Space does

Space is a Blender add-on from IKA that brings familiar align and distribute controls into the N-panel. If you have ever lined up a row of props by eye, this gives you a precise alternative.

You can set an exact gap between objects along any axis, or spread items evenly between the first and last in a selection. Alignment lines objects up to the left, right, top or bottom of the selection. The tool can work from either an object's origin or its bounding box edges, which helps prevent models from overlapping. Everything is available from one panel, making layout work quick and repeatable.

Key features

  • Equal spacing along X, Y or Z
  • Align to left, right, top or bottom of a selection
  • Origin or bounding box based measurement
  • Fixed Gap and In-Between spreading modes
  • Single panel in the N sidebar
