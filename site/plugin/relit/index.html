Generates procedural lighting rigs around a selected Blender object, with multiple independent systems.

What Relit does

Relit lights a chosen object by building a rig for you. Configure the parameters, press Generate, and lights are placed around the model immediately.

One scene can hold several independent lighting systems, each with its own settings, and you can switch, enable, disable and compare them without losing work. Light distribution is randomized and procedural, and twelve color schemes are included, such as Random, Mono and Complementary. Product artists exploring many lighting looks can test quickly.

Key features

  • Generate lights around an object
  • Multiple independent lighting systems
  • Randomized procedural distribution
  • 12 color schemes
