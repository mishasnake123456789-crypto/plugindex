What Beaver - Automatic Retopology And Retexturing does

Beaver turns heavy sculpts or scans into lighter models in Blender. It offers several retopology algorithms for different cases, and bakes the surface detail of the original into new textures.

Baked maps include color, normal, roughness and ambient occlusion, output at any resolution you choose. The add-on can also produce multiple levels of detail for game environments, and documentation covers the feature set. Game artists and 3D scan users who need optimized meshes with matching textures will save time compared with manual retopology and baking.

Key features

  • Choice of retopology algorithms
  • Bakes color, normal, roughness and AO
  • Textures at any resolution
  • Multiple LOD generation for games
