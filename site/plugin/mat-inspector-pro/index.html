What Mat Inspector Pro does

Mat Inspector Pro, from Mangoframes, is a Blender extension for examining materials and shaders. It works with both Cycles and Eevee and supports Blender 4.5 through 5.1.

It installs through the extension system and comes with documentation under the GPL. Optional detail texture bundles are sold alongside it, a 50-texture starter pack and a full set of 224 detail textures.

Key features

  • Inspects materials and shaders
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
  • Blender 4.5 to 5.1 extension
  • Optional detail texture bundles
