What Quickflip does

Quickflip, from .nook, is a panel of transform shortcuts for After Effects. Buttons flip layers horizontally or vertically, turn them clockwise or counter-clockwise, scale them up or down and set opacity low or high.

How keyframes are handled depends on the click. A plain click adds a keyframe only if the property already has keys, Shift-click forces a new keyframe when none exist, and Opt or Alt-click adjusts every existing keyframe without adding another. Animators who repeatedly nudge transforms get quick, predictable behaviour.

Key features

  • Flip horizontally or vertically
  • Rotate in both directions
  • Scale and opacity buttons
  • Click modifiers for keyframe behaviour
  • Edit all existing keyframes with Opt/Alt-click
