What Find My Addon - Instant N-Panel Add-On Finder & Manager does

Find My Addon tackles the clutter of a crowded N-panel. A pop-up opens on a shortcut, and typing a name filters the list instantly so you can jump straight to the add-on you need.

You can mark favorites, sort add-ons into custom categories, hide or reveal individual entries, and switch single add-ons or whole categories on and off. One click takes you to an add-on's exact panel tab. The pop-up layout is configurable, with adjustable columns, width and hotkey, and setups can be exported and imported.

Key features

  • Live type-ahead search by name
  • Favorites and custom categories
  • Enable or disable add-ons and categories
  • Jump to the add-on's N-panel tab
  • Configurable pop-up with custom hotkey
