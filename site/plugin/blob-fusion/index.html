What Blob Fusion does

Blob Fusion is a Blender plugin by joseconseco for quickly blocking out characters and creatures. The author likens it to a cross between ZSpheres and metaballs: simple shapes combine into a smooth, organic base mesh.

Modeling uses two primitives, spheres and round capsules, and adding a blob generates a rig-like hierarchy of objects to manipulate. A library of presets covers humanoids and animals, and blending strength controls how smoothly forms merge. Core actions such as extrude, subdivide, connect and duplicate have dedicated hotkeys for fast iteration.

Key features

  • Sphere and Round Capsule blob primitives
  • Auto-generated rig-like object hierarchy
  • Humanoid and animal presets
  • Adjustable blending for organic forms
  • Extrude, subdivide, connect and duplicate actions
