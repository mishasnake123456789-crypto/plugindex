What BOOM Library Mutate Organic does

BOOM Library Mutate Organic is built around transformation: sounds of lifeforms and organic matter that morph and mutate. It targets After Effects, Premiere Pro and other video production tools and arrives as WAV files.

The Designed collection contains 350 sounds in 112 files with several variations each, ready to use. The Construction Kit adds 4,220 source sounds in 832 files, recorded from real objects as well as synthesis, organic and mineral, wet and dry. A Bundle holds both, 4,570 sounds at 14.6 GB. Format is 24-bit, 96 kHz, with PDF and XLS content information.

Key features

  • Designed set of 350 sounds with variations
  • Construction Kit of 4,220 sources
  • Bundle of 4,570 sounds, 14.6GB
  • Real-object and synthesized source material
  • 24-bit, 96kHz WAV
