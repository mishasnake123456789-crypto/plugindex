What Raytracing Relight does

Raytracing Relight from Trioscope adds, changes or replaces lighting on video and stills in After Effects without re-rendering or reshooting. Its physically-based shading follows a metallic/roughness workflow and is driven by depth and normal maps.

Up to three lights per instance can be directional, omnidirectional or spotlight, with position, intensity, radius and tint, plus ambient light. Thickness estimation allows light to pass through and around objects, giving shadows that match structure; soft and hard shadows, translucency, ambient occlusion and optional denoising are included. Five depth map types are accepted and outputs include final, white albedo and shadows only. Handy for rim light, time-of-day shifts and plate matching.

Key features

  • Metallic/roughness PBR shading
  • Up to three lights: directional, omni, spot, plus ambient
  • Thickness estimation for physically correct shadows
  • Soft and hard shadows, occlusion and denoising
  • Five depth map types and multiple output modes
