What Barbour Typeface does

Barbour is an Animography typeface that mixes curving strokes with geometric shapes to create a stencil serif with an elegant, upscale feel. The contrast between the two elements gives it character.

The animation is deliberately minimal, adding subtle detail rather than flashy movement. It is compatible with After Effects 2020 through 2024, and a demo video is available. Good for refined titles, fashion and editorial video. Its measured movement keeps attention on the letterforms themselves, so titles feel polished instead of busy.

Key features

  • Stencil serif with elegant look
  • Curved lines contrasted with geometric shapes
  • Minimal animation with subtle details
  • Supports After Effects 2020 to 2024
  • Demo video available
