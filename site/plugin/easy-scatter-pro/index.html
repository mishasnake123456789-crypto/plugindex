What Easy Scatter Pro does

Easy Scatter Pro mixes procedural painting with manual placement for building environments in Blender 4.1 and newer. Forests, grass patches, fences, pillars and streetlights are all within its scope.

Five modes are offered: Pin, Throw for physics drops, Circle, Falloff and Billboard. Mirroring works across axes, camera culling hides out-of-view objects to protect viewport speed, and scatter layers are managed per session. Eraser and Replacer modes edit placed objects, a Lattice Deform cage reshapes them, and a Custom Shape restricts scattering to a curve or mesh.

Key features

  • Pin, Throw, Circle, Falloff and Billboard modes
  • Non-destructive axis mirroring
  • Camera culling for viewport speed
  • Eraser, Replacer and custom shape limits
