What Greenscreen Within Eevee Pro does

Greenscreen Within Eevee Pro handles chroma keying through material nodes, so greenscreen, bluescreen, redscreen or any other key color video can sit inside a 3D scene without first rendering a PNG sequence.

It functions in both Eevee and Cycles. The keyed footage responds to scene lighting, can cast and receive shadows and appears in reflections, which helps it blend with CG elements. It is aimed at cheap, fast compositing where a full compositing round trip is unnecessary.

Key features

  • Chroma key inside material nodes
  • Any screen color supported
  • No PNG sequence pre-render
  • Reacts to lighting, shadows and reflections
  • Eevee and Cycles
