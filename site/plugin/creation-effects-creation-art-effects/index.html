What Creation Effects: Creation Art Effects does

Creation Art Effects is a template from Creation Effects that makes footage look hand-made. It offers 53 art effect comps spanning roughly 27 mediums, among them charcoal, clay and pen.

HD and 4K versions are included, with controls for detail, texture size, realism, shading and outlines. A Timewarp option slows textures for smoother animation, and an optional stop motion effect adds a crafted feel. It handles transparent backgrounds and includes animated mattes, borders and frames, over 200 photo textures, a Create Your Own folder, and support for any language version of After Effects.

Key features

  • 53 art effect comps across about 27 mediums
  • HD and 4K versions
  • Detail, texture, realism, shading and outline controls
  • Timewarp and optional stop motion effect
  • 200+ photo textures and animated frames
