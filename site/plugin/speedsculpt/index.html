What Speedsculpt does

Speedsculpt, from Pitiwazou, speeds up sculpting workflows in Blender, helping you start and organize complicated models with base mesh creation and Dyntopo and Remesh helpers.

Characters can be blocked in quickly from primitives, curves and the skin modifier, with parenting and mirroring handled automatically and placement at the pointer or cursor. Booleans, curve cutting and masking are integrated, and a Remesh modifier produces hard surfaces for sculpting or printing. A decimate feature reduces mesh complexity while preserving shape. Sculptors working on concepts benefit.

Key features

  • Rapid character base creation
  • Dyntopo and Remesh management
  • Integrated booleans, curve cutting and masking
  • Decimate that preserves shape
