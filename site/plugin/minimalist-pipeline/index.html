What Minimalist Pipeline does

Minimalist Pipeline, by Samy_Rodriguez, offers a pared-back way of organising files for solo artists and small teams, without the weight of a studio pipeline. It is tagged under Pipeline, Render and System.

Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, it requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
