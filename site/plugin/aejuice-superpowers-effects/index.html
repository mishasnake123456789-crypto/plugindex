What AEJuice Superpowers Effects does

AEJuice Superpowers Effects delivers energy bursts and hero-style visuals in two forms. After Effects gets a plugin, covering CC 2015 through CC 2024 or newer, and Premiere Pro CC 2021 onward is supported as well.

Pre-rendered elements come as videos on transparent or black backgrounds, so DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, Final Cut, Nuke, OBS and other editors can use them. Older After Effects releases (CS6, CC 12, CC 2014) can open a project version supplied as is. Windows 10 and Mac OS X 10.14 or newer are supported, M1 and M2 included.
