After Effects tool for editing, applying and saving Layer Styles with a built-in preset browser.

What Dresser does

Dresser by Berend van Eerde streamlines Layer Styles in After Effects. It exposes the whole set, including Blending Options, Drop Shadow, Inner Shadow, glows, Bevel and Emboss, and Color, Gradient and Pattern Overlay, in a panel that cuts down on clicking.

Groups can be toggled, shown or hidden across several layers, applied to all targeted layers with a property link, or removed in bulk. A Preset Browser saves styles with customizable thumbnails, including moving previews and folder thumbnails. WordArt presets are available to trial users. It suits artists who rely on layer styles often.

Key features

  • Full Layer Styles set in one panel
  • Toggle, apply and remove styles across multiple layers
  • Property link for targeted layers
  • Preset Browser with custom thumbnails
  • WordArt presets for trial users
