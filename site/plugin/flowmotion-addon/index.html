What Flowmotion Addon does

Flowmotion adjusts the timing of existing animation without re-keying it. Selected keyframes can be fitted into a chosen frame range, resizing the animation while retaining relative timing and values.

One click stretches or compresses the time between keyframes, while scaling speeds up or slows down motion in the graph editor. Markers can be used to offset keyframes, and positions can be randomized for natural variation. Animators polishing pacing or creating loose, organic motion will find it quick.

Key features

  • Fit keyframes to a frame range
  • Stretch or compress timing in one click
  • Scale animation faster or slower
  • Offset with markers; randomize positions
