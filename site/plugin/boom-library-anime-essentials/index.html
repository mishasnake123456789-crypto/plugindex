What BOOM Library Anime Essentials does

BOOM Library Anime Essentials is a collection of sound effects made for animated and toon production. It works with After Effects, Premiere Pro and any other software that reads WAV files.

The 3,386 sounds sit in 566 files totaling 7.9 GB and are sorted into 15 categories, such as impacts, explosions, punches, whooshes, lasers, zaps, shimmers, stingers and wobbles. Material was created from Eurorack sources mixed with organic recordings, then processed with pitch shifts, filters and delays; construction-kit effects are included for building your own hits. Files are 24-bit, 96 kHz, named under the Universal Category System, with PDF and XLS lists.

Key features

  • 3,386 sounds in 566 files, 7.9 GB
  • 15 categories including impacts, whooshes and zaps
  • 24-bit 96 kHz WAV for Windows and Mac
  • Construction-kit effects with delays and frequency shifts
  • Universal Category System names with PDF and XLS listings
