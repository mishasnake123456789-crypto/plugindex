What YY_Ramp+ does

YY_Ramp+ is an After Effects gradient effect from Andrew Yang that extends the standard ramp with additional controls. Being a native plugin, it runs faster than script-based approaches and remains compatible with CS6.

Beyond linear and radial gradients, it adds angle, reflected and diamond shapes, a midpoint slider to move the blend centre, an opacity control and 20 further blend modes. A very fast Ramp Scatter parameter works on CS5 and later, and the effect keeps backward compatibility with projects made using versions 2.0 and 2.1.

Key features

  • Native plugin, CS6 compatible
  • Opacity control and 20 extra blend modes
  • Angle, reflected and diamond shapes
  • Ramp midpoint slider
  • Fast Ramp Scatter; works with 2.0/2.1 projects
