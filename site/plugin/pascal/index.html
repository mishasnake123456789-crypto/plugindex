What Pascal does

Pascal, from WawaSensei, is an import add-on that brings Pascal scenes into Blender. The scene's levels, zones, doors and metadata come across along with the geometry. It is listed under Import-Export.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Imports Pascal scenes into Blender
  • Brings in levels, zones and doors
  • Includes scene metadata
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later
  • Requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer
