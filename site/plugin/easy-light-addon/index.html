What Easy Light Addon does

Easy Light Addon builds lighting around your selected object so you can explore looks quickly. One click places area, point, spot or sun lights pointing at the object, and it works with Eevee and Cycles.

Live Update shows the result as you move a slider, and smart randomize varies strength, size, spread, angle and other properties within ranges you set. It's useful for product shots and quick studies of different moods.

Key features

  • One-click Area, Point, Spot or Sun setup
  • Live update while adjusting sliders
  • Smart randomize within your ranges
  • Works in Eevee and Cycles
