What Mesh Expression Retargeter does

Mesh Expression Retargeter, by vidyut, carries expressions and deformations from one mesh to another, even when their topology differs. It is tagged under Animation and Rigging.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and needs Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
