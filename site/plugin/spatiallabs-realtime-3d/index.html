What SpatialLabs Realtime 3D does

SpatialLabs Realtime 3D from Acer is an extension for Premiere Pro and After Effects aimed at stereoscopic work. It lets creators watch side-by-side 3D content directly on an Acer SpatialLabs display while editing, and apply AI-powered 2D-to-3D conversion to ordinary footage.

Version 1.0.0 integrates fully with SpatialLabs real-time 3D preview. It requires an Acer SpatialLabs Pro device and the latest SpatialLabs Experience Center Pro, and supports After Effects 16.0 and later plus Premiere Pro 13.0 and later. It is relevant to anyone producing 3D content for that hardware.

Key features

  • Live side-by-side stereoscopic preview
  • AI-powered 2D-to-3D conversion
  • Real-time 3D workflow inside Adobe apps
  • Requires Acer SpatialLabs Pro hardware
  • After Effects 16.0+ and Premiere Pro 13.0+
