What EZ Marker Align does

EZ Marker Align, from Ebberts + Zucker, solves a simple but fiddly timing problem in After Effects: matching markers on one layer to those on a second layer. Instead of sliding clips by eye, you let the script shift things into place.

It is free and works with After Effects from CC 2015.3 through 2025. Version 1.00, the initial release, came out on June 8, 2021. Editors syncing footage to beats, cues or another layer's markers can use it as a small time saver.
