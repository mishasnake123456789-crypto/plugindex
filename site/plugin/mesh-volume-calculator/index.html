What Mesh Volume Calculator does

This Blender add-on measures the volume, surface area or length of the mesh objects held in a collection. Instead of working out quantities by hand, you get the figures in one pass.

Results are written to a CSV file that opens in Excel, LibreOffice Calc or any similar spreadsheet program. That makes it handy for material cost estimates and other jobs where you need quick quantity takeoffs from a scene. The add-on is GPL-licensed and runs on Blender 2.8x through the 3.x line and on 4.0, 4.1, 4.2 and 4.5.

Key features

  • Volume, area or length per mesh object
  • Works on all meshes in a chosen collection
  • CSV export for spreadsheet software
  • Supports Blender 2.8x to 4.5
