Puts the selected Blender objects and their children alone in a clean Local View, via a button or Ctrl+/.

What C-Slash does

C-Slash keeps the viewport focused on what you are working on. Select an object, press the button or Ctrl+/, and it appears alone in a clean Local View with everything else out of the way.

It understands hierarchy, so parented and nested children come along automatically, and rigs in Pose Mode stay in Pose Mode after isolation. No manual hiding is involved. Modelers and animators working on a single part of a busy scene will move more smoothly.

Key features

  • One-click isolation of selection and children
  • Ctrl+/ hotkey
  • Hierarchy awareness
  • Rigs stay in Pose Mode
