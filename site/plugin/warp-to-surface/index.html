Warp To Surface

Developer: Christian Aufderheide

FreeBlender

Blender add-on that bends meshes onto curved target surfaces without stretching.

What Warp To Surface does

Warp To Surface lets hard-surface artists wrap decals, panels, cables, trims and engravings onto curved forms in Blender. Objects follow the curvature instead of being stretched over it.

Snap Mode places objects on any surface along a chosen axis and locks them to the contours below. Offsets can be set along the surface normal or as a fixed gap to avoid intersections. It also appears in CMA's Tools bundle with free lifetime updates, and documentation is included.

Key features

  • Surface-aware warping of meshes
  • Snap Mode along a chosen axis
  • Normal offsets or fixed gap
  • Suited to decals, panels, cables and trims
