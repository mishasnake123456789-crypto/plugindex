What Grso - Generate Random Scattered Objects does

GRSO distributes objects with natural randomness in one click. You can use a curated list or your own custom object as the source.

Location and scale spread are adjustable uniformly or per XYZ axis, and bevel and extrusion options finish the scattered results. It applies to different scene types, from landscapes to interiors. Environment artists populating rocks, debris or props quickly will like how little setup is involved. Results can be regenerated quickly until the arrangement looks right.

Key features

  • Scatters from a curated list or custom object
  • Location and scale spread per axis
  • Bevel and extrusion options
  • Suitable for landscapes and interiors
