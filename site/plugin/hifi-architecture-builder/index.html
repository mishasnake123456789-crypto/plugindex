What HiFi Architecture Builder does

HiFi Architecture Builder produces architectural structures in Blender with a single click. Walls, floors, ceilings, pillars, domes, stairs, ramps and window and door frames and balconies are among the pieces it can generate.

The modular generator works with all unit systems and shows a live preview before anything is committed, with quick export when you are ready. Level designers, architectural visualizers and environment artists can use it to block out buildings rapidly and then refine the result.

Key features

  • One-click walls, floors, stairs and domes
  • Window frames, door frames and balconies
  • Works with all unit systems
  • Live preview before generation
  • Quick export
