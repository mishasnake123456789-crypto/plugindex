What Warner Chappell Production Music does

This extension puts the Warner Chappell Production Music library inside Premiere Pro. The catalog spans over 120 curated collections in many genres, and you can search, filter and audition tracks without opening a browser.

Playlists can be created and managed, and whole playlists download straight into the project bin with a progress indicator. Download formats are MP3, AIF or WAV. It suits editors who need licensed music quickly and want to stay inside the edit while choosing.

Key features

  • Search and filter 120+ catalogs
  • Audition before downloading
  • Create and manage playlists
  • MP3, AIF or WAV download
  • Download playlists to the media bin
