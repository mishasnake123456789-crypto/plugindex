What Procedural Dissolving Effect (Pde) Addon - Blender 5.0+ does

The PDE add-on gives objects a dissolving look using a prepared material. One click appends the PDE material from its asset file and assigns it to the selected objects.

Three sub-textures, PDE_Texture_A, B and C, shape the effect. Base Color, Roughness, Normal, Metallic, AO and Emission maps import quickly with file-name detection, and mapping and shader settings sit in the 3D View sidebar. Suits disintegration reveals and magical vanishing effects.

Key features

  • One-click append and assign
  • Three sub-textures
  • Quick import of PBR maps with name detection
  • Mapping and shader controls in the sidebar
