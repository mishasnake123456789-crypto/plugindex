What Pohlke does

Pohlke is a Blender add-on by mononym, named after Karl Wilhelm Pohlke, who formulated the theorem behind axonometric projection. It creates standard axonometric and oblique projection cameras in one click, from the N tool menu or the Shift+A Add menu.

After adding a camera you can tune projection presets and parameters. Axonometric projections are set by pairs of projected axis angles, and oblique projections automatically adjust the X pixel ratio before rendering. Alpha and beta angles define custom projections, and scale information shows the axis reduction factors for x, y and z.

Key features

  • One-click axonometric and oblique cameras
  • Alpha and beta angles for custom projections
  • Axis angle pairs define axonometric views
  • Automatic X pixel ratio for oblique rendering
  • Axis reduction factor display
