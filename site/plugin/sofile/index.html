Cloud storage panel for After Effects and Premiere Pro that moves files and uploads encoded sequences.

What Sofile does

Sofile links After Effects and Premiere Pro with your cloud storage, so you can see its files and folders inside the application. Downloading and uploading happen directly from the project, avoiding trips through a browser.

It can also encode a sequence with a preset you choose and upload the result. Several Adobe applications are supported, with After Effects 22.0 and Premiere Pro 22.0 listed as the minimum versions. It suits teams that keep project media, exports and review copies in the cloud.

Key features

  • Connects to cloud storage
  • Browse files and folders in-app
  • Download and upload from the project
  • Encode and upload sequences by preset
  • After Effects and Premiere Pro 22.0+
