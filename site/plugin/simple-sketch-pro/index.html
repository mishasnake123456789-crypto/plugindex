What Simple Sketch Pro does

Simple Sketch Pro creates line art looks in Blender for both stills and animation. It comes from the makers of KIT OPS and can work with your existing scene.

Six presets are offered: Black and White, Ambient Occlusion Clay, Blueprint, Sketch, Transparent and Comic, each available from one button. Illustrators, technical artists and motion designers can turn 3D scenes into drawing-style imagery with little setup. Presets make it easy to switch between a clean technical look and a loose, hand-drawn style without rebuilding the render setup.

Key features

  • Six designed presets
  • One-button preset access
  • Works with existing scenes
  • Line art for stills and animation
