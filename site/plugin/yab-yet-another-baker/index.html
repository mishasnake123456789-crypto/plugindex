What Yab - Yet Another Baker does

YAB, Yet Another Baker, is a compact baking add-on for Blender. It bakes texture passes from Principled BSDF materials of the selected meshes through a single panel.

Several meshes can be baked together, and packed maps are supported out of the box, including AORM, which stores AO, roughness and metalness in separate channels, and RM. The pass list covers AO, shadow, diffuse, normal, emission, metalness, roughness, glossiness and more. It suits game artists wanting a simple texturing handoff.

Key features

  • Single panel for all bake passes
  • Bakes every selected mesh at once
  • Packed AORM and RM maps
  • AO, normal, roughness, metalness and more
