What Ultitools does

UltiTools from the Nyetei Team bundles various tools for modeling and workflow improvement in Blender. It is distributed as an extension-compatible add-on under the GPL license, with documentation.

It supports Blender 4.2 through 5.1 and works with Blender Internal, Cycles and Eevee render engines.

Key features

  • Assorted modeling and workflow tools
  • Blender 4.2 to 5.1
  • Extension compatible
  • GPL license
