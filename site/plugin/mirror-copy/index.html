Creates a mirrored copy of Blender mesh objects across a movable X, Y or Z plane, with optional merging.

What Mirror Copy does

Mirror Copy duplicates a mesh object as a mirror image using a plane. Choose the X, Y or Z axis and the mirrored result appears within seconds.

The mirror plane is placed at the object origin; slide it along the axis to reveal the copy, and rotate it if needed. Several objects can be mirrored together, and you may keep the originals. Auto-merge joins the original and the mirrored half into one mesh, while a Merge Distance setting welds vertices close to the plane. It is a convenient shortcut for symmetrical modeling.

Key features

  • Mirror along X, Y or Z with a plane
  • Plane placed at the object origin
  • Mirror several objects at once
  • Option to keep originals
  • Auto-merge with merge distance
