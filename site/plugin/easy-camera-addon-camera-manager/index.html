What Easy Camera Addon - Camera Manager does

Easy Camera Addon puts the camera controls you reach for most into a single N-Panel section in Blender. It cuts down on trips through properties tabs when you are framing a scene.

Add Camera creates a new camera right where you are looking, Camera View jumps into it automatically, and Camera to View lets you fly the camera around while in camera view, as you would navigate the viewport. You can switch quickly between cameras, which helps when setting up multiple angles.

Key features

  • Add Camera at the current viewport position
  • Automatic switch to camera view
  • Camera to View for viewport-style movement
  • Quick switching between cameras
