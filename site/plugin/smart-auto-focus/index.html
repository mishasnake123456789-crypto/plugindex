Blender add-on that makes the camera focus on the surface it points at using ray casting, with bakeable keyframes.

What Smart Auto Focus does

Waveus's Smart Auto Focus drives the active camera's Depth of Field automatically. Press one button, move the camera, and focus follows the surface in view.

Because it casts rays against actual surfaces, it ignores object origins. Smooth Time sets reaction speed, and Switch Delay prevents popping and jitter as the view crosses edges. Focus can update every frame and be baked into editable distance keyframes. Cinematic shots and product renders gain hands-off focus pulls.

Key features

  • One-click autofocus
  • Selective ray casting on surfaces
  • Smooth Time control
  • Switch Delay against focus popping
  • Bake focus distance keyframes
