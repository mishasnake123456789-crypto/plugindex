What AEJuice Old Film Constructor does

AEJuice Old Film Constructor lets editors assemble an aged-celluloid look from parts. Its 124 animations cover dust and scratches, film burns, grain and film assets, and frames come in 16x9, 4x3 and vertical 9x16.

In After Effects and Premiere Pro, placeholders can be replaced by drag and drop, and film looks and transitions are available there as well. Smart Controls, Batch Export and Universal Expressions help with customising, and the Pack Manager adds previews, import and favourites. DaVinci Resolve users can use the elements too, making it a versatile route to vintage footage.

Key features

  • 124 animations: dust, scratches, burns, grain
  • Film frames in 16x9, 4x3 and 9x16
  • Film looks and transitions in After Effects and Premiere Pro
  • Smart Controls plus Batch Export and Universal Expressions
  • Drag-and-drop placeholder replacement
