What SRTM Terrain Importer does

SRTM Terrain Importer is a Blender add-on by NicolasPriniotakis that converts SRTM elevation HGT files into 3D terrain. It supports the 1 arc-second, 30 meter data and calculates the terrain's dimensions from latitude, while heights stay in real meters.

Materials with an elevation color gradient are applied, in natural-terrain or grayscale schemes. A vertical scale from 0.1x to 5.0x exaggerates height, and subdivision control from 10 to 200 manages mesh density. It suits map visualizations, flyovers and landscape backdrops built from real geography.

Key features

  • Imports SRTM 1 arc-second HGT files
  • Real-world size from latitude and meter heights
  • Elevation color gradients or grayscale
  • Vertical scale from 0.1x to 5.0x
  • Subdivision control from 10 to 200
