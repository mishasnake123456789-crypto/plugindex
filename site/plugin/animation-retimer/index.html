Blender add-on for retiming keyframes across objects with ripple, replace and combine modes.

What Animation Retimer does

Animation Retimer adjusts timing for keyframes on many objects at once, useful for slowing an action down or making room for a new shot without losing animation.

Its three methods, Ripple, Replace and Combine, shift, overwrite or overlay keys. A timeline overlay marks source frames in blue and the target range in orange. NLA strips can be shifted and sliced, actions can be filtered by domain, child objects can be included, and From Range and To Range support scale and offset. The redo popover shows hints, warnings and affected actions.

Key features

  • Ripple, Replace and Combine retiming
  • Timeline overlay of source and target ranges
  • NLA support and domain filtering
  • Object hierarchy and scale or offset controls
