What Together Ai: All-In-One Ai Toolbox does

Together AI integrates several AI services into Blender for 3D work. Its workflows include generating a model from an image, producing PBR materials and estimating depth.

Separate utilities cover text-to-image, depth estimation and background removal, plus trace recognition and image restoration at high resolution. A companion Mornday UI component is also mentioned. Concept artists and small studios can prototype assets and textures without moving between separate web services. Everything is accessed from within Blender, which keeps the generate, review and refine loop in one window.

Key features

  • Image-to-3D model generation
  • PBR material generation
  • Text-to-image and background removal
  • Depth estimation and high-resolution restoration
