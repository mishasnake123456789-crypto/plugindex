What AEJuice Text To Layers does

AEJuice Text To Layers takes a text layer in After Effects and breaks it into individual layers, one per character, word or line. The result is clean: it adds no expressions and no extra characters.

It handles multiline text, any font and any language, including special characters. Having each piece on its own layer makes typography animation straightforward, whether you are building kinetic type or staging a text scene with separate treatment for every word.

Key features

  • Splits by character, word or line
  • No expressions added
  • No extra characters
  • Supports multiline text
  • Any font and any language
