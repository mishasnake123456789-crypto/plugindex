Generates properly named, parented collision meshes in Blender for Unreal, Unity or custom pipelines.

What Collision Wizard does

Collision Wizard creates game-ready collision geometry from the Blender viewport. Select a mesh, pick a shape and it produces a correctly named collider parented to your object, ready for export.

Naming follows engine conventions: Unreal's UCX_, UBX_, USP_ and UCP_ prefixes with sequential suffixes, Unity's COL_ prefix with shape suffixes, or custom prefixes for in-house pipelines. Collider shapes include box, sphere and capsule, among others. Game artists spend less time on manual naming and bounding shapes before exporting props and environment pieces.

Key features

  • Unreal, Unity and custom naming
  • Box, sphere and capsule colliders
  • Colliders parented to the source mesh
  • Sequential suffixes for multiple colliders
