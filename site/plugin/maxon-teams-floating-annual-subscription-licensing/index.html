What Maxon Teams Floating Annual Subscription Licensing does

This subscription offers floating licences for studios whose artists share seats instead of each holding a named one. It covers Maxon's applications as used alongside Cinema 4D, After Effects and Premiere Pro, and it is administered centrally through the Teams account dashboard, formerly the ORG account.

Products can be chosen individually, either Cinema 4D, Redshift or ZBrush, or as the full Red Giant range including Trapcode Suite, Magic Bullet Suite, VFX Suite, Universe and Maxon Studio. The Cinema 4D option adds Cineversity, Team Render, two command-line nodes and upgrades for the term. Teams Support and Training come with it, and renewal is possible 60 days early.

Key features

  • Floating licences deployed to a group
  • Central management in the Teams dashboard
  • Pick Cinema 4D, Redshift, ZBrush or Red Giant
  • 5 Team Render and 2 Command Line nodes with Cinema 4D
  • Teams Support and Training included
