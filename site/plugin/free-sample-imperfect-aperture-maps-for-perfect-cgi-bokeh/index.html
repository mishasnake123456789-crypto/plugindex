What Free Sample: Imperfect Aperture Maps For Perfect Cgi Bokeh does

This free sample from Add Your Light provides an aperture map that connects to a virtual camera and gives out-of-focus highlights a realistic, imperfect shape. A Blender camera rig for custom bokeh is included.

The map can have dirt or chromatic aberration added to the aperture shape. A CC0 HDRI from Poly Haven is bundled in the rig file for testing. The maps are also described as usable with V-Ray, Corona, Octane, Arnold, Lenscare and DOF Pro, which makes the sample a way to try the idea before moving to the full collection.
