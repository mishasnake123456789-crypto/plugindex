Blender add-on that pours realistic liquid into cups, glasses and bowls using geometry nodes plus a library of liquid shaders.

What Liquifeel does

Liquifeel is a Blender add-on for putting believable liquids inside drinking vessels and bowls. A geometry nodes based fill generator builds the liquid volume, so you do not have to sculpt or simulate it by hand.

A bundled shader library covers common drinks and foods such as juice, wine, coffee, milk, honey and beer, plus foam, which helps product visualizers and food or beverage scenes reach a photorealistic look quickly. Documentation explains setup, and the developer runs a Discord community where users can ask questions and suggest features.

Key features

  • Geometry nodes liquid fill generator
  • Shader library with juice, wine, coffee, milk, honey and beer
  • Foam material included
  • Fills cups, glasses and bowls
  • Documentation and Discord community support
