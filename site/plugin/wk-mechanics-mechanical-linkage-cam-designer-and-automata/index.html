What Wk_Mechanics - Mechanical Linkage, Cam Designer & Automata does

Wk_Mechanics brings mechanism design into Blender. Modular widgets let you assemble linkages, levers, sliders, walkers, drawing machines, layered assemblies and your own automata.

Motion can be tested in 3D, CAM profiles are generated automatically, and fabrication-ready blueprints are exported for laser cutting or CNC. Makers, kinetic sculptors and educators can go from idea to cut files in one program. Having design, simulation and fabrication output in one place shortens the path from concept to a physical build.

Key features

  • Widget-based mechanism design
  • Linkages, sliders, walkers and automata
  • 3D motion testing
  • Automatic CAM profile generation
  • Blueprint export for laser or CNC
