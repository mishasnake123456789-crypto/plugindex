Blender add-on providing an editable 3D motion trail for character bone animation, with controls as ordinary objects.

What Motiontrail3D does

MotionTrail3D shows a bone animation's path in the Blender 3D viewport as an editable motion trail. Its 3D controls are normal Blender objects, so you select and transform them as usual.

The trail updates live while you edit local channels, and keys can be adjusted directly. Animators polishing arcs on hands, feet and props get an easy visual way to tune motion. Since the controls are real objects, they can be selected, moved and keyed with familiar Blender tools, making path-based refinement feel natural.

Key features

  • Editable trail in the 3D viewport
  • Controls are normal Blender objects
  • Real-time update while editing
  • Designed for bone animation
