Blender add-on that reshapes a rough set of selected vertices into a neat, evenly spaced circle.

What Circularize does

AR Utilities' Circularize repairs ragged rings of vertices in Blender. Radius Type decides how the selection is read, as the Average, Minimum or Maximum radius.

Even Angles orders the vertices clockwise and spaces them equally. Scale adjusts the radius, Push shifts the circle along the normal of the selection's plane, and Spin turns it around. Modelers cleaning holes, cylinders and round cutouts can fix them in one step. Because the result is evenly spaced, subdivision and bevel operations behave more predictably afterwards.

Key features

  • Radius Type: Average, Minimum or Maximum
  • Even Angles distribution
  • Scale control
  • Push along plane normal
  • Spin rotation
