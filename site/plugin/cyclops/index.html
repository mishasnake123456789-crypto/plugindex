What Cyclops does

Cyclops, by Kyle Martinez, creates breakdown renders of After Effects work. Nulls, motion paths and layer bounding boxes become visible, which helps when sharing your process on social media or in case studies.

You can render only the composition or the whole application window, using standard, wireframe, greenscreen or bluescreen modes, with the key-colour modes simulating alpha. Render settings are customisable, it runs on macOS and Windows, and updates, installation and licensing go through the aescripts + aeplugins manager. Animators showcasing their craft can produce engaging reveals.

Key features

  • Comp-only or full application window renders
  • Standard, wireframe, greenscreen and bluescreen types
  • Simulated alpha in key-colour renders
  • Customisable render settings
  • macOS and Windows
