What Palette Master: Color Theory Made Easy does

Palette Master generates harmonious colour palettes inside Blender from a single base colour. You choose a harmony scheme, such as complementary, analogous, triadic, or rectangular or square tetradic, and the add-on produces matching colours with tonal variations.

Variations include a Tailwind-based default as well as shades, tones and tints, and changing the base colour updates the whole palette. Colours can be dragged and dropped into the Shader Editor, brush settings or any other colour field. It works in the 3D Viewport for vertex and texture painting, the Shader Editor and the Image Editor, on Blender 4.5 through 5.1.

Key features

  • Palette from one base colour
  • Five harmony schemes including complementary and triadic
  • Shades, tones, tints and a Tailwind-based default
  • Drag colours into shaders, brushes and other fields
  • Works in the Viewport, Shader Editor and Image Editor
