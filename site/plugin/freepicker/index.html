What FreePicker does

FreePicker by dirun adds a floating picker panel to Blender that you can drag and resize, for selecting bones and objects without hunting through the viewport or outliner. It is tagged under Rigging and Animation.

It is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.0.0 or newer.
