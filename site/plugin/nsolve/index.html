What Nsolve does

Nsolve brings together three TeamC toolkits for Blender mesh topology: nSolve, nSuite and nFlow. They aim to cut down friction and repetitive hotkey use while modeling.

nSolve has eight interactive, customizable modes with a reusable, exportable persona system. nSuite is an Edit Mode micro toolkit with eleven operations, and nFlow is a retopology toolkit with ten function blocks and many presets. More than twelve hours of narrated video training is included. Topology-focused modelers and retopologists get a deep toolset.

Key features

  • nSolve with 8 interactive modes
  • nSuite with 11 Edit Mode operations
  • nFlow retopology blocks and presets
  • 12+ hours of video education
