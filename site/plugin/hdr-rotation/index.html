Blender add-on for rotating the world HDR by Shift + right-drag in the 3D View.

What Hdr Rotation does

Hdr Rotation, by ACGGIT_LJ, turns HDRI rotation into a mouse gesture: hold Shift and drag with the right button in the 3D View to spin the environment. It is listed under Material and Render.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Rotate the HDR with Shift + right-drag
  • Works in the 3D View
