Blender 5.0+ add-on that fuses two meshes with a live, non-destructive blend preserving topology and UVs.

What Merge Master does

Merge Master joins one mesh to the surface of another in Blender 5.0 and up, leaving the result live and reversible. Pick two meshes, activate, and the blend updates in real time as sliders move, even on dense meshes, with no precomputation.

Counts of vertices, edges and faces, along with UV maps, remain as you made them. A Global Merge slider scales strength, radius and normals together, material blending creates a texture transition, and an optional boolean removes hidden interior geometry for 3D printing. Many objects can be chained onto one target, with custom falloff curves and Simple or Advanced subdivision.

Key features

  • Live non-destructive mesh adhesion
  • Preserves topology and UVs
  • Global Merge slider
  • Optional material blending and boolean
  • Multi-mesh chaining with custom falloff
