What DOSCH 3D: Cars 2010 - USA does

Twenty textured American vehicles make up Dosch 3D: Cars 2010 - USA. They are provided for Cinema 4D in multiple file formats, so they can be used in other applications too.

Each car scales to your needs, with freely changeable materials, textures and colors, and you can add your own textures or labels. Parts can be switched on or off, faded and animated. Typical uses are architectural visualizations, simulations and renderings where US street traffic or parking lots must look convincing.
