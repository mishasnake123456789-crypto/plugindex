What Bendio does

Bendio, from Animatable, is a plugin that curves a layer and gives you a bend you can hook other layers onto. It is described as quick and simple to use.

Supported hosts are After Effects CC 2018, CC 2019 and 2020 through 2026. Version 1.0.1 fixed a cropping issue, and a demo video is available. Motion designers animating bending paper, banners, cards or flexible objects can add curvature without complex warping setups.

Key features

  • Bends layers in After Effects
  • Attach other layers to the bend
  • Supports CC 2018 through 2026
  • 1.0.1 fixes a cropping issue
  • Demo video available
