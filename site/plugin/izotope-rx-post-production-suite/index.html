What iZotope RX Post Production Suite does

The RX Post Production Suite gathers restoration, mixing and metering tools for post-production audio in DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. Version 9 bundles Insight 2, Nectar 4 Advanced, Equinox and Neutron 5 together with RX 12 Advanced and further tools.

RX 12 Advanced offers over 50 restoration tools. Neutron 5 supplies eleven plugins, along with Tonal Balance Control 2, Relay and Visual Mixer. Nectar 4 Advanced covers dialogue and vocal mixing, while Insight 2 meters loudness, intelligibility and spectral balance, including Netflix compliance and 7.1.2 Dolby Atmos. Equinox provides reverb, and Trash offers over 60 distortion types with a Convolve module of 600+ impulse responses. Formats include AU, AAX, AudioSuite, VST3 and AU ARA, 64-bit only.

Key features

  • RX 12 Advanced with more than 50 restoration tools
  • Neutron 5 with 11 plugins and Tonal Balance Control 2
  • Nectar 4 Advanced for dialogue mixing
  • Insight 2 metering with Netflix loudness and 7.1.2 Atmos
  • Equinox reverb and Trash distortion with 600+ impulses
