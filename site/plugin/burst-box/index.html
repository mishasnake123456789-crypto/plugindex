What Burst Box does

Burst Box, made by Breton Brander, is a plugin for After Effects that supplies ready-made burst graphics. Instead of drawing and animating radiating shapes from scratch, you pick one of the 30 included bursts and apply it with a click.

Each burst can be customized extensively to match a project's style. The plugin works with After Effects from CC 2019 through 2026. Version 1.2 moved to the latest licensing system for better stability, and its expressions were updated so they work in multiple languages. It suits motion designers who need quick accents for titles, logos and social videos.

Key features

  • One-click burst animations
  • 30 bursts included
  • Extensive customization options
  • Works with After Effects CC 2019 to 2026
  • Expressions work across languages
