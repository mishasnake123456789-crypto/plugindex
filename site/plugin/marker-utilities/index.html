What Marker Utilities does

Marker Utilities, by BigHead-Himself, expands how timeline markers work in Blender. It adds marker colors, sets, overlays and playback-related tools. It falls under Workflow.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 5.2.0 or newer.
