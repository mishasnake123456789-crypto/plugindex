What 🎬Reel Director: Turn Blender Scenes Into Instagram, Tiktok & Youtube Shorts Content does

Reel Director by Bevel Graphics is aimed at artists who want to post their Blender work as short-form social video. A single panel handles the format, from vertical to square or landscape output at resolutions up to 8K, along with frame rate and duration.

On top of that it supplies 32 camera moves, object build and reveal animations, clay-style breakdowns, and 23 stylized viewport looks. Four render presets trade speed against noise, and results can go out as image sequences, MP4, EXR or hero frames. Setup can be removed afterwards, restoring your original transforms and materials.

  • Vertical, square or landscape output up to 8K
  • 32 camera moves plus dolly zoom, rack focus and handheld FX
  • Object animations like build, reveal, assemble and explode
  • Clay breakdowns and 23 stylized viewport looks
  • Four render presets for fast previews or clean finals
