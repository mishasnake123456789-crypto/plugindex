What Boom Library Fields & Spaces: Outdoor Impulse Responses - Surround does

Fields & Spaces: Outdoor Impulse Responses (Surround) provides 123 reverb captures for use with After Effects and Premiere Pro through third-party convolution reverb plugins. Files come in stereo and quad surround and total roughly 7 GB.

The responses are modeled, the result of two years of research, giving a near-infinite signal-to-noise ratio. Natural settings include canyons, forests, hills and mountains over different ground materials; urban ones include bridges, squares, alleys, ports, courtyards, dams, castles and underpasses. Most are available at multiple distances. Stereo and ambisonic editions also exist, in 24-bit WAV at 48 to 96 kHz.

Key features

  • 123 impulse responses in stereo and quad surround
  • Modeled with near-infinite signal-to-noise ratio
  • Canyons, forests, bridges, alleys, castles and more
  • Multiple distances per location
  • 24-bit WAV, 48-96kHz, about 7GB
