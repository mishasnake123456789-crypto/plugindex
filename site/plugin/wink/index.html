AI portrait retouching for Premiere Pro video covering skin, facial features, makeup and body reshaping.

What Wink does

Wink applies photo-style retouching to moving portraits inside Premiere Pro. Auto Retouch uses AI to improve skin tone and texture, and Facial Features does 3D retouching to refine contours naturally.

A Makeup module offers various effects, All-in-one Retouch lessens blemishes, and Body Reshape adjusts proportions while keeping backgrounds smooth and consistent between frames. Templates allow batch processing, and many video and image formats are supported. It is aimed at editors who handle talking-head, beauty and lifestyle footage.

Key features

  • Auto Retouch for skin tone and texture
  • 3D facial feature retouching
  • Makeup module
  • Body Reshape with stable backgrounds
  • Templates for batch processing
