What Bone & Weight Splitter does

Bone & Weight Splitter is a Blender add-on by AdmiralTMP for rigging and weight painting. It cuts a selected bone into several segments and recalculates the vertex weights so deformation carries across the new pieces.

The smoothness of the weight boundary is adjustable, with gradient visualizations to preview the transitions. Pick a mesh and vertex group and it finds the armature that goes with them. Bone and vertex group numbering is flexible, with symmetry indicator detection, which keeps naming tidy when you add more joints to a limb.

Key features

  • Splits bones into as many segments as you choose
  • Recalculates and blends vertex weights automatically
  • Adjustable boundary smoothness with gradient preview
  • Finds the armature from a mesh and vertex group
  • Flexible numbering with symmetry detection
