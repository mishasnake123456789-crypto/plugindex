What C4D Smart Sync Pro Convert Octane/Redshift does

C4D Smart Sync Pro helps artists who model in Blender but render in Cinema 4D. It packages the scene as an FBX bundle and relinks textures so lookdev can continue on the other side.

A C4D Sync panel in the 3D View sidebar sends the whole scene or selected objects, optionally with lights and cameras, to a chosen folder. A companion plugin in Cinema 4D imports the latest export and rebuilds materials as Octane or Redshift networks where available, covering channels such as base color, roughness, metallic, emission, normal and displacement. It is designed for repeated round trips.

Key features

  • One-click export from the Blender sidebar
  • C4D import plugin with texture relinking
  • Octane or Redshift material rebuild
  • Options for lights, cameras, parenting and instances
