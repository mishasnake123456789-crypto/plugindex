What Pixelan FilmTouch 2 Pro does

Pixelan FilmTouch 2 Pro applies cinematic color treatments to footage in Premiere Pro. It ships with over 500 presets grouped into sixteen categories, such as Filmic Subtle, Moderate and Strong, Cool, Warm, Desaturate, Duotone, Monochrome, Low Light and Abstract.

A large visual Effects Browser lets you preview looks quickly, and GPU acceleration keeps playback responsive. If a preset is not quite right, film-oriented controls refine subtle color shifts, or let you create a look from scratch. Colorists and editors who want fast stylized grades without building them manually are its audience.

Key features

  • 500+ presets in sixteen categories
  • Large visual Effects Browser
  • GPU hardware acceleration
  • Film-oriented controls for fine-tuning
  • Build looks from scratch
