What Imperfectit! - Instant Realism & Dynamic Weather does

ImperfectIt! is a Blender addon from Blender Procedural that makes clean surfaces look real. It adds material imperfections and micro detail, plus dynamic weather such as snow, rain, dust and grime.

It works with both Cycles and Eevee on Blender 4.1 through 4.5, includes documentation and is released under the GPL license. Environment and product artists can use it to break up the too-perfect look of CG materials and give scenes a lived-in, weathered quality.

Key features

  • Material imperfections and micro detail
  • Snow, rain, dust and dirt effects
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
  • Supports Blender 4.1 to 4.5
  • Documentation included
