Blender add-on that measures the distance between two vertices and scales the whole object to a target size.

What Measure and Scale does

Imported models often arrive at the wrong scale in Blender, and Measure and Scale corrects that. Pick two vertices, see the live distance, then type the dimension you want and the whole object is scaled uniformly.

Left-click accepts a measurement and right-click discards it. Scale is applied automatically, and distances can be entered in meters, centimeters, millimeters, feet or inches. It is reached from the 3D View. Architects, product designers and 3D printing users can bring scanned or downloaded models to the correct dimensions fast.

Key features

  • Measure between any two vertices
  • Live distance display
  • Uniform scale to a typed dimension
  • Metric and imperial units
