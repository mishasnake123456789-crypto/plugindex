What AEJuice Trapcode Elements does

AEJuice Trapcode Elements is a pack of 58 customizable effects for After Effects built to run with Trapcode Particular. They are realistic and fluid-based, sorted into five categories: Air, Earth, Fire, Lightning and Water.

Using it requires Trapcode Particular v6 (part of Red Giant) and After Effects CC2022 or later. A prerendered version is offered for editors who work outside After Effects, and a demo video plus Making Of tutorials are provided. Artists after natural-force visuals can start from ready setups rather than tuning particles from scratch.

Key features

  • 58 customizable elements in five categories
  • Air, Earth, Fire, Lightning and Water
  • Built for Trapcode Particular v6
  • Prerendered version for other editors
  • Demo video and Making Of tutorials
