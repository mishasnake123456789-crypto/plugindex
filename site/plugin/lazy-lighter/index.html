Reusable lighting rigs for Blender: 50 cinematography-based setups that stay relative to the camera.

What Lazy Lighter does

Lazy Lighter supplies whole lighting systems instead of single lights. There are fifty base rigs inspired by cinematography and photography, each in Area, Spot and Point versions, giving 150 setups.

Rigs are camera-relative, so key, fill and rim lights keep their positions as the camera moves. Global controls adjust distance scale, height offset and more. Product and character artists who need convincing lighting without building it from scratch can start from a proven pattern and tweak.

Key features

  • 50 rigs from cinematography and photography
  • Area, Spot and Point variants
  • Camera-relative key, fill and rim
  • Global distance and height controls
