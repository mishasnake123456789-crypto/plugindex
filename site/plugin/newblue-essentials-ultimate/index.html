What NewBlue Essentials Ultimate does

NewBlue Essentials Ultimate packages fix-it tools for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. It combines Stabilizer with Essentials Volumes 1, 2 and 3: 28 tools and over 275 presets in all.

Stabilizer analyses footage without needing a still point to track, with steadicam emulation and control over amount, strength and motion. Volume 1 enhances detail, boosts colour and reduces noise; Volume 2 removes camera flash, fixes lens distortion and improves colour; Volume 3 covers blemish and wrinkle removal, white balance and sharpening. Pixelator hides faces and plates, and Aspect Ratio crops to cinema formats. Great for general footage clean-up.

Key features

  • Stabilizer without a stationary tracking point
  • Volumes 1-3 with 27 plugins and 270+ presets
  • Noise, flash, lens distortion and blemish fixes
  • Pixelator for faces, plates and logos
  • Aspect Ratio crops for Cinescope, Panavision, Super8
