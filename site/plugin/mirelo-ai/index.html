What Mirelo AI does

Mirelo AI is a panel that creates sound for your picture. Select a clip or sequence and it studies the footage, then generates sound effects, ambient audio and music that suit what is on screen. You can generate, edit, preview and apply in one click.

It batch-generates across long timelines, extends audio naturally, replaces a part without affecting the rest of the clip, and supports multi-stem output. A custom output folder can be chosen.

Key features

  • Context-aware SFX, ambience and music
  • One-click generate, edit, preview and apply
  • Batch generation across long timelines
  • Extend or partially replace audio
  • Multi-stem generation
