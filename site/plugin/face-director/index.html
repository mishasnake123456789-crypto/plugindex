What Face Director does

Face Director from Baskl.ai is a plugin for reworking faces in footage after the shoot. Editors and compositors use it to rescue a weak performance, patch continuity mistakes and redirect where a subject is looking.

It handles eye retargeting and gives freer control over facial manipulation than a manual cleanup would. The plugin runs on both Mac and Windows and works in After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Nuke. Version 1.10.0 introduced a new backend that speeds up processing.

Key features

  • Improves weak on-screen performances
  • Fixes continuity errors in faces
  • Retargets eye direction
  • Mac and Windows support
  • Faster backend since version 1.10.0
