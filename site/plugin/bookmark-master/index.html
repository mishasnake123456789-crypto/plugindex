Saves Blender viewport positions, rotations and zoom as named bookmarks you can jump back to.

What Bookmark Master does

Bookmark Master lets you store 3D viewport views in Blender and return to them later. Each bookmark records the exact position, rotation and zoom, so you do not have to reframe a complex scene every time you want to check an area.

Bookmarks can be renamed, deleted and navigated from the 3D Viewport sidebar, and you can label them by task, camera angle or layout stage. It is meant to support large scenes and is useful across animation, layout, modeling and scene planning. The add-on is compatible with Blender 4.3 to 4.5 and uses the GPL license.

Key features

  • Saves exact viewport position, rotation and zoom
  • Rename, delete and navigate bookmarks
  • Sidebar integration in the 3D Viewport
  • Label views by task or camera angle
  • Blender 4.3 to 4.5 support
