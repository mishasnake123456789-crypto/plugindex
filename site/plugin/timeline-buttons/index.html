Blender add-on that adds Timeline Play, Pause, Autokey and Frame Range controls to the Sequencer, Dopesheet, Graph, Driver and NLA editors.

What Timeline Buttons does

Timeline Buttons copies the Timeline editor's playback controls into other animation-related editors in Blender, so you do not need a Timeline area open all the time.

Play and Pause buttons appear along with Autokey options and Frame Range controls, in the the Sequencer, Dopesheet, Graph, Driver and NLA editors. Animators and editors working in full-screen graph or dopesheet layouts get playback close at hand. Having playback close by avoids switching layouts just to play, pause or change the frame range.

Key features

  • Play and Pause in other editors
  • Autokey options
  • Frame Range controls
  • Sequencer, Dopesheet, Graph, Driver and NLA
