What Asset Browser: Capture Thumbnail from Viewport does

Asset Browser: Capture Thumbnail from Viewport, by Dan-Gry, lets you set a new preview thumbnail for an asset by grabbing what the viewport shows right now. It is listed under Pipeline.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
