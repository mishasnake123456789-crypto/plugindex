What Luca Visual FX Film Leaders does

Film Leaders from Luca Visual FX produces rough countdown-style leaders and title cards in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro. Type your own text, choose a font, and style the result with textures or a plain colour.

Effects and channel shifts add damage, a background can be selected or left off, and animation controls tune the movement. Fifty-seven presets serve as starting points. It runs through FxFactory and can be used as intros, transitions or overlays for projects wanting a worn, analog film feeling.

Key features

  • Custom text and font
  • Text textures or plain colour
  • Effects and channel shifting
  • Optional backgrounds and animation controls
  • 57 presets via FxFactory
