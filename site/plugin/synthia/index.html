What Synthia does

Egret Studios' Synthia is designed for artists who create forms from equations and generative logic in Blender, favouring mathematical art and advanced procedural modelling.

It works in Blender 4.0 to 5.1 and with both Cycles and Eevee. It is Blender Extension compatible, GPL licensed and documented by its developer. Generative artists and procedural modelers can use it as a springboard for complex shapes. Its output can be rendered with the normal Blender engines, so finished pieces slot into ordinary scenes and animations.

Key features

  • Mathematical art focus
  • Advanced procedural modelling
  • Blender 4.0 to 5.1
  • Cycles and Eevee support
  • Blender Extension compatible
