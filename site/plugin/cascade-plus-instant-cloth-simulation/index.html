What Cascade Plus - Instant Cloth Simulation does

Cascade Plus is a library of ready-made cloth simulations for Blender. More than 100 handcrafted assets, including parachutes, ship sails, cloaks, flags, curtains, banners and capes, can be placed at the 3D cursor in a few clicks and played back in real time.

Because they are pre-baked, there is no physics setup or baking step. The modular design lets you mix, match and layer multiple cloth simulations together. Smart search gives recommendations and color-codes results by wind speed. It saves time on scenes that need believable fabric motion.

Key features

  • 100+ pre-baked cloth assets
  • Placement at the 3D cursor, no baking
  • Layer and combine simulations
  • Smart search with wind-speed color coding
