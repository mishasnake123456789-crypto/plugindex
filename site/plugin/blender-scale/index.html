3ds Max script that brings Blender-style scaling to vertices, edges and polygons on splines, meshes and polys.

What Blender Scale does

Blender Scale lets 3ds Max users scale sub-objects in a way similar to Blender. Editable Spline, Mesh and Poly objects are all supported, at vertex, edge and face or polygon level.

Scaling follows the current coordinate system, and the coordinate center is not used. Min, Mid and Max center buttons choose the center of the scale, and pressing any of them reads the selected vertices. The sub-level is detected automatically, and a Get Selected Vertices button is included. Modelers coming from Blender who miss its scaling behavior can work more comfortably.

Key features

  • Blender-style sub-object scaling
  • Works on Editable Spline, Mesh and Poly
  • Vertex, edge and polygon levels
  • Min, Mid and Max scale center buttons
  • Automatic sub-level detection
