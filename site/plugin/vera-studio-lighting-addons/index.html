What Vera Studio Lighting Addons does

Vera Studio Lighting Addons is a bundle from EdenWeby 3D with four tools: Cinematic Lighting, Vera Product Light, Vera Camera Motion and Vera Lighting Studio Pro. Together they set up lighting and camera moves from presets.

Vera Product Light lights cars, electronics and human models and adds grounds, while Lighting Studio Pro offers presets, mesh lights and a volume option. A one-stop package for quick studio and cinematic looks. Combining these tools simplifies installation and gives consistent presets for product and cinematic scenes.

Key features

  • Four add-ons in one bundle
  • Product lighting with grounds
  • Lighting presets and mesh lights
  • Camera motion and cinematic lighting
