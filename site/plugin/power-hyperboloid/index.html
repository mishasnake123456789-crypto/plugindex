What Power Hyperboloid does

Power Hyperboloid by CROSSPHERE .Koizumi is freeware that creates a three-dimensional hyperboloid inside After Effects, controlled through power settings. It is a companion to the studio's other geometry tools.

Elliptic distortion and unfolding shape the form, while rich shading, self-shadowing and reflections give it a solid look. Depth of field is available, composition lights and cameras are respected, and Multi-Frame Rendering and Apple Silicon are supported. Motion graphics artists needing futuristic cooling-tower shapes or twisted tubes can generate them without 3D software.

Key features

  • 3D hyperboloid with power controls
  • Elliptic distortion and unfolding
  • Shading, self-shadowing and reflections
  • Depth of field
  • Comp lights and camera, MFR and Apple Silicon
