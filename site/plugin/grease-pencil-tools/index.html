What Grease Pencil Tools does

Grease Pencil Tools, by Samuel Bernou, bundles several handy utilities for drawing and animating with Grease Pencil in Blender. They remove small bits of friction that otherwise slow 2D work in the 3D viewport.

Box deform on Ctrl+T places a lattice box facing the view to deform the whole object, selected points or last strokes, with linear or spline modes and subdivision. Canvas rotation shows a HUD with the angle and can save and restore camera rotation. A timeline scrub popup appears at the mouse position, and strokes can be straightened. 2D animators and storyboard artists draw faster.

Key features

  • Box deform with Ctrl+T
  • Canvas rotation with angle HUD
  • Timeline scrub popup in the viewport
  • Stroke straightening and layer navigator
