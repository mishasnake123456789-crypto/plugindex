Adds a Christmas-themed Blender startup greeting, snow overlay, mistletoe frame and looping melody.

What Merry Christmas does

Merry Christmas is a festive add-on that decorates Blender for the holidays. On startup it shows one of eight Christmas images at random.

A real-time snow overlay falls in the 3D Viewport, mistletoes decorate the corners, and a holiday melody loops in the background. A seasonal novelty for your workspace. It does not affect your models, and is simply a seasonal touch for the working environment. Users can switch it on while celebrating, and turn it off again when serious work resumes.

Key features

  • Random greeting from eight images
  • Real-time snow overlay
  • Mistletoe in viewport corners
  • Looping holiday melody
