What Stylized Rock And Ice Cliff Generator Addon does

The Stylized Rock And Ice Cliff Generator creates stylized terrain pieces in Blender procedurally. Rocks, cliffs and ice formations are generated, and an ice smart material is included.

It renders in Cycles and Eevee, supports Blender 3.5 through 4.4, comes with documentation and is released under the GPL license. It is made by Blender Procedural.

Key features

  • Ice formations with a smart material
  • Cycles and Eevee rendering
