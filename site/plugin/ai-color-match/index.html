What AI Color Match does

AI Color Match from Baskl.ai analyses a reference shot and a target shot, then adjusts the target so the two look consistent. It works in After Effects and Premiere Pro and is useful for footage from different cameras or settings, or for borrowing the grade of a favourite scene.

Processing happens locally on the GPU, so frames never leave your computer. Match strength is adjustable. 8-bit work is standard, while the PRO version adds 16 and 32-bit support and exports the result as a .cube LUT for use elsewhere. It runs on Windows and Apple Silicon Macs.

Key features

  • Matches exposure, contrast and white balance
  • Runs locally on your GPU
  • Adjustable match strength
  • PRO: 16/32bpc and .cube LUT export
  • Windows and Apple Silicon
