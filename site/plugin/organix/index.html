What Organix does

Alt Tab developed Organix, a Blender shape generator meant for organic, alien-looking forms. It supports Blender versions 4.0 through 4.5 and is distributed under the GPL license.

Documentation is included and a demo video is available to show what it can do. It is aimed at artists who want unusual, alien-looking shapes as a starting point for sculpts, abstract art or sci-fi design.
