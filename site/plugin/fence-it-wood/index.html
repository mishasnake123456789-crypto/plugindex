What Fence It! - Wood does

Fence It! - Wood creates wooden fences in Blender procedurally using geometry nodes, aimed at gardens, landscapes and architectural scenes. Because the structure is modular and customizable, you can alter it without rebuilding the model.

It works with Blender 4.0 through 5.0, is available both as an extension and as an add-on, includes documentation and is released under the GPL. Artists who need boundaries around properties, yards or rural settings can get a fence of the right length and style quickly instead of modeling posts and rails by hand.

Key features

  • Procedural wooden fences with geometry nodes
  • Customizable fence options
  • Modular structures
