What Paint Cans Font does

Paint Cans Font by Highground is a graffiti-inspired typeface for After Effects and Premiere Pro. It includes over 200 letters, numbers and other characters drawn in a rough paintbrush style influenced by simple tag lettering.

Glyphs wobble back and forth for a loose, random look, with very rough edges featuring tiny streaks, specks and splatters. Basic Latin is supported, and version 2.0.0 refined its paths. It suits street-style titles, posters and edgy video graphics.

Key features

  • 200+ letters, numbers and characters
  • Rough tag-inspired paintbrush style
  • Streaks, specks and splatters on edges
  • Basic Latin support
  • Refined paths in v2.0.0
