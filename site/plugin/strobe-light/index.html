A pack of six animated EEVEE lamps for thunder, police car, strobe, suspense and flashing effects.

What Strobe Light does

Strobe Light supplies animated lamps for Blender's EEVEE engine, each designed for a particular situation. Effects include thunder, police car, suspense, strobe, car giro, car flash and other flashing lights.

A properties panel adjusts frequency, speed and size, and lamps can be Point, Spot or Area types. A demonstration scene for the Thunder Lamp is provided, and documentation is included. Artists working on night scenes, horror, emergency vehicles or club visuals can add pulsing light without keyframing each flash.

Key features

  • Six animated lamps
  • Thunder, police, strobe and suspense effects
  • Frequency, speed and size controls
  • Point, Spot and Area lamp types
  • Thunder Lamp demo scene
