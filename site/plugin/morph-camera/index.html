What Morph Camera does

Morph Camera by liuran focuses on making camera animation quick to produce in Blender. It works with both Cycles and Eevee.

A package of documentation, a demo video and several tutorials shows different setups, including an iPhone, a sculpture, tabletop photography, a bicycle and a map. The add-on is released under the GPL license. Product and showcase animators wanting smooth camera moves can learn from the examples and adapt them to their own scenes.

Key features

  • Quick camera animation setups
  • Cycles and Eevee support
  • Tutorials for several example scenes
  • Demo video and documentation
