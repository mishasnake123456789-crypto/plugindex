What Rune does

Rune is a brush manager for sculptors working in Blender, built by Yey!. It stores and saves sculpting brushes in one place, so you can reach your favorite tools quickly at the start of each session.

Custom brushes and textures bought on BlenderMarket or ArtStation can be added, and it comes with brushes including Stitches, Folds and Scratches. It runs as a separate PySide2 window, stays fast and simple, and includes documentation. Sculptors with growing brush collections who want a cleaner way to organize and reuse them will find it practical.

Key features

  • Stores and saves sculpting brushes
  • Adds custom brushes and textures
  • Includes Stitches, Folds and Scratches brushes
  • Runs in a separate PySide2 window
