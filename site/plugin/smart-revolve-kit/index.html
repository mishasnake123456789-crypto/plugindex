What Smart Revolve Kit does

Smart Revolve Kit from Pixel Egg Studio helps build objects with rotational symmetry, such as vases, bottles and columns. It generates revolved or symmetrical shapes without manual lathe setup.

It supports Blender 2.9 through 4.3, is distributed under the GPL license, and comes with documentation and a demo video. The developer is a Blender Development Fund contributor. Hard-surface and product modelers will find it a quick way to make round forms.

Key features

  • Revolved and symmetrical shape creation
  • Blender 2.9 through 4.3
  • Documentation and demo video
