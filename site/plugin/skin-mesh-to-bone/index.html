What Skin Mesh To Bone does

Skin Mesh To Bone is a Blender add-on by darknation that binds every selected mesh to one bone on the active armature. The command sits in the Object menu of the 3D Viewport.

It removes the repetitive work of parenting or weighting multiple objects to the same bone one at a time, which helps with props and mechanical parts that follow a joint rigidly. The add-on is a 3 KB download, requires no special permissions, supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer, and is released under the GNU GPL v3 or later.

Key features

  • Skins all vertices of selected meshes to one bone
  • Operator in the Object menu
  • Works with the active armature
  • Tiny 3 KB download
  • Requires no special permissions
