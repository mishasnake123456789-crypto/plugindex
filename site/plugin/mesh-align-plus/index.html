Blender add-on that aligns meshes and objects using Alignment Keys picked from points, lines and planes.

What Mesh Align Plus does

Mesh Align Plus positions and orients parts of a mesh, or whole objects, using points, lines and planes captured from your scene. You choose those references, known as Alignment Keys, then decide what they act on.

The toolset spans Align Points, Lines and Planes, Axis Rotate, Directional Slide and Match Edge Scale. Keys can be lines or flat surfaces from any object, and alignments apply to one object, several, or a mesh fragment while modeling. Measurements can be captured from the scene. Mechanical and architectural modelers join parts precisely.

Key features

  • Align Points, Lines and Planes
  • Alignment Keys from any object
  • Axis Rotate and Directional Slide
  • Match Edge Scale
