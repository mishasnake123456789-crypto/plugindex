What Mirror Pro (Fix Apply Scale Problem) does

Mirror Pro addresses the flipped normals that often appear in Blender after mirroring and applying negative scale. It applies scale and automatically corrects the normals, so shading stays correct without a manual flip afterwards.

It can also fix flipped normals on several selected objects at once from object mode. The commands sit in the Object, Apply menu next to Blender's standard options, and are also reachable through Ctrl+A. Documentation is included and the license is royalty free. Modelers who mirror and rescale objects regularly will skip a recurring cleanup step.
