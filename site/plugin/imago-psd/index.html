What Imago Psd does

Imago PSD is a Blender add-on for artists who finish their work in Photoshop. Rather than juggling separate image files, it writes one layered PSD that keeps the render passes apart for 2D compositing.

Passes such as AO, Mist and Depth are captured during export, and the workflow resembles the EXR-io approach. It is compatible with Blender 4.3 and 4.2, includes documentation, and is released under the GPL.

Key features

  • Exports layered PSD files for Photoshop
  • Captures AO, Mist and Depth passes
  • Workflow similar to EXR-io
  • Supports Blender 4.2 and 4.3
