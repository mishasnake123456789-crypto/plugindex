Colormap-based colour styling plugin for After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro.

What ColorVSN does

ColorVSN maps tonal values to colour palettes, taking grayscale information and turning it into vivid imagery. It also doubles as a general grading effect, giving stylized looks that differ from conventional curves and wheels.

Blend modes let you mix the result with the original picture, and further controls help refine a look once it's applied. The plugin runs in After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro, so one style can follow a project across hosts. It suits artists who like thermal-camera, false colour or heavily stylized treatments.

Key features

  • Applies colormap styles to footage
  • Turns grayscale data into colour
  • Works as a grading tool
  • Blend modes for fine-tuning
  • Runs in After Effects, Resolve and Premiere Pro
