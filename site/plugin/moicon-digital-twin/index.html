What MOICON Digital Twin does

MOICON Digital Twin, listed for 3ds Max, applies digital twinning to manufacturing. Built on Autodesk Forge, it gathers data from machines, components, sensors, devices and workers and arranges it into clear, immediately available breakdowns that everyone in an operation can consult.

An integrated factory model can be opened from anywhere on any device, giving continuous insight into process planning and operations to support decisions. It was developed by a Forge Certified Systems Integrator trained by Autodesk experts, and suits industrial staff and facility owners who want a better overview of their plant.

Key features

  • Organizes machine, sensor and worker data
  • Built on Autodesk Forge
  • Factory model accessible on any device
  • Continuous insight into process planning
  • Made by a Forge Certified Systems Integrator
