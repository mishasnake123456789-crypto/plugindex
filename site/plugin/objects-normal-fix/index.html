Object's Normal Fix

3ds Max scripts that fix the normal (extrude direction) of shapes through auto, by-face or four-point modes.

What Object's Normal Fix does

This script set repairs the normal of selected shapes in 3ds Max, which determines their extrude direction. Three modes are offered: automatic, by face and by four points.

By Face takes the normal from a picked face, with left click to pick and right click to exit, while By 4 Points defines the X and Y axes by the order of the points. A Geo Normal Fix script is included and does the same for geometry. Each mode has its own MacroScript, plus a combined macro suited to a toolbar button, and the macros become active when the selection contains shapes.

Key features

  • Fixes normal or extrude direction of shapes
  • Auto, By Face and By 4 Points modes
  • Included Geo Normal Fix for geometry
  • Separate macro per mode and a combined toolbar macro
  • Macros enable when shapes are selected
