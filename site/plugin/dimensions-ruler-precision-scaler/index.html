What Dimensions Ruler + Precision Scaler does

Dimensions Ruler + Precision Scaler pairs two measuring helpers. The ruler draws lines as you model and stays usable during modeling operations.

The Precision Scaler resizes a selection to match an exact distance between two points. It's handy for matching real-world dimensions and correcting the size of imported models. Having both measuring and exact resizing together is useful when matching real-world sizes or fixing the scale of imported assets. It keeps scale checks within the normal modeling flow.

Key features

  • Interactive ruler that stays active
  • Resize to an exact distance
  • Works while modeling
  • Measurement-driven scaling
