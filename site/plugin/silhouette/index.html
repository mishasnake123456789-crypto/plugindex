A Blender add-on that shows object silhouettes in the 3D viewport to check character and prop shapes.

What Silhouette does

Silhouette applies a concept-art habit directly inside Blender: judging a design by its outline alone. Characters, props and environments can be checked in the 3D viewport without exporting or switching programs, which keeps the whole review in a single scene.

Settings sit in a dropdown over the viewport header, controlling silhouette color, background color or transparency, opacity, and whether the shape draws in front of or behind geometry. Fixed views come from presets or from a camera, snapped to the active object, a collection or a selection, and a beta live view follows your navigation. Concept and character artists benefit most.

Key features

  • Silhouette display inside the 3D viewport
  • Color, background, opacity and draw order controls
  • Fixed views from presets or a camera
  • Snap to active object, collection or selection
  • Beta live view that follows navigation
