Fly the Blender viewport and scene camera using an Xbox controller on Windows, with five camera modes.

What Xbox Controller Camera does

Xbox Controller Camera lets you operate the Blender viewport and scene camera with a gamepad on Windows. Five modes are provided: Free, Dolly, Crane, Orbit and Tracking, aimed at producing cinematic movement.

Smoothing is adjustable, the D-Pad changes focal length while in camera view, and the Start button inserts keyframes, or you can use auto-keyframing. Previs artists and animators can improvise camera moves by feel instead of manipulating curves.

Key features

  • Free, Dolly, Crane, Orbit and Tracking modes
  • Adjustable smoothing
  • D-Pad focal length control
  • Keyframe insertion with Start button or auto-key
