Blender add-on that uses any mesh or curve as a live deformer to build distance-based soft selections and weights.

What Pro Soft Selection Deformer does

Pro Soft Selection Deformer turns a mesh or curve into a deformer that defines a soft selection by distance. As the deformer moves, the affected vertices update in real time, so shaping stays flexible and non-destructive.

Falloff types and influence distances can be customised, and the selection can be turned into vertex groups with a visual weight display or painted by hand. Sculpt-style effects such as pinch, relax, push, inflate and procedural noise act on the affected area, and Laplacian and Taubin smoothing are available. Strength controls and live rebinding make it suited to precise, organic mesh shaping.

Key features

  • Any mesh or curve acts as a live deformer
  • Customizable falloff types and influence distances
  • Vertex group generation and weight painting
  • Pinch, relax, push, inflate and noise effects
  • Laplacian and Taubin smoothing
