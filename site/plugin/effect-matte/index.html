Adds a sized, feathered matte straight onto an After Effects layer without a separate track matte layer.

What Effect Matte does

Effect Matte, from Plugin Everything, applies a matte directly to a layer in After Effects. The usual approach requires an extra layer acting as a track matte, and this effect removes that step by sizing the matte automatically when applied.

The matte can be expanded or contracted, rounded, feathered and inverted. It supports multi-frame rendering and runs natively on Apple Silicon, so it fits modern fast workflows. Artists who build many cutouts, reveals and shape-based masks get cleaner compositions with fewer layers to manage.

Key features

  • Matte applied directly to a layer
  • Positive and negative expansion
  • Roundness, feathering and invert options
  • Multi-frame rendering support
  • Native Apple Silicon support
