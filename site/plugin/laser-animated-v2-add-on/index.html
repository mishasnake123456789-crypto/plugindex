What Laser Animated V2 Add_On does

Laser Animated V2 inserts a laser beam animation into a Blender scene with a single click. The effect can be placed anywhere on the timeline, so timing it to a shot is straightforward rather than a matter of manual keyframing.

It works under both EEVEE and Cycles, supports Blender 2.93 through 4.3, is distributed under the GPL, and includes documentation and a demo video. Motion designers and VFX artists creating sci-fi action, sweeping scanners or stylized light beams can get a usable result quickly.
