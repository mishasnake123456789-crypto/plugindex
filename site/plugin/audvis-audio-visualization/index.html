What Audvis - Audio Visualization does

Audvis is a Blender add-on by example.sk for building audio visualizations. Its Real Time Analyzer drives animation from music or any sound coming from a sound card or microphone, and a Sequence Analyzer is aimed at making music videos.

Extras include Party Mode, a fullscreen 3D view without overlays or controls, and Video Capture, which refreshes an image from a webcam so it can be used as a texture. An Example Generator lays out a grid of objects and offers many settings, and a Shape Modifier animates vertex locations. Baking and spectrogram tools round out the set.

Key features

  • Real Time Analyzer for live sound input
  • Sequence Analyzer for music videos
  • Party Mode fullscreen view
  • Webcam Video Capture as a texture
  • Example Generator, shape modifier, baking and spectrogram
