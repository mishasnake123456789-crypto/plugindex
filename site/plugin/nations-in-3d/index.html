What Nations In 3D does

Nations In 3D creates relief maps of countries and territories in Blender 4.0 and later, serving artists, educators, animators and geographers.

More than 150 heightmaps follow real borders, and your own PNG heightmaps can be imported. A color ramp with five stops styles elevations, a displacement slider sets terrain height, and a subdivision surface modifier is added automatically. Controls sit in an N-panel, generated maps go into separate collections, and the ground layer can be shown or hidden.

Key features

  • 150+ curated country heightmaps
  • Custom PNG heightmap import
  • Five-stop color ramp and displacement slider
  • Maps organized into collections
