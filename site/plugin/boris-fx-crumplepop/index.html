What Boris FX CrumplePop does

CrumplePop is made up of AI-based audio tools for DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and other editors. It clears echo, fan and wind noise, mic rustle and plosives while leveling sound automatically.

Voice Enhance, Levelmatic, AudioDenoise and the remover plugins for echo, wind, traffic, clipping, pops and rustle are included. It can split music into vocals, drums, bass and other, isolate voice from music, and break a mixed scene into music, effects, background noise or dialogue. Processing runs locally on the GPU with no cloud upload or credits, and Seek and Go starts processing at the playhead. Formats include a standalone app, ARA, VST, AU and AAX.

Key features

  • Removes echo, fan, wind, rustle and plosives
  • Nine plugins including AudioDenoise and Voice Enhance
  • Stem separation of vocals, drums, bass and other
  • Runs locally on the GPU, no cloud upload
  • Standalone app plus ARA, VST, AU and AAX plugins
