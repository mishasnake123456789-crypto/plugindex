Makes bouncing-ball music animations in Blender by tapping the beat, exported to MP4 with audio.

What Music Ball does

Music Ball turns a song into a bouncing-ball animation driven by your own taps. Press Space along with the beat and the add-on generates tubes, curves and bouncing balls automatically.

No audio analysis tools or scripting are needed. You can play the song from a phone or Blender's sequencer, and the finished animation exports to MP4 with embedded audio. Creators can make rhythm visuals quickly. Highlights cover tap Spacebar to the beat and auto-generated tubes, curves and balls.

Key features

  • Tap Spacebar to the beat
  • Auto-generated tubes, curves and balls
  • No audio analysis or scripting
  • MP4 export with audio
