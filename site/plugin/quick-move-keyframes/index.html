Blender add-on that pushes keyframes left or right from the current frame in the Graph Editor, Dope Sheet or Timeline.

What Quick Move Keyframes does

Making room in an animation usually means box selecting keys. Blender.Fun's Quick Move Keyframes shifts all keys after (or before) the current frame to the right or left via hotkeys or a menu.

It operates in the Graph Editor, Dope Sheet and Timeline, and its reach can be limited to all keys, selected objects, scene objects or chosen curves. Documentation is included, it is GPL licensed and marked Blender Extension compatible. Animators adjusting timing mid-shot save effort.

Key features

  • Shift keyframes from the current frame
  • Graph Editor, Dope Sheet and Timeline
  • Hotkeys or menu
  • Scope to all, selected objects or curves
  • Blender Extension compatible
