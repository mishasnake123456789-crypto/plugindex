What Fire With Smoke does

studioromanrec's Fire With Smoke gives Blender a ready-made flame accompanied by smoke, working equally well in EEVEE and Cycles.

Documentation and a demo video come with it. Blender 3.5 to 4.3 are supported, and the GPL applies. Use it for campfires, torches, explosions or moody atmosphere where a prepared effect is more convenient than setting up a simulation. Being a prepared effect, it also keeps scene files light compared with fluid simulation caches.

Key features

  • Fire with smoke effect
  • Renders in EEVEE and Cycles
  • Blender 3.5 to 4.3
