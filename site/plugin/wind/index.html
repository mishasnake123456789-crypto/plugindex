What Wind does

Wind, by Ebberts + Zucker, is an After Effects plugin that scatters and gathers layers as if caught in a breeze. It handles hundreds of layers at once with nearly no setup, so you click and apply.

There are 17 presets with in and out transitions across 10 directions, secondary drift controls, and many interpolation and ease options. Fade, scale and auto orient to camera are available, and it works with text, vector, video and shape layers plus JPEG, AI and PSD files. Master layer controls adjust everything globally, markers control timing, and turbulence can be turned off for mechanical motion. The AE2017 C4D renderer is supported.

Key features

  • 17 presets with in and out transitions
  • 10 directions and drift controls
  • Works with text, vector, video and shape layers
  • Master controls and marker-based timing
  • Fade, scale and camera auto orient
