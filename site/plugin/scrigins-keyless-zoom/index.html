A procedural camera tool for After Effects that creates zoom moves without keyframes.

What Scrigins Keyless Zoom does

Keyless Zoom builds camera zooms from a few inputs: start, end and duration. The easing and framing math are handled for you, so you avoid setting keyframes and adjusting curves by hand.

Zoom to Fit works out the right end point for 3D layers, even rotated or parented ones, and an Overscan slider fine-tunes the framing live. Four easings are provided, including Smooth, Snappy, Cinematic and BounceBack with an Overshoot control. Previews of the first and last frames let you check the move before applying it.

Key features

  • Zoom moves with no keyframes
  • Zoom to Fit for rotated, scaled or parented 3D layers
  • Overscan slider with live update
  • Four easings including BounceBack with Overshoot
  • Context-aware camera roll
