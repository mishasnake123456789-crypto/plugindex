What Klutz GPT does

Klutz GPT, from Hyper Brew, puts ChatGPT inside After Effects to help with scripts and expressions. Its own description is playfully modest, calling it an unreliable way of getting the job done, so treat its output as a draft to check.

You can pick the model, including GPT 5.4 and 5.4-mini, and adjust the default prompt presets to suit how you work. A help prompt template is provided for producing custom After Effects tutorials. It supports After Effects 2025 and 2026.
