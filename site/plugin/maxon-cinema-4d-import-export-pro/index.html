What Maxon Cinema 4D Import-Export Pro does

Maxon Cinema 4D Import-Export Pro moves scenes between Blender and Cinema 4D without launching the other program. It reads .c4d project files straight into Blender and writes Blender scenes back out as .c4d, all from one panel.

Transfers carry meshes, materials and textures, animations, cameras, lights and object hierarchy, aiming to keep the scene intact in both directions. It helps studios and freelancers who collaborate across the two applications or want to reuse Cinema 4D assets in Blender.

Key features

  • Imports .c4d project files into Blender
  • Exports Blender scenes to .c4d
  • Preserves geometry, materials and hierarchy
  • Carries animations, cameras and lights
  • No need to keep Cinema 4D open
