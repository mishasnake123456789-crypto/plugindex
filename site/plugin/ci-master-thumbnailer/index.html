What CI Master Thumbnailer does

CI Master Thumbnailer is a panel for producing preview images without manual exporting. It can create thumbnails from compositions, layers and video clips and offers control over size, aspect and the moments that get captured.

Sizes may be pixel-based or a percentage, with crops available. Captures can happen at the start, end, markers, or specific times, with spacing by frames, seconds or equal divisions. Naming schemes can be saved as presets, and files go to chosen folders or subfolders matching your project structure. Layers can be rendered to separate files.

Key features

  • Thumbnails from comps, layers and clips
  • Pixel or percentage sizing with center crop
  • Capture at start, end, markers or set times
  • Saveable naming presets
  • Output mirrors project folder structure
