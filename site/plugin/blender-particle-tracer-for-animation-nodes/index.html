What Blender Particle Tracer For Animation Nodes does

This setup for Blender with Animation Nodes captures how one or many particles travel. Each particle is saved as a keyframed object, and splines are generated from the motion.

You choose which object is duplicated; the duplicates are placed on a separate layer for easy selection. The spline look can be jagged or straight, with or without a tip. It is useful for light trails, motion graphics and tracing paths of swarms or sparks.

Key features

  • Records particles as keyframed objects
  • Choose the duplicated object
  • Splines from particle motion
  • Jagged or straight spline styles
