Auto Paint Boundaries

Developer: Clonephaze

FreeBlender
Download Free

Blender add-on that restricts paint strokes to a connected face region chosen by clicking, in texture, vertex or weight paint.

What Auto Paint Boundaries does

Auto Paint Boundaries keeps brush work inside the area you intend. Clicking a face makes it pick the connected region according to your chosen boundary rules, turns on Face Masking and hands the stroke to your active brush.

It supports Weight Paint, Vertex Paint and Texture Paint. Artists painting clothing, armor panels or separate mesh parts no longer have to select faces manually before every stroke.

Key features

  • One click picks a connected region
  • Automatically enables Face Masking
  • Texture, Vertex and Weight Paint support
  • Passes the stroke to the active brush
