What Simple Camera Addon does

Simple Camera Addon makes it easy to experiment with camera and output settings in Blender. Instead of entering values by hand, you choose from templates.

Resolution templates include different aspect ratios, such as those with black bars, and camera templates cover sensor size and lens field of view. Frequently used presets are within easy reach in a simple interface. It suits anyone who changes formats often. Because the templates are tied to common formats, switching between a cinematic crop and a social media size becomes a quick selection instead of manual number entry.

Key features

  • Resolution templates including letterbox ratios
  • Camera templates for sensor and lens
  • Quick access to common settings
  • Simple interface
