What One-Click Edge Wear does

One-Click Edge Wear brings worn, scuffed edges to Blender materials without node building or texture painting. A single click applies the procedural effect, which is a quick way to make hard-surface models look used and less perfect.

It is built for Blender 4.0 and later and for the Cycles render engine. Documentation and a demo video are included. Product visualizers, prop artists and game environment artists who want a believable edge wear pass over many materials without manual setup will find it reduces the work to a moment.

Key features

  • Procedural edge wear on materials
  • Single-click operation
  • No node setups or texture painting
  • Built for Cycles in Blender 4.0+
  • Documentation and demo video included
