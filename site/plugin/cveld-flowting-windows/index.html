Blender add-on that opens editors in floating windows at the mouse cursor.

What Cveld Flowting Windows does

cvELD Flowting Windows pops extra Blender editors open at your cursor, including the Outliner, UV Editor, Graph Editor, Shader Editor and Geometry Nodes. The windows stay locked to your monitor.

You can assign custom shortcuts and window sizes per editor, close every floating window with a single shortcut, and add, remove or reorder editors in preferences without restarting. Windows use flicker-free native technology, translations come from a CSV with Japanese included, and it is MIT licensed for Blender 4.4 and later.

Key features

  • Editors open at the mouse cursor
  • Per-editor shortcuts and sizes
  • Close-all shortcut
  • Editable editor list, translation CSV
