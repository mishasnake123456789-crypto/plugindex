What Luca Visual FX Light Kit does

Luca Visual FX Light Kit is a plugin collection for After Effects and Premiere Pro centered on light and color. Light Flicker imitates TV glow, old projectors and strobes, while Light Pulse makes lights throb at any speed, color and style.

Multicolor Gradient and Multicolor Vignette overlay adjustable color. Regional Light places localized light, with or without flicker, such as light through gaps or venetian blinds. Stylize and Vivid Touch change light, color and shape and pair glow with sharpness. Burn Wipe and Flash supply transitions, with adjustable rotation and random movement on Burn Wipe.

Key features

  • Light Flicker and Light Pulse
  • Multicolor Gradient and Vignette
  • Regional Light, including venetian blind effects
  • Stylize and Vivid Touch
  • Burn Wipe and Flash transitions
