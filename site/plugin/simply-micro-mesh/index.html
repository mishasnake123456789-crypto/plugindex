What Simply Micro Mesh does

Simply Micro Mesh blankets surfaces in Blender with tiny repeated objects by projecting them onto a target. More than 120 ready presets are included, which can be selected, customized or replaced with your own.

Scaling and phase adjust quickly, density can be visualized, and micro mesh assets are usable in the Asset Library. It is released under the MIT license. Texture-like detail such as scales, fabric weave or mechanical patterns can be added as real geometry.

Key features

  • Projects micro objects onto targets
  • 120+ presets
  • Quick scale and phase adjustment
  • Asset Library compatible
