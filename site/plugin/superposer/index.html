Blender add-on that brings images, GIFs and video links into the viewport as reference overlays, boards or planes.

What Superposer does

Superposer helps you keep reference material next to your model in Blender. It accepts local files, clipboard images, animated GIFs and supported web video links.

Use Ctrl + Shift + V to pull references from links, or drag files in. References can stay as overlays, form boards, be projected into the view or become planes managed by the add-on. Simple controls adjust scale, brightness and rotation. It saves switching to a separate reference app.

Key features

  • Imports images, GIFs and supported video URLs
  • Ctrl + Shift + V to grab links
  • Drag and drop from local files
  • Overlays, boards, projection or planes
  • Controls for scale, brightness and rotation
