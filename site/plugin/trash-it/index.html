What Trash It does

Trash It is a procedural Blender add-on from Blender Bits for littering scenes convincingly. Subjects include general junk, bin bags and trash cans, laid out for you instead of placed by hand.

It renders in Cycles and Eevee, supports Blender 4.0 through 5.0, and is a GPL-licensed Blender extension with documentation. Environment artists can quickly build gritty alleys and streets. Because the clutter is generated procedurally, it is simple to vary the amount and arrangement, which helps urban, alley and abandoned-location scenes feel lived in.

Key features

  • Procedural trash scattering
  • Bags, bins, cans and junk
  • Cycles and Eevee compatible
  • Blender 4.0 to 5.0 extension
