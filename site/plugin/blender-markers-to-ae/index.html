What Blender Markers To Ae does

Blender Markers To Ae sends your timeline markers across to Adobe After Effects with a click. They arrive as composition markers, carrying their comments with them.

Names of cameras bound to markers are included too, which helps with cut points. Existing markers can optionally be deleted first, and the After Effects path is configurable for Windows and macOS. Documentation is included. Compositors who sync edits between the two applications avoid retyping.

Key features

  • Exports markers and comments
  • Includes names of cameras bound to markers
  • Becomes composition markers in After Effects
  • Configurable After Effects path
