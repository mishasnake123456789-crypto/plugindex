Blender add-on to apply and manage render overscan from Output Properties, with camera selection and pixel values.

What Render Overscan does

Render Overscan adds extra pixels around the frame in Blender renders. Its settings live in the Properties editor, in the Output Properties tab.

Choose a camera, enter the overscan in pixels and enable it. A Select Scene Camera button helps single-camera scenes, while multiple cameras need manual selection. Compositors who need room for lens distortion or camera shake gain extra margin. Rendering extra pixels around the visible frame gives later stages room for effects like lens distortion, camera shake stabilization or reframing without losing edge detail.

Key features

  • Overscan value in pixels
  • Controls under Output Properties
  • Select Scene Camera button
  • Toggle on and off
