Extends double-click selection in Blender to hierarchies, connected geometry and whole collections.

What Double Clicker Plus does

Double Clicker Plus adds useful behaviors to a simple gesture. Double-clicking an object can select its entire hierarchy, or the whole collection it belongs to, instead of just the one item.

In edit mode, double-clicking picks connected vertices, faces or polygons. It supports Blender 3.6 through 4.5, includes documentation and is released under the GPL license. Anyone who spends the day selecting groups of objects in busy scenes will like having fewer steps.

Key features

  • Double-click selects a hierarchy
  • Selects connected vertices, faces or polygons
  • Selects a whole collection
  • Blender 3.6 through 4.5, GPL
