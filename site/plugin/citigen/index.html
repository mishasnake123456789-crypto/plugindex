Blender add-on that generates large cities from lines and planes, with environment, camera and Unreal export tools.

What Citigen does

Citigen builds sprawling cities in Blender from simple input. Lines drawn across a plane turn into overpasses or bridges while the plane divides into city blocks. It is geared toward establishing shots of imaginary cities in 4K or larger for film.

The package includes an environment tool for sea, desert, clouds and animated sun, an animation camera system with one-click setups, and an import and export tool for custom assets and UE5. Cities save as presets that cut disk space dramatically. Citigen-Ultimate adds crowd and traffic generation and a compositor.

Key features

  • Large-scale city generation from lines and planes
  • Environment tool for sea, desert, clouds and sun
  • One-click animation cameras
  • Custom asset import and UE5 export
  • Space-saving city presets
