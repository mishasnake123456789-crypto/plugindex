What Metaballify - Organic Modeler does

Metaballify is a Blender plugin for sketching organic forms. Select a group of meshes and press convert to turn them into parts that react like metaballs, then adjust the result.

Parts can be displaced, scaled and rotated in real time in Object Mode, and tweakable objects such as M-Flex, M-Egg and M-Cone help with fast shaping. Presets and global settings control the overall form. It is simple and intuitive for concepting creatures and characters.

Key features

  • Converts meshes into metaball-like M-Objects
  • Real-time displace, scale and rotate
  • M-Flex, M-Egg and M-Cone shapes
  • Global presets and settings
