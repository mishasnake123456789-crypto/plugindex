What 3D Primitives Generator does

3D Primitives Generator by Ahmed Studio creates basic 3D objects inside After Effects without leaving native layers. A single click makes cubes, cuboids, cylinders, pyramids and prisms from shape layers, solids and pre-comps.

Every face is its own composition, so any color, pattern, texture or gradient can be applied. Sliders control every primitive, with taper options for cubes, pyramids and prisms, and materials and colors are easy to change. The redesigned interface resembles a 3D application panel. Simple primitives can be combined into larger models.

Key features

  • One-click cubes, cuboids, cylinders, pyramids and prisms
  • Built from native shape layers, solids and pre-comps
  • Each face is a separate composition
  • Taper control for cubes, pyramids and prisms
  • Slider controls and easy material changes
