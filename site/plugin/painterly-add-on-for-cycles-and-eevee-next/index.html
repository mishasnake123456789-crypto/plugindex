What Painterly Add-On For Cycles And Eevee Next does

Painterly Add-On, from Doughy Animation Studio, gives Blender scenes a hand-painted look for Cycles and EEVEE Next. It provides more than a hundred unique Bezier curve strokes with texture maps, adjustable with sliders until the stroke suits your needs.

Painterly Texture Mode version 2.5 generates painted diffuse and normal maps from any UV-mapped object, with normal map processing ten times faster than version 1. A progress display shows how many strokes are being processed across the color and normal passes. Stylized renderers wanting a painted-canvas look will find it a distinctive option.

Key features

  • 100+ unique painterly Bezier strokes
  • Painterly Texture Mode generates diffuse and normal maps
  • Faster normal map processing in v2.5
  • Progress display for stroke processing
