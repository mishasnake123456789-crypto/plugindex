What idustrial revolution Volumetrix does

Volumetrix from Idustrial Revolution, delivered through FxFactory, creates light-based effects and transitions in After Effects and Premiere Pro. It is built on FXPlug technology and the graphics card, and ships with over 60 presets.

Each preset can serve as a filter or as a transition, using a wipe that moves at any angle. Rays, glows, glints and distortions can be adjusted through wipe width and intensity controls, and it works with or without alpha. Version 2.2 added five text highlighting presets, making it handy for dressing up titles.
