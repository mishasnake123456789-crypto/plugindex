What Text Layers to ChatGPT does

Text Layers to ChatGPT links After Effects with ChatGPT so text can be processed without copying it between apps. You write a custom prompt, and the generated result is applied to the text layers in your composition.

Typical uses are translating titles into other languages, checking spelling, or creating and revising copy automatically. It is built for motion designers who produce many text-heavy videos or localized versions. Compatibility covers After Effects 2024 and 2025.

Key features

  • Custom ChatGPT prompts from within After Effects
  • Prompt-driven text translation
  • Spell-checking of text layers
  • Automated text content creation
