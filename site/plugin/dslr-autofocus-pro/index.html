What Dslr Autofocus Pro does

DSLR AutoFocus Pro brings camera-style focusing behavior to the Blender viewport. It follows the logic of Fujifilm's dual-axis autofocus, pairing a behavior mode with an area mode.

Behavior choices include AF-S, AF-C and MF, while area options are Single Point, Zone, Wide-Tracking and All. A focus engine based on the thin lens equation gives even rack pulls from near to far, and an overlay draws AF point indicators in several shapes. The resulting focus is baked to keyframes. Useful for realistic depth-of-field animation.

Key features

  • Dual-axis AF with behavior and area modes
  • Diopter-space focus engine
  • DSLR-style viewport AF overlay
  • Bakes focus to keyframes
