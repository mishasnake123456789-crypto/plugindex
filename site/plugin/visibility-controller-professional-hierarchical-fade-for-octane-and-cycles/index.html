What Visibility Controller: Professional Hierarchical Fade For Octane & Cycles does

Visibility Controller is a Blender add-on for fading objects and collections in and out. It brings layer-style opacity to scenes rendered with Octane or Cycles.

Opacity set on a parent object or collection passes to all of its children instantly, and fades can be animated. For Octane, it controls general visibility directly, with separate handling for Cycles. Motion designers and animators building reveals or sequenced appearances can control entire hierarchies with a single value.

Key features

  • Hierarchical opacity inheritance
  • Octane and Cycles support
  • Animatable fades
  • Works on objects and collections
