What Photo Relief does

Photo Relief by bahae-el-hmimdi converts a picture into 3D geometry suitable for printing, whether a relief, a lithophane or a stamp. It is listed under Import-Export.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.

Key features

  • Turns images into printable reliefs
  • Lithophane and stamp output
