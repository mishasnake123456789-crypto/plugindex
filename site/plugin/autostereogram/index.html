What Autostereogram does

Autostereogram by Motion Boutique creates the 'magic eye' style of images in which a 3D shape appears when you relax your gaze, and it can animate them in After Effects.

It runs natively on Apple Silicon and supports After Effects 2021 through 2026. The minimum macOS version is 10.14 as of 1.0.1, and the current 1.0.2 release updates the licensing framework. Good for playful graphics and optical-illusion sequences. The results suit playful title sequences, experimental graphics and anything meant to surprise viewers with hidden depth.
