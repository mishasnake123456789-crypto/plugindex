What Graph In Motion V2 - After Effects Curves In Blender does

Graph In Motion V2 brings an After Effects feel to Blender's keyframe editing. Keyframes and handles can be edited for multiple objects at once, with custom handle control along the X and Y axes.

Curves adjust automatically for responsive handling, keyframes are collected from selected objects and materials, and curve presets can be saved, applied and managed. It suits motion designers used to easing in After Effects. Notable points: multi-object keyframe editing, X and Y handle control and collect keys from objects and materials.

Key features
