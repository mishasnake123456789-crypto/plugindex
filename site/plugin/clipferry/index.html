What ClipFerry does

ClipFerry sends chosen clips from Premiere Pro to a local Topaz Video AI install, processes them using your presets, and relinks the finished files back to the project. This avoids manual exporting and importing for upscaling or enhancement.

It requires a licensed local Topaz Video AI installation and is compatible with Premiere Pro 2026. It suits editors who regularly clean up or upscale footage with Topaz and want to avoid manual file handling.

Key features

  • Batch clips through Topaz Video AI presets
  • Runs on your own local install
  • Automatic relinking of results
  • Requires licensed Topaz Video AI
  • Compatible with Premiere Pro 2026
