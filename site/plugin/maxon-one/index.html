What Maxon One does

Maxon One gathers Maxon's creative software under one subscription umbrella: Cinema 4D, ZBrush, Redshift, Red Giant and Maxon Studio. The idea is that artists can move between modeling, rendering, compositing and editing without juggling separate installs and licenses.

It connects with Adobe hosts such as After Effects and Premiere Pro, and the Spring 2026 release adds Windows on Arm support for Cinema 4D, ZBrush and Red Giant in those hosts. Cinema 4D gains a Fabric brush for soft materials, and the free-for-individuals Autograph compositor joins the lineup. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux and needs Maxon App 2024.5 or newer plus an online connection.

Key features

  • Cinema 4D, ZBrush, Redshift and Red Giant in one bundle
  • Autograph compositor included, free for individuals
  • Windows on Arm support in the Spring 2026 release
  • Fabric brush for shaping soft materials in Cinema 4D
  • Runs on Windows, macOS and 64-bit Linux
