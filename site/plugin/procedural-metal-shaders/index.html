What Procedural Metal Shaders does

Procedural Metal Shaders is a set of six procedural metal materials for Blender, offered in gold, copper and rose gold. They are ready to apply to jewelry, props and product renders.

The pack carries an extended commercial license that covers unlimited physical or digital products for sale, unlimited advertisements including paid digital ones, and unlimited business and personal social media accounts. Documentation is included.
