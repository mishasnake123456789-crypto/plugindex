What Move Anchor Point 4 does

Move Anchor Point 4 from aescripts batchframe is an After Effects script that shifts a layer's anchor point to a new location while the layer stays visually in place. It works instantly and precisely with just a few clicks.

Its modular interface shows only the controls required for the task at hand, so you can find the setup that suits your project. The listing notes compatibility with After Effects 2020 and a demo video on the developer's page. Animators setting pivots for rotation and scale can use it to avoid manual position compensation.
