What Blender Depth Map Generator does

Blender Depth Map Generator creates depth images directly from 3D models without rendering. It is useful for height and displacement maps, sculpting brush alphas, 3D print references and depth passes for concept art.

Choose from six projection directions, let Auto-Align find the largest flat surface on a tilted model, and set resolution from 256 to 4096 with padding. Output is single-channel 16-bit grayscale as EXR, PNG or PSD, and the model can be exported as OBJ, FBX, GLB or BLEND. An invert toggle switches near and far, and meshes over 500K faces are handled.

Key features

  • BVH raycasting depth bake
  • Six projection directions and auto-align
  • 16-bit grayscale as EXR, PNG or PSD
  • Resolution from 256 to 4096
