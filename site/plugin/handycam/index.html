A camera rig for After Effects with orbit, look-at, millimetre lens settings, dolly-zoom and handheld wiggle.

What HandyCam does

HandyCam from Plugin Everything simplifies working with animated cameras in After Effects. Full orbit controls and look-at targeting, with focus on the target included, replace fiddly manual rigs.

Lens values are set in millimetres instead of degrees, a single click performs a dolly zoom, and wiggle controls simulate handheld shake. A baking function lets you hand a scene to computers or animators without HandyCam. Unlike some rigs it uses no hidden or parented layers, and it works with legacy and current expression engines. Motion designers animating 3D scenes gain control without complexity.

Key features

  • Orbit and look-at targeting with focus
  • Lens controls in millimetres
  • One-click dolly-zoom
  • Wiggle for handheld looks
  • Baking to share scenes without the plugin
