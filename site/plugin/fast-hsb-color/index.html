What Fast HSB Color does

Built by Data Power, Fast HSB Color is an extension running inside After Effects aimed at sampling and tuning the colors of layer properties. It adds an open color picker and coordinate system to the interface, with adjustment in HSB, HSL and RGB.

An eyedropper and screen picker sample pixels from anywhere, even outside After Effects. A ColorList gathers most color properties automatically. A Color Chessboard inspired by PCCS tone classification offers eight hue contrast schemes and tone grids, with multiple cursors to compare regions. Triangular and quadrilateral modes, keyboard shortcuts for saturation, brightness and hue, and 3D object color in After Effects 2026 are included.

Key features

  • HSB, HSL and RGB adjustment
  • Screen color picker works outside After Effects
  • Auto-detected ColorList of properties
  • PCCS-inspired Color Chessboard
  • Keyboard shortcuts for hue, saturation and brightness
