What miauu's CameraCycler does

CameraCycler by miauu makes switching between cameras in a 3ds Max scene a matter of two key presses. One hotkey moves forward through all the cameras in the scene, and another moves backward, so you can review each viewpoint quickly.

All cameras in the scene take part, and Autodesk 3ds Max plus 3ds Max Design 2012 and newer are supported. The current version is 1.0, last updated in October 2025. Artists checking multiple shots or angles for interiors, products or animation will save the repeated trips to the viewport menu or camera lists.

Key features

  • Cycle forward through scene cameras with a hotkey
  • Cycle backward with another hotkey
  • Operates on every camera in the scene
  • Works with 3ds Max and Max Design 2012 and later
