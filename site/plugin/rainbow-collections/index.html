What Rainbow Collections does

Rainbow Collections is a Blender add-on that makes the outliner easier to scan. One click assigns colors to your collections, and another button alphabetizes the scene collections.

A seed option changes the color variation, and an experimental Name Collect feature gathers objects with similar names into groups. Documentation is included. It helps artists with large scenes who want to tell collections apart at a glance. Among its tools are one-click collection colorization and alphabetize scene collections.

Key features

  • One-click collection colorization
  • Alphabetize scene collections
  • Seed for custom color variation
  • Experimental Name Collect grouping
