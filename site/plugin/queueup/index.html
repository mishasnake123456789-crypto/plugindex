Stores output folders and render settings per job in After Effects for quick preview and final renders.

What QueueUp does

QueueUp is a script that tracks where each job should render and which settings it uses. Create a job with the plus button, give it an output folder, and switch between deliverables during the day.

Preview and Final output settings come from templates you have already created. Previews render to the chosen folder, while finals go to a /finals subfolder. It works best docked from the ScriptUI folder, and supports After Effects 2021 through 2025.

Key features

  • Saves output folder and settings per job
  • Choose Preview and Final templates
  • Finals go to a /finals subfolder
  • Dockable script panel
  • Supports After Effects 2021 to 2025
