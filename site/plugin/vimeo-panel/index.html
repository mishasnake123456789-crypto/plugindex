What Vimeo Panel does

Vimeo Panel connects Premiere Pro to a Vimeo library. Editors can send videos to Vimeo, adjust their settings and replace a video that is already there, without switching to a browser.

Time-coded review comments sync back so feedback can be handled where the edit lives. Single sign-on is supported for extra security. It simplifies client review cycles for teams that publish through Vimeo. Everything is handled from one panel, so editors can publish and gather notes without juggling browser tabs.

Key features

  • Upload directly to Vimeo
  • Edit video settings in the panel
  • Replace existing videos
  • Sync time-coded review comments
  • Single sign-on support
