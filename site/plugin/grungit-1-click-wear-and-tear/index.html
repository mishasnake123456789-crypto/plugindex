What Grungit - 1-Click Wear And Tear does

Grungit adds weathering to Blender models with a single click, creating edge wear, grunge and dirt. Results can range from subtle imperfections to heavy damage.

It works with Eevee, keeps existing materials, supports Principled BSDF-based add-ons and does not depend on topology. One-click PBR baking outputs full PBR maps with automatic unwrapping. Advanced mode gives manual control, hand painting adds or removes grunge, and Quick mode skips edge wear for imperfections only. Prop artists and environment designers age assets fast.

Key features

  • One-click edge wear, grunge and dirt
  • One-click PBR baking
  • Keeps existing materials
  • Advanced mode and hand painting
  • Topology independent
