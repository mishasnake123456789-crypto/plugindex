Blender add-on that creates an adjustable dynamic sky for the Cycles render engine.

What Dynamic Sky does

Dynamic Sky sets up a sky for Blender scenes rendered in Cycles. Originally written by Pratik Solanki, it was bundled with Blender 4.1 and is now provided as it stands.

Its controls are in the 3D View sidebar's Create tab. The package is tiny, around 4 KB, installs by drag and drop or from disk, needs no special permissions and is released under GPL v2 or later. It supports Blender 4.2 LTS and newer. Artists who need a quick environment backdrop can add one in moments.

Key features

  • Creates a dynamic sky for Cycles
  • Panel in sidebar Create tab
  • Installs by drag and drop or from disk
  • For Blender 4.2 LTS and newer
