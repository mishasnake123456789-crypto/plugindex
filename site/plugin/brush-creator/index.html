Batch-builds Blender sculpt brush assets from folders of alpha images and VDM EXR files.

What Brush Creator does

Brush Creator turns a folder of alpha images or VDM EXRs into a complete set of sculpt brushes in one run. Point it at the folder, choose an Asset Library and Catalog, set brush options and build.

The Asset Library dropdown reads your registered libraries and brushes are saved into the catalog name you enter. Settings such as strength are applied consistently across the batch. It saves hours of making brushes one at a time.

Key features

  • Batch brushes from alpha folders
  • Asset Library and catalog assignment
  • Consistent settings across the batch
  • VDM EXR support
