What Mirror and Rotate Video Toolbar does

Mirror and Rotate Video Toolbar from Vakago Tools adds a small toolbar for fixing clip orientation in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Select one or several clips in the timeline or composition, then press a button to apply the rotation.

Because it sets orientation on multiple clips at once, it spares editors from opening each clip's transform settings, which is handy with footage shot sideways or upside down. It works with After Effects 18.0 and later and Premiere Pro 15.0 and later, and the listing marks it as free.
