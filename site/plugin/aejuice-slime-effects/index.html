What AEJuice Slime Effects does

AEJuice Slime Effects provides 78 gooey, dripping animations that can be dropped straight into a project. They are organised in five categories and delivered as MOV ProRes 4444 with RGB and alpha, with black-background versions for compositing.

Resolution is 3840x2160, both vertical and horizontal, at 23.98 fps, and clip lengths run from under a second up to 10 and 20 seconds. The standalone Pack Manager works without any video editor, with an interface in nine languages. After Effects, Premiere Pro and other editors can use them for transitions, title accents or messy, playful visuals.

Key features
