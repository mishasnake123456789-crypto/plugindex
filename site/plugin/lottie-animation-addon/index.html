What Lottie Animation Addon does

Lottie Animation Addon turns a Blender timeline into a .lottie file in one click, targeting web and app use. The export follows Lottie 5.9.4 and packs the Lottie JSON and images into a ZIP archive.

PNG gives the widest compatibility, while WebP produces files about 50 to 80 percent smaller. Motion designers who want 3D-rendered animation on websites without video files will find a compact delivery format. The result is easy to embed in websites and apps.

Key features

  • One-click export of the whole timeline
  • Lottie 5.9.4 format
  • PNG or WebP image compression
  • Packaged as a .lottie ZIP
