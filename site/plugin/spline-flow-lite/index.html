What Spline Flow Lite does

Spline Flow Lite, by KSTUDIO, creates sets of splines that blend from one shape into another and follow a chosen path, which makes it possible to form complex objects from simple curves.

Distribution and curvature of the splines are adjustable, and many parameters can be animated, from subtle drifts to larger transformations. Support for 3ds Max 2027 has been added, and the tool uses a custom installer rather than the standard App Store one. Designers making parametric forms, ribbons, abstract structures or motion graphics can build them procedurally.

Key features

  • Splines that flow from one to the next along a path
  • Complex objects built from the splines
  • Control of distribution and curvature
  • Animatable parameters
  • 3ds Max 2027 support
