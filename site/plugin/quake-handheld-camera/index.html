What Quake Handheld Camera does

Quake Handheld Camera is a procedural Blender add-on that adds realistic camera movement to shots. One click creates the effect, without motion tracking or hand-editing F-curves.

More than 30 presets cover walking, phone footage, running and car chases, and a random motion generator produces shakes on a chosen axis. It suits animators and VFX artists who want footage to feel filmed rather than perfectly locked off. Because the motion is generated procedurally, it can be adjusted or replaced easily, letting you try different levels of intensity before settling on a final look.

Key features

  • One-tap procedural handheld motion
  • 30+ presets such as phone, running and car chase
  • Random shake generator per axis
  • No tracking or F-curve editing
