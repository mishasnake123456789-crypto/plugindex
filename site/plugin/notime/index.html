3ds Max script that hides or restores the time slider and track bar, handy when modeling or working on stills.

What NoTime does

NoTime hides the time slider and track bar in 3ds Max with a single action, and brings them back with another. When you are modeling or working on a still, those interface elements take up space and add nothing.

It can be used as a toolbar button or as a quad menu item, so it is always one click away. The script is by Jon Seagull and does exactly one job. Artists who want a little more room in the viewport area, or a cleaner interface while not animating, will find it a convenient switch.

Key features

  • Toggles time slider and track bar together
  • Meant for stills and modeling
  • Toolbar button
  • Also available as a quad menu item
