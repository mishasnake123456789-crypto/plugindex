What Complete Quads does

Complete Quads speeds up retopology and clean-up modeling in Blender by finishing a quad from partial geometry. It works from two edges or three vertices that share a corner vertex.

Rectangles, squares and parallelograms are supported, and Shift+R fills the quad. An optional proximity merge merges by distance near the selection, several quads can be completed at once, and warnings and tooltips explain what is wrong. Controls are also in the sidebar.

Key features

  • Fills quads from edges or vertices
  • Rectangles, squares and parallelograms
  • Optional proximity merge
  • Multi-complete with Shift+R
