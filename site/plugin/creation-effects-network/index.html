What Creation Effects: Network does

Creation Effects: Network is a native After Effects tool that generates webs of points joined by lines, handy for tech graphics, data visualisations, audio visualisers and small particle scenes. Everything is built from shape layers.

Sliders drive complex formations procedurally. Spread and Wiggle scatters or jiggles points, Grid Spacing builds even 2D or 3D grids, and points can follow a drawn path or animated mask. A leader layer creates swarming trails, and a repeller attracts or pushes points. Lines may be dotted, lightning, light beams or audio-driven waveforms. Manual 3D placement or null parenting keeps connections intact, and a point precomp accepts your own artwork.

Key features

  • Slider-driven point formations
  • Spread, Wiggle and grid spacing
  • Position along path, leader and repeller layers
  • Dotted, lightning, beam and audio waveform lines
  • Custom artwork through point precomp
