What Greeblefx: Non-Destructive Greeble Generator does

Greeblefx, by AlbertoFX, makes simple Blender models look intricate by covering them with surface detail. It offers two generation types, Basic for panels and Advanced for greebles and nurnies, applied in a couple of clicks.

The workflow is non-destructive, so the original low-poly object stays intact and settings can be changed later. An intuitive interface gives instant results without constant menu hopping, appealing to sci-fi and hard-surface artists who want complex-looking assets quickly.

Key features

  • Basic panel and Advanced greeble generation
  • Non-destructive workflow
