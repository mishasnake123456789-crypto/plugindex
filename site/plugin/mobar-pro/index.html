What MoBar Pro does

MoBar Pro gives After Effects users a way to arrange their most-used tools into personalised panels. Favourite effects, presets and scripts can sit in one place, and more than 150 prebuilt tools are included for layers, keyframes, easing, compositions, projects, guides and views.

Among them is a Composition Settings tool offering a cleaner alternative to the native dialog, with a time format toggle, preset management and arrow-key background colour tweaks. Null Controllers for Points builds null controllers for chosen vertices of shape or mask paths. A free version needs no registration, and After Effects 17.5 or later is required.

Key features

  • Custom panels for effects, presets and scripts
  • 150+ prebuilt tools
  • Cleaner Composition Settings alternative
  • Null Controllers for path vertices
  • Free version without registration
