What Quill Import/Export does

Quill Import/Export by Joan-Charmant moves work between Blender and Quill, the VR painting and animation tool. It handles Quill's own authoring format, the .json and .qbin files, in both directions rather than the compressed .imm files.

The Quill layer hierarchy comes across, with groups becoming Empties and paint layers becoming objects, and cameras, images and keyframes are handled as well. Artists who sketch in VR and finish in Blender can pass scenes back and forth.

Key features

  • Reads and writes Quill .json and .qbin files
  • Imports groups as Empties and paint layers as objects
  • Handles cameras, images and keyframes
  • Exports back to Quill's authoring format
