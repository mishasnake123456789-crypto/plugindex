What Pixelan DissolveMaster does

Pixelan DissolveMaster builds film, soft directional, blur and glow/flash dissolves in After Effects and Premiere Pro, using hardware acceleration. It can also animate other video effects inside one clip, including third-party plugins, such as color shifts, tonal changes and blurs.

Dissolves flow by luminance or by hue and color range, with adjustable softness, and you can click the image to set start or end locations. Nearly 500 presets, mixable section presets and an animated Effects Browser help. Iris geometry, blend modes, edge glows and Bezier keyframing with ease, hold and acceleration add control.

Key features

  • Film, directional, blur and glow dissolves
  • Animates other effects within a single clip
  • Luminance or hue-based dissolve flow
  • Nearly 500 presets with Effects Browser
  • Bezier keyframing with ease, hold and acceleration
