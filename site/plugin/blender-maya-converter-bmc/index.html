What Blender Maya Converter (Bmc) does

Blender Maya Converter, from Nava Lab, moves scene elements between the two applications. SEND and GET buttons carry meshes, lights, cameras and materials across in either direction.

Shading and lighting are converted automatically to match each program's render system, including V-Ray lights and materials, and a Save Setting option stores your preferences. Studios and artists who work in both packages can avoid rebuilding materials and lights by hand.

Key features

  • SEND and GET between Blender and Maya
  • Transfers meshes, lights, cameras and materials
  • Supports V-Ray lights and materials
  • Automatic shading and lighting conversion
  • Saved settings
