What AEJuice Footages to Comps does

AEJuice Footages to Comps is an After Effects script for filling templates quickly. It imports many photos and videos into selected compositions, scaling each to fit, and can populate more than 100 placeholders in a few clicks.

It handles both composition placeholders and regular layers. Footage can replace placeholders in the selected order, or be shuffled randomly with a single click. This is useful for slideshows, social templates and any project with big batches of media to place.

Key features

  • Batch imports photos and videos into comps
  • Auto-resizes each item
  • Fills 100+ placeholders in a few clicks
  • Supports comp placeholders and regular layers
  • Ordered or shuffled replacement
