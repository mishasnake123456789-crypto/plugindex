Changes the speed of audio clips in Premiere Pro without shifting their pitch.

What Perfect Pitch does

Perfect Pitch, by Leyero, lets editors speed up or slow down audio and keep the voice or music at its original pitch. Normally, speed changes make audio sound chipmunk-like or deep.

Version 1.1.0 improved how selected clips are targeted. It integrates with Spell Book, has a short built-in tutorial, and supports Premiere Pro 2024 through 2026. That makes it useful for retiming dialogue and music to fit a cut without the voice sounding unnatural.

Key features

  • Speed changes without pitch shift
  • More stable clip targeting in 1.1.0
  • Spell Book integration
  • Built-in quick tutorial
  • Supports Premiere Pro 2024 to 2026
