What Creation Effects: 3D Flip Book Effect does

3D Flip Book Effect from Creation Effects turns video clips or still images into the pages of a flipping book. Four preset compositions can be exported directly or used as a base for your own versions.

The scene offers 21 paper textures and 4 table surfaces, plus an optional comic effect that cartoonises footage. A Control Layer in each preset exposes dozens of options, including page-turn speed and frequency and content on one or both sides. The book can open, close, move and rotate in 3D, turned pages can separate for a card-toss look, and lights, camera, bending, thickness and dimensions are editable with unlimited pages.

Key features

  • Four flip book preset comps
  • 21 paper textures and 4 table surfaces
  • Optional comic book effect
  • Control Layer for speed, sides and 3D position
  • Editable lights, camera, bending and thickness
