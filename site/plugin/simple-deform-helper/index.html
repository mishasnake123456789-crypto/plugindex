Blender add-on that adds on-screen gizmos for the Simple Deform modifier to adjust bend, twist and strength.

What Simple Deform Helper does

Simple Deform Helper adds viewport gizmos to Blender's Simple Deform modifier so you do not need the properties panel. After selecting an object and adding the modifier, you adjust deformation by dragging them.

Red handles set the upper limit, green the lower limit and purple the deform strength. Holding Ctrl allows axis locking and relative-distance dragging. It supports meshes, lattices and curves, matches limits automatically in all modes, and corrects bending orientation in one click. Modelers bending or twisting objects save trips to the properties panel.

Key features

  • Viewport gizmos for limits and strength
  • Axis locking with X, Y, Z
  • Mesh, lattice and curve support
  • One-click bend orientation correction
