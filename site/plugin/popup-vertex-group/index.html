Developer: Rubel

Blender add-on that opens a popup for managing vertex groups with search, locking, renaming and bone-aware actions.

What Popup Vertex Group does

Rubel's Popup Vertex Group brings vertex group operations into one floating window in Blender. Search, including multiple search, finds the groups you need.

You can lock or unlock, add or delete, activate, rename, copy and move groups. Bone-aware commands rename or remove a group together with its bone, across several armatures. Vertices can be assigned, removed, selected and deselected without changing the active group. Riggers with many weight groups reach them faster.

Key features

  • Popup window for vertex group operations
  • Search and multiple search
  • Lock, rename, copy and move groups
  • Rename or delete with its bone
  • Assign and select vertices
