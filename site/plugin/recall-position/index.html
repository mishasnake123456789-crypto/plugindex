Blender add-on that stores object positions and rotations and restores them with a click from a 3D view panel.

What Recall Position does

Recall Position saves the transform state of any object in a Blender scene and lets you jump back to it later.

You can recall position, rotation or both, and delete saved states you no longer need. Everything is in a panel in the 3D view. It supports Blender 3.4, 3.5, 3.6 and 4.0, and is useful for animators and modelers who experiment with placement but want a quick way back.

Key features

  • Save position and rotation states
  • Recall with one click
  • Recall position, rotation or both
  • Delete unneeded states
  • Panel in the 3D view
