What Clothify does

Clothify automates the complicated setup behind bags, pouches, gift sacks and wrapped products. One click adds and sets up Hooks, Cloth, Collision, Solidify and Subdivision modifiers.

Physics presets can be stored and reloaded, and Silk, Leather, Denim, Cotton and Velvet come built in. A cinching animation is generated automatically, and the finishing steps are handled for you. Product visualizers wanting convincing packaging simulation can avoid the technical wiring. Presets keep fabric behavior consistent across several shots or products.

Key features

  • One-click Hooks, Cloth, Collision, Solidify and Subdivision setup
  • Presets for Silk, Leather, Denim, Cotton, Velvet
  • Automatic cinching animation
  • Save and load physics settings
