What Hidebar: Visibility Control Center does

Hidebar: Visibility Control Center lets you assemble your own toggle panels in Blender so you can show or hide parts of a scene without opening the Outliner.

Panels can hold Collections, Objects and Mask modifiers, which is useful when a scene has many parts that must switch on and off often. It ships as a Blender Extension, works with Cycles and Eevee, includes documentation, and comes with support and updates from Hyper.

Key features

  • Custom toggle panels
  • Controls collections, objects and Mask modifiers
  • Avoids navigating the Outliner
  • Blender Extension format
  • Works with Cycles and Eevee
