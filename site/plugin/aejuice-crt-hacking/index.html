What AEJuice CRT Hacking does

AEJuice CRT Hacking is a small pack of overlay clips themed around computer intrusion. Scrolling code, warning screens and further digital graphics sit on black with a vintage CRT finish, ready to drop above a shot in After Effects, Premiere Pro or another editor.

There are 8 elements, delivered in 4K as H.264 at 30 fps. They are managed through the AEJuice Pack Manager, which provides quick preview, drag and drop import and a favourites list. Because black backgrounds can be removed with a blend mode, the overlays suit thriller scenes, tech explainers and cyber-themed titles where a believable terminal look is wanted.
