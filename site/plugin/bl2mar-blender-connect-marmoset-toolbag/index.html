Blender add-on that readies bake meshes and sends them to Marmoset Toolbag for texture baking.

What Bl2Mar(Blender Connect Marmoset Toolbag) does

PJ PENG's Bl2Mar connects Blender to Marmoset Toolbag for baking. It gathers objects into a bake project and pushes the meshes to Toolbag, after which textures can proceed to Photoshop and Substance Painter.

Collections are prepared with automatic renaming of _low and _high meshes through center-point or bounding-box checks, using Re_Do and Re_Mul modes. A bake project can start from a low-poly material, or an ID map from a high-poly one, and meshes are exported or replaced in Toolbag. Texture artists streamline handoffs.

Key features

  • Auto-renaming of _low and _high meshes
  • Center-point or bounding-box matching
  • Bake project from a low mesh material
  • ID map from a high mesh material
  • Export or replace meshes in Toolbag
