Blender add-on that appends collection names to object names, helping match high and low poly pairs for baking.

What B-Rename does

Bake setups depend on consistent names. CGjazziQ's B-Rename speeds that up by adding each object's collection name as a suffix, which makes it easy to pair hipoly and lopoly assets.

It isn't limited to meshes; curves, lights, cameras, bones and other object kinds can be renamed too. The add-on is documented, GPL licensed and listed for Blender 2.8 through 4.2. Anyone who prepares lots of assets for baking will skip a tedious manual renaming round.

Key features

  • Adds collection names as suffixes
  • Prepares high and low poly pairs for baking
  • Works on meshes, curves, lights, cameras and bones
  • Blender 2.8 to 4.2
  • Documentation included
