Records Blender animation with one RECORD button that keyframes location, rotation, scale and visibility.

What Recorder Pro does

Recorder Pro simplifies keyframing. Rather than selecting each object and property, press RECORD or Shift+R and all selected items are keyed at once.

L, R, S and V buttons choose location, rotation, scale or visibility, and it works on objects, cameras, lights and bones in Pose Mode. A dedicated property handles visibility animation in the viewport. It speeds up blocking and quick animation passes from the sidebar panel. Notable points: single RECORD button or Shift+R, location, rotation, scale, visibility toggles and objects, cameras, lights and bones.
