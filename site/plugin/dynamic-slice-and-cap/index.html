What Dynamic Slice And Cap does

Dynamic Slice And Cap helps create section views of models in Blender, working like the Slice and Cap Holes modifiers in 3ds Max. Both slicing and capping can be animated and rendered.

Slice modes cover Remove Bottom, Remove Top, Split Mesh and Refine Mesh. Cap Holes supports material indexes and vertex groups for the capped surfaces. Workflow can be dynamic or manual, and it works with vertex groups and modifiers. Great for architectural cutaways.

Key features

  • Slice with refine, split, remove top or bottom
  • Cap Holes with material index and vertex groups
  • Dynamic and manual workflows
  • Animatable and renderable
