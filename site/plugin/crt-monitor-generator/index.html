What Crt Monitor Generator does

CRT Monitor Generator uses the compositor to give images the look of an old cathode-ray tube display. It simulates phosphor grids, scanlines and signal degradation with a node-based setup.

Ten presets range from classic television to damaged signals and retro terminals. Phosphor grid scale, sharpness and emission can be tuned, scanline density and sharpness adjusted, and the signal stage offers RGB shift, blur and hue controls. A post stack adds glow, vignette and lens distortion. Node groups are created automatically and run in the real-time compositor.

Key features

  • Ten CRT and retro presets
  • Phosphor and scanline simulation
  • RGB shift, blur, glow, vignette, distortion
  • Real-time compositor feedback
