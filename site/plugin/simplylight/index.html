Blender add-on that creates an animated circular light rig around an object in one click.

What Simplylight does

SimplyLight builds a light that orbits your object, auto-sized to the model's radius and height. It suits product turntables, hero shots and quick look-dev.

The light stays aimed at the target, with animatable pitch and yaw. Properties can be changed in the add-on or Blender's light panel, several lights can be used on the same object, and the curve can be edited. Up to six positions and six lighting parameter sets can be stored and combined for comparison.

Key features

  • One-click circular light rig
  • Auto-sized and aimed at the target
  • Animatable pitch and yaw orbit
  • Six stored positions and parameter sets
