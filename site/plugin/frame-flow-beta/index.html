What Frame Flow Beta does

Frame Flow Beta helps keep large node setups understandable. It adds smart frames that group and label nodes, plus multiline comments for explaining what a section does.

Emoji and Unicode characters are supported, with searchable categories and clipboard copy, and the look of each frame can be tuned through font, size, color and padding. It is useful for artists who share node trees or come back to complicated shader and geometry setups later.

Key features

  • Smart frames to group and name nodes
  • Multiline comments
  • Emoji and Unicode with searchable categories
  • Customizable font, size, color and padding
