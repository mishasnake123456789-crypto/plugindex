What Filmstro Pro does

Filmstro Pro works as a scoring assistant inside Premiere Pro. Rather than picking a fixed track, you keyframe the emotion of a soundtrack to follow the picture, and the music adapts.

The tracks are royalty-free, cleared for commercial and client work worldwide in perpetuity, with unlimited downloads and custom creations. Two workstations can be used, YouTube claim release includes channel whitelisting, and results are deterministic with no AI involved. It lets editors fit music to the mood of a cut instead of cutting picture around a fixed track.

Key features

  • Keyframe soundtrack emotion to the edit
  • Royalty-free, cleared for commercial work
