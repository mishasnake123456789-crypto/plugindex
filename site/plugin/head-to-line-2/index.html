What Head to Line 2 does

Head to Line 2 is Matin Kazerooni's tool for After Effects that converts a plain line into an animated arrow in one click. It is aimed at infographics, annotations and explainer videos that need arrows to draw themselves on.

Built-in heads include triangle, circle and rectangle, and a custom shape can be used. Direction, speed and head type can be changed at any time, and double-sided arrows need just a checkbox. Sliders for head size, angle and roundness can be animated, timing controls set duration, and a single click removes the setup from a layer.

Key features

  • One-click line to animated arrow
  • Triangle, circle, rectangle or custom heads
  • Double-sided arrow checkbox
  • Animatable head size, angle and roundness
  • Duration controls and one-click removal
