What Half Knife does

Half Knife is a quicker knife tool for Blender. Press K, then click where the cut should end; a prior selection is optional.

Multicuts work from face or edge mode with up to ten initial vertices. Modal options include Shift to switch off snapping, Z to cut through, Ctrl for center snapping including the middle of a face, Alt for an alternate center snap, and C for angle constraints. Modelers cleaning topology and adding loops save steps.

Key features

  • Works with or without a selection
  • Multicuts from face or edge mode
  • Modal snapping and cut-through options
  • Angle constraint with C
