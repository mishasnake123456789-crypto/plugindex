What Yarui - Boost Your Render Time does

YARUI applies neural networks to speed up Blender rendering. Rather than rendering every pixel and frame at full cost, it upscales frames and creates in-between frames.

It runs on the NCNN inference engine and works with Blender-Internal, Cycles and Eevee. Upscaling and interpolation happen inside the Blender workflow. A full PDF guide covers installation, usage, system requirements, FAQ and changelogs. Animators with long sequences can render at lower resolution or frame count and recover the rest afterward.

Key features

  • Deep learning frame upscaling
  • Frame interpolation for smoother motion
  • Powered by the NCNN engine
