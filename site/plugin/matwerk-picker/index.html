What MatWerk Picker does

MatWerk Picker by WERK lets you sample a material directly from what you see in Blender, then edit it in place next to the cursor instead of digging through the Properties editor. It is categorised under Material.

The add-on is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later and requires Blender 4.2.0 or newer.
